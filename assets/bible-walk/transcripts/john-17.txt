The talking is over. Jesus has washed their feet, comforted them, warned them, and promised them the Spirit. Now he lifts his eyes to heaven, and begins to pray out loud, while his disciples listen. They hear him pray for himself, and for them. And then, they hear him pray for people who have not yet been born. He prays for you.
In chapter sixteen, Jesus promised that the Holy Spirit, the Spirit of truth, would guide his disciples into all truth. He said their sorrow would be turned into joy. And he ended with the words, in the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.
Now, in chapter seventeen, we hear the longest recorded prayer of Jesus. It is sometimes called his high priestly prayer, because he stands before the Father on behalf of his people, as a priest. Listen to what Jesus asks for, in the hours before the cross.
These words speaks Jesus, and lifts up his eyes to heaven, and says, Father, the hour is come. Glorify thy Son, that thy Son also may glorify thee.
As thou hast given him power over all flesh, that he should give eternal life to as many as thou hast given him. And then Jesus explains what eternal life is.
I have glorified thee on the earth, Jesus prays. I have finished the work which thou gavest me to do. And now, O Father, glorify thou me with thine own self with the glory which I had with thee before the world was.
Then Jesus prays for his disciples. I have manifested thy name unto the men which thou gavest me out of the world. Thine they were, and thou gavest them me, and they have kept thy word. Now they have known that all things whatsoever thou hast given me are of thee.
I pray for them, Jesus says. I pray not for the world, but for them which thou hast given me, for they are thine. And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. 'Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.'
While I was with them in the world, Jesus says, I kept them in thy name. Those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but the son of perdition, that the scripture might be fulfilled. He is speaking of Judas.
And now come I to thee, Jesus says, and these things I speak in the world, that they might have my joy fulfilled in themselves. I have given them thy word, and the world hath hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.
Then Jesus says something important. 'I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.' Jesus does not ask the Father to remove his followers from the world. He asks the Father to protect them in it.
Then Jesus asks the Father to set them apart.
As thou hast sent me into the world, Jesus says, even so have I also sent them into the world. And for their sakes I sanctify myself, that they also might be sanctified through the truth.
And then Jesus prays for us. 'Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word'. Everyone who has ever believed in Jesus through the message of the apostles was in Jesus’ prayer.
And what does Jesus ask for us? First of all, unity.
And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them, Jesus prays, that they may be one, even as we are one. I in them, and thou in me, that they may be made perfect in one, and that the world may know that thou hast sent me, and hast loved them, as thou hast loved me.
Father, I will that they also, whom thou hast given me, be with me where I am, that they may behold my glory. Jesus wants his people to be with him for ever.
O righteous Father, the world hath not known thee, but I have known thee, and these have known that thou hast sent me. And I have declared unto them thy name, and will declare it, that the love wherewith thou hast loved me may be in them, and I in them.
Now, three verses to hold on to.
Verse 3. 'And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.' Many people think of eternal life as simply living for ever. But Jesus defines it differently. Eternal life is knowing God. Not just knowing about him, but knowing him personally, as a friend knows a friend. It begins now, and it never ends. The heart of eternal life is a relationship.
Verse 17. 'Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.' To sanctify means to set apart for God, to make holy. Jesus prayed that his followers would be made holy, and he said how: through the truth of God’s word. As we read, hear and obey the Bible, God shapes us, little by little, into the people he wants us to be. Thy word is truth. In a world full of confusion, God’s word is the solid ground.
Verse 21. 'That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me.' Jesus prayed that all who believe in him would be one, united in love, as the Father and the Son are one. And he said why: that the world may believe. When believers love each other and stand together, the world sees something of God. When we are divided and quarrelling, we hide him. Jesus’ prayer for us is that we would be one.
Paul wrote, 'There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling; One Lord, one faith, one baptism'. And the letter to the Hebrews says that Jesus 'ever liveth to make intercession for them.' He is still praying for his people.
Remember today that Jesus prayed for you, by name, before you were born, and that he still prays for you. And think of a fellow believer you are not at peace with. Take a step toward unity, so that the world may see Jesus.
Here is today’s question. Jesus prayed that all who believe in him would be one. What could you do to help the believers around you live in unity and love?
Let us pray. Holy Father, thank you that Jesus prayed for me, and still prays for me. Keep me from evil, and sanctify me through your truth. Help me to know you more, for this is eternal life. And make us one, all who believe in Jesus, so that the world may believe that you sent him. Amen.
