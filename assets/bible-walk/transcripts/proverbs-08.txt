Imagine standing in a busy market square. Everyone is rushing about, buying and selling, shouting and bargaining. And there, at the gate, someone is calling out. Not selling anything. Giving something away. Come and listen! I have something better than silver, better than gold. Most people hurry past. But a few stop, and listen. And their lives are changed for ever.
In chapter seven, a father looked out of his window and saw a young man void of understanding walk straight into temptation, like a bird into a trap. Keep my commandments, and live, the father said. Write them on the table of thine heart. And let not thine heart decline to her ways.
Now, in chapter eight, Wisdom herself speaks. Listen for what she offers, and to whom. And listen to her amazing description of the creation of the world.
The chapter begins with a question. 'Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding put forth her voice?' Is not Wisdom calling out? Remember, Proverbs pictures wisdom as a woman. And where does she stand? Not hidden away in a library, or a temple, or a school. She stands on the hilltops, beside the roads, at the crossroads, at the city gates, where everyone passes by.
'Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.' Wisdom calls to everyone. 'O ye simple, understand wisdom: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart.' Even the simple, even fools, are invited.
And Wisdom tells us what kind of words she speaks. 'Hear; for I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things.' My mouth speaks truth. Wickedness is hateful to me. All my words are right. Nothing is twisted or crooked. 'They are all plain to him that understandeth'.
Then Wisdom makes an offer. 'Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold.'
And then Wisdom describes herself. I live with prudence, which means good sense. I find knowledge and discretion. 'The fear of the LORD is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate.'
Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom, she says. Understanding and strength belong to me. 'By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.' Every good ruler, every fair judge, needs wisdom.
And then a lovely promise.
Riches and honour are with me, Wisdom says, lasting riches and righteousness. 'My fruit is better than gold, yea, than fine gold'. I walk in the way of righteousness, and give an inheritance to those who love me.
And then comes the most amazing part of the chapter. Wisdom tells us where she came from.
'The LORD possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old. I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, or ever the earth was.' Before there were oceans or springs, before the mountains and hills, before God made the earth and the fields, wisdom was there.
'When he prepared the heavens, I was there'. When he drew the horizon on the face of the deep. When he set the clouds in the sky, and made the fountains of the deep. When he told the sea where to stop, so its waters would not go beyond his command. When he laid the foundations of the earth.
'Then I was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him; Rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth; and my delights were with the sons of men.'
What a beautiful picture. When God made the world, wisdom was right beside him, like a joyful child, delighting in everything he made. And wisdom especially delighted in people.
Many Christians have seen in this passage a picture that points to Jesus. The New Testament calls Jesus the wisdom of God. And John writes that all things were made by him. Christians have understood these verses in different ways, but all agree that the wisdom God used to create the world is the same wisdom he offers to us.
Then Wisdom finishes with an appeal. 'Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children: for blessed are they that keep my ways.' Listen to instruction, and be wise. Do not reject it. 'Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.' Like someone eagerly waiting at a friend’s door every day.
And the chapter ends with a choice.
Now, three verses to hold on to.
Verse 11. 'For wisdom is better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.' Rubies are precious red jewels. Think of everything you might want: money, possessions, success, popularity. None of it compares with wisdom. Why? Because wisdom shows us how to live, and leads us to God. Riches can be lost. But wisdom stays with us, and makes our whole life richer.
Verse 17. 'I love them that love me; and those that seek me early shall find me.' Seek me early means seek me first, seek me eagerly, before anything else. This is a wonderful promise. Wisdom is not hiding from us. If we love her, and look for her, we will find her. God does not keep wisdom away from those who truly want it. He gives it to all who seek.
Verse 35. 'For whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the LORD.' Whoever finds wisdom finds life, and the favour, the kindness, of the LORD. But the next verse says that those who reject wisdom harm themselves. Wisdom is not just about being clever. It is about life itself. And for Christians, the wisdom of God has a name: Jesus, who said, I am the way, the truth, and the life.
Paul wrote of 'Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.' And John wrote, 'All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.'
Wisdom calls out every day, in God’s Word, in good advice, and in the quiet voice of conscience. Where might Wisdom be calling to you today? Stop, and listen. And seek her early, before everything else crowds in.
Here is today’s question. Wisdom says she is better than rubies, and that nothing you desire can compare with her. If you really believed that, how might it change what you spend your time and energy on?
Let us pray. Lord God, thank you that your wisdom calls out to everyone, even the simple. Thank you that the same wisdom you used to make the world, you offer to me. Help me to love wisdom and seek it early. Thank you for Jesus, the wisdom of God. Whoever finds him finds life. Amen.
