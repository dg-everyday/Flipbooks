# Strong's dictionaries

`hebrew.json` and `greek.json` are James Strong's Hebrew (1894) and Greek (1890)
dictionaries, from the JSON edition by Open Scriptures
(https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs), licensed CC BY-SA. They were
reformatted by `tools/strongs/build_strongs.py` and are shared under the same
licence. Credit "Strong's dictionaries: Open Scriptures, CC BY-SA" wherever
they are shown.
