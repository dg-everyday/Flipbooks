    How to use Daily Grace

    Today's devotional

    • Swipe through the week. Swipe the banner right to go back a day, up to a week. Swipe left to come forward again.

    • Open the Flipbook. Tap the round book button at the top right of the banner. An earlier day opens on that day's week.

    • Turn the Flipbook's pages. Swipe, or tap the left or right edge of the page. Pinch, or use the mouse wheel, to zoom in on a page.

    • Read the whole reflection. Tap the reflection under the banner to show all of it.

    • See the comic strip. Tap the day's verse poster and it turns like a page to the comic. Tap it again to turn back.

    • Share the poster. Press and hold the poster, or the comic, for half a second to copy its link. Paste it in a message to share it.

    • Listen. Tap the play button on the poster to hear the day's narration.

    Bible search

    • Look up a passage. Type a reference such as Ephesians 2:8-9 in the search box at the top. Start typing a book's name and pick it from the list that appears.

    • Search by words. Tap the padlock to unlock it, then type words. You get every verse that has all of them.

    • Bookmark a verse. Press and hold a verse for half a second. It glows gold and gets a ribbon. Hold it again to remove the bookmark. You can keep up to 100.

    • Show your bookmarks. Press and hold the search box. Tap Bible Verses, Did You Know or Bible Sayings at the top to switch between them. Hold a bookmark there to remove it, which makes room when a list is full. Bookmarks are saved on this device.

    • Understand a verse. Tap the folded corner of a verse to read its explanation. The words of Jesus are printed in red.

    More to explore

    • About Daily Grace. Tap the DG emblem at the top left of the page to read what Daily Grace is and how it can help you each day.

    • Facts and sayings. Tap the refresh button on "Did you know?" or the Bible sayings for a new set. Tap a saying to see all of it, or a Bible reference to read the passage.

    • Bookmark facts and sayings. Press and hold a fact or a saying for half a second to bookmark it. They are kept with your verses: press and hold the search box to see them. You can keep up to 50 of each.

    • Bible trivia. Now and then, up to three times a day, a quiz pops up with a Bible fact. Pick the book it comes from and see if you are right.

    • Get in touch. Tap the QR code to see a bigger one to scan, and how to sponsor or donate.

    Daily Grace

    Grace for today. Faith for tomorrow.

    What is Daily Grace?

    Daily Grace is a small daily habit: a few quiet minutes with God's Word, wherever you happen to be.

    Most of us mean to read the Bible more. Then the alarm goes off, the kids need breakfast, work piles up, and the Bible stays on the shelf at home.

    Daily Grace doesn't ask you to set aside an hour or find a quiet room. It meets you in the five spare minutes you already have: on the bus, in the break room, in line at the store, or before you fall asleep.

    Your Pocket Bible

    Daily Grace is your Digital Pocket Bible. The whole Bible is on the phone that's already in your pocket, so you don't need to carry anything extra.

    It's more than a Bible to read. It's one you can make your own:

    • Keep the verses that speak to you. When a verse stops you in your tracks, save it. Next time you need it, on a hard day or when a friend is hurting, it's right there. You won't have to remember "it was somewhere in Psalms."
    • Hold onto what you learn. The fascinating Bible facts, the old sayings that turn out to come from Scripture, and the thoughts that come to you while reading can all be kept and come back to later.
    • Look anything up in seconds. If a pastor mentions a passage or a friend quotes a verse, you can find it right away.

    Think of it like the well-worn Bible your grandmother kept, with its underlined verses, notes in the margins, and slips of paper marking favorite pages. It's the same idea, but it goes everywhere with you.

    What you'll find each day

    Every day brings something new:

    • A verse for the day, with a short, honest reflection on what it means for ordinary life.

    • A picture to go with it: a poster, a comic strip, and a voice that reads it aloud when your eyes are tired.

    • "Did you know?": surprising facts from the Bible that you can share over dinner.

    • Bible sayings: everyday phrases like "the writing on the wall" or "a drop in the bucket" that turn out to come from Scripture.

    • A weekly flipbook to page through, like a little magazine for your faith.

    How it can change your life

    Real change rarely comes from one big moment. It comes from small things done faithfully, day after day.

    1. You'll start your day on the right foot. A single verse in the morning can change how you handle a tough meeting, a traffic jam, or a hard conversation.
    2. God's Word will be close when you need it most. Fear, grief, temptation and worry don't wait until you're home with your Bible. When they come, the verses you've saved are one tap away.
    3. You'll understand your faith better. Little by little you'll learn who wrote what, where familiar sayings come from, and why a passage matters. The Bible starts to feel less like an old book and more like a letter written to you.
    4. You'll have something to share. A verse for a friend who's struggling, a fun fact for your kids, or a comic for the family group chat. Faith grows when it's shared.
    5. You'll build a habit that lasts. A few minutes a day adds up to a whole year of time spent with God, and that time changes a person.

    Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.

    Psalm 119:105

    Who is it for?

    It's for everyone: the new believer who doesn't know where to start, the lifelong Christian who wants to go deeper, the busy parent, the student, and the grandparent.

    You don't need theology training or a lot of free time, just a willingness to open it each day.

    Our hope for you

    We made Daily Grace so that no one has to go through a day without a word from God. We hope this little pocket Bible becomes a faithful companion that encourages you, teaches you and draws you closer to Him, one day at a time.
