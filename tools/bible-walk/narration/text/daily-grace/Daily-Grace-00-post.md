**Title** (under 100 characters)

Welcome to Daily Grace: A Complete Bible in Your Hand

**Description**

Some of the best things are not on the surface. They are waiting to be found.

Daily Grace may look simple from the front door, but open it and room after room is full of treasure. It was made to help you understand the Bible, remember it, and live it, one day at a time.

Take a short walk through the rooms:
📖 Look up any verse, then keep going
✍️ My Faith Journal: keep your notes, prayers and thoughts
💬 Share quotes, verse pictures and stickers
❓ Questions We All Ask: worry, forgiveness, fear, answered from the Word of God
🔴 Red-Lettered Quotes: the words of Jesus, gathered in red
👥 Bible Characters and Peoples of the Bible
🎧 The Living Voice: walk through the Bible by listening, one chapter a day
🗣️ Bible Sayings: everyday phrases that began in Scripture
💡 Did You Know? and Bible Trivia
🧭 Following God's Blueprint
🧠 The weekly Memory Verse Challenge and daily Food for Thought
🎨 Bible stories illustrated like comics
⭐ Stickers, badges and your own Sticker Album
🦁 Daily Grace Kids, for children from three to twelve

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." (Psalm 119:105, KJV)

Which room will you open first, and what do you hope God will show you there? Tell us in the comments.

👉 Start here: https://dailygrace.faith

Scripture quotations are from the King James Version.
