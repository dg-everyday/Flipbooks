Daily Grace. Welcome to Daily Grace.

Picture a house with a plain front door. Nothing about it catches your eye as you walk past. But open the door, and room after room is full of treasure. Jesus once said, 'the kingdom of heaven is like unto treasure hid in a field'. Some of the best things are not on the surface. They are waiting to be found. Daily Grace is a little like that house.

You may have come here to look up a Bible verse, and you can. But Daily Grace was made for much more than searching. It was made to help you understand the Bible, to remember it, and to live it, one day at a time. It is a complete Bible in your hand. So let us take a short walk through the rooms.

Start with your notes. When something in the Bible speaks to you, you do not need to reach for a pen and paper. Write it in My Faith Journal, and it is there, waiting for you, whenever you come back.

If you love to share, Daily Grace is ready. Quotes, verse pictures and stickers are at your fingertips, to send to a friend or to post for others to see. A word of encouragement can travel a long way.

Life brings hard questions. Why do I worry so much? How can I forgive? What do I do when I am afraid? In Questions We All Ask, the questions people ask most often are answered from the Word of God.

Would you like to hear Jesus in his own words? In Red-Lettered Quotes, the teachings of Jesus himself are gathered for you, in red, just as many Bibles print them. And when you meet a name in the Bible and wonder, who was that, Bible Characters and Peoples of the Bible help you learn the men and women of Scripture, and the part each one played.

Perhaps reading is not your thing. That is alright. The Bible says, 'So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.' On The Living Voice, you can walk through the Bible by listening, one chapter at a time, in plain words, wherever you are.

Have you ever said that someone is the salt of the earth? Or that you escaped by the skin of your teeth? Or that the writing is on the wall? Many sayings we hear every day began in the Bible, and most people never knew it. Bible Sayings shows you where they came from. And if you enjoy a good surprise, Did You Know and Bible Trivia are full of them.

For the road ahead, there is Following God’s Blueprint, a clear path through what the Lord asks of us, so that you can walk the right way. As the psalm says, 'Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.'

Every week, the Memory Verse Challenge gives you one verse to hide in your heart. And every day, there is Food for Thought, a small portion of the Word to feed on, and to think about as you go.

Some of us learn best with our eyes. The illustrated Bible stories, drawn like comics, help you see each story unfold, so that you can picture it, and appreciate it all the more.

And as you come back day after day, you earn stickers and badges along the way. Keep them in your Sticker Album, share them with your friends, or print them out and put them anywhere you like, on a notebook, a fridge, or a bedroom door.

Daily Grace is not only for grown ups. Daily Grace Kids is a place for children from three to twelve years old, where they can play, and learn the Bible early in life. Paul wrote to young Timothy, 'from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures'. What a gift to give a child.

So do not judge Daily Grace by its cover. The screen may look simple, but deep inside, it is full of wonder, waiting to be discovered. The psalmist prayed, 'Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law.' That is our prayer for you, as you embrace the Word of the Lord.

Here is a question to carry with you. Which room in Daily Grace will you open first, and what do you hope God will show you there?

Let us pray. Lord God, thank you for your Word, which is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. Open my eyes to see the wonderful things in it. Help me not only to read it, but to understand it, to remember it, and to live it, every day. Amen.

So choose a room, and step inside. There is grace waiting for you there.

This has been Daily Grace. Grace for Today. Our Faith Tomorrow.
