The Living Voice. Daniel, chapter 2. There Is a God in Heaven That Revealeth Secrets.

A king wakes up troubled by a dream. He calls in all his wise men and says, tell me what it means. They say, of course, O king. Tell us the dream. But the king says no. You tell me what I dreamed, and then tell me what it means. And if you cannot, you will die. No one on earth can do that. And they are right. No one on earth can.

In chapter one, Daniel and his three friends were taken to Babylon and given new names. Daniel purposed in his heart not to eat the king’s food. God blessed them, and gave them knowledge and wisdom, and Daniel understanding in visions and dreams.

Now, in chapter two, Daniel’s gift is put to the test, with all their lives at stake. Listen for what Daniel does first when he hears the danger. And listen to the king’s dream, which tells the story of empires, and of a kingdom that will last for ever.

In the second year of his reign, Nebuchadnezzar has dreams that trouble his spirit, so that he cannot sleep.

The king calls for the magicians, the astrologers and the wise men of Babylon, to tell him his dreams. He says, I have dreamed a dream, and my spirit is troubled to know it.

The wise men say, O king, live for ever. Tell your servants the dream, and we will tell you what it means.

But the king answers, the thing is gone from me. If you will not tell me the dream and its meaning, you will be put to death, and your houses destroyed. But if you tell me, you will receive gifts and great honour.

The wise men answer, there is not a man on earth who can do what the king asks. No one can tell it, except the gods, whose dwelling is not with flesh.

They are more right than they know.

The king is furious, and commands that all the wise men of Babylon be put to death. And the soldiers come looking for Daniel and his friends too.

Daniel speaks with wisdom and tact to Arioch, the captain of the king’s guard. Why is the king’s decree so hasty? Arioch explains. So Daniel goes in to the king, and asks for time, promising to tell him the meaning.

Then Daniel goes home, and tells his friends, Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah. He asks them to pray, to seek mercy from the God of heaven concerning this secret, so that they will not be destroyed with the rest of the wise men of Babylon.

'Then was the secret revealed unto Daniel in a night vision. Then Daniel blessed the God of heaven.' And Daniel praises God. Blessed be the name of God for ever and ever, for wisdom and might are his. He changes the times and the seasons. He removes kings, and sets up kings. He gives wisdom to the wise.

And Daniel goes on praising.

Then Daniel goes to Arioch, and asks to be brought before the king.

The king asks Daniel, are you able to make known to me the dream I have seen, and its meaning?

Daniel answers that no wise man or magician can do this.

Then Daniel tells the dream. O king, you saw a great statue, huge and dazzling, and terrifying to look at. Its head was of fine gold. Its chest and arms were of silver. Its belly and thighs were of bronze. Its legs were of iron, and its feet part iron and part clay.

As you watched, a stone was cut out, but not by human hands. It struck the statue on its feet of iron and clay, and broke them to pieces. Then the iron, the clay, the bronze, the silver and the gold were all broken together, and became like chaff on a threshing floor in summer. The wind carried them away, and not a trace was left. But the stone that struck the statue became a great mountain, and filled the whole earth.

Then Daniel explains. You, O king, are the head of gold. The God of heaven has given you a kingdom, power and glory. After you will come another kingdom, lesser than yours. Then a third kingdom, of bronze, which will rule over all the earth. Then a fourth kingdom, strong as iron, which will crush all others. But it will be divided, partly strong and partly brittle, like iron mixed with clay.

And then Daniel tells him about the stone.

King Nebuchadnezzar falls on his face before Daniel, and says, 'Of a truth it is, that your God is a God of gods, and a Lord of kings, and a revealer of secrets'. And the king makes Daniel ruler over the whole province of Babylon, and chief over all its wise men. At Daniel’s request, his three friends are given high positions too.

Now, three verses to hold on to.

Verse 22. 'He revealeth the deep and secret things: he knoweth what is in the darkness, and the light dwelleth with him.' Nothing is hidden from God. The king’s dream was locked inside his head, and no one on earth could find it. But God knew it. He knows what is in the darkness, in every hidden place, and in every heart. And light lives with him.

Verse 28. 'But there is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets, and maketh known to the king Nebuchadnezzar what shall be in the latter days. Thy dream, and the visions of thy head upon thy bed, are these;' But there is a God in heaven. The wise men had said that no one on earth could do this. Daniel agreed. And then he pointed the king beyond the earth, to heaven. Daniel’s greatest moment was not about Daniel at all. He made sure the king knew where the answer came from.

Verse 44. 'And in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed: and the kingdom shall not be left to other people, but it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever.' The empires of the world rise, and pass away. And then a stone, not made by human hands, and a kingdom that will never end. Christians have long seen this stone as a picture of the kingdom of God, which came with Jesus, and which will one day fill the whole earth.

Jesus taught his disciples to pray, 'Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.' And James wrote, 'If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.'

When Daniel faced an impossible problem, the first thing he did was pray, and ask his friends to pray with him. What impossible problem are you facing today? Bring it to God first. And ask a friend to pray with you.

Here is today’s question. Daniel said, there is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets. What does it mean to you that God knows everything that is hidden, even in your own heart?

Let us pray. God of heaven, blessed be your name for ever and ever, for wisdom and might are yours. Thank you that you know what is in the darkness, and that nothing is too hard for you. When I face impossible problems, help me to pray first. And thank you that your kingdom will never be destroyed. Thy kingdom come. Amen.
