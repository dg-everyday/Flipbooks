The Living Voice. John, chapter 16. Be of Good Cheer; I Have Overcome the World.

A mother in labour cries out in pain. Hours later, she holds her newborn baby in her arms, and the pain is forgotten in her joy. Jesus uses this picture to prepare his friends for what is coming. You will weep, he says. The world will rejoice. But your sorrow shall be turned into joy.

In chapter fifteen, Jesus said, I am the vine, ye are the branches. Abide in me, and you will bear much fruit, for without me ye can do nothing. He called his disciples his friends, and said, greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends. And he warned them that the world would hate them, as it hated him.

Now, in chapter sixteen, Jesus continues to prepare his disciples for his going away. He tells them more about the Holy Spirit, and promises that their sorrow will turn to joy. And he ends with one of the most encouraging promises in the Bible.

Jesus says, these things have I spoken unto you, that ye should not be offended, that you should not stumble and fall away. They shall put you out of the synagogues. Yea, the time cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service. And these things will they do unto you, because they have not known the Father, nor me.

I tell you this now, Jesus says, so that when the time comes, you will remember that I told you.

Now I go my way to him that sent me, Jesus says. And because I have said these things, sorrow has filled your heart.

Nevertheless I tell you the truth, he says. It is expedient for you that I go away. It is better for you. For if I go not away, the Comforter will not come unto you. But if I depart, I will send him unto you. While Jesus was on earth, he could only be in one place at a time. The Holy Spirit would be with every believer, everywhere.

And when he is come, Jesus says, he will reprove the world, he will convict the world, of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment. Of sin, because they believe not on me. Of righteousness, because I go to my Father. Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged. The Holy Spirit is the one who shows people their need of Jesus.

I have yet many things to say unto you, Jesus says, but ye cannot bear them now.

He shall glorify me, Jesus says, for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew it unto you. The Spirit always points to Jesus.

Then Jesus says, a little while, and ye shall not see me. And again, a little while, and ye shall see me, because I go to the Father. The disciples are puzzled. What is this that he saith, a little while? We cannot tell what he saith.

Jesus knows what they want to ask, and explains. Verily, verily, I say unto you, that ye shall weep and lament, but the world shall rejoice. And ye shall be sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy. He gives the picture of a woman in labour. She has sorrow, because her hour is come. But as soon as the child is born, she remembers no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world. And ye now therefore have sorrow, Jesus says. 'but I will see you again, and your heart shall rejoice, and your joy no man taketh from you.'

In a few hours they will see him die. Three days later, they will see him alive. And no one will ever be able to take that joy away.

Then Jesus speaks about prayer.

For the Father himself loveth you, Jesus says, because ye have loved me, and have believed that I came out from God. I came forth from the Father, and am come into the world. Again, I leave the world, and go to the Father.

His disciples say, lo, now speakest thou plainly. Now we are sure that thou knowest all things. By this we believe that thou camest forth from God. Jesus answers, do ye now believe? Behold, the hour cometh, yea, is now come, that ye shall be scattered, every man to his own, and shall leave me alone. And yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me.

And then Jesus speaks his final word to them before he prays.

Now, three verses to hold on to.

Verse 13. 'Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth: for he shall not speak of himself; but whatsoever he shall hear, that shall he speak: and he will shew you things to come.' Jesus did not leave his followers to work everything out on their own. He gave them a guide, the Spirit of truth. The same Spirit helps us today, as we read the scriptures and pray, to understand and to walk in the truth. And the Spirit does not draw attention to himself. He always leads us to Jesus.

Verse 24. 'Hitherto have ye asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.' To ask in Jesus’ name means to come to the Father through Jesus, trusting in him, and asking for what he would want. Jesus opened the way for us to come directly to the Father. And his purpose in answering our prayers is our joy, full joy. God delights to hear and answer his children.

Verse 33. 'These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.' Jesus did not promise his followers an easy life. In the world ye shall have tribulation. That is a promise too. But he also said, in me ye might have peace. And then the great word: be of good cheer, take heart. I have overcome the world. He said this before the cross, as if the victory were already won. Because it was.

Paul wrote, 'Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.' And John, who heard these words, wrote years later, 'this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.'

What tribulation are you facing right now? Jesus never said it would not come. But he says, be of good cheer. Bring it to the Father in Jesus’ name, and ask. And remember that whatever you face, Jesus has already overcome the world.

Here is today’s question. Jesus said, in the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world. How does knowing that Jesus has overcome help you face your troubles?

Let us pray. Lord Jesus, thank you that you have overcome the world. When trouble comes, give me your peace. Thank you for the Holy Spirit, who guides me into all truth. Teach me to ask in your name, that my joy may be full. Turn my sorrow into joy that no one can take away. Amen.
