The Living Voice. Proverbs, chapter 20. The Spirit of Man Is the Candle of the LORD.

Picture someone walking slowly through a dark house, holding up a candle. They look into every corner, under every table, behind every door. Nothing can stay hidden from the light. Proverbs says that God has given each of us something like a candle inside us, that shines into the hidden corners of our hearts.

In chapter nineteen, we heard that it is a glory to pass over a transgression. He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD. And there are many plans in a man’s heart, but the counsel of the LORD shall stand.

Now, in chapter twenty, Solomon’s sayings go on. Listen for a warning about strong drink. And listen for an honest question about our hearts.

The chapter begins with a warning. 'Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging: and whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise.' A mocker is someone who makes fun of you, and makes you look foolish. Too much wine or strong drink does exactly that. It makes people loud, angry and foolish, and it fools them into thinking they are fine. Proverbs says, do not be deceived. Do not let it control you.

'It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: but every fool will be meddling.' It takes strength to walk away from a quarrel. But fools always jump in.

Then a funny picture of a lazy farmer. 'The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold; therefore shall he beg in harvest, and have nothing.' It is too cold to plough the fields, he says. So he stays inside. And when harvest comes, he has nothing. Every season has its work.

'Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water; but a man of understanding will draw it out.' Some people have great wisdom deep inside them, like water at the bottom of a well. A wise person knows how to draw it out, by asking good questions and listening.

'Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?' Many people boast about how good they are. But a truly faithful person, one you can always depend on, is rare.

'The just man walketh in his integrity: his children are blessed after him.' A parent who lives with honesty blesses their children, even after they are gone.

Then comes an honest question.

'Divers weights, and divers measures, both of them are alike abomination to the LORD.' Divers means different. Using one set of weights for buying and another for selling is cheating. God hates it.

Then a saying about children. 'Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure, and whether it be right.' You do not have to be grown up to show what kind of person you are. Even children are known by how they behave.

'The hearing ear, and the seeing eye, the LORD hath made even both of them.' Our ability to hear and see is a gift from God. So let us use them well.

'Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes, and thou shalt be satisfied with bread.'

Then a funny picture from the market. 'It is naught, it is naught, saith the buyer: but when he is gone his way, then he boasteth.' Naught means worthless. The buyer complains, this is rubbish, it is not worth much! To get a lower price. And then he goes off and boasts about the great bargain he got. Proverbs sees through our little tricks.

'There is gold, and a multitude of rubies: but the lips of knowledge are a precious jewel.' Gold and rubies are common compared with someone who speaks wisely.

'Bread of deceit is sweet to a man; but afterwards his mouth shall be filled with gravel.' Something gained by lying tastes sweet at first. But later, it is like a mouthful of gravel.

'Every purpose is established by counsel'. Again, the value of good advice.

'He that goeth about as a talebearer revealeth secrets: therefore meddle not with him that flattereth with his lips.' Stay away from gossips and flatterers.

Then a saying about revenge.

'Man’s goings are of the LORD; how can a man then understand his own way?' God directs our steps. We do not always understand where we are going. But we can trust the one who leads us.

And then the saying about the candle.

'Mercy and truth preserve the king: and his throne is upholden by mercy.' A ruler is kept safe by kindness and faithfulness.

And near the end, a lovely saying about age. 'The glory of young men is their strength: and the beauty of old men is the grey head.' Each age has its own gifts. Young people have strength and energy. Older people have the wisdom and experience shown by grey hair. Both should be honoured.

Now, three verses to hold on to.

Verse 9. 'Who can say, I have made my heart clean, I am pure from my sin?' The answer is, no one. Not one of us can say we have made ourselves clean, or that we are free from sin. This verse is very honest. We all need forgiveness. And the good news of the Bible is that God offers to make us clean, through Jesus. We cannot do it ourselves. But he can.

Verse 22. 'Say not thou, I will recompense evil; but wait on the LORD, and he shall save thee.' Recompense means pay back. When someone hurts us, we want to get even. Proverbs says, do not. Instead, wait on the LORD. Leave it with him. He will deal with it, and he will save you. Taking revenge only makes things worse. Trusting God brings peace.

Verse 27. 'The spirit of man is the candle of the LORD, searching all the inward parts of the belly.' The inward parts mean our deepest thoughts and feelings. God has given us a spirit, a conscience, which is like a candle he uses to shine into the hidden places of our hearts. When something we have done makes us feel uneasy, that may be God’s candle shining, showing us what needs to change. Listen to it.

Paul wrote, 'Recompense to no man evil for evil.' And John wrote, 'If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves'.

Has someone hurt you? Instead of planning to pay them back, wait on the LORD, and leave it with him. And when your conscience nudges you today, do not ignore it. Let God’s candle show you what needs to change.

Here is today’s question. Proverbs asks, who can say, I have made my heart clean? Why is it good news, not bad news, that we cannot make ourselves clean?

Let us pray. Lord God, I cannot make my own heart clean. Thank you that you can, through Jesus. Shine your candle into the hidden corners of my heart, and show me what needs to change. When others hurt me, help me not to pay them back, but to wait on you. Keep me from anything that would mock me and fool me. Amen.
