The Living Voice. Psalm 1. Like a Tree Planted by the Rivers of Water.

Picture a farmer at harvest time, threshing his grain. He tosses it up into the air with a big wooden fork. The heavy grain falls back down to the ground, where he can gather it. But the light, dry husks, called chaff, are caught by the wind, and blown away. Gone, in a moment. Nearby, beside a river, a great tree stands, green and strong, its branches heavy with fruit. Psalm one says that every person is like one or the other.

In the introduction, we heard that the Psalms are Israel’s song book and prayer book, written by David and others. They are honest poems that bring every feeling to God. We heard that we will walk through thirty favourite psalms, arranged by theme. And we heard four things to watch for: honesty, pictures of God, the turn from sorrow to trust, and Jesus.

Now we begin with Psalm one. It is like the front door to the whole book of Psalms. Listen for the two ways. And listen for what makes someone like a strong tree.

The very first word of the whole book of Psalms is a happy word. Blessed. Happy. That is what God wants for us.

And who is blessed? First, the psalm tells us what the blessed person does not do.

Notice the three verbs. Walketh. Standeth. Sitteth. It is a picture of how people slide slowly into wrong. First you walk along with the ungodly, taking their advice. Then you stop and stand with them, joining in their ways. Then you sit down with them, and make yourself at home, mocking what is good. Sin rarely happens all at once. It is a slow slide, from walking, to standing, to sitting.

The ungodly are people who live without God. Sinners are those who deliberately do wrong. The scornful are mockers, people who laugh at anything good or holy.

Then the psalm tells us what the blessed person does do. 'But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.'

The law of the LORD here means God’s teaching, his Word. And the blessed person does not just read it as a duty. He delights in it. He loves it. And he meditates on it. To meditate means to think about something deeply, to turn it over in your mind, again and again, like chewing your food slowly. God’s Word is not just for a quick glance. It is for thinking about all through the day.

Do you remember what God told Joshua, before he led Israel into the promised land? 'This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night'. Psalm one says the same thing.

And then the picture of the tree.

Then the contrast. 'The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.' Chaff is the dry, light husk around the grain. It has no roots. It has no weight. When the wind blows, it is gone. A life without God may look busy and successful for a while. But it has no roots, and no weight. When the winds of trouble or judgment come, it is blown away.

'Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.' When God judges, those who have lived without him will not be able to stand.

And the psalm ends with the two ways side by side.

Psalm one is very short, only six verses. But it sets the tone for the whole book. Two ways. Two kinds of people. And the difference is not how clever or rich or popular they are. The difference is whether they are rooted in God and his Word.

And for Christians, there is something even more beautiful. Only one person has ever lived this psalm perfectly: Jesus. He never walked in the counsel of the ungodly. He always delighted in his Father’s word. And through him, we too can be made righteous, and planted by the rivers of water.

Now, three verses to hold on to.

Verse 1. 'Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful.' Counsel means advice. The first step towards a wrong path is often listening to the wrong advice. Who are you listening to? Whose ideas are shaping you? The blessed person is careful about the company they keep, because walking with the wrong people leads to standing with them, and then sitting with them.

Verse 3. 'And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.' A tree planted by a river has roots that reach the water. Even in a drought, when everything else dries up, it stays green. It bears fruit at the right time. When our roots go deep into God and his Word, we can stay strong and fruitful even in the hard, dry times of life. Notice, it is planted. Someone put it there. God plants us, and we grow.

Verse 6. 'For the LORD knoweth the way of the righteous: but the way of the ungodly shall perish.' Knoweth here means more than knowing about. It means watching over, caring for, guarding. God knows and protects the path of those who follow him. But the path of the ungodly leads nowhere. It perishes, like a track in the desert that the sand covers over. Two ways. One is watched over by God. The other disappears.

God told Joshua, 'This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night'. And Jeremiah wrote of the one who trusts in the LORD, 'For he shall be as a tree planted by the waters, and that spreadeth out her roots by the river'.

Choose one verse from God’s Word today, and meditate on it. Read it slowly, think about it through the day, and turn it into a prayer. And think about the counsel you listen to. Is it leading you towards God, or away from him?

Here is today’s question. Psalm one says the blessed person is like a tree planted by the rivers of water. What helps your roots go deep into God, so that you stay strong in dry times?

Let us pray. Lord God, I want to be like a tree planted by the rivers of water. Keep me from walking, standing or sitting with those who mock you. Give me a delight in your Word, and help me to meditate on it always. Make my roots go deep, and let me bear fruit in its season. Thank you that you know and watch over the way of the righteous. Amen.
