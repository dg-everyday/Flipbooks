# 1 John 4: try the spirits, Jesus Christ come in the flesh, greater is he that is in you, they are of the world, love is of God, God is love, he sent his only begotten Son, herein is love, if God so loved us, no man hath seen God, we dwell in him by his Spirit, the Saviour of the world, God is love and he that dwelleth in love, boldness, perfect love casteth out fear, we love him because he first loved us, love God and your brother. Keys 4:4, 4:10, 4:18.
TITLE: God Is Love

HOOK: A newborn baby lies in her mother’s arms. She cannot talk. She cannot walk. She has never done anything for her mother. She has given her sleepless hours and endless washing. And yet her mother looks at her with a love so strong it almost hurts. The baby did not earn that love. She did not start it. The love was there first. John says God’s love is like that.

WHERE: In chapter three, John said, behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God. He showed us what love is: Jesus laid down his life for us, and we ought to lay down our lives for each other. And he said, let us not love in word, neither in tongue; but in deed and in truth.

Now, in chapter four, John writes more about love than anywhere else in the Bible. Listen for how to tell truth from error. And listen for where love comes from.

STORY: John begins with a warning. 'Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.' Try means test. Not everyone who claims to speak for God really does. We remember the old prophet in Bethel, in first Kings, who said an angel had spoken to him, but he lied.

So how can we tell? John gives a simple test. 'Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God'. The false teachers were saying that Jesus did not really become a man. John says, the true Spirit of God always points to Jesus, the Son of God who truly became a man.

And then John encourages his readers. These false teachers might seem powerful and clever, but they have not won.

The false teachers are of the world, and the world listens to them.

Then John turns to his great theme. 'Beloved, let us love one another: for love is of God; and every one that loveth is born of God, and knoweth God.' Love comes from God, the way light comes from the sun. 'He that loveth not knoweth not God; for God is love.'

God is love. Three short words, and some of the most important in the whole Bible. John does not only say that God loves. He says that God is love. Love is who he is. Even when he corrects us, it is because he loves us.

And how do we know? Because of what God did. 'In this was manifested the love of God toward us, because that God sent his only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through him.' God did not just say he loved us. He showed it, by sending his only Son.

And John makes it even clearer.

So then, John says, 'Beloved, if God so loved us, we ought also to love one another.' The love we have received is meant to flow on to others.

Then John says something surprising. 'No man hath seen God at any time.' We cannot see God with our eyes. 'If we love one another, God dwelleth in us, and his love is perfected in us.' But when we love each other, God’s invisible love becomes visible. People who cannot see God can see his love in us.

And we know that God lives in us, because he has given us his Spirit. 'And we have seen and do testify that the Father sent the Son to be the Saviour of the world.' Not just the Saviour of a few people. The Saviour of the world.

Then John says it again, as if he wants us never to forget. 'God is love; and he that dwelleth in love dwelleth in God, and God in him.' To dwell means to live, to make your home. When we live in love, we are living in God, and he is living in us.

And because of this, we do not need to be afraid when we think about the day we will stand before God. We can have boldness, because his love has made us his children.

And then John tells us how to get rid of fear.

'We love him, because he first loved us.' This is the answer to everything. We do not start the love. God does. We love him because he loved us first, like the baby in her mother’s arms.

And John ends the chapter with a very practical point. 'If a man say, I love God, and hateth his brother, he is a liar: for he that loveth not his brother whom he hath seen, how can he love God whom he hath not seen?' You cannot love the invisible God and hate the person sitting right next to you. Loving God and loving people always go together.

KEY 4:4: 'Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.' He that is in you is God himself, through his Spirit. He that is in the world is the devil, and all the forces of evil. Who is stronger? God is greater, far greater. When you feel small and weak, and the world seems big and frightening, remember who lives in you. You are not on your own. Greater is he that is in you.

KEY 4:10: 'Herein is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son to be the propitiation for our sins.' Here is what real love is. It is not that we loved God first, and then he loved us back. He loved us when we were not loving him at all. And he sent his Son to be the sacrifice for our sins, to take away everything that stood between us and him. Love is not mainly a feeling. Love is God giving his Son for us.

KEY 4:18: 'There is no fear in love; but perfect love casteth out fear: because fear hath torment. He that feareth is not made perfect in love.' Casteth out means throws out. Torment means pain and worry. Fear of punishment is a terrible thing to live with. But God’s perfect love throws that fear out of the door. When we know how much God loves us, and that Jesus has dealt with our sin, we do not need to be afraid of him any more. We can come to him like a child running to a loving father.

THREAD: Paul wrote, 'But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.' And he wrote, 'Charity suffereth long, and is kind'.

TODAY: Say these words to yourself today, slowly: God is love, and he loved me first. Let them push out any fear in your heart. And think of one person who is hard to love. Ask God to love them through you.

QUESTION: John says, we love him, because he first loved us. How does knowing that God loved you first change the way you love him, and the way you love others?

PRAYER: Father God, you are love. Thank you that you loved me first, and sent your Son to be the sacrifice for my sins. Let your perfect love cast out all my fear. Remind me that greater is he that is in me, than he that is in the world. Help me to love others, so that people can see your love in me. Amen.
