# 1 John 5: born of God, love God's children, his commandments are not grievous, the victory even our faith, the witness of the Spirit, the water and the blood (told simply; no discussion of 5:7's manuscripts), the record: life in his Son, he that hath the Son hath life, that ye may know, confidence in prayer, praying for a brother (5:16 "Christians have understood... in different ways"), we know (three times), keep yourselves from idols (tied back to 1 Kings); looking back over 1 John. Keys 5:4, 5:13, 5:14.
TITLE: This Is the Victory

HOOK: A small boat is caught in a storm. The waves are huge, and the wind is howling. The sailors are terrified. But in the bottom of the boat, one man is fast asleep. They wake him up. He stands, and speaks to the wind and the waves. And there is a great calm. John was in that boat. He saw it happen. And he never forgot who it is that overcomes the world.

WHERE: In chapter four, John told us to test the spirits, and that greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world. He said that God is love. Herein is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son. Perfect love casteth out fear. And we love him, because he first loved us.

Now we come to the last chapter of John’s letter. Listen for the victory that overcomes the world. And listen for how John wants us to be sure.

STORY: John begins by tying together everything he has said. 'Whosoever believeth that Jesus is the Christ is born of God'. Faith in Jesus brings new birth, into God’s family. And if we love the Father, we will love his other children too.

How do we know we love God’s children? When we love God, and keep his commandments. 'For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.' Grievous means heavy, crushing. God’s commands are not a weight that crushes us. They are like the instructions for a good life, given by someone who loves us.

And then John speaks of victory.

Who overcomes the world? 'Who is he that overcometh the world, but he that believeth that Jesus is the Son of God?' The victory is not ours because we are strong. It is ours because we trust in Jesus, who has already won.

Then John speaks of witnesses, people or things that tell us the truth about Jesus. He mentions the Spirit, and the water, and the blood. Many think the water points to Jesus’ baptism in the Jordan, where the Father spoke from heaven, and the blood to his death on the cross. Both show that Jesus truly was the Son of God, and truly became a man, and truly died for us. And the Holy Spirit bears witness in our hearts that it is true.

If we believe what people tell us, John says, God’s witness is even greater. And here is what God says.

'He that hath the Son hath life; and he that hath not the Son of God hath not life.' It is that simple. Eternal life is not a reward for being good enough. It is found in a person, Jesus. If you have him, you have life.

And then John tells us why he wrote the whole letter.

Then John speaks about prayer.

And if we know he hears us, John says, we know that we have what we asked of him. Prayer is not shouting into an empty sky. It is talking to a Father who listens.

John encourages us to pray for each other. If you see a brother or sister sinning, pray for them, and God will give them life. John also mentions a sin unto death, and Christians have understood that in different ways. But his main point is clear. Do not give up on each other. Pray for one another.

Then, near the end, John gives us three great certainties, each beginning with the words, we know. 'We know that whosoever is born of God sinneth not'. God’s children do not go on living in sin, and God keeps them safe from the evil one. 'And we know that we are of God'. We belong to him. 'And we know that the Son of God is come, and hath given us an understanding, that we may know him that is true'. Jesus has come, and through him we know the true God. 'This is the true God, and eternal life.'

And then the very last words of the letter. They might surprise you.

'Little children, keep yourselves from idols. Amen.'

After all the talk of light and love, John ends with idols. An idol is anything we put in God’s place. Think of all we heard in first Kings. Solomon’s heart turned to the gods of his wives. Jeroboam’s golden calves. Ahab and the altars of Baal. Today, our idols may not be statues. They might be money, or popularity, or a screen, or anything we love more than God. John’s last loving word to his children is, guard your hearts. Keep the true God first.

And that is the first letter of John. Short, simple, and deep. God is light, so walk in the light. God is love, so love one another. Believe in his Son, and you may know that you have eternal life.

KEY 5:4: 'For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.' Overcometh means wins, conquers. The world, with all its pressures and temptations, and the pull to live without God, is strong. But it is not stronger than God. And our faith joins us to Jesus, who said, I have overcome the world. When we trust him, his victory becomes our victory.

KEY 5:13: 'These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.' That ye may know. Not just hope, or guess. Know. God does not want his children to spend their lives wondering whether they belong to him. If you believe in the name of the Son of God, you can know that you have eternal life. Not because you are good enough, but because God has promised it, and he cannot lie.

KEY 5:14: 'And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us'. Confidence means quiet trust. We can come to God boldly, sure that he hears. According to his will means asking for what God wants, not just what we want. The more we know God through his Word, the more our prayers will be shaped by his will. And he always hears.

THREAD: John wrote in his Gospel, 'But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name.' And Jesus said, 'be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.'

TODAY: If you believe in Jesus, thank God today that you can know you have eternal life. Then ask yourself honestly: is there anything I am putting in God’s place? Keep yourself from idols, and keep him first.

QUESTION: John says, he that hath the Son hath life. What would you say to a friend who asked you how they could know they have eternal life?

PRAYER: Lord God, thank you for walking with me through the first letter of John. Thank you that you are light, and you are love. Thank you that whoever has your Son has life, and that I can know it. Thank you that you hear me when I pray. Give me the victory of faith, and keep me from idols, so that you are always first in my heart. Amen.

NEXT: That is the end of our walk through the first letter of John. Well walked. You have finished another whole book of the Bible. Next, we go back to the story of the kings, in the second book of Kings. Elijah is carried up to heaven in a whirlwind, and Elisha takes up his mantle.
