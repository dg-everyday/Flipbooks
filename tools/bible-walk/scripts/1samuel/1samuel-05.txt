# 1 Samuel 5: the ark in the house of Dagon at Ashdod, Dagon fallen on his face twice, the threshold, the hand of the LORD heavy (emerods told as painful sores), Gath and Ekron, send it away. Avoids 5:10-11 (slay). Keys 5:2, 5:3, 5:7.
TITLE: Dagon Was Fallen upon His Face

HOOK: In a dark temple, a great statue stands in pride of place. The Philistines have just brought in a trophy, a golden chest captured in battle, and set it beside their god, as if to say, look, our god is stronger. The doors are shut, and everyone goes home. When the priests come back, they get a shock. Their god is lying face down on the floor, bowing before the chest.

WHERE: In chapter four, Israel took the ark of God into battle like a lucky charm, saying, it may save us. But Israel was defeated, Eli’s sons fell, and the ark was captured. When Eli heard that the ark of God was taken, he fell from his seat and died. And his daughter in law named her baby Ichabod, saying, the glory is departed from Israel.

Now, in chapter five, the ark is in enemy hands. It looks as if the God of Israel has been defeated. Listen for what happens to the Philistines’ god. And listen for how the Philistines feel about the ark by the end.

STORY: The Philistines take the ark of God from Eben-ezer to Ashdod, one of their great cities.

Dagon was one of the chief gods of the Philistines. In those days, when one nation defeated another, they believed their god had defeated the other nation’s god. So putting the ark in Dagon’s temple was a way of saying, Dagon has won. The God of Israel is now his prisoner.

But that is not what happens.

So the Philistines pick Dagon up, and put him back in his place.

The next day, they rise early again. And again Dagon has fallen face down on the ground before the ark of the LORD. This time, his head and both his hands have broken off, and are lying on the threshold, the doorway. Only the body of Dagon is left.

The first time, Dagon bowed down. The second time, he was broken. A god who has to be picked up and put back in his place is no god at all. And the writer adds that, ever since, the priests of Dagon would not step on the threshold of Dagon’s temple at Ashdod. The very place where their god was broken became a place they feared to tread.

Remember that Israel thought God had been defeated when the ark was captured. But God had not been defeated. He had let the ark be taken because of Israel’s sin. Now, in the heart of enemy territory, he shows that he alone is God.

And it does not stop with Dagon. The hand of the LORD is heavy on the people of Ashdod. He strikes them with painful swellings, a terrible disease, throughout the city and the land around it.

The men of Ashdod see what is happening, and they say something important.

So they call together all the lords of the Philistines, and ask, what shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel? And they decide to send it to Gath, another Philistine city.

Perhaps they thought it was just bad luck in Ashdod. Perhaps a different city would be fine.

So the ark is carried to Gath. And there too, the hand of the LORD is against the city, with great panic. He strikes the people of the city, small and great, with the same painful swellings.

So they send the ark on to Ekron. And as soon as the ark arrives, the people of Ekron cry out in terror. They have brought the ark of the God of Israel to us, to destroy us and our people!

They call the lords of the Philistines together again. Send away the ark of the God of Israel, they say. Let it go back to its own place, before it destroys us all. For there is deadly panic throughout the whole city. The hand of God is very heavy there. Those who do not die are struck with the swellings. And the cry of the city goes up to heaven.

Think about how the story has turned. In chapter four, the Philistines captured the ark and celebrated. Now, city after city is begging to be rid of it. The prize they were so proud of has become something they are desperate to give back.

Israel had tried to use the ark to control God. The Philistines tried to use the ark as a trophy over God. Both were wrong. God cannot be used, or captured, or controlled. He is the LORD.

KEY 5:2: 'When the Philistines took the ark of God, they brought it into the house of Dagon, and set it by Dagon.' Set it by Dagon. They put the God of Israel side by side with their own god, as if he were just one god among many. Many people still try to do that, to fit God in alongside everything else they worship. But God will not share his place with anything. He is not one god among many. He is the only true God.

KEY 5:3: 'And when they of Ashdod arose early on the morrow, behold, Dagon was fallen upon his face to the earth before the ark of the LORD. And they took Dagon, and set him in his place again.' Dagon fell on his face, the position of worship, before the ark of the LORD. Without anyone lifting a hand, the false god bowed before the true God. And the Philistines had to pick their god up and put him back. A god that needs your help to stand up cannot help you when you fall.

KEY 5:7: 'And when the men of Ashdod saw that it was so, they said, The ark of the God of Israel shall not abide with us: for his hand is sore upon us, and upon Dagon our god.' His hand is sore upon us, and upon Dagon our god. The Philistines had to admit it. The God of Israel was not defeated. His hand was heavy, on them and on their god. When God seems weakest, he is often about to show his strength.

THREAD: God says through Isaiah, 'I am the LORD: that is my name: and my glory will I not give to another, neither my praise to graven images.' And Paul wrote that 'at the name of Jesus every knee should bow'.

TODAY: Is there anything in your life that you have set beside God, as if it were just as important? Ask him today to take first place, and let anything else that has crept into his place fall down before him.

QUESTION: The Philistines thought they had captured the God of Israel, but he showed he was still in control. When has God seemed weak or absent to you, but later showed he was still at work?

PRAYER: Lord God, you are the only true God. You do not share your glory with anything. Forgive me for the times I have set other things beside you, as if they mattered as much as you. Take first place in my heart today. Every knee will bow before you, and I bow before you now. Amen.
