# 1 Samuel 6: the priests and diviners, the trespass offering of gold, why harden your hearts as Pharaoh, the new cart and two milk cows, the straight way to Beth-shemesh, the harvest and the great stone, men look into the ark (no count), who is able to stand before this holy LORD God, Kirjath-jearim. Keys 6:6, 6:12, 6:20.
TITLE: Who Is Able to Stand Before This Holy LORD God?

HOOK: Two cows are tied to a brand new cart. Their calves have been shut up at home, crying for them. Every instinct tells the mother cows to turn round and go back to their calves. But instead, they walk straight down the road, lowing as they go, away from home, carrying a golden chest toward a country they have never seen.

WHERE: In chapter five, the Philistines put the ark of God in the temple of Dagon. Twice Dagon fell on his face before the ark of the LORD, and the second time he was broken. The hand of the LORD was heavy on Ashdod, then on Gath, then on Ekron, and each city begged to be rid of the ark.

Now, in chapter six, the Philistines decide to send the ark home. Listen for the test they set. And listen for what happens when the ark arrives in Israel, and the question the people ask.

STORY: The ark of the LORD has been in the land of the Philistines for seven months. And the Philistines call their priests and their fortune tellers, and ask, what should we do with the ark of the LORD? How should we send it home?

The priests say, if you send it back, do not send it empty. Send a guilt offering with it, to make up for what you have done. Then you will be healed, and you will know why his hand has been against you.

What kind of offering? Five gold models of the swellings that struck them, and five gold mice, one for each of the five lords of the Philistines. It seems the disease had come with a plague of mice that were ruining the land. And they say, give glory to the God of Israel. Perhaps he will lift his hand from you, and from your gods, and from your land.

And then the priests say something remarkable.

The Philistines remembered the Exodus. They knew what happened to Pharaoh when he hardened his heart against the God of Israel. And they did not want the same thing to happen to them.

So the priests propose a test. Make a new cart. Take two cows that have calves, and that have never been yoked to a cart. Tie the cows to the cart, and take their calves away and shut them up at home. Put the ark on the cart, with the gold offerings in a box beside it. Then send it off, and watch.

If the cows go up the road toward Israel, to Beth-shemesh, then it was the God of Israel who did this great harm to us. But if not, then we will know that it was not his hand. It just happened by chance.

It was a hard test. Cows that had never pulled a cart would not know how. And mother cows would want to go back to their calves.

So they do it. And watch what happens.

The lords of the Philistines follow behind, all the way to the border of Beth-shemesh.

The people of Beth-shemesh are reaping their wheat harvest in the valley. They look up, and see the ark coming. And they are overjoyed.

The cart comes into the field of a man named Joshua, and stops beside a great stone. The people break up the wooden cart for firewood, and offer the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD. The Levites lift down the ark and the box of gold, and set them on the great stone. And the people offer sacrifices to the LORD that day.

The five lords of the Philistines watch it all, and then go home to Ekron.

But then something terrible happens. Some of the men of Beth-shemesh look into the ark of the LORD. God’s law said that only the priests could handle the holy things, and even the Levites must never look at them uncovered. The ark held the tablets of the law, and it was the place of God’s holy presence. And God strikes down many of the men of Beth-shemesh. The people mourn, because the LORD has struck them so heavily.

The Philistines had treated the ark as a trophy. Now some in Israel treat it with careless curiosity. Both forget that God is holy.

And the men of Beth-shemesh ask a question.

They send messengers to the town of Kirjath-jearim. The Philistines have sent back the ark of the LORD, they say. Come down and take it up to you.

KEY 6:6: 'Wherefore then do ye harden your hearts, as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts? when he had wrought wonderfully among them, did they not let the people go, and they departed?' Even pagan priests could see the lesson of the Exodus. Pharaoh resisted God again and again, and it brought disaster. The Philistines decided not to make the same mistake. When God makes himself known, the wise thing is not to harden our hearts, but to give way to him.

KEY 6:12: 'And the kine took the straight way to the way of Beth-shemesh, and went along the highway, lowing as they went, and turned not aside to the right hand or to the left; and the lords of the Philistines went after them unto the border of Beth-shemesh.' Kine means cows. They went straight, lowing as they went, crying for their calves, and yet they did not turn aside. It was against their nature, and it showed everyone that God was guiding them. The same God who rules over kings and armies can guide two cows down a country road.

KEY 6:20: 'And the men of Beth-shemesh said, Who is able to stand before this holy LORD God? and to whom shall he go up from us?' Who is able to stand before this holy LORD God? It is a good question. The honest answer is, no one, on their own. God is holy, perfectly pure and good, and we are not. That is why the Bible tells us that we need someone to stand for us. The New Testament says that Jesus is the one who brings us near to God.

THREAD: The letter to the Hebrews says, 'To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts'. And it also says, 'Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy'.

TODAY: Think about the holiness of God today. Read a psalm of praise, slowly, and let yourself be amazed at how pure and great he is. Then thank him that, through Jesus, you can come near to him.

QUESTION: The men of Beth-shemesh asked, who is able to stand before this holy LORD God? How would you answer that question?

PRAYER: Holy LORD God, you are pure and good, and I cannot stand before you on my own. Forgive me for the times I have treated holy things carelessly. Thank you that you guide even the smallest things. And thank you that through Jesus, I can come near to you with confidence. Amen.
