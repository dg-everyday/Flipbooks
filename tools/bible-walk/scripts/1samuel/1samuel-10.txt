# 1 Samuel 10: the vial of oil, the three signs, the Spirit and another heart, is Saul also among the prophets, the uncle, Mizpeh and the lot, hid among the stuff, God save the king, the manner of the kingdom written, men whose hearts God touched, the children of Belial, he held his peace. Keys 10:9, 10:22, 10:27.
TITLE: God Gave Him Another Heart

HOOK: The whole nation has gathered. The lots have been cast, tribe by tribe, family by family, and at last a name is called. Saul, the son of Kish. Everyone turns to look. But there is no one there. The new king of Israel has disappeared. And when they find him, he is hiding among the baggage.

WHERE: In chapter nine, Saul went looking for his father’s lost donkeys, and was led step by step to Samuel. The day before, the LORD had told Samuel, I will send thee a man out of the land of Benjamin. And when Samuel saw Saul, the LORD said, behold the man whom I spake to thee of. Saul said, am not I a Benjamite, of the smallest of the tribes? Samuel gave him the best seat at the feast, and then said, stand still, that I may shew thee the word of God.

Now, in chapter ten, Saul is anointed, and then presented to the people. Listen for the signs God gives him. And listen for where Saul is when the people come to crown him.

STORY: Samuel takes a small flask of oil, and pours it on Saul’s head, and kisses him. 'Is it not because the LORD hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance?'

To anoint means to pour oil on someone’s head. It was a sign that God had chosen them for a special task. The word for the anointed one, in Hebrew, is Messiah. In Greek it is Christ. Keep that in mind as the story goes on.

Then Samuel gives Saul three signs, so that he will know this really is from God. When you leave me, he says, you will meet two men by Rachel’s tomb, who will tell you the donkeys are found, and your father is now worried about you. Then you will meet three men going up to worship God at Bethel, carrying goats, bread and wine. They will greet you and give you two loaves. Then you will come to the hill of God, and meet a group of prophets coming down, playing music and prophesying. And the Spirit of the LORD will come upon you, and you will prophesy with them, and be turned into another man.

Samuel adds, when these signs happen, do what your hand finds to do, for God is with you. Then go down to Gilgal ahead of me, and wait seven days until I come, and I will show you what to do.

Remember that command to wait seven days. It will matter later.

And it all happens just as Samuel said.

When the people who knew Saul see him prophesying among the prophets, they are amazed. What has happened to the son of Kish? 'Is Saul also among the prophets?' It becomes a saying in Israel.

When Saul gets home, his uncle asks, where have you been? Looking for the donkeys, Saul says. And when we could not find them, we went to Samuel. His uncle asks, what did Samuel say? And Saul tells him that the donkeys were found. But he says nothing about the kingdom.

Then Samuel calls the people together before the LORD at Mizpeh. And he reminds them. The LORD brought you up out of Egypt, and saved you from all who oppressed you. But today you have rejected your God, who saves you from all your troubles, and you have said, no, set a king over us. So now, present yourselves before the LORD, by your tribes and families.

Lots are cast. The tribe of Benjamin is chosen. Then the family of Matri. Then Saul, the son of Kish. But when they look for him, he cannot be found.

Was Saul humble? Was he afraid? Perhaps both. He had been anointed, and given the Spirit. But he was not ready to face the people.

They run and bring him out. And when he stands among the people, he is head and shoulders taller than everyone.

Samuel says, do you see the one the LORD has chosen? There is no one like him among all the people. And all the people shout, 'God save the king.'

Then Samuel explains to the people the rules of the kingdom, and writes them in a book, and lays it up before the LORD. The king was not to be above God’s law. He was under it.

Then Samuel sends everyone home. Saul goes home to Gibeah. And a band of brave men go with him, men whose hearts God had touched.

But not everyone is pleased.

KEY 10:9: 'And it was so, that when he had turned his back to go from Samuel, God gave him another heart: and all those signs came to pass that day.' God gave him another heart. Saul did not make himself into a king. God changed him from the inside, and equipped him for the task. When God calls someone, he gives them what they need. The sad question in Saul’s story is what he would do with the new heart God gave him.

KEY 10:22: 'Therefore they enquired of the LORD further, if the man should yet come thither. And the LORD answered, Behold, he hath hid himself among the stuff.' Hidden among the baggage. The tallest man in Israel, trying to hide behind the luggage. There is something almost funny about it, and something very human. Many of us feel like hiding when God calls us to something big. But God knows exactly where we are. We cannot hide from his call.

KEY 10:27: 'But the children of Belial said, How shall this man save us? And they despised him, and brought him no presents. But he held his peace.' Some worthless men mocked Saul. And Saul held his peace. He said nothing, and did not take revenge. At the beginning, Saul showed real wisdom and self control. It is a good thing, when we are mocked, to stay quiet and let God defend us.

THREAD: Through Ezekiel, God promised, 'A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you'. And Paul wrote, 'Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature'.

TODAY: Is God calling you to something, and you have been hiding among the stuff? Step out today, and trust that the God who calls you will also give you what you need.

QUESTION: God gave Saul another heart, and filled him with his Spirit. What do you think it means for God to change someone from the inside?

PRAYER: Lord God, thank you that you give new hearts to those you call. Change me from the inside, and give me what I need for the work you have for me. When I want to hide, give me courage to step out. And when others mock me, help me to hold my peace and trust you. Amen.
