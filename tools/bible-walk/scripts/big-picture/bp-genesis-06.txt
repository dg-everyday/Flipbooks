# Genesis 6 in The Big Picture: judgment and grace, Noah and the ark. Keys 6:5, 6:8, 6:22.
TITLE: Noah Found Grace

HOOK: A man is building a boat. Not a small fishing boat, but a vessel as long as a city street and as tall as a house of three floors, far from any sea. Every day the neighbours watch him cut the wood and seal the seams. They do not know what he knows: that God has spoken to him, and that the world is about to change.

WHERE: In Genesis chapter three, Adam and Eve turned from God, and sin and death came into the world. Outside the garden, things grew worse quickly. In the very next chapter, their son Cain killed his brother Abel. Generation after generation, the human family grew larger, and sin grew with it.

Now, in chapter six, the whole earth is filled with violence. In The Big Picture, this is the first great judgment, and also the first time the Bible uses a word you will hear at every step of this walk: grace.

STORY: As people multiply on the earth, God looks at their hearts, and what he sees is sin, every day and everywhere. Genesis says something startling: 'it repented the LORD that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him at his heart.' God is not cold or distant. The evil of the world breaks his heart.

God says he will wipe away the people he made, along with the animals and the birds. 'But Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD.'

Genesis tells us who Noah was. He was 'a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.' He had three sons, Shem, Ham and Japheth. All around him the earth was corrupt and full of violence, but Noah walked with God.

So God tells Noah what is coming. He is going to bring a flood of waters upon the earth. And he tells Noah to build an ark of gopher wood, with rooms inside, sealed with pitch inside and out. God gives him the size: three hundred cubits long, fifty wide, and thirty high. A cubit was the length from a man’s elbow to the tip of his fingers, so this ark was huge, with three decks, a window, and a door in its side.

Then God makes a promise: 'But with thee will I establish my covenant'. A covenant is a solemn promise that binds two together. Noah, his wife, his sons and their wives will come into the ark. And with them, two of every kind of living creature, male and female, to keep them alive, and food of every kind for them all.

And the chapter ends with one simple sentence about Noah. He did not argue, he did not delay, and he did not ask for a sign. He did what God said.

In the chapters that follow, the rain comes, the waters cover the earth, and the ark carries Noah, his family and the animals safely through. When the waters go down, God makes a covenant with all the earth, never again to destroy it with a flood, and he sets his bow in the cloud as the sign of that promise. Every rainbow you see is a reminder.

KEY 6:5: 'And GOD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.' This is one of the most serious verses in the Bible. The problem was not just bad actions, but bad hearts: every imagination, only evil, continually. This is the sickness that came into the world in the garden, and it had spread to everyone. The flood would wash the earth, but it could not wash the human heart. Only God would be able to do that.

KEY 6:8: 'But Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD.' This is the first time the word grace appears in the King James Bible. Grace means the kindness and favour of God that we do not earn. Notice the order. First Noah found grace, then we are told he was just and walked with God. God’s grace came first, and Noah’s faithful life was his answer to it. That is always the order. And notice the word but. In a world under judgment, one word turned everything: but grace.

KEY 6:22: 'Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he.' Building the ark must have taken years. People surely laughed. There was no rain yet, and no flood in sight. But Noah trusted the word of God more than what he could see. Faith is not only believing that God is real. It is doing what he says, day after day, even when no one else understands.

THREAD: The New Testament remembers Noah as a man of faith: 'By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house'. And the ark itself became a picture of salvation. There was one way to be safe from judgment, one door, and God himself invited people in. Later, Jesus would say, I am the door.

TODAY: Noah lived faithfully in a world that did not. You may feel the same at school, at work, or even at home. Walk with God anyway. Do the next thing he has told you to do, even if no one else understands. And when you see a rainbow, remember that God keeps his promises.

QUESTION: Noah did all that God commanded him, even before he saw any rain. What is one thing God has asked of you that you will do today, by faith?

PRAYER: Lord, thank you for grace, your kindness that I could never earn. Like Noah, help me to walk with you in a world that has turned away, and to do all that you command. Thank you that you keep your promises, and that you have made a way to be safe. Amen.
