# Deuteronomy 27: great stones plastered and written with the law after crossing Jordan, an altar of whole stones on mount Ebal, offerings and rejoicing, this day thou art become the people of the LORD, six tribes on Gerizim to bless and six on Ebal, the Levites' twelve curses with the people's Amen (idols, parents, landmarks, the blind, justice for the stranger, secret violence; the family and other sins summarised, 27:20-25 not quoted), fulfilled in Joshua 8, Galatians 3. Keys 27:8, 27:18, 27:19.
TITLE: And All the People Shall Say, Amen

HOOK: Imagine two mountains facing each other across a narrow valley. On one mountain stand six tribes of Israel. On the other mountain, six more. In the valley between them, the priests call out, one sentence at a time. And after each one, the whole nation, hundreds of thousands of voices, answers together, so loudly it echoes off the hills: Amen.

WHERE: In chapter twenty six, a farmer brought his basket of firstfruits to the priest, and told the story: a Syrian ready to perish was my father. The LORD heard our voice, and brought us out of Egypt. And the covenant was sealed: the people declared the LORD to be their God, and the LORD declared them to be his peculiar people.

Now, in chapter twenty seven, Moses gives instructions for a great ceremony, to be held once they have crossed the Jordan. Listen for what is written on the stones. And listen for the word the people say again and again.

STORY: Moses and the elders of Israel give the people a command. Keep all the commandments I am giving you today.

On the day you cross the Jordan into the land, Moses says, set up some large stones, and cover them with plaster. And write on them all the words of this law.

Plaster made a smooth white surface, easy to write on, and easy to read. God’s law was to be displayed for everyone to see, the moment they entered the land.

When you have crossed the Jordan, Moses says, set up these stones on mount Ebal. And build an altar there to the LORD your God, an altar of stones. Do not use any iron tool on them. Build it with whole, uncut stones.

An altar of rough, natural stones, untouched by human tools. God’s altar was not to be a showpiece of human skill. It was simple, given by God, not made impressive by people.

There, Moses says, offer burnt offerings and peace offerings, and eat there, and rejoice before the LORD your God.

Then Moses and the priests speak to all Israel. Be silent, and listen, O Israel. This day you have become the people of the LORD your God. So obey his voice, and do his commandments.

Then Moses describes the great ceremony. Six tribes will stand on mount Gerizim to bless the people: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph and Benjamin. And six tribes will stand on mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan and Naphtali.

And the Levites will call out to all Israel, in a loud voice, twelve curses. And after each one, all the people will say, Amen.

Amen means, so be it. It is true. We agree.

The first curse was on anyone who makes a carved or metal idol, and sets it up in secret. And all the people say, Amen.

Cursed is anyone who treats his father or mother with contempt. Amen.

Cursed is anyone who moves his neighbour’s boundary stone. Amen.

And then comes a curse that shows God’s heart for the helpless.

Then there are curses for sins within families, for sexual sins that God forbade, and for secret violence against a neighbour, and for taking a bribe to harm an innocent person. And after each one, the people say, Amen.

Notice how many of these curses are about secret sins. The idol set up in secret. The neighbour attacked in secret. The boundary stone moved when no one was looking. These were sins that a court might never discover. But God sees everything. And the people agreed, out loud, that such sins deserved God’s curse.

And the last curse covers everything.

Cursed is anyone who does not confirm all the words of this law by doing them. And all the people shall say, Amen.

Who could keep every word, perfectly? No one. Paul later wrote about this very verse. He said that everyone who relies on keeping the law is under its curse, because no one keeps it perfectly. And then he wrote, Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us.

In the book of Joshua, chapter eight, we heard that Joshua did exactly what Moses commanded here. He built an altar on mount Ebal, wrote a copy of the law on the stones, and read every word to all the people, between the two mountains.

KEY 27:8: 'And thou shalt write upon the stones all the words of this law very plainly.' Very plainly. God did not hide his law, or make it hard to read. He wanted everyone to understand it. God speaks clearly, so that we can know what he wants. His word is meant to be read, understood, and lived.

KEY 27:18: 'Cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of the way. And all the people shall say, Amen.' Imagine misleading a blind person, sending them the wrong way, for a cruel joke. God takes it seriously. Taking advantage of someone who cannot see, or cannot defend themselves, is a great evil in God’s eyes.

KEY 27:19: 'Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow. And all the people shall say, Amen.' Again, the stranger, the fatherless and the widow. God mentions them again and again in Deuteronomy. To twist justice against them was to bring a curse on yourself. God is the defender of the defenceless.

THREAD: Paul wrote, 'Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us'. And Paul wrote, 'For all the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen'.

TODAY: Think about any secret sin, something no one else knows about. God sees it. Bring it to him today, and receive his forgiveness, through Christ, who took the curse for us. And look for a way to help someone who cannot help themselves.

QUESTION: Many of the curses in this chapter were about secret sins that no court would ever discover. Why do you think God cares so much about what we do when no one is watching?

PRAYER: Lord God, you see everything, even what is done in secret. I cannot keep every word of your law. Thank you that Jesus took the curse for me. Help me to live honestly, in secret and in public. And give me a heart for the blind, the stranger, the fatherless and the widow. Amen.
