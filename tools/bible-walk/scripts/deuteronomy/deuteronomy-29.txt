# Deuteronomy 29: the covenant in the land of Moab, besides Horeb, signs seen but no heart to perceive, clothes and shoes not worn out in the wilderness, all standing before the LORD from leaders to the drawer of water, the covenant made also with those not here this day, the root of gall and wormwood (Hebrews 12:15), the heart that says I shall have peace, the land laid waste and the nations asking why, the secret things belong unto the LORD. Keys 29:5, 29:11, 29:29.
TITLE: The Secret Things Belong unto the LORD

HOOK: Some questions have no answers we can find. Why did this happen? Why did God allow that? What will happen next? We can lie awake wondering. But at the end of Deuteronomy chapter twenty nine, Moses says something that has comforted God’s people ever since. There are things God keeps to himself. And there are things he has told us plainly. And the things he has told us are enough.

WHERE: In chapter twenty eight, Moses described the blessings of obedience. Blessed shalt thou be when thou comest in, and blessed shalt thou be when thou goest out. The blessings would overtake them. But he also gave long and sobering warnings, because the people had not served God with joyfulness and gladness of heart.

Now, in chapter twenty nine, Moses gathers the whole people to renew the covenant, on the plains of Moab. Listen for who is standing before God. And listen for the things Moses says belong to us.

STORY: These are the words of the covenant, the chapter begins, which the LORD commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant he made with them at Sinai.

A whole new generation stood here. Most of those who stood at the foot of the mountain of Sinai had passed away in the wilderness. So the covenant was made again, with their children.

Moses calls all Israel together, and says to them, You have seen everything the LORD did in Egypt, before your eyes, to Pharaoh and to all his servants and his land. The great trials, the signs, the wonders.

And then Moses says something surprising. Yet, until this day, the LORD has not given you a heart to understand, or eyes to see, or ears to hear.

They had seen the greatest miracles. But seeing miracles did not change their hearts. Only God could give them hearts to truly understand.

And then Moses reminds them of a small miracle that had been there all along.

Think about that. All those years walking in the desert, and their sandals never wore through. God had cared for the smallest details of their lives, the whole way. You did not eat bread, Moses says, or drink wine, so that you would know that I am the LORD your God.

When we came to this place, Moses goes on, Sihon and Og came out to fight us, and we defeated them, and gave their land to Reuben, Gad and half of Manasseh. So keep the words of this covenant, and do them, so that you may prosper in all you do.

Then Moses looks out over the people.

You are all standing here today before the LORD your God, he says. Your leaders, your tribes, your elders, your officers, all the men of Israel.

And then he goes on, all the way down the line.

From the most important leader to the humblest worker who chopped wood and carried water. Everyone stood before God together. Children, women, foreigners. No one was too small or too unimportant to be included in God’s covenant.

And Moses goes even further. I am not making this covenant only with you who are standing here today, he says, but also with those who are not here with us today.

That meant future generations, children not yet born. The covenant stretched forward, through time, to them as well.

Then Moses gives a warning. Make sure there is no one among you, man or woman, family or tribe, whose heart turns away from the LORD to serve other gods. Make sure there is no root among you that bears bitter poison.

A bitter root starts small, hidden under the ground. But it grows, and spreads, and poisons everything around it. Long after, the letter to the Hebrews used this picture. It warned against any root of bitterness springing up, to trouble God’s people.

And Moses warns against a person who hears the warnings, and says in his heart, I will be fine, even though I go my own stubborn way. The LORD will not spare such a person, Moses says.

And Moses describes a day when the land itself lies empty and ruined. Future generations, and foreigners passing by, will ask, Why has the LORD done this to this land? What does this great anger mean? And people will answer, Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD, the God of their fathers, and went and served other gods.

And then the chapter ends with a single, beautiful verse.

Moses does not explain everything. Some things God keeps secret. Why things happen as they do. What the future holds. But God has revealed what we need to know, and what we need to do.

KEY 29:5: 'And I have led you forty years in the wilderness: your clothes are not waxen old upon you, and thy shoe is not waxen old upon thy foot.' Not waxen old means not worn out. God cared for the smallest things, clothes and sandals, the whole journey. He knows what we need, even the little things, and he provides.

KEY 29:11: 'Your little ones, your wives, and thy stranger that is in thy camp, from the hewer of thy wood unto the drawer of thy water:' The hewer of wood and the drawer of water had the humblest jobs. But they stood before God just as the elders did. In God’s family, everyone is included, from the greatest to the least.

KEY 29:29: 'The secret things belong unto the LORD our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.' We do not have to understand everything. Some things belong to God alone. But what he has shown us, his word, belongs to us and our children, to live by. We can trust him with the rest.

THREAD: The letter to the Hebrews says, 'lest any root of bitterness springing up trouble you'. And Paul wrote, 'For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face: now I know in part; but then shall I know even as also I am known.'

TODAY: Is there a question troubling you that you cannot answer? Hand it over to God today. The secret things belong to him. Then focus on the things he has made clear, and do them.

QUESTION: Moses said the secret things belong to the LORD, but the revealed things belong to us. What is one thing God has made clear in his word, that you can do this week?

PRAYER: Lord God, there are so many things I do not understand. Thank you that the secret things belong to you, and that I can trust you with them. Thank you for what you have revealed in your word. Help me to live by it. And keep any bitter root from growing in my heart. Amen.
