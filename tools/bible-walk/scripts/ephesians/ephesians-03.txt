# Ephesians 3: the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles, the mystery revealed (the Gentiles fellowheirs and of the same body), made a minister by grace, less than the least of all saints, the unsearchable riches of Christ, the manifold wisdom of God made known by the church, boldness and access with confidence, faint not at my tribulations; Paul's prayer (I bow my knees, the whole family, strengthened with might in the inner man, Christ dwell in your hearts by faith, rooted and grounded in love, the breadth and length and depth and height, the love of Christ which passeth knowledge, filled with all the fulness of God), the doxology (exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think). Keys 3:8, 3:19, 3:20.
TITLE: The Love of Christ, Which Passeth Knowledge

HOOK: A little boy stands on the beach, looking out at the ocean. He tries to see where it ends, but it just goes on and on. He picks up a bucket, and scoops up some water. Look, Dad, he says, I have got the sea! His father smiles. You have a little bit of it, he says. But the sea is far bigger than your bucket. Paul says the love of Christ is like that ocean.

WHERE: In chapter two, Paul told us that we were dead in our sins. But God, rich in mercy, made us alive with Christ. By grace are ye saved through faith, not of works. We are his workmanship. And Jesus is our peace, who has broken down the middle wall that divided people, and made us one family, a home for God.

Now, in chapter three, Paul tells us about the secret God revealed to him. Listen for how Paul describes himself. And listen to one of the most beautiful prayers in the Bible.

STORY: Paul begins, 'For this cause I Paul, the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles'. Gentiles means people who are not Jews. Paul is in prison because he preached the good news to non-Jews. And he does not call himself a prisoner of Rome. He calls himself a prisoner of Jesus Christ. Even in chains, he belongs to Jesus.

Then Paul explains the mystery. Remember, a mystery in the Bible means a secret that God kept hidden for a long time, but has now revealed. What is it? 'That the Gentiles should be fellowheirs, and of the same body, and partakers of his promise in Christ by the gospel'. Fellowheirs means they share the same inheritance. People from every nation, not just Israel, can belong to God’s family, on exactly the same terms, through Jesus. In earlier times, this was not clearly known. Now God has revealed it.

And God chose Paul to share this news. But listen to how Paul speaks of himself.

God’s plan was that through the church, all believers together, the many sided wisdom of God would be shown, even to the powers in the heavenly places. When people from every nation and background come together as one family in Christ, even the angels look on and marvel at God’s wisdom.

'In whom we have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of him.' Through faith in Jesus, we can come to God boldly, with confidence, not afraid.

So, Paul says, do not lose heart because I am suffering for you. My suffering is for your glory.

And then Paul prays. 'For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, Of whom the whole family in heaven and earth is named'. Paul kneels before the Father of the whole family of God.

And what does he pray for?

First, that God, out of the riches of his glory, would strengthen them with power through his Spirit in the inner man, deep inside.

Second, 'That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith'. Dwell means live, make his home. Not just visit, but settle down and live in our hearts.

Third, that they would be 'rooted and grounded in love'. Rooted like a tree with deep roots. Grounded like a building with a firm foundation. Love is the soil and the foundation of the Christian life.

Fourth, that they would be able to understand, together with all God’s people, how wide, and long, and deep, and high is the love of Christ.

How wide? Wide enough to reach every person, every nation. How long? Long enough to last for ever. How deep? Deep enough to reach us at our lowest. How high? High enough to lift us up to heaven.

And then the fifth part of the prayer.

And then Paul ends his prayer with a great burst of praise.

'Unto him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus throughout all ages, world without end. Amen.'

So ends the first half of the letter. Paul has told us who we are in Christ: chosen, forgiven, made alive, saved by grace, brought near, made one family, filled with God’s love. And next, he will tell us how to live because of it.

KEY 3:8: 'Unto me, who am less than the least of all saints, is this grace given, that I should preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ'. Less than the least of all saints. Paul, the great apostle, calls himself the smallest of all God’s people. He never forgot that he had once persecuted the church. And yet God gave him grace, to preach the unsearchable riches of Christ, riches too great to be fully explored. The more we know of our own unworthiness, the more amazing God’s grace becomes.

KEY 3:19: 'And to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge, that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God.' Passeth knowledge means it is greater than we could ever fully understand. Paul prays that we would know a love that is too big to know! It sounds impossible. But we can know it truly, even if we can never know all of it. Like the boy with his bucket at the sea, we can have a real share of Christ’s love, even though the ocean is far bigger.

KEY 3:20: 'Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us'. Exceeding abundantly above means far, far more. Whatever we ask God for, whatever we can imagine, he is able to do far more. And the power that does it is already at work in us, the same power that raised Jesus from the dead. We can never ask too much of God, or imagine too much. He is always greater.

THREAD: Paul wrote to the Romans that nothing 'shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.' And Jesus said, 'As the Father hath loved me, so have I loved you: continue ye in my love.'

TODAY: Pray Paul’s prayer today, for yourself and for someone you love: that Christ would dwell in your hearts, that you would be rooted in love, and that you would know the love of Christ. And ask God for something big today. He is able to do far more than you ask.

QUESTION: Paul prays that we would know how wide, long, deep and high is the love of Christ. Which of those four measurements of his love means the most to you right now, and why?

PRAYER: Father, I bow my knees before you. Strengthen me with power by your Spirit in my inner being. Let Christ dwell in my heart by faith. Root me and ground me in love. Help me to know how wide and long and deep and high is the love of Christ, which passes knowledge. Now unto you, who are able to do exceeding abundantly above all that I ask or think, be glory for ever. Amen.

NEXT: Next on the walk, Ephesians chapter four. Paul now tells us how to walk. Keep the unity of the Spirit. Speak the truth in love. And be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another.
