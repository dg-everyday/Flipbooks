# Exodus 6: I am the LORD, the covenant remembered, the seven I wills, the people do not listen, the families of Reuben, Simeon and Levi, Moses and Aaron. Keys 6:6, 6:7, 6:9.
TITLE: I Will Take You to Me for a People

HOOK: A discouraged man stands before God, with nothing left to say. He has done what God asked, and everything has gone wrong. The people he came to help now blame him. So God speaks. And instead of a plan, or an explanation, God tells him one thing first: who God is. I am the LORD.

WHERE: In chapter five, Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and said, let my people go. Pharaoh answered, who is the LORD, that I should obey his voice? Then he made the slaves gather their own straw, while still making the same number of bricks. The people turned on Moses. And Moses returned to the LORD, and asked, why is it that thou hast sent me?

Now, in chapter six, God answers Moses. It is one of the great promises of the Bible, full of the words I will. Then the chapter pauses to list the families of Israel, to show us exactly who Moses and Aaron are.

STORY: The LORD says to Moses, now shalt thou see what I will do to Pharaoh. With a strong hand he shall let them go, and with a strong hand he shall drive them out of his land.

Then God speaks again. I am the LORD. I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac and unto Jacob, by the name of God Almighty, but by my name JEHOVAH was I not known to them. The fathers knew God as God Almighty, who makes promises. Now Israel will know him by his name, as the God who keeps them.

God says, I have established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land where they were strangers. And I have heard the groaning of the children of Israel, whom the Egyptians keep in bondage. And I have remembered my covenant.

Then God gives Moses a message for the people. Listen for how many times God says, I will.

And God goes on.

And I will bring you in unto the land, which I did swear to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob. And I will give it you for an heritage. I am the LORD.

It is a wonderful message. I will bring you out. I will rid you of their bondage. I will redeem you. I will take you to me for a people. I will be to you a God. I will bring you in. I will give it you. God does it all. And it begins and ends with the same words: I am the LORD.

Moses goes and tells the children of Israel. But they do not listen.

Then the LORD tells Moses to go in again to Pharaoh, and tell him to let the children of Israel go out of his land. But Moses says, behold, the children of Israel have not listened to me. How then shall Pharaoh hear me, when I speak so poorly? And again the LORD gives Moses and Aaron a charge, to bring the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

Then the story pauses, and gives a family list. It begins with the sons of Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn, and then the sons of Simeon. Then it slows down for the family of Levi, the third son. Levi’s sons are Gershon, Kohath and Merari. Kohath has a son named Amram. And Amram marries Jochebed, and she bears him Aaron and Moses. Aaron marries Elisheba, and has four sons, Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. Eleazar has a son named Phinehas. Some of these names will become important later in the story of Israel’s priests.

The list ends by underlining who these men are. 'These are that Aaron and Moses, to whom the LORD said, Bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt according to their armies.' They are the ones who spoke to Pharaoh. Not heroes from nowhere, but real men, from a real family, with real weaknesses, chosen by God.

Then the chapter returns to the conversation. The LORD says to Moses, I am the LORD. Speak to Pharaoh all that I say to you. And Moses says once more, behold, I am not a good speaker, and how shall Pharaoh hearken unto me?

KEY 6:6: 'Wherefore say unto the children of Israel, I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians, and I will rid you out of their bondage, and I will redeem you with a stretched out arm, and with great judgments:' This is the first time in the Bible that God says, I will redeem you. To redeem means to buy back, to set free by paying a price. A relative would redeem a family member who had been sold as a slave. God was saying, you are my family, and I will pay the price to set you free. Long after, God would redeem us by the blood of his own Son.

KEY 6:7: 'And I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God: and ye shall know that I am the LORD your God, which bringeth you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians.' This is the heart of the covenant. I will be your God, and you will be my people. God did not only want to rescue Israel from Egypt. He wanted a relationship with them. He wanted them to know him. The goal of salvation is not only escape from what is bad. It is belonging to God, and knowing him.

KEY 6:9: 'And Moses spake so unto the children of Israel: but they hearkened not unto Moses for anguish of spirit, and for cruel bondage.' The people did not refuse to listen because they were stubborn. They were simply too crushed to hope. Their suffering was so great that even good news could not get through. God understood that. He did not give up on them. When people are in deep pain, they may not be able to hear words of hope. That is when we need to be patient, and keep trusting God for them.

THREAD: Paul wrote that we are 'justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus'. And Peter wrote that we were not redeemed with silver and gold, 'But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot'.

TODAY: Read again the I will promises God made to Israel, and remember that in Jesus, God has done the same for you. He has brought you out, redeemed you, and made you his own. And if someone you know is too crushed to hope, be patient with them, and pray for them.

QUESTION: God said, I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God. What does it mean to you that God wants you to belong to him?

PRAYER: Lord God, you are the LORD. Thank you for every I will in your promises, and that in Jesus you have redeemed me and made me your own. When I am too discouraged to hope, hold on to me. And help me to be patient with those who are crushed by pain. Amen.
