# Exodus 14: Pharaoh pursues, trapped by the sea, stand still and see, the pillar between the camps, the sea divided, dry ground, the Egyptians drowned. Keys 14:13, 14:14, 14:31.
TITLE: Stand Still, and See the Salvation of the LORD

HOOK: Behind them, the sound of six hundred chariots, the pride of Egypt, racing across the desert. In front of them, the sea. On either side, there is nowhere to go. A million frightened people turn to Moses and scream at him. And Moses, with an army bearing down on them, tells them to stand still.

WHERE: In chapter thirteen, Israel left Egypt free. God told them to remember this day, and to tell their children. Moses carried Joseph’s bones. And God led his people the long way through the wilderness, going before them in a pillar of cloud and a pillar of fire.

Now, in chapter fourteen, comes one of the greatest moments in the whole Bible. God brings his people to a place where there is no way out, so that he can show them that he is the way out. This is the rescue the Old Testament never stops remembering.

STORY: The LORD tells Moses to have the people turn back and camp by the sea. Pharaoh will think, they are entangled in the land, the wilderness has shut them in. And I will harden Pharaoh’s heart, God says, that he shall follow after them. And I will be honoured upon Pharaoh, and upon all his host, that the Egyptians may know that I am the LORD.

It is told the king of Egypt that the people have fled. And the heart of Pharaoh and his servants is turned against the people. They say, why have we done this, that we have let Israel go from serving us? So Pharaoh makes ready his chariot, and takes his army with him. He takes six hundred chosen chariots, and all the chariots of Egypt, with captains over every one of them. They pursue the children of Israel, and overtake them camping by the sea.

When Pharaoh draws near, the children of Israel lift up their eyes, and behold, the Egyptians are marching after them. They are sore afraid, and they cry out to the LORD. And they turn on Moses. Because there were no graves in Egypt, they say, hast thou taken us away to die in the wilderness? Did we not tell you in Egypt, let us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians? It would have been better for us to serve the Egyptians, than to die in the wilderness.

And Moses answers.

The LORD says to Moses, wherefore criest thou unto me? 'speak unto the children of Israel, that they go forward'. Lift up your rod, and stretch out your hand over the sea, and divide it. And the children of Israel shall go on dry ground through the middle of the sea.

Then the angel of God, who went before the camp of Israel, moves and goes behind them. The pillar of cloud moves from in front of them, and stands behind them. It comes between the camp of the Egyptians and the camp of Israel. To the Egyptians it is a cloud and darkness. But to Israel it gives light. So the one does not come near the other all the time of darkness.

Moses stretches out his hand over the sea. And the LORD causes the sea to go back by a strong east wind all that time, and makes the sea dry land. The waters are divided. The children of Israel go into the middle of the sea on the dry ground. 'and the waters were a wall unto them on their right hand, and on their left.'

The Egyptians pursue, and go in after them into the middle of the sea, all Pharaoh’s horses, his chariots and his horsemen. Toward the end of the darkness, the LORD looks down on the Egyptian army through the pillar of fire and of the cloud, and troubles them. He takes off their chariot wheels, so that they drive heavily. The Egyptians say, let us flee from the face of Israel, for the LORD fighteth for them against the Egyptians.

The LORD tells Moses, stretch out thine hand over the sea, that the waters may come again upon the Egyptians. Moses stretches out his hand, and as the day begins to break, the sea returns to its strength. The waters return, and cover the chariots and the horsemen, and all the host of Pharaoh that came into the sea after them. There remains not so much as one of them.

But the children of Israel walk upon dry land in the middle of the sea. The waters are a wall to them on their right hand, and on their left. Thus the LORD saves Israel that day out of the hand of the Egyptians. And Israel sees the Egyptians dead upon the sea shore.

KEY 14:13: 'And Moses said unto the people, Fear ye not, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD, which he will shew to you to day: for the Egyptians whom ye have seen to day, ye shall see them again no more for ever.' Fear not. Stand still. See. When we face an impossible situation, our first instinct is to panic, or to run, or to try to fix it ourselves. Moses told the people to stop, and watch what God would do. Salvation was not something they would win. It was something they would see. Sometimes the bravest thing we can do is to be still and trust God.

KEY 14:14: 'The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.' Israel had no army that could stand against Pharaoh’s chariots. But they did not need one. The LORD himself fought for them. Their part was to trust, and to stop their panicked crying. Then God told them to go forward. Faith is both. Stand still and trust God to fight. Then, when he opens the way, step forward.

KEY 14:31: 'And Israel saw that great work which the LORD did upon the Egyptians: and the people feared the LORD, and believed the LORD, and his servant Moses.' Just a few hours before, the people were screaming at Moses. Now they believed. They had seen with their own eyes what God could do. Every time God brings us through something impossible, it is meant to strengthen our faith for the next time. Do not forget the seas God has already parted for you.

THREAD: Paul wrote that our fathers 'all passed through the sea; And were all baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea'. And Isaiah heard God say, 'When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee'.

TODAY: Is there a situation where you feel trapped, with no way out? Bring it to God. Hear his words: fear not, stand still, and see. Trust him to fight for you. And when he opens a way, step forward in faith.

QUESTION: Moses said, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD. What situation do you need to stop panicking about, and trust God to handle?

PRAYER: Lord God, you made a way through the sea, where there was no way. When I am afraid and trapped, help me to stand still and trust you. Fight for me, Lord, and when you open the way, give me faith to go forward. Thank you for every sea you have already parted in my life. Amen.
