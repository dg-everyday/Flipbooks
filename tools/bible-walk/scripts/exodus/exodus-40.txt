# Exodus 40: the last chapter; God tells Moses to set up the tabernacle (the date of 40:2 and 40:17 not given), put in the ark and cover it with the vail, the table, the candlestick, the golden altar, the door, the altar of burnt offering, the laver, the court; anoint everything, wash and clothe and anoint Aaron and his sons; Thus did Moses; Moses rears it up piece by piece in the order of approach from the inside out, the refrain as the LORD commanded Moses seven times; So Moses finished the work; the cloud covered the tent and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle, Moses not able to enter; the cloud leads their journeys (by day and night only inside quotes). The whole of Exodus: from slaves building for Pharaoh to a free people building for God, from God far off to God dwelling among them; 1 Kings 8:10-11, John 1:14, 1 Corinthians 3:16, Revelation 21:3. Final NEXT: Numbers. Keys 40:16, 40:34, 40:38.
TITLE: The Glory of the LORD Filled the Tabernacle

HOOK: Imagine the whole camp of Israel, gathered around and watching. In the middle, Moses is setting up the tabernacle. Board by board, curtain by curtain, the tent rises. The lamps are lit. The last curtain is hung at the gate. And then, as the people watch in silence, a great cloud comes down from the sky, and covers the tent. And the glory of God fills it, so brightly that even Moses cannot go in.

WHERE: In chapter thirty nine, the holy garments were made for Aaron and his sons, with gold beaten into thin wires, twelve precious stones, golden bells, and the plate that said HOLINESS TO THE LORD. Seven times we heard, as the LORD commanded Moses. The people brought all the work to Moses. And Moses saw that they had done it just as God commanded, and Moses blessed them.

Now we come to the last chapter of Exodus. Listen for the same phrase, repeated seven times again. And listen for what happens when the work is finished.

STORY: The LORD spoke to Moses, and told him to set up the tabernacle. And God gave him the order, step by step, from the ark inside the veil, out to the curtain at the gate.

Then anoint it all with the holy oil, the tabernacle and everything in it, to make it holy. And bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the tabernacle, wash them with water, dress them in the holy garments, and anoint them, so that they may serve as priests.

And then.

So Moses set to work. He set up the tabernacle. He fastened the silver sockets, set up the boards, put in the bars, and raised the pillars. He spread the tent over the top, and the coverings over that, 'as the LORD commanded Moses.'

He took the two tables of stone, the testimony, and put them in the ark. He put the poles on the ark, and the mercy seat on top. He carried the ark into the tabernacle, and hung the veil in front of it, 'as the LORD commanded Moses.'

He put the table on the north side, outside the veil, and set the bread on it, 'as the LORD had commanded Moses.' He put the lampstand on the south side, and lit the lamps before the LORD, 'as the LORD commanded Moses.' He put the golden altar in front of the veil, and burned sweet incense on it, 'as the LORD commanded Moses.'

He hung the curtain at the door. He set the altar of burnt offering by the door, and offered a burnt offering, 'as the LORD commanded Moses.' He set the laver between the tent and the altar, and filled it with water. And Moses and Aaron and his sons washed their hands and feet there, 'as the LORD commanded Moses.'

Notice the order. Moses began in the most holy place, with the ark, where God would dwell. And then he worked outwards, towards the gate. From God’s presence, out towards the people.

'And he reared up the court round about the tabernacle and the altar, and set up the hanging of the court gate. So Moses finished the work.'

So Moses finished the work. And then.

All the giving and building was for this moment. So that God could dwell among his people.

The glory was so great that even Moses, who had spoken with God face to face, could not go in. 'And Moses was not able to enter into the tent of the congregation, because the cloud abode thereon, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.'

And from then on, the cloud guided them. 'And when the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel went onward in all their journeys: But if the cloud were not taken up, then they journeyed not till the day that it was taken up.' When the cloud moved, they packed up and followed. When it stayed, they stayed.

And so the book of Exodus ends. Think how it began. God’s people were slaves in Egypt, forced to build cities for Pharaoh. Now they are free, and they have built a house for God, willingly, with joy. At the beginning, God seemed far away, and the people cried out in their suffering. Now, God lives right in the middle of their camp, and his glory fills his house. From slavery to freedom. From building for Pharaoh to building for God. From God far off, to God dwelling among them.

And the story goes on. Years later, when Solomon built the temple, the same glory filled it. Then Jesus came, and John wrote that the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory. Paul wrote that God’s people are now his temple, and his Spirit dwells in us. And at the very end of the Bible, John heard a great voice from heaven saying, behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them.

KEY 40:16: 'Thus did Moses: according to all that the LORD commanded him, so did he.' Moses did exactly what God asked, step by step. All through this chapter, we hear it again and again: as the LORD commanded Moses. Obedience was the way to God’s glory. When we obey God, we make room for his presence in our lives.

KEY 40:34: 'Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.' This is the high point of the whole book of Exodus. God had promised, let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them. And now he kept his promise. The glory of the LORD means his shining, holy, awesome presence. The holy God came to live with his forgiven people. That is what God has always wanted. To be with us.

KEY 40:38: 'For the cloud of the LORD was upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.' At every hour, everyone could see that God was with them. A cloud in the light, and a fire in the dark. Throughout all their journeys. Wherever they went, God went with them. And Jesus promises the same to everyone who follows him. Lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.

THREAD: John wrote, 'And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us'. And John heard, 'Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them, and they shall be his people'.

TODAY: Thank God that he wants to dwell with you. Through Jesus, and by his Spirit, he is with you wherever you go today. And think back over the whole book of Exodus. What is one thing you will remember?

QUESTION: Exodus begins with God’s people as slaves building for Pharaoh, and ends with God’s glory filling the house they built for him. What does this whole story teach you about God?

PRAYER: Lord God, thank you that you rescued your people, and came to dwell among them. Thank you that your glory filled the tabernacle. Thank you for Jesus, who came to live among us, and for your Spirit, who lives in your people. Go with me throughout all my journeys. Help me to follow where you lead. And one day, bring me home, to dwell with you for ever. Amen.
