# Ezra 5: Haggai and Zechariah prophesy, Zerubbabel and Jeshua rise up and build, the prophets helping them, Tatnai the governor asks who commanded you, the eye of their God upon the elders, the letter to Darius (the house of the great God, the work goeth fast on), the elders' answer (servants of the God of heaven and earth; our fathers provoked God; Cyrus made a decree; Sheshbazzar), search the records. Haggai 1 told briefly. Keys 5:1, 5:5, 5:11.
TITLE: The Eye of Their God Was upon the Elders

HOOK: A group of builders are hard at work on a big stone building. Suddenly, the governor of the province arrives, with his officials and soldiers. He stands with his arms folded. Who gave you permission to build this? he demands. What are your names? The builders could have dropped their tools and run. Instead, they keep working, and give him an answer.

WHERE: In chapter four, the enemies of Judah offered to help build the temple, and were refused. Then they weakened the hands of the builders, wrote letters to the kings of Persia, and frightened them, until the work of the house of God stopped. For years, the foundation sat untouched.

Now, in chapter five, God gets his people building again. Listen for who God sends. And listen for how the builders answer the governor.

STORY: For years, the temple foundation had lain there, with nothing built on it. The people had settled down. They built their own homes. They worked their own fields. And the house of God stayed a ruin.

Then God sends two prophets.

We will walk through the book of Haggai later, but here is a taste. Haggai asked the people, is it time for you to live in your fine panelled houses, while this house lies in ruins? You have planted much, but harvested little. You earn wages, but put them in a bag with holes. Why? Because my house is in ruins, while each of you is busy with his own house. Go up into the hills, God said, bring wood, and build the house.

And the people listen. 'Then rose up Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and began to build the house of God which is at Jerusalem: and with them were the prophets of God helping them.' The prophets did not just preach. They rolled up their sleeves, and helped.

But as soon as the building starts again, so does the opposition. Tatnai, the Persian governor of the whole province west of the river Euphrates, comes with another official, Shethar-boznai, and their companions. And they ask, 'Who hath commanded you to build this house, and to make up this wall?' And they ask for the names of the men in charge.

It was a threatening question. Last time, the work had been stopped by force. Would it happen again?

But something was different this time.

The governor does not stop them. He writes a letter to king Darius, and lets them keep building until the answer comes.

And the writer gives us a copy of Tatnai’s letter. It is surprisingly fair. To king Darius, all peace. We went to the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. It is being built with great stones, and timber is being laid in the walls. And this work goes on quickly, and prospers in their hands.

We asked the elders, who commanded you to build this house? And we asked their names, so we could tell you.

And then comes their answer.

They go on, honestly. We know why the first house was destroyed. Our fathers made the God of heaven angry, so he gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, who destroyed the house, and carried the people away.

But in the first year of Cyrus, king of Babylon, Cyrus made a decree to build this house of God. He gave back the gold and silver vessels that Nebuchadnezzar took from the temple, to a man named Sheshbazzar, and told him, take these, carry them to the temple in Jerusalem, and let the house of God be built in its place. Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundation. And it has been in building ever since, and is not yet finished.

So, Tatnai writes, if it please the king, let a search be made in the royal records in Babylon, to see whether king Cyrus really did make this decree. And let the king tell us what he wants.

And the builders keep building, waiting for the king’s answer.

KEY 5:1: 'Then the prophets, Haggai the prophet, and Zechariah the son of Iddo, prophesied unto the Jews that were in Judah and Jerusalem in the name of the God of Israel, even unto them.' When God’s people were stuck, God sent his Word. The people did not need more money or more soldiers. They needed to hear God speak. And when they heard, they got up and built. God’s Word has power to get stuck people moving again.

KEY 5:5: 'But the eye of their God was upon the elders of the Jews, that they could not cause them to cease, till the matter came to Darius: and then they returned answer by letter concerning this matter.' The eye of their God was upon them. The builders were not alone. God was watching over them. The governor had power, and soldiers, and the right to stop them. But he could not, because God’s eye was on his people. When we do what God has asked, we work under his watchful care.

KEY 5:11: 'And thus they returned us answer, saying, We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and build the house that was builded these many years ago, which a great king of Israel builded and set up.' When asked who they were, they did not give their own names first. They said, we are the servants of the God of heaven and earth. That was their identity. They were not ashamed of God before a powerful official. They told the truth, boldly and respectfully.

THREAD: Haggai heard God say, 'Go up to the mountain, and bring wood, and build the house; and I will take pleasure in it'. And Zechariah heard God say, 'Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.'

TODAY: Is there something God has asked you to do that you have put off, while you got busy with your own things? Today could be the day to start again. And if someone asks you who you are, do not be ashamed to say that you belong to God.

QUESTION: The builders said, we are the servants of the God of heaven and earth. How would you describe who you are, if someone asked about your faith?

PRAYER: Lord God, thank you that you send your Word to get your people moving again. Thank you that your eye is upon those who serve you. Forgive me for putting my own things before your work. Give me courage to say, I am a servant of the God of heaven and earth, and to keep on building what you have asked me to build. Amen.
