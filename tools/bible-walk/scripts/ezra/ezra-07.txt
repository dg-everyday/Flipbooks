# Ezra 7: after these things (the gap; Esther's time), Ezra's line back to Aaron, a ready scribe, the king granted all his request according to the hand of the LORD, the journey (dates inside quotes), Ezra had prepared his heart to seek, do and teach, Artaxerxes' letter (freewill, silver and gold, whatever the God of heaven commands, no tax on temple servants, set magistrates and teach), Ezra blesses the LORD who put it in the king's heart, I was strengthened. 7:26 not quoted. Keys 7:6, 7:10, 7:27.
TITLE: Ezra Had Prepared His Heart

HOOK: A man sits at a table in Babylon, with an old scroll spread out before him. He reads it slowly, word by word. He has read it many times before. But he reads it again, because he wants to understand it better, and to live it, and to teach it to others. He is a scribe, a teacher of God’s law. And soon, he will make a long journey, to bring that law home.

WHERE: In chapter six, king Darius found Cyrus’s decree, and ordered that the temple be built, and even paid for it from his own taxes. The house of God was finished, and the people dedicated it with joy. They kept the passover, for the LORD had made them joyful.

Now, in chapter seven, the story jumps forward many years. We meet the man the book is named after. Listen for what kind of man Ezra was. And listen for the good hand of God.

STORY: 'Now after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia'. After these things. Many years have passed since the temple was finished. The people who first came home are old now, or have died. And in between, in the reign of the king before Artaxerxes, the events of the book of Esther took place. A young Jewish woman became queen of Persia, and God used her to save her people from destruction. Now, under the next king, the story of Ezra begins.

The writer gives us Ezra’s family line, going all the way back, through Zadok and Phinehas and Eleazar, to Aaron, the first high priest, the brother of Moses. Ezra was a priest from the most important priestly family of all.

But he was more than a priest.

A ready scribe means a skilled teacher, an expert in God’s law. And the king of Persia gave Ezra everything he asked for. Why? According to the hand of the LORD his God upon him.

So Ezra sets out from Babylon, with a group of Israelites, priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers and temple servants. The journey takes them about four months. 'according to the good hand of his God upon him.' There is that phrase again.

And then the writer tells us the secret of Ezra’s life.

Then the writer gives us a copy of the letter king Artaxerxes gave to Ezra. It is an amazing letter, for a Persian king. Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, a scribe of the law of the God of heaven. Any of the people of Israel, the priests and the Levites in my kingdom, who want to go to Jerusalem, may go with you.

The king and his advisers give silver and gold as a gift to the God of Israel. Ezra may also collect gifts from all the province of Babylon, and from the people and priests. Use it, the king says, to buy animals for the sacrifices, and offer them on the altar of the house of your God. Do whatever seems good to you with the rest of the money, according to the will of your God. And deliver the vessels for the service of the house of your God.

The king also commands all his treasurers in the region to give Ezra whatever he asks, up to a generous limit. 'Whatsoever is commanded by the God of heaven, let it be diligently done for the house of the God of heaven'. The king seems to want to stay on the right side of the God of heaven.

He also orders that the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers and temple servants should not have to pay any taxes. And he tells Ezra, with the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint judges who know the laws of your God, to judge all the people in the region. 'and teach ye them that know them not.'

And when Ezra reads this letter, he bursts into praise.

And Ezra goes on, God has shown me mercy before the king, and his advisers, and all his mighty princes. 'And I was strengthened as the hand of the LORD my God was upon me, and I gathered together out of Israel chief men to go up with me.'

Notice that from this point, Ezra starts speaking for himself, saying I, and me. We are hearing his own story, in his own words.

KEY 7:6: 'This Ezra went up from Babylon; and he was a ready scribe in the law of Moses, which the LORD God of Israel had given: and the king granted him all his request, according to the hand of the LORD his God upon him.' The hand of the LORD his God upon him. Ezra knew that his success was not because he was clever, or because the king liked him. It was because God’s hand was on him. When good things happen in our lives, it is good to remember whose hand is behind them.

KEY 7:10: 'For Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of the LORD, and to do it, and to teach in Israel statutes and judgments.' This is the heart of Ezra, and one of the best verses in the Bible for anyone who loves God’s Word. Notice the order. First, seek: study God’s Word. Then, do it: obey it in your own life. And only then, teach it to others. Ezra did not just know the Bible. He lived it. And that is why his teaching had power.

KEY 7:27: 'Blessed be the LORD God of our fathers, which hath put such a thing as this in the king’s heart, to beautify the house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem'. Ezra did not praise the king. He praised the LORD, who put the idea in the king’s heart. When someone does something kind for us, we can thank them, but we can also thank God who moved their heart.

THREAD: The psalmist wrote, 'But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.' And James wrote, 'But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only'.

TODAY: Prepare your heart today to seek God’s Word. Read a few verses, slowly. Then ask, what is one thing here I can do today? And who could I share it with?

QUESTION: Ezra prepared his heart to seek God’s Word, to do it, and to teach it. Which of those three do you find easiest, and which is hardest?

PRAYER: Lord God, thank you for your Word, and for people like Ezra who loved it. Prepare my heart to seek your Word, to do it, and to share it with others. Thank you that your good hand is upon those who seek you. When good things happen, help me to remember that they come from you. Amen.
