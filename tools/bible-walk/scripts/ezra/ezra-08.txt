# Ezra 8: the heads of families who went up (told briefly), gathered at the river Ahava, no Levites found, Ezra sends for ministers, by the good hand of our God they come, the fast at Ahava, ashamed to ask for soldiers (the hand of our God is upon all them for good that seek him), he was intreated of us, the treasure weighed out to twelve priests (ye are holy, the vessels are holy), the journey, delivered from those that lay in wait, weighed again in Jerusalem, the offerings for all Israel, the king's commissions. Keys 8:21, 8:22, 8:23.
TITLE: The Hand of Our God Is upon All Them for Good

HOOK: A group of travellers camps beside a river, with a long and dangerous journey ahead. They are carrying a great fortune in silver and gold, through lands full of bandits. The king would happily give them soldiers to protect them. All they have to do is ask. But their leader has told the king, our God protects those who seek him. How can he ask for soldiers now?

WHERE: In chapter seven, we met Ezra, a priest from the family of Aaron, and a ready scribe in the law of Moses. Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of the LORD, and to do it, and to teach it. King Artaxerxes gave him a letter, and gold and silver for the house of God. And Ezra blessed the LORD, for the good hand of his God was upon him.

Now, in chapter eight, Ezra gathers his travellers and sets out. Listen for what Ezra does instead of asking for soldiers. And listen for how God answers.

STORY: Ezra begins by listing the leaders of the families who went with him, and how many men came with each one. Among them is a descendant of king David. In all, there were well over a thousand men, as well as their families.

Ezra gathers them all by a river called Ahava, and they camp there for three days. And as Ezra looks over the people and the priests, he notices something. There are no Levites. The Levites were the helpers in the temple, and they were needed for the service of God’s house. But not one had come.

So Ezra sends a group of leaders to a place called Casiphia, where many Levites lived. He tells them exactly what to say. Please send us ministers for the house of our God.

And they come. 'And by the good hand of our God upon us they brought us a man of understanding'. Several families of Levites come, and many temple servants as well. Every one listed by name.

Then comes the moment that shows Ezra’s faith.

Why fast? Fasting means going without food, to give ourselves fully to seeking God. They had a long journey ahead, with children and possessions, through dangerous country. They needed God to lead them and protect them.

And Ezra explains why he did not ask the king for an army.

So what did they do? They prayed.

Then Ezra chooses twelve of the leading priests. And he weighs out the silver, the gold and the vessels, the offering for the house of God from the king, his advisers, his princes and all Israel. A huge amount of silver and gold, gold bowls, and two vessels of fine bronze, as precious as gold. Ezra weighs everything carefully, and hands it over.

And he says to the priests, 'Ye are holy unto the LORD; the vessels are holy also; and the silver and the gold are a freewill offering unto the LORD God of your fathers.' Guard them carefully, until you weigh them again before the leaders in Jerusalem, in the house of the LORD.

Ezra was careful and honest. Everything was weighed before they left, and would be weighed again when they arrived, so that no one could ever say anything had gone missing.

Then they set out from the river Ahava. 'and the hand of our God was upon us, and he delivered us from the hand of the enemy, and of such as lay in wait by the way.' There were enemies and bandits waiting along the road. But God kept them safe, all the way.

They arrive in Jerusalem, and rest for three days. On the fourth day, the silver, the gold and the vessels are weighed in the house of God. Everything is there, by number and by weight, and it is all written down.

Then those who had come home from exile offer burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, and rams, lambs, and twelve goats for a sin offering. Again, twelve, for all the tribes. And they deliver the king’s orders to the governors of the region, who help the people and the house of God.

KEY 8:21: 'Then I proclaimed a fast there, at the river of Ahava, that we might afflict ourselves before our God, to seek of him a right way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance.' A right way means a straight and safe road. Ezra did not set off and hope for the best. Before the journey began, he sought God. Before big decisions, before journeys, before new beginnings, it is wise to stop, and seek God, and ask him to lead us.

KEY 8:22: 'For I was ashamed to require of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy in the way: because we had spoken unto the king, saying, The hand of our God is upon all them for good that seek him; but his power and his wrath is against all them that forsake him.' Ezra had told the king that God protects his people. Now he had to live what he said. It is one thing to tell others that we trust God. It is another to act on it, when it costs us something. Our actions show whether we really believe what we say.

KEY 8:23: 'So we fasted and besought our God for this: and he was intreated of us.' He was intreated of us means God listened to their request, and answered it. One short sentence. They prayed, and God heard. Prayer is not a last resort. It is the first and best thing we can do. And God hears.

THREAD: Solomon wrote, 'In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.' And the psalmist wrote, 'The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.'

TODAY: Is there a journey, a decision, or a new beginning ahead of you? Stop, and seek God first. Ask him for a right way. And if you have told others that you trust God, look for a way to show that trust today.

QUESTION: Ezra was ashamed to ask for soldiers, after telling the king that God protects those who seek him. How do your actions show what you really believe about God?

PRAYER: Lord God, your hand is upon all those who seek you, for good. Before I make decisions or set out on journeys, help me to seek you first. Lead me in a right way, and protect those I love. Help me to live what I say I believe. And thank you that when we pray, you hear. Amen.
