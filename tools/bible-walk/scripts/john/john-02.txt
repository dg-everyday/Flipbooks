# John 2: the wedding at Cana, water into wine, the first sign, Jesus cleanses the temple, destroy this temple, he knew what was in man. Keys 2:5, 2:11, 2:19.
TITLE: Whatsoever He Saith unto You, Do It

HOOK: At a village wedding, the celebration is in full swing. Then a servant whispers some terrible news to the master of the feast: the wine has run out. In that culture, it was a deep embarrassment for the family. A woman quietly goes to her son, and tells him. Then she says to the servants, whatever he tells you, do it.

WHERE: In chapter one, John told us that in the beginning was the Word, and the Word was God, and the Word was made flesh. John the Baptist pointed to Jesus and said, behold the Lamb of God. The first disciples followed him. Nathanael said, thou art the Son of God. And Jesus said, thou shalt see greater things than these.

Now, in chapter two, the greater things begin. Jesus does the first of his signs at a wedding. Then he goes to Jerusalem, to the temple, and does something that shocks everyone. Two very different scenes. Both show us who Jesus is.

STORY: On the third day, there is a marriage in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus is there. Jesus and his disciples are invited too.

When the wine runs out, the mother of Jesus says to him, they have no wine. Jesus says, woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come. That sounds cold to us. But woman was a respectful way to address someone, and Jesus is telling his mother that he now acts on his Father’s timing. Mary does not argue. She trusts him completely.

Mary turns to the servants.

There are six stone waterpots there, used for the Jewish washing customs. Each holds two or three firkins, about a hundred litres. Jesus says to the servants, fill the waterpots with water. And they fill them up to the brim. Then he says, draw out now, and bear unto the governor of the feast. And they bring it.

When the ruler of the feast tastes the water that was made wine, not knowing where it came from, though the servants who drew the water know, he calls the bridegroom. Every man at the beginning sets forth good wine, he says, and when the guests have had plenty, then that which is worse. 'but thou hast kept the good wine until now.'

The jars for washing were turned into the best wine, far more than anyone needed. Jesus brings joy, and he brings it in abundance.

After this, Jesus goes down to Capernaum, with his mother, his brothers and his disciples, and they stay there a few days.

The Passover is near, and Jesus goes up to Jerusalem. In the temple, he finds people selling oxen, sheep and doves, and the changers of money sitting there. People came from far away to worship, and needed animals for sacrifice and the right coins for the temple tax. But the outer courts of God’s house had become a noisy, money-making market.

Jesus makes a whip of small cords, and drives them all out of the temple, the sheep and the oxen. He pours out the changers’ money and overturns their tables. And he says to those who sell doves, take these things hence; make not my Father’s house an house of merchandise. His disciples remember that it was written, 'The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up.'

The Jewish leaders ask him, what sign do you show us, seeing that you do these things? What gives you the right?

Then the leaders say, forty and six years was this temple in building, and wilt thou rear it up in three days? But he spoke of the temple of his body. When he was risen from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this to them, and they believed the scripture, and the word which Jesus had said.

While he is in Jerusalem at the Passover, many believe in his name, when they see the miracles he does. But Jesus does not commit himself to them, because he knows all men. He does not need anyone to tell him about people, 'for he knew what was in man.' He knows the difference between people who are excited by miracles, and people who truly trust him.

KEY 2:5: 'His mother saith unto the servants, Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it.' These are the last recorded words of Mary in the Gospels, and they are wonderful advice. She did not tell Jesus what to do, or tell the servants what to expect. She simply pointed to Jesus, and said, do whatever he tells you. The servants filled the jars with water, not knowing why. Their obedience made room for the miracle. Whatever Jesus tells you, do it.

KEY 2:11: 'This beginning of miracles did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested forth his glory; and his disciples believed on him.' John calls it a sign. A sign points beyond itself. Turning water into wine showed Jesus’ power over creation, and his generosity. Jesus came not to make life smaller, but fuller. And it showed his glory, and his disciples believed. Every sign in John’s Gospel has the same purpose: to show us who Jesus is, so that we will believe.

KEY 2:19: 'Jesus answered and said unto them, Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.' The temple was where God met his people. Jesus was saying that he himself is now the place where we meet God. They would destroy his body on the cross, but in three days he would rise. Even here, at the start of his ministry, Jesus knew where his road was going: to the cross, and to the empty tomb.

THREAD: The prophet Malachi wrote, 'the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple'. And Paul wrote to believers, 'Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?'

TODAY: Is there something Jesus has told you to do, in his Word, that you have not done because you do not see the point? Take Mary’s advice. Whatever he says to you, do it. And ask him to clear out anything in your heart that does not belong in God’s house.

QUESTION: Mary said, whatsoever he saith unto you, do it. What is one thing Jesus is asking you to do, that you need to obey even before you understand it?

PRAYER: Lord Jesus, thank you that you bring joy and fullness of life. Help me to do whatever you tell me, as the servants did at Cana. Clean out anything in my heart that does not belong there. You know what is in me. Make me a true believer, not just someone excited by what you can do. Amen.
