# John 6: feeding the five thousand, Jesus walks on the sea, the bread of life, him that cometh to me, many turn back, to whom shall we go. Keys 6:35, 6:37, 6:68.
TITLE: I Am the Bread of Life

HOOK: Five thousand men, and women and children besides, sit on the grass on a hillside, hungry. A boy has brought a small lunch: five barley loaves and two small fish. One of the disciples asks, but what are they among so many? Jesus takes the bread, and gives thanks. And everyone eats until they are full.

WHERE: In chapter five, Jesus healed a man who had been waiting by the pool of Bethesda for thirty-eight years. He asked him, wilt thou be made whole? Because it was the sabbath, the religious leaders turned against Jesus. And Jesus said, search the scriptures, for they are they which testify of me.

Now, in chapter six, Jesus does two more signs: feeding a great crowd, and walking on the sea. Then he explains what the feeding meant, in one of his most famous I AM sayings. But many people do not like what he says, and walk away.

STORY: Jesus goes over the sea of Galilee, and a great multitude follows him, because they see the miracles he does on the sick. Jesus goes up into a mountain, and sits with his disciples. The Passover is near.

Jesus lifts up his eyes, and sees a great company coming toward him. He says to Philip, whence shall we buy bread, that these may eat? He says this to test Philip, for he himself knows what he will do. Philip says, two hundred pennyworth of bread is not enough for each of them to have even a little. That was more than six months’ wages.

Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, says, there is a lad here, which hath five barley loaves, and two small fishes: but what are they among so many?

Jesus says, make the men sit down. There is much grass in the place. So the men sit down, about five thousand. Jesus takes the loaves, and when he has given thanks, he distributes to the disciples, and the disciples to those who are sitting down, and likewise of the fishes, as much as they want.

When they are filled, he says to his disciples, gather up the fragments that remain, that nothing be lost. They gather them, and fill twelve baskets with the leftovers of the five barley loaves.

The people say, this is of a truth that prophet that should come into the world. And when Jesus sees that they will come and take him by force, to make him a king, he departs again into a mountain by himself alone.

As the day ends, his disciples go down to the sea, get into a boat, and start across toward Capernaum. It is now dark, and Jesus has not come to them. The sea rises because of a great wind. When they have rowed three or four miles, they see Jesus walking on the sea, and drawing near to the boat, and they are afraid. But he says to them, 'It is I; be not afraid.' Then they willingly receive him into the boat, and immediately the boat is at the land where they were going.

The next day, the crowd finds Jesus at Capernaum. Jesus says to them, you are seeking me, not because you saw the signs, but because you ate of the loaves, and were filled. Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life, which the Son of man shall give you.

They say, what sign do you show, that we may believe? Our fathers ate manna in the desert. Jesus says, Moses gave you not that bread from heaven. My Father gives you the true bread from heaven. For the bread of God is he which cometh down from heaven, and giveth life unto the world. They say, Lord, evermore give us this bread.

And Jesus answers.

Jesus goes on to say that the bread he will give is his flesh, which he will give for the life of the world. Whoever eats his flesh and drinks his blood has eternal life. Many find this very hard to understand. Christians have understood these words in different ways. But Jesus is clearly pointing to his death on the cross, when he would give himself for us. And he is calling us to depend on him completely for life, as we depend on food.

Many of his disciples, when they hear this, say, this is an hard saying; who can hear it? Jesus says, 'the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.' But from that time many of his disciples go back, and walk no more with him.

Then Jesus turns to the twelve, and asks, will ye also go away?

KEY 6:35: 'And Jesus said unto them, I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst.' This is the first of the seven great I AM sayings in John. Bread is the most basic food, eaten every day. Jesus is saying that he is not an extra in life, a luxury. He is what we need to live. Just as our bodies hunger for food, our souls hunger for God. And only Jesus can satisfy that hunger.

KEY 6:37: 'All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.' In no wise means not in any way, never. Whoever you are, whatever you have done, however unworthy you feel, if you come to Jesus, he will not turn you away. You will never be rejected by him. That is a promise you can stand on.

KEY 6:68: 'Then Simon Peter answered him, Lord, to whom shall we go? thou hast the words of eternal life.' Many walked away. Jesus asked the twelve, will you also go away? Peter did not understand everything Jesus had said. But he knew one thing: there was nowhere else to go. No one else has the words of eternal life. Sometimes faith is not having all the answers. It is knowing there is no one else worth following.

THREAD: In the wilderness, God rained bread from heaven, and the people called it manna. Moses said, 'This is the bread which the LORD hath given you to eat.' And Jesus taught us to pray, 'Give us this day our daily bread.' Jesus is the true bread from heaven, given for the life of the world.

TODAY: Are there hard sayings of Jesus that you struggle with? Do not walk away. Bring your questions to him, and say with Peter, to whom shall I go? And remember that whoever comes to him, he will never cast out.

QUESTION: Peter said, Lord, to whom shall we go? thou hast the words of eternal life. When following Jesus feels hard, what keeps you from walking away?

PRAYER: Lord Jesus, you are the bread of life. Satisfy the hunger of my soul. Thank you that whoever comes to you, you will never cast out. When I do not understand, help me to stay close to you, and to say with Peter, to whom shall I go? You have the words of eternal life. Amen.
