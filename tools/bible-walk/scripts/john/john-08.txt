# John 8: the woman brought to Jesus, he that is without sin, go and sin no more, the light of the world, the truth shall make you free, before Abraham was, I am. Keys 8:12, 8:32, 8:36. Told gently for all ages.
TITLE: The Truth Shall Make You Free

HOOK: A crowd pushes a frightened woman into the temple court and forces her to stand before Jesus. Her accusers are holding stones. They say she has been caught in sin, and the law says she should die. What do you say? they ask him. Jesus does not answer. He stoops down, and writes with his finger in the dust.

WHERE: In chapter seven, at the Feast of Tabernacles, the crowds argued about who Jesus was. On the great day of the feast, Jesus cried, if any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink. The officers sent to arrest him came back saying, never man spake like this man.

Now, in chapter eight, the arguments grow sharper. The chapter begins with a woman in great danger, and a moment of mercy. Then Jesus makes two great claims: I am the light of the world, and before Abraham was, I am.

STORY: Jesus goes to the mount of Olives. Early the next day he comes again into the temple, and all the people come to him, and he sits down and teaches them.

The scribes and Pharisees bring to him a woman caught in sin, the sin of unfaithfulness in marriage. They set her in the middle, and say, Master, Moses in the law commanded us that such should be stoned. But what do you say? They say this to trap him. If Jesus says let her go, they can say he breaks the law of Moses. If he says stone her, he loses his reputation for mercy, and goes against the Romans, who did not allow the Jews to put people to death.

But Jesus stoops down, and with his finger writes on the ground, as though he did not hear them.

They keep asking him. So he lifts himself up, and says to them, 'He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her.' And again he stoops down, and writes on the ground.

And they which hear it, convicted by their own conscience, go out one by one, beginning at the eldest, even unto the last. Jesus is left alone, with the woman standing in the middle.

Jesus lifts himself up, and says, woman, where are those thine accusers? Hath no man condemned thee? She says, no man, Lord. And Jesus says to her, 'Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more.'

Notice both halves. Neither do I condemn thee: that is grace. Go, and sin no more: that is truth. Jesus did not excuse her sin. But he did not throw stones either. He set her free, and called her to a new life.

Then Jesus speaks again to the people.

The Pharisees say, you bear record of yourself, so your record is not true. Jesus answers that his Father bears witness of him too. They ask, where is thy Father? Jesus answers, ye neither know me, nor my Father. If ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also.

Jesus warns them, if ye believe not that I am he, ye shall die in your sins. Many believe on him as he speaks these words. And Jesus says to those who believed, if ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed.

Some of them answer, we are Abraham’s seed, and were never in bondage to any man. How can you say, ye shall be made free? Jesus answers, verily, verily, I say unto you, whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin. And the servant abideth not in the house for ever, but the Son abideth ever.

The argument grows heated. They say Abraham is their father. Jesus says, if ye were Abraham’s children, ye would do the works of Abraham. They say God is their Father. Jesus says, if God were your Father, ye would love me, for I proceeded forth and came from God.

Then Jesus says, your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day, and he saw it, and was glad. They say, thou art not yet fifty years old, and hast thou seen Abraham? And Jesus says, 'Verily, verily, I say unto you, Before Abraham was, I am.'

Jesus does not say, before Abraham was, I was. He says, I am. It is the name God gave Moses at the burning bush. Jesus is claiming to be the eternal God. They understand him exactly. They take up stones to throw at him. But Jesus hides himself, and goes out of the temple, passing through the middle of them.

KEY 8:12: 'Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.' During the Feast of Tabernacles, great lamps were lit in the temple courts, lighting up the whole city. Jesus said, I am the light, not just of the temple, or of Jerusalem, but of the world. Without him, we walk in darkness, unable to see the way. With him, we have the light of life. To follow Jesus is to walk in the light.

KEY 8:32: 'And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.' These words are often quoted on their own. But Jesus said them to people who believed in him, and he began with a condition: if ye continue in my word. The truth that sets us free is not just facts. It is Jesus himself, and his word. When we live in his word, we come to know the truth, and it breaks the chains of lies, fear and sin.

KEY 8:36: 'If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed.' The people said they had never been in bondage. But Jesus said that everyone who sins is a slave to sin. We cannot free ourselves. But the Son can. And the freedom he gives is not partial, or temporary. It is real freedom. Free indeed. Whatever has held you, if the Son makes you free, you are free.

THREAD: The psalm says, 'The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?' And Paul wrote, 'Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.'

TODAY: Is there someone you are quick to condemn? Remember the woman in the temple, and lay down your stones. And is there a sin or a fear that holds you? Bring it to Jesus, and continue in his word. If the Son makes you free, you will be free indeed.

QUESTION: Jesus said to the woman, neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more. How do you need to receive both his mercy and his call today?

PRAYER: Lord Jesus, you are the light of the world, and the great I AM. Thank you that you do not condemn me, and that you call me to a new life. Help me to continue in your word, and to know the truth. Set me free from every chain, so that I am free indeed. Amen.
