# Matthew 9: the paralysed man forgiven and healed (power on earth to forgive sins); Matthew called (9:9, the author); eating with publicans and sinners (the physician; mercy, not sacrifice, Hosea 6:6); new wine in new bottles; the ruler's daughter and the woman who touched his hem; two blind men (Son of David); the man who could not speak; compassion on the multitudes, sheep without a shepherd, the harvest. Keys 9:13, 9:36, 9:38.
TITLE: The Harvest Truly Is Plenteous

HOOK: A tax collector sits at his booth, surrounded by coins and lists of names. People hate him. The religious leaders will not eat with him. Then Jesus walks past, stops, and says, follow me. And that same day, the tax collector throws a dinner party, and invites all his friends to meet Jesus. The religious leaders are horrified. But this is the very man who will one day write this book.

WHERE: In chapter eight, Jesus came down from the mountain and showed his authority. He touched a leper, healed a soldier’s servant with a word, calmed a storm, and set two men free from evil spirits. The disciples asked, what manner of man is this?

Now, in chapter nine, Jesus shows his power to forgive sins, and calls Matthew to follow him. Listen for the kind of people Jesus spends time with. And listen for what Jesus feels when he looks at the crowds.

STORY: Jesus crosses the lake and comes to his own town, Capernaum. Some people bring him a paralysed man, lying on a bed. Jesus sees their faith and says to the man, 'Son, be of good cheer; thy sins be forgiven thee.'

Some of the scribes think to themselves, this man is blaspheming, speaking as if he were God. Only God can forgive sins. Jesus knows their thoughts. Which is easier to say, your sins are forgiven, or, get up and walk? Anyone can say the first, because no one can see it. So Jesus does the second, so they will know he has power to forgive sins. He says to the man, 'Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house.' And the man gets up and goes home. The crowds are amazed, and they praise God.

Then Jesus passes by a tax booth, and sees a man named Matthew sitting there. Jesus says, 'Follow me.' 'And he arose, and followed him.' This is the man whose name is on this Gospel. He tells his own story in one short verse.

Later, Jesus is eating in the house, and many tax collectors and sinners come and sit down with him and his disciples. When the Pharisees see it, they ask the disciples, why does your Master eat with tax collectors and sinners?

Jesus hears, and answers, 'They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick.' A doctor does not stay away from sick people. He goes to them. And then Jesus quotes the prophet Hosea.

Some of John’s disciples ask, why do we and the Pharisees fast, but your disciples do not? Jesus says, wedding guests do not mourn while the bridegroom is with them. A day will come when he is taken away, and then they will fast. And he says, no one pours new wine into old, dry wineskins, or they burst. New wine goes into new wineskins. Jesus was bringing something new, and it would not fit into the old ways.

While he is speaking, a ruler of the synagogue comes and kneels before him. My daughter has just died, he says. But come and lay your hand on her, and she will live. Jesus gets up and goes with him.

On the way, a woman who has been ill with bleeding for twelve years comes up behind him, and touches the hem of his garment. She thinks, if I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole. Jesus turns and sees her. 'Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole.' And she is healed.

When Jesus reaches the ruler’s house, there are musicians and mourners making a noise. Jesus says, go away, the girl is not dead, but sleeping. And they laugh at him. But when the people have been sent out, Jesus goes in, takes her by the hand, and the girl gets up. The news spreads through all that land.

As Jesus goes on, two blind men follow him, crying, 'Thou Son of David, have mercy on us.' Son of David was a name for the promised King. Jesus asks them, 'Believe ye that I am able to do this?' 'Yea, Lord.' Then he touches their eyes. 'According to your faith be it unto you.' And their eyes are opened.

Then people bring a man who cannot speak, troubled by a devil. Jesus casts out the devil, and the man speaks. The crowds say, nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel. But the Pharisees say, he casts out devils by the prince of the devils.

Jesus goes through all the towns and villages, teaching, preaching and healing. And then Matthew tells us what Jesus felt.

And Jesus says to his disciples, the harvest is great, but the workers are few.

KEY 9:13: 'But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.' The Pharisees kept all the rules, but they looked down on sinners. Jesus says, go and learn what God really wants. Mercy. God cares more about a kind heart than about religious show. And notice why Jesus came. Not for people who think they are good enough, but for sinners who know they need him. That includes every one of us.

KEY 9:36: 'But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.' Compassion means to feel deep pity and love, right down inside. Jesus did not see the crowd as a nuisance. He saw tired, lost people, like sheep wandering with no one to care for them. That is how Jesus sees people today, including you.

KEY 9:38: 'Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.' A harvest is a field full of ripe grain, ready to be gathered. Jesus says there are so many people ready to come to God, but not enough workers to bring them in. And the first thing he tells his disciples to do is pray. Very soon, in the next chapter, the disciples who prayed for workers will be sent out as workers themselves.

THREAD: The prophet Hosea heard God say, 'For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.' And Ezekiel heard God say, 'I will feed my flock, and I will cause them to lie down, saith the Lord GOD.'

TODAY: Jesus ate with people others avoided. Is there someone around you who is left out, that you could welcome? And pray today for the harvest. Ask God to send workers, and be ready in case he sends you.

QUESTION: Matthew left his tax booth and threw a dinner so his friends could meet Jesus. Who in your life would you like to introduce to Jesus?

PRAYER: Lord Jesus, thank you that you came to call sinners, and that you called me. Thank you for your compassion on tired and lost people. Lord of the harvest, send workers into your harvest, and make me willing to be one of them. Amen.
