# Matthew 28: the resurrection ("dawn" and "first day of the week" not said: "very early on the third day", as john-20); the earthquake and the angel, the stone rolled back, the guards as dead men; he is not here, for he is risen; fear and great joy; Jesus meets the women, go tell my brethren; the guards bribed ("by night" not said; 28:15 told without "among the Jews"); the mountain in Galilee, some doubted; the great commission; lo, I am with you alway (Emmanuel at the start; Haggai's "I am with you"). The book ends; NEXT points to Proverbs (step 19). Differs from mark-16 / luke-24. Keys 28:6, 28:19, 28:20.
TITLE: Lo, I Am with You Alway

HOOK: Two women walk slowly through the grey light towards a tomb. Their hearts are broken. They watched their friend and teacher die. Now they only want to sit near his grave. But as they come close, the ground begins to shake beneath their feet. A dazzling figure rolls the great stone away and sits on top of it. And the soldiers guarding the tomb fall to the ground like dead men.

WHERE: In chapter twenty seven, Pilate asked, what shall I do then with Jesus? The crowd chose Barabbas, and Jesus was crucified. As he died, the veil of the temple was torn in two, and a Roman centurion said, truly this was the Son of God. Jesus was buried in a new tomb, and the stone was sealed and guarded.

Now, in chapter twenty eight, the last chapter of Matthew, everything changes. Listen for what the angel says. Listen for what Jesus tells his disciples to do. And listen for the very last words of the book.

STORY: After the sabbath, very early on the third day, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary go to see the tomb.

Suddenly there is a great earthquake. An angel of the Lord comes down from heaven, rolls back the stone from the door, and sits on it. His face shines like lightning, and his clothes are as white as snow. The guards are so terrified that they shake and fall down like dead men.

But the angel speaks to the women. 'Fear not ye: for I know that ye seek Jesus, which was crucified.'

And then he tells them the news.

Go quickly, the angel says, and tell his disciples that he has risen from the dead, and he is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see him.

The women hurry away from the tomb 'with fear and great joy'. They run to tell the disciples. And on the way, Jesus himself meets them. 'All hail', he says, which means, greetings, rejoice. They come to him, take hold of his feet, and worship him. Jesus says, 'Be not afraid: go tell my brethren that they go into Galilee, and there shall they see me.' Notice what Jesus calls the disciples. My brethren. These were the men who had run away and left him. But he still calls them his brothers.

Meanwhile, some of the guards go into the city and tell the chief priests everything that happened. The chief priests meet with the elders, and they pay the soldiers a large amount of money. Tell people, his disciples came and stole the body while we were asleep. And if the governor hears about it, we will keep you out of trouble. So the soldiers take the money and do as they are told. And that story was still being spread in Matthew’s day. But think about it. If the guards were asleep, how could they know who took the body?

Then the eleven disciples go to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus told them to go. When they see him, they worship him. 'but some doubted.' Matthew is honest. Even then, not everyone found it easy to believe. And Jesus does not send the doubters away. He comes near to all of them.

Jesus comes and speaks to them. 'All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth.' In the wilderness, the devil offered Jesus all the kingdoms of the world, if he would bow down. Jesus refused. Now, after the cross, the Father has given him all authority, in heaven and on earth.

And with that authority, Jesus gives them a command.

And then come the last words of Matthew’s Gospel.

KEY 28:6: 'He is not here: for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay.' Three times in Matthew, Jesus told his disciples he would be killed and raised on the third day. Now the angel says, as he said. Jesus kept his word. The tomb is empty. Death could not hold him. And because he is risen, everything he said is true, and every promise he made will be kept. The angel says, come and see. The resurrection is not a story to be afraid of. It is good news to come and see for ourselves.

KEY 28:19: 'Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:' At the start of Matthew, wise men came from the nations to worship Jesus. Earlier, Jesus sent the twelve only to the lost sheep of Israel. Now he sends his followers to all nations. Every country, every language, every people. Jesus’ followers are to make disciples, people who learn from him and follow him, and to baptize them in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost. That is how the good news reached you.

KEY 28:20: 'Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.' Matthew began with a baby named Emmanuel, God with us. And Matthew ends with Jesus saying, I am with you alway. The whole book is held between those two promises. Haggai’s people heard God say, I am with you. Now Jesus says it to all his followers, everywhere, every day, until the very end. Wherever you go, Jesus goes with you.

THREAD: Isaiah wrote, 'And the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.' And the psalm says, 'I will declare the decree: the LORD hath said unto me, Thou art my Son'.

TODAY: The women ran with fear and great joy to tell the news. Who could you tell about Jesus? You do not have to go far away. Start where you are. And remember the last words of Matthew today. Whatever you face, Jesus says, I am with you alway.

QUESTION: Matthew begins with Emmanuel, God with us, and ends with Jesus saying, I am with you alway. How does it change your day to know that Jesus is with you?

PRAYER: Lord Jesus, thank you that you are risen, just as you said. Thank you that all power in heaven and earth is yours. Help me to follow you, and to share your good news with others. And thank you for your promise, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

NEXT: That is the end of our walk through the Gospel of Matthew. Well walked. You have finished another whole book of the Bible, and now you have walked through all four Gospels. Next on the walk, the book of Proverbs, one chapter at a time: wise words about friends, words, work and money, for every day of life.
