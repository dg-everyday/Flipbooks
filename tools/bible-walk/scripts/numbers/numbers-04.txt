# Numbers 4: the Levites from thirty to fifty years old, the Kohathites' burden, Aaron and his sons cover the ark with the vail, badgers' skins and blue cloth, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the Kohathites carry but must not touch or look, cut not off the Kohathites, Gershon's curtains, Merari's boards, the totals, every one according to his service. Keys 4:5, 4:19, 4:49.
TITLE: Every One According to His Service

HOOK: The cloud lifts from the tabernacle. It is time to move. And before anyone else touches it, the priests go into the most holy place, and carefully wrap the ark of God in layers of cloth, the veil, then skins, then a cloth of deep blue. Only when every holy thing is covered may the carriers come in and lift it onto their shoulders.

WHERE: In chapter three, God set apart the tribe of Levi in place of all the firstborn of Israel, saying, all the firstborn are mine, the Levites shall be mine. The three Levite families, Gershon, Kohath and Merari, each camped on one side of the tabernacle, with Moses and Aaron at the entrance. And every firstborn was counted, or redeemed with a price.

Now, in chapter four, God tells the Levites exactly how to carry the tabernacle on the journey. Listen for how carefully the holy things are handled. And listen for how each family has its own burden.

STORY: The LORD tells Moses and Aaron to count the men of the family of Kohath who are between thirty and fifty years old. These are the men who will do the work of carrying the tabernacle.

The Kohathites have the most holy burden. They carry the most holy things. But first, the priests must prepare them.

Aaron and his sons cover the table of shewbread with a blue cloth, and put the dishes and bowls on it, and the bread, and cover it with a scarlet cloth and with skins. They cover the lampstand, with its lamps and tools and oil, in a blue cloth and skins. They cover the golden altar in the same way. They clear the ashes from the great altar, and spread a purple cloth over it, and cover it, and all its tools.

Blue, scarlet, purple. Rich colours, for the holiest things. And every item wrapped and covered before it could be moved.

Only then do the sons of Kohath come in to carry them. But there is a warning.

Eleazar, Aaron’s son, is put in charge of the oil for the light, the sweet incense, the daily offering, and the anointing oil, and the oversight of the whole tabernacle.

Then God says something tender to Moses and Aaron. Do not let the family of the Kohathites be cut off from among the Levites.

Then the LORD tells Moses to count the family of Gershon, also between thirty and fifty. Their burden is the fabric of the tabernacle. The curtains, the coverings of skins, the hanging at the door, the hangings of the court, and all their cords. Ithamar, Aaron’s other son, oversees their work.

And then the family of Merari. Their burden is the heaviest of all. The boards of the tabernacle, the bars, the pillars, the sockets, the pins and the cords. And God says that each man is to be told by name what he is to carry. Ithamar oversees them too.

Think about how detailed this is. Not just, carry the tabernacle. But, you carry the curtains. You carry these boards. You carry this pin. Every man knew exactly what his burden was. No one had to carry everything, and no one was left with nothing.

Then Moses and Aaron count them all. The Kohathites, two thousand seven hundred and fifty. The Gershonites, two thousand six hundred and thirty. The Merarites, three thousand two hundred. More than eight thousand men, between thirty and fifty years old, to carry God’s house through the wilderness.

And the chapter ends with words of order and obedience.

Imagine the scene each time the cloud lifted. Thousands of Levites, working together, each knowing his job, taking down God’s house piece by piece, wrapping, lifting, carrying. And setting it all up again, perfectly, wherever the cloud stopped. It was hard, faithful, ordinary work, done for the glory of God.

KEY 4:5: 'And when the camp setteth forward, Aaron shall come, and his sons, and they shall take down the covering vail, and cover the ark of testimony with it:' The veil was the curtain that hung in front of the ark, separating the most holy place. When Israel travelled, that very curtain became the covering for the ark. God’s presence was holy, and had to be treated with great care, even on the road. The New Testament says that when Jesus died, the veil of the temple was torn in two, opening the way to God for all who come through him.

KEY 4:19: 'But thus do unto them, that they may live, and not die, when they approach unto the most holy things: Aaron and his sons shall go in, and appoint them every one to his service and to his burden:' That they may live, and not die. God gave these careful instructions not to make life hard, but to keep the Kohathites safe. God’s rules are often a form of protection. And notice, every one to his service and to his burden. Each person had a task suited to them.

KEY 4:49: 'According to the commandment of the LORD they were numbered by the hand of Moses, every one according to his service, and according to his burden: thus were they numbered of him, as the LORD commanded Moses.' Every one according to his service. God’s people are like a body, as Paul would later say, with many parts, each with its own work. Some carry the holy things, some carry the curtains, some carry the heavy boards. None is less important. All are needed for God’s house to move forward.

THREAD: Paul wrote, 'For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ.' And he wrote, 'Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.'

TODAY: What burden has God given you to carry? It may be a job, a family, a ministry, or a person who needs you. Carry it faithfully today, as service to God. And thank God for others who carry their part alongside you.

QUESTION: Every Levite knew exactly what he was to carry, and no one had to carry everything. How does it help to know that you do not have to carry everything yourself?

PRAYER: Lord God, thank you that you give each of your people a part to play. Help me to carry my burden faithfully, and not to try to carry what is not mine. Teach me to treat holy things with reverence. And thank you that through Jesus, the way into your presence is open. Amen.
