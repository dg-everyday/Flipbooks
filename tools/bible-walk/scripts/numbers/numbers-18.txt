# Numbers 18: Aaron and his sons bear the iniquity of the sanctuary, the Levites joined to them as a gift, that there be no wrath any more, the priest's office a service of gift, the priests' portions (the holy offerings, the best of oil, wine and wheat, the firstfruits; avoids 18:15 "matrix"), I am thy part and thine inheritance, the tithe to the Levites, the Levites tithe of the tithe from the best. Keys 18:6, 18:20, 18:31.
TITLE: I Am Thy Part and Thine Inheritance

HOOK: Everyone else in the nation is going to receive land. Fields, vineyards, olive groves, hills and valleys, passed down in families for generations. But one family is told, you will get none of it. Not a single field. And then God tells them why. Because they are getting something better.

WHERE: In chapter seventeen, God told each tribe leader to lay his rod before the LORD. The next day, Aaron’s rod had budded, blossomed and yielded almonds, and it was kept before the ark as a sign. But the people were terrified, crying, we perish, we all perish, anyone who comes near the tabernacle will die.

Now, in chapter eighteen, God answers their fear. He explains how the priests and Levites will guard the holy things, so the people can live safely near him. And he tells the priests what their inheritance will be.

STORY: The LORD speaks directly to Aaron. This is one of the few times in these books when God speaks to Aaron without Moses.

You and your sons, God says, will bear the responsibility for the sanctuary, and for the priesthood. Your brothers, the tribe of Levi, will join you and help you. They will look after the tabernacle. But only you and your sons may come near the altar, and the holy things inside the veil.

This was God’s answer to the people’s fear. The priests and Levites would stand guard around God’s holy dwelling. They would carry the responsibility. And so the people could live near a holy God, without fear.

God says that the Levites are a gift.

And the priesthood itself was a gift too. God says, I have given your priest’s office to you as a gift.

To serve God was not a burden. It was a gift. The priests and Levites had not earned it. God gave it to them, and he gave them to his people.

Then God tells Aaron how the priests will be provided for. Since they would have no land, they would live from the offerings the people brought to God.

The holy offerings, the parts not burned on the altar, would belong to the priests. The best of the oil, the best of the wine and grain, the firstfruits the people brought to the LORD. Everything set apart for God would be theirs. And God calls it a covenant of salt, an everlasting agreement, because salt was something that did not spoil.

Then God says the words that are at the heart of this chapter.

Every other tribe would have a portion of the land. Aaron’s portion was God himself.

Then God speaks about the rest of the Levites. I have given them every tenth part, the tithe, of everything Israel produces, as their inheritance, in return for their service at the tabernacle. They too would have no land of their own. They would live from the tithes the people gave.

But the Levites were to give too. God says, when you receive the tithe from the people, you must give a tenth of it to the LORD, a tithe of the tithe. And it must come from the best of what you receive. That portion would go to Aaron the priest.

Even those who received gifts were to give. No one in God’s people was only a taker. The people gave to the Levites, and the Levites gave to the priests, and everyone gave the best to God.

And God tells the Levites, you may eat the rest anywhere, you and your families, for it is your reward for your service at the tabernacle. And as long as you give the best of it to God, you will not bear any guilt.

Think about what this chapter teaches. The priests and Levites gave their lives to serving God and his people. And God made sure they were provided for, through the gifts of the people. Those who serve God are worthy of support. And those who are supported should give too.

KEY 18:6: 'And I, behold, I have taken your brethren the Levites from among the children of Israel: to you they are given as a gift for the LORD, to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.' A gift for the LORD. The Levites were God’s gift to the priests, to help them in their work. God provides people to help us in the work he gives us. Those who serve alongside us are a gift from him, and should be treasured.

KEY 18:20: 'And the LORD spake unto Aaron, Thou shalt have no inheritance in their land, neither shalt thou have any part among them: I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel.' I am thy part. This is one of the most beautiful promises in the Bible. Aaron gave up land, but he received God. Land can be lost, fields can fail, but God never fails. The psalmist would later say, the LORD is the portion of mine inheritance. All who belong to God can say the same.

KEY 18:31: 'And ye shall eat it in every place, ye and your households: for it is your reward for your service in the tabernacle of the congregation.' Your reward for your service. God saw the work of the Levites, and made sure it was rewarded. Jesus said, the labourer is worthy of his hire. God never forgets the service of his people.

THREAD: A psalm says, 'The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot.' And Paul wrote, 'Even so hath the Lord ordained that they which preach the gospel should live of the gospel.'

TODAY: Is God your portion, or are you looking for security in other things? Thank him today that he himself is your inheritance. And think of how you could support someone who serves God and his people.

QUESTION: God told Aaron, I am thy part and thine inheritance. What would it mean for you to see God himself as your greatest treasure?

PRAYER: Lord God, you are my portion and my inheritance. Thank you that you are better than any land or riches. Thank you for those you have given to help me in your work. Teach me to give my best to you, and to support those who serve your people. Amen.
