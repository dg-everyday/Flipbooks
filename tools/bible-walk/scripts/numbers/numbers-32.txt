# Numbers 32: Reuben and Gad with much cattle see Jazer and Gilead, bring us not over Jordan, shall your brethren go to war and shall ye sit here, the spies at Kadesh recalled, Caleb and Joshua wholly followed, the promise to go armed before their brethren, be sure your sin will find you out, the agreement told to Eleazar and Joshua, the cities built, half Manasseh in Gilead (fulfilled in Joshua 1, 4, 22). Keys 32:6, 32:23, 32:31.
TITLE: Be Sure Your Sin Will Find You Out

HOOK: Two tribes look out over green, rolling hills, perfect grazing land, and think about their huge herds of cattle. This is it, they say. This is where we want to live. Right here. We do not need to cross the river. But there is a problem. Their brothers are about to go into battle on the other side. And the two tribes are asking to stay behind.

WHERE: In chapter thirty one, Israel went to war against Midian. We learned that Balaam’s counsel had been behind the sin at Peor. Twelve thousand men went out, and the officers said, there lacketh not one man of us. And they brought gold to the tabernacle as a memorial before the LORD.

Now, in chapter thirty two, Israel is camped on the east side of the Jordan, and two tribes make a request. Listen for why Moses is worried. And listen for his famous warning.

STORY: The tribes of Reuben and Gad have very large herds of cattle. And they see the land of Jazer and the land of Gilead, east of the Jordan, the land Israel had taken from Sihon and Og. And it is good land for cattle.

So the leaders of Reuben and Gad come to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the leaders of the congregation. This land is perfect for cattle, they say, and we have cattle. If we have found favour with you, let this land be given to us. Do not take us across the Jordan.

It was a reasonable request in some ways. The land was good, and they needed pasture. But there was a danger in it.

Moses answers sharply.

And Moses goes on. Why are you discouraging the people from going over into the land the LORD has given them? This is exactly what your fathers did, when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to see the land. They went up to the valley of Eshcol, saw the land, and then discouraged the people from going in. And the LORD was angry, and swore that none of them would see the land, except Caleb and Joshua, because they had wholly followed the LORD. And he made them wander in the wilderness, until that whole generation was gone.

And now, Moses says, here you are, rising up in your fathers’ place, a new brood of sinners, to make the LORD even angrier with Israel. If you turn away from following him, he will leave this people in the wilderness again, and you will have destroyed them all.

Moses had seen this happen before. Long ago, ten spies had discouraged the whole nation, and a generation was lost. He was terrified it would happen again, right at the edge of the promise.

But the leaders of Reuben and Gad come closer, and explain. We will build sheepfolds here for our cattle, and towns for our children. But we ourselves will arm for battle, and go ahead of the rest of Israel, until we have brought them into their land. Our children will stay in walled towns, safe from the people of the land. We will not come home until every Israelite has received his inheritance.

That changes things. They were not trying to avoid the battle. They were offering to go first.

And Moses agrees, on one condition. If you will do this, if you arm yourselves and go over the Jordan before the LORD, until he has driven out his enemies, then afterward you may come back, and be free of guilt before the LORD and before Israel. And this land will be yours.

And then Moses gives a warning.

The children of Gad and Reuben answer.

So Moses gives instructions to Eleazar the priest, and to Joshua, and to the leaders of the tribes. If Gad and Reuben cross over armed for battle, and help take the land, give them the land of Gilead. But if they do not, they must take their land with everyone else in Canaan.

Notice that Moses passed the agreement on to Joshua. Moses knew he would not be there to see it kept. Joshua would make sure it happened.

Then Moses gives the tribes of Gad and Reuben, and half the tribe of Manasseh, the kingdoms of Sihon and Og. And they build up the towns, and pens for their sheep.

And they kept their word. In the book of Joshua, we heard how the fighting men of Reuben, Gad and half Manasseh crossed the Jordan ahead of their brothers, and fought for years, and only went home when the whole land was at rest. And Joshua sent them home with a blessing.

KEY 32:6: 'And Moses said unto the children of Gad and to the children of Reuben, Shall your brethren go to war, and shall ye sit here?' Shall your brethren go to war, and shall ye sit here? It is a question for every member of God’s people. When others are carrying the load, it is not right to sit comfortably and watch. We belong to one another, and we should share each other’s struggles.

KEY 32:23: 'But if ye will not do so, behold, ye have sinned against the LORD: and be sure your sin will find you out.' Be sure your sin will find you out. These are some of the most famous words in the Bible. We may think a sin is hidden, or forgotten. But sin has a way of coming back to us. Its consequences catch up with us. Nothing is hidden from God. The way to be free is not to hide sin, but to confess it.

KEY 32:31: 'And the children of Gad and the children of Reuben answered, saying, As the LORD hath said unto thy servants, so will we do.' As the LORD hath said, so will we do. They promised, and they kept their promise. After the last chapter about vows, here are two tribes who made a solemn promise and kept it, even though it cost them years away from their families.

THREAD: Paul wrote, 'Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.' And John wrote, 'If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins'.

TODAY: Is there someone carrying a heavy load, while you sit comfortably? Ask God how you can help them today. And if there is a sin you have been hiding, remember that it will find you out. Bring it into the light, to God.

QUESTION: Moses said, be sure your sin will find you out. Why do you think hidden sin so often catches up with us?

PRAYER: Lord God, nothing is hidden from you. Forgive me for the sins I have tried to hide, and help me to bring them into the light. Keep me from sitting comfortably while others carry the load. Help me to keep my promises, and to stand with my brothers and sisters. Amen.
