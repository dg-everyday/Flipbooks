# Proverbs 4: hear ye children, I was my father's son (three generations of teaching), get wisdom get understanding, wisdom is the principal thing, she shall bring thee to honour, I have led thee in right paths, enter not into the path of the wicked (avoid it, pass not by it), they sleep not except they have done mischief, the path of the just as the shining light, the way of the wicked as darkness, attend to my words, keep thy heart, a froward mouth put away, let thine eyes look right on, ponder the path of thy feet, turn not to the right hand nor to the left. Keys 4:7, 4:18, 4:23.
TITLE: Keep Thy Heart with All Diligence

HOOK: A spring bubbles up out of the ground, high in the hills. From that one spring flows a stream, then a river, that waters fields and villages all the way down the valley. If someone poisons the spring, the whole river is poisoned. If the spring is kept clean, the whole valley is blessed. Proverbs says your heart is like that spring.

WHERE: In chapter three, the father told his son, trust in the LORD with all thine heart, and in all thy ways acknowledge him. He said, honour the LORD with thy substance, and withhold not good from them to whom it is due. And he said that wisdom’s ways are ways of pleasantness, and she is a tree of life.

Now, in chapter four, the father remembers his own father. Listen for how wisdom is passed down through the generations. And listen for what the father says is most important to guard.

STORY: The chapter begins with an invitation to all children. 'Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding. For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law.' Doctrine means teaching.

And then the father tells a little bit of his own story. 'For I was my father’s son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother.' When I was a young boy, my parents loved me dearly. 'He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words: keep my commandments, and live.'

If this is Solomon speaking, then his father was David. Think of it. David taught Solomon, and now Solomon passes the same wisdom on to his own son. Three generations, one chain of wisdom, handed down from father to son.

And what did his father say? 'Get wisdom, get understanding: forget it not'. Do not let go of her, and she will protect you. Love her, and she will guard you.

Then comes a famous verse.

Treasure wisdom, the father says, and she will lift you up. Hold her close, and she will bring you honour. She will put a beautiful garland on your head, and a crown of glory.

'Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the years of thy life shall be many. I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in right paths.' When you walk, your steps will not be hindered. When you run, you will not stumble. 'Take fast hold of instruction; let her not go: keep her; for she is thy life.'

Then the father gives a strong warning. 'Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men.' And he repeats himself four times, in different words. 'Avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away.' Do not just avoid doing what they do. Do not even go near. Take a different road altogether.

Why? Because wicked people cannot rest until they have done wrong. They feed on wickedness, and drink violence like wine.

And then comes one of the most beautiful pictures in Proverbs.

'The way of the wicked is as darkness: they know not at what they stumble.' They keep tripping over things in the dark, and do not even know what they are tripping over.

Then the father gathers everything together. 'My son, attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings.' Keep them in front of your eyes, and in the middle of your heart. 'For they are life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh.'

And then the most important command of all.

The rest of the chapter shows how a well kept heart shows itself in the rest of the body. Your mouth: put away twisted speech and lying lips. Your eyes: 'Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee.' Keep your eyes on where you are going, and do not let them wander after things that will pull you off course. Your feet: 'Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established.' Think about where you are going. 'Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil.'

Heart, mouth, eyes and feet. A whole person, kept on the path of wisdom.

KEY 4:7: 'Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.' Principal means first, most important. Of all the things you could try to get in life, money, fame, success, the most important is wisdom. And with all thy getting means, whatever it costs you, get understanding. Wisdom is worth more than anything else you could gain.

KEY 4:18: 'But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day.' The just are those who walk rightly with God. Their path is like the first light of a new day, which grows brighter and brighter, until the sun is fully up. Following God does not mean we understand everything at once. But as we keep walking with him, the way gets clearer and brighter. And one day, we will see him face to face, in the full light.

KEY 4:23: 'Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.' Keep means guard, protect. With all diligence means with all your effort. The issues of life means everything that flows out of you, your words, your choices, your actions. Like a spring that feeds a river, your heart is the source of your whole life. What you let into your heart will flow out of it. So guard your heart more carefully than anything else.

THREAD: Jesus said, 'But those things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart'. And he said, 'I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.'

TODAY: Think about what you let into your heart through your eyes and ears: what you watch, read and listen to. Is it keeping your heart clean, or polluting it? And think of someone younger than you. What wisdom could you pass on to them?

QUESTION: Proverbs says, keep thy heart with all diligence, for out of it are the issues of life. What helps you to guard your heart, and what tends to pollute it?

PRAYER: Lord God, thank you for those who passed wisdom on to me. Help me to get wisdom, more than anything else. Guard my heart, so that what flows from it is good. Keep my eyes looking straight ahead, and my feet on the path of the just, shining brighter and brighter. Amen.
