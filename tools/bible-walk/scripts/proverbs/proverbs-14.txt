# Proverbs 14: every wise woman buildeth her house, where no oxen are the crib is clean, a faithful witness, go from the presence of a foolish man, fools make a mock at sin, the heart knoweth his own bitterness, there is a way which seemeth right, even in laughter the heart is sorrowful, the simple believeth every word, he that is soon angry, the poor hated even of his neighbour, in all labour there is profit, in the fear of the LORD is strong confidence, a fountain of life, slow to wrath, envy the rottenness of the bones, he that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker, righteousness exalteth a nation. Keys 14:12, 14:26, 14:31.
TITLE: There Is a Way Which Seemeth Right

HOOK: A traveller comes to a fork in the road. One path is wide and smooth, and looks as though it goes straight to the town he wants to reach. The other is narrow and rough. He takes the easy one, of course. It seems right. But after many miles, the smooth path ends at the edge of a cliff. If only he had asked someone who knew the way.

WHERE: In chapter thirteen, we heard that he that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life. Hope deferred maketh the heart sick, but when the desire comes, it is a tree of life. And he that walketh with wise men shall be wise, but a companion of fools shall be destroyed.

Now, in chapter fourteen, Solomon’s sayings go on. Listen for a warning about ways that only seem right. And listen for what God thinks about how we treat the poor.

STORY: The chapter begins with a picture of a home. 'Every wise woman buildeth her house: but the foolish plucketh it down with her hands.' A wise person builds up their family, with kindness, care and good choices. A foolish person tears it down, with their own hands, by selfishness and careless words.

Then a saying from the farm. 'Where no oxen are, the crib is clean: but much increase is by the strength of the ox.' The crib is the feeding trough. If you have no oxen, your barn stays clean and tidy. But you will not get much of a harvest either. An ox makes mess, but it also ploughs the fields. Good things often come with a bit of mess. A tidy life with no work, no animals, no children, and no friends, may be clean, but it is empty.

'A faithful witness will not lie'. 'Go from the presence of a foolish man, when thou perceivest not in him the lips of knowledge.' If someone only talks foolishness, walk away.

'Fools make a mock at sin'. Foolish people laugh about doing wrong, and treat it as a joke. But sin is never funny to God.

'The heart knoweth his own bitterness; and a stranger doth not intermeddle with his joy.' No one else fully knows the sorrows we carry, or the joys we feel. Only God knows our hearts completely.

Then comes a very serious saying.

And then, 'Even in laughter the heart is sorrowful; and the end of that mirth is heaviness.' Some people laugh on the outside while hurting on the inside. Not every smiling face is a happy heart.

'The simple believeth every word: but the prudent man looketh well to his going.' Simple people believe everything they hear. Wise people check where they are going. That is good advice today, when so many voices tell us what to believe.

'A wise man feareth, and departeth from evil: but the fool rageth, and is confident.' Wise people are careful, and stay away from danger. Fools rush in, angry and overconfident.

'He that is soon angry dealeth foolishly'. Quick anger leads to foolish actions.

Then a sad observation. 'The poor is hated even of his own neighbour: but the rich hath many friends.' People often want to be friends with the rich, and avoid the poor. Proverbs is honest about how people behave. And then it tells us how God sees it. 'He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he.'

'In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.' Penury means poverty. Hard work brings reward. Just talking about what you will do brings nothing.

Then comes a beautiful promise about the fear of the LORD.

'The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.'

'He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding'. Patience is a sign of great wisdom.

'A sound heart is the life of the flesh: but envy the rottenness of the bones.' A peaceful heart brings health to the whole body. But envy, wanting what others have, eats us up from the inside, like rot in our bones.

Then a saying about the poor that goes straight to the heart of God.

And near the end of the chapter, a famous saying about nations. 'Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.' A country is lifted up when its people do what is right. Sin brings shame on any nation, however powerful.

KEY 14:12: 'There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.' This saying is so important that it appears twice in Proverbs. Some paths look right to us. They make sense. Everyone else is taking them. But they lead to death. Our own feelings and opinions are not a safe guide. We need God’s Word to show us which way is truly right. Jesus said, I am the way. He is the path that leads to life.

KEY 14:26: 'In the fear of the LORD is strong confidence: and his children shall have a place of refuge.' Strong confidence means firm trust and security. People who honour God have a deep confidence that does not depend on circumstances. And their children have a place of refuge, a safe place. When parents fear the LORD, they give their children something more precious than money: a safe place, and a God to run to.

KEY 14:31: 'He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker: but he that honoureth him hath mercy on the poor.' To oppress means to treat harshly, to take advantage of. To reproach means to insult. When someone mistreats a poor person, they are insulting God, who made that person. But when we show kindness to the poor, we honour God. Every person, rich or poor, is made by God. How we treat them shows what we think of him.

THREAD: Jesus said, 'I am the way, the truth, and the life'. And he said, 'Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.'

TODAY: Is there a decision you are making simply because it seems right to you? Check it against God’s Word, and ask a wise friend. And look for a way to show kindness to someone who is poor or overlooked this week.

QUESTION: Proverbs says, there is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death. How can you tell the difference between what seems right and what is truly right?

PRAYER: Lord God, my own feelings are not always a safe guide. Show me the way that is truly right, not just the way that seems right. Thank you that in the fear of the LORD there is strong confidence. Help me to honour you by being kind to the poor. Give me a sound heart, free from envy. Amen.
