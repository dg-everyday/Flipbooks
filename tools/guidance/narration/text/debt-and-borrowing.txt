Today’s Question. Is it wrong to be in debt?

Borrowing is not a sin, but debt is a kind of servitude. Count the cost, pay what you owe, and let love be the only debt you keep.

The Bible does not forbid borrowing, but it is honest about what debt does: 'the borrower is servant to the lender'. Jesus told people to count the cost before they build, and to give back what is owed. Paul sums it up: owe nothing except love. And where others are in debt to us, the Law asks for mercy, not profit from the poor.

The Bible says, in Luke chapter 14, verse 28: For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?

And in Proverbs chapter 22, verse 7: The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender.

And in Romans chapter 13, verse 8: Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.

Here is one thing to try. Write down everything you owe, and make a plan to pay back one debt, however small.

Let us pray. Lord, keep me from being a slave to debt. Give me wisdom with money, and a heart that owes nothing but love. Amen.
