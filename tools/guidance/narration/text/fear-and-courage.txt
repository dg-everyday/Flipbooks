Today’s Question. How can I be brave when I'm afraid?

Courage in the Bible is not the absence of fear. It is trusting that God is with you in it.

'Fear not' is one of the most repeated commands in Scripture, and it almost always comes with a reason: 'for I am with thee'. God does not ask his people to feel strong. He asks them to remember who goes with them, and to take the next step.

The Bible says, in John chapter 14, verse 27: Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

And in Psalm 27, verse 1: The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the Lord is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?

And in Second Timothy chapter 1, verse 7: For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

Here is one thing to try. Name the fear out loud, then say who is with you in it. Take one small step anyway.

Let us pray. Lord, I am afraid. Remind me that you are with me, and give me courage for the next step. Amen.
