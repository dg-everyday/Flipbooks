Today’s Question. What makes a true friend?

A true friend loves at all times, tells the truth, and sticks closer than a brother. Choose friends who make you better.

Proverbs is full of friendship: loyal in hard times, honest enough to sharpen you, and better than going it alone. Jonathan and David show a friendship that put the other first. Jesus calls his disciples friends, and shows the greatest love a friend can give.

The Bible says, in John chapter 15, verses 13 to 15: Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends. Ye are my friends, if ye do whatsoever I command you. Henceforth I call you not servants; for the servant knoweth not what his lord doeth: but I have called you friends; for all things that I have heard of my Father I have made known unto you.

And in Proverbs chapter 17, verse 17: A friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity.

And in First Corinthians chapter 15, verse 33: Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners.

Here is one thing to try. Reach out to a friend who is going through something hard, and just be there.

Let us pray. Thank you, Jesus, for calling me your friend. Help me to be a faithful friend to others. Amen.
