Today’s Question. How much should I give?

Give freely, cheerfully and in proportion to what you have. God loves a cheerful giver, and no one out-gives him.

Jesus praised a widow who gave two small coins, because she gave all she had. The Law told Israel to open their hands wide to the poor and to bring the tithe. Paul says each should give as they have decided in their heart, not reluctantly, for God loves a cheerful giver.

The Bible says, in Luke chapter 6, verse 38: Give, and it shall be given unto you; good measure, pressed down, and shaken together, and running over, shall men give into your bosom. For with the same measure that ye mete withal it shall be measured to you again.

And in Second Corinthians chapter 9, verse 7: Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

And in Proverbs chapter 19, verse 17: He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the Lord; and that which he hath given will he pay him again.

Here is one thing to try. Give something away this week, quietly, to someone who cannot pay you back.

Let us pray. Generous God, you gave your Son for me. Make me a cheerful giver. Amen.
