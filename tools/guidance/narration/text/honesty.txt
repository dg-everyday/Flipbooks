Today’s Question. Is a small lie really a big deal?

Yes. God hates a false balance. Faithfulness in small things shows who we are in big ones.

The Law demanded honest weights and measures in the market. Proverbs says the one who walks in integrity walks securely. Jesus said that faithfulness in very little shows faithfulness in much, and the apostles tell believers to put away lying and speak the truth to one another.

The Bible says, in Luke chapter 16, verse 10: He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much.

And in Proverbs chapter 11, verse 1: A false balance is abomination to the Lord: but a just weight is his delight.

And in Ephesians chapter 4, verse 25: Wherefore putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour: for we are members one of another.

Here is one thing to try. If you have shaded the truth somewhere, go and set it straight.

Let us pray. Lord of truth, make me honest in small things and large, when I am seen and when I am not. Amen.
