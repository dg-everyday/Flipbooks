Today’s Question. What does God expect me to do about injustice?

Do justly, love mercy, and speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves. Serving the least is serving Christ.

The prophets thundered against worship that ignored the poor, the widow and the orphan. The Law built care for the poor into farming itself. Jesus said that whatever we do for the least of his brothers, we do for him, and James calls caring for widows and orphans pure religion.

The Bible says, in Micah chapter 6, verse 8: He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?

And in James chapter 1, verse 27: Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.

And in Proverbs chapter 31, verses 8 and 9: Open thy mouth for the dumb in the cause of all such as are appointed to destruction. Open thy mouth, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy.

Here is one thing to try. Find one person or cause near you that speaks for the voiceless, and give your time or help.

Let us pray. Lord, open my eyes to the people I pass by. Teach me to do justly and love mercy. Amen.
