Today’s Question. What if I feel completely alone?

God has promised never to leave you or forsake you. He sets the lonely in families.

Moses told Israel, and later Joshua, that God would never fail or forsake them. David wrote that even if father and mother abandon us, the LORD takes us in. Paul, deserted by everyone at his trial, wrote that the Lord stood with him. And Jesus' last words in Matthew are a promise: I am with you always.

The Bible says, in Matthew chapter 28, verse 20: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

And in Psalm 27, verse 10: When my father and my mother forsake me, then the Lord will take me up.

And in Second Timothy chapter 4, verses 16 and 17: At my first answer no man stood with me, but all men forsook me: I pray God that it may not be laid to their charge. Notwithstanding the Lord stood with me, and strengthened me; that by me the preaching might be fully known, and that all the Gentiles might hear: and I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.

Here is one thing to try. Reach out to one person today, even with a short message. Someone else may be lonely too.

Let us pray. Lord, you are with me even here. Set me in a family of your people. Amen.
