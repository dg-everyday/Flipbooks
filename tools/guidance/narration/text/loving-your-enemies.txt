Today’s Question. How do I treat people who are against me?

Don't repay evil with evil. Bless, pray for and do good to those who hate you, and overcome evil with good.

Jesus' command to love enemies was not new to the Law, which told Israel to return an enemy's straying ox. But he made it the mark of God's children. The apostles show how: don't take revenge, feed your enemy when he is hungry, and answer insults with blessing.

The Bible says, in Matthew chapter 5, verses 43 and 44: Ye have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate thine enemy. But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute you;

And in Romans chapter 12, verses 20 and 21: Therefore if thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink: for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head. Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.

And in Proverbs chapter 25, verse 21: If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he be thirsty, give him water to drink:

Here is one thing to try. Pray by name for someone who has hurt you, and ask God to bless them.

Let us pray. Lord Jesus, you prayed for your enemies. Soften my heart toward the people who are against me. Amen.
