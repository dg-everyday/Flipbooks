Today’s Question. How should I think about money?

Money is a good servant and a terrible master. You cannot serve God and money.

Jesus spoke about money more than almost anything else, because it so easily takes God's place. Store up treasure in heaven, he said, because your heart follows your treasure. Paul warns that the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil, and tells the rich to be generous and hope in God, not in wealth.

The Bible says, in Matthew chapter 6, verses 19 to 21: Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth, where moth and rust doth corrupt, and where thieves break through and steal: But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt, and where thieves do not break through nor steal: For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.

And in First Timothy chapter 6, verse 10: For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

And in Proverbs chapter 30, verses 8 and 9: Remove far from me vanity and lies: give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me: Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the Lord? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain.

Here is one thing to try. Look at where your money went last month. What does it say your heart treasures?

Let us pray. Lord, everything I have is yours. Keep me from loving money, and make me generous. Amen.
