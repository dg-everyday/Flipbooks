Today’s Question. How should I pray?

Simply, honestly and often. Ask, seek and knock; God is near to all who call on him.

Jesus taught his disciples to pray in secret, not for show, and to keep asking without giving up. He promised that those who ask receive. The apostles say to pray without ceasing and in everything give thanks. The prophets and psalms promise that God is near and answers when we call.

The Bible says, in Matthew chapter 6, verse 6: But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly.

And in First Thessalonians chapter 5, verses 17 and 18: Pray without ceasing. In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.

And in Psalm 145, verse 18: The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him, to all that call upon him in truth.

Here is one thing to try. Pray the Lord's Prayer slowly (Matthew chapter 6, verses 9 to 13), stopping on each line to make it your own.

Let us pray. Lord, teach me to pray. Amen.
