Today’s Question. How do I stay humble?

Pride puts self at the centre. Humility is not thinking less of yourself, but thinking of others and of God first.

Scripture turns the world's ladder upside down: the one who lifts himself up is brought down, and the one who humbles himself is lifted up. Humility is walking with God and counting other people's needs as important as your own.

The Bible says, in Luke chapter 14, verse 11: For whosoever exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.

And in Proverbs chapter 16, verse 18: Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.

And in Philippians chapter 2, verses 3 and 4: Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves. Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.

Here is one thing to try. Today, quietly do something for someone that no one will thank you for.

Let us pray. Lord, keep me low before you. Teach me to walk humbly and to put others first. Amen.
