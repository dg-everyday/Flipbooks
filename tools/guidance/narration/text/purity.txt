Today’s Question. Does God care what I think about and do with my body?

Yes. Purity starts in the heart and the thought life, and the body is a temple of the Holy Spirit.

Jesus blessed the pure in heart, because they will see God. The Bible treats the body as precious, bought at a price, and asks us to honour God with it. The way to a clean life is to fill the mind with what is true, lovely and good, and to keep to God's word.

The Bible says, in Matthew chapter 5, verse 8: Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God.

And in Psalm 119, verse 9: Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.

And in First Corinthians chapter 6, verses 19 and 20: What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God’s.

Here is one thing to try. Notice what you feed your eyes and mind on this week, and swap one thing for something good.

Let us pray. Create in me a clean heart, O God. Help me to honour you with my mind and body. Amen.
