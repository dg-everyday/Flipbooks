Today’s Question. Why should I read the Bible?

Because it is God speaking. His word feeds the soul, lights the path and lasts for ever.

Jesus met temptation with 'It is written', and said the Scriptures point to him. Joshua was told to think on God's law day and night, and Jeremiah found God's words the joy of his heart. The apostles say that 'All scripture is given by inspiration of God', and that it is meant to change how we live: be doers of the word, not hearers only. Reading the Bible is how we come to know the God who speaks in it.

The Bible says, in Luke chapter 11, verse 28: But he said, Yea rather, blessed are they that hear the word of God, and keep it.

And in Jeremiah chapter 15, verse 16: Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart: for I am called by thy name, O Lord God of hosts.

And in Second Timothy chapter 3, verses 16 and 17: All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works.

Here is one thing to try. Pick one Gospel and read a short passage each day this week. Before you close it, ask: what does this show me about Jesus?

Let us pray. Lord, open my eyes to see wonderful things in your word, and help me to do what it says. Amen.
