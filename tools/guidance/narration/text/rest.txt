Today’s Question. Is it all right to stop?

Yes. God rested, commanded rest, and invites the weary to come to him. Rest is a gift, not a weakness.

God rested on the seventh day and made the sabbath a gift for people who would otherwise never stop. Jesus took his tired disciples away to rest, and invited everyone who labours and is heavy laden to come to him. Stillness is where we learn again that he is God and we are not.

The Bible says, in Genesis chapter 2, verses 2 and 3: And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made. And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made.

And in Psalm 46, verse 10: Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.

And in Isaiah chapter 30, verse 15: For thus saith the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel; In returning and rest shall ye be saved; in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength: and ye would not.

Here is one thing to try. Set aside one unhurried hour this week with no screen, and spend some of it with God.

Let us pray. Lord, I am tired. I come to you for rest. Teach me to be still and know that you are God. Amen.
