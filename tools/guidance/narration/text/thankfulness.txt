Today’s Question. How can I be thankful when life is hard?

Give thanks in everything, not for everything. Thankfulness remembers what God has done and trusts him for what he will do.

Ten lepers were healed and only one came back to say thank you, and Jesus noticed. Jesus himself gave thanks at Lazarus' grave before he called him out. David told his own soul to forget not all God's benefits. Habakkuk chose to rejoice when the fig tree did not blossom, and Daniel gave thanks three times a day even when prayer had been forbidden. Paul writes, 'In every thing give thanks', because thankfulness is not a feeling but a choice to remember God.

The Bible says, in Luke chapter 17, verses 17 and 18: And Jesus answering said, Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine? There are not found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger.

And in Psalm 103, verses 1 and 2: Bless the Lord, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless his holy name. Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:

And in First Thessalonians chapter 5, verse 18: In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.

Here is one thing to try. Each day this week, write down three things you can thank God for, even on a hard day.

Let us pray. Lord, you are good, and your mercy endures for ever. Teach me to give thanks in everything. Amen.
