Today’s Question. What do I do when I can't stop worrying?

Worry pulls tomorrow's troubles into today. Scripture's answer is not to stop caring, but to hand the care to God.

Jesus does not tell worried people to try harder. He points them to a Father who feeds the birds and knows what they need, and tells them to put his kingdom first and take one day at a time. The apostles turn that into a habit: bring every care to God in prayer, with thanks, and his peace will guard your heart and mind.

The Bible says, in Matthew chapter 6, verses 33 and 34: But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you. Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.

And in Philippians chapter 4, verses 6 and 7: Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

And in Psalm 55, verse 22: Cast thy burden upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

Here is one thing to try. When a worry comes, turn it into a one-line prayer, then name one thing you are thankful for.

Let us pray. Father, you know what I need before I ask. I give you what I am carrying today, and I trust you with tomorrow. Amen.
