R: 'And the sons of Shimon were, Amnon, and Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. And the sons of Ishi were, Zoheth, and Ben-zoheth.' Notice and the sons of Shimon were. A family. Notice Amnon, and Rinnah. Sons. Notice Ben-hanan, and Tilon. More sons. Notice and the sons of Ishi were. Another family. Notice Zoheth, and Ben-zoheth. Father and son names. Notice that Rinnah means joyful shout. Notice that Ben-hanan means son of the gracious one. Notice that joy and grace appear in names. Notice that grace produces joy. Notice that the joyful shout of praise fills the Psalms. Notice that families named their children for joy. Notice that joy is contagious. Notice that it passes through generations. Notice that grace gives lasting joy. Psalm 5 says, 'let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice: let them ever shout for joy.' Notice shouting for joy. Today, rejoice as a child of the gracious One. Grace produces joy.
S: The sons of Shimon were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-Hanan and Tilon, and the descendants of Ishi were Zoheth and Ben-Zoheth. Rinnah means joyful shout and Ben-Hanan means son of the gracious one, joining grace and joy in one family. Rejoice as a child of the gracious One, for let all those that put their trust in Thee rejoice, let them ever shout for joy.
P: Grace and joy. 1 Chronicles 4:20: 'Amnon, and Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon.' Joy from grace. Let them ever shout for joy. Trust Him with it today.

== 4:21
T: Them that wrought fine linen
R: 'The sons of Shelah the son of Judah were, Er the father of Lecah, and Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the families of the house of them that wrought fine linen, of the house of Ashbea.' Notice the sons of Shelah the son of Judah. Judah's surviving son. Notice were, Er the father of Lecah. A grandson named after his uncle. Notice and Laadah the father of Mareshah. A town founder. Notice and the families of the house of them that wrought fine linen. Weavers. Notice of the house of Ashbea. A guild. Notice that Shelah was the son Judah withheld from Tamar. Notice that his line is recorded too. Notice that he named a son Er after his dead brother. Notice that families remember the lost. Notice that families passed on skills. Notice that work became heritage. Notice that righteousness is woven daily. Revelation 19 says, 'the fine linen is the righteousness of saints.' Notice fine linen. Today, weave righteousness into daily life. Every thread matters.
S: The sons of Shelah son of Judah included Er the father of Lekah, Laadah the father of Mareshah and the clans of linen workers at Beth Ashbea. Families of skilled weavers made the fine linen used in worship, and Scripture uses fine linen as a picture of the saints' righteous living. Weave righteousness into daily life, for the fine linen is the righteousness of saints.
P: Weavers of fine linen. 1 Chronicles 4:21: 'the families of the house of them that wrought fine linen.' Righteousness woven daily. The righteousness of saints.

== 4:22
T: These are ancient things
R: 'And Jokim, and the men of Chozeba, and Joash, and Saraph, who had the dominion in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. And these are ancient things.' Notice and Jokim. A descendant. Notice and the men of Chozeba. A town. Notice and Joash, and Saraph. Leaders. Notice who had the dominion in Moab. Rulers abroad. Notice and Jashubi-lehem. Perhaps returned to Bethlehem. Notice and these are ancient things. Old records. Notice that the writer admits these are old records. Notice that he preserved them faithfully. Notice that he did not invent details. Notice that he respected sources. Notice that Scripture values history. Notice that returning to the house of bread is a picture of repentance. Notice that the prodigal returned to his father's bread. Notice that God welcomes returners. Notice that ancient things still teach. Notice that history is God's classroom. Romans 15 says, 'whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning.' Notice learning. Today, learn from ancient things. Return to the house of bread.
S: The list includes Jokim, the men of Kozeba, Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab, and Jashubi Lehem, and these records are from ancient times. The writer faithfully preserved old records, including a hint of returning to Bethlehem, the house of bread, like Naomi and the prodigal son. Learn from ancient things, for whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning.
P: Ancient records. 1 Chronicles 4:22: 'And these are ancient things.' History teaches. Written for our learning. Walk in His ways today. Let this shape your day.
