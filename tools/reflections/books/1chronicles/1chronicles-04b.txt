== 4:43
T: The rest of the Amalekites that were escaped
R: 'And they smote the rest of the Amalekites that were escaped, and dwelt there unto this day.' Notice and they smote the rest of the Amalekites. Battle. Notice that were escaped. Survivors from earlier wars. Notice and dwelt there unto this day. Settlement. Notice that the Amalekites had long attacked Israel. Notice that they struck the weak and weary in the wilderness. Notice that Saul failed to complete God's command against them. Notice that Haman was later called an Agagite. Notice that Amalek pictures persistent hostility. Notice that Scripture records this battle plainly. Notice that it must be dealt with thoroughly. Notice that half-measures let it return. Notice that God gives victory over spiritual enemies. Notice that the cross defeated the powers. Notice that we stand in Christ's victory. Notice that the chapter ends with settled homes. Ephesians 6 says, 'we wrestle not against flesh and blood.' Notice the real battle. Today, fight your battles in the Spirit, not the flesh. Deal thoroughly with persistent sin.
S: They killed the remaining Amalekites who had escaped and have lived there to this day. This old-covenant battle against Israel's persistent enemy is recorded plainly and is not a model for Christians, whose battle is spiritual, not against people. Fight your battles in the Spirit, for we wrestle not against flesh and blood.
