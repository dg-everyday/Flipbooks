Look to God and rest in His shadow, for he that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.
P: Under His shadow. 1 Chronicles 8:20: 'And Elienai, and Zilthai, and Eliel.' Looking and resting. Under the shadow of the Almighty. Take it to God in prayer.
