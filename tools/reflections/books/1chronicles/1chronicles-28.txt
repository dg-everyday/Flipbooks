== 28:21
T: Every willing skilful man
R: 'And, behold, the courses of the priests and the Levites, even they shall be with thee for all the service of the house of God: and there shall be with thee for all manner of workmanship every willing skilful man, for any manner of service: also the princes and all the people will be wholly at thy commandment.' Notice and, behold, the courses of the priests and the Levites. Prepared servants. Notice even they shall be with thee. Support. Notice for all the service of the house of God. Ministry. Notice and there shall be with thee. More help. Notice for all manner of workmanship. Skill. Notice every willing skilful man. Willing and able. Notice for any manner of service. Every task. Notice also the princes and all the people. Everyone. Notice will be wholly at thy commandment. Full support. Psalm 110 says, 'Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power.' Notice willingness. Today, offer God a willing heart and your skill. Willingness comes first.
S: David told Solomon that the divisions of priests and Levites were ready for all the work on the temple of God, that every willing person skilled in any craft would help him, and that the officials and all the people would obey his every command. Solomon would not build alone, for God had prepared willing and skilful helpers, and willingness came before skill. Offer God a willing heart and your skill, for Thy people shall be willing in the day of Thy power.
P: Willing and skilful. 1 Chronicles 28:21: 'every willing skilful man, for any manner of service.' Help provided. Thy people shall be willing. Let this shape your day.
