R: 'And ye are Christ's; and Christ is God's.' Notice and ye are Christ's. And you are of Christ. Notice and Christ is God's. And Christ is of God. Notice that we belong to Christ. Notice that Christ belongs to God. Notice that this is our identity. Notice that we are not our own. Notice that we are held securely. Notice that belonging gives peace. Notice that everything flows from God. Notice that we too belong to Christ. Notice that we rest in His ownership. Notice that we live for Him. Notice that we rejoice. Notice that we worship. Notice that Romans 14 declared this. Romans 14 says, 'whether we live therefore, or die, we are the Lord's.' Notice we are the Lord's. Today, rest in knowing you belong to Christ.
S: Paul says the believers belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God. This is our identity, for we are not our own but held securely in Him. Rest in it, for whether we live therefore, or die, we are the Lord's.
P: His. 1 Corinthians 3:23: 'And ye are Christ's; and Christ is God's.' The Lord's. Whether we live or die, we are the Lord's. Rest in Christ today. Let this shape your day.
