Notice that God listens for love. Notice that kindness speaks loudest. Notice that love is essential. Notice that we too speak with love. Notice that we check our hearts before we speak. Notice that we let love fill our words. Notice that we grow. Notice that 1 John 4 declared this. 1 John 4 says, 'He that loveth not knoweth not God; for God is love.' Notice God is love. Today, let love fill every word you speak.
