== 8:43
T: That all people of the earth may know thy name
R: 'Hear thou in heaven thy dwelling place, and do according to all that the stranger calleth to thee for: that all people of the earth may know thy name, to fear thee, as do thy people Israel.' Notice hear thou in heaven. Listening. Notice and do according to all that the stranger calleth. Answering foreigners. Notice that all people of the earth may know thy name. Global knowledge. Notice to fear thee. Reverence. Notice as do thy people Israel. Equal standing. Notice that the verse adds that they may know the house bears God's name. Recognition. Notice that Solomon asked God to answer foreigners fully. Notice that the nations are invited. Notice that the gospel opens this to all. Notice that missionary hope runs through the Old Testament. Habakkuk 2 says, 'For the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD.' Notice the filled earth. Today, pray for all nations to know God's name. His glory will fill the earth.
