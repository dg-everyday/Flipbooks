Notice unto the river of Egypt. The south. Notice before the LORD our God. In His presence. Notice seven days and seven days, even fourteen days. Extended joy. Notice that the feast lasted two weeks. Notice that people came from every border. Notice that joy filled the whole land. Notice that joy can be prolonged. Notice that heaven will be a great feast. Revelation 19 says, 'Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb.' Notice the coming feast. Today, celebrate before the LORD. Look forward to the great feast to come.

== 8:66

Notice joyful and glad of heart. Deep joy. Notice for all the goodness that the LORD had done. God's goodness. Notice for David his servant, and for Israel his people. Faithfulness. Notice that the people went home joyful. Notice that joy was rooted in God's goodness. Notice that God's goodness is the reason for joy. Notice that this is the high point of Solomon's reign.
