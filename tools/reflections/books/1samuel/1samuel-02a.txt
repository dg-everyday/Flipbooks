# 1 Samuel 2:1-18: Hannah's song and the sons of Eli

== 2:1
T: My heart rejoiceth in the LORD
R: 'And Hannah prayed, and said, My heart rejoiceth in the LORD, mine horn is exalted in the LORD: my mouth is enlarged over mine enemies; because I rejoice in thy salvation.' Notice and Hannah prayed. Her first prayer was tears; this one is joy. Notice my heart rejoiceth in the LORD. Joy in God Himself. Notice mine horn is exalted. Strength restored. Notice my mouth is enlarged over mine enemies. No longer silenced by mockery. Notice because I rejoice in thy salvation. The reason for joy. Notice that Hannah rejoiced in the LORD more than in the child. Notice that she had just given her son away, yet she sang. Notice that true joy rests in God, not in gifts. Luke 1 records Mary's song, 'My soul doth magnify the Lord, And my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour.' Notice that Mary echoed Hannah. Today, let your joy rest in God Himself. Rejoice in His salvation, even when you have given much away.
S: Hannah prayed that her heart rejoiced in the LORD, her strength was exalted, and she delighted in His salvation. Having just given her son away, she sang, rejoicing in God more than in the gift, a song Mary would later echo. Let your joy rest in God Himself, rejoicing in His salvation even when you have given much away.
P: Joy in God Himself. 1 Samuel 2:1: 'My heart rejoiceth in the LORD… because I rejoice in thy salvation.' Mary echoed this: my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour.

== 2:2
T: None holy as the LORD

Notice for there is none beside thee. No other God. Notice neither is there any rock like our God. Stable and secure. Notice that Hannah's praise centred on God's character. Notice that she praised who God is, not only what He gave. Notice that holiness means God is utterly unique and pure. Notice that a rock pictures a firm foundation. Isaiah 6 records the seraphim crying, 'Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts.' Notice that heaven worships God's holiness. Today, praise God for who He is. There is none like Him. Build your life on Him, the Rock who never moves.
S: Hannah declared that no one is holy like the LORD, there is none besides Him, and there is no rock like our God. Her praise centred on God's unique holiness and steadfast character, not only on His gifts. Praise God for who He is, building your life on Him, the Rock who never moves.
P: None holy as the LORD. 1 Samuel 2:2: 'neither is there any rock like our God.' Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts. Praise Him for who He is.

== 2:3
T: By him actions are weighed
R: 'Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.' Notice talk no more so exceeding proudly. A warning. Notice let not arrogancy come out of your mouth. Guarding speech. Notice for the LORD is a God of knowledge. He knows all. Notice and by him actions are weighed. He evaluates every deed. Notice that Hannah may have had Peninnah's taunts in mind. Notice that proud words do not escape God's notice. Notice that God measures motives as well as deeds. Notice that arrogance is foolish before an all-knowing God. Psalm 75 says, 'Lift not up your horn on high: speak not with a stiff neck.' Notice that pride is warned against. Today, guard your words from arrogance. God knows everything and weighs every action. Speak humbly before Him.
S: Hannah warned against proud and arrogant talk, for the LORD is a God of knowledge who weighs every action. Proud words, perhaps like Peninnah's taunts, do not escape God's notice, for He measures motives and deeds. Guard your words from arrogance, speaking humbly before the God who knows all.
P: Actions weighed. 1 Samuel 2:3: 'Talk no more so exceeding proudly… the LORD is a God of knowledge.' Lift not up your horn on high. Speak humbly.

== 2:4
T: Girded with strength
R: 'The bows of the mighty men are broken, and they that stumbled are girded with strength.' Notice the bows of the mighty men are broken. The strong brought low. Notice and they that stumbled. The weak. Notice are girded with strength. Empowered by God. Notice the great reversal in Hannah's song. Notice that God overturns human expectations. Notice that weapons of the proud fail. Notice that those who stumble are strengthened. Notice that God's kingdom reverses the world's order. Notice that Hannah, once mocked, now sang of this reversal. Psalm 18 says, 'It is God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect.' Notice that strength comes from God. Today, if you feel weak, take heart. God girds the stumbling with strength. And if you feel strong, beware; human might can be broken.
S: Hannah sang that the bows of the mighty are broken while those who stumbled are clothed with strength. God reverses human expectations, failing the weapons of the proud and strengthening the weak. If you feel weak, take heart, for it is God who girds you with strength, and if you feel strong, beware.
P: The great reversal. 1 Samuel 2:4: 'The bows of the mighty men are broken, and they that stumbled are girded with strength.' God girdeth me with strength.

== 2:5
T: The barren hath born seven
R: 'They that were full have hired out themselves for bread; and they that were hungry ceased: so that the barren hath born seven; and she that hath many children is waxed feeble.' Notice they that were full have hired out themselves. The rich become poor. Notice and they that were hungry ceased. The hungry satisfied. Notice so that the barren hath born seven. Full blessing. Notice and she that hath many children is waxed feeble. The proud humbled. Notice that Hannah's own story was in this verse. Notice that seven means completeness. Notice that God reverses fortunes. Luke 1 records Mary singing, 'He hath filled the hungry with good things; and the rich he hath sent empty away.' Notice the same reversal in Mary's song. Today, trust God's justice in reversing fortunes. Those who are empty now, He can fill. Those who are proudly full, He can humble.
S: Hannah sang that the full hire themselves out for bread, the hungry are satisfied, the barren bears seven and the mother of many grows weak. Her own story was in the song, as God reverses fortunes, a theme Mary echoed. Trust God's justice, for He fills the hungry with good things and sends the proud away empty.
P: Fortunes reversed. 1 Samuel 2:5: 'the barren hath born seven.' He hath filled the hungry with good things. God fills the empty and humbles the proud.

P: He bringeth up. 1 Samuel 2:6: 'he bringeth down to the grave, and bringeth up.' God holds life and death. I kill, and I make alive. Resurrection hope.
