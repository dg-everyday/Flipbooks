# 1 Samuel 2:19-36: Samuel grows, and judgment on Eli's house

== 2:19
T: A little coat
R: 'Moreover his mother made him a little coat, and brought it to him from year to year, when she came up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.' Notice moreover his mother made him a little coat. Hannah's handiwork. Notice and brought it to him from year to year. Each year a new one. Notice when she came up with her husband. On their visits. Notice to offer the yearly sacrifice. Worship and love together. Notice that Hannah's love did not end when she gave Samuel to God. Notice that she measured his growth each year by a larger coat. Notice that giving a child to God does not mean ceasing to care. Notice the tender picture of a mother's devotion. Proverbs 31 says of the virtuous woman, 'she is not afraid of the snow for her household: for all her household are clothed with scarlet.' Notice that a godly mother clothes her family. Today, express love in practical, faithful ways year after year. Surrender to God and tender care go together.
S: Each year Hannah made Samuel a little coat and brought it when she came with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice. Her love did not end when she gave him to God, as each larger coat marked his growth. Express love in practical, faithful ways year after year, for surrender to God and tender care go together.
P: A coat each year. 1 Samuel 2:19: 'his mother made him a little coat, and brought it to him from year to year.' Surrender and love together. A mother's faithful care.

== 2:22
T: Eli heard all that his sons did
R: 'Now Eli was very old, and heard all that his sons did unto all Israel; and how they lay with the women that assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.' Notice now Eli was very old. Advanced in years. Notice and heard all that his sons did. Reports reached him. Notice unto all Israel. Their sin was public. Notice and how they lay with the women. Sexual immorality. Notice at the door of the tabernacle. At the very place of worship. Notice that Eli's sons defiled God's house with immorality. Notice that their sins were known to everyone. Notice that Eli heard but did not act decisively. First Corinthians 6 says, 'Flee fornication.' Notice that God calls His people to sexual purity. Today, take purity seriously, especially in God's house and among those who lead. Flee immorality. Leaders' sins harm many.
S: Eli, now very old, heard everything his sons were doing to Israel, including their sexual immorality with the women who served at the tabernacle entrance. They defiled God's house publicly, and Eli heard but did not act decisively. Take purity seriously, especially among those who lead, and flee immorality.
P: Defiling God's house. 1 Samuel 2:22: 'Eli… heard all that his sons did.' Immorality at the tabernacle. Flee fornication. Leaders' sins harm many.

== 2:23

R: 'And he said unto them, Why do ye such things? for I hear of your evil dealings by all this people.' Notice and he said unto them. Eli confronted his sons. Notice why do ye such things? A question, not action. Notice for I hear of your evil dealings. Public reports. Notice by all this people. Everyone knew. Notice that Eli's rebuke was mild for such serious sin. Notice that he asked why but did not remove them from office. Notice that words without consequences change little. Notice that a father's indulgence enabled his sons' sin. Proverbs 13 says, 'He that spareth his rod hateth his son: but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes.' Notice that loving discipline is timely and firm. Today, if you have responsibility over others, do not settle for weak words when action is needed. Loving correction is firm. Address sin before it destroys.
S: Eli asked his sons why they did such evil things, since he heard about them from all the people. His rebuke was mild for such serious sin, and he did not remove them, for words without consequences change little. If you have responsibility over others, do not settle for weak words when action is needed, for love corrects firmly and in time.
P: Too mild. 1 Samuel 2:23: 'Why do ye such things? for I hear of your evil dealings.' Words without action. He that loveth his son chasteneth him betimes.

Pleading. Notice for it is no good report that I hear. Shame. Notice ye make the LORD's people to transgress. Leading others into sin. Notice that Eli recognised the wider damage of his sons' sin. Notice that they caused others to stumble. Notice that leaders' sins multiply as others follow. Notice that Eli understood but did not stop them. Notice how seriously Jesus treats causing others to stumble. Today, consider how your example affects others. Do not cause others to stumble. Your influence matters greatly to God.
S: Eli told his sons the report was not good, for they were making the LORD's people transgress. He recognised that their sin caused others to stumble but did not stop them. Consider how your example affects others, for Jesus treats causing others to stumble with great seriousness.
P: Making others stumble. 1 Samuel 2:24: 'Nay, my sons; for it is no good report that I hear.' Their sin spread to others. Whoso offendeth a little one… Your example matters.

== 2:25

R: 'If one man sin against another, the judge shall judge him: but if a man sin against the LORD, who shall intreat for him? Notwithstanding they hearkened not unto the voice of their father, because the LORD would slay them.' Notice if one man sin against another. Human disputes. Notice the judge shall judge him. Human courts. Notice but if a man sin against the LORD. Far more serious. Notice who shall intreat for him? Who can mediate? Notice notwithstanding they hearkened not. They refused to listen. Notice because the LORD would slay them. Hardened beyond repentance. Notice that sin against God needs a mediator. Notice that Eli's sons had rejected every warning. First John 2 says, 'we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.' Notice that Christ is the answer to Eli's question. Today, rejoice that you have an Advocate. Christ intercedes for sinners. But do not harden your heart against warnings.
S: Eli asked who could intercede for someone who sins against the LORD, but his sons would not listen, for the LORD intended to put them to death. Sin against God needs a mediator, and Eli's sons had rejected every warning until hardened. Rejoice that Jesus Christ is your Advocate with the Father, but do not harden your heart against warnings.
P: Who shall intercede? 1 Samuel 2:25: 'if a man sin against the LORD, who shall intreat for him?' We have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.

== 2:26
T: In favour with the LORD and with men
R: 'And the child Samuel grew on, and was in favour both with the LORD, and also with men.' Notice and the child Samuel grew on. Steady growth. Notice and was in favour. Approved. Notice both with the LORD. Pleasing God. Notice and also with men. Respected by people. Notice the contrast again with Eli's sons. Notice that Samuel grew while they decayed. Notice that Samuel points to Christ. Today, seek to grow in favour with God first, and then with people. Steady growth in godliness wins lasting respect. Let your life increase in Christlikeness.
S: The boy Samuel kept growing and was in favour with the LORD and with people. He grew while Eli's sons decayed, and the same description is later used of Jesus. Seek to grow in favour with God first and then with people, increasing in Christlikeness.
P: Growing in favour. 1 Samuel 2:26: 'the child Samuel grew on, and was in favour both with the LORD, and also with men.' Like Jesus, who increased in favour with God and man.

== 2:30

R: 'Wherefore the LORD God of Israel saith, I said indeed that thy house… should walk before me for ever: but now the LORD saith, Be it far from me; for them that honour me I will honour, and they that despise me shall be lightly esteemed.' Notice I said indeed that thy house… should walk before me for ever. A past promise. Notice but now the LORD saith, Be it far from me. A reversal. Notice for them that honour me I will honour. A great principle. Notice and they that despise me shall be lightly esteemed. The opposite. Notice that God's promises to Eli's house depended on faithfulness. Notice that honour is reciprocal with God. Notice that this principle runs through all Scripture. John 12 records Jesus saying, 'if any man serve me, him will my Father honour.' Notice that God honours those who serve Him. Today, honour God in your choices, words and worship. He sees and He rewards. Those who honour Him, He will honour.
S: God said He had promised that Eli's house would serve before Him forever, but now He declared that He honours those who honour Him and lightly esteems those who despise Him. His promise to Eli's house depended on faithfulness, for honour with God is reciprocal. Honour God in your choices, words and worship, for whoever serves Christ, the Father will honour.
P: Honour for honour. 1 Samuel 2:30: 'them that honour me I will honour, and they that despise me shall be lightly esteemed.' If any man serve me, him will my Father honour.
