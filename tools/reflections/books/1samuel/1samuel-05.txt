# 1 Samuel 5: Dagon fallen before the ark

== 5:1

Notice from Eben-ezer. The place of defeat. Notice unto Ashdod. A chief Philistine city. Notice that the ark seemed to be a captive. Notice that the Philistines thought they had conquered Israel's God. Notice that what looked like defeat was the start of God's own campaign. Notice that God was not captured; He went to fight on enemy ground. Notice that Israel had lost, but God had not. Notice that God needs no army to defend His honour. Isaiah 42 says, 'I am the LORD: that is my name: and my glory will I not give to another.' Notice that God guards His glory. Today, when God's cause seems defeated, wait. He is not captive. He defends His own name.
S: The Philistines captured the ark of God and carried it from Eben-ezer to Ashdod. They thought they had conquered Israel's God, but what looked like defeat was the start of God's own campaign on enemy ground. When God's cause seems defeated, wait, for He will not give His glory to another.
