# 1 Samuel 8: Make us a king

R: 'And it came to pass, when Samuel was old, that he made his sons judges over Israel.' Notice and it came to pass, when Samuel was old. Years had passed. Notice that he made his sons judges. Delegating. Notice over Israel. A serious responsibility. Notice that Samuel planned for succession. Notice that he chose his own sons. Notice that this echoed Eli's situation. Notice that godly parents cannot guarantee godly children. Notice that position does not create character. Notice that Samuel may have assumed too much. Notice that leadership must be earned by integrity, not inherited. Notice that God had not directed this appointment. First Timothy 3 says of leaders, 'not greedy of filthy lucre… one that ruleth well his own house.' Notice that character is the qualification. Today, choose leaders for character, not connections. And if you lead, plan succession prayerfully. Family ties do not guarantee faithfulness.
S: When Samuel grew old, he appointed his sons as judges over Israel. This echoed Eli's situation, for godly parents cannot guarantee godly children and position does not create character. Choose leaders for character rather than connections, for a leader must be not greedy of filthy lucre.
