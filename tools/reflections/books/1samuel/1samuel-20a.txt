Notice that this phrase would be repeated at their parting. Genesis 31 says, 'The LORD watch between me and thee, when we are absent one from another.' Notice God's watch over friends. Today, place God at the centre of your relationships. Let Him be between you and those you love.
S: Jonathan said that concerning all they had spoken, the LORD would be between them forever. He sealed their agreement with God as witness and guarantor, the true bond of their friendship. Place God at the centre of your relationships, for the LORD watches between us when we are absent from one another.
