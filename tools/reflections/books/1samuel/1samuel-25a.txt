R: 'So and more also do God unto the enemies of David.' David had sworn a rash oath that by morning he would leave no male alive in Nabal's household. Notice so and more also do God. A curse on himself. Notice unto the enemies of David. An oath. Notice that David's vow was rash and violent. Notice that he planned to punish the whole household. Notice that innocent servants would die for Nabal's sin. Notice that anger exaggerates punishment. Notice that David's oath echoed Saul's rash vows. Notice that even good men can be carried away by rage. Notice that he forgot his mercy toward Saul. Notice that the oath was not God's will. Notice that God was about to stop him. Proverbs 29 says, 'An angry man stirreth up strife, and a furious man aboundeth in transgression.' Notice the danger of fury. Today, do not make vows in anger. Rage punishes the innocent. Let God stop you before you act.
S: David had sworn that by morning he would leave no male alive in Nabal's household. His rash oath in anger would punish innocent servants for one man's sin, echoing Saul's rash vows. Do not make vows in anger, for a furious man abounds in transgression.
P: A rash vow. 1 Samuel 25:22: 'So and more also do God unto the enemies of David.' Rage punishes the innocent. A furious man aboundeth in transgression.
