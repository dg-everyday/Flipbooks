R: 'But Saul had given Michal his daughter… wife, to Phalti the son of Laish, which was of Gallim.' But Saul had given his daughter Michal, David's wife, to Phalti son of Laish from Gallim. Notice but Saul had given Michal his daughter. Taken away. Notice to Phalti the son of Laish. Another man. Notice which was of Gallim. A town. Notice that Saul took David's wife from him. Notice that he treated his daughter as property. Notice that this was another act of spite against David. Notice that Michal had no say. Notice that people were used as pawns in Saul's feud. Notice that Saul's envy harmed his own family again. Notice the sorrow of broken marriages. Notice that God sees those who are used and wronged. Malachi 2 says, 'let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth.' Notice God's concern for faithfulness in marriage. Today, never use people as pawns. Honour marriage and treat every person with dignity.
S: Saul had given his daughter Michal, David's wife, to Phalti son of Laish from Gallim. In spite against David, Saul treated his daughter as a pawn, harming his own family again. Never use people as pawns but honour marriage and every person's dignity, for let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth.
