Notice then said Saul unto his armourbearer. A desperate request. Notice that Saul feared capture and humiliation. Notice but his armourbearer would not. Refusal. Notice for he was sore afraid. Psalm 34 says, 'The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.' Notice that God is near. Today, if you are in despair, do not face it alone. Reach out to God and to people who can help. There is hope.
S: Saul asked his armour-bearer to kill him rather than be captured and abused, but the armour-bearer refused in fear, and Saul took his own life. Scripture records this deep tragedy with sorrow, not approval, for despair isolates and deceives while life is God's gift. If you are in despair, do not face it alone but reach out to God and to people who can help, for the LORD is near the brokenhearted.
P: A tragic end. 1 Samuel 31:4: 'his armourbearer would not; for he was sore afraid.' If you are in despair, reach out to God and to others. The LORD is nigh.
