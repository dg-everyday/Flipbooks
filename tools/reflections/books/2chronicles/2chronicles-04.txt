== 4:22
T: The doors of the house were of gold
R: 'And the snuffers, and the basons, and the spoons, and the censers, of pure gold: and the entry of the house, the inner doors thereof for the most holy place, and the doors of the house of the temple, were of gold.' Notice and the snuffers, and the basons. Tools. Notice and the spoons, and the censers. Incense vessels. Notice of pure gold. Precious. Notice and the entry of the house. The entrance. Notice the inner doors thereof for the most holy place. The inmost door. Notice and the doors of the house of the temple. Main doors. Notice were of gold. Glory. Notice that the chapter ends with doors. Notice that doors control access. Notice that the inner doors led to God's presence. Notice that salvation is entrance into God's presence. Notice that the golden doors pointed to Christ. John 10 says, 'I am the door.' Notice the door. Today, enter through Christ, the true door. The way to God is open.
S: Solomon made the pure gold wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, dishes and censers, and the gold doors of the temple, the inner doors to the Most Holy Place and the doors of the main hall. The chapter ends with golden doors that controlled access to God's presence, pointing to Christ who opens the way to all who believe. Enter through Christ, the true door, for He said, I am the door.
