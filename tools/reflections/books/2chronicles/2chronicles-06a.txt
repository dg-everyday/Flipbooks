Confess and receive God's forgiveness, for if we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins.
P: Hear and forgive. 2 Chronicles 6:21: 'hear thou from thy dwelling place, even from heaven; and when thou hearest, forgive.' Pardon. Faithful and just to forgive.
