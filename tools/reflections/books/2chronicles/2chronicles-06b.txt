The king. Notice remember the mercies of David thy servant. Covenant love. Notice that Solomon closed by appealing to God's mercies. Notice that he did not appeal to his own merit. Notice that he rested on God's covenant with David. Notice that the mercies of David were God's promises. Notice that God's steadfast love was the ground of hope. Notice that the anointed king needed God's face. Notice that Paul linked them to the resurrection. Notice that the true Anointed rose. Notice that God's face never turns from Him. Notice that in Him we are accepted. Notice that our hope rests on covenant mercy. Isaiah 55 says, 'even the sure mercies of David.' Notice sure mercies. Today, rest your hope on God's sure mercies in Christ. Grace, not merit, is your appeal.
S: Solomon closed by asking the LORD God not to reject His anointed one but to remember the great love promised to His servant David. He rested his whole prayer on God's covenant mercy rather than his own merit, mercies fulfilled in Christ, the risen Anointed One. Rest your hope on God's sure mercies in Christ, for He promised even the sure mercies of David.
