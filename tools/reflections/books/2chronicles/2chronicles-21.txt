One of Scripture's saddest epitaphs shows that power cannot buy love, and a life without God may leave no one missing it. Choose a life that will be missed, for a good name is rather to be chosen than great riches.
P: Not desired. 2 Chronicles 21:20: 'and departed without being desired.' A sad epitaph. A good name is rather to be chosen. Trust Him with it today.
