== 31:21
T: With all his heart, and prospered
R: 'And in every work that he began in the service of the house of God, and in the law, and in the commandments, to seek his God, he did it with all his heart, and prospered.' Notice and in every work that he began. Every project. Notice in the service of the house of God. Worship. Notice and in the law. Scripture. Notice and in the commandments. Obedience. Notice to seek his God. Purpose. Notice he did it with all his heart. Wholehearted. Notice and prospered. Blessing. Notice that every work was wholehearted. Notice that his purpose was seeking God. Notice that worship, Scripture and obedience were joined. Notice that prosperity followed devotion. Notice that seeking God is the centre. Notice that the next chapter will test it. Notice that trials follow faithfulness. Psalm 9 says, 'I will praise thee, O LORD, with my whole heart.' Notice the whole heart. Today, do every work for God with all your heart. Wholeheartedness prospers.
