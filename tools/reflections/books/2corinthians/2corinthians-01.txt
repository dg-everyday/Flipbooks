R: 'that to spare you I came not as yet unto Corinth.' Notice moreover I call God for a record upon my soul. I call God as my witness. Notice that to spare you I came not as yet unto Corinth. That it was in order to spare you that I did not return to Corinth. Notice that Paul delayed to spare them. Notice that his delay was love. Notice that he did not want to come harshly. Notice that he called God as witness. Notice that motives can be misunderstood. Notice that love sometimes waits. Notice that gentleness is wise. Notice that we too act with gentle motives. Notice that we wait when love requires. Notice that we explain ourselves honestly. Notice that we love. Notice that Psalm 103 declared this. Psalm 103 says, 'Like as a father pitieth his children' Notice pitieth his children. Today, let love guide your timing.
S: Paul calls God as witness that he delayed returning to Corinth in order to spare the believers. His delay was an act of love, not fickleness, for he did not want to come harshly. Let love guide you, Like as a father pitieth his children.
P: Spared. 2 Corinthians 1:23: 'that to spare you I came not as yet unto Corinth.' Pity. Like as a father pitieth his children. Let love guide today. Trust Him with it today.

== 1:24
T: Helpers of your joy
R: 'Not for that we have dominion over your faith, but are helpers of your joy: for by faith ye stand.' Notice not for that we have dominion over your faith. Not that we lord it over your faith. Notice but are helpers of your joy: for by faith ye stand. But we work with you for your joy, because it is by faith you stand firm. Notice that Paul did not control them. Notice that he worked for their joy. Notice that leaders serve. Notice that faith is personal. Notice that each stands by faith. Notice that joy is the aim. Notice that partnership replaces control. Notice that we too help others' joy. Notice that we do not control. Notice that we serve humbly. Notice that we rejoice. Notice that Nehemiah 8 declared this. Nehemiah 8 says, 'for the joy of the LORD is your strength.' Notice the joy of the LORD. Today, work for someone else's joy.
S: Paul says he does not lord it over the believers' faith but works with them for their joy, for by faith they stand firm. Leaders serve rather than control, and joy is the aim. Help others' joy, for the joy of the LORD is your strength.
P: Joy. 2 Corinthians 1:24: 'but are helpers of your joy.' Strength. The joy of the LORD is your strength. Help someone's joy today. Let this shape your day.
