T: The proof of your love
R: 'Wherefore shew ye to them' Notice wherefore shew ye to them. Therefore show these men. Notice that they should show the proof of their love before all the believers. The proof of your love and the reason for our pride in you, so that the believers can see it. Notice that love should be shown. Notice that it should be visible. Notice that others are watching. Notice that action proves love. Notice that welcome shows love. Notice that generosity shows love. Notice that witness grows. Notice that we too show our love. Notice that we welcome others. Notice that we live so love is seen. Notice that we love. Notice that John 13 declared this. John 13 says, 'By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples' Notice all men know. Today, show your love in a way others can see.
S: Paul asks the believers to show these men the proof of their love and the reason for his pride in them, so others can see it. Love must be visible in welcome and generosity. Show it, for By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples.
