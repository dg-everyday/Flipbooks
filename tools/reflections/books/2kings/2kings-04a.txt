R: 'And she called unto her husband, and said, Send me, I pray thee, one of the young men, and one of the asses, that I may run to the man of God, and come again.' Notice and she called unto her husband. Asking. Notice send me, I pray thee, one of the young men. A servant. Notice and one of the asses. Transport. Notice that I may run to the man of God. Urgency. Notice and come again. Return. Notice that she did not tell her husband the child had died. Notice that she was focused on getting help. Notice that she moved with urgency. Notice that faith expects an answer. Notice that she ran toward hope. Notice that her focus was remarkable. Notice that she did not stop to explain. Psalm 46 says, 'God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.' Notice present help. Today, run to God in your crisis. He is a very present help.
S: She called to her husband and asked for a servant and a donkey so she could hurry to the man of God and come back. Without even telling her husband of the death, she turned grief into urgent action, running toward God's help. Run to God in your crisis, for God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
P: Running for help. 2 Kings 4:22: 'that I may run to the man of God, and come again.' Grief turned to action. A very present help in trouble.
