R: 'And that bringeth me forth from mine enemies: thou also hast lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.' Notice and that bringeth me forth from mine enemies. Rescue. Notice thou also hast lifted me up on high. Exaltation. Notice above them that rose up against me. Over enemies. Notice thou hast delivered me from the violent man. Deliverance. Notice that God rescued and exalted David. Notice that the violent man may recall Saul. Notice that God lifted him above opposition. Notice that deliverance leads to exaltation. Notice that God raises the humble. Notice that David's life showed this pattern. Notice that persecution was followed by vindication. Notice that God lifts His servants in His time. First Peter 5 says, 'that he may exalt you in due time.' Notice God's exaltation. Today, wait for God to lift you up. He delivers and exalts in due time.
S: David sang that God brought him out from his enemies, lifted him above those who rose against him and rescued him from the violent man. God rescued and then exalted David above opposition, following persecution with vindication. Wait for God to lift you up, for He will exalt you in due time.
P: Lifted on high. 2 Samuel 22:49: 'thou also hast lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me.' He may exalt you in due time.

== 22:50
T: I will give thanks unto thee among the heathen
R: 'Therefore I will give thanks unto thee, O LORD, among the heathen, and I will sing praises unto thy name.' Notice therefore I will give thanks unto thee. Gratitude. Notice O LORD. Personal. Notice among the heathen. Among the nations. Notice and I will sing praises unto thy name. Worship. Notice that David's praise would reach the nations. Notice that gratitude becomes public witness. Notice that God's name would be known among the Gentiles. Notice that Paul quoted this verse about the gospel to the nations. Notice that worship is missionary. Notice that thanksgiving testifies. Notice that God's deeds deserve a world audience. Notice that praise is the response to rescue. Romans 15 quotes, 'For this cause I will confess to thee among the Gentiles, and sing unto thy name.' Notice the gospel to the nations. Today, give thanks to God publicly. Let your praise reach those who do not yet know Him.
S: David sang that he would give thanks to the LORD among the nations and sing praises to His name. His gratitude became a public witness to the Gentiles, a verse Paul quoted about the gospel reaching the nations. Give thanks to God publicly and let your praise reach those who do not yet know Him, for Paul said, I will confess to thee among the Gentiles.
P: Thanks among the nations. 2 Samuel 22:50: 'I will give thanks unto thee, O LORD, among the heathen.' Worship as witness. Let your praise reach the nations.

== 22:51
T: Mercy to his anointed
R: 'He is the tower of salvation for his king: and sheweth mercy to his anointed, unto David, and to his seed for evermore.' Notice he is the tower of salvation. Strong refuge. Notice for his king. David. Notice and sheweth mercy to his anointed. Covenant love. Notice unto David. Personal. Notice and to his seed for evermore. Eternal promise. Notice that the song ends with God's covenant mercy. Notice that God's love extends to David's descendants. Notice that for evermore points to Christ. Notice that Jesus is the Son of David and the Anointed One. Notice that God's mercy never ends. Notice that the song moves from personal rescue to eternal promise. Notice that God's tower of salvation stands forever. Notice that we share in this mercy through Christ. Luke 1 records Mary singing, 'his mercy is on them that fear him from generation to generation.' Notice eternal mercy. Today, rest in God's eternal mercy through Christ, David's Son. His salvation is a tower forever.
S: David sang that God is a tower of salvation for His king and shows mercy to His anointed, to David and his seed forever. The song ends with covenant mercy reaching David's descendants and fulfilled in Christ, the Son of David and the Anointed One. Rest in God's eternal mercy through Christ, for His mercy is on them that fear Him from generation to generation.
P: Mercy for evermore. 2 Samuel 22:51: 'sheweth mercy to his anointed, unto David, and to his seed for evermore.' Fulfilled in Christ. His mercy is from generation to generation.
