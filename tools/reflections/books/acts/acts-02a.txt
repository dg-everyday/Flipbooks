Notice that unity prepared them. Notice that God's promise was about to come. Notice that the waiting was ending. Notice that we too gather in unity. Notice that we wait on God together. Notice that He moves among united people. Notice that we stay together. Notice that we pray. Notice that Psalm 133 declared this. Psalm 133 says, 'for there the LORD commanded the blessing, even life for evermore.' Notice there the LORD commanded the blessing. Today, value unity with God's people as you wait on Him.

Stay united with God's people, for where brethren dwell together, there the LORD commanded the blessing, even life for evermore.
