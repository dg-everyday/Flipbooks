S: The man who was miraculously healed was over forty years old. He had been lame since birth for decades and no one expected change, yet time does not limit God. Keep hoping however long you wait, for the LORD asks whether there is any thing too hard for Him.
P: Over forty. Acts 4:22: 'the man was above forty years old.' Not too hard. Is there any thing too hard for me. Keep hoping today. Take it to God in prayer.
