# Amos 4: Prepare to meet thy God, O Israel

== 4:1
T: Which oppress the poor, which crush the needy
R: 'Hear this word, ye kine of Bashan, that are in the mountain of Samaria, which oppress the poor, which crush the needy' Notice hear this word, ye kine of Bashan, that are in the mountain of Samaria. Hear this word, you who live at ease on Mount Samaria, well fed like the cattle of Bashan. Notice which oppress the poor, which crush the needy. You who oppress the poor and crush the needy. Notice which say to their masters, Bring, and let us drink. And say to your husbands, Bring us some drinks! Notice that the wealthy are addressed. Notice that they live in comfort. Notice that their comfort is built on the poor. Notice that we must open our eyes. Notice that James warned the rich. James 5 says, 'Ye have lived in pleasure on the earth, and been wanton' Notice ye have lived in pleasure. Today, let your comfort make you generous, not blind, and if drinking has a hold on you, reach out for help.
S: Amos addressed those living at ease on Mount Samaria, well fed like the cattle of Bashan, who oppressed the poor, crushed the needy and called for more drinks. Their comfort was built on the poor, and indulgence blinded them to the needy, whom God sees. Let comfort make you generous and reach out for help if drinking has a hold on you, for James warned, ye have lived in pleasure on the earth, and been wanton.
P: Living at ease. Amos 4:1: 'which oppress the poor, which crush the needy.' Pleasure. Ye have lived in pleasure on the earth. Be generous today, and reach out for help if you need it. Take it to God in prayer.

When you will be taken away with hooks, the last of you with fishhooks. Notice that God swears by His holiness. Notice that there is nothing higher. Notice that His holiness guarantees His word. Notice that oppression offends His holiness. Notice that the comfortable will be led away. Notice that their ease will end. Notice that God's holiness demands justice. Notice that it is not indifferent to the poor. Notice that holiness and justice go together. Notice that God is holy in justice. Notice that Isaiah declared this. Isaiah 5 says, 'God that is holy shall be sanctified in righteousness.' Notice sanctified in righteousness. Today, honour God's holiness by doing justice.
S: The Sovereign LORD swore by His holiness that the time would surely come when the comfortable oppressors would be led away with hooks. God's holiness guarantees His word and demands justice for the poor. Honour it, for God that is holy shall be sanctified in righteousness.
P: Sworn by holiness. Amos 4:2: 'The Lord GOD hath sworn by his holiness.' Holy justice. God that is holy shall be sanctified in righteousness. Do justice today. Trust Him with it today.

== 4:4

Go to Gilgal and sin yet more. Notice and bring your sacrifices every morning, and your tithes after three years. Bring your sacrifices every morning, your tithes every three years. Notice that Amos speaks with irony. Notice that their worship is called sin. Notice that they are very religious. Notice that they sacrifice daily. Notice that they tithe faithfully. Notice that it is still sin. Notice that worship without justice is empty. Notice that God is not impressed by activity. Notice that He looks at the heart. Notice that He looks at how we treat others. Notice that Isaiah said the same. Isaiah 1 says, 'Bring no more vain oblations' Notice bring no more vain oblations. Today, let your worship be matched by justice and mercy.
S: Amos ironically invited the people to go to Bethel and Gilgal and sin more, bringing daily sacrifices and regular tithes. Their busy religion was called sin, for worship without justice is empty and God looks at the heart. Let your worship be matched by mercy, for God said, bring no more vain oblations.
P: Religious sin. Amos 4:4: 'Come to Bethel, and transgress.' Vain offerings. Bring no more vain oblations. Worship with justice today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 4:5

Notice for this liketh you, O ye children of Israel, saith the Lord GOD. For this is what you love to do, you Israelites, declares the Sovereign LORD. Notice that they announce their giving. Notice that they enjoy being seen. Notice that their worship pleases themselves. Notice that it is for show. Notice that God sees motives. Notice that true worship seeks God's praise. Notice that it is not for applause. Notice that the reward is from God. Notice that Jesus warned of this. Matthew 6 says, 'Take heed that ye do not your alms before men, to be seen of them' Notice to be seen of them. Today, give and worship for God's eyes, not for applause.
S: The people burned leavened bread as thank offerings and bragged about their freewill offerings, for this was what they loved to do. Their worship was for show and pleased themselves, but God sees motives. Give and worship for Him alone, for take heed that ye do not your alms before men, to be seen of them.
P: For show. Amos 4:5: 'for this liketh you.' Unseen. Take heed that ye do not your alms before men. Give quietly today. Trust Him with it today.

Notice one piece was rained upon, and the piece whereupon it rained not withered. One field had rain; another had none and dried up. Notice that the rain was selective. Notice that it was a sign. Notice that it falls on all. Matthew 5 says, 'and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust.' Notice sendeth rain. Today, thank God for daily provisions you rely on.
S: The LORD said He withheld rain before the harvest, sending it on one town and not another, so that one field was watered and another dried up. The selective rain was a sign that God gives and withholds even our basic needs. Thank Him for daily provision, for He sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust.
P: Withheld rain. Amos 4:7: 'I have withholden the rain from you.' Grace. Sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust. Thank God for provision today. Take it to God in prayer.
