# Amos 6: Woe to them that are at ease in Zion

== 6:1
T: Woe to them that are at ease in Zion
R: 'Woe to them that are at ease in Zion, and trust in the mountain of Samaria' Notice woe to them that are at ease in Zion. Woe to you who are complacent in Zion. Notice and trust in the mountain of Samaria. And to you who feel secure on Mount Samaria. Notice which are named chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel came! You notable men of the foremost nation, to whom the people of Israel come! Notice that complacency is warned. Notice that they feel secure. Notice that they trust their location. Notice that they are proud of their status. Notice that ease can make us careless. Notice that comfort can make us forget God. Notice that we should stay alert. Notice that we should trust God alone. Notice that Jesus warned of this. Luke 12 says, 'Soul, thou hast much goods laid up for many years; take thine ease' Notice take thine ease. Today, guard against complacency and keep trusting God.
S: Amos warned those who were complacent in Zion and felt secure on Mount Samaria, the notable leaders of a proud nation. Their ease made them careless, for security in anything but God is false. Guard against complacency, unlike the rich man who told himself, soul, thou hast much goods laid up for many years; take thine ease.
P: At ease. Amos 6:1: 'Woe to them that are at ease in Zion.' Complacency. Take thine ease. Stay alert to God today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 6:2
T: Pass ye unto Calneh, and see
R: 'Pass ye unto Calneh, and see; and from thence go ye to Hamath the great' Notice pass ye unto Calneh, and see. Go to Calneh and look at it. Notice and from thence go ye to Hamath the great: then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Go from there to great Hamath, and then go down to Gath in Philistia. Notice that the verse asks whether those kingdoms were better or larger than Israel. Are they better off than your two kingdoms? Is their land larger than yours? Notice that other great cities had fallen. Notice that Israel should learn from them. Notice that size did not save them. Notice that we can learn from others' falls. Notice that we are not immune. Notice that we should take warning. Notice that Paul said examples were written for us. Notice that they are for our learning. Romans 15 says, 'whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning' Notice written for our learning. Today, learn from history and stay humble.
S: Amos told Israel to look at Calneh, great Hamath and Gath, asking whether those fallen kingdoms were better or larger than Israel. Other great cities had fallen despite their size, and history teaches humility. Learn from it, for whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning.
P: Look and see. Amos 6:2: 'Pass ye unto Calneh, and see.' Learning. Written for our learning. Learn from history today. Trust Him with it today.

== 6:4

Notice that they live in luxury. Notice that they lounge. Notice that they eat the best. Notice that they ignore the suffering. Notice that luxury itself is not condemned alone. Notice that it is luxury without compassion. Notice that it is ease while others suffer. Notice that God sees the contrast. Notice that He wants generosity. Notice that He wants care for the needy. Notice that John urged this. 1 John 3 says, 'But whoso hath this world's good, and seeth his brother have need' Notice seeth his brother have need. Today, let what you have open your heart to those in need.
S: The comfortable lay on ivory-adorned beds, lounged on couches and dined on choice lambs and fattened calves. Their luxury was without compassion, ease while others suffered, and God sees the contrast. Let what you have open your heart, for whoso hath this world's good, and seeth his brother have need, should not shut up his compassion.
P: Beds of ivory. Amos 6:4: 'That lie upon beds of ivory.' Seeing need. Seeth his brother have need. Share what you have today. Take it to God in prayer.
