# Deuteronomy 3: Og of Bashan, the eastern tribes and Moses' prayer to cross

== 3:1

Israel kept moving forward after the first victory. Notice Og the king of Bashan. Og was famous, a giant king of a fortified land. A second battle followed close behind the first. Notice that one victory does not end all conflict. New challenges often come right after a triumph. Notice that Israel did not retreat. Having seen God defeat Sihon, they faced Og. Face it with the confidence the last one gave you.
