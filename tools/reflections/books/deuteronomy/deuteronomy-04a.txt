# Deuteronomy 4:1-24: Hearken and do; remember Horeb; make no graven image

== 4:1
T: Hearken, O Israel
R: 'Now therefore hearken, O Israel, unto the statutes and unto the judgments, which I teach you, for to do them, that ye may live…' Now, Israel, listen to the decrees and laws I am about to teach you. Follow them so that you may live and go in and take possession of the land. Notice now therefore. After the history of chapters 1 to 3, Moses turns to application. Remembering leads to responding. Notice hearken. Listen closely, with the intent to obey. Notice for to do them. God's word is given to be done, not merely admired. Notice that ye may live. Obedience is the path of life, not a burden that crushes it. Notice go in and possess. Blessing and obedience walk together. John 14 says, 'If ye love me, keep my commandments.' Today, let what God has done in your past move you to listen to Him now. Hear His word with a heart ready to obey, and find the life it brings.
S: Moses called Israel to hearken to the statutes and judgments he taught them so that they would do them and live. After recounting their history, he turned to application, linking obedience with life and possession of the land. Let what God has done in your past move you to hear His word with a heart ready to obey.
P: Hear and do. Deuteronomy 4:1: 'hearken, O Israel, unto the statutes and unto the judgments… for to do them, that ye may live.' God's word is given to be lived. Obedience is the path of life.

== 4:2
T: Neither add nor diminish
R: 'Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish ought from it, that ye may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you.' Do not add to what I command you and do not subtract from it, but keep the commands of the LORD your God. Notice not add. Adding human rules to God's word makes religion heavy and self-made. Notice neither… diminish. Taking away what we dislike makes faith soft and convenient. Notice that both errors change God's word into our word. Notice that ye may keep. The purpose of preserving the word exactly is to obey it fully. Proverbs 30 says, 'Add thou not unto his words, lest he reprove thee.' Today, take God's word as it is. Do not burden others with extra rules or excuse yourself from inconvenient ones.
S: Moses commanded Israel neither to add to nor take away from the word God gave, so that they would keep His commandments. Adding makes faith heavy and man-made, while subtracting makes it soft and convenient. Take God's word as it is, without burdening others with extra rules or excusing yourself from the inconvenient ones.
P: Nothing added, nothing taken. Deuteronomy 4:2: 'Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish ought from it.' Take God's word as it is.

== 4:3
T: Because of Baal-peor
R: 'Your eyes have seen what the LORD did because of Baal-peor: for all the men that followed Baal-peor, the LORD thy God hath destroyed them from among you.' You saw with your own eyes what the LORD did at Baal Peor. The LORD your God destroyed from among you everyone who followed the Baal of Peor. Notice your eyes have seen. This was recent history, witnessed by those listening. Notice followed Baal-peor. Israel had joined in Moab's idol worship and immorality. Notice destroyed them from among you. The consequences were swift and severe. Notice that Moses uses this memory as a warning. Idolatry is not a harmless curiosity; it is deadly. Notice that following is the key word. Whom we follow shapes what becomes of us. First Corinthians 10 says, 'these things were our examples, to the intent we should not lust after evil things.' Today, remember the warnings you have witnessed. Let others' falls teach you to follow the LORD alone.
S: Moses reminded Israel that they had seen how the LORD destroyed everyone who followed Baal-peor. The recent memory of idolatry's deadly cost served as a warning for the generation entering the land. Remember the warnings you have witnessed and let them teach you to follow the LORD alone.
P: Remember the warning. Deuteronomy 4:3: 'Your eyes have seen what the LORD did because of Baal-peor.' Whom we follow shapes what becomes of us. Follow the LORD alone.

== 4:4
T: Ye that did cleave unto the LORD
R: 'But ye that did cleave unto the LORD your God are alive every one of you this day.' But all of you who held fast to the LORD your God are still alive today. Notice but. The contrast with verse 3 is sharp. Some followed Baal; others clung to God. Notice cleave. The word means to stick, to hold tight, to cling like glue. It is the same word used of a husband cleaving to his wife. Notice alive every one of you. Not one who clung to God perished. Faithfulness was life. Notice this day. They were living proof that holding fast to God is safe. Notice that the listeners themselves were the evidence. Psalm 63 says, 'My soul followeth hard after thee: thy right hand upholdeth me.' Today, cling to the LORD. In a world of competing loyalties, hold fast to Him with both hands. Those who cleave to Him find life.
S: Moses told Israel that everyone who had clung to the LORD their God was still alive that day. The word cleave means to hold tight, and their survival was living proof that faithfulness to God is life. Cling to the LORD with both hands amid competing loyalties, for those who cleave to Him find life.
P: Cling to Him. Deuteronomy 4:4: 'But ye that did cleave unto the LORD your God are alive every one of you this day.' Hold tight to God. Those who cleave to Him find life.

== 4:24
T: A consuming fire
R: 'For the LORD thy God is a consuming fire, even a jealous God.' For the LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God. Notice a consuming fire. God's holiness burns away what is false. He is not tame or safe in the way idols are. Notice even a jealous God. His jealousy is not insecurity but covenant love. Like a faithful husband, He will not share His people with rivals. Notice that the fire on the mountain was a picture of God Himself. Notice that these words explain the warnings. God forbids idols because He loves His people completely and wants them wholly. Hebrews 12 echoes, 'For our God is a consuming fire.' Today, let God's holy jealousy comfort you rather than frighten you. His fierce love wants all of you. Bring Him the rivals in your heart and let His fire purify you.
