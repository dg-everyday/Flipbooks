# Deuteronomy 4:25-49: Scattering and return, the LORD alone is God, and the cities of refuge

== 4:25
T: When ye have remained long
R: 'When thou shalt beget children, and children… children, and ye shall have remained long in the land, and shall corrupt yourselves, and make a graven image…' After you have had children and grandchildren and have lived in the land a long time, if you then become corrupt and make any kind of idol, doing evil in the eyes of the LORD, Notice remained long in the land. The danger would come not in the wilderness but in comfort. Notice children's children. Faith can fade across generations. Notice corrupt yourselves. Settled ease can slowly erode devotion. Notice that prosperity is often more dangerous than hardship. In hard times we cling to God; in good times we drift. Notice to provoke him to anger. Idolatry wounds God's love. Proverbs 30 prays, 'lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the LORD?' Today, watch your heart most carefully when life is comfortable. Do not let long years of blessing make you forget the Giver.
S: Moses warned that after Israel had children and grandchildren and remained long in the land, they might corrupt themselves with idols. The danger would come in comfort rather than hardship, as faith faded across generations. Watch your heart most carefully when life is comfortable, and do not let long blessing make you forget the Giver.
P: The danger of comfort. Deuteronomy 4:25: 'ye shall have remained long in the land, and shall corrupt yourselves.' Prosperity can erode devotion. Guard your heart in good times.

== 4:26

R: 'I call heaven and earth to witness against you this day, that ye shall soon utterly perish from off the land whereunto ye go over Jordan to possess it…' I call the heavens and the earth as witnesses against you this day that you will quickly perish from the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess. The warning is strong and urgent. Notice that the land was not owned unconditionally. Israel could lose what God had given if they abandoned Him. Notice that this warning came true centuries later in the exiles to Assyria and Babylon. Notice that a loving father warns clearly. Galatians 6 says, 'whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.' Today, take God's warnings as acts of love. He tells us the truth about where our paths lead so we can choose life.
S: Moses called heaven and earth to witness that if Israel turned to idols they would soon perish from the land they were about to possess. The land was not held unconditionally, and this warning later came true in the exiles. Take God's warnings as acts of love that show where our paths lead so we can choose life.
