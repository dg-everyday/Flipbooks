# Deuteronomy 8: Remember the wilderness, and do not forget God in the good land

== 8:1
T: That ye may live, and multiply
R: 'All the commandments which I command thee this day shall ye observe to do, that ye may live, and multiply, and go in and possess the land which the LORD sware unto your fathers.' Be careful to follow every command I am giving you today, so that you may live, increase and enter and possess the land the LORD promised. Notice all the commandments. Not a selection, but the whole. Notice observe to do. Careful attention and action. Notice live, and multiply, and go in and possess. Four blessings flow from obedience. Notice which the LORD sware. The land was promised long before; obedience was the path of receiving it. Notice this day again. Moses keeps bringing the call into the present moment. Psalm 128 says, 'Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD; that walketh in his ways.' Today, trust that God's commands lead to life and growth. Walk in all His ways, not just the convenient ones, and watch Him lead you into His promises.
S: Moses urged Israel to observe all the commandments he gave that day so that they would live, multiply and possess the land God had sworn to their fathers. Obedience was the path to receiving God's long-promised blessings. Walk in all God's ways, not only the convenient ones, and trust Him to lead you into His promises.
P: Obey and live. Deuteronomy 8:1: 'All the commandments… shall ye observe to do, that ye may live, and multiply, and go in and possess the land.' All His ways lead to life.

== 8:2
T: To know what was in thine heart
R: 'And thou shalt remember all the way which the LORD thy God led thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart…' Remember how the LORD your God led you all the way in the wilderness these forty years, to humble and test you, to know what was in your heart, whether or not you would keep His commands. Notice remember all the way. Every step mattered. Notice the LORD thy God led thee. The wilderness was not an accident; God led them there. Notice to humble thee. Hardship stripped away pride. Notice to prove thee. Testing revealed what was really inside. Notice to know what was in thine heart. Trials expose the heart, not to inform God but to show us ourselves. James 1 says, 'the trying of your faith worketh patience.' Today, look at your wilderness seasons differently. God may be using them to humble you, test you and show you what is in your heart.
S: Moses told Israel to remember how God led them forty years in the wilderness to humble them, test them and reveal what was in their hearts. The wilderness was not an accident but a place where trials exposed the heart. See your wilderness seasons as times when God humbles you, tests you and shows you what is in your heart.
P: The purpose of the wilderness. Deuteronomy 8:2: 'the LORD thy God led thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart.' Trials reveal the heart.

== 8:3
T: Not by bread only
R: 'And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna… that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD doth man live.' He let you hunger, then fed you manna, to teach you to live on His word. Notice suffered thee to hunger. God allowed need. Notice fed thee with manna. Then He met it in an unexpected way. Notice not by bread only. Physical food is necessary but not sufficient. Notice every word… of the LORD. Our deepest life depends on God's word. Notice that Jesus quoted this verse in the wilderness when tempted to turn stones to bread. Matthew 4 records Him saying, 'It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone.' Today, feed on God's word as truly as you eat food. Let hunger remind you of your deeper need for Him.
S: God humbled Israel, let them hunger and fed them with manna to teach them that man does not live by bread only but by every word from the mouth of the LORD. Jesus quoted this verse when He was tempted in the wilderness. Feed on God's word as truly as you eat food, and let hunger remind you of your deeper need for Him.
P: Not by bread alone. Deuteronomy 8:3: 'man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD.' Jesus quoted it in the desert. Feed on His word daily.

Notice thy raiment waxed not old. Forty years in the desert, and their clothes held together. Notice neither did thy foot swell. Endless walking, yet their feet were protected. Notice these forty years. Daily, quiet miracles over four decades. Notice that God cared for small, ordinary things: clothes and feet. Your health, your clothing, your daily strength. Thank God for the miracles that are so ordinary you barely notice them.
S: Moses reminded Israel that for forty years their clothes did not wear out and their feet did not swell. God cared for small, ordinary needs with daily, quiet miracles that were easy to overlook. Look for the constant mercies you may have taken for granted and thank God for them today.
P: Quiet miracles. Deuteronomy 8:4: 'Thy raiment waxed not old upon thee, neither did thy foot swell, these forty years.' God cares for the small things. Thank Him for mercies you barely notice.

Discipline is a father's act of love. Notice so the LORD thy God chasteneth thee. The hard parts of the wilderness were not rejection but training. Notice that God disciplines those He considers His children. Discipline is proof of belonging. Notice that the goal of discipline is growth, not punishment for its own sake. His discipline is a sign that you are His and that He cares too much to leave you as you are.
S: Moses told Israel to consider in their hearts that as a father disciplines his son, so the LORD disciplined them. The hard parts of the wilderness were not rejection but loving training, proof that they belonged to Him. In hard seasons, consider whether God is training you as a loving Father who cares too much to leave you as you are.
P: A Father's discipline. Deuteronomy 8:5: 'as a man chasteneth his son, so the LORD thy God chasteneth thee.' Discipline proves you belong. He loves you too much to leave you unchanged.

== 8:6

Notice therefore. Because God is a loving Father who disciplines for good, His children should obey. Notice keep the commandments. Guard and do His word. Notice to walk in his ways. Obedience is a lifestyle, a path walked daily. Notice and to fear him. Reverence is the root of faithful walking. Notice that discipline is meant to produce this response. A child who understands a father's love responds with trust and respect. Notice the three things together: keep, walk, fear. Micah 6 says God requires us 'to walk humbly with thy God.' Today, respond to God's fatherly care with faithful obedience. Keep His word, walk His ways and honour Him with reverence, step by step.
S: Moses told Israel that because God disciplined them as a loving Father, they should keep His commandments, walk in His ways and fear Him. Discipline was meant to produce a life of daily obedience rooted in reverence. Respond to God's fatherly care by keeping His word, walking His ways and honouring Him step by step.
P: Keep, walk, fear. Deuteronomy 8:6: 'keep the commandments of the LORD thy God, to walk in his ways, and to fear him.' Respond to a Father's love with daily, reverent obedience.

P: A land of plenty. Deuteronomy 8:8: 'A land of wheat, and barley, and vines, and fig trees, and pomegranates; a land of oil olive, and honey.' God delights to give good things. Taste them with thanks.
