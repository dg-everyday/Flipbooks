# Deuteronomy 11:17-32: Lay up these words; every place your feet tread; blessing and curse

== 11:17
T: He shut up the heaven
R: 'And then the LORD'S wrath be kindled against you, and he shut up the heaven, that there be no rain, and that the land yield not her fruit…' Then God's anger would burn, He would shut the sky so no rain fell, and you would soon perish from the good land. Notice he shut up the heaven. The God who gives rain can withhold it. Notice that the land yield not her fruit. Drought would expose the emptiness of false gods. Baal could not bring rain. Notice lest ye perish quickly. The consequences of idolatry were swift. Notice that Elijah's drought in First Kings was exactly this judgment. Notice that God sometimes withholds blessing to bring His people back. Dry seasons can be wake-up calls. Amos 4 records God saying, 'I have withholden the rain from you… yet have ye not returned unto me.' Today, if you are in a dry season, ask whether God is calling you back. Turn to Him, the true giver of rain.
S: Moses warned that if Israel served other gods, the LORD's wrath would shut up the heavens so no rain fell and the land yielded no fruit. Drought would expose the emptiness of false gods, as later happened in Elijah's day. If you are in a dry season, ask whether God is calling you back to Himself as the true giver of rain.
P: Shut heavens. Deuteronomy 11:17: 'he shut up the heaven, that there be no rain.' Dry seasons can be wake-up calls. Return to the true giver of rain.

== 11:18
T: Lay up these my words in your heart
R: 'Therefore shall ye lay up these my words in your heart and in your soul, and bind them for a sign upon your hand, that they may be as frontlets between your eyes.' Store up My words in your heart and mind; tie them on your hands and foreheads. Notice lay up. Like storing treasure. Notice in your heart and in your soul. Deep inside, not just on the surface. Notice bind them… upon your hand. Let them guide your actions. Notice frontlets between your eyes. Let them shape your vision. Notice that this repeats chapter 6 almost word for word. Moses says the most important things more than once. Notice that the answer to the danger of deception in verse 16 is God's word laid up in the heart. Psalm 119 says, 'Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee.' Today, store God's word deep inside you. Memorise it, meditate on it and let it guard your heart from deception.
S: Moses told Israel to lay up God's words in their hearts and souls and bind them on their hands and between their eyes. Repeating chapter 6, he showed that God's word stored deep within is the answer to the heart's deception. Store God's word deep inside you through memorising and meditation, and let it guard your heart.
P: Store it deep. Deuteronomy 11:18: 'lay up these my words in your heart and in your soul.' God's word guards against deception. Hide it in your heart.

== 11:19
T: Teach them your children
R: 'And ye shall teach them your children, speaking of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.' Teach them to your children, talking about them at home, on the road, at bedtime and when you wake. Notice teach them your children. Again, the next generation. Notice speaking of them. Conversation, not only formal instruction. Notice sittest… walkest… liest down… risest up. All day, every day, in ordinary moments. Notice that this repetition shows how important passing on faith is to God. Notice that children learn what is talked about naturally at home. If God is part of daily conversation, faith becomes part of life. Proverbs 22 says, 'Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.' Today, find one natural moment to talk about God with a child or young person. Let faith be woven into the ordinary.
S: Moses repeated the call to teach God's words to children, speaking of them at home, on the road, at bedtime and on rising. Faith is passed on most naturally when God is part of everyday conversation. Find one natural moment today to talk about God with a child or young person.
P: Every ordinary moment. Deuteronomy 11:19: 'ye shall teach them your children, speaking of them when thou sittest in thine house.' Weave faith into daily conversation.

== 11:20
T: Upon the door posts of thine house
R: 'And thou shalt write them upon the door posts of thine house, and upon thy gates.' Write them on the doorframes of your houses and on your gates. Notice write them. Visible reminders. Notice door posts of thine house. Every time you go out or come in. Notice upon thy gates. Public spaces too. Notice that the home is the first place where God's word should be visible. Notice that physical reminders help our forgetful hearts. A verse on the wall, a Bible on the table or a prayer at the door can turn our thoughts to God. Notice that these reminders are meant to point to a living relationship, not to replace it. Psalm 121 says, 'The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth.' Today, consider placing a reminder of God's word somewhere you will see it daily. Let your doorway remind you that you belong to Him, every time you leave and return.
S: Moses told Israel to write God's words on the door posts of their houses and on their gates. Visible reminders at home and in public help forgetful hearts turn to God in daily life. Place a reminder of God's word somewhere you will see it daily, letting it point you to your living relationship with Him.
P: Reminders at the door. Deuteronomy 11:20: 'thou shalt write them upon the door posts of thine house, and upon thy gates.' Let your doorway remind you whose you are.

== 11:21
T: As the days of heaven upon the earth
R: 'That your days may be multiplied, and the days of your children, in the land which the LORD sware unto your fathers to give them, as the days of heaven upon the earth.' So that you and your children may live long in the land, as long as the heavens are above the earth. Notice your days… and the days of your children. Blessing for two generations. Notice multiplied. Increased and extended. Notice as the days of heaven upon the earth. A beautiful phrase. Life on earth tasting of heaven. Notice that this is the fruit of teaching children God's word. Faithful homes produce lasting blessing. Notice that heaven touching earth is the goal of God's covenant. Matthew 6 teaches us to pray, 'Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.' Today, pray for heaven's days on earth in your home. Let God's word, taught and lived, bring a taste of heaven to your family life.
S: Moses promised that teaching God's words would multiply the days of Israel and their children in the land, as the days of heaven upon the earth. Faithful homes produce lasting blessing and a taste of heaven on earth. Pray that God's word, taught and lived, will bring heaven's days into your home.
P: Heaven on earth. Deuteronomy 11:21: 'That your days may be multiplied, and the days of your children… as the days of heaven upon the earth.' Let God's word bring heaven into your home.

== 11:22
T: To cleave unto him
R: 'For if ye shall diligently keep all these commandments which I command you, to do them, to love the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways, and to cleave unto him.' If you carefully keep all these commands, loving God, walking in His ways and holding fast to Him. Notice diligently keep. Careful obedience. Notice to love the LORD. Always love at the centre. Notice to walk in all his ways. Daily obedience. Notice and to cleave unto him. Hold tight. This is the language of close devotion. Notice that the list moves from doing to loving to walking to clinging. Obedience grows into intimacy. Notice that cleaving is the deepest response. It means we do not let go even when tested. Psalm 73 says, 'it is good for me to draw near to God.' Today, move from duty to devotion. Keep God's commands, but go further: love Him, walk with Him and cleave to Him with your whole heart.
S: Moses described the condition for victory: diligently keeping God's commandments, loving Him, walking in His ways and cleaving to Him. The list moves from doing to loving to walking to clinging, as obedience grows into intimacy. Move from duty to devotion, holding fast to God with your whole heart.
P: From duty to devotion. Deuteronomy 11:22: 'to love the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways, and to cleave unto him.' Obedience grows into intimacy. Hold tight to Him.

== 11:23
T: Greater nations and mightier
R: 'Then will the LORD drive out all these nations from before you, and ye shall possess greater nations and mightier than yourselves.' Then the LORD will drive out all these nations, and you will dispossess peoples larger and stronger than you. Notice then. Victory follows faithful love and obedience. Notice the LORD drive out. God does the decisive work. Notice greater nations and mightier than yourselves. Israel would overcome the impossible. Notice that faithfulness to God brings power beyond our natural ability. What we could never do alone, God does through obedient people. Notice that Israel's history proves this. When they were faithful, they prevailed; when unfaithful, they were defeated. Philippians 4 says, 'I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.' Today, do not measure what is possible by your own size. When you walk with God, He can overcome what is greater and mightier than you.
S: Moses promised that if Israel loved and obeyed God, He would drive out nations greater and mightier than they. Faithfulness to God brings power beyond natural ability, as Israel's history later showed. Do not measure what is possible by your own size, for God can overcome what is greater and mightier than you.
P: Greater than you. Deuteronomy 11:23: 'ye shall possess greater nations and mightier than yourselves.' Don't measure possibility by your size. God does through you what you can't.

== 11:24
T: Every place your feet shall tread
R: 'Every place whereon the soles of your feet shall tread shall be yours: from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the river Euphrates, even unto the uttermost sea shall your coast be.' Every place you set your foot will be yours, from the desert to Lebanon and from the Euphrates to the western sea. Notice every place whereon… your feet shall tread. Possession required walking. Faith had to move. Notice shall be yours. The promise was generous. Notice from the wilderness… to the uttermost sea. Vast borders. Notice that God's promise awaited Israel's steps. The land was given, but they had to go and claim it. Notice that God repeated this promise to Joshua. Joshua 1 says, 'Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread upon, that have I given unto you.' Today, step forward in faith into what God has promised. Possession often follows obedience. Where He leads you to walk, He gives you ground.
S: God promised that every place Israel's feet would tread would be theirs, from the wilderness to Lebanon and from the Euphrates to the sea. The land was given, but Israel had to step forward and claim it, a promise repeated to Joshua. Step forward in faith into what God has promised, trusting Him to give you ground where He leads you.
P: Where your feet tread. Deuteronomy 11:24: 'Every place whereon the soles of your feet shall tread shall be yours.' The promise awaited their steps. Walk forward in faith.

== 11:25
T: No man able to stand before you
R: 'There shall no man be able to stand before you: for the LORD your God shall lay the fear of you and the dread of you upon all the land that ye shall tread upon, as he hath said unto you.' No one will withstand you; God will put the fear of you on every land you enter, as He promised. Notice no man be able to stand. Total victory promised. Notice for the LORD your God. The reason is God, not Israel. Notice lay the fear of you. God would prepare the way by working in the hearts of enemies. Notice as he hath said. His promise was the guarantee. Notice that God works ahead of His people. He prepares hearts before we arrive. Notice that Rahab later testified to this exact fear in Jericho. Joshua 2 records her saying, 'your terror is fallen upon us.' Today, trust that God goes ahead of you. He can prepare hearts and situations long before you arrive. Walk in confidence.
S: Moses promised that no one would stand before Israel, for the LORD would lay the fear and dread of them on every land they trod, as He had said. God worked ahead of His people, as Rahab later testified in Jericho. Trust that God goes ahead of you, preparing hearts and situations before you arrive.
P: God goes ahead. Deuteronomy 11:25: 'the LORD your God shall lay the fear of you and the dread of you upon all the land.' He prepares hearts before you arrive. Walk in confidence.

== 11:26
T: A blessing and a curse
R: 'Behold, I set before you this day a blessing and a curse.' See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse. Notice behold. Look carefully. This is important. Notice I set before you. Moses laid out two paths like objects on a table. Notice this day. The choice was now. Notice a blessing and a curse. Two outcomes, two directions, no middle ground. Notice that God gives real choices with real consequences. He does not force obedience. Notice that this verse introduces a major theme of Deuteronomy, developed later in chapters 27 to 30. Notice that God sets both before us, but He clearly wants us to choose blessing. Joshua 24 says, 'choose you this day whom ye will serve.' Today, recognise that you face real choices before God. Every day, blessing and curse are set before you through obedience or disobedience. Choose the path of blessing by choosing Him.
S: Moses told Israel to behold that he was setting before them that day a blessing and a curse. God gives real choices with real consequences, while clearly desiring His people to choose blessing. Recognise that every day you choose between paths before God, and choose blessing by choosing Him.
P: Two paths. Deuteronomy 11:26: 'Behold, I set before you this day a blessing and a curse.' Real choices, real consequences. Choose blessing by choosing God.

== 11:27
T: A blessing, if ye obey
R: 'A blessing, if ye obey the commandments of the LORD your God, which I command you this day.' The blessing if you obey the commands of the LORD your God. Notice a blessing. God's first word is blessing. Notice if ye obey. The path to blessing is obedience. Notice the commandments of the LORD your God. Not human rules, but God's own word. Notice which I command you this day. Present, specific, clear. Notice that the condition is simple: obey. Not perfect performance, but a heart turned toward God's word. Notice that blessing comes as fruit of the relationship, not as wages earned. God delights to bless those who walk with Him. James 1 says, 'a doer of the work, this man shall be blessed in his deed.' Today, choose obedience in one specific area. Trust that God's blessing follows those who walk in His ways. Blessing is not a bribe; it is the natural fruit of living as He designed.
S: Moses told Israel that the blessing would come if they obeyed the commandments of the LORD their God. Blessing is the fruit of a relationship with God expressed through obedience, not wages earned. Choose obedience in one specific area today, trusting that blessing is the natural fruit of living as God designed.
P: The blessing path. Deuteronomy 11:27: 'A blessing, if ye obey the commandments of the LORD your God.' Blessing isn't a bribe; it's the fruit of walking with Him.

== 11:28
T: Other gods which ye have not known
R: 'And a curse, if ye will not obey the commandments of the LORD your God, but turn aside out of the way which I command you this day, to go after other gods, which ye have not known.' And the curse if you disobey and turn from the way, following other gods you have not known. Notice a curse. The other path has consequences too. Notice if ye will not obey. A deliberate refusal. Notice turn aside out of the way. Leaving the path God set. Notice to go after other gods. Disobedience leads to new loyalties. Notice which ye have not known. Strangers. Gods who had never rescued, fed or loved them. Notice the foolishness of trading the God who brought them out of Egypt for unknown gods. Jeremiah 2 says, 'they have forsaken me the fountain of living waters, and hewed them out cisterns, broken cisterns.' Today, do not trade the God you know for unknown substitutes. He has proved His love. Stay on His path.
S: Moses warned that the curse would come if Israel refused to obey and turned aside to follow other gods they had not known. It would be foolish to trade the God who rescued them for strangers who had never loved them. Do not trade the God who has proved His love for unknown substitutes, but stay on His path.
P: Strangers for gods. Deuteronomy 11:28: 'to go after other gods, which ye have not known.' Why trade the God who rescued you for strangers? Stay on His path.

== 11:29
T: Gerizim and Ebal
R: 'And it shall come to pass, when the LORD thy God hath brought thee in unto the land whither thou goest to possess it, that thou shalt put the blessing upon mount Gerizim, and the curse upon mount Ebal.' When God brings you into the land, proclaim the blessings on Mount Gerizim and the curses on Mount Ebal. Notice when the LORD… hath brought thee in. Moses speaks confidently of arrival. Notice mount Gerizim… mount Ebal. Two mountains facing each other near Shechem. Notice the blessing… the curse. The choice of verse 26 was to be acted out in the landscape. Notice that God gave Israel a vivid, unforgettable picture of their choice. Mountains do not move; neither do God's covenant terms. Notice that Joshua later carried this out in Joshua 8. Notice that physical places can anchor spiritual truths. Today, think of a place that reminds you of God's promises and warnings. Let ordinary landmarks call you back to the choice to follow Him.
S: Moses commanded that when Israel entered the land, the blessing should be proclaimed on mount Gerizim and the curse on mount Ebal. The two facing mountains would make the covenant choice vivid and unforgettable, as Joshua later did. Let ordinary landmarks in your life call you back to the choice to follow God.
P: Two mountains. Deuteronomy 11:29: 'thou shalt put the blessing upon mount Gerizim, and the curse upon mount Ebal.' A choice written in the landscape. Let places remind you to choose God.

== 11:30
T: Beside the plains of Moreh
R: 'Are they not on the other side Jordan, by the way where the sun goeth down, in the land of the Canaanites, which dwell in the champaign over against Gilgal, beside the plains of Moreh?' These mountains lie west of the Jordan, in Canaanite land near Gilgal, beside the great trees of Moreh. Notice on the other side Jordan. Across the river, in the promised land. Notice where the sun goeth down. West. Notice beside the plains of Moreh. Moreh was where God first appeared to Abraham in Canaan and promised him the land. Notice that the place of blessing and curse was the place of the first promise. Notice that God's story circles back. What He promised Abraham at Moreh would be confirmed there to his descendants. Genesis 12 records, 'the LORD appeared unto Abram, and said, Unto thy seed will I give this land.' Today, remember that God's promises have deep roots. The God who promised long ago keeps His word in the same places, centuries later.
S: Moses located Gerizim and Ebal west of the Jordan near Gilgal, beside the plains of Moreh. Moreh was where God first appeared to Abraham in Canaan and promised him the land. Remember that God's promises have deep roots, for He keeps His word in the very places He first spoke it.
P: Back to Moreh. Deuteronomy 11:30: 'beside the plains of Moreh.' Where God first promised Abraham the land. Centuries later, the same place. His promises have deep roots.

== 11:31
T: Ye shall possess it, and dwell therein
R: 'For ye shall pass over Jordan to go in to possess the land which the LORD your God giveth you, and ye shall possess it, and dwell therein.' You are about to cross the Jordan to take the land God is giving you; you will possess it and live there. Notice ye shall pass over Jordan. Certain, not doubtful. Notice which the LORD your God giveth you. God is the giver. Notice ye shall possess it. A promise. Notice and dwell therein. Not just conquer, but settle and make a home. Notice the confidence in Moses' words. After forty years, the crossing was near. Notice that God's purpose was not only victory but rest and home. He wanted His people to live in the land, not just win it. Hebrews 4 says, 'There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God.' Today, trust that God is leading you not only through battles but toward rest. His goal is a home with Him.
S: Moses assured Israel that they would cross the Jordan, possess the land God was giving them and dwell in it. God's purpose was not only victory but rest and a settled home. Trust that God is leading you through battles toward rest and a home with Him.
P: A home, not just a victory. Deuteronomy 11:31: 'ye shall possess it, and dwell therein.' God's goal is rest and home. He's leading you there.

== 11:32
T: Observe to do all the statutes
R: 'And ye shall observe to do all the statutes and judgments which I set before you this day.' Be sure to obey all the decrees and laws I am setting before you today. Notice observe to do. Careful obedience. Notice all the statutes and judgments. The whole of God's word. Notice which I set before you this day. Placed before them like a gift. Notice that this verse closes the first great section of Moses' sermon. Chapters 12 to 26 will spell out the specific laws. Notice that the call is the same as always: hear and do. Notice that after all the history, warnings and promises, the conclusion is simple obedience. Matthew 7 records Jesus saying, 'whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock.' Today, build your life on doing God's word, not just hearing it. Let His word be the rock beneath your house.
S: Moses closed the first great section of his sermon by calling Israel to observe and do all the statutes and judgments he set before them that day. After the history, warnings and promises, the conclusion was simple obedience. Build your life on doing God's word and not just hearing it, letting it be the rock beneath your house.
P: Hear and do. Deuteronomy 11:32: 'ye shall observe to do all the statutes and judgments which I set before you this day.' Build your house on doing His word.
