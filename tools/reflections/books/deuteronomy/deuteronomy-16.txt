# Deuteronomy 16: Passover, Weeks and Tabernacles, and judges who judge justly

Keep the Passover in Abib, the month God brought you out of Egypt by night. Notice observe the month of Abib. Abib means fresh ears of grain, the spring month. Notice keep the passover. The great feast of deliverance. Notice unto the LORD thy God. Worship centred on Him. Notice brought thee forth… by night. Rescue came in the darkness. Notice that Israel's year began with remembering salvation. Their calendar was shaped by God's acts. Notice that Jesus was crucified at Passover, the true Lamb. First Corinthians 5 says, 'For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us.' Today, let your calendar and your rhythms be shaped by what God has done. Remember your salvation regularly. Christ, our Passover, brought you out of darkness by night.

Notice that God's hatred of idols flows from His love for His people. Idols deceive and destroy. Notice that what God hates, His people should not tolerate. Notice that the chapter moves from joyful feasts to just judges to forbidden idols. All belong to faithful life. First John 5 says, 'Little children, keep yourselves from idols.' Today, let God's hatred of idolatry shape your heart. Do not set up any substitute for Him, however small. Keep yourself from idols, and keep your heart for Him.
