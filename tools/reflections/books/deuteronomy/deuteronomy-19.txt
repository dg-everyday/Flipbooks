# Deuteronomy 19: Cities of refuge, the ancient landmark and the honest witness

== 19:1
T: Dwellest in their cities
R: 'When the LORD thy God hath cut off the nations, whose land the LORD thy God giveth thee, and thou succeedest them, and dwellest in their cities, and in their houses.' When God has given you the land and you live in its cities and houses. Notice the LORD thy God hath cut off. God's action came first. Notice whose land the LORD… giveth thee. The land was a gift. Notice dwellest in their cities, and in their houses. Settled life. Notice that this verse introduces laws for life in the land. As soon as Israel settled, they would need structures of justice and mercy. Notice that blessing brings responsibility. With homes come neighbours, disputes and accidents. Notice that God prepared Israel for the realities of settled life. Psalm 127 says, 'Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it.' Today, as God gives you a place to live and work, ask how you can build justice and mercy into it.
S: Moses introduced laws for the time when God had cut off the nations and Israel dwelt in their cities and houses. Settled life would bring neighbours, disputes and accidents, so God prepared structures of justice and mercy in advance. As God gives you a place to live and work, ask how you can build justice and mercy into it.
P: Building a just home. Deuteronomy 19:1: 'thou succeedest them, and dwellest in their cities, and in their houses.' With blessing comes responsibility. Build justice and mercy where you live.
