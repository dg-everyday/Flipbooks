# Deuteronomy 20: Courage for battle, mercy in the ranks, peace offered first and the trees spared

== 20:1
T: Be not afraid of them
R: 'When thou goest out to battle against thine enemies, and seest horses, and chariots, and a people more than thou, be not afraid of them: for the LORD thy God is with thee, which brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.' When you face enemies with horses, chariots and a larger army, do not fear, for God, who brought you out of Egypt, is with you. Notice seest horses, and chariots. The enemy looked superior. Notice a people more than thou. Outnumbered again. Notice be not afraid. The command comes right after the honest description. Notice for the LORD thy God is with thee. The reason for courage is presence. Notice which brought thee up out of… Egypt. Past rescue fuels present faith. Egypt had the greatest chariots, and God defeated them. Isaiah 41 says, 'Fear thou not; for I am with thee.' Today, face what intimidates you with the knowledge that God is with you. The One who defeated Egypt's chariots stands with you now.
S: Moses told Israel that when they saw horses, chariots and a larger army, they must not be afraid, for the LORD who brought them out of Egypt was with them. God's presence was the reason for courage, and His past victory over Egypt's chariots fuelled present faith. Face what intimidates you knowing that the God who defeated Egypt stands with you now.
P: Outnumbered, not alone. Deuteronomy 20:1: 'seest horses, and chariots, and a people more than thou, be not afraid of them: for the LORD thy God is with thee.' He's with you.

Whether it is faintness, anxiety, trembling or terror, God speaks to it directly. Hear His word over your fear.
