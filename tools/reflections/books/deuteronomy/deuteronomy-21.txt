R: 'And if a man have committed a sin worthy of death, and he be to be put to death, and thou hang him on a tree.' If someone guilty of a capital offence is put to death and his body is hung on a tree. Notice a sin worthy of death. A capital crime. Notice he be to be put to death. Executed first. Notice thou hang him on a tree. The body was displayed afterward as a public warning. Notice that hanging was not the method of execution but a display of shame. Notice that this verse sets the scene for one of the most important connections in Scripture. Notice that Jesus was hung on a tree, a cross of wood. Acts 5 records Peter saying, 'The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom ye slew and hanged on a tree.' Today, look at the cross and see the shame Jesus bore. He was hung on a tree in the place of those worthy of death. That includes you and me.
S: Moses addressed the case of a man executed for a capital crime whose body was then hung on a tree as a public display of shame. This sets the scene for the cross, for Jesus was hung on a tree in the place of those worthy of death. Look at the cross and see the shame Jesus bore in your place.
P: Hung on a tree. Deuteronomy 21:22: 'if a man have committed a sin worthy of death… and thou hang him on a tree.' Jesus was hanged on a tree, in the place of those worthy of death.

== 21:23
T: Accursed of God
R: 'His body shall not remain all night upon the tree, but thou shalt in any wise bury him that day; (for he that is hanged is accursed of God;) that thy land be not defiled…' Bury him the same day, for the hanged are cursed. Notice not remain all night. Even the condemned were treated with dignity in death. Notice bury him that day. Jesus was taken down and buried before sunset for this very reason. Notice he that is hanged is accursed of God. The deepest meaning of the cross. Notice that Jesus bore that curse. Galatians 3 says, 'Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree.' Notice that He became the cursed one so we could be blessed. Today, marvel at this exchange. Jesus took your curse on the tree. In Him, you receive the blessing.
S: Moses required that a hanged body be buried the same day, for he that is hanged is accursed of God, and the land must not be defiled. Jesus was taken down and buried before sunset, and Galatians declares He became a curse for us on the tree. Marvel at this exchange: Jesus took your curse on the tree so that in Him you receive the blessing.
P: He became the curse. Deuteronomy 21:23: 'he that is hanged is accursed of God.' Christ redeemed us from the curse, being made a curse for us. He took yours.
