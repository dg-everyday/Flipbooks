# Deuteronomy 22:16-30: Justice for the slandered, the seriousness of adultery, and protection for the wronged

Notice I gave my daughter. He had entrusted her to this man. Notice and he hateth her. The root of the problem was the husband's heart, not the wife's conduct. Notice that the father named the real issue. The accusation was a cover for hatred. Notice that God's law allowed the truth to be spoken publicly. Notice that a parent's advocacy matters. Proverbs 31 says, 'Open thy mouth for the dumb in the cause of all such as are appointed to destruction.' Today, speak up for those who cannot easily defend themselves. Name injustice honestly. And examine whether any accusation you make about someone is really rooted in your own resentment.
S: The young woman's father was to tell the elders that he had given his daughter in marriage and that her husband hated her. The real issue was the husband's heart, and the father spoke openly in her defence. Speak up for those who cannot easily defend themselves, and check whether any accusation you make is really rooted in resentment.
