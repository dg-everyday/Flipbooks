# Deuteronomy 28:1-22: The blessings of obedience, and the first of the curses

== 28:1
T: Set thee on high
R: 'And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the LORD thy God, to observe and to do all his commandments… that the LORD thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth.' Obey fully, and God will lift you high. Notice if thou shalt hearken diligently. Careful listening. Notice to observe and to do. Hearing must become action. Notice all his commandments. Wholehearted obedience. Notice set thee on high. God would lift them up. Notice that this chapter begins with blessing before curse. God's first word is always His desire to bless. Notice that elevation was not for pride but for witness among the nations. Notice that obedience opens the door to God's favour. First Peter 5 says, 'Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in due time.' Today, listen to God's voice and obey it. Trust Him to lift you up in His time and for His glory.
S: Moses promised that if Israel hearkened diligently to God's voice and did all His commandments, the LORD would set them on high above all nations. The chapter begins with blessing, showing God's first desire to bless, and elevation was for witness rather than pride. Listen to God's voice and obey it, trusting Him to lift you up in His time and for His glory.
P: Lifted up. Deuteronomy 28:1: 'if thou shalt hearken diligently… the LORD thy God will set thee on high.' God's first word is blessing. Humble yourself; He exalts in due time.

== 28:2
T: Blessings shall overtake thee
R: 'And all these blessings shall come on thee, and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the LORD thy God.' All these blessings will come on you and accompany you if you obey the LORD your God. Notice all these blessings. A long list follows. Notice shall come on thee. They would arrive. Notice and overtake thee. A vivid picture. Blessings would chase and catch up with the obedient. Notice that the obedient do not need to chase blessings; blessings chase them. Notice if thou shalt hearken. The condition again. Notice that God delights to pour out good on those who walk with Him. Psalm 23 says, 'Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life.' Today, stop chasing blessings and pursue God. Walk with Him, listen to His voice, and let His goodness follow you. You will find that His blessings overtake you.
S: Moses promised that all these blessings would come on Israel and overtake them if they hearkened to God's voice. The obedient do not need to chase blessings, for blessings chase them, like goodness and mercy following the psalmist. Stop chasing blessings and pursue God, letting His goodness follow and overtake you.
P: Blessings that chase you. Deuteronomy 28:2: 'all these blessings shall come on thee, and overtake thee.' Pursue God, not blessings. Goodness and mercy will follow.

Urban life: markets, homes, community. Notice blessed… in the field. Rural life: farming, flocks, work. Notice that God's blessing covers every setting. There is no place beyond His favour. Notice that the city and field represent all of life, public and private, busy and quiet. Notice that God does not bless only religious spaces. He blesses ordinary places of work and living. Psalm 121 says, 'The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in.' Today, wherever you are, in the city or the countryside, at work or at home, ask for God's blessing there. He is Lord of every place and delights to bless you in all of them.
S: Moses promised that the obedient would be blessed in the city and blessed in the field. God's blessing covers every setting, urban and rural, public and private, not only religious spaces. Wherever you are today, ask for God's blessing there, for He is Lord of every place.
P: Blessed everywhere. Deuteronomy 28:3: 'Blessed shalt thou be in the city, and blessed shalt thou be in the field.' God blesses every place you live and work.
