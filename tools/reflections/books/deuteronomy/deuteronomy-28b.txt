# Deuteronomy 28:23-45: Heaven of brass, scattering and loss, the consequences of forsaking God

== 28:23
T: Heaven of brass, earth of iron
R: 'And thy heaven that is over thy head shall be brass, and the earth that is under thee shall be iron.' The sky above you will be bronze, and the ground beneath you iron. Notice heaven… brass. A sky that gives no rain, hard and shining. Notice earth… iron. Ground too hard to plough or grow anything. Notice the vivid picture of drought. Heaven closed, earth unyielding. Notice that it can also picture spiritual dryness. Prayers seem to bounce off a brass sky. Notice that the open heaven of verse 12 becomes a closed heaven here. Notice that disobedience can make heaven feel shut. Isaiah 59 says, 'your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you.' Today, if heaven feels like brass, examine whether something stands between you and God. Confess it, and ask Him to open the heavens again. He delights to restore.
S: Moses warned that disobedience would make the heaven over Israel like brass and the earth beneath like iron. The picture of drought can also describe spiritual dryness when sin separates us from God, closing the open heaven of verse 12. If heaven feels like brass, examine what stands between you and God, confess it and ask Him to open the heavens again.
P: Brass heavens. Deuteronomy 28:23: 'thy heaven that is over thy head shall be brass, and the earth… iron.' Sin can make heaven feel closed. Confess and ask Him to open it again.

== 28:24
T: Rain of powder and dust
R: 'The LORD shall make the rain of thy land powder and dust: from heaven shall it come down upon thee, until thou be destroyed.' God will turn the rain of your land into dust and powder from the sky until you are destroyed. Notice the rain… powder and dust. Instead of water, dust storms. Notice from heaven shall it come down. What should bring life brings choking dust. Notice that blessings can be reversed into burdens. Notice that this reversal is one of the most painful images in the chapter. Life-giving rain becomes life-taking dust. Notice that Elijah's drought fulfilled this in Israel's history. First Kings 17 records, 'there shall not be dew nor rain these years, but according to my word.' Notice that even then, God fed Elijah and the widow. Today, if your life feels dry and dusty, cry out to God. The God who withheld rain in Elijah's day also sent it again when the people turned back.
S: Moses warned that the LORD would turn the rain of the land into powder and dust until Israel was destroyed. Life-giving rain reversed into choking dust, as in Elijah's drought, yet God still fed Elijah and the widow and later sent rain when the people turned. If your life feels dry and dusty, cry out to God, who sends rain again when His people return.
P: Dust instead of rain. Deuteronomy 28:24: 'The LORD shall make the rain of thy land powder and dust.' Elijah's drought ended when hearts turned. Cry out; He sends rain again.
H: #DailyGrace #Deuteronomy2824 #Drought #Return #Hope #BibleVerse

== 28:25
T: Flee seven ways before them
R: 'The LORD shall cause thee to be smitten before thine enemies: thou shalt go out one way against them, and flee seven ways before them: and shalt be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth.' God will let your enemies defeat you; you will march out one way and flee in seven, and be scattered among all nations. Notice the reversal of verse 7. Notice go out one way… flee seven ways. Now Israel would be the one scattered. Notice removed into all the kingdoms. Exile across the world. Notice that without God, Israel's strength vanished. Notice that this came true at the hands of Assyria and Babylon. Notice that God's protection is the difference between victory and defeat. Psalm 127 says, 'except the LORD keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain.' Today, do not rely on your own strength for security. Without God, even strong positions collapse. With Him, the weak stand firm.
S: Moses warned that the LORD would let Israel be smitten before their enemies, going out one way and fleeing seven, and be removed into all kingdoms of the earth. Without God's protection Israel's strength vanished, as happened under Assyria and Babylon. Do not rely on your own strength for security, for without God even strong positions collapse.
P: The reversal of victory. Deuteronomy 28:25: 'thou shalt go out one way against them, and flee seven ways before them.' Except the LORD keep the city, the watchman wakes in vain.
H: #DailyGrace #Deuteronomy2825 #Dependence #Defeat #Security #BibleVerse

== 28:26
T: No man shall fray them away
R: 'And thy carcase shall be meat unto all fowls of the air, and unto the beasts of the earth, and no man shall fray them away.' Your bodies will be food for birds and beasts, and no one will frighten them off. Notice thy carcase. Death in battle. Notice meat unto all fowls… and… beasts. Unburied bodies. Notice no man shall fray them away. No one left to care for the dead. Notice that in the ancient world, lack of burial was the ultimate shame. Notice that this shows the depth of devastation. Not even enough survivors to bury the dead. Notice the solemn weight of sin's consequences. Jeremiah repeated this warning before Jerusalem's fall. Jeremiah 7 says, 'the carcases of this people shall be meat for the fowls of the heaven.' Notice that God does not hide hard truths. Today, let the seriousness of sin humble you. And thank God that Christ was buried with honour and rose again, so that death is not the end for those in Him.
S: Moses warned that the dead of disobedient Israel would be food for birds and beasts with no one to drive them away. Lack of burial was the ultimate shame, showing the depth of devastation that Jeremiah later warned of. Let the seriousness of sin humble you, and thank God that Christ rose again so that death is not the end for those in Him.
P: No one to bury. Deuteronomy 28:26: 'thy carcase shall be meat unto all fowls of the air… and no man shall fray them away.' Sin's consequences are grave. In Christ, death isn't the end.
H: #DailyGrace #Deuteronomy2826 #Consequences #Death #Hope #BibleVerse

== 28:27
T: The botch of Egypt
R: 'The LORD will smite thee with the botch of Egypt, and with the emerods, and with the scab, and with the itch, whereof thou canst not be healed.' God will afflict you with the boils of Egypt, tumours, festering sores and itch, which cannot be cured. Notice the botch of Egypt. The boils that struck Egypt in the plagues. Notice the emerods… scab… itch. Painful skin diseases. Notice whereof thou canst not be healed. No cure. Notice the irony. The plagues God sent on Egypt to free Israel would fall on Israel if they turned from Him. Notice that God is consistent. He does not exempt His own people from the consequences He sent on others. Notice that the One who once said I am the LORD that healeth thee would withhold healing. Exodus 15 says, 'I will put none of these diseases upon thee… for I am the LORD that healeth thee.' Today, remember that God is the healer. Stay close to Him. And when you are sick, seek Him first.
S: Moses warned that disobedient Israel would suffer the botch of Egypt and other incurable diseases. The plagues once sent on Egypt to free Israel would fall on Israel if they turned away, for God is consistent. Remember that God is the healer, stay close to Him and seek Him first when you are sick.
P: Egypt's diseases. Deuteronomy 28:27: 'The LORD will smite thee with the botch of Egypt… whereof thou canst not be healed.' God is the healer. Stay close and seek Him first.
H: #DailyGrace #Deuteronomy2827 #Healing #Consistency #Warning #BibleVerse

== 28:28
T: Madness, blindness and astonishment
R: 'The LORD shall smite thee with madness, and blindness, and astonishment of heart.' God will afflict you with madness, blindness and confusion of mind. Notice madness. Loss of reason. Notice blindness. Inability to see. Notice astonishment of heart. Bewilderment, panic. Notice that these may be physical or spiritual. Turning from God darkens the mind and confuses the heart. Notice that Romans 1 describes this spiritual blindness: 'their foolish heart was darkened.' Notice that sin does not only bring outward troubles; it clouds our thinking. Notice that clarity comes from walking in God's light. Psalm 119 says, 'The entrance of thy words giveth light; it giveth understanding unto the simple.' Today, if your thinking feels confused or dark, turn to God's word. Let His light bring clarity. Ask Him to open your eyes and steady your heart.
S: Moses warned that disobedience would bring madness, blindness and astonishment of heart. Turning from God darkens the mind and confuses the heart, as Romans later describes, while clarity comes from God's word. If your thinking feels confused or dark, turn to God's word and ask Him to open your eyes and steady your heart.
P: Clouded minds. Deuteronomy 28:28: 'madness, and blindness, and astonishment of heart.' Sin darkens thinking and confuses the heart. The entrance of His word gives light.
H: #DailyGrace #Deuteronomy2828 #Clarity #Light #WordOfGod #BibleVerse

== 28:29
T: Grope at noonday
R: 'And thou shalt grope at noonday, as the blind gropeth in darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways: and thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall save thee.' You will grope at noon with no one to save. Notice grope at noonday. Lost even in full daylight. Notice as the blind gropeth in darkness. Total disorientation. Notice not prosper in thy ways. Every plan fails. Notice oppressed and spoiled. Victimised. Notice no man shall save thee. Helplessness. Notice that groping at noon is a picture of spiritual confusion despite having the light. Israel had God's word but would be unable to see it. Notice that Isaiah 59 laments, 'We grope for the wall like the blind… we stumble at noonday as in the night.' Notice that Christ is the light of the world. Today, if you feel lost, turn to Jesus. In Him there is light, direction and rescue when no one else can save.
S: Moses warned that disobedient Israel would grope at noonday like the blind, fail in their ways and be oppressed with no one to save them. Groping at noon pictures spiritual confusion despite having the light, a lament Isaiah later echoed. If you feel lost, turn to Jesus, the light of the world, who gives direction and rescue when no one else can.
P: Groping at noon. Deuteronomy 28:29: 'thou shalt grope at noonday, as the blind gropeth in darkness… and no man shall save thee.' Jesus is the light. He saves.
H: #DailyGrace #Deuteronomy2829 #Light #Jesus #Rescue #BibleVerse

== 28:30
T: Another shall take what is thine
R: 'Thou shalt betroth a wife, and another man shall lie with her: thou shalt build an house, and thou shalt not dwell therein: thou shalt plant a vineyard, and shalt not gather the grapes thereof.' What you love and build, others will take. Notice betroth a wife. Love and hope. Notice another man… The violation and loss of what was most precious. Notice build an house… not dwell therein. Labour lost. Notice plant a vineyard… not gather. Hopes unfulfilled. Notice that these are the very things God protected in chapter 20, when soldiers were sent home to enjoy them. Now they would be lost. Notice that disobedience reverses God's tender protections. Notice the grief of plans and loves stolen. Psalm 127 says, 'Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it.' Today, build your life with God at the centre. What we build without Him can be lost; what is built with Him endures.
S: Moses warned that the disobedient would lose a betrothed wife to another, build houses they would not live in and plant vineyards they would not harvest. These were the very joys God had protected in chapter 20, now reversed by disobedience. Build your life with God at the centre, for what is built without Him can be lost.
P: Labour lost. Deuteronomy 28:30: 'thou shalt build an house, and thou shalt not dwell therein.' What chapter 20 protected, disobedience loses. Build with God at the centre.
H: #DailyGrace #Deuteronomy2830 #Loss #Foundation #Warning #BibleVerse

== 28:31
T: None to rescue them
R: 'Thine ox shall be slain before thine eyes, and thou shalt not eat thereof: thine ass shall be violently taken away from before thy face… thy sheep shall be given unto thine enemies, and thou shalt have none to rescue them.' Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, your donkey stolen, your sheep given to enemies, and no one will rescue them. Notice slain before thine eyes. Helpless watching. Notice violently taken away. Theft by force. Notice given unto thine enemies. Possessions transferred to oppressors. Notice none to rescue them. Powerlessness. Notice that this reverses chapter 22, where neighbours were commanded to return lost animals. Now no one would help. Notice that a society that abandons God loses its compassion. Notice that God's laws of care become meaningless without Him. Psalm 72 says the righteous King 'shall deliver the needy when he crieth.' Today, be the neighbour who rescues and returns. And when you feel powerless to save what you love, turn to the King who delivers.
S: Moses warned that oxen would be slain before Israel's eyes, donkeys taken by force and sheep given to enemies, with none to rescue them. This reversed the compassion of chapter 22, showing that a society that abandons God loses its care for others. Be the neighbour who rescues and returns, and when you feel powerless, turn to the King who delivers.
P: None to rescue. Deuteronomy 28:31: 'thy sheep shall be given unto thine enemies, and thou shalt have none to rescue them.' Be the neighbour who helps. Turn to the King who delivers.

== 28:32
T: Eyes fail with longing
R: 'Thy sons and thy daughters shall be given unto another people, and thine eyes shall look, and fail with longing for them all the day long: and there shall be no might in thine hand.' Your children will be taken, and you will long for them. Notice thy sons and thy daughters. The deepest loss. Notice given unto another people. Taken into captivity. Notice thine eyes shall look, and fail with longing. Parents watching the horizon, hoping for their return. Notice all the day long. Endless grief. Notice no might in thine hand. Helplessness. Notice the heartbreak of this verse. God does not describe this coldly; He paints the pain so that Israel would avoid it. Notice that many parents today know this ache of separated children. Jeremiah 31 says, 'Rahel weeping for her children refused to be comforted.' Then: 'thy children shall come again.' Today, pray for children who are lost or far away. God sees parents' longing, and He is able to bring them home.
S: Moses warned that disobedient Israel's sons and daughters would be given to another people, and their parents' eyes would fail with longing all day, powerless to help. God painted this heartbreak so Israel would avoid it, and Jeremiah later promised that weeping Rachel's children would come again. Pray for children who are lost or far away, for God sees parents' longing and can bring them home.
P: Eyes failing with longing. Deuteronomy 28:32: 'thine eyes shall look, and fail with longing for them all the day long.' God sees parents' ache. Thy children shall come again.

== 28:33
T: Oppressed and crushed alway
R: 'The fruit of thy land, and all thy labours, shall a nation which thou knowest not eat up; and thou shalt be only oppressed and crushed alway.' A people you do not know will eat what your land and labour produce, and you will be continually oppressed and crushed. Notice the fruit of thy land. What they grew. Notice all thy labours. Everything they worked for. Notice a nation which thou knowest not. Strangers would take it. Notice oppressed and crushed alway. Constant suffering. Notice that labour without God's blessing feeds others, not ourselves. Notice that this describes life under foreign domination. Israel later knew it under Assyria, Babylon and Rome. Notice that God's warnings were precise. Ecclesiastes 2 laments labouring for what another will enjoy. Today, remember that the fruit of your labour is secure only in God's hands. Commit your work to Him. Trust Him to guard what you build.
S: Moses warned that a nation Israel did not know would eat the fruit of their land and labour, leaving them oppressed and crushed always. Labour without God's blessing feeds others, as Israel later knew under foreign powers. Commit your work to God and trust Him to guard the fruit of your labour.
P: Labour for others. Deuteronomy 28:33: 'The fruit of thy land, and all thy labours, shall a nation which thou knowest not eat up.' Your work is secure only in God's hands.

== 28:35
T: From the sole unto the crown
R: 'The LORD shall smite thee in the knees, and in the legs, with a sore botch that cannot be healed, from the sole of thy foot unto the top of thy head.' God will afflict your knees and legs with painful, incurable boils that spread from the soles of your feet to the top of your head. Notice in the knees, and in the legs. Unable to walk. Notice a sore botch that cannot be healed. Incurable. Notice from the sole of thy foot unto the top of thy head. Total affliction. Notice that Job suffered exactly this, though not as punishment. Job 2 says Satan 'smote Job with sore boils from the sole of his foot unto his crown.' Notice that the same suffering can come for different reasons. Not all suffering is judgment. Notice that Job's story teaches us not to assume why someone suffers. Today, be careful not to judge others' suffering as punishment. Offer compassion. And if you suffer, take comfort that God was with Job and restored him.
S: Moses warned of incurable boils from the sole of the foot to the top of the head. Job suffered the same affliction not as punishment, teaching that the same suffering can come for different reasons. Do not assume others' suffering is punishment but offer compassion, and if you suffer, remember that God was with Job and restored him.
P: Like Job. Deuteronomy 28:35: 'a sore botch… from the sole of thy foot unto the top of thy head.' Job suffered this too, not as punishment. Don't judge suffering; show compassion.

== 28:36

You and your king will be exiled. Notice and thy king. Moses foresaw a king, centuries before Saul. Notice unto a nation which neither thou nor thy fathers have known. Exile to a foreign land. Notice serve other gods, wood and stone. The punishment matched the sin. Those who turned to idols would end up serving idols. Notice that this was fulfilled when kings Jehoiachin and Zedekiah were taken to Babylon. Notice that a king cannot save a nation that abandons God. Psalm 146 says, 'Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help.' Today, trust God above any leader. Human rulers cannot protect those who forsake Him. Let your hope rest in the King of kings.
S: Moses foresaw that God would bring disobedient Israel and their king to an unknown nation, where they would serve gods of wood and stone. Centuries later, Judah's kings were taken to Babylon, showing that a king cannot save a nation that abandons God. Trust God above any human leader, letting your hope rest in the King of kings.
P: No king can save. Deuteronomy 28:36: 'The LORD shall bring thee, and thy king… unto a nation which neither thou nor thy fathers have known.' Put not your trust in princes.

Today, if your efforts seem to produce little, do not lose heart. Return to God and trust His promise to restore what the locust has eaten.

If your efforts seem to produce little, return to God and trust His promise to restore what has been lost.

== 28:44
T: The head and the tail reversed
R: 'He shall lend to thee, and thou shalt not lend to him: he shall be the head, and thou shalt be the tail.' They will lend to you, but you will not lend to them; they will be the head and you the tail. Notice he shall lend to thee. Dependence. Notice thou shalt not lend to him. No strength to help. Notice he shall be the head, and thou… the tail. The exact reversal of verses 12 and 13. Notice that blessing and curse mirror each other perfectly. What obedience gives, disobedience removes. Notice that financial dependence and loss of leadership follow turning from God. Notice that God's design for Israel's leadership was lost through disobedience, not taken by force. Proverbs 22 says, 'the borrower is servant to the lender.' Today, see how closely blessing is tied to walking with God. Do not trade God's best for your own way. Choose the path that leads to life.
S: Moses warned that the stranger would lend to disobedient Israel, becoming the head while Israel became the tail. This exactly reversed the blessings of verses 12 and 13, showing that what obedience gives, disobedience removes. See how closely blessing is tied to walking with God, and do not trade His best for your own way.

== 28:45
T: Because thou hearkenedst not
R: 'Moreover all these curses shall come upon thee, and shall pursue thee, and overtake thee, till thou be destroyed; because thou hearkenedst not unto the voice of the LORD thy God…' All these curses will pursue and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not obey the LORD or keep His commands. Notice all these curses. The full list. Notice pursue thee, and overtake thee. Relentless. Notice till thou be destroyed. Total. Notice because thou hearkenedst not. The root cause: not listening. Notice that everything traces back to refusing God's voice. Notice that the problem was not lack of information but lack of response. Israel had heard; they would not hearken. Hebrews 3 says, 'To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts.' Today, listen to God's voice and respond. The difference between blessing and curse is not knowledge but obedience. Do not harden your heart.
S: Moses summarised that all these curses would pursue and overtake Israel until destroyed, because they did not hearken to the voice of the LORD. The root problem was not lack of information but refusal to respond to what they had heard. Listen to God's voice and respond today, for the difference between blessing and curse is obedience, not knowledge.
