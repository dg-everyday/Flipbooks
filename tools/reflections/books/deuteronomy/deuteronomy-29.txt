# Deuteronomy 29: The covenant renewed in Moab, the root of bitterness, and the secret things

== 29:1
T: The words of the covenant in Moab
R: 'These are the words of the covenant, which the LORD commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, beside the covenant which he made with them in Horeb.' These are the terms of the covenant God told Moses to make with Israel in Moab, in addition to the one made at Horeb. Notice the words of the covenant. A formal agreement. Notice in the land of Moab. A second ceremony, forty years after Sinai. Notice beside the covenant… in Horeb. Not replacing it, but renewing it. Notice that each generation needed to make the covenant its own. Notice that God renews relationship with His people. Notice that renewal comes at turning points, here at the edge of the promised land. Lamentations 5 prays, 'Turn thou us unto thee, O LORD, and we shall be turned; renew our days as of old.' Today, consider renewing your commitment to God. At life's turning points, return to Him afresh. He welcomes covenant renewal.
S: Moses introduced the words of the covenant made with Israel in the land of Moab, in addition to the covenant at Horeb. Forty years later, each generation needed to make the covenant its own, and God renewed the relationship at a turning point. Consider renewing your commitment to God at life's turning points, for He welcomes covenant renewal.
P: Covenant renewed. Deuteronomy 29:1: 'These are the words of the covenant… in the land of Moab, beside the covenant which he made with them in Horeb.' Renew your commitment at life's turning points.
