# Deuteronomy 30: Return and restoration, a circumcised heart, the near word, and choose life

== 30:1
T: Call them to mind among the nations
R: 'And it shall come to pass, when all these things are come upon thee, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before thee, and thou shalt call them to mind among all the nations…' When you remember these things in exile. Notice when all these things are come upon thee. Moses foresees both blessing and curse happening. Notice thou shalt call them to mind. Remembering in exile. Notice among all the nations. Far from home. Notice that the turning point would be memory. Recalling God's word in a foreign land. Notice that even in exile, God's word could still reach their hearts. Notice that this chapter shifts from warning to hope. Notice that the prodigal son also came to himself in a far country. Luke 15 says, 'when he came to himself, he said… I will arise and go to my father.' Today, if you feel far from God, call His word to mind. Remembering is the first step home.
S: Moses foresaw that after the blessings and curses had come, Israel would call them to mind among the nations where God had driven them. The turning point would be remembering God's word in exile, like the prodigal who came to himself in a far country. If you feel far from God, call His word to mind, for remembering is the first step home.
P: Remembering in exile. Deuteronomy 30:1: 'thou shalt call them to mind among all the nations, whither the LORD thy God hath driven thee.' Like the prodigal, remember and arise.

Notice that God's door was never closed. Return was always possible. Notice that repentance is not only regret but turning toward God and obeying. Notice that the same wholeheartedness required in chapter 6 is required in return. Joel 2 says, 'turn ye even to me with all your heart.' Today, if you have drifted, return to the LORD. Do not wait. He is ready to receive you when you turn with all your heart.
S: Moses foresaw that Israel and their children would return to the LORD and obey His voice with all their heart and soul. God's door was never closed, and repentance meant wholeheartedly turning back to Him and obeying. If you have drifted, return to the LORD with all your heart without waiting, for He is ready to receive you.
P: Return to Him. Deuteronomy 30:2: 'shalt return unto the LORD thy God… with all thine heart, and with all thy soul.' His door is never closed. Turn with all your heart.

S: Moses promised that the LORD would bring the returning exiles into the land their fathers possessed and multiply them above their fathers. Believe that God's restoration can exceed what was lost, and that your future with Him can be greater than your past.

== 30:6

R: 'And the LORD thy God will circumcise thine heart, and the heart of thy seed, to love the LORD thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, that thou mayest live.' God will change your heart so you can love Him. Notice the LORD thy God will circumcise thine heart. God Himself would do it. In chapter 10, Israel was told to do this; here, God promises to do it. Notice and the heart of thy seed. Future generations transformed. Notice to love the LORD… with all thine heart. Love made possible. Notice that thou mayest live. Life as the result. Notice that this is the heart of the new covenant. What Israel could not do, God would do. Notice that grace changes hearts from within. Ezekiel 36 says, 'A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you.' Today, ask God to do what you cannot. Let Him change your heart so that you can truly love Him.
S: Moses promised that the LORD would circumcise their hearts and their children's hearts so they could love Him with all their heart and soul and live. What Israel was earlier commanded to do, God promised to do Himself, the heart of the new covenant. Ask God to do what you cannot, changing your heart so that you can truly love Him.
P: God changes hearts. Deuteronomy 30:6: 'the LORD thy God will circumcise thine heart… to love the LORD thy God with all thine heart.' What we can't do, He does.
