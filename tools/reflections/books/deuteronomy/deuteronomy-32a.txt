# Deuteronomy 32:1-26: The Song of Moses, the Rock, the eagle, and a people who forgot

== 32:1
T: Give ear, O ye heavens
R: 'Give ear, O ye heavens, and I will speak; and hear, O earth, the words of my mouth.' Listen, heavens, and I will speak; hear, earth, the words of my mouth. Notice give ear, O ye heavens. Moses addresses all creation. Notice and hear, O earth. Heaven and earth as witnesses. Notice I will speak. A solemn proclamation. Notice that the song begins on a cosmic scale. What follows matters to the whole universe. Notice that heaven and earth were also called as witnesses in chapters 4 and 30. They outlast every generation. Notice that God's truth deserves the attention of all creation. Notice that Isaiah opened his prophecy the same way. Isaiah 1 says, 'Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth: for the LORD hath spoken.' Today, give God's word your full attention. If heaven and earth are called to listen, so are you. Be still and hear what He says.
S: The Song of Moses opens by calling the heavens and earth to give ear to the words of his mouth. The song begins on a cosmic scale, with creation as lasting witness, as Isaiah later echoed. Give God's word your full attention today, being still to hear what He says.
P: Heaven and earth, listen. Deuteronomy 32:1: 'Give ear, O ye heavens, and I will speak; and hear, O earth, the words of my mouth.' If creation listens, so should you.

Isaiah 55 says, 'as the rain cometh down… so shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth.' Today, receive God's word as refreshing rain. Let it soak into the dry places of your heart. And when you teach others, be gentle like dew.
S: Moses prayed that his teaching would drop as the rain and his speech distil as the dew, like gentle showers on tender herbs and grass. God's word is meant to nourish and refresh rather than batter, as Isaiah later compared it to rain. Receive God's word as refreshing rain in the dry places of your heart, and teach others gently like dew.
