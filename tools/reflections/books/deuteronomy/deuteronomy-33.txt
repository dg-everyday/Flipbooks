R: 'And of Naphtali he said, O Naphtali, satisfied with favour, and full with the blessing of the LORD: possess thou the west and the south.' About Naphtali he said: Naphtali is abounding with the favour of the LORD and is full of His blessing; he will inherit southward to the lake. Notice satisfied with favour. Content in God's kindness. Notice full with the blessing of the LORD. Overflowing. Notice possess thou the west and the south. Land by the Sea of Galilee. Notice that Naphtali's blessing is contentment. Satisfied and full. Notice that true satisfaction comes from God's favour, not from possessions alone. Notice that Naphtali's land later saw much of Jesus' ministry around Galilee. Isaiah 9 says, 'the land of Naphtali… The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light.' Today, seek satisfaction in God's favour. You may not have everything, but His blessing can fill you. Be content and full in Him.
S: Moses blessed Naphtali as satisfied with favour and full with the blessing of the LORD, possessing the west and south. True satisfaction comes from God's favour rather than possessions, and Naphtali's land later saw great light in Jesus' Galilean ministry. Seek satisfaction in God's favour, letting His blessing fill you with contentment.
P: Satisfied with favour. Deuteronomy 33:23: 'O Naphtali, satisfied with favour, and full with the blessing of the LORD.' Find your contentment in His favour.

== 33:24
T: Dip his foot in oil
R: 'And of Asher he said, Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil.' About Asher he said: Most blessed of sons is Asher; let him be favoured by his brothers and let him bathe his feet in oil. Notice blessed with children. Fruitfulness. Notice acceptable to his brethren. Good relationships. Notice let him dip his foot in oil. So much olive oil he could bathe his feet in it. Notice that Asher's land was rich in olive groves. Notice that the blessing included family, friendships and abundance. Notice that being acceptable to one's brothers is a real blessing. Harmony matters. Notice that the prophetess Anna, who greeted the infant Jesus, was of the tribe of Asher. Luke 2 says, 'Anna, a prophetess… of the tribe of Aser.' Today, value good relationships as a blessing. Seek to be a person others are glad to be with. Harmony with your brothers and sisters is a gift from God.
S: Moses blessed Asher with children, acceptance among his brothers and so much oil he could dip his foot in it. The blessing included family, friendships and abundance, and the prophetess Anna who greeted the infant Jesus was of Asher's tribe. Value good relationships as a blessing and seek to be a person others are glad to be with.
P: Acceptable to his brothers. Deuteronomy 33:24: 'Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren.' Harmony is a blessing. Be someone others are glad to know.

== 33:25
T: As thy days, so shall thy strength be
R: 'Thy shoes shall be iron and brass; and as thy days, so shall thy strength be.' The bolts of your gates will be iron and bronze, and your strength will equal your days. Notice thy shoes shall be iron and brass. Strong protection, perhaps fortified gates. Notice as thy days, so shall thy strength be. Strength matched to each day's need. Notice that God does not give tomorrow's strength today. He gives enough for each day. Notice that this promise has comforted believers for centuries. Notice that no day will demand more than God supplies. Notice that strength is daily, like manna. Lamentations 3 says, 'his compassions fail not. They are new every morning.' Second Corinthians 12 says, 'My grace is sufficient for thee.' Today, do not worry about tomorrow's strength. God will give you exactly what each day requires. As your days, so shall your strength be.
S: Moses blessed Asher with shoes of iron and brass and the promise that as his days, so would his strength be. God gives strength matched to each day's need rather than tomorrow's strength today, like daily manna. Do not worry about tomorrow's strength, for God will give exactly what each day requires.
P: Strength for each day. Deuteronomy 33:25: 'as thy days, so shall thy strength be.' Not tomorrow's strength today, but enough for each day. His grace is sufficient.

== 33:26
T: None like the God of Jeshurun
R: 'There is none like unto the God of Jeshurun, who rideth upon the heaven in thy help, and in his excellency on the sky.' There is no one like the God of Jeshurun, who rides across the heavens to help you and on the clouds in His majesty. Notice there is none like unto the God of Jeshurun. Incomparable. Notice who rideth upon the heaven. Majestic and powerful. Notice in thy help. He rides to help His people. Notice and in his excellency on the sky. Glorious. Notice that God's majesty is used for His people's help. Notice the picture of God riding the heavens to come to their aid. Notice that after blessing each tribe, Moses turns to praise the God behind every blessing. Psalm 68 says, 'Sing unto God… extol him that rideth upon the heavens by his name JAH.' Today, remember that there is no one like your God. He rides the heavens to help you. His majesty is at work on your behalf.
S: Moses declared that there is none like the God of Jeshurun, who rides upon the heaven to help His people and in His excellency on the sky. After blessing each tribe, Moses praised the God behind every blessing, whose majesty is used for His people's help. Remember that there is no one like your God, who rides the heavens to help you.
P: None like Him. Deuteronomy 33:26: 'There is none like unto the God of Jeshurun, who rideth upon the heaven in thy help.' His majesty works for you.

== 33:27
T: Underneath are the everlasting arms
R: 'The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms: and he shall thrust out the enemy from before thee; and shall say, Destroy them.' The eternal God is your refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms. He will drive out your enemies before you. Notice the eternal God. Without beginning or end. Notice is thy refuge. A safe place, a home. Notice and underneath are the everlasting arms. When you fall, His arms are there. Notice that there is no bottom beneath God's arms. Notice he shall thrust out the enemy. He fights for you. Notice that this is one of the most comforting verses in all of Scripture. Notice that it does not say you will never fall, but that His arms are underneath. Psalm 46 says, 'God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.' Today, whatever is giving way beneath you, know that underneath are the everlasting arms. You cannot fall beyond God's reach. Rest in His embrace.
S: Moses declared that the eternal God is our refuge and underneath are the everlasting arms, and He thrusts out the enemy before His people. The promise does not say we will never fall, but that God's arms are beneath us with no bottom beyond them. Whatever is giving way beneath you, know that underneath are the everlasting arms and rest in His embrace.
P: Everlasting arms. Deuteronomy 33:27: 'The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms.' You cannot fall beyond His reach. Rest in His embrace.

== 33:28
T: Israel shall dwell in safety
R: 'Israel then shall dwell in safety alone: the fountain of Jacob shall be upon a land of corn and wine; also his heavens shall drop down dew.' So Israel will live in safety; Jacob will dwell secure in a land of grain and new wine, where the heavens drop dew. Notice dwell in safety. Security. Notice alone. Set apart, distinct, depending on God alone. Notice the fountain of Jacob. Israel's descendants. Notice a land of corn and wine. Abundance. Notice his heavens shall drop down dew. Gentle, constant refreshment. Notice that safety and abundance come from God's protection. Notice that dew is quiet but essential. God's blessings often come gently. Notice that dwelling alone means relying on God, not alliances. Psalm 4 says, 'for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety.' Today, find your safety in God alone. Let Him be your security, and watch for the quiet dew of His daily blessings.
S: Moses declared that Israel would dwell in safety alone in a land of corn and wine where the heavens drop down dew. Safety and abundance came from God's protection, often through quiet, gentle blessings like dew. Find your safety in God alone and watch for the quiet dew of His daily blessings.
P: Safe in Him alone. Deuteronomy 33:28: 'Israel then shall dwell in safety alone… also his heavens shall drop down dew.' He alone makes you dwell in safety.

== 33:29
T: O people saved by the LORD
R: 'Happy art thou, O Israel: who is like unto thee, O people saved by the LORD, the shield of thy help, and who is the sword of thy excellency!…' Blessed are you, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD? He is your shield and helper and your glorious sword. Notice happy art thou. Blessed, truly fortunate. Notice who is like unto thee. Unique among nations. Notice O people saved by the LORD. The reason for their happiness: salvation. Notice the shield of thy help. Protection. Notice the sword of thy excellency. Victory. Notice that Moses' final words are joyful. After everything, he declares Israel blessed. Notice that true happiness is being saved by the LORD. Notice that this applies to all who belong to God in Christ. Psalm 144 says, 'happy is that people, whose God is the LORD.' Today, count yourself blessed. If you are saved by the LORD, you are among the happiest people on earth. He is your shield and your sword.
S: Moses ended his blessing by declaring Israel happy, a people saved by the LORD, who is their shield of help and sword of excellency. His final words were joyful, for true happiness is being saved by the LORD. Count yourself blessed if you are saved by the LORD, for He is your shield and your sword.
P: Saved by the LORD. Deuteronomy 33:29: 'Happy art thou, O Israel: who is like unto thee, O people saved by the LORD.' Moses' last word was joy. Count yourself blessed.
