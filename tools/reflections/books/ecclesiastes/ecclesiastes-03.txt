== 3:22
T: A man should rejoice in his own works
R: 'Wherefore I perceive that there is nothing better, than that a man should rejoice in his own works; for that is his portion' Notice wherefore I perceive. So I see. Notice that there is nothing better. The best thing. Notice than that a man should rejoice. Be glad. Notice in his own works. In his work. Notice for that is his portion. His share. Notice for who shall bring him to see. Who can show him. Notice what shall be after him. The future. Notice that we cannot see what comes after us. Notice that we cannot control it. Notice that we can enjoy today's work. Notice that it is our portion. Notice that it is enough for today. Notice that worrying about tomorrow steals today. Notice that Jesus taught this. Notice that each day has enough. Matthew 6 says, 'Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself.' Notice the morrow. Today, enjoy your work. Leave tomorrow with God.
S: The Preacher saw that nothing is better than for a person to enjoy their work, for that is their share, since no one can show them what comes after. We cannot control tomorrow, but we can receive today's work as God's gift. Do not borrow worry, for take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself.
P: Your portion. Ecclesiastes 3:22: 'that a man should rejoice in his own works; for that is his portion.' Today. Take therefore no thought for the morrow. Enjoy today's work. Walk in His ways today.
