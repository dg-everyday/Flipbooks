# Ecclesiastes 6: Who knoweth what is good for man

== 6:1
T: It is common among men
R: 'There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it is common among men' Notice there is an evil. A sorrow. Notice which I have seen. I observed. Notice under the sun. On earth. Notice and it is common among men. It happens often. Notice that the Preacher introduces a familiar problem. Notice that it is not rare. Notice that many people face it. Notice that common sorrows are still real. Notice that they still hurt. Notice that God sees common pain. Notice that He does not ignore it because it is ordinary. Notice that He cares about everyday struggles. Notice that our trials are shared by others. Notice that we are not alone in them. Notice that God gives a way through. Notice that He is faithful. Notice that Paul said so. 1 Corinthians 10 says, 'There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful' Notice faithful. Today, remember others share your struggle. God is faithful in it.
S: The Preacher introduced an evil he had seen under the sun, one that is common among people. Common sorrows still hurt, and God cares about everyday struggles that many share. Take heart, for there hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful.
P: Common sorrow. Ecclesiastes 6:1: 'There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it is common among men.' Faithful. There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful. You are not alone. Trust Him with it today.

== 6:2
T: God giveth him not power to eat thereof
R: 'yet God giveth him not power to eat thereof, but a stranger eateth it' Notice a man to whom God hath given. A blessed man. Notice riches, wealth, and honour. Everything. Notice so that he wanteth nothing. Lacks nothing. Notice yet God giveth him not power to eat thereof. Cannot enjoy it. Notice but a stranger eateth it. Someone else does. Notice this is vanity. Empty. Notice and it is an evil disease. A grievous ill. Notice that having is not the same as enjoying. Notice that some have everything but enjoy nothing. Notice that enjoyment is a separate gift. Notice that God gives it. Notice that we should not take it for granted. Notice that we should thank Him for the joy, not just the things. Notice that true riches are in Him. Psalm 16 says, 'The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup' Notice portion. Today, thank God for the ability to enjoy. Make Him your portion.
S: A man may have riches, wealth and honour, lacking nothing he desires, yet God does not let him enjoy them and a stranger enjoys them instead. Having is not the same as enjoying, for the joy of good things is a separate gift from God. Make Him your portion, saying, the LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup.
P: Having but not enjoying. Ecclesiastes 6:2: 'yet God giveth him not power to eat thereof.' Portion. The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup. Thank God for the gift of enjoyment. Walk in His ways today.

== 6:3
T: His soul be not filled with good
R: 'If a man beget an hundred children, and live many years, so that the days of his years be many, and his soul be not filled with good' Notice if a man beget an hundred children. A large family. Notice and live many years. A long life. Notice and his soul be not filled with good. No satisfaction. Notice and also that he have no burial. No honour at death. Notice an untimely birth is better than he. A strong comparison. Notice that a long life and big family were the highest blessings then. Notice that without a satisfied soul they are empty. Notice that God cares for every life, however short. Notice that He grieves with parents who lose a child. Notice that He comforts them. Notice that the soul needs God to be filled. Notice that He alone satisfies. Psalm 107 says, 'and filleth the hungry soul with goodness.' Notice filled. Today, ask God to fill your soul. Long life needs His goodness.
S: The Preacher said that a man may have a hundred children and live many years, yet if his soul is not satisfied and he has no burial, a stillborn child is better off. A long life and large family mean little without a satisfied soul, and God grieves with parents who lose a child. Ask Him to fill you, for He filleth the hungry soul with goodness.
P: A full soul. Ecclesiastes 6:3: 'and his soul be not filled with good.' Goodness. And filleth the hungry soul with goodness. Ask God to fill your soul today. Let this shape your day.

== 6:4
T: His name shall be covered with darkness
R: 'For he cometh in with vanity, and departeth in darkness, and his name shall be covered with darkness.' Notice for he cometh in with vanity. Arrives in emptiness. Notice and departeth in darkness. Leaves in darkness. Notice and his name. His identity. Notice shall be covered with darkness. Forgotten. Notice that the Preacher speaks of the child who never lived. Notice that it comes and goes unseen. Notice that the world never learns its name. Notice that this is a deep sorrow. Notice that God sees what the world does not. Notice that no life is hidden from Him. Notice that He knew each child in the womb. Notice that He holds the little ones. Notice that He comforts the grieving. If you carry the grief of a lost child, please share it with someone you trust. You do not have to carry it alone. Psalm 139 says, 'thou hast covered me in my mother's womb.' Notice covered. Today, remember God sees every hidden life. Comfort a grieving parent.
S: The Preacher said the stillborn child comes in emptiness, departs in darkness, and its name is covered in darkness. The world may never learn its name, but God sees every hidden life, and anyone carrying the grief of a lost child should share it with someone they trust. Take comfort that God knows each child, for thou hast covered me in my mother's womb.
P: Hidden life. Ecclesiastes 6:4: 'and his name shall be covered with darkness.' Known. Thou hast covered me in my mother's womb. God sees every hidden life. Comfort someone grieving today.

== 6:5
T: This hath more rest than the other
R: 'Moreover he hath not seen the sun, nor known any thing: this hath more rest than the other.' Notice moreover he hath not seen the sun. Never saw daylight. Notice nor known any thing. Knew nothing. Notice this hath more rest. More peace. Notice than the other. Than the unsatisfied man. Notice that the little one never knew trouble. Notice that it has rest. Notice that the restless rich man has none. Notice that the point is the misery of an empty life. Notice that restlessness is a heavy burden. Notice that many live without rest. Notice that they have much but no peace. Notice that God offers rest now. Notice that He offers it to the weary. Notice that He gives peace the world cannot give. Notice that Jesus promised it. Notice that it is His own peace. John 14 says, 'Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you' Notice my peace. Today, receive Christ's peace. Rest in Him.
S: The stillborn child never saw the sun or knew anything, yet has more rest than the restless man. The point is the misery of a life with much but no peace, for restlessness is a heavy burden. Receive the rest Jesus gives, for He said, peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you.
P: Rest. Ecclesiastes 6:5: 'this hath more rest than the other.' His peace. Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you. Receive Christ's peace today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 6:6
T: Though he live a thousand years twice told
R: 'Yea, though he live a thousand years twice told, yet hath he seen no good: do not all go to one place?' Notice though he live a thousand years. A very long life. Notice twice told. Two thousand years. Notice yet hath he seen no good. No joy. Notice do not all go to one place. The same end. Notice that length of life is not the goal. Notice that quality matters more. Notice that a long empty life is not a blessing. Notice that a short full life can be. Notice that all lives end. Notice that what matters is how we live. Notice that what matters is whom we know. Notice that eternal life is knowing God. Notice that it starts now. Notice that it never ends. Notice that Jesus defined it. John 17 says, 'And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God' Notice know thee. Today, do not just add days. Fill them with God.
S: Even if a man lived two thousand years but saw no good, all still go to the same place. Length of life is not the goal, for what matters is how we live and whom we know. Seek real life, for this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God.
P: Long life. Ecclesiastes 6:6: 'Yea, though he live a thousand years twice told, yet hath he seen no good.' Know Him. This is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God. Fill your days with God. Trust Him with it today.

== 6:7

R: 'All the labour of man is for his mouth, and yet the appetite is not filled.' Notice all the labour of man. All our work. Notice is for his mouth. To feed ourselves. Notice and yet the appetite. The desire. Notice is not filled. Is never satisfied. Notice that we work to eat. Notice that we eat and get hungry again. Notice that the cycle repeats. Notice that appetite goes deeper than food. Notice that the soul has hunger too. Notice that we try to feed it with things. Notice that things do not satisfy it. Notice that the soul needs God. Notice that He made us for Himself. Notice that Jesus is the bread of life. Notice that He satisfies the deepest hunger. Notice that those who come to Him are filled. John 6 says, 'I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger' Notice never hunger. Today, feed your body with thanks. Feed your soul with Christ.
S: All our work is for our mouths, yet the appetite is never satisfied. We eat and grow hungry again, and the soul has a deeper hunger that things cannot fill. Come to Jesus, who said, I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger.
P: Hungry. Ecclesiastes 6:7: 'All the labour of man is for his mouth, and yet the appetite is not filled.' Bread of life. I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger. Feed your soul with Christ. Walk in His ways today.

== 6:8
T: What hath the wise more than the fool
R: 'For what hath the wise more than the fool? what hath the poor, that knoweth to walk before the living?' Notice for what hath the wise. What advantage. Notice more than the fool. Over the foolish. Notice what hath the poor. What gain for the poor. Notice that knoweth to walk before the living. Who knows how to behave. Notice that the Preacher asks hard questions. Notice that both wise and fool get hungry. Notice that both poor and rich have appetites. Notice that wisdom does not remove need. Notice that good manners do not fill the soul. Notice that the question is honest. Notice that the answer is not found in comparison. Notice that it is found in God. Notice that He gives the poor riches of faith. Notice that He honours the humble. Notice that He chooses the lowly. James 2 says, 'Hath not God chosen the poor of this world rich in faith' Notice rich in faith. Today, stop comparing. Be rich toward God.
S: The Preacher asked what advantage the wise have over the fool, and what the poor gain by knowing how to conduct themselves. Wisdom and good manners do not remove our deepest needs, and the answer is found in God rather than in comparison. Be rich toward Him, for hath not God chosen the poor of this world rich in faith?
P: Comparison. Ecclesiastes 6:8: 'For what hath the wise more than the fool?' Rich in faith. Hath not God chosen the poor of this world rich in faith? Stop comparing and trust God. Let this shape your day.

P: Content. Ecclesiastes 6:9: 'Better is the sight of the eyes than the wandering of the desire.' Learned. I have learned, in whatsoever state I am, therewith to be content. Enjoy what is before you. Take it to God in prayer.

== 6:10

Already known. Notice and it is known that it is man. Humanity is known. Notice neither may he contend. He cannot argue. Notice with him that is mightier than he. With God. Notice that God has already named all things. Notice that He knows what man is. Notice that we cannot argue with Him. Notice that He is mightier. Notice that this calls for humility. Notice that we can bring Him our questions. Notice that we cannot overrule Him. Notice that the clay does not argue with the potter. Notice that the potter shapes with care. Isaiah 64 says, 'we are the clay, and thou our potter; and we all are the work of thy hand.' Notice the potter. Today, submit to God's shaping. He is mightier and good.
S: Whatever exists has already been named, and what people are is known, so no one can contend with One who is stronger. God knows us fully, and we can bring Him our questions without overruling Him. Submit to His shaping, for we are the clay, and thou our potter; and we all are the work of thy hand.
P: Mightier. Ecclesiastes 6:10: 'neither may he contend with him that is mightier than he.' The potter. We are the clay, and thou our potter. Submit to God's shaping. Trust Him with it today.

== 6:12

Notice in this life. On earth. Notice all the days of his vain life. His brief days. Notice which he spendeth as a shadow. Passing like a shadow. Notice for who can tell a man. Who can say. Notice what shall be after him. The future. Notice that life is like a shadow. Notice that we do not know what is best. Notice that we cannot see the future. Notice that God knows both. Notice that we can trust Him to guide us. Notice that we should not lean on our own understanding. Proverbs 3 says, 'Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.' Notice trust. Today, ask God what is good. Let Him guide your days.
S: The Preacher asked who knows what is good for a person during the few days of their fleeting life, which pass like a shadow, and who can tell them what will come after. We cannot see what is best or what lies ahead, but God knows both and guides us. Trust Him, for trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.
P: Who knows. Ecclesiastes 6:12: 'For who knoweth what is good for man in this life?' Trust. Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. Let God guide your days. Let this shape your day.
