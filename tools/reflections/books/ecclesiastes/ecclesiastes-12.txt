# Ecclesiastes 12: Remember now thy Creator

== 12:1
T: Remember now thy Creator in the days of thy youth
R: 'Remember now thy Creator in the days of thy youth, while the evil days come not' Notice remember now. Remember today. Notice thy Creator. The One who made you. Notice in the days of thy youth. While young. Notice while the evil days come not. Before hard days. Notice nor the years draw nigh. Before old age. Notice when thou shalt say, I have no pleasure in them. When life feels hard. Notice that the Preacher now gives his great call. Notice that it is to remember God. Notice that remembering means more than recalling. Notice that it means living for Him. Notice that youth is the best time to start. Notice that habits formed early last. Notice that it is never too late. Notice that God is our Creator. Notice that Timothy knew Scripture from a child. 2 Timothy 3 says, 'And that from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures' Notice from a child. Today, remember your Creator. Start now, whatever your age.
S: Remember your Creator in the days of your youth, before the days of trouble come and the years approach when you will say you find no pleasure in them. Remembering God means living for Him, and youth is the best time to begin, though it is never too late. Start early, as Timothy did, for from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures.
P: Remember. Ecclesiastes 12:1: 'Remember now thy Creator in the days of thy youth.' From a child. And that from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures. Remember your Creator today. Trust Him with it today.

Notice and those that look out of the windows. The eyes. Notice be darkened. Grow dim. Notice that the body is pictured as a house. Notice that age weakens it. Notice that hands shake. Notice that legs bend. Notice that teeth go. Notice that eyes dim. Notice that this is not to frighten us. Notice that it is to make us wise. Notice that the outward body fades. Notice that the inner life can grow. 2 Corinthians 4 says, 'yet the inward man is renewed day by day.' Notice renewed. Today, care for your body. Let your inner life grow stronger.
S: Remember Him before the keepers of the house tremble and the strong men stoop, before the grinders cease because they are few and those looking through the windows grow dim. The body is pictured as a house weakened by age, with shaking hands, bending legs, few teeth and dim eyes. Take heart, for though the body fades, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.
