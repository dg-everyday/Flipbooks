R: 'And the saying pleased the king and the princes; and the king did according to the word of Memucan:' Notice and the saying pleased the king. Approval. Notice and the princes. Agreement. Notice and the king did. Action. Notice according to the word of Memucan. His advice. Notice that the advice pleased them. Notice that pleasing is not the same as right. Notice that they liked what protected their pride. Notice that no one questioned it. Notice that groupthink took over. Notice that consensus can be wrong. Notice that popular decisions need examination. Notice that the king acted quickly. Notice that he would later regret it. Notice that pleasing advice is dangerous. Notice that truth sometimes displeases. Notice that wisdom weighs before acting. Proverbs 14 says, 'There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.' Notice the way that seems right. Today, test pleasing advice by God's word. What seems right may not be.
S: The king and his nobles were pleased with this advice, so the king did as Memukan proposed. They liked advice that protected their pride, but what pleases is not always what is right. Test pleasing advice by God's word, for there is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.
P: Pleasing advice. Esther 1:21: 'And the saying pleased the king and the princes.' Seeming right. A way which seemeth right unto a man.

== 1:22
T: Bear rule in his own house
R: 'that every man should bear rule in his own house,' Notice that letters went to all the king's provinces. Communication. Notice that each was written in its own script. Translation. Notice that each people received it in their language. Reach. Notice that every man should bear rule in his own house. The decree. Notice that it was published in every language. Thoroughness. Notice that the empire was told to enforce male rule. Notice that the decree came from wounded pride. Notice that it sought control through law. Notice that Christian homes are built on love. Notice that leadership in the home is service. Notice that the Persian decree was hollow. Notice that laws cannot create loving homes. Notice that only grace can. Notice that Christ transforms relationships. Notice that tenderness reflects Him. Notice that homes flourish with kindness. Colossians 3 says, 'Husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them.' Notice love without bitterness. Today, build your home on love, not control. Laws cannot create loving homes.
S: The king sent dispatches to all parts of the kingdom, to each province in its own script and each people in its own language, proclaiming that every man should be ruler over his own household. A decree born of wounded pride sought control through law, but only love and grace build a godly home. Build your home on love, for husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them.
P: Rule decreed. Esther 1:22: 'that every man should bear rule in his own house.' Love instead. Husbands, love your wives. Take it to God in prayer.
