# Exodus 26: Curtains, boards and the veil

== 26:1
T: Ten curtains of fine linen
R: 'Moreover thou shalt make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet: with cherubims of cunning work shalt thou make them.' The innermost covering of the tabernacle would be ten curtains of fine linen, woven with blue, purple, and scarlet, with cherubim skillfully embroidered. This was the ceiling the priests would see from inside. The most beautiful layer was on the inside, visible only to those who entered. From the outside, the tabernacle looked plain. Inside, it was glorious. God's deepest beauty is seen by those who come close. The cherubim woven into the ceiling recalled heaven. Priests serving inside would look up and see angelic figures. The tabernacle was a small picture of heaven on earth. Psalm 27 says, One thing have I desired… to behold the beauty of the LORD, and to enquire in his temple. Today, draw near to God. His greatest beauty is revealed to those who enter His presence.
S: The tabernacle's innermost covering was ten curtains of fine twined linen in blue, purple and scarlet, with cherubims skillfully worked. Its greatest beauty was visible only from inside. Draw near to God, for His deepest beauty is revealed to those who enter His presence.
P: Beauty inside. Exodus 26:1: 'ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet.' The glory was seen from within. Draw near to God and behold His beauty.

== 26:2
T: One measure
R: 'The length of one curtain shall be eight and twenty cubits, and the breadth of one curtain four cubits: and every one of the curtains shall have one measure.' Each curtain was the same size: twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide. Every curtain had one measure. Uniformity allowed them to fit together perfectly. Notice the phrase one measure. No curtain was larger or more important than another. Each played the same role. Together they formed a complete covering. This pictures equality among God's people. In Christ, no believer is more valuable than another. Galatians 3 says, ye are all one in Christ Jesus. We have different gifts, but equal standing. Precise measurements also show God's care for order. Nothing was haphazard. Everything fit as designed. First Corinthians 14 says, Let all things be done decently and in order. Today, rejoice in your equal standing before God. You are not more or less than other believers. Together, you form His dwelling.
S: Each tabernacle curtain was twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide, and all had one measure. Equal size allowed them to fit together perfectly, picturing equal standing among God's people. Rejoice in your equal standing before God and your place in His dwelling.

== 26:3

R: 'The five curtains shall be coupled together one to another; and other five curtains shall be coupled one to another.' The ten curtains were joined into two sets of five. Each set was coupled together, curtain to curtain. They would then be joined into one. Individual curtains alone could not cover the tabernacle. They had to be joined. Their strength and purpose came from being connected. This pictures the body of Christ. One believer alone cannot fulfill God's purposes. We need to be joined together. Ephesians 2 says, In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord. We are being built together. Notice coupled… one to another. Each curtain was connected to the next. Relationships among believers matter. We are not just connected to God but to each other. Hebrews 10 says, Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together. Today, value your connections with other believers. You are not meant to stand alone. Together, you form God's dwelling place.
S: The ten curtains were coupled together in two sets of five, each joined one to another. Individual curtains fulfilled their purpose only when connected, picturing the body of Christ. Value your connections with other believers, for together you form God's dwelling.

T: One tabernacle

Fifty gold clasps would join the two sets of curtains through the loops. When fastened, it would be one tabernacle. Many pieces became a single dwelling. The goal was unity: one tabernacle. Not two halves, but one whole. God's dwelling was unified. The gold clasps made it complete. Gold represents divine value. What joins God's people is precious. John 17 records Jesus praying, That they all may be one… that the world may believe. Our unity is a witness to the world. Notice that the clasps were placed where the veil would hang, at the boundary between the Holy Place and Most Holy Place. Unity was established at the place nearest God. The closer we draw to God, the more united we become with one another. Today, pursue unity with God's people. Let what joins you be more precious than what divides you. Together, you are one dwelling for God.
