# Exodus 36: More than enough; the tabernacle structure made

== 36:1
T: Then wrought Bezaleel
R: 'Then wrought Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every wise hearted man, in whom the LORD put wisdom and understanding to know how to work all manner of work for the service of the sanctuary.' Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled worker began the work. God had given them wisdom and understanding to do everything for the sanctuary. The building finally started. Notice then wrought. After all the instructions, the planning, and the offerings, work began. Planning is necessary, but at some point we must start. Many good intentions never move beyond planning. Notice also in whom the LORD put wisdom. Their ability came from God. They worked with skill He supplied. Their success depended on Him. Nehemiah 2 records the people saying, Let us rise up and build. So they strengthened their hands for this good work. Today, move from planning to doing. Start the work God has called you to, trusting Him to supply the wisdom you need.

Notice also to come unto the work to do it. Willingness was not enough; they had to show up and work. Today, pay attention to both God's call and your heart's stirring. When they align, step forward and do the work.
