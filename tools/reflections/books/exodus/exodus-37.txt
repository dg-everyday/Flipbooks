# Exodus 37: The ark, table, lampstand and incense altar made

== 37:1
T: Bezaleel made the ark
R: 'And Bezaleel made the ark of shittim wood: two cubits and a half was the length of it, and a cubit and a half the breadth of it.' Bezalel himself made the ark of acacia wood. The most sacred object was crafted by the chief craftsman. He did not delegate the heart of the work. Notice that Bezalel made the ark personally. Others made curtains and boards, but the ark was his task. The most important work received the most skilled attention. Leaders sometimes delegate everything. But some tasks deserve personal care. The ark was the center of God's dwelling. Bezalel gave it his own hands. God's presence deserves our best and most personal devotion. We should not hand off our relationship with God to others. Psalm 27 says, One thing have I desired of the LORD, that will I seek after. Today, give personal attention to what matters most. Do not delegate your devotion to God. Let Him have your own hands and heart.
S: Bezaleel himself made the ark of shittim wood, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half broad. The most sacred object received the chief craftsman's personal attention. Give personal attention to what matters most and do not delegate your devotion to God.

Integrity means being the same inside and out. God sees what others do not. The hidden parts of our lives matter to Him as much as the visible. Psalm 51 says, thou desirest truth in the inward parts. Matthew 23 records Jesus rebuking those who cleaned the outside of the cup while the inside was full of corruption. God wants consistency. Today, let your inner life be as pure as your outer life. What no one else sees, God sees. Let it be golden.
