# Exodus 40: The tabernacle set up; the glory of the LORD fills it

== 40:1
T: God speaks to complete
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' With all the parts finished, the LORD spoke to Moses again. Now He would give instructions for setting up the tabernacle. The final step was at hand. God was involved from beginning to end. He gave the design, inspired the workers, and now directed the assembly. Nothing was left to human guesswork. Philippians 1 says, he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ. God finishes what He starts. Notice that God spoke at each stage. He did not give all instructions at once and then step back. He remained present, guiding each phase. Our walk with God is similar. He speaks to us at each stage of life. Psalm 32 says, I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye. Today, trust God to guide you through every stage, from beginning to completion. He is with you in the final steps too.
S: With all the parts finished, the LORD spoke to Moses again to direct the tabernacle's assembly. God was involved from beginning to end, guiding each stage. Trust God to guide you through every stage of life, from beginning to completion.
P: God guides to the end. Exodus 40:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' He gave the design and now directed the finish. He's with you in every stage.

== 40:2
T: The first day of the first month
R: 'On the first day of the first month shalt thou set up the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation.' God told Moses to set up the tabernacle on the first day of the first month. It would mark a new beginning. Nearly a year after leaving Egypt, God's dwelling would stand among them. Notice the date. The first day of the first month was New Year's Day in Israel's calendar. It was also near the anniversary of the exodus. A new year would begin with God dwelling among His people. New beginnings are a gift from God. He gives fresh starts. Lamentations 3 says, his compassions fail not. They are new every morning. Second Corinthians 5 says, if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new. Notice that the timing was God's. He chose the day. His timing is perfect. Today, embrace the new beginning God offers. Let Him dwell at the center of your new season.
S: God told Moses to set up the tabernacle on the first day of the first month. The new year would begin with God dwelling among His people. Embrace the new beginning God offers and let Him dwell at the center of your new season.
P: A new beginning. Exodus 40:2: 'On the first day of the first month shalt thou set up the tabernacle.' God gives fresh starts. Let Him be at the center of your new season.

The center was set before the surroundings. This teaches priorities. God must be at the center first. When He is in His rightful place, everything else finds its order. It was not casually displayed. Reverence surrounded the ark. Today, put God first in your life. Establish His throne at your center before arranging everything else.
