# Ezekiel 2: Be not afraid of their words

== 2:1
T: Son of man, stand upon thy feet
R: 'And he said unto me, Son of man, stand upon thy feet, and I will speak unto thee.' Notice and he said unto me, Son of man. He said to me, son of man. Notice stand upon thy feet, and I will speak unto thee. Stand up on your feet and I will speak to you. Notice that Ezekiel had fallen down. Notice that God tells him to stand. Notice that God calls him son of man. Notice that this name stresses his humanity. Notice that he is mortal. Notice that standing up comes next. Notice that God raises those who humble themselves. Notice that He gives them a task. Notice that Paul had a similar experience. Notice that he fell on the road. Notice that Jesus told him to rise. Acts 26 says, 'But rise, and stand upon thy feet: for I have appeared unto thee for this purpose' Notice for this purpose. Today, rise from your humility ready to hear God's purpose for you.
S: God said, son of man, stand up on your feet and I will speak to you. Ezekiel had fallen in awe, and God, calling him a mere mortal, lifted him to stand and hear, for He raises the humble and gives them a purpose. Rise ready to hear, as Jesus told Paul, but rise, and stand upon thy feet: for I have appeared unto thee for this purpose.
P: Stand up. Ezekiel 2:1: 'Son of man, stand upon thy feet, and I will speak unto thee.' For this purpose. Rise, and stand upon thy feet. Hear God's purpose today. Take it to God in prayer.

Rely on that power, for ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you.

Notice that they are rebellious. Notice that many did not receive Him. Notice that He came anyway. Notice that some received Him. Notice that they became God's children. Notice that John described this. John 1 says, 'He came unto his own, and his own received him not.' Notice his own received him not. Today, be faithful even among people who resist God's message.
S: God said, son of man, I am sending you to the Israelites, a rebellious nation that has rebelled against Me, and the verse adds that they and their ancestors have revolted to this very day. God sent Ezekiel to his own long-rebellious people, for God does not give up on rebels but keeps sending messengers, as He sent Jesus. Be faithful among those who resist, for He came unto his own, and his own received him not.
P: Sent to rebels. Ezekiel 2:3: 'I send thee to the children of Israel, to a rebellious nation.' His own. He came unto his own, and his own received him not. Be faithful today. Let this shape your day.

== 2:4
T: Impudent children and stiffhearted
R: 'For they are impudent children and stiffhearted. I do send thee unto them; and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD.' Notice for they are impudent children and stiffhearted. The people to whom I am sending you are obstinate and stubborn. Notice I do send thee unto them. I am sending you to them. Notice and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD. Say to them, this is what the Sovereign LORD says. Notice that the people are obstinate. Notice that they are hard-faced. Notice that they are hard-hearted. Notice that Ezekiel's message is simple. Notice that it is thus saith the Lord GOD. Notice that he spoke to stubborn leaders. Notice that their fathers did the same. Notice that he named it plainly. Acts 7 says, 'Ye stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Ghost' Notice ye do always resist. Today, guard against a stubborn heart, and speak God's word faithfully.
S: The people God sent Ezekiel to are obstinate and stubborn, and he was to say to them, this is what the Sovereign LORD says. His authority was not persuasion but faithfulness to God's word, spoken to hard hearts that have long resisted, as Stephen also faced. Guard against stubbornness, unlike those of whom it was said, ye stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Ghost.
P: Stubborn hearts. Ezekiel 2:4: 'For they are impudent children and stiffhearted.' Stiffnecked. Ye stiffnecked, ye do always resist. Keep a soft heart today. Take it to God in prayer.

Do not fear, for Jesus said, behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions.

Notice that Ezekiel speaks God's words. Notice that they are not his own. Notice that the audience may refuse. Notice that he speaks anyway. Notice that God tests the heart. Notice that He knows motives. Notice that He entrusts the message to the faithful. Notice that Paul spoke this way. Notice that he was entrusted with the gospel. Notice that he did not flatter. 1 Thessalonians 2 says, 'so we speak; not as pleasing men, but God, which trieth our hearts.' Notice not as pleasing men. Today, speak God's truth to please Him, not to win human approval.
S: You must speak My words to them, whether they listen or fail to listen, for they are rebellious. Ezekiel spoke God's words rather than his own, aiming to please God rather than his audience, for pleasing people corrupts the message. Speak to please Him, as Paul said, so we speak; not as pleasing men, but God, which trieth our hearts.
P: God's words. Ezekiel 2:7: 'thou shalt speak my words unto them.' Not pleasing men. Not as pleasing men, but God. Please God today. Let this shape your day.
