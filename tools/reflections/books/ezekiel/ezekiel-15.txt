# Ezekiel 15: The vine tree

== 15:1
T: The word of the LORD came unto me
R: 'And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying' Notice and the word of the LORD came unto me. The word of the LORD came to me. Notice saying. Saying. Notice that God now uses a parable. Notice that it is about a vine. Notice that Israel was often called God's vine. Notice that He planted it. Notice that He cared for it. Notice that He expected fruit. Notice that a vine exists for fruit. Notice that this is its whole purpose. Notice that Jesus took up this picture. Notice that He called Himself the true vine. Notice that where Israel failed, He succeeded. Notice that He bears the fruit God desired. Notice that believers are branches in Him. Notice that the Father tends the vine. John 15 says, 'I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman.' Notice I am the true vine. Today, stay connected to Christ, the true vine.
S: The word of the LORD came to Ezekiel. God now gave a parable of a vine, the picture of Israel, planted and tended for the fruit it was meant to bear. Stay connected to Christ, who succeeded where Israel failed, for He said, I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman.
P: The vine. Ezekiel 15:1: 'the word of the LORD came unto me.' True vine. I am the true vine. Abide in Christ today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 15:2
T: What is the vine tree more than any tree
R: 'Son of man, What is the vine tree more than any tree, or than a branch which is among the trees of the forest?' Notice son of man, What is the vine tree more than any tree. Son of man, how is the wood of a vine different from that of any other tree. Notice or than a branch which is among the trees of the forest. Or from a branch in the forest. Notice that vine wood is not special. Notice that it is crooked. Notice that it is weak. Notice that a vine's only value is its fruit. Notice that without fruit it is worthless. Notice that Israel's value was not in itself. Notice that our value comes from union with God. Notice that apart from Him we cannot bear fruit. Notice that Jesus said so plainly. Notice that branches depend on the vine. John 15 says, 'for without me ye can do nothing.' Notice without me. Today, remember that your fruitfulness depends entirely on Christ.
S: God asked how the wood of a vine is any different from that of any other tree or forest branch. Vine wood is crooked and weak, valuable only for its fruit, just as Israel's worth lay in what God did through it. Depend on Christ for fruit, for He said, without me ye can do nothing.
P: Only for fruit. Ezekiel 15:2: 'What is the vine tree more than any tree?' Without Me. Without me ye can do nothing. Depend on Christ today. Trust Him with it today.
H: #DailyGrace #Ezekiel152 #Dependence #Fruit #Christ #BibleVerse

== 15:3
T: Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work
R: 'Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work? or will men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?' Notice shall wood be taken thereof to do any work. Is wood ever taken from it to make anything useful. Notice or will men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon. Do people make pegs from it to hang things on. Notice that vine wood is useless for building. Notice that it cannot even make a peg. Notice that its purpose is fruit alone. Notice that God's people are made to bear fruit. Notice that this fruit is character. Notice that it is love. Notice that it is joy. Notice that it is peace. Notice that it is produced by the Spirit. Notice that Paul listed this fruit. Galatians 5 says, 'But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith' Notice the fruit of the Spirit. Today, ask the Spirit to grow His fruit in you.
S: God asked whether wood from a vine is ever used to make anything useful, even a peg to hang things on. Vine wood is useless for building, made for fruit alone, just as God's people are made to bear the fruit of His Spirit. Ask Him to grow it in you, for the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith.
P: Made for fruit. Ezekiel 15:3: 'Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work?' Fruit of the Spirit. The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace. Bear fruit today. Let this shape your day.

== 15:4
T: It is cast into the fire for fuel
R: 'Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel; the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned.' Notice behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel. And after it is thrown on the fire as fuel. Notice the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned. And the fire burns both ends and chars the middle. Notice is it meet for any work. Is it then useful for anything. Notice that fruitless vine wood is burned. Notice that both ends are consumed. Notice that the middle is charred. Notice that this pictures Jerusalem. Notice that it had already been partly burned. Notice that Jesus warned of this. Notice that branches must abide. Notice that cut-off branches wither. John 15 says, 'If a man abide not in me, he is cast forth as a branch, and is withered' Notice cast forth as a branch. Today, abide in Christ so that your life bears lasting fruit.
S: After vine wood is thrown on the fire as fuel and both ends burn and the middle is charred, it is useful for nothing. This pictured Jerusalem, already partly burned with more fire to come, for fruitlessness has consequences. Abide in Christ, for if a man abide not in me, he is cast forth as a branch, and is withered.
P: Into the fire. Ezekiel 15:4: 'it is cast into the fire for fuel.' Abide. If a man abide not in me, he is cast forth. Abide in Christ today. Take it to God in prayer.
H: #DailyGrace #Ezekiel154 #Abiding #Fruit #Warning #BibleVerse

== 15:5
T: When it was whole, it was meet for no work
R: 'Behold, when it was whole, it was meet for no work: how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned?' Notice behold, when it was whole, it was meet for no work. If it was not useful for anything when it was whole. Notice how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned. How much less can it be made into something useful when the fire has burned it and it is charred. Notice that even whole, it was not useful for building. Notice that God had done everything for His vine. Notice that He had cleared the ground. Notice that Isaiah sang of this. Isaiah 5 says, 'What could have been done more to my vineyard, that I have not done in it?' Notice what could have been done more. Today, respond to all God has done for you with fruitful love.
S: If vine wood was useless when whole, how much less useful would it be after fire had charred it. Israel had failed even in good times, though God had done everything for His vine and waited for fruit. Respond to His care, for He asked, what could have been done more to my vineyard, that I have not done in it.
P: All God has done. Ezekiel 15:5: 'when it was whole, it was meet for no work.' What more. What could have been done more to my vineyard. Respond to God today. Trust Him with it today.

== 15:6
T: So will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem
R: 'As the vine tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem.' Notice therefore thus saith the Lord GOD. Therefore this is what the Sovereign LORD says. Notice as the vine tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel. As I have given the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest as fuel for the fire. Notice so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem. So will I treat the people living in Jerusalem. Notice that the parable is explained. Notice that Jerusalem is the vine. Notice that it bore no fruit. Notice that the axe was already at the root. Notice that fruitless trees are cut down. Matthew 3 says, 'therefore every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.' Notice good fruit. Today, let repentance show itself in good fruit.
S: This is what the Sovereign LORD says, as I have given the wood of the vine to the fire for fuel, so will I treat the people of Jerusalem. The parable was explained, for Jerusalem was the fruitless vine, and God looks for real fruit. Let your repentance bear it, for every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.
P: The vine explained. Ezekiel 15:6: 'so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem.' Good fruit. Every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down. Bear good fruit today. Let this shape your day.

== 15:7
T: They shall go out from one fire
R: 'they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them' Notice and I will set my face against them. I will set My face against them. Notice they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them. Although they have come out of the fire, the fire will yet consume them. Notice and ye shall know that I am the LORD, when I set my face against them. And when I set My face against them, you will know that I am the LORD. Notice that escaping one disaster leads to another. Notice that there is no way out apart from God. Notice that running does not help. Notice that Amos used a vivid picture. Notice that a man fled a lion and met a bear. Notice that escape apart from God fails. Amos 5 says, 'As if a man did flee from a lion, and a bear met him' Notice a bear met him. Today, stop running from trouble to trouble and turn to God.
S: God would set His face against them, and although they had come out of one fire, another would consume them, and they would know that He is the LORD. Escaping one disaster only led to another, for there is no way out apart from turning to God. Stop running and turn, for running alone is as if a man did flee from a lion, and a bear met him.
P: From fire to fire. Ezekiel 15:7: 'they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them.' A bear. As if a man did flee from a lion. Turn to God today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 15:8
T: Because they have committed a trespass
R: 'And I will make the land desolate, because they have committed a trespass, saith the Lord GOD.' Notice and I will make the land desolate. I will make the land desolate. Notice because they have committed a trespass, saith the Lord GOD. Because they have been unfaithful, declares the Sovereign LORD. Notice that the reason is unfaithfulness. Notice that the word means breaking trust. Notice that it is covenant betrayal. Notice that the land suffers for it. Notice that unfaithfulness costs much. Notice that faithfulness is precious. Notice that God remains faithful. Notice that He calls the unfaithful back. Notice that He forgives those who confess. Notice that He cleanses. Notice that this is His promise. Notice that John declared it. Notice that confession opens the door. 1 John 1 says, 'If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins' Notice faithful and just to forgive. Today, confess any unfaithfulness to God and receive His forgiveness.
S: God would make the land desolate because the people had been unfaithful. Their trespass was covenant betrayal and it cost the land dearly, yet God remains faithful and calls the unfaithful back. Confess and receive His forgiveness, for if we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins.
P: Unfaithfulness. Ezekiel 15:8: 'because they have committed a trespass.' Forgive. He is faithful and just to forgive us. Confess today. Trust Him with it today.
