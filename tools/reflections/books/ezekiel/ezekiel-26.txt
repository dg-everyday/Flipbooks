R: 'though thou be sought for, yet shalt thou never be found again, saith the Lord GOD.' Notice I will make thee a terror, and thou shalt be no more. I will bring you to a horrible end and you will be no more. Notice though thou be sought for, yet shalt thou never be found again, saith the Lord GOD. You will be sought, but you will never again be found, declares the Sovereign LORD. Notice that Tyre disappears. Notice that people search for it. Notice that it is gone. Notice that God's mercy does not fade. Notice that it is everlasting. Notice that it rests on those who fear Him. Notice that it reaches generations. Notice that this is the contrast. Notice that the psalmist set them side by side. Psalm 103 says, 'But the mercy of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear him' Notice from everlasting to everlasting. Today, build your life on God's everlasting mercy rather than passing greatness.
S: God would bring Tyre to a horrible end, and though it was sought, it would never again be found. Human greatness passes like grass, while God's mercy lasts forever for those who fear Him. Build on that mercy, for the mercy of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear him.
P: Never found. Ezekiel 26:21: 'yet shalt thou never be found again.' Everlasting. The mercy of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting. Build on God's mercy today. Let this shape your day.
