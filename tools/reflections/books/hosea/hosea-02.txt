== 2:23
T: Thou art my people
R: 'and I will say to them which were not my people, Thou art my people; and they shall say, Thou art my God.' Notice and I will sow her unto me in the earth. I will plant her for Myself in the land. Notice and I will have mercy upon her that had not obtained mercy. I will show My love to the one I called Not my loved one. Notice and I will say to them which were not my people, Thou art my people. I will say to those called Not my people, You are My people. Notice and they shall say, Thou art my God. And they will say, You are my God. Notice that every sad name is reversed. Notice that the covenant is complete. Notice that this is the gospel. Notice that Peter quoted it. 1 Peter 2 says, 'which had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.' Notice but now have obtained mercy. Today, answer God's love with, You are my God.
S: The LORD said He would plant His people for Himself, show love to the one called Not loved and say to those called Not my people, You are My people, and they would answer, You are my God. Every sad name was reversed, and outsiders received mercy and belonging. Answer His love with faith, for we which had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.
