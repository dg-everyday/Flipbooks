# Hosea 3: According to the love of the LORD

== 3:1
T: According to the love of the LORD
R: 'Then said the LORD unto me, Go yet, love a woman' Notice then said the LORD unto me, Go yet, love a woman. The LORD said to me, Go, show your love to your wife again. Notice that the verse says she was loved by another and had been unfaithful. Though she has been with another and has broken faith. Notice according to the love of the LORD toward the children of Israel. Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites. Notice who look to other gods. Though they turn to other gods. Notice go yet. Notice that he must go after his wife. Notice that this is how God loves. Notice that He loves the unfaithful. Notice that He pursues. Notice that His love is not earned. Notice that it is the love of the gospel. 1 John 4 says, 'not that we loved God, but that he loved us' Notice but that he loved us. Today, receive God's pursuing love and show it to others.
S: The LORD told Hosea to go and show his love to his wife again, though she had broken faith, loving her as the LORD loves the Israelites though they turn to other gods. Hosea's renewed love pictured God's undeserved, pursuing love for the unfaithful. Receive it and share it, for herein is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us.
P: Love again. Hosea 3:1: 'Go yet, love a woman.' He loved us. Not that we loved God, but that he loved us. Show undeserved love today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 3:2
T: So I bought her to me
R: 'So I bought her to me for fifteen pieces of silver, and for an homer of barley, and an half homer of barley' Notice so I bought her to me for fifteen pieces of silver. So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver. Notice and for an homer of barley, and an half homer of barley. And about a homer and a lethek of barley. Notice that Hosea pays to bring her back. Notice that she had fallen into bondage. Notice that he redeems her. Notice that redemption costs something. Notice that love pays the price. Notice that this pictures God's redemption. Notice that He paid for us. Notice that the price was not silver. Notice that it was precious blood. Notice that we are bought back. Notice that Peter declared this. 1 Peter 1 says, 'Forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold' Notice not redeemed with corruptible things. Today, thank God for the price He paid to redeem you.
S: Hosea bought his wife back for fifteen shekels of silver and some barley. He paid to redeem her from bondage, picturing how God's love pays the price to bring us back. Thank Him, for ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, but with the precious blood of Christ.
P: Bought back. Hosea 3:2: 'So I bought her to me.' Redeemed. Not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold. Thank God for redemption today. Trust Him with it today.

== 3:3
T: So will I also be for thee
R: 'And I said unto her, Thou shalt abide for me many days' Notice and I said unto her, Thou shalt abide for me many days. Then I told her, You are to live with me many days. Notice that he asks her to be faithful to him alone. You must not be unfaithful or belong to another. Notice so will I also be for thee. And I will behave the same way toward you. Notice that faithfulness is mutual. Notice that Hosea commits too. Notice that he asks for faithfulness. Notice that he gives it. Notice that there is a season of waiting. Notice that trust is rebuilt over time. Notice that restoration takes patience. Notice that God commits Himself to us. Notice that He asks for our faithfulness. Notice that He remains faithful even when we are not. 2 Timothy 2 says, 'If we believe not, yet he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.' Notice yet he abideth faithful. Today, commit yourself faithfully to God, who is faithful to you.
S: Hosea told his wife she was to live with him many days, faithful to him alone, and he would be the same toward her. Faithfulness was mutual, and trust was rebuilt patiently over time. Commit yourself to God, for if we believe not, yet he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.
P: Faithful to each other. Hosea 3:3: 'Thou shalt abide for me many days.' Faithful God. Yet he abideth faithful. Be faithful to God today. Let this shape your day.

== 3:4
T: Many days without a king
R: 'For the children of Israel shall abide many days without a king, and without a prince, and without a sacrifice' Notice for the children of Israel shall abide many days without a king. For the Israelites will live many days without king. Notice and without a prince, and without a sacrifice. Or prince, without sacrifice. Notice and without an image, and without an ephod, and without teraphim. Or sacred stones, without ephod or household gods. Notice that Israel will go without. Notice that both true and false supports are removed. Notice that this is a season of waiting. Notice that it strips away distractions. Notice that it prepares for return. Notice that emptiness can lead to God. Notice that we wait for Him. Notice that our soul waits. Notice that the psalmist waited too. Psalm 130 says, 'My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning' Notice my soul waiteth for the Lord. Today, wait on God in seasons when supports are removed.
S: The Israelites would live many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or sacred stone, without ephod or household gods. Both true and false supports were removed in a season of waiting that prepared them to return. Wait on God in such seasons, for my soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning.
P: Without. Hosea 3:4: 'many days without a king.' Waiting. My soul waiteth for the Lord. Wait on God today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 3:5
T: Fear the LORD and his goodness
R: 'Afterward shall the children of Israel return, and seek the LORD their God, and David their king' Notice afterward shall the children of Israel return. Afterward the Israelites will return. Notice and seek the LORD their God, and David their king. And seek the LORD their God and David their king. Notice and shall fear the LORD and his goodness in the latter days. They will come trembling to the LORD and to His blessings in the last days. Notice afterward. Notice that waiting ends in return. Notice that they seek God. Notice that they seek David's king. Notice that this points to Christ, the Son of David. Notice that they come trembling. Notice that they come to His goodness. Notice that God's goodness draws them. Notice that reverence and goodness go together. Notice that God is good. Notice that the psalmist tasted it. Psalm 34 says, 'O taste and see that the LORD is good' Notice the LORD is good. Today, return to the LORD and His goodness.
S: Afterward the Israelites would return and seek the LORD their God and David their king, coming in reverence to the LORD and His goodness in the last days. The waiting would end in return to God and to the Son of David, drawn by His goodness. Return to Him, for O taste and see that the LORD is good.
P: Returning. Hosea 3:5: 'fear the LORD and his goodness.' Good. O taste and see that the LORD is good. Return to God today. Trust Him with it today.
