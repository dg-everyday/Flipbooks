# Hosea 6: Come, and let us return unto the LORD

Notice for he hath torn, and he will heal us. He has torn us to pieces but He will heal us. Notice he hath smitten, and he will bind us up. He has injured us but He will bind up our wounds. Notice that the people call each other back. Notice that they say let us. Notice that return is shared. Notice that the One who wounded will heal. Notice that discipline is not the end. Notice that healing follows. Notice that God binds wounds. Notice that He restores. Notice that return is the way. Notice that it is always open. Notice that Lamentations urged this. Lamentations 3 says, 'Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the LORD.' Notice turn again to the LORD. Today, encourage someone to return to the LORD with you.
S: The people called one another to return to the LORD, for He had torn them but would heal them, injured them but would bind up their wounds. Discipline is not the end, for the One who wounds also heals and restores. Return together, for let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the LORD.
P: Let us return. Hosea 6:1: 'Come, and let us return unto the LORD.' Turning again. Let us … turn again to the LORD. Return to God today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 6:3

Let us acknowledge the LORD; let us press on to acknowledge Him. Notice his going forth is prepared as the morning. As surely as the sun rises, He will appear. Notice and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth. He will come to us like the winter rains, like the spring rains that water the earth. Notice follow on to know. Notice that knowing God is a pursuit. Notice that God is as sure as morning. Notice that He comes like rain. Notice that He refreshes. Notice that He revives dry ground. Notice that Paul pressed on too. Philippians 3 says, 'That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection' Notice that I may know him. Today, press on to know the LORD more.
S: The people urged one another to press on to know the LORD, for He will come as surely as the morning and refresh them like the winter and spring rains. P: Pressing on. Hosea 6:3: 'if we follow on to know the LORD.' Knowing Him. That I may know him. Press on to know God today. Let this shape your day.

== 6:4

Notice that He wants faithfulness. Notice that He wants roots. Notice that devotion should endure. Notice that shallow faith withers. Notice that Jesus described this. Their devotion was shallow and did not last, while God wants steady, rooted love. Ask Him for lasting love, unlike the one who hath not root in himself, but dureth for a while.
P: Morning mist. Hosea 6:4: 'your goodness is as a morning cloud.' Rooted. Hath he not root in himself. Ask for lasting love today. Take it to God in prayer.
