== 8:7

Empty choices bring multiplied consequences and an empty harvest. Sow good, for he that soweth to the Spirit shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.
P: Sowing wind. Hosea 8:7: 'they have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind.' Sowing to the Spirit. He that soweth to the Spirit shall … reap life. Sow good today. Take it to God in prayer.
