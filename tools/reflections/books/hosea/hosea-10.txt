# Hosea 10: It is time to seek the LORD

== 10:1
T: Israel is an empty vine
R: 'Israel is an empty vine, he bringeth forth fruit unto himself' Notice Israel is an empty vine, he bringeth forth fruit unto himself. Israel was a spreading vine; he brought forth fruit for himself. Notice according to the multitude of his fruit he hath increased the altars. As his fruit increased, he built more altars. Notice according to the goodness of his land they have made goodly images. As his land prospered, he adorned his sacred stones. Notice that Israel's fruit was for himself. Notice that prosperity fed idolatry. Notice that more blessing meant more altars to idols. Notice that the vine was empty. Notice that fruit for ourselves is empty fruit. Notice that God wants fruit for Him. Notice that it comes by abiding. Notice that it lasts. Notice that it glorifies the Father. Notice that it shows we are His. Notice that Jesus taught this. John 15 says, 'Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit' Notice herein is my Father glorified. Today, bear fruit for God, not merely for yourself.
S: Israel was a spreading vine that brought forth fruit for himself, and as he prospered he built more altars and adorned more sacred stones. Fruit kept for ourselves is empty, while God wants fruit that glorifies Him. Bear it, for herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit.
P: An empty vine. Hosea 10:1: 'Israel is an empty vine.' Fruit for God. Herein is my Father glorified. Bear fruit for God today. Take it to God in prayer.

T: Their heart is divided
R: 'Their heart is divided; now shall they be found faulty' Notice their heart is divided. Their heart is deceitful. Notice now shall they be found faulty. And now they must bear their guilt. Notice he shall break down their altars, he shall spoil their images. The LORD will demolish their altars and destroy their sacred stones. Notice that their heart is divided. Notice that this is the root problem. Notice that they want God and idols. Notice that divided hearts fail. Notice that God wants undivided hearts. Notice that He wants wholehearted love. Notice that we can ask Him for this. Notice that He unites hearts. Notice that He makes us one-hearted. Notice that we fear His name. Notice that the psalmist prayed this. Psalm 86 says, 'unite my heart to fear thy name.' Notice unite my heart. Today, ask God to give you an undivided heart.
S: Their heart was divided, so they would bear their guilt, and the LORD would demolish their altars and sacred stones. A divided heart that wants both God and idols fails, while God wants wholehearted love. Ask Him for it, as the psalmist prayed, unite my heart to fear thy name.
P: Divided. Hosea 10:2: 'Their heart is divided.' United. Unite my heart to fear thy name. Ask for a whole heart today. Trust Him with it today.

Notice because we feared not the LORD. Because we did not revere the LORD. Notice what then should a king do to us? But even if we had a king, what could he do for us? Notice that they admit the cause. Notice that they did not fear the LORD. Notice that they lose their king. Notice that they see kings cannot help. Notice that the real King was rejected. Notice that fearing God is the foundation. Notice that it is the beginning of wisdom. Notice that it orders life. Notice that it brings blessing. Notice that it keeps us. Notice that Proverbs taught this. Proverbs 9 says, 'The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom' Notice the beginning of wisdom. Today, honour the LORD with reverent fear.
S: The people would say they had no king because they did not revere the LORD, and that even a king could do nothing for them. They rejected the true King, for fearing God is the foundation of life. Honour Him, for the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom.
P: No fear. Hosea 10:3: 'because we feared not the LORD.' Wisdom. The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom. Revere God today. Let this shape your day.

== 10:4
T: Swearing falsely in making a covenant
R: 'They have spoken words, swearing falsely in making a covenant' Notice they have spoken words, swearing falsely in making a covenant. They make many promises, take false oaths and make agreements. Notice thus judgment springeth up as hemlock in the furrows of the field. Therefore lawsuits spring up like poisonous weeds in a ploughed field. Notice that words are empty. Notice that promises are false. Notice that injustice spreads like weeds. Notice that broken promises poison society. Notice that God wants our word to be true. Notice that He keeps His word. Notice that we should keep ours. Notice that even when it hurts. Notice that this marks the righteous. Notice that they stand by their oath. Notice that the psalmist described this. Psalm 15 says, 'He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not.' Notice changeth not. Today, keep your promises, even when it costs you.
S: They made many promises, took false oaths and made agreements, so lawsuits sprang up like poisonous weeds in a field. Broken promises poison society, while God keeps His word and wants us to keep ours. Keep your promises, like the one who sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not.
P: False oaths. Hosea 10:4: 'swearing falsely in making a covenant.' Faithful word. He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not. Keep your word today. Take it to God in prayer.

Because its splendour is taken away. Notice that the people fear for their idol. Notice that they mourn its loss. Notice that they mourn the wrong thing. Notice that an idol needs protecting. Notice that it is real. Notice that Haggai promised this. Haggai 2 says, 'and I will fill this house with glory, saith the LORD of hosts.' Notice I will fill this house with glory. S: The people of Samaria feared for their calf-idol at Beth Aven and mourned its departed splendour. They mourned the wrong thing, for idols need protecting while God protects His people. Seek His glory, for He says, I will fill this house with glory, saith the LORD of hosts.
P: Departed glory. Hosea 10:5: 'the calves of Beth-aven.' Filled with glory. I will fill this house with glory. Seek God's glory today. Trust Him with it today.

Shame makes people want to disappear, but no one can hide from God, only in Him. Hide in Him, remembering that Jesus warned they would say to the mountains, Fall on us.
P: Hiding. Hosea 10:8: 'Cover us; and to the hills, Fall on us.' Shelter. They shall say to the mountains, Fall on us. Hide in God today. Trust Him with it today.
