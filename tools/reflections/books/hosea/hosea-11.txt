# Hosea 11: I drew them with bands of love

== 11:1
T: When Israel was a child, then I loved him
R: 'When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt.' Notice when Israel was a child, then I loved him. When Israel was a child, I loved him. Notice and called my son out of Egypt. And out of Egypt I called My son. Notice that God speaks as a parent. Notice that His love began early. Notice that He loved Israel as a child. Notice that He called him out of slavery. Notice that this is tender. Notice that it is personal. Notice that Matthew saw this fulfilled in Jesus. Notice that Jesus too came out of Egypt. Notice that He is the true Son. Notice that He fulfilled what Israel could not. Matthew 2 says, 'Out of Egypt have I called my son.' Notice out of Egypt have I called my son. Today, rest in God's fatherly love that called you out.
S: The LORD said that when Israel was a child He loved him and called His son out of Egypt. God spoke as a tender parent whose love began early, and Matthew saw these words fulfilled in Jesus, the true Son. Rest in His fatherly love, remembering it is written, out of Egypt have I called my son.
P: Loved as a child. Hosea 11:1: 'When Israel was a child, then I loved him.' Called. Out of Egypt have I called my son. Rest in God's love today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 11:2

But the more they were called, the more they went away from Me. Notice they sacrificed unto Baalim, and burned incense to graven images. They sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to images. Notice that the more God called, the more they left. Notice that this is heartbreaking. Notice that love was refused. Notice that calling was ignored. Notice that God kept calling. Notice that He did not give up. Notice that He calls still. Notice that He stretches out His hands. Notice that He waits all day. Notice that this is patience. Notice that Isaiah described it. Isaiah 65 says, 'I have spread out my hands all the day unto a rebellious people' Notice I have spread out my hands. Today, answer God's call instead of turning away.
S: The more the LORD called Israel, the more they went away, sacrificing to the Baals and burning incense to images. Love was refused, yet God kept calling with open hands. Answer His call, for He says, I have spread out my hands all the day unto a rebellious people.
P: Called away. Hosea 11:2: 'As they called them, so they went from them.' Open hands. I have spread out my hands all the day. Answer God today. Trust Him with it today.

== 11:3
T: I taught Ephraim also to go
R: 'I taught Ephraim also to go, taking them by their arms; but they knew not that I healed them.' Notice I taught Ephraim also to go. It was I who taught Ephraim to walk. Notice taking them by their arms. Taking them by the arms. Notice but they knew not that I healed them. But they did not realise it was I who healed them. Notice that God taught them to walk. Notice that He held their arms. Notice that this is a parent with a toddler. Notice that He healed their falls. Notice that they did not realise it. Notice that God's care is often unnoticed. Notice that He holds us up. Notice that He heals us. Notice that we forget. Notice that we should remember. Notice that Moses described God carrying Israel. Deuteronomy 1 says, 'as a man doth bear his son, in all the way that ye went' Notice as a man doth bear his son. Today, notice the ways God has held you up.
S: The LORD said He taught Ephraim to walk, taking them by the arms, but they did not realise He was the one who healed them. Like a parent with a toddler, God held them up and healed their falls, though they did not notice. Notice His care, for He carried Israel as a man doth bear his son, in all the way that ye went.
P: Taught to walk. Hosea 11:3: 'I taught Ephraim also to go.' Carried. As a man doth bear his son. Notice God's care today. Let this shape your day.

== 11:4
T: Cords of a man, with bands of love
R: 'I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love' Notice I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love. I led them with cords of human kindness, with ties of love. Notice and I was to them as they that take off the yoke on their jaws. To them I was like one who lifts a little child to the cheek. Notice and I laid meat unto them. And I bent down to feed them. Notice that God leads with love. Notice that He uses kindness, not force. Notice that He lifts burdens. Notice that He bends down. Notice that He feeds. Notice that this is humble love. Notice that the great God stoops. Notice that He serves His people. Notice that He draws gently. Notice that His love is the bond. Notice that Jeremiah heard this. Jeremiah 31 says, 'therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.' Notice with lovingkindness have I drawn thee. Today, let God's gentle love draw you close.
S: The LORD said He led Israel with cords of human kindness and ties of love, lifting the yoke from them and bending down to feed them. God leads with love rather than force, stooping humbly to serve His people. Let His love draw you, for therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.
P: Bands of love. Hosea 11:4: 'I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love.' Drawn. With lovingkindness have I drawn thee. Let God's love draw you today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 11:5
T: Because they refused to return
R: 'He shall not return into the land of Egypt, but the Assyrian shall be his king, because they refused to return.' Notice he shall not return into the land of Egypt. Will they not return to Egypt? Notice but the Assyrian shall be his king. Will not Assyria rule over them? Notice because they refused to return. Because they refuse to repent. Notice that they refused to return to God. Notice that another king will rule them. Notice that refusing God's rule brings harsher rule. Notice that we all serve someone. Notice that God's service is freedom. Notice that sin's service is bondage. Notice that we choose. Notice that returning is still possible. Notice that God invites it. Notice that it is the only way to freedom. Notice that Paul explained this. Romans 6 says, 'to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are' Notice his servants ye are. Today, choose to return and serve God freely.
S: The LORD said Israel would not go back to Egypt but Assyria would rule them, because they refused to return to Him. Refusing God's rule brings harsher masters, for we all serve someone. Choose God's freedom, for to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are.
P: Refusing. Hosea 11:5: 'because they refused to return.' Servants. To whom ye yield yourselves … his servants ye are. Return to God today. Trust Him with it today.
