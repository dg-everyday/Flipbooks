Notice that it pays tribute. Notice that this is empty. Notice that it multiplies lies. Notice that chasing wind is vanity. Notice that the Preacher said so. Notice that only God fills. Notice that He gives substance. Ecclesiastes 1 says, 'all is vanity and vexation of spirit.' Notice vanity and vexation of spirit. Today, stop chasing wind and feed on what God gives.

Chasing what cannot satisfy is empty, for only God gives substance. Stop chasing wind, for the Preacher found that all is vanity and vexation of spirit.
P: Feeding on wind. Hosea 12:1: 'Ephraim feedeth on wind.' Vanity. All is vanity and vexation of spirit. Feed on God today. Take it to God in prayer.
