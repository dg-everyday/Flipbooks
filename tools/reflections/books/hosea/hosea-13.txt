Notice that people listened to him. Notice that idolatry ruined him. Notice that sin brought death. Notice that past honour does not protect. Notice that one turning can undo much. Notice that sin pays wages. Notice that the wage is death. Notice that God offers a gift instead. Notice that the gift is life. Notice that Paul contrasted these. Romans 6 says, 'For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life' Notice the gift of God is eternal life. Today, choose God's gift of life over sin's wages.
S: When Ephraim spoke, people trembled and he was honoured in Israel, but he became guilty of Baal worship and died. Past honour did not protect him, for sin pays its wage in death. Choose God's gift instead, for the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life.
