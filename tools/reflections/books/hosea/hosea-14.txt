# Hosea 14: I will heal their backsliding

== 14:1
T: O Israel, return unto the LORD thy God
R: 'O Israel, return unto the LORD thy God; for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity.' Notice O Israel, return unto the LORD thy God. Return, Israel, to the LORD your God. Notice for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity. Your sins have been your downfall. Notice that the book ends with an invitation. Notice that after all the warnings comes return. Notice that God calls Israel back. Notice that He names the problem. Notice that sin caused the fall. Notice that the way back is open. Notice that it is open to the fallen. Notice that falling is not final. Notice that God lifts up. Notice that He calls us home. Notice that Isaiah gave the same invitation. Isaiah 55 says, 'let him return unto the LORD, and he will have mercy upon him' Notice he will have mercy upon him. Today, wherever you have fallen, return to the LORD.
S: The prophet called Israel to return to the LORD their God, for their sins had been their downfall. After all the warnings the book ends with an open invitation, for falling is not final. Return wherever you have fallen, for let him return unto the LORD, and he will have mercy upon him.
P: Return. Hosea 14:1: 'O Israel, return unto the LORD thy God.' Mercy. He will have mercy upon him. Return to God today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 14:2
T: Take with you words
R: 'Take with you words, and turn to the LORD: say unto him, Take away all iniquity, and receive us graciously' Notice take with you words, and turn to the LORD. Take words with you and return to the LORD. Notice say unto him, Take away all iniquity, and receive us graciously. Say to Him, Forgive all our sins and receive us graciously. Notice so will we render the calves of our lips. That we may offer the fruit of our lips. Notice that God gives the words to pray. Notice that He makes return easy. Notice that we offer praise. Notice that this is all He asks. Notice that it is not costly offerings. Notice that it is honest words. Notice that it is praise from the lips. Notice that Hebrews echoes this. Hebrews 13 says, 'let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips' Notice the fruit of our lips. Today, use these words to come back to God.
S: The prophet told Israel to take words and return to the LORD, saying, Forgive all our sins and receive us graciously, that we may offer the fruit of our lips. God gives the very words to pray, asking only for honest confession and praise rather than costly offerings. Use them, and let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips.
P: Taking words. Hosea 14:2: 'Take with you words, and turn to the LORD.' Praise. The fruit of our lips. Pray these words today. Trust Him with it today.

== 14:3
T: In thee the fatherless findeth mercy
R: 'for in thee the fatherless findeth mercy.' Notice Asshur shall not save us; we will not ride upon horses. Assyria cannot save us; we will not mount warhorses. Notice neither will we say any more to the work of our hands, Ye are our gods. We will never again say Our gods to what our own hands have made. Notice for in thee the fatherless findeth mercy. For in You the fatherless find compassion. Notice that the prayer renounces false hopes. Notice that Assyria cannot save. Notice that horses cannot save. Notice that idols cannot save. Notice that only God shows mercy. Notice that He shows it to the fatherless. Notice that He cares for the helpless. Notice that we come as helpless children. Notice that He receives us. Notice that He is a Father to the fatherless. Notice that the psalmist declared this. Psalm 146 says, 'he relieveth the fatherless and widow' Notice he relieveth the fatherless. Today, renounce false hopes and come to God as a helpless child.
S: The prayer renounced Assyria, warhorses and handmade gods, for in God the fatherless find compassion. Every false hope was laid down, for only God shows mercy to the helpless who come to Him. Come to Him as a child, for he relieveth the fatherless and widow.
P: Fatherless mercy. Hosea 14:3: 'in thee the fatherless findeth mercy.' Relief. He relieveth the fatherless and widow. Come to God today. Let this shape your day.

== 14:4

R: 'I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely: for mine anger is turned away from him.' Notice I will heal their backsliding. I will heal their waywardness. Notice I will love them freely. And love them freely. Notice for mine anger is turned away from him. For My anger has turned away from them. Notice that God answers the prayer. Notice that He heals. Notice that He loves freely. Notice that His love is not earned. Notice that His anger turns away. Notice that this is the heart of the gospel. Notice that God's love is free. Notice that it is undeserved. Notice that it heals wandering hearts. Notice that it changes us. Notice that Paul declared this. Romans 3 says, 'Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus' Notice justified freely by his grace. Today, receive God's free love and healing.
S: The LORD answered that He would heal their waywardness and love them freely, for His anger had turned away from them. God's love is free and undeserved, healing wandering hearts, the heart of the gospel. Receive it, for we are justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.
P: Loved freely. Hosea 14:4: 'I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely.' Freely justified. Justified freely by his grace. Receive God's love today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 14:5
T: I will be as the dew unto Israel
R: 'I will be as the dew unto Israel: he shall grow as the lily, and cast forth his roots as Lebanon.' Notice I will be as the dew unto Israel. I will be like the dew to Israel. Notice he shall grow as the lily. He will blossom like a lily. Notice and cast forth his roots as Lebanon. Like a cedar of Lebanon he will send down his roots. Notice that God becomes dew. Notice that their goodness was like vanishing dew. Notice that now God is the dew. Notice that He refreshes daily. Notice that they blossom. Notice that they put down deep roots. Notice that beauty and strength grow together. Notice that God's presence brings growth. Notice that He waters quietly. Notice that He renews each morning. Notice that Isaiah described this. Isaiah 26 says, 'for thy dew is as the dew of herbs' Notice thy dew. Today, receive God's quiet refreshing and grow in Him.
S: The LORD said He would be like the dew to Israel, who would blossom like a lily and send down roots like a cedar of Lebanon. Their goodness had vanished like dew, but now God Himself would be their daily refreshing, bringing beauty and strength. Receive it, for thy dew is as the dew of herbs.
P: Like dew. Hosea 14:5: 'I will be as the dew unto Israel.' Refreshing. Thy dew is as the dew of herbs. Grow in God today. Trust Him with it today.

Restoration is beautiful, making God's people a blessing whose lives give off a sweet fragrance. Let yours do the same, for we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ.
