# Isaiah 4: The branch of the LORD shall be beautiful and glorious

== 4:1
T: Seven women shall take hold of one man
R: 'And in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread' Notice and in that day. On that day. Notice seven women shall take hold of one man. Many women will beg one man. Notice saying, We will eat our own bread. We will provide our own food. Notice and wear our own apparel. Our own clothes. Notice only let us be called by thy name. Just give us your name. Notice to take away our reproach. To remove our shame. Notice that war had killed so many men. Notice that widows and unmarried women faced shame. Notice that they were desperate for protection. Notice that God sees the vulnerable. Notice that He cares for widows. Notice that He gives His own name. Notice that we can be called by it. Isaiah 54 says, 'For thy Maker is thine husband; the LORD of hosts is his name' Notice thy Maker. Today, remember God cares for the vulnerable. You can bear His name.
S: On that day seven women will take hold of one man, offering to provide their own food and clothing if only they can bear his name and lose their shame. So many men had died in war that widows and unmarried women were desperate for protection, but God sees the vulnerable and removes their reproach. Take comfort, for thy Maker is thine husband; the LORD of hosts is his name.
P: Desperate. Isaiah 4:1: 'only let us be called by thy name, to take away our reproach.' Thy Maker. For thy Maker is thine husband. God cares for the vulnerable. Let this shape your day.

== 4:2
T: The branch of the LORD
R: 'In that day shall the branch of the LORD be beautiful and glorious' Notice in that day. On that day. Notice shall the branch of the LORD. God's Branch. Notice be beautiful and glorious. Will be lovely and glorious. Notice and the fruit of the earth. The land's fruit. Notice shall be excellent and comely. Will be the pride and glory. Notice for them that are escaped of Israel. For the survivors. Notice that after judgment comes hope. Notice that a Branch grows. Notice that it is the LORD's Branch. Notice that the prophets use this name for the coming Messiah. Notice that He grows from what seemed dead. Notice that He is beautiful and glorious. Notice that He brings fruit for the survivors. Notice that hope sprouts after ruin. Notice that Jeremiah used the same name. Notice that the Branch reigns in righteousness. Jeremiah 23 says, 'that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch' Notice Branch. Today, look for new growth after hard seasons. Hope in the Branch.
S: On that day the Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the land will be the pride of Israel's survivors. After judgment comes hope, and the prophets use this name for the coming Messiah, who grows from what seemed dead. Hope in Him, for I will raise unto David a righteous Branch.
P: The Branch. Isaiah 4:2: 'In that day shall the branch of the LORD be beautiful and glorious.' Righteous. I will raise unto David a righteous Branch. Hope in the Branch today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 4:3
T: Every one that is written among the living
R: 'shall be called holy, even every one that is written among the living in Jerusalem' Notice and it shall come to pass. It will happen. Notice that he that is left in Zion. Those left in Zion. Notice and he that remaineth in Jerusalem. Those who remain. Notice shall be called holy. Will be called holy. Notice even every one that is written among the living. Everyone recorded among the living. Notice in Jerusalem. In the city. Notice that the remnant will be holy. Notice that God has written their names. Notice that He keeps a record. Notice that He knows His own. Notice that being written down means being remembered. Notice that it means belonging. Notice that holiness is His gift to them. Notice that it marks them as His. Notice that Jesus said to rejoice in this. Notice that it matters more than power. Luke 10 says, 'but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.' Notice written. Today, rejoice that God knows your name. Live as one called holy.
S: Those who are left in Zion and remain in Jerusalem will be called holy, all who are recorded among the living. God writes the names of His remnant and calls them holy as His gift, marking them as His own. Rejoice in that, for Jesus said, rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.
P: Written. Isaiah 4:3: 'even every one that is written among the living.' In heaven. Rejoice, because your names are written in heaven. Live as one called holy today. Trust Him with it today.

== 4:4
T: By the spirit of judgment, and by the spirit of burning
R: 'When the Lord shall have washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion' Notice when the Lord shall have washed away. When God washes. Notice the filth. The stains. Notice of the daughters of Zion. Of the women of Jerusalem. Notice and shall have purged the blood of Jerusalem. Cleansed its bloodshed. Notice by the spirit of judgment. By a spirit of justice. Notice and by the spirit of burning. By a spirit of fire. Notice that holiness comes through cleansing. Notice that God washes His people. Notice that He purges their guilt. Notice that the cleansing is by fire. Notice that it is painful. Notice that it is thorough. Notice that it makes them holy. Notice that God does not leave us dirty. Notice that He cleans us completely. Notice that His Spirit does the work. Notice that John spoke of fire. Matthew 3 says, 'he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire' Notice fire. Today, let God cleanse you. Welcome His refining Spirit.
S: The Lord will wash away the filth of the women of Zion and cleanse the bloodshed from Jerusalem by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of fire. Holiness comes through God's thorough cleansing, which can be painful but leaves us clean. Welcome His Spirit, for he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire.
P: Washed. Isaiah 4:4: 'by the spirit of judgment, and by the spirit of burning.' Fire. He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire. Let God cleanse you today. Walk in His ways today.

== 4:5
T: A cloud and smoke by day, and ... fire by night
R: 'a cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night: for upon all the glory shall be a defence.' Notice and the LORD will create. God will make. Notice upon every dwelling place of mount Zion. Over all of Zion. Notice and upon her assemblies. Over those gathered. Notice a cloud and smoke by day. A cloud by day. Notice and the shining of a flaming fire by night. Fire by night. Notice for upon all the glory shall be a defence. A canopy over all the glory. Notice that this recalls the exodus. Notice that God led Israel by cloud and fire. Notice that His presence covered them. Notice that He will do it again. Notice that His presence protects. Notice that He guides day and night. Exodus 13 says, 'And the LORD went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, to lead them the way' Notice went before. Today, trust God's presence over your home. He leads day and night.
S: The LORD will create over all of Mount Zion and those gathered there a cloud of smoke by day and a glow of flaming fire by night, a canopy over all the glory. This recalls the exodus, when God's presence led and protected His people day and night. Trust Him, for the LORD went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, to lead them the way.
P: Cloud and fire. Isaiah 4:5: 'a cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night.' Led. The LORD went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud. Trust God's presence today. Let this shape your day.

== 4:6
T: A place of refuge, and ... a covert from storm
R: 'And there shall be a tabernacle for a shadow in the daytime from the heat, and for a place of refuge, and for a covert from storm and from rain.' Notice and there shall be a tabernacle. A shelter. Notice for a shadow in the daytime from the heat. Shade from the heat. Notice and for a place of refuge. A refuge. Notice and for a covert from storm and from rain. A hiding place from storms. Notice that the chapter ends with shelter. Notice that God's presence is shade in the heat. Notice that it is cover in the storm. Notice that life has heat and storms. Notice that we can shelter in God. Notice that He is our refuge. Notice that it is for His cleansed people. Psalm 61 says, 'I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever: I will trust in the covert of thy wings.' Notice covert. Today, shelter in God from the heat and storm. He is your refuge.
S: There will be a shelter to give shade from the heat by day, and a refuge and hiding place from the storm and rain. Life has heat and storms we cannot avoid, but God's presence is our shelter after His cleansing. Abide in Him, saying, I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever: I will trust in the covert of thy wings.
P: Shelter. Isaiah 4:6: 'and for a place of refuge, and for a covert from storm and from rain.' Abide. I will trust in the covert of thy wings. Shelter in God today. Take it to God in prayer.
