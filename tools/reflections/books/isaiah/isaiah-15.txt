# Isaiah 15: My heart shall cry out for Moab

== 15:1
T: The burden of Moab
R: 'The burden of Moab. Because in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence' Notice the burden of Moab. A message about Moab. Notice because in the night. In one night. Notice Ar of Moab is laid waste. Ar is ruined. Notice and brought to silence. Destroyed. Notice because in the night Kir of Moab is laid waste. Kir is ruined in one night. Notice that Moab was Israel's neighbour. Notice that it was related through Lot. Notice that its cities fell suddenly. Notice that destruction came overnight. Notice that security can vanish quickly. Notice that we cannot count on tomorrow. Notice that God alone is sure. Notice that we should not boast of strength. Proverbs 27 says, 'Boast not thyself of to morrow; for thou knowest not what a day may bring forth.' Notice a day. Today, hold your security lightly. God alone is sure.
S: This is the message about Moab: Ar of Moab is ruined and destroyed in one night, and Kir of Moab is ruined in one night. Moab was Israel's neighbour and relative through Lot, and its cities fell overnight, showing how quickly security can vanish. Hold it lightly, for boast not thyself of to morrow; for thou knowest not what a day may bring forth.
P: In one night. Isaiah 15:1: 'Because in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste.' Tomorrow. Boast not thyself of to morrow. Hold your security lightly today. Trust Him with it today.

Notice that shaving was a sign of mourning. Notice that sorrow was everywhere. Notice that where we run in grief matters. Notice that idols cannot comfort. Notice that God is near the brokenhearted. Notice that He binds wounds. Psalm 34 says, 'The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart' Notice nigh. Today, in grief, run to God, not to empty comforts.
