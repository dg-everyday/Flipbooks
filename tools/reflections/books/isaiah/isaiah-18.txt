# Isaiah 18: A present brought unto the LORD of hosts

== 18:1
T: The land shadowing with wings
R: 'Woe to the land shadowing with wings, which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia' Notice woe to the land. Alas for the land. Notice shadowing with wings. Of whirring wings. Notice which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia. Along the rivers of Cush. Notice that ancient Cush lay south of Egypt. Notice that it was a far land. Notice that whirring wings may picture insects or ships. Notice that God speaks to distant nations. Notice that no land is beyond His notice. Notice that He knows every people. Notice that He has a word for each. Notice that this word will end in worship. Notice that God's concern reaches the ends of the earth. Notice that all peoples matter to Him. Notice that His love is wide. Acts 8 says, 'and, behold, a man of Ethiopia, an eunuch of great authority' Notice Ethiopia. Today, remember God's love reaches every nation.
S: Woe to the land of whirring wings along the rivers of Cush. Ancient Cush lay far south of Egypt, yet no land is beyond God's notice, and this word will end in worship. His love reaches every nation, as when the good news reached a man of Ethiopia, an eunuch of great authority.
P: Far lands. Isaiah 18:1: 'which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia.' Reached. A man of Ethiopia, an eunuch of great authority. God's love reaches every nation today. Trust Him with it today.

== 18:2
T: Go, ye swift messengers
R: 'Go, ye swift messengers, to a nation scattered and peeled' Notice that sendeth ambassadors by the sea. Which sends envoys by sea. Notice even in vessels of bulrushes upon the waters. In papyrus boats. Notice saying, Go, ye swift messengers. Go, swift messengers. Notice to a nation scattered and peeled. To a people tall and smooth-skinned. Notice to a people terrible from their beginning hitherto. Feared far and wide. Notice a nation meted out and trodden down. A strong and conquering nation. Notice whose land the rivers have spoiled. Whose land is divided by rivers. Notice that Cush sent envoys in reed boats. Notice that they sought alliances. Notice that God describes them with respect. Notice that they were tall and strong. Notice that God sees the dignity of every people. Notice that every nation is His creation. Notice that He made all peoples. Acts 17 says, 'And hath made of one blood all nations of men' Notice one blood. Today, honour the dignity of every people. All are God's creation.
S: Cush sends envoys by sea in papyrus boats, and God says, go, swift messengers, to a people tall and smooth-skinned, feared far and wide, a strong nation whose land is divided by rivers. God describes them with respect, for every people is His creation with dignity. Honour all, for He hath made of one blood all nations of men.
P: Swift messengers. Isaiah 18:2: 'Go, ye swift messengers, to a nation scattered and peeled.' One blood. And hath made of one blood all nations of men. Honour every people today. Walk in His ways today.

== 18:3

Notice and dwellers on the earth. Everyone on earth. Notice see ye. Watch. Notice when he lifteth up an ensign on the mountains. When a banner is raised. Notice and when he bloweth a trumpet. When a trumpet sounds. Notice hear ye. Listen. Notice that God calls the whole world to watch. Notice that He will act publicly. Notice that a banner and trumpet signal His work. Notice that everyone will see. Notice that God's works are not hidden. Notice that He wants the world to notice. Notice that we should watch for His signals. Notice that we should listen for His trumpet. Notice that one day a final trumpet will sound. 1 Thessalonians 4 says, 'and with the trump of God' Notice trump of God. Today, watch and listen for God's work in the world.
S: All people of the world and everyone on earth, watch when a banner is raised on the mountains and listen when a trumpet sounds. God acts publicly and calls the whole world to notice His work. Watch and listen, for one day the Lord shall descend with the trump of God.
P: Watch and listen. Isaiah 18:3: 'see ye, when he lifteth up an ensign on the mountains.' Trumpet. And with the trump of God. Watch for God's work today. Let this shape your day.

== 18:4
T: I will take my rest, and I will consider
R: 'I will take my rest, and I will consider in my dwelling place like a clear heat upon herbs' Notice for so the LORD said unto me. God told me. Notice I will take my rest. I will remain quiet. Notice and I will consider in my dwelling place. I will look on from My dwelling. Notice like a clear heat upon herbs. Like shimmering heat in sunshine. Notice and like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest. Like dew in harvest heat. Notice that God is calm. Notice that He watches quietly. Notice that He is not anxious. Notice that He waits for the right time. Notice that His stillness is not inaction. Notice that it is patient control. Notice that He sees everything from His place. Notice that we can share His calm. Notice that we need not panic. Notice that He is in control. Psalm 46 says, 'Be still, and know that I am God' Notice be still. Today, share God's calm. He watches over all.
S: The LORD said He will remain quiet and look on from His dwelling place, like shimmering heat in sunshine and like a cloud of dew in harvest heat. God's stillness is not inaction but patient control, for He watches everything and waits for the right time. Share His calm, be still, and know that I am God.
P: God's calm. Isaiah 18:4: 'I will take my rest, and I will consider in my dwelling place.' Be still. Be still, and know that I am God. Share God's calm today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 18:5

When the blossom is gone. Notice and the sour grape is ripening in the flower. When the grapes ripen. Notice he shall both cut off the sprigs with pruninghooks. He cuts off the shoots with knives. Notice and take away and cut down the branches. Cuts down the spreading branches. Notice that God acts at just the right moment. Notice that He waits until the plans are nearly ripe. Notice that then He cuts them off. Notice that enemy plans never reach harvest. Notice that God prunes. Notice that pruning can also be good. Notice that He prunes His people to grow. Notice that it hurts but helps. Notice that fruit follows. John 15 says, 'every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it, that it may bring forth more fruit.' Notice purgeth. Today, trust God's timing in cutting and pruning.
S: Before the harvest, when the blossom is gone and the grapes ripen, God cuts off the shoots with pruning knives and cuts down the spreading branches. He acts at the right moment so enemy plans never reach harvest, and He also prunes His own people to grow. Trust it, for every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it, that it may bring forth more fruit.
P: Pruned. Isaiah 18:5: 'he shall both cut off the sprigs with pruninghooks.' More fruit. Every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it. Trust God's pruning today. Trust Him with it today.
