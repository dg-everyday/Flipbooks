Notice he shall smite and heal it. He will strike and heal. Notice and they shall return even to the LORD. They will turn to God. Notice and he shall be intreated of them. He will respond to their pleas. Notice and shall heal them. And heal them. Notice that God strikes to heal. Notice that discipline leads to restoration. Notice that wounds lead to turning. Notice that turning leads to healing. Notice that God listens to pleas. Notice that His blows are not final. Notice that healing is His goal. Notice that pain can be part of grace. Notice that He binds up what He wounds. Hosea 6 says, 'for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up.' Notice heal us. Today, trust that God's discipline leads to healing.

God strikes in order to heal, for His discipline leads to turning and restoration. Trust Him, for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up.
