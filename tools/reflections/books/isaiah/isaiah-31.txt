# Isaiah 31: The Egyptians are men, and not God

== 31:1
T: They look not unto the Holy One of Israel
R: 'Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help; and stay on horses, and trust in chariots, because they are many' Notice woe to them that go down to Egypt for help. Woe to those who go to Egypt for help. Notice and stay on horses. Who rely on horses. Notice and trust in chariots, because they are many. Who trust in their many chariots. Notice and in horsemen, because they are very strong. In the great strength of their horsemen. Notice but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel. But do not look to the Holy One. Notice neither seek the LORD. Or seek help from God. Notice that the problem was where they looked. Notice that they counted chariots. Notice that we can count our resources and forget God. Psalm 20 says, 'Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the LORD our God.' Notice remember. Today, look to God before you count your resources.
S: Woe to those who go to Egypt for help, rely on horses and trust in their many chariots and strong horsemen, but do not look to the Holy One of Israel or seek help from the LORD. Judah counted chariots and horsemen and was impressed by numbers and strength, while ignoring God. Look to Him, for some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the LORD our God.
P: Look to God. Isaiah 31:1: 'but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel.' Remember. We will remember the name of the LORD our God. Look to God first today. Trust Him with it today.

== 31:2
T: Yet he also is wise
R: 'Yet he also is wise, and will bring evil, and will not call back his words' Notice yet he also is wise. Yet He too is wise. Notice and will bring evil. Can bring disaster. Notice and will not call back his words. He does not take back His words. Notice but will arise against the house of the evildoers. He will rise up against that wicked nation. Notice and against the help of them that work iniquity. Against those who help evildoers. Notice that Judah thought its plan was wise. Notice that God is wiser. Notice that His words stand. Notice that He does not take them back. Notice that human cleverness cannot outwit God. Notice that we should lean on it. Notice that we should not trust our own cleverness. Notice that He keeps His word. Notice that both warnings and promises stand. 1 Corinthians 1 says, 'Because the foolishness of God is wiser than men' Notice wiser. Today, trust God's wisdom over your own cleverness.
S: Yet God too is wise and can bring disaster, He does not take back His words, and He will rise against the wicked nation and those who help evildoers. Judah thought its plan clever, but God's wisdom is greater and His words always stand. Lean on Him, for the foolishness of God is wiser than men.
P: Wiser. Isaiah 31:2: 'Yet he also is wise.' Wiser than men. The foolishness of God is wiser than men. Trust God's wisdom today. Walk in His ways today.

== 31:3
T: The Egyptians are men, and not God
R: 'Now the Egyptians are men, and not God; and their horses flesh, and not spirit.' Notice now the Egyptians are men. But the Egyptians are mere mortals. Notice and not God. And not God. Notice and their horses flesh. Their horses are flesh. Notice and not spirit. And not spirit. Notice when the LORD shall stretch out his hand. When God stretches out His hand. Notice both he that helpeth shall fall. The helper will stumble. Notice and he that is holpen shall fall down. The one helped will fall. Notice and they all shall fail together. All will perish together. Notice that this is a simple truth. Notice that people are only people. Notice that when God acts, human help collapses. Notice that both helper and helped fall. Notice that we should not put people in God's place. Psalm 118 says, 'It is better to trust in the LORD than to put confidence in man.' Notice better. Today, remember people are not God. Trust Him.
S: The Egyptians are mere mortals and not God, their horses are flesh and not spirit, and when the LORD stretches out His hand, helper and helped will both stumble and fall and perish together. No human power equals God, so we should never put people in His place. Remember, it is better to trust in the LORD than to put confidence in man.
P: Not God. Isaiah 31:3: 'Now the Egyptians are men, and not God.' Better. It is better to trust in the LORD than to put confidence in man. Trust God today. Let this shape your day.

== 31:4
T: As the lion ... so shall the LORD of hosts come down
R: 'so shall the LORD of hosts come down to fight for mount Zion, and for the hill thereof.' Notice for thus hath the LORD spoken unto me. God told me. Notice like as the lion and the young lion roaring on his prey. As a lion growls over its prey. Notice when a multitude of shepherds is called forth against him. Though shepherds are called against it. Notice he will not be afraid of their voice. It is not frightened by their shouts. Notice nor abase himself for the noise of them. Or disturbed by their clamour. Notice so shall the LORD of hosts come down. So God will come down. Notice to fight for mount Zion. To do battle on Mount Zion. Notice that God is like a lion. Notice that no noise intimidates Him. Notice that He fights for His people. Revelation 5 says, 'behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda' Notice Lion. Today, trust the Lion who fights for you. He is not afraid.
S: The LORD said that as a lion growls over its prey and is not frightened or disturbed though shepherds shout against it, so the LORD of hosts will come down to fight for Mount Zion and its heights. God is not intimidated by any enemy's noise, for He guards His people like a lion. Trust Him, for behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda.
P: Like a lion. Isaiah 31:4: 'so shall the LORD of hosts come down to fight for mount Zion.' Lion. Behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda. Trust the Lion who fights for you today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 31:5
T: As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem
R: 'As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem; defending also he will deliver it' Notice as birds flying. Like birds hovering overhead. Notice so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem. So God will shield Jerusalem. Notice defending also he will deliver it. He will shield it and deliver it. Notice and passing over he will preserve it. He will pass over it and rescue it. Notice that God is like a hovering bird. Notice that a mother bird guards her nest. Notice that this is tender. Notice that the lion was fierce. Notice that the bird is gentle. Notice that God is both. Notice that passing over recalls the Passover. Notice that God protected Israel then. Notice that He does it again. Notice that His protection is fierce and tender. Notice that Jesus wanted to gather Jerusalem like a hen. Matthew 23 says, 'even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings' Notice under her wings. Today, rest under God's wings. He hovers over you.
S: Like birds hovering overhead, the LORD of hosts will shield Jerusalem, He will shield it and deliver it, pass over it and rescue it. God is both a fierce lion and a tender mother bird, and passing over recalls His protection at the Passover. Rest under His wings, as Jesus longed to gather Jerusalem even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings.
P: Hovering. Isaiah 31:5: 'As birds flying, so will the LORD of hosts defend Jerusalem.' Wings. Even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings. Rest under God's wings today. Trust Him with it today.

Notice that victory is His. Zechariah 4 says, 'Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.' Notice my spirit. Today, trust God's power, not human might.
