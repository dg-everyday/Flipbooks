R: 'and there is no God else beside me; a just God and a Saviour; there is none beside me.' Notice tell ye, and bring them near. Declare what is to be, present it. Notice yea, let them take counsel together. Let them take counsel together. Notice who hath declared this from ancient time. Who foretold this long ago. Notice have not I the LORD. Was it not I, the LORD. Notice and there is no God else beside me. And there is no God apart from Me. Notice a just God and a Saviour. A righteous God and a Saviour. Notice there is none beside me. There is none but Me. Notice that God is just. Notice that He is also Saviour. Notice that these seem in tension. Notice that no other god offers this. Romans 3 says, 'that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus.' Notice just and justifier. Today, thank God that He is both just and Saviour.
S: Let them take counsel together and say who foretold this long ago, for it was the LORD, and there is no God apart from Him, a righteous God and a Saviour, there is none but Him. Justice condemns sin and a Saviour rescues sinners, and God is both at once, which the cross makes possible. Thank Him, that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus.
