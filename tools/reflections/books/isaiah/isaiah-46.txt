# Isaiah 46: Even to hoar hairs will I carry you

== 46:1
T: Bel boweth down, Nebo stoopeth
R: 'Bel boweth down, Nebo stoopeth, their idols were upon the beasts, and upon the cattle' Notice Bel boweth down. Bel bows down. Notice Nebo stoopeth. Nebo stoops low. Notice their idols were upon the beasts. Their idols are borne by beasts of burden. Notice and upon the cattle. By cattle. Notice your carriages were heavy loaden. The images that are carried about are burdensome. Notice they are a burden to the weary beast. A burden for the weary. Notice that Bel and Nebo were Babylon's chief gods. Notice that they were carried in parades. Notice that now they are carried away. Notice that they are loaded on tired animals. Notice that false gods must be carried. Notice that they weigh people down. Notice that the true God is different. Notice that He carries His people. 1 Samuel 5 says, 'behold, Dagon was fallen upon his face to the earth before the ark of the LORD.' Notice fallen. Today, set down every false god that weighs you down.
S: Bel bows down and Nebo stoops low, and their idols are loaded on beasts of burden, a heavy load for weary animals. Babylon's chief gods, once carried in parades, are now hauled away as cargo, for false gods must be carried and only weigh people down. Set them down, as when Dagon was fallen upon his face to the earth before the ark of the LORD.
P: Carried gods. Isaiah 46:1: 'Bel boweth down, Nebo stoopeth.' Fallen. Dagon was fallen upon his face to the earth. Set down every false god today. Trust Him with it today.

P: Cannot answer. Isaiah 46:7: 'one shall cry unto him, yet can he not answer.' Cannot go. They must needs be borne, because they cannot go. Cry to the God who answers today. Let this shape your day.
