# Isaiah 50: The tongue of the learned, a word in season to him that is weary

== 50:1
T: For your iniquities have ye sold yourselves
R: 'Behold, for your iniquities have ye sold yourselves, and for your transgressions is your mother put away.' Notice thus saith the LORD. This is what the LORD says. Notice where is the bill of your mother's divorcement. Where is your mother's certificate of divorce. Notice whom I have put away. With which I sent her away. Notice or which of my creditors is it to whom I have sold you. Or to which of My creditors did I sell you. Notice behold, for your iniquities have ye sold yourselves. Because of your sins you were sold. Notice and for your transgressions is your mother put away. Because of your transgressions your mother was sent away. Notice that God asks for the paperwork. Notice that there is none. Notice that He never divorced His people. Notice that sin separates. Notice that God restores. Hosea 2 says, 'And I will betroth thee unto me for ever' Notice for ever. Today, know that God has not divorced you. Come back to Him.
S: The LORD asks where the certificate of divorce is with which He sent their mother away, or to which creditor He sold them, for it was because of their own sins that they were sold and sent away. God never divorced or sold His people, for exile came from their sin and not from His rejection, and His covenant still stands. Come back, for He says, and I will betroth thee unto me for ever.
P: Not divorced. Isaiah 50:1: 'for your iniquities have ye sold yourselves.' For ever. I will betroth thee unto me for ever. Come back to God today. Trust Him with it today.

== 50:2
T: Is my hand shortened at all?
R: 'Is my hand shortened at all, that it cannot redeem? or have I no power to deliver?' Notice wherefore, when I came, was there no man. When I came, why was there no one. Notice when I called, was there none to answer. When I called, why was there no one to answer. Notice is my hand shortened at all, that it cannot redeem. Was My arm too short to deliver you. Notice or have I no power to deliver. Do I lack the strength to rescue you. Notice behold, at my rebuke I dry up the sea. By a mere rebuke I dry up the sea. Notice I make the rivers a wilderness. I turn rivers into a desert. Notice that we must answer His call. Notice that His power is unlimited. Ephesians 3 says, 'Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think' Notice able. Today, answer God's call. His arm is not too short.
S: God came and no one answered, and He asks whether His arm was too short to redeem or whether He lacked strength to rescue, when by a mere rebuke He dries up the sea and turns rivers into desert. The problem was never God's power but His people's failure to answer His call. Answer Him, for He is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think.
P: Not too short. Isaiah 50:2: 'Is my hand shortened at all, that it cannot redeem?' Able. Able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think. Answer God's call today. Walk in His ways today.

== 50:3
T: I clothe the heavens with blackness
R: 'I clothe the heavens with blackness, and I make sackcloth their covering.' Notice I clothe the heavens with blackness. I clothe the heavens with darkness. Notice and I make sackcloth their covering. And make sackcloth its covering. Notice that God controls the sky. Notice that He can darken it. Notice that sackcloth is mourning cloth. Notice that the sky is dressed for grief. Notice that this recalls the plague of darkness in Egypt. Notice that God's power is cosmic. Notice that this also points to the cross. Notice that the sky went dark when Jesus died. Notice that creation mourned. Notice that the Servant's suffering follows next. Notice that the darkness was purposeful. Notice that God was at work in it. Notice that dark skies are not out of His control. Notice that even darkness serves Him. Matthew 27 says, 'Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land unto the ninth hour.' Notice darkness. Today, trust that God is at work even when the sky is dark.
S: God clothes the heavens with darkness and makes sackcloth, the cloth of mourning, their covering. God's power reaches the sky itself, as in Egypt's plague of darkness, and this points ahead to the dark sky when Jesus died. Trust Him in the dark, for from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land unto the ninth hour.
P: Dark skies. Isaiah 50:3: 'I clothe the heavens with blackness.' Darkness. There was darkness over all the land. Trust God in dark times today. Let this shape your day.

== 50:4
T: A word in season to him that is weary
R: 'The Lord GOD hath given me the tongue of the learned, that I should know how to speak a word in season to him that is weary' Notice the Lord GOD hath given me the tongue of the learned. The Sovereign LORD has given Me a well-instructed tongue. Notice that I should know how to speak a word in season. To know the word that sustains. Notice to him that is weary. The weary. Notice he wakeneth morning by morning. He wakens Me morning by morning. Notice he wakeneth mine ear to hear as the learned. Wakens My ear to listen like one being instructed. Notice that this is the third Servant Song. Notice that the Servant is taught by God. Notice that we can learn from Him. Notice that we can speak timely words. Proverbs 25 says, 'A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in pictures of silver.' Notice fitly spoken. Today, listen to God first, then speak a word to the weary.
S: The Sovereign LORD has given the Servant a well-instructed tongue to know the word that sustains the weary, wakening Him morning by morning to listen like one being taught. In this third Servant Song the Servant listens before He speaks, as Jesus rose early to pray and then spoke words that sustained the weary. Do likewise, for a word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in pictures of silver.
P: A word in season. Isaiah 50:4: 'to speak a word in season to him that is weary.' Fitly spoken. A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold. Listen, then encourage someone today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 50:5

Notice and I was not rebellious. I have not been rebellious. Notice neither turned away back. I have not turned away. Notice that the Servant listens. Notice that He obeys. Notice that He does not rebel. Notice that He does not turn back. Notice that Israel was rebellious. Notice that Jesus obeyed perfectly. Notice that He did the Father's will. Notice that He did not turn back even at the cross. Notice that His obedience is our hope. Notice that we can follow Him. Notice that an open ear leads to a willing heart. John 6 says, 'For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me.' Notice the will of Him. Today, ask God to open your ear and keep you from turning back.
S: The Sovereign LORD opened the Servant's ears, and He was not rebellious and did not turn away. Where Israel rebelled the Servant obeyed, as Jesus did the Father's will without turning back even at the cross. Follow Him, for He said, I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me.
P: Not turning back. Isaiah 50:5: 'I was not rebellious, neither turned away back.' His will. Not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me. Do not turn back today. Trust Him with it today.

I did not hide My face from mocking and spitting. Notice that the Servant suffers. Notice that He gives His back willingly. Notice that He does not resist. Notice that He endures shame. Notice that He endures spitting. Notice that this happened to Jesus. Notice that He was beaten. Notice that He was mocked. Notice that He did not hide His face. Notice that we owe Him everything. Matthew 26 says, 'Then did they spit in his face, and buffeted him' Notice spit. Today, thank Jesus for the shame He bore for you.

He suffered willingly and without resisting, exactly as Jesus did, choosing shame out of love for us. Thank Him, for then did they spit in his face, and buffeted him.
P: Willing suffering. Isaiah 50:6: 'I gave my back to the smiters.' Spit upon. Then did they spit in his face, and buffeted him. Thank Jesus for His suffering today. Walk in His ways today.

His resolve, like hard stone, did not crack, as Jesus did not waver on the road to Jerusalem. Be steadfast, as Jesus stedfastly set his face to go to Jerusalem.
P: Face like flint. Isaiah 50:7: 'therefore have I set my face like a flint.' Stedfastly. He stedfastly set his face to go to Jerusalem. Be steadfast today. Let this shape your day.
