R: 'As for me, this is my covenant with them, saith the LORD; My spirit that is upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth' Notice my spirit that is upon thee. My Spirit, who is on you. Notice and my words which I have put in thy mouth. And My words that I have put in your mouth. Notice shall not depart out of thy mouth. It will not depart from your mouth, nor from your children's mouths. Notice from henceforth and for ever. From this time on and forever. Notice that God makes a covenant. Notice that it includes His Spirit. Notice that it includes His words. Notice that it lasts through generations. Notice that it never departs. John 14 says, 'that he may abide with you for ever' Notice for ever. Today, trust God's Spirit and Word to stay with you and your children.
S: As for Me, this is My covenant with them, says the LORD, My Spirit who is on you and My words that I have put in your mouth will not depart from your mouth, or from your children's or their descendants', from this time on and forever. God's covenant gives His Spirit and His Word to His people for every generation without ever departing. Trust it, for the Spirit is given that he may abide with you for ever.
P: Spirit and Word. Isaiah 59:21: 'My spirit that is upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth.' For ever. That he may abide with you for ever. Trust God's Spirit today. Trust Him with it today.
