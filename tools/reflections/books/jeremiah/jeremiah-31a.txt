S: God asks whether Ephraim is not His dear son, the child in whom He delights, for though He often speaks against him He still remembers him, His heart yearns for him and He has great compassion for him, declares the LORD. God answered Ephraim's prayer with deep, moved emotion, for His discipline never stopped His love for His child. Know that He runs to meet you, like the father who saw him, and had compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him.
