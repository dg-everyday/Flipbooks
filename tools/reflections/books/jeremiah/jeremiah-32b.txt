R: 'And fields shall be bought in this land, whereof ye say, It is desolate without man or beast' Notice and fields shall be bought in this land. Once more fields will be bought in this land. Notice whereof ye say, It is desolate without man or beast. Of which you say, it is a desolate waste, without people or animals. Notice it is given into the hand of the Chaldeans. For it has been given into the hands of the Babylonians. Notice that Jeremiah's purchase is explained. Notice that he was first. Notice that others will follow. Notice that God reverses desolation. Notice that Isaiah promised the same. Notice that old ruins would be rebuilt. Notice that former desolations would be raised. Notice that this is God's work. Isaiah 61 says, 'And they shall build the old wastes, they shall raise up the former desolations' Notice the old wastes. Today, do not accept despair's verdict. God can bring life to what others call desolate.
S: Once more fields will be bought in this land, of which they say it is a desolate waste without people or animals, given into the hands of the Babylonians. Jeremiah's purchase was the first of many, for what despair calls empty God calls a future market, and human verdicts are not final. Reject despair's verdict, for they shall build the old wastes, they shall raise up the former desolations.
P: Fields again. Jeremiah 32:43: 'fields shall be bought in this land.' Old wastes. They shall build the old wastes. Reject despair today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 32:44
T: Men shall buy fields for money
R: 'for I will cause their captivity to return, saith the LORD.' Notice men shall buy fields for money, and subscribe evidences, and seal them, and take witnesses. Fields will be bought for silver, and deeds will be signed, sealed and witnessed. Notice that the verse adds this will happen in Benjamin, around Jerusalem, in the towns of Judah, the hill country, the western foothills and the Negev. Notice for I will cause their captivity to return, saith the LORD. Because I will restore their fortunes, declares the LORD. Notice that the whole land is named. Notice that every region is included. Notice that normal business will return. Notice that the reason is God. Notice that He will restore. Notice that the psalmist prayed for it. Notice that dry riverbeds would flow again. Notice that God answered. Psalm 126 says, 'Turn again our captivity, O LORD, as the streams in the south.' Notice as the streams. Today, believe that one small act of faith can point to a great restoration.
S: Fields will be bought for silver and deeds signed, sealed and witnessed, and the verse adds this will happen in Benjamin, around Jerusalem, in the towns of Judah, the hill country, the western foothills and the Negev, because God will restore their fortunes, declares the LORD. Jeremiah's one act of faith in prison pointed to a whole nation's future, restored by God like water returning to dry riverbeds. Believe in that restoration, for turn again our captivity, O LORD, as the streams in the south.
P: The whole land. Jeremiah 32:44: 'for I will cause their captivity to return, saith the LORD.' As the streams. Turn again our captivity, O LORD, as the streams in the south. Believe God's restoration today. Trust Him with it today.
