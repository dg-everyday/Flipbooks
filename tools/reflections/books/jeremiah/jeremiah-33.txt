Then My covenant with David My servant can be broken. Notice that he should not have a son to reign upon his throne. And David will no longer have a descendant to reign on his throne. Notice and with the Levites the priests, my ministers. Nor will I keep My covenant with the Levites who are priests ministering before Me. Notice that God links two covenants. Notice that day and night cannot be broken. Notice that so David's covenant cannot be broken. Notice that his throne would be forever. Notice that Nathan first spoke this. Notice that God promised David a house. Notice that He promised an everlasting kingdom. 2 Samuel 7 says, 'thy throne shall be established for ever.' Notice for ever. Today, rest in promises God has made as sure as day and night.
S: Only then could God's covenant with David His servant be broken so that he had no descendant to reign on his throne, nor His covenant with the Levites who are priests ministering before Him. God tied His covenants with David and the priests to the unbreakable rhythm of day and night, staking His own reputation on them. Rest in promises that sure, for God told David, thy throne shall be established for ever.

== 33:22
T: As the host of heaven cannot be numbered
R: 'As the host of heaven cannot be numbered, neither the sand of the sea measured: so will I multiply the seed of David my servant, and the Levites that minister unto me.' Notice as the host of heaven cannot be numbered. I will make the descendants of David My servant and the Levites who minister before Me as countless as the stars in the sky. Notice neither the sand of the sea measured. And as measureless as the sand on the seashore. Notice so will I multiply the seed of David my servant, and the Levites that minister unto me. So I will multiply David's descendants and the Levites. Notice that this echoes Abraham. Notice that stars could not be counted. Notice that Abraham heard this first. Notice that God swore by Himself. Genesis 22 says, 'as the stars of the heaven, and as the sand which is upon the sea shore' Notice as the stars. Today, rejoice that God's family is beyond counting, and you can belong to it.
S: As the stars in the sky cannot be counted and the sand on the seashore cannot be measured, so God will multiply the descendants of David His servant and the Levites who minister before Him. Echoing His promise to Abraham, God answered the seeming end of David's line with countless descendants, a family that grows to include people from every nation. Rejoice that you can belong, for God promised a seed as the stars of the heaven, and as the sand which is upon the sea shore.
P: Beyond counting. Jeremiah 33:22: 'As the host of heaven cannot be numbered.' As the stars. As the stars of the heaven, and as the sand. Belong to God's family today. Take it to God in prayer.
