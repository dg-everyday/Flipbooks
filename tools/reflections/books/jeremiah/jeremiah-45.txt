# Jeremiah 45: Thy life will I give unto thee

This is what Jeremiah the prophet told Baruch son of Neriah. Notice when he had written these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah. After Baruch had written on a scroll the words Jeremiah was dictating. Notice in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, saying. In the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah. Notice that this chapter is short. Notice that it is personal. Notice that it is for one man. Notice that Isaiah heard this promise. Notice that God calls and redeems. Isaiah 43 says, 'I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.' Notice thou art mine. Today, know that God knows your name and calls you His own.

In this short, personal chapter the faithful scribe receives his own word from God, for God notices those in supporting roles. Know that He knows you, for I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.

== 45:5

Notice for, behold, I will bring evil upon all flesh, saith the LORD. For I will bring disaster on all people, declares the LORD. Notice but thy life will I give unto thee for a prey in all places whither thou goest. But wherever you go I will let you escape with your life. Notice that Baruch had ambitions. Notice that he wanted greatness. Notice that this was the wrong season. Notice that God gently corrects. Notice that He redirects his desire. Notice that He promises life. Notice that we should seek Him above all. Notice that Jesus taught this. Notice that seeking God first frees us. S: God asked Baruch whether he should seek great things for himself and told him not to, for He would bring disaster on all people, declares the LORD, but wherever Baruch went God would let him escape with his life. God gently redirected Baruch's ambition in a season of judgment, offering life itself as the prize, for ambition can add to sorrow while seeking God brings rest. Seek Him above all, for seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness.
P: Seek them not. Jeremiah 45:5: 'seekest thou great things for thyself? seek them not.' Seek first. Seek ye first the kingdom of God. Seek God first today. Trust Him with it today.
