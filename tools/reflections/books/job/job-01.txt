R: 'In all this Job sinned not, nor charged God foolishly.' Notice in all this. Every loss. Notice Job sinned not. Integrity. Notice nor charged God foolishly. No accusation. Notice that Job passed the first test. Notice that Satan was wrong. Notice that Job's faith was real. Notice that he did not blame God with folly. Notice that he worshipped instead. Notice that this is remarkable. Notice that it was not the end of his struggle. Notice that the Lord is full of pity. Notice that He is tender in mercy. Notice that Job's story ends in mercy. Notice that God's compassion frames the whole book. Notice that endurance is honoured. Notice that faith under fire is precious. James 5 says, 'Ye have heard of the patience of Job, and have seen the end of the Lord; that the Lord is very pitiful, and of tender mercy.' Notice God's tender mercy. Today, endure with faith and trust God's tender mercy. Your story is not over.
S: In all this, Job did not sin by charging God with wrongdoing. His faith proved real, though later chapters show he would still wrestle deeply, and his endurance is held up as an example. Endure with faith, for ye have heard of the patience of Job, and have seen the end of the Lord; that the Lord is very pitiful, and of tender mercy.
P: Sinned not. Job 1:22: 'In all this Job sinned not, nor charged God foolishly.' Endurance. The Lord is very pitiful, and of tender mercy.
