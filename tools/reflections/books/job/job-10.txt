R: 'A land of darkness, as darkness itself;' Notice a land of darkness. Gloom. Notice as darkness itself. Deep dark. Notice that it was without order. Chaos. Notice that even its light is darkness. Hopelessness. Notice that Job's lament ended in darkness. Notice that he saw no light at all. Notice that his despair was complete. Notice that Scripture lets this stand for now. Notice that the story continues. Notice that light will come. Notice that Job's darkness was not final. Notice that God would speak. Notice that light would break. Notice that eternity holds no night for God's people. Notice that Job's worst fear would be overturned. Notice that light wins. Notice that God is light. Notice that darkness cannot overcome it. Revelation 22 says, 'And there shall be no night there; and they need no candle, neither light of the sun; for the Lord God giveth them light.' Notice no night. Today, hold to the promise of a day with no night. Light wins.
S: Job described the land of deepest night, of utter darkness and disorder, where even the light is like darkness. His lament ended in total darkness, but the story continues toward the promise of eternal light. Hold to the promise of light, for there shall be no night there; for the Lord God giveth them light.
P: Darkness. Job 10:22: 'A land of darkness, as darkness itself.' No night there. The Lord God giveth them light. Live it out today.
