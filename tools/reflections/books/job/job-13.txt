Notice that if he had sinned, he wanted to know. Notice that this is a humble request. Notice that the psalmist prayed similarly. Notice that he asked to be shown any wicked way. Notice that this is healthy self-examination. Notice that it trusts God's verdict. Notice that it is not morbid. Notice that God would not list Job's sins. Today, invite God to search your heart. Honest examination brings peace.
