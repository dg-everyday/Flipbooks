S: Job said that in only a few years he would go on the journey of no return. He felt his time was short, yet God gave him many more years, and in Christ death becomes a journey home. See death in Christ with hope, for to be absent from the body is to be present with the Lord.
P: No return. Job 16:22: 'then I shall go the way whence I shall not return.' Going home. Absent from the body, present with the Lord.
