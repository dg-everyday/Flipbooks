== 27:23
T: Men shall clap their hands at him
R: 'Men shall clap their hands at him, and shall hiss him out of his place.' Notice men shall clap their hands. Scorn. Notice at him. Target. Notice and shall hiss him. Derision. Notice out of his place. Driven out. Notice that the wicked's fall brings public scorn. Notice that people clap in derision. Notice that they hiss. Notice that Job had experienced this scorn. Notice that he had been mocked. Notice that Christ experienced it too. Notice that passers-by mocked Him on the cross. Notice that they wagged their heads. Notice that they reviled Him. Notice that He was innocent. Notice that the scorn was unjust. Notice that God vindicated Him. Notice that Job's scorn was unjust too. Notice that God would vindicate him. Notice that public derision is not final. Notice that God's verdict is. Matthew 27 says, 'And they that passed by reviled him, wagging their heads.' Notice Christ mocked. Today, trust God's verdict over public scorn. Christ was mocked too.
S: Job said people clap their hands in derision and hiss the wicked man out of his place. Job himself had been scorned unjustly, as Christ was mocked by passers-by on the cross, yet both were vindicated by God. Trust God's verdict over public scorn, remembering that they that passed by reviled Him, wagging their heads.
P: Derided. Job 27:23: 'Men shall clap their hands at him.' Christ mocked. They that passed by reviled him. Let this shape your day.
