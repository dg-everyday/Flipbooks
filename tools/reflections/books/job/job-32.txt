S: Elihu said he did not know how to flatter, and if he did, his Maker would soon take him away. He answered to God, which made him honest, for truthful words help more than flattery. Speak honestly before your Maker, for he that rebuketh a man afterwards shall find more favour than he that flattereth with the tongue.
