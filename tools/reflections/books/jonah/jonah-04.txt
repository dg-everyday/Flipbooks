# Jonah 4: Should not I spare Nineveh

Notice but. Notice that God showed mercy. Notice that Jonah was angry. Notice that he did not want his enemies forgiven. Notice that his heart did not match God's. Notice that we can resent mercy shown to others. Notice that we want justice for them. Notice that we want mercy for ourselves. Notice that God's heart is bigger. Notice that He loves enemies. Notice that He calls us to do the same. Notice that the elder brother was angry too. Luke 15 says, 'And he was angry, and would not go in' Notice he was angry. Today, ask God to give you joy when others receive mercy.
S: When God spared Nineveh, Jonah thought it very wrong and became angry. He did not want his enemies forgiven, for his heart did not yet match God's larger mercy. Ask God for joy when others receive mercy, unlike the elder brother of whom it says, and he was angry, and would not go in.
P: Angry at mercy. Jonah 4:1: 'it displeased Jonah exceedingly.' Resentment. He was angry, and would not go in. Rejoice in mercy today. Take it to God in prayer.

S: Jonah prayed that this was why he fled to Tarshish, for he knew God was gracious and compassionate, slow to anger, abounding in love and relenting from calamity. He ran because God is merciful, yet what he complained of is our hope. Thank God for His character, for the LORD is gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy.
P: Gracious God. Jonah 4:2: 'thou art a gracious God, and merciful.' Compassion. The LORD is gracious, and full of compassion. Thank God for His mercy today. Trust Him with it today.
