== 4:24
T: That all the people of the earth might know
R: 'That all the people of the earth might know the hand of the LORD, that it is mighty: that ye might fear the LORD your God for ever.' So the world may know His might. Notice that all the people of the earth might know. A worldwide witness. Notice the hand of the LORD, that it is mighty. God's power displayed. Notice that ye might fear the LORD your God for ever. Lasting reverence for Israel. Notice the two purposes: witness to the nations and reverence in God's people. Notice that God's works are not private. They are meant to be seen. Notice that miracles should produce lasting awe, not just momentary excitement. Psalm 67 says, 'That thy way may be known upon earth, thy saving health among all nations.' Today, let what God has done for you lead to lasting reverence and a witness to others. His works are for His glory among all people.
S: Joshua explained that God dried up the Jordan so that all the people of the earth might know His mighty hand and Israel might fear Him for ever.
