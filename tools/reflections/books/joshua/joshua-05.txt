# Joshua 5: Circumcision at Gilgal, the manna ceases, and the captain of the LORD's host

== 5:1

Notice all the kings… heard. News of God's works spread fast. Notice the LORD had dried up the waters. They credited Israel's God. Notice their heart melted. Fear replaced resistance. Notice neither was there spirit in them. They lost the will to fight. Notice that God fought the battle in the enemy's heart before a sword was drawn. Notice that the miracle served as a witness to the nations. Notice that forty years earlier Israel's heart melted at reports of giants; now the giants' hearts melted. Exodus 15 says, 'all the inhabitants of Canaan shall melt away.' Today, trust that God can soften opposition before you face it. He goes ahead into hearts you cannot reach.
S: When all the kings of the Amorites and Canaanites heard that the LORD had dried up the Jordan, their hearts melted and their courage failed. God fought the battle in the enemy's heart before a sword was drawn, reversing the fear Israel once felt. Trust that God can soften opposition before you face it, for He goes ahead into hearts you cannot reach.
