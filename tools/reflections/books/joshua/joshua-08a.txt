# Joshua 8:1-17: Restored after failure, and the ambush at Ai

== 8:1
T: Fear not, neither be thou dismayed
R: 'And the LORD said unto Joshua, Fear not, neither be thou dismayed: take all the people of war with thee, and arise, go up to Ai: see, I have given into thy hand the king of Ai…' Notice fear not, neither be thou dismayed. God spoke comfort after failure. Notice take all the people of war. Not a small, overconfident force this time. Notice arise, go up to Ai. Back to the place of defeat. Notice I have given into thy hand. Victory promised again. Notice that once sin was dealt with, God's presence and promises returned. Notice that God sends us back to the place of failure to bring victory there. Micah 7 says, 'Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy: when I fall, I shall arise.' Today, if you have failed and repented, hear God's fear not. Go back with Him to the place of defeat. He gives victory there.
S: After Achan's sin was dealt with, the LORD told Joshua not to fear or be dismayed but to take all the people of war back to Ai, for He had given it into his hand. God's presence and promises returned after repentance, and He sent Israel back to the place of defeat to give victory there. If you have failed and repented, hear God's fear not and go back with Him to the place of defeat.
P: Back to Ai. Joshua 8:1: 'Fear not, neither be thou dismayed… arise, go up to Ai.' After repentance, God sends you back to the place of failure to win.

== 8:2
T: Lay thee an ambush
R: 'And thou shalt do to Ai and her king as thou didst unto Jericho and her king: only the spoil thereof, and the cattle thereof, shall ye take for a prey unto yourselves: lay thee an ambush for the city behind it.' Notice as thou didst unto Jericho. Judgment on the city. Notice only the spoil… shall ye take. This time God allowed them to keep the plunder. Notice the irony: if Achan had waited, he could have had spoil legally. Notice lay thee an ambush. God gave a different strategy. Notice that God's methods vary. At Jericho, walls fell; at Ai, an ambush. Notice that impatience costs us what God would have given. Notice that God's provision often comes if we wait for His timing. Psalm 37 says, 'Rest in the LORD, and wait patiently for him.' Today, do not grab what God has not yet given. Wait for His timing. What you take early, you may lose; what He gives, He blesses.
S: God told Joshua to treat Ai as Jericho but allowed Israel to take its spoil and cattle, and to lay an ambush behind the city. Had Achan waited, he could have had spoil legally, showing that impatience costs what God would give in His time. Do not grab what God has not yet given, but wait patiently for His timing and blessing.
P: If only Achan had waited. Joshua 8:2: 'only the spoil thereof, and the cattle thereof, shall ye take.' God gives in His timing. Rest in the LORD and wait.

== 8:6

Notice till we have drawn them from the city. Leaving it undefended. Notice they will say, They flee before us. Overconfidence. Notice as at the first. They expected history to repeat. S: Joshua expected Ai's men to come out and pursue, saying Israel fled as at the first, until they were drawn away from the city.
