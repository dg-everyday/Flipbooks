Notice heard thereof. Hearing produced a reaction. Notice that the same news that melted Rahab's heart hardened these kings' hearts. Notice that people respond differently to the same evidence of God's power. Some turn to Him; others resist. Notice that victory often stirs up new opposition. And examine your own response to God's works: do they soften or harden your heart?
S: All the kings of Canaan, in the hills, valleys and along the great sea, heard of Israel's victories. Expect that faithfulness may provoke resistance, and examine whether God's works soften or harden your own heart.
P: Same news, different hearts. Joshua 9:1: 'when all the kings… heard thereof.' Rahab believed; these kings resisted. How does God's work affect your heart?
