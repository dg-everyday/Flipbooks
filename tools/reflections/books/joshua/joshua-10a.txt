# Joshua 10:1-21: The day the sun stood still

== 10:1
T: Adoni-zedek heard
R: 'Now it came to pass, when Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem had heard how Joshua had taken Ai… and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them.' Notice Adoni-zedek. His name means lord of righteousness, yet he set himself against God. Notice king of Jerusalem. The city God would one day choose for His name. Notice had heard. News again provoked a response. Notice made peace with Israel. Gibeon's choice alarmed its neighbours. Notice that a name can claim righteousness while the heart resists God. Notice the contrast with Melchizedek, an earlier king of Salem who blessed Abraham. Hebrews 7 calls Melchizedek 'King of righteousness.' Today, let your life match what you profess. A righteous name means nothing without a heart surrendered to the true King of righteousness, Jesus Christ.
S: Adoni-zedek, king of Jerusalem, heard that Joshua had taken Ai and that Gibeon had made peace with Israel. His name meant lord of righteousness, yet he set himself against God, unlike Melchizedek, the earlier king of Salem. Let your life match what you profess, surrendering your heart to Jesus, the true King of righteousness.
P: A name versus a heart. Joshua 10:1: 'when Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem had heard.' His name meant lord of righteousness, yet he fought God. Let your life match what you profess.

S: The king of Jerusalem feared greatly because Gibeon was a great royal city, greater than Ai, with mighty men who had chosen peace with Israel. When the strong submit to God others take notice, and one decision to side with Him can influence many. Siding with God may unsettle others, but those who belong to Him need not fear.
P: When the strong submit. Joshua 10:2: 'they feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city… and all the men thereof were mighty.' Your choice for God can shake the world around you.
