Notice Joshua gave it for an inheritance. From conquest to gift. Notice according to their divisions by their tribes. Ordered sharing. Notice and the land rested from war. The goal was rest. Notice that Joshua points to Jesus, whose name is the same, who brings us into a greater rest. Hebrews 4 says, 'There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God.' Today, enter the rest Jesus gives. Your inheritance is secure, and He has won the war.
