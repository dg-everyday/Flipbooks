# Joshua 12: The kings God gave into Israel's hand

== 12:1
T: These are the kings

Notice now these are the kings. A record is being made. Notice which the children of Israel smote. Victories remembered. Notice and possessed their land. Promise turned into possession. Notice on the other side Jordan. The list begins east of the river, with the victories under Moses. Notice that before dividing the land, Israel paused to count God's victories. Notice that remembering is an act of worship. Notice that written records keep faith alive for future generations. Psalm 103 says, 'Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits.' Today, make a list of what God has done for you. Write it down. Counting His victories strengthens your faith for what lies ahead.
S: Joshua 12 records the kings Israel defeated and whose land they possessed, beginning east of the Jordan from the Arnon to mount Hermon. Before dividing the land, Israel paused to count God's victories, for remembering is an act of worship. Make a list of what God has done for you, because counting His victories strengthens your faith for what lies ahead.
