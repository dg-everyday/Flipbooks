# Joshua 13:1-14: Very much land yet to be possessed

== 13:1
T: Very much land to be possessed
R: 'Now Joshua was old and stricken in years; and the LORD said unto him, Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed.' Notice Joshua was old. Years had passed since the Jordan crossing. Notice the LORD said unto him. God spoke honestly about his age. Notice there remaineth yet very much land. The work was not finished. Notice that God did not flatter Joshua; He told him the truth. Notice that age did not end Joshua's usefulness. It changed his task, from conquest to distribution. Notice that every generation leaves work for the next. Psalm 92 says, 'They shall still bring forth fruit in old age.' Today, whatever your age, ask God what remains for you to do. There is always more land to possess in His kingdom. Your season may change, but your calling continues.
S: When Joshua was old, the LORD told him plainly that very much land remained to be possessed. God spoke honestly, and age did not end Joshua's usefulness but changed his task from conquest to distribution. Whatever your age, ask God what remains for you to do, for your season may change but your calling continues.
P: More land remains. Joshua 13:1: 'Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed.' Your season may change, but your calling continues.

== 13:2
T: The land that yet remaineth
R: 'This is the land that yet remaineth: all the borders of the Philistines, and all Geshuri.' This is the land that remains: all the regions of the Philistines and the Geshurites. Notice this is the land that yet remaineth. God named what was left. Notice all the borders of the Philistines. The coastal plain. Notice and all Geshuri. A region to the south. Notice that God was specific about the unfinished work. Notice that the Philistines, left unconquered, would trouble Israel for centuries. Notice that God does not hide the incomplete parts of our story. Notice that honest assessment is the beginning of further progress. Philippians 3 says, 'Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect: but I follow after.' Today, ask God to show you specifically what remains unfinished in your life. Name it honestly. Clear sight of what remains is the first step toward taking it.
S: God named the land that remained, including all the borders of the Philistines and Geshuri. He was specific about the unfinished work, and the Philistines left unconquered would trouble Israel for centuries. Ask God to show you specifically what remains unfinished in your life, for clear sight is the first step toward taking it.
P: Name what remains. Joshua 13:2: 'This is the land that yet remaineth: all the borders of the Philistines.' Ask God to show you what's unfinished in your life.

== 13:3
T: Five lords of the Philistines
R: 'From Sihor, which is before Egypt, even unto the borders of Ekron northward, which is counted to the Canaanite: five lords of the Philistines; the Gazathites, and the Ashdothites, the Eshkalonites, the Gittites, and the Ekronites; also the Avites.' Notice from Sihor… before Egypt. The southern edge. Notice five lords of the Philistines. Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath and Ekron. Notice that these five cities would be Israel's enemies through the time of Samson, Saul and David. Notice that Goliath would come from Gath. Notice that Judges 3 says, 'these are the nations which the LORD left, to prove Israel by them.' Notice that God used remaining enemies to test and train His people. Today, see your ongoing struggles as part of God's training. He uses them to teach you dependence on Him.
S: The remaining land ran from Sihor before Egypt to Ekron and included the five lords of the Philistines at Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath and Ekron. These cities would trouble Israel for generations, as God left nations to prove His people. See your ongoing struggles as part of God's training, teaching you dependence on Him.
P: Left to prove Israel. Joshua 13:3: 'five lords of the Philistines.' God used remaining enemies to train His people. Your struggles can teach dependence on Him.

== 13:4
T: All the land of the Canaanites
R: 'From the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah that is beside the Sidonians, unto Aphek, to the borders of the Amorites.' Notice from the south. The list continues. Notice all the land of the Canaanites. Large areas remained. Notice Mearah that is beside the Sidonians. Toward the north coast. Notice unto Aphek. Another Aphek, in the north. Notice to the borders of the Amorites. Notice that the conquest, though great, was far from complete. Notice that the summary in chapter 11 described the war as done, yet much land remained. Notice that both statements are true: the decisive battles were won, but full possession would take time. Notice that this is also true of the Christian life. Hebrews 10 says, 'by one offering he hath perfected for ever them that are sanctified.' Today, rejoice that Christ's victory is complete, and keep growing into it day by day.
S: Much land remained, from the south through the land of the Canaanites, Mearah beside the Sidonians and on to Aphek. The decisive battles were won, but full possession would take time, as is also true of the Christian life. Rejoice that Christ's victory is complete, and keep growing into it day by day.
P: Won and still growing. Joshua 13:4: 'all the land of the Canaanites… unto Aphek.' The decisive battle is won in Christ. Keep growing into that victory day by day.

== 13:5

The region of Gebal, on the northern coast. Notice and all Lebanon. The mountains famous for cedars. Notice toward the sunrising. Eastward. Notice unto the entering into Hamath. The far northern border. Notice that God's promise reached further than Israel had yet gone. Notice that Lebanon's cedars would later build the temple. Notice that the land God promised included resources for worship. Psalm 92 says, 'he shall grow like a cedar in Lebanon.' Notice that God's vision for His people is always larger than their present reach. Today, do not settle for less than God intends. His purposes for your life reach beyond where you are now. Let Him enlarge your vision and grow you like a cedar.
S: The remaining land included the Giblites, all Lebanon and the region from Baal-gad under Hermon to the entering into Hamath. God's promise reached further than Israel had gone, and Lebanon's cedars would later build the temple. Do not settle for less than God intends, but let Him enlarge your vision and grow you like a cedar.
P: Larger than your reach. Joshua 13:5: 'and all Lebanon, toward the sunrising.' God's vision for you is bigger than where you are now. Grow like a cedar in Lebanon.

== 13:6
T: Them will I drive out
R: 'All the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon unto Misrephoth-maim, and all the Sidonians, them will I drive out from before the children of Israel: only divide thou it by lot unto the Israelites for an inheritance, as I have commanded thee.' Notice them will I drive out. God took responsibility for the remaining work. Notice from before the children of Israel. God would act through His people. Notice only divide thou it by lot. Joshua's task was simpler: distribute. Notice for an inheritance. Even unconquered land was already assigned. Notice that God told Joshua to divide land not yet possessed. Notice that faith treats God's promises as already given. Notice that each tribe would finish what Joshua began. First Thessalonians 5 says, 'Faithful is he that calleth you, who also will do it.' Today, trust God with what you cannot finish. Do your part, and let Him do His.
S: God promised to drive out the inhabitants of the remaining hill country and told Joshua simply to divide it by lot as an inheritance. Joshua was to assign land not yet possessed, treating God's promise as already given. Trust God with what you cannot finish, doing your part and letting Him do His.
P: Do your part. Joshua 13:6: 'them will I drive out… only divide thou it by lot.' God takes responsibility for what you can't finish. Faithful is He who calls you.

== 13:7
T: Divide this land
R: 'Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance unto the nine tribes, and the half tribe of Manasseh.' So now divide this land as an inheritance among the nine tribes and half of the tribe of Manasseh. Notice now therefore. Because God promised. Notice divide this land. A new phase. Notice for an inheritance. Not a reward but a gift. Notice unto the nine tribes, and the half tribe. Every tribe included. Notice that the work shifted from fighting to receiving. Notice that inheritance is received, not earned. Notice that each tribe had a place. Notice that no tribe was forgotten. First Peter 1 speaks of 'an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away.' Today, rejoice in the inheritance God gives you in Christ. It is a gift, not wages. And it is reserved for you; no one is left out of His plan.
S: God told Joshua to divide the land as an inheritance among the nine tribes and half of Manasseh. The work shifted from fighting to receiving, for inheritance is a gift, not a reward, and every tribe had a place. Rejoice in the inheritance God gives you in Christ, which is a gift reserved for you.
P: A gift, not wages. Joshua 13:7: 'divide this land for an inheritance unto the nine tribes, and the half tribe of Manasseh.' Your inheritance in Christ is a gift reserved for you.

== 13:8
T: Beyond Jordan eastward
R: 'With whom the Reubenites and the Gadites have received their inheritance, which Moses gave them, beyond Jordan eastward, even as Moses the servant of the LORD gave them.' Notice the Reubenites and the Gadites have received. Their portion was already given. Notice which Moses gave them. Under the previous leader. Notice beyond Jordan eastward. Outside the main land. Notice even as Moses the servant of the LORD gave them. Joshua honoured that decision. Notice that these tribes had chosen land that looked good for cattle. Notice that they kept their promise to fight alongside their brothers. Notice that Joshua respected what Moses had done instead of undoing it. Notice that continuity honours God's work. Romans 13 says, 'Render therefore to all their dues… honour to whom honour.' Today, respect the commitments and decisions of those who served before you. Build on them rather than tearing them down.
S: The Reubenites and Gadites had already received their inheritance from Moses beyond the Jordan eastward. Joshua honoured that decision rather than undoing it, and those tribes had kept their promise to fight alongside their brothers. Respect the commitments of those who served before you, building on them rather than tearing them down.
P: Honouring the past. Joshua 13:8: 'which Moses gave them, beyond Jordan eastward, even as Moses the servant of the LORD gave them.' Build on what others began.

One tribe was different. Notice he gave none inheritance. No territory. Notice the sacrifices… are their inheritance. Their portion was in God's service. Notice as he said unto them. God had promised this. Notice that the Levites gave up land to serve at God's altar. Notice that their provision came through worship. Notice that their inheritance was not less, but different. Notice that serving God was itself the reward. Numbers 18 records God saying, 'I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel.' Today, remember that God Himself is your greatest inheritance. Whatever you lack on earth, if you have Him, you have everything.
S: The tribe of Levi received no land, for the sacrifices of the LORD made by fire were their inheritance. They gave up territory to serve at God's altar, and their portion was not less but different. Remember that God Himself is your greatest inheritance, for if you have Him you have everything.
