# Joshua 15:1-20: Judah's borders and Caleb's household

== 15:1
T: The lot of the tribe of Judah
R: 'This then was the lot of the tribe of the children of Judah by their families; even to the border of Edom the wilderness of Zin southward was the uttermost part of the south coast.' Notice this then was the lot. The first allotment west of the Jordan. Notice the tribe of the children of Judah. Judah came first. Notice by their families. Carefully ordered. Notice to the border of Edom. Esau's descendants were neighbours. Notice that Judah, the royal tribe, received the first lot. Notice that Jacob had prophesied that kings would come from Judah. Genesis 49 says, 'The sceptre shall not depart from Judah.' Notice that David would come from Judah, and from David's line, Jesus. Notice that the lot fell in line with God's long plan. Today, trust that God's plans unfold in order. What He promised long ago is still unfolding. Jesus, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, reigns.
S: The first lot west of the Jordan fell to Judah, reaching south to the border of Edom and the wilderness of Zin. Judah was the royal tribe from whom David and ultimately Jesus would come, as Jacob had prophesied. Trust that God's plans unfold in order, for Jesus, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, reigns.
P: Judah first. Joshua 15:1: 'This then was the lot of the tribe of the children of Judah.' The royal tribe received the first lot. Jesus, the Lion of Judah, reigns.
