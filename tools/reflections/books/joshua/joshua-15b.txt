# Joshua 15:21-41: The cities of the south and the lowland

== 15:21
T: The uttermost cities toward Edom
R: 'And the uttermost cities of the tribe of the children of Judah toward the coast of Edom southward were Kabzeel, and Eder, and Jagur.' Notice the uttermost cities. The farthest south. Notice toward the coast of Edom. Frontier towns. Notice Kabzeel. The hometown of one of David's bravest men. Second Samuel 23 describes Benaiah as 'the son of a valiant man, of Kabzeel.' Notice that Benaiah went down into a pit on a snowy day and slew a lion. Notice that heroes can come from frontier towns. Notice that the edges of the land produced courage. Notice that small places can raise great faith. Notice that God's list includes towns whose people would one day serve the king. Today, do not despise where you come from. God raises valiant people from the edges. Your frontier can be a training ground for courage.
S: The southernmost cities of Judah toward Edom were Kabzeel, Eder and Jagur. Kabzeel was the hometown of Benaiah, one of David's bravest men, showing that heroes can come from frontier towns. Do not despise where you come from, for God raises valiant people from the edges.
P: Heroes from the edges. Joshua 15:21: 'the uttermost cities… were Kabzeel, and Eder, and Jagur.' Kabzeel raised Benaiah, a valiant man. God raises courage from the edges.

Take comfort that you are known by God, for even if your name never appears in history, it is engraved on His hands.

Today, rest in the truth that God knows you individually. You are not confused with anyone else. He calls you by your own name and knows your own story.
S: Judah's southern towns included Kedesh, Hazor and Ithnan, sharing names with more famous northern places. God does not confuse them, for He distinguishes each place and person precisely. Rest in the truth that God knows you individually and calls you by your own name.
P: Known by name. Joshua 15:23: 'And Kedesh, and Hazor, and Ithnan.' A different Hazor, not confused with the famous one. God calls His own sheep by name.

First Samuel 23 says Jonathan 'strengthened his hand in God.' Notice that in a place of betrayal, God sent a true friend. Notice that encouragement shines brightest in dark places. Notice that a friend who points you to God is worth more than one who only sympathises. Notice that Jonathan asked nothing for himself. Today, if you feel betrayed, look for the Jonathan God sends. And be a Jonathan to someone else. Strengthen their hand in God. Encouragement can come in the wilderness.
S: Judah's towns included Ziph, Telem and Bealoth. In the wilderness of Ziph, David was betrayed, yet there Jonathan came and strengthened his hand in God. If you feel betrayed, look for the friend God sends, and be that kind of friend to someone else.
P: A friend in the wilderness. Joshua 15:24: 'Ziph, and Telem, and Bealoth.' At Ziph, Jonathan strengthened David's hand in God. Be that friend to someone today.
