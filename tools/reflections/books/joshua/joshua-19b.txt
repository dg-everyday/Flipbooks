# Joshua 19:17-31: Issachar and Asher

== 19:17
T: The fourth lot to Issachar
R: 'And the fourth lot came out to Issachar, for the children of Issachar according to their families.' The fourth lot came out for Issachar, clan by clan. Notice the fourth lot came out. Issachar's turn. Notice for the children of Issachar. A tribe from Leah. Notice according to their families. Each household included. Notice that Issachar later became known for wisdom about the times. First Chronicles 12 describes them as 'men that had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do.' Notice that a tribe receiving its land would one day give Israel discernment. Notice that understanding the times is a gift God's people still need. Notice that knowledge must lead to right action. Notice that Issachar's men knew what Israel ought to do. Today, ask God for understanding of your times. Read the world through the lens of His word. And let your understanding lead to faithful action.
S: The fourth lot came out to Issachar according to their families. Issachar later became known for men who understood the times and knew what Israel ought to do. Ask God for understanding of your times through His word, and let that understanding lead to faithful action.
P: Understanding the times. Joshua 19:17: 'the fourth lot came out to Issachar.' Issachar's men later understood the times and knew what to do. Ask God for that discernment.
H: #DailyGrace #Joshua1917 #Discernment #Wisdom #Action #BibleVerse

== 19:18
T: Shunem
R: 'And their border was toward Jezreel, and Chesulloth, and Shunem.' Notice toward Jezreel. The great valley. Notice and Chesulloth. A border town. Notice and Shunem. A town known for a remarkable woman. Notice that a woman of Shunem later built a room for the prophet Elisha. Second Kings 4 records her saying, 'this is an holy man of God, which passeth by us continually.' Notice that her hospitality was rewarded with a son. Notice that when the son died, Elisha raised him to life. Notice that simple kindness opened the way for a miracle. Hebrews 13 says, 'Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares.' Today, practise hospitality. Make room in your home for God's servants and strangers. You never know what blessing God will bring through an open door and a prepared room.
S: Issachar's border reached toward Jezreel, Chesulloth and Shunem. A woman of Shunem later prepared a room for Elisha, and her hospitality opened the way for a son and a miracle of life. Practise hospitality and make room for God's servants and strangers, for God brings blessing through an open door.
P: A room for the prophet. Joshua 19:18: 'and Shunem.' The Shunammite made room for Elisha and saw a miracle. Practise hospitality. Be not forgetful to entertain strangers.
H: #DailyGrace #Joshua1918 #Hospitality #Kindness #Blessing #BibleVerse

== 19:19
T: Haphraim, Shihon and Anaharath
R: 'And Haphraim, and Shihon, and Anaharath.' Notice Haphraim. A town of Issachar. Notice and Shihon. Another. Notice and Anaharath. A third. Notice that Jacob had described Issachar as a strong donkey resting between burdens. Genesis 49 says of him, 'he saw that rest was good, and the land that it was pleasant; and bowed his shoulder to bear.' Notice that Issachar's fertile land was pleasant. Notice that love of comfort could lead to servitude. Notice that rest is good, but rest can become complacency. Notice that pleasant land called for vigilance. Notice that a people who prize ease may accept bondage to keep it. Today, enjoy God's good gifts, but do not trade freedom for comfort. Do not bow your shoulder to burdens God never meant you to bear. Rest in Him, not in ease.
S: Issachar's towns included Haphraim, Shihon and Anaharath. Jacob said Issachar saw that rest was good and the land pleasant, and bowed his shoulder to bear, warning that love of comfort can lead to servitude. Enjoy God's good gifts without trading freedom for comfort, resting in Him rather than in ease.
P: Rest or complacency? Joshua 19:19: 'And Haphraim, and Shihon, and Anaharath.' Issachar loved pleasant rest and bowed to burdens. Don't trade freedom for comfort.
H: #DailyGrace #Joshua1919 #Comfort #Freedom #Vigilance #BibleVerse

== 19:20
T: Rabbith, Kishion and Abez
R: 'And Rabbith, and Kishion, and Abez.' Notice Rabbith. A town of Issachar. Notice and Kishion. Later a Levitical city. Notice and Abez. A small town. Notice that Issachar's tribal image was a burden-bearer. Notice that every person carries some burden. Notice that some burdens are imposed by others and some are chosen. Notice that Jesus invites us to exchange heavy burdens for His. Matthew 11 records Him saying, 'For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.' Notice that a yoke is still a yoke; Christ does not promise no work. Notice that His burden is light because He carries it with us. Notice that Kishion would become home to Levites who bore the burden of service. Today, bring your heavy burdens to Jesus. Take His yoke instead. Serving with Him is lighter than striving alone.
S: Issachar's towns included Rabbith, Kishion and Abez. Issachar was pictured as a burden-bearer, and Jesus invites us to exchange heavy burdens for His easy yoke and light burden. Bring your heavy burdens to Jesus and take His yoke, for serving with Him is lighter than striving alone.
P: An easy yoke. Joshua 19:20: 'And Rabbith, and Kishion, and Abez.' Issachar bore burdens. Jesus says: my yoke is easy, and my burden is light. Bring Him yours.
H: #DailyGrace #Joshua1920 #Rest #Burdens #Jesus #BibleVerse

== 19:21
T: En-gannim
R: 'And Remeth, and En-gannim, and En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez.' Notice Remeth. A height. Notice and En-gannim. Spring of gardens. Notice and En-haddah. Spring of sharpness. Notice and Beth-pazzez. A house of dispersion. Notice that En-gannim lay on the edge of the valley at the route into Samaria. Notice that travellers between Galilee and Jerusalem passed this way. Notice that Jesus chose to travel through Samaria when others avoided it. John 4 says, 'And he must needs go through Samaria.' Notice that His route led to a woman at a well and a whole town's faith. Notice that springs and travel routes are places for encounters. Today, do not avoid the roads others shun. Jesus went where others would not go. Be willing to cross boundaries for the sake of those who need living water.
S: Issachar's towns included Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah and Beth-pazzez. En-gannim lay on the route into Samaria, the road Jesus chose when He must needs go through Samaria and met a woman at a well. Do not avoid the roads others shun, but be willing to cross boundaries for those who need living water.
P: He must needs go through Samaria. Joshua 19:21: 'and En-gannim.' This town lay on the road to Samaria. Jesus went where others avoided. Cross boundaries for His sake.
H: #DailyGrace #Joshua1921 #Mission #Boundaries #Love #BibleVerse

== 19:22
T: Tabor
R: 'And the coast reacheth to Tabor, and Shahazimah, and Beth-shemesh; and the outgoings of their border were at Jordan: sixteen cities with their villages.' Notice the coast reacheth to Tabor. The rounded mountain. Notice and Shahazimah. A height. Notice and Beth-shemesh. House of the sun, a northern town of that name. Notice at Jordan. The eastern edge. Notice sixteen cities with their villages. Issachar's total. Notice that Mount Tabor stood as a landmark visible across the valley. Notice that the psalmist made it a picture of praise. Psalm 89 says, 'Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.' Notice that even mountains are pictured as praising God. Notice that creation joins in worship. Notice that the land itself was meant to declare God's glory. Today, join creation in praise. Let the mountains remind you to rejoice in God's name. If Tabor rejoices, so can you.
S: Issachar's border reached to Tabor, Shahazimah and Beth-shemesh and ended at the Jordan, sixteen cities with their villages. Mount Tabor, visible across the valley, is pictured in Psalm 89 as rejoicing in God's name. Join creation in praise, letting the mountains remind you to rejoice in the name of the LORD.
P: Mountains rejoice. Joshua 19:22: 'the coast reacheth to Tabor.' Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name. If the mountains praise God, so can you.
H: #DailyGrace #Joshua1922 #Praise #Creation #Worship #BibleVerse

Named fully. Notice according to their families. Each household. Notice the cities and their villages. Both. Notice that Issachar received some of the most fertile land in the country. Notice that the valley of Jezreel was a breadbasket. Notice that fertile land is a gift and a responsibility. Notice that abundance calls for gratitude and generosity. Deuteronomy 8 says, 'When thou hast eaten and art full, then thou shalt bless the LORD thy God for the good land which he hath given thee.' Today, bless the LORD for the good things He has given you. When you are full, remember to give thanks.
S: This was the inheritance of Issachar by their families, cities and villages. Issachar received some of the most fertile land in the country, a gift that called for gratitude and generosity. Bless the LORD for the good things He has given you, remembering to give thanks when you are full.
P: When you are full. Joshua 19:23: 'This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Issachar.' Fertile land, rich gifts. When thou hast eaten and art full, bless the LORD.

== 19:24
T: The fifth lot for Asher
R: 'And the fifth lot came out for the tribe of the children of Asher according to their families.' The fifth lot came out for the tribe of Asher, clan by clan. Notice the fifth lot came out. Asher's turn. Notice for the tribe of the children of Asher. A son of Leah's servant Zilpah. Notice according to their families. Every household. Notice that Asher's name means happy or blessed. Notice that Leah named him saying she was happy. Notice that Moses blessed Asher with abundance. Deuteronomy 33 says, 'Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil.' Notice that the blessing included both prosperity and good relationships. Notice that being acceptable to one's brothers is a blessing worth seeking. Today, seek the blessing of good relationships. Be a person who is acceptable to your brothers and sisters, a source of happiness to others.
S: The fifth lot came out for Asher, whose name means happy or blessed. Moses blessed Asher with children, acceptance among his brothers and abundance, joining prosperity with good relationships. Seek the blessing of good relationships, being a source of happiness to your brothers and sisters.
P: Happy and blessed. Joshua 19:24: 'the fifth lot came out for the tribe of the children of Asher.' Asher means happy. Be acceptable to your brethren, a source of joy to others.

== 19:25
T: Helkath
R: 'And their border was Helkath, and Hali, and Beten, and Achshaph.' Notice Helkath. A name meaning portion or smooth field. Notice and Hali. A town. Notice and Beten. Another. Notice and Achshaph. A city whose king Joshua had defeated. Notice that Asher's border began with a town named portion. Notice that every tribe received its portion, but the deepest portion is God Himself. Psalm 73 says, 'God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.' Notice that land portions could be lost, but God as our portion never fails. Notice that Asaph wrote this after envying the prosperous. Notice that he found contentment by looking to God. Today, make God your portion. When you are tempted to envy others' portions, remember that He is the strength of your heart and your portion forever.
S: Asher's border included Helkath, Hali, Beten and Achshaph, and Helkath means portion. Land portions could be lost, but God as our portion never fails, as the psalmist found after envying the prosperous. Make God your portion, for He is the strength of your heart and your portion forever.
P: My portion for ever. Joshua 19:25: 'their border was Helkath.' Helkath means portion. God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.

== 19:26
T: Carmel westward
R: 'And Alammelech, and Amad, and Misheal; and reacheth to Carmel westward.' Notice Alammelech. A name possibly meaning king's oak. Notice and Amad. A town. Notice and Misheal. Another. Notice reacheth to Carmel westward. The great mountain by the sea. Notice that Mount Carmel would later be the scene of Elijah's contest with the prophets of Baal. First Kings 18 says, 'Then the fire of the LORD fell, and consumed the burnt sacrifice.' Notice that God answered by fire when Elijah prayed. Notice that the people fell on their faces in worship. Notice that Asher's border mountain became the place where God proved Himself. Notice that one faithful man stood against many. Today, trust the God who answers by fire. Stand for Him even when outnumbered. He will vindicate His name.
S: Asher's border included Alammelech, Amad and Misheal and reached to Carmel westward. On Mount Carmel, Elijah later prayed and the fire of the LORD fell, proving God against the prophets of Baal. Trust the God who answers by fire and stand for Him even when outnumbered.
P: Fire on Carmel. Joshua 19:26: 'reacheth to Carmel westward.' On Carmel, the fire of the LORD fell when Elijah prayed. Stand for God even when outnumbered.

== 19:27
T: Cabul
R: 'And turneth toward the sunrising to Beth-dagon, and reacheth to Zebulun, and to the valley of Jiphthah-el toward the north side of Beth-emek, and Neiel, and goeth out to Cabul on the left hand.' Notice toward the sunrising. Eastward. Notice to Beth-dagon. Another house of Dagon. Notice reacheth to Zebulun. Neighbours. Notice to Cabul. A town later involved in a famous disappointment. Notice that Solomon gave Hiram king of Tyre twenty cities in this region. Notice that Hiram was unimpressed. First Kings 9 says, 'And he called them the land of Cabul unto this day,' meaning as good as nothing. Notice that a gift that cost little was valued little. Second Samuel 24 records David saying he would not offer to God 'of that which doth cost me nothing.' Today, give God and others your best. Do not offer leftovers. Gifts that cost you something honour the receiver.
S: Asher's border turned east to Beth-dagon, reached Zebulun and the valley of Jiphthah-el and went out to Cabul. Solomon later gave cities here to Hiram, who called them Cabul, as good as nothing, a gift valued little because it cost little. Give God and others your best rather than leftovers, for gifts that cost you something honour the receiver.
P: As good as nothing? Joshua 19:27: 'and goeth out to Cabul.' Hiram called Solomon's gift worthless. Don't offer God what costs you nothing. Give your best.

== 19:29

Notice the coast turneth to Ramah. A height. Notice and to the strong city Tyre. A fortified island city. Notice to Hosah. A refuge. Notice at the sea… to Achzib. The coastline. Notice that Tyre became a symbol of pride and commercial power. Ezekiel 28 says to its ruler, 'Because thine heart is lifted up, and thou hast said, I am a God.' Notice that Tyre's strength led to arrogance. Notice that strong cities can become proud hearts. Notice that God humbled Tyre in time. Notice that Hosah, a name linked with refuge, sits beside it. Today, find your refuge in God, not in your strength. Do not let success lift up your heart. Pride goes before a fall; humility finds refuge in Him.
S: Asher's border turned to Ramah, the strong city Tyre and Hosah, ending at the sea by Achzib. Tyre became a symbol of pride, its ruler's heart lifted up until God humbled it, while Hosah's name speaks of refuge. Find your refuge in God rather than your strength, and do not let success lift up your heart.
P: Strong city, proud heart. Joshua 19:29: 'and to the strong city Tyre.' Tyre's heart was lifted up. Find your refuge in God, not in your strength.

== 19:30
T: Twenty and two cities

Notice twenty and two cities with their villages. Asher's total. Notice that Asher's list was long and its land rich. Notice that Judges later reports Asher's failure. Judges 1 says, 'But the Asherites dwelt among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land: for they did not drive them out.' Notice the reversal: instead of the Canaanites dwelling among Asher, Asher dwelt among them. Notice that compromise can make God's people the minority in their own inheritance. Notice that a long list on paper did not guarantee possession. Today, do not let the world absorb you. Live in your inheritance as God's people, not as guests among the culture. Be distinct and faithful.

Yet Judges reports that the Asherites dwelt among the Canaanites, becoming a minority in their own inheritance through compromise. Do not let the world absorb you, but live distinctly and faithfully as God's people.
P: Absorbed? Joshua 19:30: 'twenty and two cities with their villages.' Later, Asher dwelt among the Canaanites. Don't let the world absorb you. Be distinct and faithful.

== 19:31
T: The inheritance of Asher
R: 'This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Asher according to their families, these cities with their villages.' These towns and their villages were the inheritance of the tribe of Asher, clan by clan. Notice this is the inheritance. Asher's portion completed. Notice according to their families. Each household. Notice these cities with their villages. All counted. Notice that though the tribe later struggled, faithful individuals remained. Notice that centuries later, a woman of Asher waited in the temple for the Messiah. Luke 2 describes Anna, who 'departed not from the temple, but served God with fastings and prayers night and day.' Notice that Anna saw the infant Jesus and spoke of Him to all who looked for redemption. Notice that one faithful widow from a struggling tribe welcomed the Saviour. Today, be faithful even when your community drifts. Wait, pray and serve. God honours faithful individuals.
S: This was the inheritance of Asher by their families, its cities and villages. Though the tribe later struggled, Anna of Asher served God in the temple with fasting and prayer and welcomed the infant Jesus. Be faithful even when your community drifts, waiting, praying and serving, for God honours faithful individuals.
P: Faithful Anna. Joshua 19:31: 'This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Asher.' Anna of Asher prayed night and day and saw the Saviour. God honours faithful individuals.
