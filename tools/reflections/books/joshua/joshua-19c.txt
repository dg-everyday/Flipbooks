# Joshua 19:32-51: Naphtali, Dan and Joshua's city

== 19:32
T: The sixth lot to Naphtali
R: 'The sixth lot came out to the children of Naphtali, even for the children of Naphtali according to their families.' The sixth lot came out for Naphtali, clan by clan. Notice the sixth lot came out. Naphtali's turn. Notice to the children of Naphtali. A son of Rachel's servant Bilhah. Notice according to their families. Each household included. Notice that Jacob blessed Naphtali with an image of freedom. Genesis 49 says, 'Naphtali is a hind let loose: he giveth goodly words.' Notice that a deer set free is swift and graceful. Notice that Naphtali was to be known for goodly words. Notice that freedom and good speech go together. Notice that this tribe would later fight bravely under Barak. Today, let your freedom in Christ be expressed in goodly words. Speak words that bless, encourage and give life. A heart set free should overflow in gracious speech.
S: The sixth lot came out to Naphtali according to their families. Jacob pictured Naphtali as a deer let loose who gives goodly words, joining freedom with gracious speech. Let your freedom in Christ be expressed in goodly words that bless, encourage and give life.
P: Goodly words. Joshua 19:32: 'The sixth lot came out to the children of Naphtali.' Naphtali is a hind let loose: he giveth goodly words. Let your freedom overflow in gracious speech.

== 19:35
T: Chinnereth
R: 'And the fenced cities are Ziddim, Zer, and Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth.' Notice the fenced cities. Fortified towns. Notice Ziddim, Zer. Small strongholds. Notice and Hammath. Hot springs. Notice Rakkath. Near the lakeshore. Notice and Chinnereth. The town that named the Sea of Galilee. Notice that Naphtali's land bordered the sea where Jesus would later calm the storm. Mark 4 records Jesus saying, 'Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.' Notice that the disciples, fishermen on that sea, were terrified. Notice that Jesus slept, then spoke peace. Notice that the same waters that frightened them obeyed Him. Notice that fenced cities offered human security, but Jesus offered true peace. Today, bring your storms to Jesus. The One who calmed Chinnereth's waters can calm your heart. Hear Him say, Peace, be still.
S: Naphtali's fenced cities included Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath and Chinnereth, by the Sea of Galilee. On that sea Jesus later rebuked the storm, saying Peace, be still, and there was a great calm. Bring your storms to Jesus, for the One who calmed those waters can calm your heart.
P: Peace, be still. Joshua 19:35: 'and Chinnereth.' On this sea Jesus calmed the storm. The One who stilled those waters can still your heart.
