# Joshua 21:1-19: Cities for the Levites and the priests

== 21:1
T: The heads of the Levites came near
R: 'Then came near the heads of the fathers of the Levites unto Eleazar the priest, and unto Joshua the son of Nun, and unto the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel.' Notice then came near. The Levites took initiative. Notice the heads of the fathers of the Levites. Family leaders. Notice unto Eleazar… Joshua… and the heads of the fathers. The proper authorities. Notice that the Levites, like Zelophehad's daughters and Caleb, came to claim what God had promised. Notice that God's promises were not automatically delivered; people asked. Notice that asking is part of receiving. Notice that the Levites did not assume they would be remembered. James 4 says, 'ye have not, because ye ask not.' Notice that bold, humble asking is a pattern in this book. Today, ask God for what He has promised. Come near and make your request. Do not assume; ask, and receive.
S: The heads of the Levite families came near to Eleazar, Joshua and the tribal leaders. Like Caleb and Zelophehad's daughters, they came to claim what God had promised rather than assuming they would be remembered. Ask God for what He has promised, for ye have not because ye ask not.
P: Come near and ask. Joshua 21:1: 'Then came near the heads of the fathers of the Levites.' They asked for what God promised. Ye have not, because ye ask not.

Notice with the suburbs thereof for our cattle. Pasture for their animals. Notice that those who served God still needed homes and food. Notice that God provided for His ministers through His people. Notice that ministry is not meant to leave servants destitute. Notice that the request was grounded in Scripture. First Corinthians 9 says, 'they which preach the gospel should live of the gospel.' Today, support those who serve God among you. Help provide for their needs. God's servants are cared for through God's people.
S: At Shiloh, the Levites reminded the leaders that the LORD commanded through Moses to give them cities to dwell in with pasture for their cattle. Those who served God still needed homes and food, and God provided for them through His people. Support those who serve God among you, for God's servants are cared for through God's people.
P: Homes for God's servants. Joshua 21:2: 'The LORD commanded… to give us cities to dwell in.' Those who preach the gospel should live of the gospel. Support those who serve.

S: The children of Israel gave the Levites cities and pasture lands out of their own inheritance, as the LORD commanded. Each tribe's new inheritance was immediately shared, joining receiving with giving. Give cheerfully out of what God has given you, holding it loosely enough to open your hand.
P: Giving from the gift. Joshua 21:3: 'the children of Israel gave unto the Levites out of their inheritance.' What God gives is meant to be shared. God loves a cheerful giver.

== 21:4
T: The children of Aaron
R: 'And the lot came out for the families of the Kohathites: and the children of Aaron the priest, which were of the Levites, had by lot out of the tribe of Judah, and out of the tribe of Simeon, and out of the tribe of Benjamin, thirteen cities.' Notice thirteen cities. Notice that the priests' cities fell near where Jerusalem and the temple would later be. Notice that the lot placed the priests exactly where they would one day be needed. Notice that God arranged the future through a lot cast centuries before the temple. Isaiah 46 speaks of God 'Declaring the end from the beginning.' Notice that God's providence works through ordinary means. Today, trust that God is arranging your life for purposes you cannot yet see. Where He places you now may matter greatly later.
S: The Kohathite priests, the children of Aaron, received thirteen cities by lot from Judah, Simeon and Benjamin. The lot placed them near where Jerusalem and the temple would later stand, as God declared the end from the beginning. Trust that God is arranging your life for purposes you cannot yet see.
P: Placed for the future. Joshua 21:4: 'the children of Aaron… out of the tribe of Judah… thirteen cities.' Near the future temple. God declares the end from the beginning.
