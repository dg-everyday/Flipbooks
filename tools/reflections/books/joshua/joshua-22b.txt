# Joshua 22:21-34: The altar Ed, a witness that the LORD is God

== 22:21
T: Then they answered
R: 'Then the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh answered, and said unto the heads of the thousands of Israel.' Notice then. After hearing the accusation. Notice the children of Reuben… Gad… Manasseh. The accused. Notice answered. They responded with words, not weapons. Notice unto the heads of the thousands. To the delegation. Notice that the eastern tribes did not retaliate. Notice that they listened to the whole charge before speaking. Notice that a calm reply can defuse a crisis. Notice that being falsely suspected is painful, yet they stayed respectful. Proverbs 15 says, 'A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger.' Notice that their tone mattered as much as their content. Today, when you are misunderstood or accused, answer gently. Listen fully, then reply calmly. A soft answer can turn away wrath and preserve relationships.
S: The eastern tribes answered the heads of the thousands of Israel with words, not weapons. They listened to the whole accusation and replied respectfully though falsely suspected, defusing a crisis. When you are misunderstood or accused, listen fully and answer gently, for a soft answer turns away wrath.
P: A soft answer. Joshua 22:21: 'Then the children of Reuben… answered.' Falsely suspected, they answered calmly and with respect. A soft answer turneth away wrath.

They appealed first to God's knowledge, having nothing to hide. Live in such a way that you can say God knows, keeping your motives clean before Him.

== 22:25

Notice the LORD hath made Jordan a border. A natural divide. Notice ye have no part in the LORD. Words they feared would be spoken. Notice so shall your children make our children cease from fearing the LORD. Exclusion leading to unbelief. Notice that the eastern tribes feared that a geographical border would become a spiritual one. Notice that exclusion can drive people from faith. Today, make sure no one feels they have no part in the LORD because of where they live, where they come from or how they differ. Receive one another as Christ received you.
S: The eastern tribes feared that the Jordan border would lead future Israelites to tell their children they had no part in the LORD, causing them to stop fearing Him. They knew that exclusion can drive people from faith. Make sure no one feels they have no part in the LORD because of their differences, but receive one another as Christ received you.

Be patient when others misjudge your actions, and slow to judge others by appearance.

Notice that we might do the service of the LORD. They intended to worship at the true altar. Notice ye have no part in the LORD. The words they wanted to prevent. Notice that the altar was a monument, not a place of sacrifice. Notice that it testified to shared faith. Notice that symbols can carry truth across generations. Psalm 145 says, 'One generation shall praise thy works to another.' Today, create witnesses for the next generation: traditions, stories and reminders of your shared faith.
S: The eastern tribes said the altar was a witness between the tribes and their future generations that they too served the LORD, so no one could say they had no part in Him. It was a monument testifying to shared faith rather than a place of sacrifice. Create witnesses for the next generation, traditions and stories that carry your shared faith forward.
P: A witness for generations. Joshua 22:27: 'that it may be a witness between us, and you, and our generations after us.' One generation shall praise thy works to another.

== 22:28
T: The pattern of the altar
R: 'Therefore said we, that it shall be, when they should so say to us or to our generations in time to come, that we may say again, Behold the pattern of the altar of the LORD, which our fathers made, not for burnt offerings, nor for sacrifices; but it is a witness between us and you.' Notice behold the pattern. A copy, not the original. Notice of the altar of the LORD. It pointed to the real altar at Shiloh. Notice which our fathers made. A legacy. Notice not for burnt offerings. Not functional. Notice but it is a witness. Its only purpose. Notice that the copy pointed to the true altar rather than competing with it. Hebrews 8 speaks of priests 'Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things.' Notice that even the tabernacle was a copy of heavenly realities. Today, let every symbol in your life point to the real thing. Let your traditions direct people to Christ, not replace Him.
S: The eastern tribes said that if challenged, their descendants could point to the pattern of the LORD's altar their fathers made as a witness, not for sacrifices. The copy pointed to the true altar rather than competing with it, as even the tabernacle was a shadow of heavenly realities. Let every symbol in your life point to Christ rather than replace Him.
P: The pattern points beyond. Joshua 22:28: 'Behold the pattern of the altar of the LORD.' A copy that pointed to the real altar. Let every symbol in your life point to Christ.

Notice beside the altar of the LORD our God. There was only one true altar. Notice that is before his tabernacle. At Shiloh. Notice that the eastern tribes affirmed one place of sacrifice.
