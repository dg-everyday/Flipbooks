# Joshua 24:16-33: The covenant renewed and Joshua's death

== 24:16
T: God forbid that we should forsake the LORD
R: 'And the people answered and said, God forbid that we should forsake the LORD, to serve other gods.' Then the people answered: Far be it from us to forsake the LORD to serve other gods! Notice the people answered. They responded to Joshua's challenge. Notice God forbid. A strong refusal. Notice that we should forsake the LORD. Unthinkable to them in that moment. Notice to serve other gods. They rejected the alternatives. Notice that the people spoke with conviction. Notice that a clear choice had been set before them, and they chose. Notice that a declaration of loyalty is a good beginning. Notice that Peter once spoke the same way when others turned away from Jesus. John 6 records him saying, 'Lord, to whom shall we go? thou hast the words of eternal life.' Notice that when we see who God is, the alternatives lose their pull. Today, declare your loyalty to the Lord. Where else would you go? He alone has the words of eternal life.
S: The people answered Joshua that they would never forsake the LORD to serve other gods. A clear choice had been set before them, and they declared their loyalty with conviction, as Peter later did when others left Jesus. Declare your loyalty to the Lord, for He alone has the words of eternal life.
P: To whom shall we go? Joshua 24:16: 'God forbid that we should forsake the LORD, to serve other gods.' Lord, thou hast the words of eternal life. Declare your loyalty today.

Notice which did those great signs in our sight. They had seen it. Notice and preserved us in all the way. Daily protection. Notice that their loyalty rested on what God had done. Notice that God's preserving care covers every step. Today, list the ways God has brought you out and kept you. Let your commitment to Him rest on His proven faithfulness.
S: The people said they would serve the LORD because He brought them out of Egypt's house of bondage, did great signs before them and preserved them all the way. Their loyalty rested on what God had done and on His care at every step. List the ways God has brought you out and kept you, and let your commitment rest on His proven faithfulness.

P: Because He first loved us. Joshua 24:18: 'therefore will we also serve the LORD; for he is our God.' Service flows from gratitude. We love Him, because He first loved us.

== 24:19
T: Ye cannot serve the LORD
R: 'And Joshua said unto the people, Ye cannot serve the LORD: for he is an holy God; he is a jealous God; he will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins.' Notice Joshua said. A surprising reply. Notice ye cannot serve the LORD. A shocking warning. Notice for he is an holy God. Perfect purity. Notice he is a jealous God. He will not share His people. Notice he will not forgive your transgressions. He would not treat deliberate rebellion lightly. Notice that Joshua tested their easy enthusiasm. Notice that he wanted them to count the cost. Notice that human strength alone cannot keep covenant with a holy God. Romans 7 says, 'for to will is present with me; but how to perform that which is good I find not.' Notice that this points to our need for a Saviour. Today, admit that you cannot serve God in your own strength. Lean on Christ, who kept the covenant for you, and walk by His Spirit.
S: Joshua told the people they could not serve the LORD, for He is a holy and jealous God who would not overlook their rebellion. He tested their easy enthusiasm, for human strength alone cannot keep covenant with a holy God, which points to our need for a Saviour. Admit that you cannot serve God in your own strength, and lean on Christ, who kept the covenant for you.
P: Ye cannot. Joshua 24:19: 'Ye cannot serve the LORD: for he is an holy God.' Human strength falls short. Lean on Christ, who kept the covenant for you.

Past blessing would not protect them from future rebellion. Notice that God's past kindness does not license present sin. Notice that a relationship with a holy God is serious. Notice that grace received increases responsibility. Hebrews 12 says, 'For our God is a consuming fire.' Notice that God's holiness is not to be taken lightly. Today, do not presume on God's past goodness. Let His kindness lead you to faithfulness, not carelessness. Walk humbly with the holy God who has done you good.
S: Joshua warned that if Israel forsook the LORD for strange gods, He would turn and bring harm on them after having done them good. God's past kindness does not license present sin, for our God is a consuming fire. Do not presume on God's past goodness, but let His kindness lead you to faithfulness.
P: Don't presume. Joshua 24:20: 'he will turn and do you hurt… after that he hath done you good.' God's kindness isn't a licence for sin. Our God is a consuming fire.
