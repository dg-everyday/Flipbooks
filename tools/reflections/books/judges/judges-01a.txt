# Judges 1:1-18: After Joshua, Judah goes up first

== 1:1
T: Who shall go up for us?

Notice against the Canaanites first. Work remained. Notice that Israel began the post-Joshua era by praying. Notice that the loss of a leader drove them to God. Notice that asking before acting is wisdom. Notice the contrast with Gibeon, where they had not asked. Proverbs 3 says, 'In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.' Notice that the book opens well, though it will not continue so. Today, when a leader is gone or a season changes, begin by asking God. Let prayer be your first step into every new chapter.
