God recalled His grace. Notice I will never break my covenant with you. God's faithfulness. Notice that God began His rebuke by reminding them of His goodness. Notice that the journey from Gilgal to Bochim pictures Israel's slide from consecration to tears. Second Timothy 2 says, 'If we believe not, yet he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.' Today, remember that God has not broken faith with you. Even when you drift, His covenant stands. Let His faithfulness draw you back.

R: 'Therefore the LORD left those nations, without driving them out hastily; neither delivered he them into the hand of Joshua.' The LORD had allowed those nations to remain; he did not drive them out at once by giving them into the hands of Joshua. Notice therefore the LORD left those nations. A deliberate choice. Notice without driving them out hastily. Not all at once. Notice neither delivered he them into the hand of Joshua. Even in Joshua's day, some remained. Notice that God's timing was purposeful. Notice that gradual conquest required ongoing faith. Notice that God does not always remove challenges quickly. Notice that ongoing struggle can build endurance. James 1 says, 'the trying of your faith worketh patience.' Notice that patience grows through trials. Today, do not be discouraged if God has not removed every challenge at once. He may be building patience and faith in you. Trust His timing.
S: The LORD left those nations and did not drive them out hastily, nor deliver them all into Joshua's hand. God's timing was purposeful, and gradual conquest required ongoing faith that builds endurance. Do not be discouraged if God has not removed every challenge at once, for the trying of your faith works patience.
