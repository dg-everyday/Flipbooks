# Judges 3:1-14: Nations left to prove Israel, and Othniel the first judge

== 3:1
T: The nations which the LORD left
R: 'Now these are the nations which the LORD left, to prove Israel by them, even as many of Israel as had not known all the wars of Canaan.' Notice these are the nations which the LORD left. A deliberate purpose. Notice to prove Israel by them. To test. Notice even as many of Israel as had not known all the wars. The new generation. Notice that God used the remaining nations to test those who had not seen the conquest. Notice that each generation's faith must be tested for itself. Notice that untested faith is untried faith. Notice that God brings purpose even out of Israel's failures. First Peter 1 speaks of 'the trial of your faith, being much more precious than of gold.' Notice that tested faith is precious. Notice that trials refine rather than destroy those who trust. Today, see your trials as a proving ground. Your faith is being tested, not to break it but to show its worth and strengthen it.
S: The LORD left certain nations to prove Israel, especially the generation that had not known the wars of Canaan. Each generation's faith must be tested for itself, and God brought purpose even out of Israel's failures. See your trials as a proving ground, for tested faith is more precious than gold.
P: Faith tested. Judges 3:1: 'these are the nations which the LORD left, to prove Israel by them.' The trial of your faith is more precious than gold. Let trials refine you.
H: #DailyGrace #Judges31 #Testing #Faith #Refining #BibleVerse

== 3:2
T: To teach them war
R: 'Only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, at the least such as before knew nothing thereof.' Notice that the generations… might know. Learning by experience. Notice to teach them war. Training. Notice such as before knew nothing thereof. The inexperienced. Notice that God wanted each generation to learn how to fight. Notice that faith is not passive. Notice that what one generation learned in battle, the next had to learn too. Notice that comfort can leave us unprepared. Notice that the believer's battle today is spiritual. Ephesians 6 says, 'Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.' Notice that spiritual warfare must be learned. Notice that God trains His people through conflict. Today, do not resent the battles that train you. Learn to stand. Put on God's armour and grow strong in the fight of faith.
S: God left the nations so that each generation of Israel might learn war, especially those who had never known it. Faith is not passive, and each generation had to learn to fight, just as believers today must learn spiritual warfare. Do not resent the battles that train you, but put on God's armour and learn to stand.
P: Trained for battle. Judges 3:2: 'that the generations… might know, to teach them war.' Put on the whole armour of God. Let your battles train you to stand.
H: #DailyGrace #Judges32 #SpiritualWarfare #Training #Strength #BibleVerse

== 3:3
T: Five lords of the Philistines
R: 'Namely, five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites, and the Sidonians, and the Hivites that dwelt in mount Lebanon, from mount Baal-hermon unto the entering in of Hamath.' These are the nations: the five rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians and the Hivites living in the Lebanon mountains. Notice five lords of the Philistines. The future great enemy. Notice and all the Canaanites. The native peoples. Notice the Sidonians. Phoenician traders. Notice the Hivites… in mount Lebanon. Northern mountain dwellers. Notice from mount Baal-hermon. Even mountains named for Baal. Notice that the enemies surrounded Israel on every side. Notice that each would trouble Israel in turn. Ephesians 6 says, 'For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers.' Notice that behind visible enemies stand spiritual forces. Today, recognise that your real battle is spiritual. Do not fight people; resist the powers behind temptation and oppression, in God's strength.
S: The nations left were the five lords of the Philistines, the Canaanites, the Sidonians and the Hivites of mount Lebanon. Enemies surrounded Israel on every side, each to trouble them in turn, just as spiritual forces stand behind visible struggles. Recognise that your real battle is spiritual, and resist the powers behind temptation in God's strength.
P: The real battle. Judges 3:3: 'five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites, and the Sidonians.' We wrestle not against flesh and blood. Fight the spiritual battle in God's strength.

== 3:4
T: Whether they would hearken
R: 'And they were to prove Israel by them, to know whether they would hearken unto the commandments of the LORD, which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.' Notice they were to prove Israel. The purpose restated. Notice to know whether they would hearken. The heart of the test. Notice unto the commandments of the LORD. God's standard. Notice which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses. Long-established truth. Notice that the test was about listening and obeying. Notice that the question was not military skill but obedience. Notice that God's commands had not changed since Moses. Notice that the same word applied to the new generation. First Samuel 15 says, 'to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.' Notice that God values obedience above religious activity. Today, the test is the same: will you hearken to God's word? Not just hear it, but obey it. That is what He looks for.
S: The nations were left to prove whether Israel would hearken to the LORD's commandments given through Moses. The test was not military skill but listening and obeying, for God's word had not changed. Hearken to God's word and obey it, for to obey is better than sacrifice.
P: Will you hearken? Judges 3:4: 'to know whether they would hearken unto the commandments of the LORD.' To obey is better than sacrifice. The test is obedience.
H: #DailyGrace #Judges34 #Obedience #Listening #Testing #BibleVerse

== 3:5
T: Dwelt among the Canaanites
R: 'And the children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites.' Notice the children of Israel dwelt among. Living in the midst. Notice the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites. Six nations listed. Notice that this was the very list of peoples God had promised to drive out. Notice that Israel now lived surrounded by them. Notice that the promise had become a predicament. Notice that dwelling among is the first step toward becoming like. Psalm 120 cries, 'Woe is me, that I sojourn in Mesech, that I dwell in the tents of Kedar!' Notice the pain of living among those who do not know God. Notice that the psalmist longed for peace. Today, if you live surrounded by a culture far from God, stay rooted in Him. Be in the world, but let your heart dwell with God. Do not let your surroundings define you.
S: The children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites. They lived surrounded by the very nations God promised to drive out, and dwelling among was the first step toward becoming like them. If you live surrounded by a culture far from God, stay rooted in Him and do not let your surroundings define you.
P: Surrounded. Judges 3:5: 'the children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites.' Living among is the first step to becoming like. Stay rooted in God.
H: #DailyGrace #Judges35 #Culture #Holiness #Rootedness #BibleVerse

== 3:6
T: And served their gods
R: 'And they took their daughters to be their wives, and gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods.' Notice they took their daughters to be their wives. Intermarriage. Notice and gave their daughters to their sons. Both directions. Notice and served their gods. The result. Notice the progression: dwelling among, marrying among, worshipping with. Notice that close relationships shaped spiritual loyalty. Notice that marriage bound families to foreign gods. Notice that God's warning through Joshua came true. Notice that even wise Solomon fell this way. First Kings 11 says, 'his wives turned away his heart after other gods.' Notice that the heart follows its closest attachments. Today, be wise in the relationships you form. Marriage especially shapes your walk with God. Choose those who will draw your heart toward Him, not away.
S: Israel took the Canaanites' daughters as wives, gave their own daughters to Canaanite sons and served their gods. The progression ran from living among to marrying among to worshipping with, as Solomon's wives later turned his heart. Be wise in the relationships you form, choosing those who will draw your heart toward God.
P: Hearts turned. Judges 3:6: 'they took their daughters to be their wives… and served their gods.' Close bonds shape devotion. Choose relationships that draw you to God.
H: #DailyGrace #Judges36 #Marriage #Relationships #Devotion #BibleVerse

== 3:7
T: Forgat the LORD their God
R: 'And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD, and forgat the LORD their God, and served Baalim and the groves.' Notice did evil in the sight of the LORD. The refrain begins. Notice and forgat the LORD their God. Forgetting came first. Notice and served Baalim and the groves. Baal and Asherah poles. Notice that forgetting God led to serving idols. Notice that spiritual amnesia is dangerous. Notice that the God who did such great works was simply forgotten. Notice that what we neglect to remember, we soon stop loving. Deuteronomy 8 warned, 'Beware that thou forget not the LORD thy God.' Notice that Moses saw this danger long before. Notice that forgetting is often gradual and unnoticed. Today, guard against forgetting God. Build daily habits of remembrance: prayer, Scripture and thanksgiving. A remembered God is a worshipped God.
S: The children of Israel did evil in the LORD's sight, forgot the LORD their God and served the Baals and Asherah poles. Forgetting came first and led to idolatry, as Moses had warned. Guard against forgetting God by building daily habits of prayer, Scripture and thanksgiving.
P: Forgetting God. Judges 3:7: 'the children of Israel… forgat the LORD their God, and served Baalim.' Beware that thou forget not the LORD. Build daily habits of remembrance.

== 3:8
T: Chushan-rishathaim
R: 'Therefore the anger of the LORD was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Chushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia: and the children of Israel served Chushan-rishathaim eight years.' Notice therefore. Consequence followed sin. Notice the anger of the LORD was hot. Righteous displeasure. Notice he sold them. Into bondage. Notice Chushan-rishathaim. A name meaning double wickedness. Notice king of Mesopotamia. From far away. Notice eight years. A long oppression. Notice that the first oppressor came from Abraham's homeland. Notice the irony: Israel was handed back to the land God had called Abraham out of. Proverbs 5 says, 'His own iniquities shall take the wicked himself, and he shall be holden with the cords of his sins.' Notice that sin binds us. Today, recognise that sin leads back into bondage. What God freed you from, sin will hand you back to. Stay free in Him.
S: The LORD's anger burned and He sold Israel to Chushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia, whom they served eight years. The first oppressor came from Abraham's homeland, as Israel was handed back to what God had called them out of. Recognise that sin leads back into bondage, and stay free in God.
P: Back into bondage. Judges 3:8: 'he sold them into the hand of Chushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia.' Sin hands you back to what God freed you from. Stay free in Him.
H: #DailyGrace #Judges38 #Bondage #Sin #Freedom #BibleVerse

== 3:9
T: Othniel, a deliverer
R: 'And when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD, the LORD raised up a deliverer to the children of Israel, who delivered them, even Othniel the son of Kenaz.' Notice when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD. Prayer in distress. Notice the LORD raised up a deliverer. God answered. Notice who delivered them. Rescue. Notice even Othniel the son of Kenaz. A faithful man from a faithful family. Notice that the cry came after eight years. Notice that God answered as soon as they cried. Notice that God had already prepared Othniel through the victory at Debir. Notice that Caleb's family produced the first judge. Psalm 107 says, 'Then they cried unto the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses.' Notice the pattern of crying and deliverance. Today, cry out to the LORD in your trouble. Do not wait eight years. He has already prepared a way of deliverance.
S: When Israel cried to the LORD, He raised up Othniel son of Kenaz as a deliverer. God answered their cry at once, having already prepared Othniel through his courage at Debir. Cry out to the LORD in your trouble without delay, for He has already prepared a way of deliverance.
P: They cried; He raised a deliverer. Judges 3:9: 'when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD, the LORD raised up a deliverer.' Don't wait eight years. Cry out now.

== 3:10
T: The Spirit of the LORD came upon him
R: 'And the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war.' The Spirit of the LORD came on him, so that he became Israel's judge and went to war. The LORD gave Cushan-Rishathaim king of Aram into his hands. Notice the Spirit of the LORD came upon him. Divine empowerment. Notice and he judged Israel. Leadership. Notice and went out to war. Action. Notice that the LORD delivered the enemy into his hand. God gave the victory. Notice and his hand prevailed. Othniel won. Notice that the Spirit came first, then judging and fighting. Notice that the first judge's power came from God, not from himself. Notice that this is the pattern for every true deliverer. Acts 1 says, 'ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you.' Notice that God's Spirit empowers God's work. Today, ask for the Spirit's power for what God has called you to. Do not rely on your own strength. Let Him come upon you.
S: The Spirit of the LORD came upon Othniel, who judged Israel, went to war and prevailed over the king of Mesopotamia. The Spirit came first and then judging and fighting, for the first judge's power came from God. Ask for the Spirit's power for what God has called you to, rather than relying on your own strength.
P: Spirit first. Judges 3:10: 'the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel.' Ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you.

== 3:11
T: The land had rest forty years
R: 'And the land had rest forty years. And Othniel the son of Kenaz died.' Notice and the land had rest. Peace came. Notice forty years. A full generation. Notice and Othniel the son of Kenaz died. The deliverer passed on. Notice that rest followed deliverance. Notice that peace lasted while the judge lived. Notice that Othniel's legacy was a generation of rest. Notice that faithful leadership produces peace. Notice the hint of danger: his death would end the rest. Isaiah 32 says, 'the work of righteousness shall be peace; and the effect of righteousness quietness and assurance for ever.' Notice that righteousness brings peace. Notice that Othniel is the model judge, with no recorded failings. Today, thank God for seasons of rest. Use them well. And remember that lasting peace comes from righteousness rooted in God, not merely in a good leader.
S: The land had rest forty years, and then Othniel son of Kenaz died. Faithful leadership produced a generation of peace, yet his death would end the rest. Thank God for seasons of rest and use them well, remembering that lasting peace comes from righteousness rooted in God.
P: Forty years of rest. Judges 3:11: 'the land had rest forty years. And Othniel the son of Kenaz died.' The work of righteousness shall be peace. Use your seasons of rest well.

== 3:12
T: The LORD strengthened Eglon
R: 'And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the LORD: and the LORD strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done evil in the sight of the LORD.' Notice did evil again. The cycle repeated. Notice in the sight of the LORD. God saw. Notice the LORD strengthened Eglon. God used an enemy. Notice the king of Moab. A neighbouring nation. Notice because they had done evil. The reason given twice. Notice that God was sovereign even over Israel's enemies. Notice that God used Moab as an instrument of discipline. Notice that the oppressor's strength came from God's permission. Isaiah 10 calls Assyria 'the rod of mine anger.' Notice that God can use even the wicked to discipline His people. Today, when hardship comes, ask whether God is speaking through it. He rules over every power, even those that trouble you. His discipline aims at your return.
S: Israel did evil again, and the LORD strengthened Eglon king of Moab against them because of their sin. God was sovereign even over Israel's enemies, using Moab as an instrument of discipline. When hardship comes, ask whether God is speaking through it, for He rules every power and His discipline aims at your return.
P: The rod of discipline. Judges 3:12: 'the LORD strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel.' God rules over every power. Ask what He is saying through hardship.

They became subjects. Notice Eglon the king of Moab. A foreign ruler. Notice eighteen years. Longer than before. Notice that the oppression lasted more than twice as long as the first. Notice that each cycle grew worse. Notice that eighteen years is most of a childhood. Notice that a whole generation grew up under oppression. Notice that sin's consequences can last long. Proverbs 14 says, 'Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.' Notice that national sin brings national shame. Today, pray for your nation and community. Sin brings long seasons of reproach, but righteousness lifts a people. Be part of the righteousness that exalts.
S: Israel served Eglon king of Moab eighteen years, more than twice as long as the first oppression. Each cycle grew worse, and a whole generation grew up under the reproach of sin. Pray for your nation and community, and be part of the righteousness that exalts a people.
