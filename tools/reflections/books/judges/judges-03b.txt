# Judges 3:15-31: Ehud the left-handed deliverer, and Shamgar

== 3:15
T: A man lefthanded
R: 'But when the children of Israel cried unto the LORD, the LORD raised them up a deliverer, Ehud the son of Gera, a Benjamite, a man lefthanded.' Notice but when the children of Israel cried. Again they called out. Notice the LORD raised them up a deliverer. Again God answered. Notice Ehud the son of Gera, a Benjamite. From the tribe whose name means son of the right hand. Notice a man lefthanded. An unusual trait. Notice the irony: a left-handed man from the tribe of the right hand. Notice that God chose someone who did not fit the expected mould. Notice that what seemed a limitation became an advantage. First Corinthians 1 says, 'But God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise.' Notice that God delights to use the unexpected. Today, do not dismiss yourself because you are different. God may use the very thing you think disqualifies you. He chooses the unlikely.
S: When Israel cried again, the LORD raised up Ehud son of Gera, a left-handed man from Benjamin, the tribe whose name means son of the right hand. God chose someone who did not fit the expected mould, turning an apparent limitation into an advantage. Do not dismiss yourself because you are different, for God delights to use the unlikely.
P: God uses the unlikely. Judges 3:15: 'the LORD raised them up a deliverer, Ehud… a man lefthanded.' God hath chosen the foolish things to confound the wise. Your difference may be His tool.
