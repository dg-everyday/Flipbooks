# Judges 5:16-31: Who came, who stayed home, and let them that love Him shine

== 5:16
T: Among the sheepfolds
R: 'Why abodest thou among the sheepfolds, to hear the bleatings of the flocks? For the divisions of Reuben there were great searchings of heart.' Notice why abodest thou among the sheepfolds? A pointed question. Notice to hear the bleatings of the flocks. Comfortable routine. Notice for the divisions of Reuben. Again Reuben. Notice there were great searchings of heart. Deep reflection, but no action. Notice that Reuben chose the comfort of home over the call to battle. Notice that the sound of sheep drowned out the trumpet of God. Notice that daily business can become an excuse for absence. Amos 6 says, 'Woe to them that are at ease in Zion.' Notice that ease in a time of need is dangerous. Notice that Reuben searched his heart but stayed with the sheep. Today, do not let routine keep you from God's call. When He summons, leave the sheepfolds. Comfort is no excuse when God's people need you.
S: Deborah asked why Reuben stayed among the sheepfolds listening to the flocks, for Reuben had great searchings of heart. Reuben chose the comfort of home over the call to battle, letting daily business excuse his absence. Do not let routine keep you from God's call, for ease is no excuse when His people need you.
P: Staying with the sheep. Judges 5:16: 'Why abodest thou among the sheepfolds, to hear the bleatings of the flocks?' Don't let routine drown out God's call. Woe to them that are at ease.

== 5:17
T: Why did Dan remain in ships?
R: 'Gilead abode beyond Jordan: and why did Dan remain in ships? Asher continued on the sea shore, and abode in his breaches.' Notice Gilead abode beyond Jordan. Stayed away. Notice why did Dan remain in ships? Busy with trade. Notice Asher continued on the sea shore. Stayed by the coast. Notice and abode in his breaches. In his harbours. Notice that several tribes stayed home. Notice that each had a reason: distance, business, location. Notice that the absent tribes are remembered for their absence. Notice that history records who came and who did not. Esther 4 asks, 'who knoweth whether thou art come to the kingdom for such a time as this?' Notice that God places us for moments of need. Today, do not miss your moment. When God's people need you, show up. Busyness, distance and comfort are poor reasons to stay away.
S: Deborah noted that Gilead stayed beyond the Jordan, Dan remained with his ships and Asher stayed on the seashore. Each tribe had its reason for staying home, and history records their absence. Do not miss your moment when God's people need you, for you may be placed for such a time as this.
P: Who stayed home. Judges 5:17: 'why did Dan remain in ships? Asher continued on the sea shore.' Busyness kept them away. You may be here for such a time as this.
