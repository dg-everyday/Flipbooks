# Judges 6:1-20: Midian's oppression and the call of Gideon

== 6:1
T: Into the hand of Midian
R: 'And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD: and the LORD delivered them into the hand of Midian seven years.' Notice did evil in the sight of the LORD. The cycle again. Notice and the LORD delivered them. Discipline followed. Notice into the hand of Midian. A desert people. Notice seven years. A complete period of hardship. Notice that forty years of rest after Deborah ended in the same sin. Notice that Midian had once been defeated under Moses. Notice that old enemies returned when Israel wandered. Notice that God allowed hardship so that Israel would return. Lamentations 3 asks, 'Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins?' Notice that suffering caused by sin calls for self-examination, not only complaint. Today, when hardship comes, ask honestly whether you have wandered. Do not only complain; examine your heart and return to God.
S: Israel did evil again, and the LORD delivered them into the hand of Midian for seven years. Forty years of rest ended in the same sin, and an old enemy defeated under Moses returned. When hardship comes, examine your heart honestly rather than only complaining, and return to God.
P: Seven years of Midian. Judges 6:1: 'the LORD delivered them into the hand of Midian seven years.' When hardship comes, examine your heart. Don't only complain; return.

Today, if you are hiding, ask why. Is it persecution for righteousness, or the fruit of wandering? God can bring you out of the cave.
S: Midian prevailed, and Israel made dens, caves and strongholds in the mountains to hide. God's people, once conquerors, now lived as fugitives in the land they had been given. If you are hiding, ask honestly why, and trust God to bring you out of the cave.
P: Hiding in caves. Judges 6:2: 'the children of Israel made them the dens which are in the mountains, and caves.' Once conquerors, now fugitives. God can bring you out.
