== 7:25
T: Oreb and Zeeb
R: 'And they took two princes of the Midianites, Oreb and Zeeb.' They also captured two of the Midianite leaders, Oreb and Zeeb, and brought word of them to Gideon across the Jordan. Notice they took two princes. Leaders captured. Notice Oreb and Zeeb. Names meaning raven and wolf. Notice the rock Oreb and the winepress of Zeeb. Places named after their defeat. Notice that a winepress, where Gideon once hid, now marked the enemy's fall. Notice the reversal. Notice that the oppressors' names became landmarks of God's victory. Psalm 83 prays, 'Make their nobles like Oreb, and like Zeeb.' Notice that later generations remembered this victory in prayer. Notice that God's victories become part of His people's memory. Today, remember that God can turn places of fear into memorials of victory. The winepress where you once hid can become the place where He triumphs.
S: The Ephraimites captured the Midianite princes Oreb and Zeeb and brought word of them to Gideon across the Jordan. A winepress, where Gideon once hid in fear, now marked the enemy's fall, and later generations remembered this victory in prayer. Remember that God can turn your places of fear into memorials of His victory.
P: From hiding place to victory. Judges 7:25: 'they took two princes of the Midianites, Oreb and Zeeb.' Zeeb fell at a winepress, where Gideon once hid. God turns fear into triumph.
