# Judges 8:1-17: Ephraim's complaint, faint yet pursuing, Succoth and Penuel

== 8:1
T: They did chide with him sharply
R: 'And the men of Ephraim said unto him, Why hast thou served us thus, that thou calledst us not, when thou wentest to fight with the Midianites? And they did chide with him sharply.' Notice the men of Ephraim said. A powerful tribe complained. Notice why hast thou served us thus? Wounded pride. Notice that thou calledst us not. They felt left out. Notice and they did chide with him sharply. Angry words. Notice that Ephraim complained after the victory, not before. Notice that they were more concerned with their honour than with God's deliverance. Notice that pride can turn victory into conflict. Notice that God's people often fight one another after fighting the enemy. Proverbs 13 says, 'Only by pride cometh contention.' Notice that pride lies behind many quarrels. Today, check your heart when you feel overlooked. Is your concern for God's glory or your own? Do not let wounded pride stir up conflict among God's people.
S: The men of Ephraim sharply rebuked Gideon for not calling them when he went to fight Midian. They complained after the victory, more concerned with their honour than with God's deliverance, for pride turns victory into conflict.
