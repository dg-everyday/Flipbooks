Notice Abimelech the son of Jerubbaal. Gideon's son by a concubine. Notice went to Shechem. His mother's city. Notice that he went to his mother's relatives. Seeking allies. Notice that ambition drove him. Notice that his name, my father is king, was shaping his desires. Notice that he used family ties to build a power base.
