# Judges 12: Shibboleth, and the quiet judges Ibzan, Elon and Abdon

== 12:1

Notice we will burn thine house upon thee with fire. A violent threat. Notice that Ephraim's pride had grown worse since Gideon's day. Notice that they threatened a fellow Israelite who had just delivered them. Notice that wounded pride had become murderous. Notice that unaddressed pride returns stronger. Proverbs 21 says, 'Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.' Notice that pride produces wrath. Today, deal with pride before it grows. A grievance nursed becomes a threat. Humble yourself before resentment hardens into anger.
S: The men of Ephraim gathered and threatened to burn Jephthah's house because he had not called them to fight Ammon. Their pride had grown worse since Gideon's day, turning a grievance into a murderous threat against their deliverer. Deal with pride before it grows, for a nursed grievance hardens into proud wrath.

P: Life in his hands. Judges 12:3: 'I put my life in my hands… and the LORD delivered them into my hand.' Risk with courage, credit God, and don't let ingratitude make you forget Him.
