# Judges 15: Revenge, the jawbone and the spring of him that called

== 15:1
T: Her father would not suffer him

He assumed the marriage stood. Notice but her father would not suffer him. Refused. Notice that Samson returned as if nothing had happened. Notice that he had left in anger without resolving the conflict. Notice that unresolved anger leaves broken relationships. Notice that time alone does not heal wounds.
