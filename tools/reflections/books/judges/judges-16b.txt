# Judges 16:18-31: Samson's fall, his prayer and his death

== 16:18
T: Money in their hand
R: 'And when Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart, she sent and called for the lords of the Philistines, saying, Come up this once, for he hath shewed me all his heart. Then the lords of the Philistines came up unto her, and brought money in their hand.' Notice Delilah saw. She knew it was true this time. Notice she sent and called for the lords. Betrayal set in motion. Notice come up this once. Confident now. Notice for he hath shewed me all his heart. Using his openness against him. Notice and brought money in their hand. Payment ready. Notice that Samson's trust became Delilah's profit. Notice that the payment of silver recalls a later betrayal. Matthew 26 records Judas: 'And they covenanted with him for thirty pieces of silver.' Notice that betrayal for money is an ancient sin. Today, be careful whom you trust with your heart. And never sell out those who trust you. No price is worth betrayal.

== 16:21

Notice and he did grind in the prison house. Slave labour. Notice that the eyes that led him astray were taken. Notice that the strong man now did a servant's work. Notice that sin blinds, binds and grinds. Notice that he was brought low to the place of his earlier sin. Today, recognise sin's true cost: it blinds, binds and grinds. What promises pleasure ends in bondage. Turn to God before sin takes you that far.
S: The Philistines seized Samson, blinded him, took him to Gaza, bound him with bronze chains and set him grinding grain in prison. The eyes that led him astray were taken, and the strong man did a servant's work, for sin blinds, binds and grinds. Recognise sin's true cost and turn to God before it takes you that far.
P: Blinded, bound, grinding. Judges 16:21: 'put out his eyes… bound him with fetters of brass; and he did grind in the prison house.' Sin blinds, binds and grinds.

== 16:23

Notice our god hath delivered Samson. Crediting Dagon. Notice our enemy into our hand. Boasting. Notice that Samson's fall brought dishonour to God's name. Notice that the Philistines claimed their idol had beaten Israel's God. Notice that a believer's failure can give the world reason to mock God. Your failures can give others reason to scoff, but God will defend His glory. Live to honour His name.

Samson's fall brought dishonour to God's name as the Philistines credited their idol. Remember that your life reflects on God's name, and live to honour it, for He will defend His glory.
P: God's name at stake. Judges 16:23: 'Our god hath delivered Samson our enemy into our hand.' A believer's fall gave Dagon credit. Live to honour God's name.
