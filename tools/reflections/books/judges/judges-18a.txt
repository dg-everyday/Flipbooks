# Judges 18:1-16: Dan looks for land and finds Micah's priest

== 18:1
T: The Danites sought an inheritance
R: 'In those days there was no king in Israel: and in those days the tribe of the Danites sought them an inheritance to dwell in; for unto that day all their inheritance had not fallen unto them among the tribes of Israel.' Notice the tribe of the Danites sought them an inheritance. Looking for land. Notice for unto that day all their inheritance had not fallen unto them. They had not taken what God allotted. Notice that Dan had been given land by lot under Joshua. Notice that they failed to possess it and now sought easier land elsewhere. Notice that they sought their own inheritance rather than God's assignment. Matthew 6 says, 'seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.' Notice that seeking God first changes what we seek. Today, do not abandon what God has assigned you for something easier. Seek first His kingdom, and trust Him to provide your place.
S: In those days, with no king in Israel, the Danites were seeking land because they had not taken the inheritance allotted to them. Having failed to possess God's assignment, they sought easier land elsewhere. Do not abandon what God has assigned you for something easier, but seek first His kingdom and trust Him for your place.
P: Seeking their own way. Judges 18:1: 'the tribe of the Danites sought them an inheritance.' They abandoned God's allotment. Seek ye first the kingdom of God.
