# Leviticus 4: The sin offering

== 4:1
T: The LORD spake
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' After the burnt, grain, and peace offerings, God spoke again. Now He would address sin directly. The sin offering was about to be introduced. God had instructions for dealing with failure. Notice that God initiated the solution. Israel did not invent a way to deal with sin. God provided it. He knew His people would fail, and He prepared a remedy in advance. This is grace. God anticipates our failures and provides forgiveness. Revelation 13 speaks of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world. Before we ever sinned, God planned our redemption. First John 2 says, if any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous. Today, take comfort that God has already provided for your failures. He knew, and He made a way. Come to Him with confidence.
S: After the burnt, grain and peace offerings, the LORD spoke to Moses again, now about sin. God provided a remedy for failure before His people needed it. Take comfort that God has already provided for your failures through Christ.
P: Grace planned ahead. Leviticus 4:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' God provided for sin before it happened. Christ is your advocate. Come with confidence.
H: #DailyGrace #Leviticus41 #Grace #Forgiveness #Advocate #BibleVerse

== 4:2
T: Sin through ignorance
R: 'Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a soul shall sin through ignorance against any of the commandments of the LORD concerning things which ought not to be done, and shall do against any of them.' God addressed sins done through ignorance, unintentionally, against any of His commands. Even unintentional sin was sin, and it needed atonement. We often think sin only counts if we meant it. But God's holiness is real whether we are aware of our failures or not. Unintentional sin still separates and needs cleansing. Yet this is also gracious. God provided a remedy for sins we do not even recognize. Psalm 19 says, Who can understand his errors? cleanse thou me from secret faults. We have faults we do not see. God covers those too. First John 1 says the blood of Jesus Christ cleanseth us from all sin. All, including the sins we never noticed. Today, ask God to cleanse you from hidden faults. And rest in Christ's blood, which covers even what you do not know.
S: God addressed sins committed through ignorance against any of His commandments. Even unintentional sin needed atonement, and God graciously provided for it. Ask God to cleanse you from hidden faults and rest in Christ's blood, which covers all sin.
P: Hidden faults. Leviticus 4:2: 'If a soul shall sin through ignorance.' Even unknown sins need cleansing. Christ's blood covers all. Pray: cleanse me from secret faults.
H: #DailyGrace #Leviticus42 #Forgiveness #Cleansing #Grace #BibleVerse

== 4:3
T: If the priest sin
R: 'If the priest that is anointed do sin according to the sin of the people; then let him bring for his sin, which he hath sinned, a young bullock without blemish unto the LORD for a sin offering.' If the anointed high priest sinned and brought guilt on the people, he had to bring a young bull without defect as a sin offering. Leaders were not exempt from sin. Notice the priest that is anointed. The high priest was the most holy person in Israel. Yet God anticipated that he would sin. Notice also according to the sin of the people. His sin affected the people. A leader's sin has wider consequences. Greater responsibility brought greater accountability. James 3 says, be not many masters, knowing that we shall receive the greater condemnation. Hebrews 7 contrasts earthly priests, who needed sacrifices for themselves, with Christ, who needed none. Today, pray for your leaders. They are human and need grace. And thank God for Christ, the High Priest without sin.
S: If the anointed priest sinned and brought guilt on the people, he had to bring a young bullock without blemish as a sin offering. Leaders were not exempt, and their sin carried wider consequences. Pray for your leaders, who need grace, and thank God for Christ, the sinless High Priest.
P: Leaders need grace too. Leviticus 4:3: 'If the priest that is anointed do sin.' Even the high priest needed a sin offering. Pray for your leaders today.

== 4:4
T: Before the LORD
R: 'And he shall bring the bullock unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation before the LORD; and shall lay his hand upon the bullock's head, and kill the bullock before the LORD.' The high priest brought the bull to the tabernacle entrance, laid his hand on its head, and killed it before the LORD. Even the high priest had to identify with a substitute. Notice that the high priest went through the same process as anyone. He laid his hand on the bull's head. He acknowledged his own sin. Position did not exempt him from confession. Leaders must model repentance. When leaders confess their sins openly, they show others the way. Pride hides sin; humility confesses it. Proverbs 28 says, whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy. Notice before the LORD, twice. The confession and sacrifice were directed to God. Sin is ultimately against Him. Psalm 51 says, Against thee, thee only, have I sinned. Today, whatever your position, humble yourself before God. Confess your sin and receive His mercy.
S: The high priest brought the bullock to the door, laid his hand on its head and killed it before the LORD. Even the highest leader identified with a substitute and acknowledged his sin. Whatever your position, humble yourself before God and confess your sin to receive mercy.
P: No one is exempt. Leviticus 4:4: 'shall lay his hand upon the bullock's head, and kill the bullock before the LORD.' Even leaders confess. Humble yourself and receive mercy.

== 4:5
T: Into the tabernacle
R: 'And the priest that is anointed shall take of the bullock's blood, and bring it to the tabernacle of the congregation.' The anointed priest took some of the bull's blood and brought it into the tabernacle. For the high priest's sin, the blood went inside, closer to God's presence. Notice that the blood was carried inside. For ordinary people's sins, blood was applied to the outer altar. But for the high priest's sin, it went into the Holy Place. The greater the person's position, the deeper the defilement reached. The high priest's sin affected the sanctuary itself. His sin touched holy things. Leaders who sin can damage the very places of worship. Yet the blood went where the sin reached. Atonement reached as far as the defilement. Hebrews 9 says Christ entered not into holy places made with hands but into heaven itself. His blood reaches the very presence of God. Today, rejoice that Christ's blood reaches as deep as your sin. No defilement is beyond its reach.
S: The anointed priest brought some of the bullock's blood into the tabernacle. Because the high priest's sin reached the sanctuary, the blood went where the defilement reached. Rejoice that Christ's blood reaches as deep as your sin and into heaven itself.
P: Blood reaches deep. Leviticus 4:5: 'shall take of the bullock's blood, and bring it to the tabernacle.' Atonement went where sin reached. Christ's blood reaches heaven itself.

First Peter 1 speaks of the sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ. Today, rejoice that Christ's blood speaks for you before God. Its atonement is complete, sprinkled seven times over.

We acknowledge His holiness and His right to the best. First John 1 says, If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us. Confession is worship, because it declares that God is right and we are wrong. Psalm 51 says, that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest. Today, let your confession be worship. Honor God as you acknowledge your sin, giving Him glory for His holiness and mercy.
