The law was for everyone. Women were not overlooked. Their health mattered as much as men's. God's care is not divided by gender. Galatians 3 says we are all one in Christ Jesus. Notice the head. Problems on the head were more visible. They were harder to hide. Some struggles are public and embarrassing. People can see them. Yet God gave the same careful attention to these. He does not despise those whose struggles are visible. Psalm 3 says, thou, O LORD, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head. When shame makes us hang our heads, God lifts them. Today, if your struggle is visible and embarrassing, know that God lifts your head. He sees you with compassion, not contempt.

The priest looked for a specific sign. Healthy hair was replaced by thin, discolored hair. Something was wrong beneath the surface. Spiritual health shows in signs too. A surface issue was different from a rooted one. Second Corinthians 13 says, Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith. Today, look for signs of your spiritual health. Where love and joy have grown thin, ask God to restore what lies beneath.

Its absence was a concern, though not proof of disease. The priest waited for more evidence. Wise people do not panic over one sign. They look at the whole picture and wait for clarity. It was a precaution, not a punishment. Today, be prudent in uncertain times. Look carefully, wait patiently, and do not jump to conclusions about yourself or others.
