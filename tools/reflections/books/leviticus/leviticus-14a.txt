# Leviticus 14:1-32: The cleansing of the healed leper

== 14:1
T: A law of restoration
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' After the long chapter on diagnosing disease, God now spoke about restoration. How would a healed person come back? Notice the shift. Chapter 13 was about examination and exclusion. Chapter 14 is about cleansing and return. God did not stop at diagnosis. He made a way home. This is God's heart. He does not only identify our problems. He provides restoration. Jeremiah 30 says, I will restore health unto thee, and I will heal thee of thy wounds, saith the LORD; because they called thee an Outcast. God sees the outcast and brings them back. Notice that God spoke these words. Restoration was His idea. He wanted the excluded to return. Luke 15 shows the father running to his returning son. God longs to welcome us home. Today, rejoice that God does not stop at naming your problem. He makes a way for you to come home.
S: After the chapter on diagnosis, the LORD spoke to Moses about restoring the healed. God did not stop at exclusion but made a way home for the outcast. Rejoice that God makes a way for you to come home.
P: A way home. Leviticus 14:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' God restores the outcast. He doesn't stop at naming your problem; He brings you home.
H: #DailyGrace #Leviticus141 #Restoration #Homecoming #Grace #BibleVerse

== 14:2
T: The day of his cleansing
R: 'This shall be the law of the leper in the day of his cleansing: He shall be brought unto the priest.' This was the law for the day a healed person was cleansed. He was to be brought to the priest. Notice the day of his cleansing. There was a specific day of restoration. Isolation had an end. A day of celebration was coming. For those who suffered in exile, this was the day they longed for. Second Corinthians 6 says, behold, now is the day of salvation. God has a day of cleansing for us. Notice he shall be brought. Again, others helped. The healed person was brought back. Restoration involves community. In Luke 5, Jesus told a cleansed leper, go, and shew thyself to the priest. Jesus honored this law. He sent healed lepers to complete this process. Today, rejoice that God has a day of cleansing for you. Now is the day of salvation. Come to Him.
S: This was the law for the leper in the day of his cleansing, when he was brought to the priest. Isolation had an end, and Jesus sent healed lepers to complete this law. Rejoice that now is the day of salvation and come to God for cleansing.
P: The day of cleansing. Leviticus 14:2: 'This shall be the law of the leper in the day of his cleansing.' Now is the day of salvation. Come to Him today.

== 14:3
T: The priest shall go forth
R: 'And the priest shall go forth out of the camp; and the priest shall look, and, behold, if the plague of leprosy be healed in the leper.' The priest went outside the camp to examine the person. If the disease was healed, the cleansing process could begin. Notice the priest shall go forth out of the camp. The leper could not come in. So the priest went out to him. The priest crossed the boundary to reach the outcast. This is exactly what Christ did. He left heaven and came to us. Philippians 2 says He made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant. Luke 19 says, the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost. Notice if the plague… be healed. The priest did not heal. He recognized healing. Healing came from God. Exodus 15 says, I am the LORD that healeth thee. Today, rejoice that Christ came out to find you. He crossed every boundary to bring you home.
S: The priest went forth out of the camp to see whether the leper was healed. He crossed the boundary to reach the outcast, as Christ came to seek and save the lost. Rejoice that Christ came out to find you and crossed every boundary to bring you home.
P: He came out to us. Leviticus 14:3: 'the priest shall go forth out of the camp.' The leper couldn't come in, so the priest went out. Christ came to seek and save you.

== 14:4
T: Cedar, scarlet and hyssop
R: 'Then shall the priest command to take for him that is to be cleansed two birds alive and clean, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop.' The priest ordered two live clean birds, some cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop for the cleansing. Notice cedar wood. Cedar is durable and resists decay. Notice scarlet. Its color recalls blood. Isaiah 1 says, though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow. Notice hyssop. It was used to apply the Passover blood in Exodus 12. Psalm 51 says, Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean. David was thinking of this very ritual. Notice also that at the cross, John 19 says they put a sponge of vinegar upon hyssop and lifted it to Jesus. These humble materials point to Christ's cleansing work. Today, pray David's prayer: Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean. God provides everything needed for your cleansing.
S: The priest commanded two living clean birds with cedar wood, scarlet and hyssop for the one to be cleansed. Hyssop recalls the Passover blood and David's prayer, Purge me with hyssop. Pray David's prayer and trust that God provides everything for your cleansing.

== 14:5
T: Over running water
R: 'And the priest shall command that one of the birds be killed in an earthen vessel over running water.' One of the birds was killed over a clay pot filled with fresh, running water. Notice the bird was killed. Cleansing required a death. Even for a healed person, restoration came through sacrifice. Notice an earthen vessel. A humble clay pot. Some see here a picture of Christ's humanity. Second Corinthians 4 speaks of treasure in earthen vessels. The Son of God took on human flesh, fragile like clay. Notice running water. The Hebrew says living water. Fresh, flowing water, not stagnant. John 7 records Jesus saying, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water… this spake he of the Spirit. Death and living water came together. The cross and the Spirit both bring cleansing. Today, rejoice in both Christ's death and the living water of the Spirit. Together they make you clean and alive.
S: One bird was killed in an earthen vessel over running water. Cleansing came through death, joined with living water, picturing the cross and the Spirit. Rejoice in Christ's death and the living water of the Spirit, which make you clean and alive.
