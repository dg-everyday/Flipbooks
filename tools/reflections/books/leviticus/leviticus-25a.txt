# Leviticus 25:1-28: The sabbath year and the jubilee

== 25:1
T: In mount Sinai
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses in mount Sinai, saying.' God spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, giving laws about the land. Notice in mount Sinai. This reminds us where these laws came from. The same mountain where God gave the Ten Commandments. These laws about land and freedom carry the same authority. Notice that this chapter is about economics. Land, debt, poverty, and slavery. God's law reached into finances. Faith is not only spiritual. It shapes how we handle money and treat the poor. Notice that God gave these laws before Israel owned any land. He was shaping their society in advance. He planned for justice before injustice could take root. Psalm 24 says, The earth is the LORD'S, and the fulness thereof. Everything belongs to Him, including our money and property. Today, let God's word shape how you handle your finances. Your possessions belong to Him.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses in mount Sinai about laws for the land. These economic laws carried Sinai's authority and shaped Israel's society before they owned land. Let God's word shape how you handle your finances, for your possessions belong to Him.
P: The earth is the LORD's. Leviticus 25:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses in mount Sinai.' God's law reaches into your finances. Let Him shape how you use money.

== 25:2
T: The land shall keep a sabbath
R: 'Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land which I give you, then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the LORD.' When Israel entered the land, the land itself was to observe a sabbath to the LORD. Notice the land shall keep a sabbath. Not just people, but the land rested. Creation itself was included in God's rhythm of rest. God cared for the soil. Notice which I give you. The land was a gift. Israel received it from God. They were stewards, not absolute owners. Genesis 2 says God put man in the garden to dress it and to keep it. Humans care for God's earth. Notice unto the LORD. The land's rest was an act of worship. Letting the land rest honored God as its true owner. Today, care for the earth as God's creation. Rest, too, is part of faithful stewardship.
S: When Israel came into the land God gave them, the land itself was to keep a sabbath unto the LORD. Creation was included in God's rhythm of rest, and Israel were stewards of His gift. Care for the earth as God's creation and see rest as part of faithful stewardship.
P: The land rests too. Leviticus 25:2: 'then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the LORD.' God cares for creation. Be a faithful steward of His earth.

== 25:4
T: A sabbath of rest unto the land
R: 'But in the seventh year shall be a sabbath of rest unto the land, a sabbath for the LORD: thou shalt neither sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.' In the seventh year, the land would have a sabbath of rest. Israel was not to sow or prune. Notice the seventh year. A whole year without planting. That required enormous faith. How would they eat? They had to trust God completely. Notice a sabbath for the LORD. The year belonged to God. Israel stopped their work to honor Him. They declared that their provision came from Him, not from their labor. Notice what Second Chronicles 36 says. Israel failed to keep these sabbath years. So God sent them into exile for seventy years, until the land had enjoyed her sabbaths. Neglecting God's rest had consequences. Today, practice trust. Rest from your striving and rely on God's provision. Your security comes from Him, not from endless work.
S: In the seventh year the land had a sabbath of rest, with no sowing or pruning. It required great faith that provision came from God, and neglecting it later brought exile. Rest from your striving and trust that your security comes from God, not endless work.

== 25:25
T: His kin shall redeem
R: 'If thy brother be waxen poor, and hath sold away some of his possession, and if any of his kin come to redeem it, then shall he redeem that which his brother sold.' If an Israelite became poor and sold some of his land, his nearest relative was to redeem what he had sold. Notice his kin. The kinsman redeemer. A close relative had the right and duty to buy back the land. Family members cared for one another. Ruth 4 shows Boaz acting as kinsman redeemer for Naomi's land and marrying Ruth. Their son Obed was the grandfather of David. Notice that Christ is our kinsman redeemer. He became our relative by taking on human flesh. Hebrews 2 says, he also himself likewise took part of the same… he is not ashamed to call them brethren. He became our kin so He could redeem us. Today, rejoice that Christ became your brother to redeem you. He is your kinsman redeemer.
