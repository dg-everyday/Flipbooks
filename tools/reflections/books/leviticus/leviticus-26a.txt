# Leviticus 26:1-23: Blessings for obedience and the first warnings

== 26:1
T: No idols
R: 'Ye shall make you no idols nor graven image, neither rear you up a standing image, neither shall ye set up any image of stone in your land, to bow down unto it: for I am the LORD your God.' Israel was not to make idols, set up carved images or sacred stones, or bow down to them, for the LORD is their God. Notice the chapter begins with idolatry. Before blessings and warnings, God named the root issue. Everything depended on worshiping Him alone. Notice the many forms. Idols, graven images, standing images, carved stones. Idolatry takes many shapes. It adapts to every culture. Notice for I am the LORD your God. No image could represent Him. He alone deserved worship. Exodus 20 says, Thou shalt have no other gods before me. Colossians 3 calls covetousness idolatry. Anything we love more than God becomes an idol. First John 5 says, Little children, keep yourselves from idols. Today, examine your heart for hidden idols. Give God the first place He deserves.
S: Israel was to make no idols, graven images, standing images or carved stones to bow down to, for the LORD is their God. The chapter began with the root issue of worshiping Him alone. Examine your heart for hidden idols and give God the first place He deserves.
P: No other gods. Leviticus 26:1: 'Ye shall make you no idols.' Anything loved more than God becomes an idol. Keep yourself from idols today.

== 26:2
T: Sabbaths and sanctuary
R: 'Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am the LORD.' Israel was to observe God's sabbaths and revere His sanctuary, for He is the LORD. Notice the pairing with verse 1. Avoid idols; keep sabbaths. Reject false worship; embrace true worship. Holiness has both a no and a yes. Notice sabbaths and sanctuary. Holy time and holy place. These were the heartbeat of Israel's worship. Neglecting them led to drift. Notice this command appeared in chapter 19 too. God repeated it at this crucial moment, before the blessings and curses. Israel's future depended on their worship. Hebrews 10 says, Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together. Regular worship anchors us. Psalm 84 says, How amiable are thy tabernacles, O LORD of hosts! Today, keep a rhythm of rest and worship. Let God's house and God's day anchor your life.
S: Israel was to keep God's sabbaths and reverence His sanctuary. Rejecting idols went together with embracing true worship, which anchored their future. Keep a rhythm of rest and worship and let God's house and day anchor your life.
P: Rest and reverence. Leviticus 26:2: 'Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary.' Let God's day and God's house anchor your life today.
H: #DailyGrace #Leviticus262 #Sabbath #Worship #Reverence #BibleVerse

== 26:3
T: If ye walk
R: 'If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them.' If Israel followed God's decrees and carefully obeyed His commands, the blessings that follow would come. Notice if. The blessings were conditional. They depended on Israel's response. God gave a choice. Notice walk in my statutes. Obedience was a way of life, a daily walk. Not occasional obedience, but a steady direction. Notice keep… and do. Guarding God's word and practicing it. Both heart and hands. Deuteronomy 28 gives a parallel list of blessings and curses. Joshua 24 says, choose you this day whom ye will serve. God always invites a response. John 15 records Jesus saying, If ye keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love. Obedience keeps us close to Him. Today, choose to walk in God's ways. Let obedience be the steady direction of your life.
S: If Israel walked in God's statutes, kept His commandments and did them, blessings would follow. God offered a choice, calling for obedience as a daily walk. Choose to walk in God's ways and let obedience be the steady direction of your life.
P: If ye walk. Leviticus 26:3: 'If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments.' Obedience is a daily walk. Choose His ways today.
H: #DailyGrace #Leviticus263 #Obedience #Walk #Choice #BibleVerse

== 26:4
T: Rain in due season
R: 'Then I will give you rain in due season, and the land shall yield her increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.' God promised to send rain at the right time, so the land would produce crops and the trees fruit. Notice rain in due season. In Israel, rain was everything. No rain meant famine. God controlled the rain. Israel depended on Him for life itself. Notice in due season. Timing mattered. Rain at the wrong time could destroy crops. God promised perfect timing. Notice I will give. Provision came from God's hand. Not from Baal, the Canaanite storm god. Israel would be tempted to worship Baal for rain. But God alone sends rain. Matthew 5 says God sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust. James 5 says, the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth… until he receive the early and latter rain. Today, thank God for His provision. Every good thing comes in His season.
S: God promised rain in due season so the land would yield her increase and the trees their fruit. Israel depended on God, not Baal, for the rain that meant life. Thank God for His provision and trust that every good thing comes in His season.
P: Rain in due season. Leviticus 26:4: 'I will give you rain in due season.' Every good gift comes from God's hand. Trust His timing today.
H: #DailyGrace #Leviticus264 #Provision #Timing #Blessing #BibleVerse

== 26:5
T: Bread to the full
R: 'And your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage shall reach unto the sowing time: and ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in your land safely.' The harvest would be so great that threshing would continue until grape harvest, and grape harvest until planting time. They would eat their fill and live safely. Notice the overlap. One harvest would run into the next. So much abundance that there would be no gap. Amos 9 echoes this: the plowman shall overtake the reaper. Notice to the full. Not barely enough, but satisfaction. God's blessing is generous. Notice dwell in your land safely. Abundance and security together. No fear of hunger or enemies. Psalm 4 says, I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety. John 10 records Jesus saying, I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly. Today, rest in God's abundant provision. He gives life to the full.
S: The threshing would reach the vintage and the vintage the sowing time, and Israel would eat bread to the full and dwell safely. God's blessing was generous abundance and security. Rest in God's abundant provision, for Christ came to give life more abundantly.
P: Life to the full. Leviticus 26:5: 'ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in your land safely.' Christ came to give abundant life. Rest in His provision.

God would grant peace in the land. They would lie down without fear. He would remove dangerous animals, and no sword would pass through. Notice peace in the land. Shalom. God's blessing brought deep peace. Notice ye shall lie down. Rest without fear. Sleeping safely is a gift. God promised His people security. Notice none shall make you afraid. Fear removed. John 14 records Jesus saying, Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you… Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid. Today, receive God's peace. == 26:7
T: Ye shall chase your enemies
R: 'And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.' Israel would pursue their enemies, and those enemies would fall before them. Notice ye shall chase. Victory belonged to God's obedient people. Not because of their strength, but because God fought for them. Notice the reversal. In the curses later, Israel would flee from enemies. Here, they chase enemies. Obedience made the difference. Notice that our enemies today are spiritual. Ephesians 6 says, we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers. Our battles are against sin, temptation, and the evil one. Romans 8 says, we are more than conquerors through him that loved us. James 4 says, Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. Today, stand firm against spiritual enemies. In Christ, you are more than a conqueror.
S: Israel would chase their enemies, and they would fall before them by the sword. Victory came because God fought for His obedient people, and our battles today are spiritual. Stand firm against spiritual enemies, for in Christ you are more than a conqueror.

== 26:8
T: Five shall chase an hundred
R: 'And five of you shall chase an hundred, and an hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight: and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.' Five Israelites would chase a hundred, and a hundred would chase ten thousand. Their enemies would fall before them. Notice the impossible odds. Five against a hundred. A hundred against ten thousand. These numbers made no sense humanly. Victory came from God, not numbers. Notice that this happened. Judges 7 tells of Gideon defeating Midian with three hundred men. First Samuel 14 tells of Jonathan and his armorbearer routing a Philistine garrison. Jonathan said, there is no restraint to the LORD to save by many or by few. Notice that God delights to work through the few. Second Corinthians 12 says, my strength is made perfect in weakness. Zechariah 4 says, Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit. Today, do not be discouraged by your small numbers or limited strength. God can win great victories through the few.
S: Five would chase a hundred, and a hundred would put ten thousand to flight. Victory came from God, not numbers, as Gideon and Jonathan discovered. Do not be discouraged by small numbers or limited strength, for God wins great victories through the few.
P: Not by might. Leviticus 26:8: 'five of you shall chase an hundred.' No restraint to the LORD to save by many or by few. Trust Him today.

Second Corinthians 1 says, all the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen. In Christ, every covenant promise is confirmed. Today, rejoice that God looks on you with favor in Christ. His covenant with you is established and sure.

The greatest blessing was not rain, harvest, or victory. It was God's presence. He would live among them. This was the heart of the covenant. Notice my soul shall not abhor you. God would not turn away from them. He would delight in them. His presence would be welcoming, not hostile. John 1 says, the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us. In Christ, God tabernacled with us. First Corinthians 3 says believers are the temple of God, and the Spirit of God dwelleth in you. Revelation 21 says, Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men. Notice will be your God, and ye shall be my people. The covenant formula. It appears throughout Scripture. Jeremiah 31, Ezekiel 37, Second Corinthians 6, and Revelation 21 all repeat it. Notice that Second Corinthians 6 quotes this verse: I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people. Paul applied it to the church. Revelation 21 says, God himself shall be with them, and be their God. Today, rejoice that God walks with you. You are His, and He is yours.
