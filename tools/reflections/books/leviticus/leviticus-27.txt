# Leviticus 27: Vows, dedications and tithes

== 27:1
T: A final word
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' God spoke to Moses one last time in Leviticus, now about vows and gifts dedicated to Him. Notice that the book ends with voluntary devotion. Chapter 26 closed the covenant with blessings and warnings. Chapter 27 adds instructions for people who want to give more than what is required. Leviticus ends not with obligation but with freewill dedication. Notice that God cares about our promises. When people made vows, God wanted them handled properly. Promises to God are serious. Ecclesiastes 5 says, When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools: pay that which thou hast vowed. Notice that the last word of Leviticus concerns giving. After all the laws about holiness, the book closes with generous devotion. Today, consider what you might give to God freely, beyond what is required. And keep the promises you have made to Him.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses a final time in Leviticus, now about vows and dedicated gifts. The book closes not with obligation but with freewill devotion and the seriousness of promises to God. Consider what you might give God freely and keep the promises you have made to Him.
P: Beyond what is required. Leviticus 27:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' Leviticus ends with freewill devotion. Give freely and keep your vows to God.

== 27:2
T: A singular vow
R: 'Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When a man shall make a singular vow, the persons shall be for the LORD by thy estimation.' If someone made a special vow to dedicate a person to the LORD, it would be settled by paying an equivalent value. Notice a singular vow. A special, extraordinary promise. Someone might vow to give a person, perhaps himself or a family member, to God's service. Hannah did this with Samuel in First Samuel 1. Notice by thy estimation. Not everyone dedicated could serve at the tabernacle. So a fair value was set, and money could be given instead. The vow was honored in a practical way. Notice that people could be dedicated to God. This is the heart of every vow. Romans 12 says, present your bodies a living sacrifice. We give ourselves to God. Second Corinthians 8 says the Macedonians first gave their own selves to the Lord. Today, give yourself to God. Let your life be your greatest offering to Him.
S: When someone made a singular vow dedicating a person to the LORD, it was settled by an estimated value. People could be dedicated to God, as Hannah dedicated Samuel. Give yourself to God first and let your life be your greatest offering.
P: First gave themselves. Leviticus 27:2: 'When a man shall make a singular vow, the persons shall be for the LORD.' Give yourself to God first, as a living sacrifice.

== 27:3
T: Fifty shekels of silver
R: 'And thy estimation shall be of the male from twenty years old even unto sixty years old, even thy estimation shall be fifty shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary.' For a man between twenty and sixty, the value was fifty shekels of silver, using the sanctuary standard. Notice the estimation. These values were not a measure of a person's worth before God. They reflected the economic value of a person's labor in that society. Notice after the shekel of the sanctuary. A standard, honest measure. Notice that every person was given a value. No one was worthless. Matthew 10 says, ye are of more value than many sparrows. God values every person. First Peter 1 says we were redeemed not with corruptible things, as silver and gold, But with the precious blood of Christ. Your true value is measured by what Christ paid for you. Today, remember that your worth is not measured in shekels. It is measured by the blood of Christ.
S: For a male from twenty to sixty, the estimation was fifty shekels of silver after the shekel of the sanctuary. These values reflected labor in that economy, not a person's worth before God. Remember that your worth is measured by the precious blood of Christ.

Notice that the Savior who honored women throughout His ministry was Himself sold for this sum. Women were the first witnesses of His resurrection. Today, honor every person equally as God's image-bearer. And marvel at the price Jesus allowed to be placed on Himself for you.
