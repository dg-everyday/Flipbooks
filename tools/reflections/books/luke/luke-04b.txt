S: Jesus kept on preaching in the synagogues of Galilee. He went faithfully from place to place without slowing, for steady, consistent service spreads the message. Keep going, for preach the word; be instant in season, out of season.
P: Kept preaching. Luke 4:44: 'he preached in the synagogues of Galilee.' Instant. Preach the word; be instant in season, out of season. Keep faithful today. Take it to God in prayer.
