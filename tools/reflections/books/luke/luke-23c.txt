Then they went home and prepared spices and perfumes. Notice and rested the sabbath day according to the commandment. But they rested on the Sabbath in obedience to the commandment. Notice that the women prepared spices. Notice that they planned to honour His body. Notice that they obeyed God's command to rest. Notice that grief did not stop obedience. Notice that they waited. Notice that love prepared. Notice that faith rested. Notice that they did not know what was coming. Notice that resurrection was near. Notice that we too obey in grief. Notice that we rest and wait. Notice that God has surprises ahead. Notice that Psalm 37 urged this. Psalm 37 says, 'Rest in the LORD, and wait patiently for him' Notice rest in the LORD. Today, obey and rest in God, trusting that hope is near.
S: The women went home and prepared spices and perfumes, but they rested on the Sabbath in obedience to the commandment. Grief did not stop their obedience, and they waited without knowing resurrection was near. Obey and wait in hope, for rest in the LORD, and wait patiently for him.
