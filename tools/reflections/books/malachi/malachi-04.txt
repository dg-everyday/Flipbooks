# Malachi 4: The Sun of righteousness

== 4:1
T: The day cometh, that shall burn as an oven
R: 'For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven' Notice for, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven. Surely the day is coming; it will burn like a furnace. Notice and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble. All the arrogant and every evildoer will be stubble. Notice that it shall leave them neither root nor branch. Not a root or a branch will be left to them. Notice that the day comes. Notice that it burns. Notice that the proud are stubble. Notice that stubble burns quickly. Notice that nothing remains. Notice that this answers the complaint. Notice that the wicked do not escape. Notice that justice is certain. Notice that it is serious. Notice that we should turn from pride. Notice that Jesus also warned. Matthew 3 says, 'but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.' Notice the chaff. Today, turn from pride, for God's justice is certain.
S: The LORD said the day is coming that will burn like a furnace, and all the arrogant and evildoers will be stubble with neither root nor branch left. The wicked who seemed to prosper do not escape, for justice is certain. Turn from pride, for He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.
P: The day burns. Malachi 4:1: 'the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven.' Chaff. He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire. Turn from pride today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 4:2
T: The Sun of righteousness with healing in his wings
R: 'But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings' Notice but unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings. But for you who revere My name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its rays. Notice and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall. And you will go out and frolic like well-fed calves. Notice that the same day brings two outcomes. Notice that for the proud it burns. Notice that the sun rises. Notice that it brings warmth. Notice that it brings healing. Notice that the faithful leap for joy. Notice that this points to Christ. Notice that He is the light of the world. Notice that He heals. Notice that Luke spoke of this dawn. Luke 1 says, 'the dayspring from on high hath visited us' Notice the dayspring from on high. Today, let Christ, the Sun of righteousness, bring healing and joy.
S: For those who revere God's name, the Sun of righteousness would rise with healing in its wings, and they would leap like well-fed calves. The same day that burns the proud heals the faithful, pointing to Christ, the light who heals. Let Him bring you joy, for the dayspring from on high hath visited us.
P: Healing in His wings. Malachi 4:2: 'shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings.' Dayspring. The dayspring from on high hath visited us. Let Christ heal you today. Trust Him with it today.

Notice for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this, saith the LORD of hosts. They will be ashes under the soles of your feet on the day when I act, says the LORD Almighty. Notice that the situation is reversed. Notice that the oppressed triumph. Notice that the proud fall. Notice that God acts. Notice that the faithful share His victory. Notice that this is His doing. Notice that it is not revenge. Notice that it is justice. Notice that evil is finally defeated. Notice that the faithful are vindicated. Notice that Paul spoke of this victory. Romans 16 says, 'And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly.' Notice under your feet. Today, take heart, for evil will be defeated.
S: The LORD said the faithful would trample on the wicked, who would be ashes under their feet on the day He acts. The situation is reversed by God's justice, not by revenge, and evil is finally defeated. Take heart, for the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly.

== 4:4
T: Remember ye the law of Moses
R: 'Remember ye the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel' Notice remember ye the law of Moses my servant. Remember the law of My servant Moses. Notice which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel, with the statutes and judgments. The decrees and laws I gave him at Horeb for all Israel. Notice that the Old Testament ends with remembering. Notice that God points back to His word. Notice that the law was a gift. Notice that it guided Israel. Notice that it was to be remembered. Notice that while waiting, they should obey. Notice that God's word guides us in waiting. Notice that we remember what He has said. Notice that we live by it. Notice that it prepares us for what is coming. Notice that the psalmist loved the law. Psalm 119 says, 'Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.' Notice a lamp unto my feet. Today, remember God's word and live by it.
S: The LORD told His people to remember the law of His servant Moses, given at Horeb for all Israel. While they waited, God pointed them back to His word to guide and prepare them. Remember it and live by it, for thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.
P: Remember. Malachi 4:4: 'Remember ye the law of Moses my servant.' A lamp. Thy word is a lamp unto my feet. Remember God's word today. Take it to God in prayer.

Notice that God promises a forerunner. Notice that Elijah was a bold prophet. Notice that one would come in his spirit. Notice that Jesus identified him. Notice that John came in Elijah's spirit and power. Notice that he called people to repent. Notice that God sends warning before judgment. Notice that He is merciful. Notice that He gives time. Notice that we do not set dates. Notice that we listen to His call. Notice that Jesus said this of John. Matthew 11 says, 'this is Elias, which was for to come.' Notice which was for to come. Today, heed God's call to repent, for He warns in mercy.
S: The LORD promised to send the prophet Elijah before the great and dreadful day of the LORD. Jesus said John came in Elijah's spirit, calling people to repent, for God mercifully warns before He judges. Heed that call without setting dates, for Jesus said of John, this is Elias, which was for to come.
P: Elijah sent. Malachi 4:5: 'Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet.' Fulfilled. This is Elias, which was for to come. Heed God's call today. Trust Him with it today.

== 4:6

He will turn the hearts of the parents to their children. Notice and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse. And the hearts of the children to their parents; or else I will come and strike the land with total destruction. Notice that the Old Testament ends with families. Notice that hearts are turned. Notice that parents turn to children. Notice that children turn to parents. Notice that the last word is a warning. Notice that the next word is the gospel. Notice that Christ brings this healing. Notice that Luke recorded this. Luke 1 says, 'to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children' Notice to turn the hearts. Today, pray for God to turn hearts in your family toward one another.
S: The promised messenger would turn the hearts of parents to their children and children to their parents, lest the land be struck with a curse. The Old Testament ends with God's care for families and restoration beginning at home, and the next word is the gospel. Pray for your family, for John came to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children.
