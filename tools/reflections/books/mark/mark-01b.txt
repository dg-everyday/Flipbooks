R: 'But he went out, and began to publish it much, and to blaze abroad the matter' Notice but he went out, and began to publish it much, and to blaze abroad the matter. Instead he went out and began to talk freely, spreading the news. Notice that as a result Jesus could no longer enter a town openly, yet people came to Him from everywhere. As a result, Jesus could no longer enter a town openly but stayed outside in lonely places. Yet the people still came to Him from everywhere. Notice that the man disobeyed. Notice that his joy overflowed. Notice that he told everyone. Notice that this made things harder for Jesus. Notice that God still worked. Notice that people were drawn to Jesus anyway. Notice that nothing stops seekers. Notice that Jeremiah promised seekers would find. Jeremiah 29 says, 'And ye shall seek me, and find me' Notice ye shall seek me, and find me. Today, seek Jesus wherever He is to be found.
S: The man went out and spread the news freely, so Jesus could no longer enter a town openly but stayed in lonely places, yet people still came to Him from everywhere. Joyful disobedience had a cost, but God still worked and seekers found Jesus anyway. Seek Him, for ye shall seek me, and find me.
P: From everywhere. Mark 1:45: 'they came to him from every quarter.' Finding. Ye shall seek me, and find me. Seek Jesus today. Trust Him with it today.
