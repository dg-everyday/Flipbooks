R: 'And they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh: but he received it not.' Notice and they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh. Then they offered Him wine mixed with myrrh. Notice but he received it not. But He did not take it. Notice that they offered a drink. Notice that it would dull pain. Notice that Jesus refused. Notice that He chose to bear it fully. Notice that He kept His mind clear. Notice that He faced the cross with full awareness. Notice that He held nothing back. Notice that He drank the Father's cup instead. Notice that His love was complete. Notice that He suffered fully for us. Notice that nothing was diminished. Notice that we are loved completely. Notice that John recorded His resolve. John 18 says, 'the cup which my Father hath given me, shall I not drink it?' Notice the cup which my Father hath given me. Today, receive the complete love of Jesus who suffered fully for you.
S: They offered Him wine mixed with myrrh to dull the pain, but He did not take it. He chose to bear the cross fully and clearly, drinking the Father's cup instead, for His love held nothing back. Receive His complete love, for He said, the cup which my Father hath given me, shall I not drink it.
