R: 'And he came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophets' Notice and he came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth. And he went and lived in a town called Nazareth. Notice that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophets, He shall be called a Nazarene. So was fulfilled what was said through the prophets, that He would be called a Nazarene. Notice that Nazareth was obscure. Notice that it was looked down on. Notice that Jesus grew up there. Notice that He was humble. Notice that He was despised by some. Notice that the prophets foretold a lowly Messiah. Notice that God chose humble beginnings. Notice that Jesus identifies with the overlooked. Notice that He grew quietly. Notice that humility was His way. Notice that Nathanael questioned it. John 1 says, 'Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?' Notice any good thing. Today, remember that God works through humble places.
S: Joseph went and lived in Nazareth, fulfilling what the prophets said, that He would be called a Nazarene. Jesus grew up in an obscure town that some looked down on, choosing humble beginnings and identifying with the overlooked. Remember that, even when people asked whether any good thing could come out of Nazareth.
P: A Nazarene. Matthew 2:23: 'He shall be called a Nazarene.' Good thing. Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth. Remember God's humble ways today. Trust Him with it today.
