Therefore, if you are offering your gift at the altar. Notice and there rememberest that thy brother hath ought against thee. And there remember that your brother or sister has something against you. Notice that Jesus links worship and relationships. Notice that it is about someone with something against you. Notice that you may have wronged them. Notice that you remember it while worshipping. Notice that God brings it to mind. Notice that worship surfaces broken relationships. Notice that we cannot ignore them. Notice that God cares about reconciliation. Notice that relationships matter to Him. Notice that worship and life connect. Notice that the psalmist asked God to search him. == 5:24
T: First be reconciled to thy brother
R: 'Leave there thy gift before the altar, and go thy way; first be reconciled to thy brother' Notice leave there thy gift before the altar, and go thy way. Leave your gift there in front of the altar. First go. Notice first be reconciled to thy brother, and then come and offer thy gift. And be reconciled to them; then come and offer your gift. Notice that Jesus says leave the gift. Notice that reconciliation comes first. Notice that worship can wait. Notice that relationships cannot. Notice that we must go. Notice that we take the initiative. Notice that we seek peace. Notice that then worship is free. Notice that God values peace. Notice that it may be hard. Notice that it is worth it. Notice that Paul urged forgiveness. Colossians 3 says, 'even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye.' Notice so also do ye. Today, take the first step to make peace with someone.
S: Jesus said to leave the gift before the altar, go and be reconciled first, then come and offer it. Reconciliation comes before worship, and we must take the initiative to seek peace, however hard. Take the first step, for even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye.
