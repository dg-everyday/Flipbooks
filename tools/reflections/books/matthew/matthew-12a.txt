R: 'And all the people were amazed, and said, Is not this the son of David?' Notice and all the people were amazed. All the people were astonished. Notice and said, Is not this the son of David? And said, Could this be the Son of David? Notice that the people were amazed. Notice that they asked the right question. Notice that Son of David meant Messiah. Notice that they were beginning to see. Notice that faith often starts with a question. Notice that wonder opens the door. Notice that they were close to the truth. Notice that God was revealing Jesus. Notice that questions can lead to belief. Notice that honest wonder is good. Notice that we should follow it. Notice that Jesus asked this question later. Matthew 22 says, 'What think ye of Christ? whose son is he?' Notice what think ye of Christ. Today, let wonder at Jesus lead you to faith.
S: All the people were astonished and asked whether this could be the Son of David. Their amazement led to the right question about the Messiah, for faith often begins with honest wonder. Follow it, for Jesus later asked the crowd, What think ye of Christ.
P: Son of David. Matthew 12:23: 'Is not this the son of David?' Question. What think ye of Christ? Wonder at Jesus today. Trust Him with it today.

== 12:24
T: By Beelzebub the prince of the devils
R: 'This fellow doth not cast out devils, but by Beelzebub the prince of the devils.' Notice but when the Pharisees heard it, they said. But when the Pharisees heard this, they said. Notice this fellow doth not cast out devils, but by Beelzebub the prince of the devils. It is only by Beelzebul, the prince of demons, that this fellow drives out demons. Notice that the crowd wondered. Notice that the Pharisees accused. Notice that they could not deny the miracle. Notice that they attributed it to evil. Notice that they called God's work the devil's. Notice that this is serious. Notice that hardness twists truth. Notice that they would rather blame evil than believe. Notice that envy blinds. Notice that pride resists. Notice that we must keep our hearts soft. Notice that Hebrews warned of this. Hebrews 3 says, 'harden not your hearts' Notice harden not your hearts. Today, keep your heart soft toward the work of God.
S: When the Pharisees heard the crowd, they said Jesus drove out demons only by Beelzebul, the prince of demons. Unable to deny the miracle, they called God's work evil, for hardness twists truth and envy blinds. Keep your heart soft, for harden not your hearts.
P: Twisted truth. Matthew 12:24: 'but by Beelzebub the prince of the devils.' Soft hearts. Harden not your hearts. Keep your heart soft today. Let this shape your day.

== 12:25
T: Every kingdom divided against itself
R: 'And Jesus knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation' Notice and Jesus knew their thoughts, and said unto them. Jesus knew their thoughts and said to them. Notice every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand. Every kingdom divided against itself will be ruined, and every city or household divided against itself will not stand. Notice that Jesus knew their thoughts again. Notice that He answered with logic. Notice that division destroys. Notice that kingdoms fall from within. Notice that homes fall from within. Notice that unity is strength. Notice that families need unity. Notice that communities need unity. Notice that God's people need unity. Notice that Jesus prayed for this. Notice that the psalmist praised unity. Psalm 133 says, 'Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!' Notice dwell together in unity. Today, work for unity in your home and community.
S: Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said every kingdom, city or household divided against itself will not stand. Division destroys from within, so families, communities and God's people need unity. Work for it, for behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity.
P: Divided houses. Matthew 12:25: 'every city or house divided against itself shall not stand.' Unity. How good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity. Work for unity today. Take it to God in prayer.
