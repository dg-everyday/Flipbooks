R: 'But and if that evil servant shall say in his heart, My lord delayeth his coming' Notice but and if that evil servant shall say in his heart. But suppose that servant is wicked and says to himself. Notice my lord delayeth his coming. My master is staying away a long time. Notice that the wicked servant thought about delay. Notice that he assumed time. Notice that he grew careless. Notice that delay led to complacency. Notice that this is a danger. Notice that we can think we have time. Notice that we postpone faithfulness. Notice that we put off repentance. Notice that we assume tomorrow. Notice that this is foolish. Notice that we do not know. Notice that today matters. Notice that Peter warned of scoffers. 2 Peter 3 says, 'Where is the promise of his coming?' Notice where is the promise. Today, refuse complacency and live faithfully now.
S: Jesus said that if the servant is wicked and says to himself that his master is staying away a long time, trouble follows. Assuming delay led to complacency and postponed faithfulness, a foolish danger since we do not know the time. Refuse complacency, unlike scoffers who ask, Where is the promise of his coming.
P: Delay. Matthew 24:48: 'My lord delayeth his coming.' Scoffing. Where is the promise of his coming. Stay faithful today. Trust Him with it today.

Notice that the verse adds that he joins in carousing. And to eat and drink with those who carouse. Notice that complacency led to cruelty. Notice that he mistreated others. Notice that he indulged himself. Notice that unaccountability corrupts. Notice that he thought no one would see. Notice that he abused his position. Notice that he harmed those under him. Notice that this is serious. Notice that God sees how we treat others. Notice that if someone is harming you, it is right to seek help and safety. Notice that God defends the mistreated. Notice that Proverbs warned of this. Proverbs 23 says, 'Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters' Notice be not among. Today, treat others well, remembering God sees.
S: Jesus said the wicked servant begins to beat his fellow servants and to carouse with others. Thinking himself unaccountable, he abused his position and harmed those under him, which God sees, and if someone is harming you, it is right to seek help and safety. Treat others well, and be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters.
P: Mistreatment. Matthew 24:49: 'shall begin to smite his fellowservants.' Not among. Be not among winebibbers. Treat others well today, and if you are being harmed, seek help. Let this shape your day.

== 24:50

The master of that servant will come on a day when he does not expect him. Notice and in an hour that he is not aware of. And at an hour he is not aware of. Notice that the master came unexpectedly. Notice that the servant was caught. Notice that he was unprepared. Notice that complacency was exposed. Notice that the surprise was his fault. Notice that we should not be caught unprepared. Notice that we can be ready now. Notice that faithfulness is always the best policy. Notice that God's coming will reveal hearts. Notice that Revelation warned of this. Revelation 3 says, 'I will come on thee as a thief, and thou shalt not know what hour' Notice thou shalt not know what hour. Today, stay ready so you are not caught unprepared.
S: Jesus said the master of that servant will come on a day he does not expect and at an hour he is not aware of. The complacent servant was caught unprepared because he chose not to watch, a warning to stay faithful now. Stay ready, for I will come on thee as a thief, and thou shalt not know what hour.
P: Unexpected. Matthew 24:50: 'in a day when he looketh not for him.' Unknown hour. Thou shalt not know what hour. Stay ready today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 24:51
T: His portion with the hypocrites
R: 'And shall cut him asunder, and appoint him his portion with the hypocrites' Notice and shall cut him asunder, and appoint him his portion with the hypocrites. He will cut him to pieces and assign him a place with the hypocrites. Notice there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth. Where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth. Notice that the judgment is severe. Notice that the servant was a hypocrite. Notice that he claimed to serve but did not. Notice that his end was sorrow. Notice that Jesus warned strongly. Notice that the warning is love. Notice that He wants us ready. Notice that He wants us faithful. Notice that the way is clear. Notice that we can avoid this. Notice that we watch and serve. Notice that 2 Peter described God's patience. 2 Peter 3 says, 'The Lord is not slack concerning his promise … but is longsuffering to us-ward' Notice longsuffering to us-ward. Today, use God's patience to become faithful, not complacent.
S: Jesus said the master will cut the wicked servant to pieces and assign him a place with the hypocrites, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth. His strong warning comes from love, wanting us ready and faithful, and the way to avoid this is clear. Use His patience well, for the Lord is not slack concerning his promise, but is longsuffering to us-ward.
P: With hypocrites. Matthew 24:51: 'appoint him his portion with the hypocrites.' Patience. The Lord … is longsuffering to us-ward. Be faithful today. Trust Him with it today.
