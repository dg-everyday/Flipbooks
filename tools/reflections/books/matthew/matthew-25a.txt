Notice thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord. You have been faithful with a few things; I will put you in charge of many things. Come and share your master's happiness! Notice that the praise was identical. Notice that he received the same words. Notice that he received the same joy. Notice that joy awaits all the faithful. Notice that we need not be the greatest. Notice that we need to be faithful. Notice that Psalm 16 described this joy. Psalm 16 says, 'in thy presence is fulness of joy' Notice fulness of joy. Today, rejoice that God honours all faithfulness equally.
S: The master gave the second servant the identical praise, Well done, good and faithful servant, come and share your master's happiness. God does not rank faithful servants, for equal faithfulness receives equal praise and joy regardless of the amount. Rejoice, for in thy presence is fulness of joy.
P: The same joy. Matthew 25:23: 'enter thou into the joy of thy lord.' Fulness. In thy presence is fulness of joy. Rejoice today. Trust Him with it today.
