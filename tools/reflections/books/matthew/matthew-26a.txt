T: He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish
R: 'He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, the same shall betray me.' Notice and he answered and said. Jesus replied. Notice he that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, the same shall betray me. The one who has dipped his hand into the bowl with Me will betray Me. Notice that the betrayer shared His dish. Notice that he ate with Jesus. Notice that this was intimacy. Notice that betrayal was from the closest. Notice that sharing a meal meant friendship. Notice that this made it worse. Notice that Jesus still shared the dish. Notice that He did not withdraw. Notice that His love remained. Notice that grace offered to the end. Notice that we can learn from this. Notice that love does not retaliate. Notice that the psalm foretold it. Psalm 41 says, 'Yea, mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted' Notice mine own familiar friend. Today, keep loving even when love is not returned.
S: Jesus replied that the one who dipped his hand into the bowl with Him would betray Him. Sharing a meal meant friendship, making the betrayal worse, yet Jesus kept offering love and did not withdraw. Keep loving, as Jesus did toward mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted.
P: Same dish. Matthew 26:23: 'He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish.' Familiar friend. Mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted. Keep loving today. Trust Him with it today.

== 26:24
T: The Son of man goeth as it is written
R: 'The Son of man goeth as it is written of him: but woe unto that man by whom the Son of man is betrayed!' Notice the Son of man goeth as it is written of him. The Son of Man will go just as it is written about Him. Notice but woe unto that man by whom the Son of man is betrayed! it had been good for that man if he had not been born. But woe to that man who betrays the Son of Man! It would be better for him if he had not been born. Notice that Jesus' death was foretold. Notice that it was God's plan. Notice that yet Judas was responsible. Notice that both are true. Notice that woe is grief. Notice that betrayal has consequences. Notice that Acts recorded this balance. Acts 2 says, 'Him, being delivered by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God, ye have taken' Notice the determinate counsel. Today, trust God's plan while taking your own choices seriously.
S: Jesus said the Son of Man will go just as it is written, but woe to the man who betrays Him, for it would be better if he had not been born. God's plan does not excuse sin, for Judas chose freely and was responsible, and Jesus grieved for him. Trust God's plan and take your choices seriously, for Jesus was delivered by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God.
P: As it is written. Matthew 26:24: 'The Son of man goeth as it is written of him.' Counsel. By the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God. Choose faithfully today. Let this shape your day.

== 26:25
T: Master, is it I
R: 'Then Judas, which betrayed him, answered and said, Master, is it I? He said unto him, Thou hast said.' Notice then Judas, which betrayed him, answered and said, Master, is it I? Then Judas, the one who would betray Him, said, Surely you do not mean me, Rabbi? Notice he said unto him, Thou hast said. Jesus answered, You have said so. Notice that Judas asked too. Notice that he called Jesus Rabbi. Notice that the others said Lord. Notice that he pretended innocence. Notice that Jesus answered quietly. Notice that He exposed Judas privately. Notice that He did not shame him publicly. Notice that He gave him another chance. Notice that Judas did not repent. Notice that he hardened. Notice that God gives many chances. Notice that we must respond. Notice that Hebrews warned of this. Hebrews 3 says, 'harden not your hearts' Notice harden not your hearts. Today, respond to Jesus' gentle confrontation with repentance.
S: Judas, who would betray Him, asked, Surely you do not mean me, Rabbi, and Jesus answered, You have said so. Jesus exposed him quietly without public shame, giving yet another chance, but Judas hardened instead of repenting. Respond to His gentle confrontation, for harden not your hearts.
P: Is it I. Matthew 26:25: 'Master, is it I? He said unto him, Thou hast said.' Soft hearts. Harden not your hearts. Repent when confronted today. Take it to God in prayer.
