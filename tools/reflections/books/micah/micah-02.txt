Woe to those who plan iniquity, to those who plot evil on their beds! Notice when the morning is light, they practise it, because it is in the power of their hand. At morning's light they carry it out because it is in their power to do it. Notice that they plan evil at night. Notice that they carry it out at dawn. Notice that they do it because they can. Notice that power without conscience is dangerous. Notice that evil begins in the mind. Notice that it grows in private. Notice that our night thoughts matter. Notice that we should meditate on good. Notice that the psalmist did. Psalm 63 says, 'When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches.' Notice meditate on thee in the night watches. Today, fill your quiet thoughts with God rather than schemes.
S: Micah warned those who planned iniquity and plotted evil on their beds, then carried it out at morning because they had the power. Evil began in their night thoughts and acted when they could, for what we dwell on we do. Fill your quiet thoughts with God, as the psalmist said, when I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches.
P: Night plans. Micah 2:1: 'work evil upon their beds!' Night meditation. Meditate on thee in the night watches. Think on God today. Take it to God in prayer.
