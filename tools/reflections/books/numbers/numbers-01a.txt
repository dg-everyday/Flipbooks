# Numbers 1:1-27: The census begins

== 1:1
T: In the wilderness of Sinai
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tabernacle of the congregation, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, saying.' A new book begins. Israel was still at Sinai, a little over a year after leaving Egypt. Now God spoke from the tabernacle. The Hebrew title of this book means in the wilderness. That describes it well. Numbers is the story of a journey between deliverance and the promised land. Many believers know that season well. We are rescued, but not yet home. Notice that God spoke in the wilderness. He did not wait for Israel to reach Canaan before guiding them. Hosea 2 says, I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak comfortably unto her. Notice the exact date. God works in real time and real places. Today, listen for God's voice in your wilderness. He still speaks to His people on the way home.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses from the tabernacle in the wilderness of Sinai, a year after the exodus. Numbers tells of the journey between deliverance and the promised land. Listen for God's voice in your own wilderness, for He speaks to His people on the way.
P: In the wilderness. Numbers 1:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai.' Rescued but not yet home? God still speaks to you on the journey today.
H: #DailyGrace #Numbers11 #Wilderness #Journey #Guidance #BibleVerse

== 1:2
T: The number of their names
R: 'Take ye the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel, after their families, by the house of their fathers, with the number of their names, every male by their polls.' God told Moses to count the whole community by families and clans, listing every man by name. Notice with the number of their names. This was not a faceless count. Each man was named. God's people are not statistics to Him. Isaiah 43 says, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine. John 10 says the shepherd calleth his own sheep by name. Notice after their families. People were counted within their households. Identity came through family and tribe. God places us in communities. Notice that God knows exactly who belongs to Him. Second Timothy 2 says, The Lord knoweth them that are his. Luke 10 says, rejoice, because your names are written in heaven. Today, rejoice that God knows your name. You are not lost in the crowd. You are counted and known.
S: God told Moses to take the sum of Israel by families, with the number of their names. Each man was counted by name, for God's people are never faceless to Him. Rejoice that God knows your name and that you are counted among His own.
P: Known by name. Numbers 1:2: 'with the number of their names.' You're not a statistic to God. Rejoice that your name is written in heaven.

== 1:3
T: Able to go forth to war
R: 'From twenty years old and upward, all that are able to go forth to war in Israel: thou and Aaron shall number them by their armies.' Moses and Aaron were to count all men twenty years and older who could serve in the army. Notice able to go forth to war. Israel was not only a congregation but an army. They were about to march toward a land held by enemies. They needed to be ready. Believers are also called to spiritual battle. Ephesians 6 says, Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. Second Timothy 2 says, endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ. Notice by their armies. Organized, prepared, ready. God's people are not a scattered crowd but a disciplined host. Notice twenty years old. A time of maturity and responsibility. Today, take your place in God's army. Put on His armour and stand ready for the battles of faith.
S: Moses and Aaron were to number every man from twenty years old who was able to go forth to war. Israel was an army preparing to march, as believers are called to spiritual battle. Take your place in God's army, put on His armour and stand ready.
P: A good soldier. Numbers 1:3: 'all that are able to go forth to war in Israel.' You're called to spiritual battle. Put on the whole armour of God today.

== 1:4
T: A man of every tribe
R: 'And with you there shall be a man of every tribe; every one head of the house of his fathers.' One man from each tribe, the head of his family, was to help Moses and Aaron with the count. Notice a man of every tribe. Every tribe was represented. No group was left out. Leadership was shared across the nation. Moses did not do it all himself. Exodus 18 shows Jethro warning Moses, Thou wilt surely wear away… thou art not able to perform it thyself alone. Shared leadership protects leaders and serves people better. Notice head of the house of his fathers. The helpers were proven leaders in their own families. First Timothy 3 says a leader must rule well his own house. Faithfulness at home prepares for wider service. Today, value shared leadership. Do your part in your family and community, and support those who lead alongside you.
S: A man from every tribe, the head of his father's house, was to assist Moses and Aaron. Leadership was shared across the nation, with proven family leaders helping. Value shared leadership, do your part, and support those who serve beside you.
P: Shared leadership. Numbers 1:4: 'with you there shall be a man of every tribe.' No one leads alone. Do your part and support those who serve with you.

T: Elizur of Reuben
R: 'And these are the names of the men that shall stand with you: of the tribe of Reuben; Elizur the son of Shedeur.' The leaders were named, beginning with Elizur son of Shedeur from Reuben. Notice the names. Scripture records them carefully. These men may seem obscure to us, but God remembered them. Elizur means my God is a rock. His name was a confession of faith. Notice Reuben listed first. Reuben was Jacob's firstborn. Though he lost the birthright through sin in Genesis 49, his tribe was still honored and counted. Exodus 17 shows Aaron and Hur holding up Moses' hands. Psalm 18 says, The LORD is my rock. Today, stand with those God has called to lead. And let your life, like Elizur's name, confess that God is your rock.

T: Shelumiel of Simeon

Jacob said in Genesis 49, Cursed be their anger, for it was fierce. Simeon's history was troubled. Yet here a leader from Simeon stands with Moses. God can use people from difficult backgrounds. Notice the names. Shelumiel means at peace with God. Zurishaddai means my rock is the Almighty. From a tribe known for violence came a man whose name spoke of peace with God. Romans 5 says, being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Grace can change a family's story. Second Corinthians 5 says, if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature. Today, take hope if your family has a troubled past. God can write peace into your story.

T: Nahshon of Judah

His line led to Boaz, to David, and finally to Christ. This name in a list connects to the gospel. Notice Judah. Genesis 49 says, The sceptre shall not depart from Judah. Judah was the royal tribe. Revelation 5 calls Jesus the Lion of the tribe of Juda. Notice also Exodus 6. Aaron married Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, sister of Naashon. Nahshon was Aaron's brother-in-law. The priestly and royal lines were linked. Notice that God's plan runs through ordinary genealogies. A census list holds a link in the chain of salvation. Today, remember that God is working through ordinary lives toward His great purpose. Your faithfulness may matter far beyond what you see.
S: From Judah came Nahshon son of Amminadab, an ancestor of David and of Jesus. A name in a census list became a link in the chain of salvation. Remember that God works through ordinary lives toward His purpose, and your faithfulness may matter beyond what you see.

== 1:23
T: Fifty and nine thousand
R: 'Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Simeon, were fifty and nine thousand and three hundred.' Simeon numbered 59,300 men. Notice the size. Simeon was one of the larger tribes here. But compare Numbers 26, the second census about thirty-eight years later. Simeon had dropped to 22,200. A loss of over half.
