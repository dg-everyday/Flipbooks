# Numbers 2: The camp around the tabernacle

== 2:1
T: Order in the camp
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying.' God spoke to Moses and Aaron about how the tribes should camp and march. Notice that God cared about arrangement. After counting His people, He arranged them. A crowd became a camp. Disorder became order. God does not leave His people in confusion. Notice the design. Chapter 2 will place the tabernacle at the center, with tribes on every side. God at the middle, His people around Him. This is a picture of what life should look like. God at the center, everything else arranged around Him. Colossians 1 says, in all things he might have the preeminence. Matthew 6 says, seek ye first the kingdom of God. Today, ask whether God is truly at the center of your life. Let Him arrange everything else around His presence.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron about how the tribes should camp. A crowd became an ordered camp with God's presence at the center. Ask whether God is truly at the center of your life and let Him arrange everything else around Him.
P: God at the center. Numbers 2:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses and unto Aaron.' God arranged His people around His presence. Is He at the center of your life?
H: #DailyGrace #Numbers21 #Order #Priorities #Presence #BibleVerse

== 2:2
T: Far off about the tabernacle
R: 'Every man of the children of Israel shall pitch by his own standard, with the ensign of their father's house: far off about the tabernacle of the congregation shall they pitch.' Each Israelite was to camp under his tribe's banner, with the flag of his family, at a distance around the tabernacle. Notice far off about the tabernacle. The tribes camped some distance away, with the Levites between them and the tabernacle. God was near, yet holy. Reverence required space. Notice that every tent faced the tabernacle. Wherever an Israelite stepped out of his tent, God's dwelling was in view. The cloud above it was visible to all. Notice ensign of their father's house. Each family had its banner. Identity and belonging were clear. Hebrews 12 says, Looking unto Jesus the author and finisher of our faith. Today, keep your eyes on God's presence. Wherever you step out, let Him be in view.
S: Every Israelite pitched by his own standard and ensign, at a distance around the tabernacle. God's dwelling stood at the center, in view of every tent, near yet holy. Keep your eyes on God's presence and let Him be in view wherever you go.
P: Facing His presence. Numbers 2:2: 'far off about the tabernacle of the congregation shall they pitch.' Every tent faced God's dwelling. Look unto Jesus today.

On the east, toward the sunrise, the divisions of Judah were to camp under their banner, led by Nahshon. Notice the east, toward the rising of the sun. Judah camped at the entrance of the tabernacle, which faced east. The royal tribe guarded the door. Notice the sunrise. From Judah, the Messiah would come like the dawn. Notice that Judah faced the light every morning. Their position looked toward a new day. Lamentations 3 says His mercies are new every morning. Today, face the rising sun with hope. The Sun of righteousness has risen from Judah, with healing in His wings.
