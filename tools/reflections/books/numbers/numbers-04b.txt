# Numbers 4:25-49: Gershon, Merari and the numbering of the workers

== 4:25
T: The curtains
R: 'And they shall bear the curtains of the tabernacle, and the tabernacle of the congregation, his covering, and the covering of the badgers' skins that is above upon it, and the hanging for the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.' The Gershonites carried the tabernacle curtains, the tent, its coverings, the outer leather covering, and the entrance curtain. Notice the curtains. Soft, heavy fabrics. They formed the walls and roof of God's dwelling. Notice that the curtains gave shelter. They separated holy space from the outside world. They created a home for God's presence. Notice that Gershon carried the fabric of the dwelling. Psalm 27 says, in the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion: in the secret of his tabernacle shall he hide me. God's dwelling is a shelter. Isaiah 54 says, Enlarge the place of thy tent, and let them stretch forth the curtains of thine habitations. Today, find shelter in God's presence. And help make His house a welcoming place for others.
S: The Gershonites bore the curtains of the tabernacle, its coverings and the hanging for the door. The fabrics formed the walls and roof that sheltered God's presence. Find shelter in God's presence and help make His house a welcoming place for others.

== 4:49
T: Every one according to his service
R: 'According to the commandment of the LORD they were numbered by the hand of Moses, every one according to his service, and according to his burden: thus were they numbered of him, as the LORD commanded Moses.' At the LORD's command through Moses, each was assigned his work and told what to carry. They were counted as the LORD commanded. Notice every one according to his service. Each man had a specific role. Each knew his task. Notice according to his burden. Each knew what to carry. Notice as the LORD commanded Moses. The chapter ends with obedience, as so many do in Numbers. God's word was followed. Notice that every believer has a service and a burden. First Peter 4 says, As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another. Romans 12 says, Having then gifts differing according to the grace that is given to us. Today, know your service and carry your burden faithfully. God has a specific role for you.
