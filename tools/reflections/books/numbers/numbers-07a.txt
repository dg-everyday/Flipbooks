# Numbers 7:1-23: Wagons for the Levites and the first offerings of the princes

== 7:1
T: Fully set up
R: 'And it came to pass on the day that Moses had fully set up the tabernacle, and had anointed it, and sanctified it, and all the instruments thereof… and had anointed them, and sanctified them.' When Moses finished setting up the tabernacle, he anointed it and consecrated it with all its furnishings, the altar and its utensils. Notice fully set up. The work was complete. Exodus 40 describes this day, when the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle. Notice anointed… sanctified. Everything was set apart for God. Notice that this chapter steps back in time to the day of dedication. Numbers is not always in strict order. Scripture sometimes groups events by theme. Notice that God's dwelling was ready before the people gave their gifts. God prepared a place first; then His people responded. John 14 says, I go to prepare a place for you. Today, rejoice that God has prepared a place for His presence among His people. Respond to His readiness with your own gifts.
S: On the day Moses fully set up the tabernacle, he anointed and sanctified it with all its instruments and vessels. God's dwelling was complete and set apart before the people brought their gifts. Rejoice that God prepares a place for His presence and respond with your own gifts.
P: Fully set up. Numbers 7:1: 'on the day that Moses had fully set up the tabernacle.' God prepared His dwelling first. Respond to His readiness with your gifts today.

== 7:2
T: The princes offered
R: 'That the princes of Israel, heads of the house of their fathers, who were the princes of the tribes, and were over them that were numbered, offered.' Then the leaders of Israel, the heads of families who were tribal leaders in charge of those counted, made offerings. Notice the princes… offered. The leaders gave first. Before asking the people to give, the leaders brought their own gifts. Example matters. Notice over them that were numbered. These were the same men who helped with the census. Leadership in organization led to leadership in generosity. Notice that leaders set the tone for giving. First Chronicles 29 tells how David and his leaders gave willingly for the temple. Then the people rejoiced, for that they offered willingly. Leaders' generosity inspires the whole community. Second Corinthians 8 says the Macedonians first gave their own selves to the Lord. Today, if you lead, lead in generosity. Let your giving inspire others to give willingly.
S: The princes of Israel, heads of their fathers' houses and leaders over the numbered, brought offerings. The leaders gave first, setting the tone for the whole community's generosity. If you lead, lead in generosity and let your giving inspire others.
P: Leaders give first. Numbers 7:2: 'the princes of Israel… offered.' The leaders set the tone. Lead in generosity, and inspire others to give willingly.

== 7:3
T: Six covered wagons
R: 'And they brought their offering before the LORD, six covered wagons, and twelve oxen; a wagon for two of the princes, and for each one an ox: and they brought them before the tabernacle.' They brought six covered carts and twelve oxen, one ox from each leader and a cart from every two. They presented these before the tabernacle. Notice six covered wagons. A practical gift. The leaders saw a need. The Levites would carry heavy loads. Wagons would help. Notice a wagon for two of the princes. Shared giving. Two leaders together provided each wagon. Cooperation made the gift possible. Notice that practical gifts are holy too. Not everything given to God is spiritual in appearance. Wagons and oxen served God's work. Philippians 4 says Paul received gifts from Philippi as an odour of a sweet smell, a sacrifice acceptable, wellpleasing to God. Practical support pleases God. Today, look for practical needs in God's work. Your practical gifts, given together with others, serve His purposes.
S: The princes brought six covered wagons and twelve oxen, a wagon for every two princes and an ox each. The practical gift met the Levites' need, made possible by shared giving. Look for practical needs in God's work, for practical gifts given together serve His purposes.
P: Practical gifts. Numbers 7:3: 'six covered wagons, and twelve oxen.' Wagons and oxen served God's work. Practical support pleases God. Meet a need today.

== 7:4
T: The LORD spake
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' The LORD spoke to Moses about the leaders' gift. Notice that God responded to the gift. Moses did not decide alone what to do with the wagons. He waited for God's direction. Notice that the princes' initiative was not commanded. They gave on their own. Then God confirmed it. Spontaneous generosity was welcomed by God. Notice that God guides how gifts are used. Even voluntary offerings need God's direction. Proverbs 16 says, A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps. The princes devised a gift; God directed its use. Notice the partnership. Human initiative, divine direction. James 4 says, ye ought to say, If the Lord will. Today, offer your gifts and ideas to God. Then let Him direct how they are used.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses about the princes' voluntary gift of wagons and oxen. God welcomed their spontaneous generosity and then directed its use. Offer your gifts and ideas to God, then let Him direct how they are used.
P: Initiative and direction. Numbers 7:4: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' The princes gave; God directed. Offer your gifts, and let Him guide their use.

== 7:5
T: According to his service
R: 'Take it of them, that they may be to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation; and thou shalt give them unto the Levites, to every man according to his service.' God told Moses to accept the gifts for use in the tabernacle work and to give them to the Levites as each one's work required. Notice take it of them. God accepted the gift. He was pleased with their generosity. Notice according to his service. The wagons were distributed by need. Not equally, but according to each group's work. Fairness meant meeting needs, not dividing evenly. Notice that God's provision is fitted to our tasks. He gives what each calling requires. Romans 12 says gifts differ according to the grace that is given to us. Second Corinthians 9 says God makes all grace abound… that ye… may abound to every good work. Today, trust that God gives you what your calling needs. He provides according to your service.
S: God told Moses to take the wagons for the tabernacle's service and give them to the Levites, each according to his service. The gifts were distributed by need rather than equally. Trust that God gives you what your calling needs, providing according to your service.

== 7:6
T: Moses took and gave
R: 'And Moses took the wagons and the oxen, and gave them unto the Levites.' So Moses took the carts and oxen and gave them to the Levites. Notice took… and gave. Moses received the gift and passed it on. He did not keep it for himself. Leaders are channels, not reservoirs. Notice the simplicity. God said do it; Moses did it. No delay, no debate. Obedience was immediate. Notice that gifts flow through leaders to those who need them. Acts 4 shows the early church laying gifts at the apostles' feet, and distribution was made unto every man according as he had need. Leaders received and distributed faithfully. Second Corinthians 8 says Paul handled gifts carefully, providing for honest things. Today, if you receive gifts for God's work, pass them on faithfully. Be a channel of blessing, not a reservoir.
S: Moses took the wagons and the oxen and gave them to the Levites. He passed the gift on immediately, a channel rather than a reservoir. If you receive gifts for God's work, pass them on faithfully and be a channel of blessing.

Daily bread, enough for the task. Matthew 6 says, Give us this day our daily bread. Notice that Gershon did not complain about having fewer wagons than Merari. Each received what fit. Today, be content with what God provides for your task. It is exactly what you need.
S: Moses gave two wagons and four oxen to the sons of Gershon according to their service. Their measured provision fit their task of carrying curtains, neither too much nor too little. Be content with what God provides for your task, for it is what you need.

Merari carried the heaviest loads: boards, bars, pillars, sockets. They needed more help. Notice that God gives more help where burdens are heavier. Merari did not have to carry everything on their backs. God provided wagons. Notice under the hand of Ithamar. Leadership supervised the work. Provision came with oversight. First Corinthians 10 says God will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able. He does not give burdens without help. Psalm 68 says, Blessed be the Lord, who daily loadeth us with benefits. == 7:9
T: Upon their shoulders
R: 'But unto the sons of Kohath he gave none: because the service of the sanctuary belonging unto them was that they should bear upon their shoulders.' But he gave no carts to the Kohathites, because they were to carry the holy things on their shoulders. Notice he gave none. Kohath received no wagons. Not because they were less important, but because their task was too holy for carts. Notice bear upon their shoulders. The most sacred objects were carried personally. Close to the body. Not delegated to machines or animals. Notice Second Samuel 6. David later put the ark on a new cart. When the oxen stumbled, Uzzah touched it and died. Later David said, None ought to carry the ark of God but the Levites. Some things must be carried God's way, even if harder. Notice that holy things require personal care. Some ministry cannot be automated. Today, carry the holy things in your life personally. Some responsibilities must be borne on your own shoulders.
S: Moses gave no wagons to the sons of Kohath, for they were to bear the holy things upon their shoulders. The most sacred objects were carried personally, as David later learned when the ark was put on a cart. Carry the holy things in your life personally, for some responsibilities must be on your own shoulders.
P: On their shoulders. Numbers 7:9: 'they should bear upon their shoulders.' The holiest things weren't put on carts. Carry what's holy personally, God's way.
