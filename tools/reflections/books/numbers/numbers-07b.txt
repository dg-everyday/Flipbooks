== 7:47
T: Eliasaph's offering
R: 'And for a sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five he goats, five lambs of the first year: this was the offering of Eliasaph the son of Deuel.' For the peace offering he brought two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five year-old lambs. This was Eliasaph's offering. Notice that the southern camp was now complete. Reuben, Simeon, and Gad had all given. Two camps finished. Half the nation had dedicated the altar. Notice that God's work progresses step by step. Each day added to the whole. Patient faithfulness built something complete. Notice this was the offering of Eliasaph. Another name in God's record. Revelation 20 speaks of books opened before God. Our deeds are recorded. Luke 10 says, rejoice, because your names are written in heaven. Today, take one faithful step at a time. God sees each one and records it. Step by step, His work is completed.
S: Eliasaph's peace offerings were two oxen, five rams, five he goats and five lambs, completing the southern camp. Step by step, half the nation had dedicated the altar, each name recorded. Take one faithful step at a time, for God records each one and completes His work.
P: Step by step. Numbers 7:47: 'this was the offering of Eliasaph the son of Deuel.' Half the nation had given. God records each faithful step. Keep going today.
