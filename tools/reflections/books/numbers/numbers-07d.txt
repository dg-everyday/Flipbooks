# Numbers 7:72-89: Asher, Naphtali, the totals and the voice from the mercy seat

== 7:72
T: Asher on the eleventh day
R: 'On the eleventh day Pagiel the son of Ocran, prince of the children of Asher, offered.' On the eleventh day, Pagiel son of Ocran, leader of Asher, brought his offering. Notice the eleventh day. Near the end. Only one day remained after this. Endurance was almost complete. Notice Asher. The tribe whose name means happy or blessed. A happy tribe came to worship. Joy and worship belong together. Notice Pagiel. His name may mean met by God or God's intervention. Notice that Asher's day came late. Some wait longer than others for their turn. Yet every turn comes. Notice Matthew 20. The workers hired at the eleventh hour received the same wage as those who started early. God's generosity does not depend on when we come. Today, if you feel your turn has come late, take heart. God receives the eleventh-hour worker with the same grace.
S: On the eleventh day Pagiel son of Ocran, prince of Asher, offered. The happy tribe came late in the order, yet its turn came, as eleventh-hour workers receive the same grace. If your turn feels late, take heart, for God receives every worker with the same grace.
P: The eleventh hour. Numbers 7:72: 'On the eleventh day Pagiel the son of Ocran.' Asher came late, yet was received fully. God's grace is the same for latecomers.

== 7:73
T: Asher's silver
R: 'His offering was one silver charger, the weight whereof was an hundred and thirty shekels, one silver bowl of seventy shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary; both of them full of fine flour mingled with oil for a meat offering.' His offering was the same silver plate and bowl filled with fine flour and oil. Notice oil. Deuteronomy 33 says of Asher, let him dip his foot in oil. Asher's land would be rich in olive groves. Notice that blessing should return to God. Asher's abundant oil would come from God's land. Here they gave oil back to Him. Notice that what we have in abundance is meant to be shared with God. Proverbs 3 says, Honour the LORD with thy substance, and with the firstfruits of all thine increase. Luke 12 warns of the rich fool who stored up for himself and was not rich toward God. Today, give back from your abundance. Let what you have most of be offered to God.
S: Pagiel offered the same silver charger and bowl full of fine flour mingled with oil. Asher, later rich in olive oil, offered oil back to God from His blessing. Give back to God from your abundance, honoring Him with what you have most of.
P: From your abundance. Numbers 7:73: 'full of fine flour mingled with oil.' Asher would dip his foot in oil and gave oil back to God. Give from your abundance.

Anna prayed for decades. Then she saw the answer. Luke 18 says, men ought always to pray, and not to faint. Today, keep praying, like Anna. Your persistent prayers may lead you to see God's redemption with your own eyes.
S: Pagiel brought a golden spoon of ten shekels full of incense. Centuries later Anna of Asher prayed night and day in the temple and saw the infant Jesus. Keep praying like Anna, for persistent prayer may lead you to see God's redemption.

Notice that true happiness comes through forgiveness. Psalm 32 says, Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered. The deepest blessing is forgiveness. Notice that Asher's name means blessed or happy. Psalm 32's word blessed is related. The happiest person is the forgiven person. Notice Romans 4. Paul quotes this psalm: Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven. Justification brings blessedness. Today, find your deepest happiness in God's forgiveness. You are blessed because your sin is covered.
S: Pagiel brought one kid of the goats for a sin offering. The tribe named blessed also needed atonement, for the deepest blessedness is forgiveness. Find your deepest happiness in God's forgiveness, for you are blessed when your sin is covered.
P: Truly blessed. Numbers 7:76: 'One kid of the goats for a sin offering.' Blessed is he whose sin is covered. The forgiven are the truly happy. Rejoice today.

== 7:78
T: Naphtali on the twelfth day
R: 'On the twelfth day Ahira the son of Enan, prince of the children of Naphtali, offered.' On the twelfth day, Ahira son of Enan, leader of Naphtali, brought his offering. Notice the twelfth day. The last. Every tribe had now given. The circle was complete. Notice Naphtali last. The final tribe was just as honored as the first. Last did not mean least. Notice Ahira. His name may mean brother of evil or my brother is a friend; scholars differ. Whatever its meaning, Ahira stood before God like every other prince. Notice that completion is a blessing. Twelve days, twelve tribes, twelve offerings. Nothing missing. Philippians 1 says, he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it. God completes what He begins. Today, rejoice in God's completing work. Every tribe had its turn, and God finished what He started.
S: On the twelfth day Ahira son of Enan, prince of Naphtali, offered, completing the twelve days. The last tribe was as honored as the first, and the circle of worship was complete. Rejoice in God's completing work, for He finishes what He starts.
P: Complete. Numbers 7:78: 'On the twelfth day Ahira the son of Enan.' Twelve days, twelve tribes, nothing missing. God completes the good work He begins.

== 7:80

Hebrews 5 says Jesus offered up prayers and supplications with strong crying and tears. Today, pray as Jesus prayed. Let your prayers rise to God, as His did on Naphtali's hills.

== 7:85
T: Two thousand four hundred shekels
R: 'Each charger of silver weighing an hundred and thirty shekels, each bowl seventy: all the silver vessels weighed two thousand and four hundred shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary.' Each silver plate weighed 130 shekels and each bowl 70. Altogether the silver dishes weighed 2,400 shekels by the sanctuary standard. Notice the total. Two thousand four hundred shekels of silver. A great sum. The nation gave generously. Notice the math. 200 shekels per prince times 12 equals 2,400. Scripture is precise. God keeps careful account. Notice that collective generosity achieves what individuals cannot. No single prince gave 2,400 shekels. Together they did. Notice Second Corinthians 9. He which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully. Exodus 36 shows the people bringing much more than enough. Generous communities flourish. Today, join others in generous giving. Together, God's people accomplish more than any one person could.
S: Each silver charger weighed one hundred thirty shekels and each bowl seventy, totaling two thousand four hundred shekels. Together the princes gave far more than any one could alone. Join others in generous giving, for together God's people accomplish more than any one person.
P: Together, generous. Numbers 7:85: 'all the silver vessels weighed two thousand and four hundred shekels.' No one gave it all, but together they did. Give generously with others.

== 7:89

When Moses entered the tabernacle to speak with the LORD, he heard the voice speaking to him from between the two cherubim above the mercy seat on the ark. And the LORD spoke to him. Notice the climax. After all the gifts and sacrifices, God spoke. The goal of everything was communion with God. Notice from off the mercy seat. God's voice came from the place of mercy. Notice to speak with him. Exodus 33 says, the LORD spake unto Moses face to face, as a man speaketh unto his friend. Notice Hebrews 1. God… Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son. Today, rejoice that God speaks from His mercy seat. Through Christ, you can hear His voice and speak with Him.
S: When Moses went into the tabernacle to speak with God, he heard His voice from the mercy seat between the cherubims. After all the gifts, the goal was communion, and God spoke from the place of mercy. Rejoice that God speaks to you through Christ and draw near to hear His voice.
P: He spoke from mercy. Numbers 7:89: 'he heard the voice of one speaking unto him from off the mercy seat.' After all the gifts, God spoke. Through Christ, hear His voice today.
