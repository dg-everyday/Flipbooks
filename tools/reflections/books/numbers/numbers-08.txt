# Numbers 8: The lamps and the consecration of the Levites

== 8:1
T: After the voice
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' After God spoke from the mercy seat, He gave Moses instructions about the lamps and the Levites. Notice the sequence. Chapter 7 ended with God's voice from the mercy seat. Chapter 8 begins with what that voice said. Communion led to instruction. Notice that time with God leads to direction. When we meet God, He guides us. Prayer and guidance go together. Notice that the instructions concerned light and service. The lamps would shine; the Levites would serve. God's word led to illumination and action. Psalm 119 says, The entrance of thy words giveth light. James 1 says, be ye doers of the word. Today, spend time in God's presence. Then listen for His direction, and act on what He shows you.
S: After speaking from the mercy seat, the LORD gave Moses instructions about the lamps and the Levites. Communion with God led to light and to service. Spend time in God's presence, listen for His direction, and act on what He shows you.
P: From communion to direction. Numbers 8:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' Meeting God leads to guidance. The entrance of His words gives light.

== 8:2
T: The seven lamps
R: 'Speak unto Aaron, and say unto him, When thou lightest the lamps, the seven lamps shall give light over against the candlestick.' God told Moses to instruct Aaron that when he set up the seven lamps, they should light the area in front of the lampstand. Notice the seven lamps. A complete light. Seven signifies fullness. The holy place was fully lit. Notice over against the candlestick. The light shone forward. It illuminated the table of showbread opposite it. The light was meant to reveal, not to be admired for itself. Notice that light serves others. John the Baptist was a burning and a shining light in John 5, yet he pointed to Christ. Matthew 5 says, Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father. Our light should direct attention to God. Revelation 1 shows seven golden candlesticks representing the churches. Today, let your light shine forward, illuminating others and pointing them to God, not to yourself.
S: Aaron was to light the seven lamps so that they gave light in front of the candlestick. The full light shone forward to illuminate the holy place rather than itself. Let your light shine to illuminate others and point them to God, not to yourself.
P: Light that points forward. Numbers 8:2: 'the seven lamps shall give light over against the candlestick.' Let your light shine so others glorify your Father.

Many understand that twenty-five marked the start of a training period. Five years of learning before full responsibility at thirty. Apprenticeship. Notice that service begins with learning. The young Levites watched and helped before carrying the heaviest loads. Notice the value of mentoring. Experienced Levites trained the younger. Knowledge passed down. Second Timothy 2 says, the things that thou hast heard of me… the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also. Titus 2 says, the aged women… teach the young women. Today, be willing to learn before leading. And if you are experienced, train someone younger.
S: Levites from twenty-five years old went in to wait on the tabernacle service, likely a training period before full duty at thirty. Service began with learning, and experienced Levites mentored the younger. Be willing to learn before leading, and if you are experienced, train someone younger.
P: Learn, then lead. Numbers 8:24: 'from twenty and five years old and upward they shall go in.' Training before full duty. Commit what you know to faithful others.

== 8:25
T: At fifty, cease
R: 'And from the age of fifty years they shall cease waiting upon the service thereof, and shall serve no more.' At age fifty, they were to retire from their regular service and work no longer. Notice cease. The heavy work ended at fifty. Carrying boards and curtains was for younger bodies. God provided rest. Notice that God cares for our bodies. He did not demand heavy labor forever. He set limits. Notice that rest after labor is God's gift. Hebrews 4 says, There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God. Rest is part of His design. Notice that this was not the end of usefulness, as the next verse shows. It was a change of role. Psalm 92 says, They shall still bring forth fruit in old age. Today, accept the seasons of rest God gives. Changes in your capacity are not the end of your usefulness.
S: At fifty the Levites ceased their regular heavy service. God cared for their bodies and provided rest, though not an end of usefulness. Accept the seasons of rest God gives, for changes in capacity are not the end of your usefulness.
P: Rest is a gift. Numbers 8:25: 'from the age of fifty years they shall cease.' God provides rest for His servants. They still bring forth fruit in old age.

== 8:26
T: Minister with their brethren
R: 'But shall minister with their brethren in the tabernacle of the congregation, to keep the charge, and shall do no service. Thus shalt thou do unto the Levites touching their charge.' Older Levites could assist their brothers in performing their duties at the tabernacle, but they were not to do the heavy work. This was how Moses was to assign the Levites' responsibilities. Notice minister with their brethren. The older Levites stayed involved. They helped, guided, and supported. Their experience was valuable. Notice keep the charge. They guarded and oversaw. Wisdom replaced heavy lifting. Notice that older believers have a vital role. Titus 2 calls aged men and women to teach and model. Job 12 says, With the ancient is wisdom. Psalm 71 says, O God, forsake me not; until I have shewed thy strength unto this generation. Today, if you are older, keep ministering with your brethren. Share your wisdom. And if you are younger, honor and learn from those who have gone before.
S: Older Levites ceased heavy work but ministered with their brethren, keeping the charge. Their experience and oversight remained valuable to the younger servants. If you are older, keep ministering and share your wisdom, and if younger, learn from those before you.
P: Wisdom continues. Numbers 8:26: 'But shall minister with their brethren… to keep the charge.' Older servants still guide and guard. Share your wisdom today.
