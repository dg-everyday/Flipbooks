# Numbers 9: Passover in the wilderness and the guiding cloud

== 9:1
T: The first month
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, saying.' In the first month of the second year after leaving Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses in the Sinai desert. Notice the first month of the second year. One year since the exodus. An anniversary. God called them to remember. Notice that this is earlier than Numbers 1, which began in the second month. Scripture arranges events by theme, not always by date. Notice that anniversaries of deliverance should be remembered. God marked time by His saving acts. Exodus 12 says, This month shall be unto you the beginning of months. Their calendar began with redemption. Notice Psalm 103. forget not all his benefits. Remembering God's past salvation strengthens present faith. Today, remember the anniversaries of God's work in your life. Mark the day He saved you, and give thanks.
S: In the first month of the second year after the exodus, the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai. It was the anniversary of their deliverance, a time to remember God's saving act. Remember the anniversaries of God's work in your life and give thanks for His benefits.
P: Remember the anniversary. Numbers 9:1: 'in the first month of the second year after they were come out of… Egypt.' Forget not all His benefits. Give thanks today.

== 9:2
T: Keep the passover
R: 'Let the children of Israel also keep the passover at his appointed season.' The Israelites were to celebrate the Passover at its appointed time. Notice keep the passover. Even in the wilderness, they remembered. The journey did not cancel worship. Remembrance traveled with them. Busy seasons tempt us to postpone worship until life settles. Israel did not wait for the promised land to remember the Lord. Notice at his appointed season. God set the time. Not whenever convenient, but at the appointed season. Faithfulness to God's calendar. Notice that the Passover recalled their salvation. The blood on the doorposts. The death of Egypt's firstborn. The night of deliverance. Notice Luke 22. Jesus celebrated Passover with His disciples and said, This do in remembrance of me. The Lord's Supper fulfills the Passover. Today, keep the Lord's Supper faithfully. Remember Christ, our Passover Lamb, who was sacrificed for you.
S: The children of Israel were to keep the passover at its appointed season, even in the wilderness. The journey did not cancel remembrance of their salvation, fulfilled in the Lord's Supper. Keep the Lord's Supper faithfully and remember Christ, our Passover Lamb.

== 9:3

Today, honor the forms of worship that tell the gospel story. Let them teach you and your children.
S: The passover was to be kept at even on the fourteenth day, according to all its rites and ceremonies. The full ritual told the story of salvation for every generation. Honor the forms of worship that tell the gospel and let them teach you and your children.

At the LORD's command they camped, and at His command they set out. They obeyed the LORD's order, in accordance with His command through Moses. Notice the threefold repetition: at the commandment of the LORD. Rest, journey, charge. Israel's whole life was ordered by Him. Notice they kept the charge. Faithful obedience. Notice that this verse summarizes a life led by God. Rest when He says rest. Move when He says move. Keep His charge always. Romans 8 says, as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God. Galatians 5 says, If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit. Today, let God direct your whole life. Rest at His command, move at His command, and keep His charge faithfully.
