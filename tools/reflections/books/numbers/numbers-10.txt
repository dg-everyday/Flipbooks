# Numbers 10: The silver trumpets and the departure from Sinai

== 10:1
T: Signals for the journey
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' God spoke to Moses about making trumpets to signal the people. Notice that God prepared communication. A camp of millions needed clear signals. God provided a way to gather and move them. Without clear signals, some would leave too early and others would be left behind. Order protected every family. Notice that guidance includes communication. The cloud showed when to move; the trumpets told everyone. God's leading was both seen and heard. Notice that God's people need clear signals. Confusion leads to disorder. First Corinthians 14 says, if the trumpet give an uncertain sound, who shall prepare himself to the battle? Clear communication matters in God's work. Notice that God cares about practical details. Trumpets were tools. God designed them. Today, listen for God's clear signals in your life. And communicate clearly with those you lead or serve.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses about making trumpets to signal the camp. God provided clear communication so millions could gather and move together. Listen for God's clear signals and communicate clearly with those you lead or serve.
P: Clear signals. Numbers 10:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' A camp of millions needed clear sounds. If the trumpet is uncertain, who will prepare?

== 10:2
T: Two trumpets of silver
R: 'Make thee two trumpets of silver; of a whole piece shalt thou make them: that thou mayest use them for the calling of the assembly, and for the journeying of the camps.' Moses was to make two trumpets of hammered silver and use them to call the community together and to signal when the camps should set out. Notice silver. The metal of redemption. The tabernacle's sockets were made from redemption silver. The trumpets that called God's people were silver too. Notice of a whole piece. Hammered from a single piece. Like the lampstand. Unity in their making. Notice two purposes. Calling the assembly and moving the camps. Gathering and going. Both are part of the life of God's people. Notice that the gospel is a trumpet call. Isaiah 58 says, lift up thy voice like a trumpet. Romans 10 says, how shall they hear without a preacher? Today, hear God's call to gather and to go. Answer the silver trumpet of His word.
S: Moses was to make two trumpets of silver, each from a whole piece, for calling the assembly and moving the camps. The silver trumpets called God's redeemed people to gather and to go. Hear God's call to gather and to go, and answer the trumpet of His word.
P: Gather and go. Numbers 10:2: 'Make thee two trumpets of silver… for the calling of the assembly, and for the journeying.' Answer God's call to gather and to go.
