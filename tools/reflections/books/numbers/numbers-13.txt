# Numbers 13: The twelve spies search the land

== 13:1
T: Send men
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' The LORD spoke to Moses about sending men to explore Canaan. Notice the setting. Israel was at the edge of the promised land. After Sinai, after the journey, after the trials, they had arrived. Notice Deuteronomy 1. There Moses recalls that the people asked for spies first, and the idea pleased him. God then commanded it. Human request and divine permission came together. Notice that this moment was decisive. What happened next would shape a generation. Faith or fear would decide their future. Notice that God led them to the threshold. He brought them right to the edge of His promise. Joshua 1 says, Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage. Today, if you stand at the edge of something God has promised, prepare your heart. Choose faith over fear at the threshold.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses about sending men to search Canaan, as Israel stood at the edge of the promised land. This decisive moment would show whether faith or fear would shape a generation. If you stand at the edge of God's promise, prepare your heart and choose faith over fear.
P: At the threshold. Numbers 13:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' Israel stood at the edge of promise. Choose faith over fear at your threshold today.
H: #DailyGrace #Numbers131 #Faith #Promise #Threshold #BibleVerse

== 13:2
T: Which I give
R: 'Send thou men, that they may search the land of Canaan, which I give unto the children of Israel: of every tribe of their fathers shall ye send a man, every one a ruler among them.' Send some men to explore the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites. From each ancestral tribe send one of its leaders. Notice which I give. God had already promised the land. The spies were not deciding whether to take it. They were seeing what God was giving. Notice every one a ruler. Leaders were chosen. Their report would carry weight. Leadership influences many. Notice that the outcome was already guaranteed by God's word. The question was not whether God would give it, but whether Israel would believe. Notice Hebrews 4. the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it. Today, remember that God's promises are already given. Your task is to believe and receive, not to decide whether God can deliver.
S: God told Moses to send a ruler from every tribe to search the land of Canaan, which He was giving to Israel. The land was already promised, and the question was whether Israel would believe. Remember that God's promises are already given and your task is to believe and receive them.
P: Already given. Numbers 13:2: 'the land of Canaan, which I give unto the children of Israel.' God's promise was settled. The question is faith. Believe and receive today.

Your words influence many. And follow leaders whose faith is worth imitating.
S: Moses sent the spies from Paran at the LORD's command, all of them heads of the children of Israel. Respected leaders carried great influence, yet ten would bring a faithless report. If you lead, lead with faith, and follow leaders whose faith is worth imitating.
