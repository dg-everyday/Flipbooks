# Numbers 14:24-45: Caleb's different spirit, forty years, and the presumptuous attack

== 14:24
T: Another spirit
R: 'But my servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him, and hath followed me fully, him will I bring into the land whereinto he went; and his seed shall possess it.' But because my servant Caleb has a different spirit and follows me wholeheartedly, I will bring him into the land he went to, and his descendants will inherit it. Notice my servant Caleb. God honored Caleb by name. In a chapter of judgment, one man stood out. Notice another spirit. Caleb was different. His heart was not like the crowd's. Faith set him apart. Notice followed me fully. Wholehearted. Not partial, not hesitant. Caleb's devotion was complete. Notice his seed shall possess it. Caleb's faith blessed his children. Joshua 14 shows Caleb receiving Hebron at eighty-five. Notice Deuteronomy 1. he hath wholly followed the LORD. Today, follow God fully, with a different spirit from the fearful crowd. Wholehearted faith blesses you and your descendants.
S: God said His servant Caleb had another spirit and followed Him fully, so He would bring him into the land and his seed would possess it. Caleb's wholehearted faith set him apart from the crowd and blessed his children. Follow God fully with a different spirit, for wholehearted faith blesses you and your descendants.
P: Followed fully. Numbers 14:24: 'my servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him, and hath followed me fully.' Be different from the fearful crowd. Follow God fully.

== 14:25
T: Turn you
R: '(Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites dwelt in the valley.) To morrow turn you, and get you into the wilderness by the way of the Red sea.' Since the Amalekites and the Canaanites are living in the valleys, turn back tomorrow and set out toward the desert along the route to the Red Sea. Notice to morrow turn you. Back toward the wilderness. Away from the promised land. A painful reversal. Notice by the way of the Red sea. Back the way they came. The road to victory became the road to wandering. Notice that unbelief turns us backward. They were at the edge of promise. Now they turned away. Notice Hebrews 3. So we see that they could not enter in because of unbelief. They had the opportunity but refused it. Notice Second Corinthians 6. now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation. Opportunities can pass. Today, do not let unbelief turn you away from God's promises. When He says go, go in faith.
S: God told Israel to turn tomorrow and go into the wilderness by the way of the Red sea. At the edge of promise, unbelief turned them back toward wandering. Do not let unbelief turn you away from God's promises, but go in faith when He says go.
P: Turned back. Numbers 14:25: 'To morrow turn you, and get you into the wilderness.' At the edge of promise, unbelief turned them away. Now is the accepted time.

They would deliver the hard news together. Notice that God spoke a second time. After pardon came sentence. God's response had two parts. Notice that leaders must deliver hard truths. Moses and Aaron would tell a nation it would die in the wilderness. Not pleasant, but necessary. Notice Ezekiel 3. if thou give him not warning… his blood will I require at thine hand. Faithful leaders speak hard truths. Second Timothy 4 says, Preach the word; be instant in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort. Today, if you must deliver hard truths, do it faithfully and with love. And receive hard truths from God with humility.
S: The LORD spoke again to Moses and Aaron about the people's sentence. After pardon came consequence, and the leaders had to deliver hard news together. Deliver hard truths faithfully and with love, and receive them from God with humility.

They murmured against Moses, but God said it was against Him. Complaining about God's leaders and plan is complaining against God. Notice I have heard. Every complaint reached God. Nothing was hidden. Notice how long shall I bear? God's patience was real but not infinite. There comes a point of consequence. Notice First Corinthians 10. Neither murmur ye, as some of them also murmured, and were destroyed of the destroyer. Philippians 2 says, Do all things without murmurings. Today, stop complaining against God's providence. He hears every murmur. Replace complaint with trust.

Notice that faith-filled words shape a different future. Caleb said, we are well able. He entered. Today, speak words of faith, not fear. Your words reveal your heart and shape your path.
S: God declared that as the people had spoken in His ears, so He would do to them. Their own words of wishing to die in the wilderness became their sentence. Speak words of faith rather than fear, for your words reveal your heart and shape your path.
P: As ye have spoken. Numbers 14:28: 'as ye have spoken in mine ears, so will I do to you.' Their words became their sentence. Speak faith, not fear, today.

The census of chapter 1 became a list of the doomed. Those counted for battle would never fight in Canaan. Notice from twenty years old and upward. The adults who chose fear. They bore responsibility. Notice that the same census that counted their strength now counted their judgment. Privilege without faith leads to loss. Notice First Corinthians 10. But with many of them God was not well pleased: for they were overthrown in the wilderness. Today, do not waste the privileges God gives you. Being counted among His people means little without faith.
S: God said the carcases of all those numbered from twenty years and upward who murmured would fall in the wilderness. The census that counted their strength now counted their judgment. Do not waste the privileges God gives you, for being counted among His people means little without faith.

Out of hundreds of thousands, two would enter. Faith made the difference. Notice that God remembers the faithful. In judgment, He distinguished the two. They were not swept away with the crowd. Notice that faithfulness is never wasted. Caleb and Joshua waited forty years in the wilderness with the unbelieving generation. But they entered. Notice Second Timothy 2. The Lord knoweth them that are his. Malachi 3 says, they shall be mine, saith the LORD of hosts, in that day when I make up my jewels. Today, remain faithful even when you are in the minority. Notice that personal responsibility cannot be transferred. Parents could not pass their faith or unbelief to their children. Each generation chose. Ezekiel 18 says, The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son. Notice Hebrews 3. take heed, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief. Each person answers for his own heart. Today, take responsibility for your own faith. Do not let unbelief cause you to fall short of God's promise.
S: God said, But as for you, your carcases shall fall in this wilderness. Children would enter while parents would not, for each generation answered for its own faith. Take responsibility for your own faith so that unbelief does not cause you to fall short.

Your children will be shepherds here for forty years, suffering for your unfaithfulness, until the last of your bodies lies in the wilderness. Notice your children shall wander. The parents' unbelief cost their children forty years. Families feel the consequences of choices. Notice bear your whoredoms. Unfaithfulness to God is described as adultery. Spiritual betrayal. Notice that even in this, God was preparing the children. Forty years in the wilderness shaped a new generation of faith. Deuteronomy 8 says God led them forty years to humble thee, and to prove thee. Today, remember that your choices affect others. And trust God to use even hard seasons to prepare you for His promises.

Forty days of spying became forty years of wandering. Every day of unbelief cost a year. Notice ye shall know my breach of promise. Or, my opposition. They would experience God against them. Not because He broke His word, but because they broke faith. Notice that unbelief turned a blessing into a sentence. The days that could have led to entry led to exile. Notice Hebrews 3. Wherefore I was grieved with that generation… They shall not enter into my rest. Today, value the opportunities God gives. Respond with faith, so that days of testing become days of blessing.
S: For each of the forty days they searched the land, Israel would bear their iniquities a year, forty years, and know God's opposition. Days that could have led to entry became years of exile through unbelief. Value the opportunities God gives and respond with faith.
P: A day for a year. Numbers 14:34: 'each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years.' Forty days of doubt cost forty years. Respond to God with faith.
