== 16:49
T: Fourteen thousand seven hundred
R: 'Now they that died in the plague were fourteen thousand and seven hundred, beside them that died about the matter of Korah.' But 14,700 people died from the plague, in addition to those who had died because of Korah. Notice fourteen thousand and seven hundred. A tragic number. Thousands died because of murmuring. Notice beside them that died about the matter of Korah. More deaths beyond the rebels. The cost of rebellion spread. Notice that sin's cost is high. One man's rebellion led to thousands of deaths. Pride and complaint spread destruction. Notice that it could have been worse. Without Aaron's intercession, the whole nation might have perished. Mercy limited the judgment. Notice Lamentations 3. It is of the LORD'S mercies that we are not consumed. Today, take seriously the cost of rebellion and complaint. And thank God that His mercy limits judgment.

== 16:50
T: The plague was stayed
R: 'And Aaron returned unto Moses unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation: and the plague was stayed.' Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance to the tabernacle, for the plague had stopped. Notice Aaron returned unto Moses. After saving the people, Aaron went back. The two brothers stood together at the tabernacle. Notice the plague was stayed. Repeated. Emphasized. The danger was over. Life won. Notice that the chapter began with rebellion against Moses and Aaron. It ended with Aaron saving the rebels. Grace triumphed. Notice that God vindicated Aaron not only by judgment on Korah but by Aaron's saving work. His priesthood was proven by life, not just death. Notice Romans 5. where sin abounded, grace did much more abound. Today, rejoice that grace triumphs over rebellion. Christ, our priest, has stopped the plague of sin for all who come to Him.
S: Aaron returned to Moses at the tabernacle door, and the plague was stayed. The chapter that began with rebellion against Aaron ended with Aaron saving the rebels, his priesthood proven by life. Rejoice that grace triumphs over rebellion, for Christ has stayed the plague of sin for all who come.
