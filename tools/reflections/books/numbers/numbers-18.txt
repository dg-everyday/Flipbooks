R: 'But the Levites shall do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation, and they shall bear their iniquity: it shall be a statute for ever throughout your generations, that among the children of Israel they have no inheritance.' It is the Levites who are to do the work at the tent of meeting and bear the responsibility for any offenses they commit against it. This is a lasting ordinance for the generations to come. They will receive no inheritance among the Israelites. Notice they shall bear their iniquity. The Levites took on responsibility. If something went wrong, they bore it. Notice no inheritance. The Levites gave up land to serve God and protect the people. Notice that servants of God often give up much. Notice Mark 10. Peter said, we have left all, and have followed thee. Jesus promised an hundredfold now… and in the world to come eternal life. Today, honor those who sacrifice to serve God. And trust that what you give up for Him is repaid many times over.
S: The Levites were to do the tabernacle service and bear the iniquity, receiving no inheritance among Israel. They gave up land and carried responsibility to protect the people. Honor those who sacrifice to serve God and trust that what you give up for Him is repaid many times over.
P: Ministry costs. Numbers 18:23: 'they shall bear their iniquity… they have no inheritance.' The Levites gave up much. Leave all for Christ, and receive an hundredfold.

== 18:24
T: The tithes to inherit
R: 'But the tithes of the children of Israel, which they offer as an heave offering unto the LORD, I have given to the Levites to inherit: therefore I have said unto them, Among the children of Israel they shall have no inheritance.' Instead, I give to the Levites as their inheritance the tithes that the Israelites present as an offering to the LORD. That is why I said concerning them: They will have no inheritance among the Israelites. Notice unto the LORD. The tithe was offered to God. Giving to God's servants is giving to God. Notice to inherit. The tithe was their inheritance. Notice that God's economy works through faithful giving. The people gave to God; God gave to the Levites; the Levites served the people. A cycle of blessing. Notice Second Corinthians 9. he which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully. Today, see your giving as an offering to God. It flows through His servants back to bless His people.
S: The tithes offered to the LORD were given to the Levites as their inheritance instead of land. Giving to God's servants was giving to God, creating a cycle of blessing for His people. See your giving as an offering to God that flows through His servants to bless His people.
P: A cycle of blessing. Numbers 18:24: 'the tithes… which they offer as an heave offering unto the LORD, I have given to the Levites.' Giving to God blesses all. Sow bountifully.

Everything comes from God, and everyone gives back. Today, remember that you are both receiver and giver. Whatever you receive, give back a portion to God.

They model generosity. Notice Luke 6. Give, and it shall be given unto you. The principle applies to everyone. Notice Second Corinthians 8. the Macedonians in deep poverty abounded unto the riches of their liberality. Even those with little gave generously. Today, give a portion of everything you receive. Model generosity, whatever your role.
S: The Levites were to offer a tenth of the tithes they received as a heave offering to the LORD. Those supported by others' gifts also gave, modeling generosity. Give a portion of everything you receive and model generosity, whatever your role.
P: Tithe on the tithe. Numbers 18:26: 'ye shall offer up an heave offering of it for the LORD, even a tenth part of the tithe.' Ministers give too. Model generosity today.

== 18:28

R: 'Thus ye also shall offer an heave offering unto the LORD of all your tithes, which ye receive of the children of Israel; and ye shall give thereof the LORD'S heave offering to Aaron the priest.' In this way you also will present an offering to the LORD from all the tithes you receive from the Israelites. From these tithes you must give the LORD's portion to Aaron the priest. Notice to Aaron the priest. The Levites' tithe went to the priests. A chain of giving. People to Levites, Levites to priests. Notice that everyone gave to someone. Even the Levites supported others. Notice that giving flows in many directions. The community supports leaders; leaders support others. Galatians 6 says, Let him that is taught in the word communicate unto him that teacheth in all good things. Notice that the chain ended with God. All giving ultimately went to Him. Today, give generously, knowing your gift flows to God and blesses many along the way.
S: The Levites were to give the LORD's heave offering from all their tithes to Aaron the priest. Giving flowed in a chain from people to Levites to priests, and all of it to God. Give generously, knowing your gift flows to God and blesses many along the way.

By presenting the best part of it you will not be guilty in this matter; then you will not defile the holy offerings of the Israelites, and you will not die. Notice ye shall bear no sin. When they gave properly, their consciences were clear. Notice the best of it. Giving God the best kept them guiltless. Notice neither shall ye pollute the holy things. Handling God's gifts carelessly was pollution. Notice that faithful stewardship protects us. When we handle God's resources well, we avoid sin. Notice First Corinthians 4. it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful. Luke 16 says, If therefore ye have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will commit to your trust the true riches? Today, handle God's resources faithfully. A clear conscience comes from honest stewardship.
