# Numbers 20: Meribah, Edom and the death of Aaron

== 20:1
T: Miriam died there
R: 'Then came the children of Israel, even the whole congregation, into the desert of Zin in the first month: and the people abode in Kadesh; and Miriam died there, and was buried there.' The whole community reached the Desert of Zin and stayed at Kadesh, where Miriam died and was buried. Notice in the first month. Most likely the fortieth year. Nearly four decades of wandering had passed in silence between these chapters. Notice Kadesh. The very place where Israel had refused to enter the land years before. They had come full circle. Notice Miriam died there. The prophetess who led Israel in song at the Red Sea. Exodus 15 says she took a timbrel and sang, Sing ye to the LORD, for he hath triumphed gloriously. Now her song was ended. Notice the brevity. One line for a life of faithful service. Psalm 116 says, Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of his saints. Today, honor those who have finished their course. God remembers every one.
S: The whole congregation came to Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, and there Miriam died and was buried. The prophetess who once sang at the Red Sea finished her course near the end of the forty years. Honor those who have finished their course, for their death is precious in God's sight.
P: Her song was ended. Numbers 20:1: 'Miriam died there, and was buried there.' She once led Israel in praise at the sea. Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of His saints.

Grief and pressure came together. Leaders often face complaints at their lowest moments. Notice that real needs can be brought to God in faith or in anger. Israel chose anger.
