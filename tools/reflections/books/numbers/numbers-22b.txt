# Numbers 22:21-41: Balaam's ass and the angel of the LORD

== 22:21

Notice rose up in the morning. Eager. He did not wait to be called. Verse 20 said, If the men come to call thee. Balaam went at once. Notice saddled his ass. He prepared himself. His mind was made up. Notice went with the princes of Moab. He joined those who wanted Israel cursed. His company revealed his heart. Notice Second Peter 2. Balaam loved the wages of unrighteousness. His eagerness showed what he truly wanted. Notice that haste toward temptation reveals desire. We move quickly toward what we love. Psalm 1 says, Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly. Today, watch where your feet hurry. Your eagerness reveals your heart.
S: Balaam rose up in the morning, saddled his ass and went with the princes of Moab. His eagerness and his company revealed that his heart loved the wages Balak promised. Watch where your feet hurry, for your eagerness reveals your heart.

== 22:22
T: An adversary in the way
R: 'And God's anger was kindled because he went: and the angel of the LORD stood in the way for an adversary against him…' But God was very angry when he went, and the angel of the LORD stood in the road to oppose him. Notice God's anger was kindled because he went. God had permitted the journey, yet He was angry. Balaam's heart was set on the reward, not on obedience. Notice the angel of the LORD. A divine messenger. Many see in such appearances the LORD Himself. Notice for an adversary. The Hebrew word is satan, meaning one who opposes. Here God's angel opposed Balaam's path. Notice that God opposes us in mercy. He blocked the way before Balaam went further. Hebrews 12 says, whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth. Today, if God is blocking your path, stop and listen. His opposition may be His mercy.
S: God's anger was kindled because Balaam went, and the angel of the LORD stood in the way as an adversary against him. God had permitted the journey, but Balaam's heart was set on reward, so God opposed him in mercy. If God is blocking your path, stop and listen, for His opposition may be His mercy.
P: Opposed in mercy. Numbers 22:22: 'the angel of the LORD stood in the way for an adversary against him.' God blocked a greedy path. Whom the Lord loveth He chasteneth. Stop and listen today.

== 22:23

The animal saw what the prophet could not. Balaam, the famous seer, was blind. Notice his sword drawn. Real danger. Balaam was riding toward death. Notice the ass turned aside. The animal saved Balaam's life. Notice Balaam smote the ass. He punished the one protecting him. Notice that greed blinds. Balaam's desire for gain made him spiritually blind.
