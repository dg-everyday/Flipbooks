Three feasts would fill it: trumpets, atonement and tabernacles. Notice a day of blowing the trumpets. A call to attention. The trumpets announced that the holiest season had begun. Notice that trumpets in Numbers 10 gathered the people and sounded alarm. Here they called Israel to prepare its heart.
