# Numbers 29:21-40: The Feast of Tabernacles completed

== 29:21
T: According to their number
R: 'And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner.' With the bulls, rams and lambs, prepare their grain offerings and drink offerings according to the number specified. Notice according to their number. Eleven bullocks meant eleven portions. The gifts matched the sacrifice exactly. Notice that giving was proportional to what was offered. Not a fixed amount, but a measured response. Notice the principle for us. First Corinthians 16 says, Upon the first day of the week let every one of you lay by him in store, as God hath prospered him. Our giving should reflect God's blessing. Notice that this guards against both stinginess and showiness. We give what fits what God has given. Notice Luke 12. unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required. Today, let your giving match God's blessing. Give as He has prospered you.
S: The grain and drink offerings on the third day matched the number of bullocks, rams and lambs, after the manner. Giving was proportional to the sacrifice, a measured response rather than a fixed amount. Let your giving match God's blessing and give as He has prospered you.
