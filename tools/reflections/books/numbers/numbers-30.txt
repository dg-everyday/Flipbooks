# Numbers 30: The law of vows

== 30:1
T: Unto the heads of the tribes
R: 'And Moses spake unto the heads of the tribes concerning the children of Israel, saying, This is the thing which the LORD hath commanded.' Moses said to the heads of the tribes of Israel: This is what the LORD commands. Notice unto the heads of the tribes. Tribal leaders received this law. They would apply it in families and courts. Notice this is the thing which the LORD hath commanded. Not Moses' opinion. God's word. Notice the setting. Chapter 29 ended with vows and freewill offerings. Now God explained how vows worked. Notice that promises to God mattered so much that He gave a whole chapter to them. Words spoken to God are not casual. Notice Ecclesiastes 5. When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools. Today, take your words to God seriously. Promises made to Him matter to Him.
S: Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes about what the LORD commanded concerning vows. After mentioning vows and freewill offerings, God gave a whole chapter to explain how promises to Him worked. Take your words to God seriously, for promises made to Him matter to Him.
P: Words to God matter. Numbers 30:1: 'This is the thing which the LORD hath commanded.' A whole chapter on vows. When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it. Keep your word to Him.

== 30:2
T: He shall not break his word
R: 'If a man vow a vow unto the LORD, or swear an oath to bind his soul with a bond; he shall not break his word, he shall do according to all that proceedeth out of his mouth.' When a man makes a vow to the LORD or takes an oath to obligate himself by a pledge, he must not break his word but must do everything he said. Notice vow a vow unto the LORD. A voluntary promise. Notice to bind his soul with a bond. A vow bound the inner person. Notice he shall not break his word. Absolute. Once spoken, it must be kept. Notice all that proceedeth out of his mouth. Notice Psalm 15. He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not. The godly keep their word even when it costs. Notice Matthew 5. let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay. Jesus called for such integrity that oaths are unnecessary. Today, keep your word. Let your yes mean yes, even when keeping it costs you.
S: A man who vowed to the LORD or bound his soul with an oath must not break his word but do all that came out of his mouth. Vows were voluntary, but once spoken they bound the whole person, as Jesus later called for a simple yes and no. Keep your word and let your yes mean yes, even when keeping it costs you.
P: Keep your word. Numbers 30:2: 'he shall not break his word, he shall do according to all that proceedeth out of his mouth.' He sweareth to his own hurt and changeth not. Let your yes be yes.

Notice that women's devotion fills Scripture. Mary, Hannah, Anna, Lydia. Today, honor the devotion of every believer. Men and women alike may offer themselves wholly to God.

== 30:4
T: Her vows shall stand
R: 'And her father hear her vow, and her bond wherewith she hath bound her soul, and her father shall hold his peace at her: then all her vows shall stand, and every bond wherewith she hath bound her soul shall stand.' And her father hears about her vow or pledge but says nothing to her, then all her vows and every pledge by which she obligated herself will stand. Notice her father hear her vow. Notice hold his peace at her. Silence meant consent. By not objecting, he affirmed it. Notice all her vows shall stand. Her commitment was valid. Notice that the father's role was protective. He bore responsibility for the household, so he had a voice. Notice that silence can be agreement. What we allow, we often affirm. Notice Ephesians 6. Fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord. Today, if you are a parent, be attentive to your children's commitments to God. Encourage and support their devotion.
S: If a father heard his daughter's vow and held his peace, all her vows and bonds would stand. His silence meant consent, for he bore responsibility for the household and had a protective voice. If you are a parent, be attentive to your children's commitments to God and encourage their devotion.
P: Silence as consent. Numbers 30:4: 'her father shall hold his peace at her: then all her vows shall stand.' A protective voice. Fathers, bring up children in the nurture of the Lord.

== 30:5
T: The LORD shall forgive her
R: 'But if her father disallow her in the day that he heareth; not any of her vows… shall stand: and the LORD shall forgive her, because her father disallowed her.' But if her father forbids her when he hears about it, none of her vows or the pledges by which she obligated herself will stand; the LORD will release her because her father has forbidden her. Notice in the day that he heareth. The objection had to be prompt. He could not wait and then cancel. Notice the LORD shall forgive her. She was not guilty for an unkept vow. God released her. A rash vow made in youth could be undone without guilt. Notice that God is gentle with sincere hearts. He does not trap people in vows they cannot keep. Notice Psalm 103. Like as a father pitieth his children, so the LORD pitieth them that fear him. For he knoweth our frame. Today, rest in God's gentleness. He knows your frame and does not hold you guilty unjustly.
S: If a father promptly disallowed his daughter's vow, it would not stand, and the LORD would forgive her. God protected the young woman from guilt for a vow released by her father. Rest in God's gentleness, for He knows your frame and pities those who fear Him.
P: The LORD shall forgive her. Numbers 30:5: 'the LORD shall forgive her, because her father disallowed her.' God released her from guilt. Like as a father pitieth his children, so the LORD pitieth you.
