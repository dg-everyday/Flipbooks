# Numbers 32:1-21: Reuben and Gad ask for land east of the Jordan

== 32:1
T: A place for cattle
R: 'Now the children of Reuben and the children of Gad had a very great multitude of cattle: and when they saw the land of Jazer, and the land of Gilead, that, behold, the place was a place for cattle.' The Reubenites and Gadites, who had very large herds and flocks, saw that the lands of Jazer and Gilead were suitable for livestock. Notice a very great multitude of cattle. Their wealth had grown, partly from Midian's spoil. Notice they saw the land. Rich pasture. Good grazing. Notice behold, the place was a place for cattle. Their eyes went to what suited their possessions. Notice that possessions can shape our desires. Their cattle influenced their choice. Notice Genesis 13. Lot lifted up his eyes, and beheld all the plain of Jordan, that it was well watered. He chose by sight and drifted toward Sodom. Notice First John 2. the lust of the eyes. Today, examine what shapes your choices. Let God's purposes, not your possessions, guide your decisions.
S: Reuben and Gad had a great multitude of cattle and saw that Jazer and Gilead were good land for livestock. Their possessions shaped their desires, as Lot once chose by sight. Examine what shapes your choices and let God's purposes, not your possessions, guide your decisions.
P: What guides your choices? Numbers 32:1: 'they saw… that, behold, the place was a place for cattle.' Possessions shaped their desire, like Lot's. Let God's purposes guide you, not your stuff.

Notice unto Moses, and to Eleazar… and unto the princes. All the leadership. An official request. Notice Gad listed first here. Perhaps Gad led the request. Notice that asking is not wrong. Notice Philippians 4. in every thing by prayer and supplication… let your requests be made known. Today, bring your desires openly to God and to those He has placed over you. Asking rightly is a mark of humility.
S: Gad and Reuben came and spoke to Moses, Eleazar and the princes of the congregation. They brought their desire openly through the proper leaders rather than grumbling or acting alone. Bring your desires openly to God and to those He has placed over you, for asking rightly is a mark of humility.
P: Ask rightly. Numbers 32:2: 'The children of Gad and the children of Reuben came and spake unto Moses.' An open request, not rebellion. Let your requests be made known with humility.

Others would fight while they rested. Notice shall ye sit here? A pointed question. Comfort while others struggled. Notice that Moses defended fairness. Every tribe had shared in the victories east of the Jordan. Every tribe should share in the battles west of it. Notice that the body of Christ shares burdens. Share in the struggles of God's people.
