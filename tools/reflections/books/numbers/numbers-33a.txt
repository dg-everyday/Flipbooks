# Numbers 33:1-28: The journeys of Israel, from Egypt into the wilderness

== 33:1
T: These are the journeys
R: 'These are the journeys of the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt with their armies under the hand of Moses and Aaron.' Here are the stages in the journey of the Israelites when they came out of Egypt by divisions under the leadership of Moses and Aaron. Notice these are the journeys. A travel record. Forty-two stops from Egypt to the Jordan. Notice went forth out of the land of Egypt. The story began with deliverance. Notice with their armies. Not as slaves, but as an organized people. Notice under the hand of Moses and Aaron. God led through human leaders. Notice that God keeps a record of the journey. Every stop mattered to Him. Notice Psalm 56. Thou tellest my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle: are they not in thy book? Today, take comfort that God records your journey. Every step, even the wandering ones, is known to Him.
S: Moses recorded the journeys of Israel from Egypt under the hand of Moses and Aaron, forty-two stages in all. God kept a record of every stop, from deliverance to the edge of the promise. Take comfort that God records your journey, for every step, even the wandering ones, is known to Him.
P: God records the journey. Numbers 33:1: 'These are the journeys of the children of Israel.' Every stop mattered. Thou tellest my wanderings; are they not in Thy book? He knows your path.

== 33:2
T: Moses wrote
R: 'And Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the LORD: and these are their journeys according to their goings out.' At the LORD's command Moses recorded the stages in their journey. This is their journey by stages. Notice Moses wrote. A written record. Moses was a writer as well as a leader. Notice by the commandment of the LORD. God commanded the record. He wanted it remembered. Notice their goings out. Each departure was noted. Each new beginning. Notice that God values remembering. Written history guards against forgetting. Notice Exodus 17. Write this for a memorial in a book. Habakkuk 2 says, Write the vision, and make it plain. Notice that writing down God's leading is an act of faith. It helps the next generation see His hand. Today, record God's faithfulness in your life. Write it down so you and others will remember.
S: Moses wrote down Israel's journeys by the LORD's commandment, recording each departure. God wanted the journey remembered, and the written record would show the next generation His hand. Record God's faithfulness in your life and write it down so you and others will remember.
P: Write it down. Numbers 33:2: 'Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the LORD.' God wanted it remembered. Write down His faithfulness today.

== 33:3
T: With an high hand
R: 'And they departed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the morrow after the passover the children of Israel went out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians.' The Israelites set out from Rameses on the fifteenth day of the first month, the day after the Passover. They marched out defiantly in full view of all the Egyptians. Notice Rameses. A store city built by Israelite slaves. They left the place of their bondage. Notice on the morrow after the passover. Redemption came first, then departure. Notice with an high hand. Boldly. Confidently. Not sneaking away. Notice in the sight of all the Egyptians. Publicly. God's deliverance was seen by all. Notice that salvation leads to bold freedom. Galatians 5 says, Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free. Today, walk in the freedom Christ has given you. Leave bondage boldly, in full view.
S: Israel departed from Rameses on the day after the passover, going out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians. Redemption came first, and then they left the place of bondage boldly and publicly. Walk in the freedom Christ has given you and leave bondage boldly.
P: Out with a high hand. Numbers 33:3: 'the children of Israel went out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians.' Bold freedom after redemption. Stand fast in the liberty of Christ.

Notice that God turned Marah's water sweet with a tree. Exodus 15 says, I am the LORD that healeth thee. Today, do not be surprised when hardship follows victory. Do not be surprised when hardship follows victory, for the God who parted the sea can sweeten your bitter waters.
P: From sea to bitter water. Numbers 33:8: 'passed through the midst of the sea… and pitched in Marah.' Triumph then trial. I am the LORD that healeth thee. He sweetens bitter waters.
