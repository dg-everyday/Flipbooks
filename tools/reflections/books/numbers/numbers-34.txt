# Numbers 34: The borders of the land and the men who would divide it

== 34:1
T: God speaks of borders
R: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' The LORD said to Moses. Notice that God now spoke about the shape of the land. After the warnings of chapter 33, He gave the boundaries. Notice that God's promises have definite shape. Canaan was not a vague hope but a real land with real borders. Notice that Moses received these words knowing he would never cross the Jordan. He described a land he would only see from a distance. Yet he passed on every detail faithfully. Notice that God's servants sometimes prepare blessings they will not personally enjoy. Hebrews 11 says, These all died in faith, not having received the promises, but having seen them afar off. Notice John 4. one soweth, and another reapeth. Today, serve faithfully even when others will reap the harvest. Your preparation matters to God's plan.
S: The LORD spoke to Moses to describe the borders of the land, though Moses would never cross into it. He faithfully passed on every detail of a promise others would enjoy. Serve faithfully even when others will reap the harvest, for your preparation matters to God's plan.
P: Preparing for others. Numbers 34:1: 'And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying.' Moses described a land he would not enter. One soweth, and another reapeth. Serve faithfully for those who follow.

It is reserved for you and will never fade.
S: God commanded Moses to tell Israel that when they came into Canaan, it would fall to them as an inheritance. The land was received as a gift to God's children, pointing to an incorruptible inheritance reserved in heaven. Rejoice in your inheritance in Christ, for it is reserved for you and will never fade.
