Notice he is a murderer. The weapon revealed the heart. Notice the murderer shall surely be put to death. Israel's civil law, given under God's direct rule, treated murder with utmost seriousness. Genesis 9 says, Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man. Notice the reason. Human life bears God's image. Taking it deliberately was the gravest crime. Notice that refuge was not for murderers. Mercy did not cancel justice. Today, honor the sacredness of every human life. Each person bears the image of God.
