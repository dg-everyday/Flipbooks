# Numbers 35:18-34: Murder, manslaughter and the death of the high priest

== 35:18
T: A hand weapon of wood
R: 'Or if he smite him with an hand weapon of wood, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.' A fatal blow with a wooden club also marked a murderer. Notice an hand weapon of wood. A club or staff. Iron, stone, wood. Three kinds of weapons, one principle. Notice wherewith he may die. The weapon had to be capable of killing. Intent was judged by means. Notice the thoroughness of the law. No loophole by choice of weapon. Notice that God closes every escape route for deliberate violence. Whatever the method, murder is murder. Notice Exodus 20. Thou shalt not kill. The command underlies every case. Notice that Israel's civil courts applied these penalties within God's covenant nation, while believers today are called to love and to leave vengeance to God and the governing authorities, as Romans 13 teaches. Today, reject violence in every form. Honor life, and leave justice to God and those He appoints.
S: Striking someone with a wooden weapon able to kill, so that he died, also marked a murderer under Israel's law. Whether iron, stone or wood, the law left no loophole for deliberate violence. Reject violence in every form, honor life and leave justice to God and those He appoints.
P: No loophole. Numbers 35:18: 'if he smite him with an hand weapon of wood… he is a murderer.' Iron, stone, wood, one principle. Thou shalt not kill. Honor every life today.

== 35:19
T: The revenger of blood
R: 'The revenger of blood himself shall slay the murderer: when he meeteth him, he shall slay him.' The avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death. Notice the revenger of blood. The go'el. The nearest relative of the victim. Notice himself shall slay the murderer. In Israel's tribal society, family carried out justice for murder. Notice that this was regulated. Only after the congregation judged, as verse 24 shows. Not private revenge, but structured justice. Notice the word go'el. The same word used for redeemer. Boaz was Ruth's go'el. Job 19 says, I know that my redeemer liveth. Notice the double role. The go'el avenged and redeemed. Christ is our near Kinsman who both defeats our enemy and redeems us. Hebrews 2 says He destroyed him that had the power of death. Today, rejoice in your Redeemer. Christ is the near Kinsman who rescues His people.
S: The revenger of blood, the victim's nearest kinsman, was to put the murderer to death. In Israel's society this was regulated justice, and the same Hebrew word, go'el, also meant redeemer. Rejoice in Christ your Redeemer, the near Kinsman who defeats the enemy and rescues His people.
P: Avenger and redeemer. Numbers 35:19: 'The revenger of blood himself shall slay the murderer.' The go'el, kinsman and redeemer. I know that my redeemer liveth. Rejoice in Christ.
H: #DailyGrace #Numbers3519 #Redeemer #Christ #Justice #BibleVerse

== 35:20
T: Of hatred
R: 'But if he thrust him of hatred, or hurl at him by laying of wait, that he die.' If anyone with malice aforethought shoves another or throws something at them intentionally so that they die. Notice of hatred. The motive mattered. Hatred turned a death into murder. Notice by laying of wait. Ambush. Planning. Premeditation. Notice that the law examined motive and method. Was there hatred? Was there planning? Notice that hatred is the seed of murder. First John 3 says, Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer. Notice Genesis 4. Cain was very wroth before he rose up against his brother. God warned him: sin lieth at the door. Notice that God sees hatred long before it acts. Today, root out hatred from your heart. Bring every resentment to God before it becomes a weapon.
S: If someone thrust another out of hatred or threw at him while lying in wait so that he died, he was a murderer. Hatred and premeditation turned a death into murder, as Cain's anger led to his brother's death. Root out hatred from your heart and bring every resentment to God before it becomes a weapon.
P: Hatred's seed. Numbers 35:20: 'if he thrust him of hatred, or hurl at him by laying of wait.' Motive mattered. Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer. Root out hatred today.

== 35:21
T: In enmity
R: 'Or in enmity smite him with his hand, that he die: he that smote him shall surely be put to death; for he is a murderer…' Even striking with the hand, in hostility, so that someone died, made one a murderer. Notice in enmity smite him with his hand. No weapon needed. Even bare hands, used with hostility, could murder. Notice for he is a murderer. The enmity made it murder. Notice that the heart's hostility, not the tool, defined the crime. Notice that enmity is the opposite of God's love. Romans 8 says, the carnal mind is enmity against God. Notice that Christ came to end enmity. Ephesians 2 says He abolished in his flesh the enmity… so making peace. Notice Romans 5. when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God by the death of his Son. Today, let Christ remove enmity from your heart. He makes peace where hostility once ruled.
S: If someone in enmity struck another with his hand so that he died, he was a murderer. The heart's hostility, not the tool, defined the crime, and Christ came to abolish enmity and make peace. Let Christ remove enmity from your heart, for He makes peace where hostility once ruled.
P: Enmity ended. Numbers 35:21: 'in enmity smite him with his hand… he is a murderer.' Hostility defined the crime. Christ abolished the enmity, making peace. Let Him heal your heart.
H: #DailyGrace #Numbers3521 #Peace #Reconciliation #Heart #BibleVerse

== 35:22
T: Suddenly without enmity
R: 'But if he thrust him suddenly without enmity, or have cast upon him any thing without laying of wait.' But if without enmity someone suddenly pushes another or throws something at them unintentionally. Notice suddenly without enmity. An accident. No hatred. No plan. Notice without laying of wait. No ambush. No premeditation. Notice the contrast with verses 20 and 21. Same outcome, different heart. Notice that God distinguished carefully. The same death could be murder or accident, depending on the heart. Notice Deuteronomy 19. As when a man goeth into the wood with his neighbour to hew wood… and the head slippeth from the helve. A tragic accident. Notice that God's law was compassionate toward the unintentional. Today, show compassion to those who cause harm by accident. And trust that God judges your heart, not only outcomes.
S: If someone struck another suddenly without enmity or threw something without lying in wait, the law treated it differently. The same death could be murder or accident depending on the heart, and God's law was compassionate toward the unintentional. Show compassion to those who cause harm by accident and trust that God judges your heart.
P: Same outcome, different heart. Numbers 35:22: 'if he thrust him suddenly without enmity.' An accident, not murder. God judges the heart. Show compassion to those who harm unintentionally.

== 35:23
T: Neither sought his harm
R: 'Or with any stone, wherewith a man may die, seeing him not, and cast it upon him, that he die, and was not his enemy, neither sought his harm.' Or drops a stone that could kill without seeing them, and the person dies, though they were not enemies and no harm was intended. Notice seeing him not. He did not see the person. A tragic accident. Notice was not his enemy. No history of hostility. Notice neither sought his harm. No intent. Notice that the law listed signs of innocence. No enmity, no planning, no seeing. Notice that even a deadly stone could be innocent if the heart was clean. Notice that accidents happen. People cause harm they never intended. Grief and guilt can follow. Notice Psalm 103. Like as a father pitieth his children, so the LORD pitieth them that fear him. Today, if you carry guilt for an accident, bring it to God. He knows your heart and offers refuge.
S: If someone cast a stone without seeing the person, was not his enemy and sought no harm, the death was an accident. The law listed signs of innocence, recognizing that people can cause harm they never intended. If you carry guilt for an accident, bring it to God, for He knows your heart and offers refuge.
P: Refuge for the grieving. Numbers 35:23: 'seeing him not… and was not his enemy, neither sought his harm.' An accident, not a crime. The LORD pitieth them that fear Him. Bring your guilt to Him.

== 35:24
T: The congregation shall judge
R: 'Then the congregation shall judge between the slayer and the revenger of blood according to these judgments.' The assembly must judge between the accused and the avenger of blood according to these regulations. Notice the congregation shall judge. Not the avenger alone. The community decided. Notice between the slayer and the revenger. Both sides heard. A fair process. Notice according to these judgments. By God's standards, not emotion. Notice that justice required a hearing. No one could act on anger alone. Notice that God protects against both injustice and vengeance. Proverbs 18 says, He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him. Notice John 7. Doth our law judge any man, before it hear him? Today, judge fairly. Hear both sides before deciding, and let God's standards guide you, not your feelings.
S: The congregation was to judge between the slayer and the revenger of blood according to God's judgments. Justice required a fair hearing of both sides by God's standards, not by anger. Judge fairly, hear both sides before deciding and let God's standards guide you, not your feelings.
P: Hear both sides. Numbers 35:24: 'the congregation shall judge between the slayer and the revenger of blood.' A fair hearing. He that answereth before he heareth, it is folly. Judge fairly today.

== 35:25
T: Unto the death of the high priest
R: 'And the congregation shall deliver the slayer out of the hand of the revenger of blood… and he shall abide in it unto the death of the high priest, which was anointed with the holy oil.' The assembly must protect the one accused of murder from the avenger of blood and send the accused back to the city of refuge to which they fled. The accused must stay there until the death of the high priest. Notice the congregation shall deliver the slayer. Protection was provided. Notice he shall abide in it. The slayer stayed in the city. Notice unto the death of the high priest. Freedom came through the high priest's death. Notice which was anointed with the holy oil. The anointed one. Messiah means anointed. Notice the picture. A death set the guilty free. Hebrews 9 says Christ, our High Priest, by his own blood… obtained eternal redemption for us. Today, rejoice that the death of your High Priest has set you free. Christ died so that you may go home.
S: The congregation would deliver the manslayer to his city of refuge, where he remained until the death of the anointed high priest. A high priest's death brought the slayer freedom, picturing Christ, our anointed High Priest, whose death obtained eternal redemption. Rejoice that the death of your High Priest has set you free.
P: Freed by a death. Numbers 35:25: 'he shall abide in it unto the death of the high priest.' A death set the slayer free. Christ, our High Priest, obtained eternal redemption. You are free.

But if the accused ever goes outside the limits of the city of refuge to which they fled. Notice without the border. Outside the city. Beyond protection. Notice at any time. The danger was constant. Leaving the city meant exposure. Notice that refuge required remaining. The slayer had to stay inside to stay safe. Notice John 15. Abide in me, and I in you. Safety in Christ means remaining in Him. Notice First John 2. abide in him; that, when he shall appear, we may have confidence. Notice that wandering from refuge is dangerous. The world outside is not safe. Today, abide in Christ. Stay within the refuge He provides, and you will be safe.
S: If the slayer went outside the border of his city of refuge, he left its protection. Refuge required remaining inside, just as safety in Christ means abiding in Him. Abide in Christ and stay within the refuge He provides, for there you are safe.
P: Stay in the refuge. Numbers 35:26: 'if the slayer shall at any time come without the border of the city of his refuge.' Safety meant remaining. Abide in Me, and I in you. Stay close to Christ.

== 35:27
T: He shall not be guilty
R: 'And the revenger of blood find him without the borders of the city of his refuge, and the revenger of blood kill the slayer; he shall not be guilty of blood.' If the avenger of blood finds them outside the city and kills them, the avenger is not guilty. Notice find him without the borders. Outside protection. Exposed. Notice he shall not be guilty of blood. The avenger was legally permitted. Leaving refuge forfeited protection. Notice the seriousness of leaving refuge. The slayer chose to step out. Notice that this pictures the danger of turning from grace. Hebrews 10 warns, if we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins. Notice Hebrews 2. How shall we escape, if we neglect so great salvation? Today, do not neglect the refuge God has given. Stay in Christ, where you are safe from every accuser.
S: If the revenger of blood found the slayer outside the city of refuge and killed him, he was not guilty of blood. Leaving refuge forfeited protection, a sobering picture of neglecting God's salvation. Do not neglect the refuge God has given, but stay in Christ, where you are safe.
P: Don't leave the refuge. Numbers 35:27: 'the revenger of blood find him without the borders… he shall not be guilty.' Leaving forfeited protection. How shall we escape if we neglect so great salvation?

== 35:30
T: By the mouth of witnesses
R: 'Whoso killeth any person, the murderer shall be put to death by the mouth of witnesses: but one witness shall not testify against any person to cause him to die.' Anyone who kills a person is to be put to death as a murderer only on the testimony of witnesses. But no one is to be put to death on the testimony of only one witness. Notice by the mouth of witnesses. Evidence was required. Not rumor or accusation. Notice one witness shall not testify. One person's word was not enough to condemn. Notice that God protected the accused from false charges. Multiple witnesses were needed. Notice Deuteronomy 19. at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established. Jesus and Paul used this principle. Matthew 18 and Second Corinthians 13. Notice that this protects against slander and injustice. Today, be careful with accusations. Seek truth through fair evidence, and never condemn on a single voice.
S: A murderer could be put to death only by the mouth of witnesses, never on the testimony of one witness alone. God protected the accused from false charges, a principle Jesus and Paul later upheld. Be careful with accusations and seek truth through fair evidence, never condemning on a single voice.
P: Two or three witnesses. Numbers 35:30: 'one witness shall not testify against any person to cause him to die.' Protection from false charges. Seek truth fairly; never condemn on a single voice.

A life could not be paid for with money. Notice that this protected the poor. The wealthy could not buy their way out of justice. Notice that human life is beyond price. Psalm 49 says, none of them can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him. Notice that only God could pay the ransom for sinners. Mark 10 says, the Son of man came… to give his life a ransom for many. Notice First Peter 1. not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold. Today, rejoice that Christ paid the ransom no one else could. Your life was bought with His blood.
S: Israel was to take no satisfaction or ransom for the life of a murderer. No money could buy off the penalty, which protected the poor and showed that human life is beyond price. Rejoice that Christ paid the ransom no one else could, buying your life with His blood.
P: Beyond price. Numbers 35:31: 'ye shall take no satisfaction for the life of a murderer.' Life couldn't be bought with money. The Son of man came to give His life a ransom for many.

Notice Hebrews 12. the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel. Abel's blood cried for justice; Christ's blood cries for mercy. Today, thank God for the blood of Jesus.
