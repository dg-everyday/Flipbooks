== 36:4
T: When the jubile shall be
R: 'And when the jubile of the children of Israel shall be, then shall their inheritance be put unto the inheritance of the tribe whereunto they are received: so shall their inheritance be taken away from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers.' Even the Year of Jubilee would not bring the land back. Notice the jubile. Every fiftieth year, land returned to its original family. Leviticus 25 describes it. Notice the problem. Normally jubilee restored land. Here it would make the transfer permanent. Notice that the clan understood the law well. They knew how jubilee worked and saw the gap. Notice that knowing God's law helps us apply it wisely. Notice Psalm 1. his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night. Notice that jubilee pointed to Christ. Luke 4 says He came to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord. Today, meditate on God's word. Knowing it deeply helps you live it wisely.

The land God gave each tribe was to stay with that tribe. Notice keep himself to the inheritance. Each Israelite was to hold onto what God had given his family.
