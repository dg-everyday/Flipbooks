# Psalms 1: Blessed is the man

== 1:1
T: Blessed is the man
R: 'Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly' Notice blessed is the man. Happiness from God. Notice that walketh not. A path refused. Notice nor standeth in the way of sinners. A lingering refused. Notice nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful. A settling refused. Notice the downward slide. Walking, then standing, then sitting. Notice that sin rarely begins with a leap. Notice that it begins with listening to the wrong voice. Notice that the Psalms open with a choice of roads. Notice that blessing is tied to what we refuse as well as what we embrace. Notice that companions shape us. Notice that scorn is the last stop on the slide. Notice that God's blessing is a gift, not a wage. Proverbs 4 says, 'Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men.' Notice the warning. Today, check whose counsel you are walking in. Choose the road of blessing.
S: The first psalm says blessed is the one who does not walk in the counsel of the ungodly, stand in the way of sinners, or sit in the seat of the scornful. Sin often slides from walking to standing to sitting, so blessing begins with the voices we refuse to follow. Choose your road with care, for enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men.
P: Two roads. Psalms 1:1: 'Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly.' Choose well. Enter not into the path of the wicked.

== 1:2
T: His delight is in the law of the LORD
R: 'But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.' Notice but. The contrast. Notice his delight. Joy, not duty. Notice is in the law of the LORD. God's word. Notice and in his law. Repeated focus. Notice doth he meditate. Slow thinking. Notice day and night. Constant. Notice that the blessed person is defined by what fills the mind. Notice that delight comes before discipline. Notice that meditation is chewing, not skimming. Notice that day and night means all of life. Notice that God's word becomes a companion. Notice that Joshua was told the same. Notice that meditation was his secret of courage. Notice that delight grows with time spent. Notice that what we love we think about. Joshua 1 says, 'but thou shalt meditate therein day and night.' Notice the same rhythm. Today, choose one verse to carry with you all day. Let it fill your thoughts.
S: The blessed person delights in the law of the LORD and meditates on it day and night. Delight comes before discipline, and meditation means turning God's word over slowly until it fills the mind. Carry one verse with you today, for God told Joshua, thou shalt meditate therein day and night.
P: Delight. Psalms 1:2: 'But his delight is in the law of the LORD.' Day and night. Thou shalt meditate therein day and night.

== 1:3
T: Like a tree planted by the rivers of water
R: 'And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season' Notice and he shall be like a tree. A picture. Notice planted. Not wild. Notice by the rivers of water. Constant supply. Notice that bringeth forth his fruit. Produce. Notice in his season. Right timing. Notice that his leaf shall not wither. Lasting. Notice that a planted tree was put there on purpose. Notice that roots reach water out of sight. Notice that fruit comes in season, not every day. Notice that dry spells do not kill a rooted tree. Notice that meditation is the root system. Notice that God is the river. Notice that Jeremiah used this picture too. Notice that the rooted tree does not fear heat. Jeremiah 17 says, 'For he shall be as a tree planted by the waters, and that spreadeth out her roots by the river.' Notice deep roots. Today, push your roots toward God's word. Fruit comes in its season.
S: The one who delights in God's word is like a tree planted by streams of water, bearing fruit in season with leaves that do not wither. A planted tree draws from water out of sight, so rooted lives endure dry seasons and bear fruit in God's timing. Push your roots deep, for he shall be as a tree planted by the waters, and that spreadeth out her roots by the river.
P: Planted. Psalms 1:3: 'like a tree planted by the rivers of water.' Rooted. As a tree planted by the waters. Trust Him with it today.

== 1:4
T: Like the chaff which the wind driveth away
R: 'The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.' Notice the ungodly are not so. A sharp contrast. Notice but are like the chaff. Husks. Notice which the wind driveth away. Blown off. Notice that the tree has roots and the chaff has none. Notice that chaff looks like part of the harvest. Notice that it has no weight. Notice that wind reveals what is real. Notice that life's storms sift us. Notice that weightless lives are carried anywhere. Notice that rooted lives stand. Notice that the difference is not visible on a calm day. Notice that it shows when the wind blows. Notice that God offers weight through His word. Notice that no one has to stay chaff. Hosea 13 says, 'as the chaff that is driven with the whirlwind out of the floor.' Notice the whirlwind. Today, ask whether your life has roots or only appearance. Let God give it weight.
S: The psalm says the ungodly are not like the rooted tree but are like chaff that the wind blows away. Chaff can look like part of the harvest, but storms reveal what has weight and what does not. Ask God to give your life roots and substance, unlike the chaff that is driven with the whirlwind out of the floor.
P: Chaff. Psalms 1:4: 'but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.' Weightless. As the chaff that is driven with the whirlwind.

== 1:5
T: The ungodly shall not stand in the judgment
R: 'Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.' Notice therefore. A conclusion. Notice the ungodly shall not stand. Collapse. Notice in the judgment. Final accounting. Notice nor sinners. The unrepentant. Notice in the congregation of the righteous. God's assembly. Notice that the psalm looks ahead. Notice that judgment is real. Notice that standing in the end depends on roots now. Notice that chaff cannot stand. Notice that God's people will gather. Notice that the unrepentant will not belong. Notice that this is sobering. Notice that it is also an invitation. Notice that anyone may turn and be rooted. Notice that Paul said all will appear before Christ. Notice that no one is exempt. 2 Corinthians 5 says, 'For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ.' Notice the appointment. Today, live with the end in view. Turn to God while there is time.
S: Therefore the ungodly will not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous. Judgment is real and no one is exempt, which makes this both a warning and an invitation to turn to God now. Live with the end in view, for we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ.
P: Judgment. Psalms 1:5: 'the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment.' All appear. We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ.

== 1:6
T: The LORD knoweth the way of the righteous
R: 'For the LORD knoweth the way of the righteous: but the way of the ungodly shall perish.' Notice for the LORD knoweth. Intimate care. Notice the way of the righteous. Their path. Notice but the way of the ungodly. Another road. Notice shall perish. Ends in ruin. Notice that the psalm ends with two roads again. Notice that knowing here means watching over with love. Notice that God is not a distant observer. Notice that He knows every step. Notice that the other road simply runs out. Notice that it leads nowhere. Notice that the difference is God's care. Notice that righteousness is a relationship. Notice that Paul echoed this. Notice that God knows His own. Notice that He holds them fast. 2 Timothy 2 says, 'The Lord knoweth them that are his.' Notice being known. Today, walk the road God watches over. You are known by Him.
S: The LORD watches over the way of the righteous, but the way of the ungodly will perish. God's knowing is loving care over every step, while the other road simply runs out. Walk the road He watches, for the Lord knoweth them that are His.
P: Known. Psalms 1:6: 'For the LORD knoweth the way of the righteous.' His own. The Lord knoweth them that are his. Walk in His ways today.
