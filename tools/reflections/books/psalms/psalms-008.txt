# Psalms 8: How excellent is thy name in all the earth

== 8:1
T: How excellent is thy name
R: 'O LORD our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth' Notice O LORD our Lord. Covenant name and Master. Notice how excellent. Majestic. Notice is thy name. His character. Notice in all the earth. Everywhere. Notice that He has set His glory above the heavens. Beyond. Notice that the psalm opens in wonder. Notice that God's name is His revealed nature. Notice that it is majestic across the whole earth. Notice that His glory exceeds the sky. Notice that we live inside His greatness. Notice that David said our Lord. Notice that God's greatness is shared with His people. Notice that Paul spoke of an exalted name. Notice that it belongs to Jesus. Notice that it is above every name. Notice that every knee will bow. Philippians 2 says, 'God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name.' Notice the exalted name. Today, let wonder at God's name begin your worship. It is majestic everywhere.
S: David exclaimed, LORD our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth, You who have set Your glory above the heavens. Worship begins with wonder at God's revealed character, a name that Paul says belongs supremely to Jesus. Let wonder lead you, for God also hath highly exalted Him, and given Him a name which is above every name.
P: Majestic name. Psalms 8:1: 'O LORD our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth.' Above all. A name which is above every name.

== 8:2
T: Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings
R: 'Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies' Notice out of the mouth. Speech. Notice of babes and sucklings. Infants. Notice hast thou ordained strength. Power appointed. Notice because of thine enemies. Opposition. Notice that God silences the foe and the avenger. Defeat. Notice that God uses the weakest voices. Notice that infants confound the proud. Notice that strength comes from small mouths. Notice that God does not need impressive people. Notice that He chooses the humble. Notice that Jesus quoted this verse. Notice that children praised Him in the temple. Notice that leaders objected. Notice that Jesus defended the children. Notice that their praise was perfect. Notice that childlike faith pleases God. Notice that it silences mockers. Matthew 21 says, 'Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise.' Notice children's praise. Today, value the faith of children. God uses small voices.
S: David said that through the praise of children and infants God has established strength to silence the foe. God uses the weakest voices to confound the proud, as Jesus showed when He defended children praising Him. Value childlike faith, for out of the mouth of babes and sucklings Thou hast perfected praise.
P: Small voices. Psalms 8:2: 'Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings.' Perfected. Thou hast perfected praise. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.

== 8:3
T: When I consider thy heavens
R: 'When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained' Notice when I consider. Reflection. Notice thy heavens. God's sky. Notice the work of thy fingers. Fine craftsmanship. Notice the moon and the stars. Night lights. Notice which thou hast ordained. Set in place. Notice that David looked up at night. Notice that the sky is called finger work. Notice that the vast heavens are God's delicate art. Notice that considering them takes time. Notice that wonder grows with attention. Notice that God set each star in place. Notice that nothing is random. Notice that Isaiah urged looking up. Notice that God created the stars. Notice that He calls them by name. Notice that not one is missing. Notice that the night sky preaches. Isaiah 40 says, 'Lift up your eyes on high, and behold who hath created these things.' Notice looking up. Today, look at the sky and consider. It is the work of God's fingers.
S: David said, when I consider Your heavens, the work of Your fingers, the moon and the stars You have set in place. The vast heavens are God's fine craftsmanship, and taking time to look up grows wonder. Look up tonight, for lift up your eyes on high, and behold who hath created these things.
P: Consider. Psalms 8:3: 'When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers.' Look up. Lift up your eyes on high. Let this shape your day.

== 8:4

R: 'What is man, that thou art mindful of him' Notice what is man. Smallness. Notice that thou art mindful of him. God's attention. Notice that he asked about the son of man. Humanity. Notice that God visits him. Personal care. Notice that the heavens made David feel small. Notice that he marvelled that God noticed people. Notice that greatness and care meet. Notice that God thinks of us. Notice that He visits us. Notice that this is astonishing. Notice that size does not measure worth. Notice that God's attention gives worth. Notice that Jesus taught this. Notice that sparrows are not forgotten. Notice that our hairs are numbered. Notice that we are known in detail. Notice that fear is answered by care. Luke 12 says, 'But even the very hairs of your head are all numbered.' Notice numbered hairs. Today, marvel that the God of the stars thinks of you. You are known in detail.
S: David asked, what is man that You are mindful of him, the son of man that You care for him. The God of the vast heavens pays personal attention to small people, and that attention gives us worth. Marvel at His care, for even the very hairs of your head are all numbered.
P: Mindful. Psalms 8:4: 'What is man, that thou art mindful of him.' Known. The very hairs of your head are all numbered. Take it to God in prayer.

== 8:5
T: Crowned him with glory and honour
R: 'For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast crowned him with glory and honour.' Notice for thou hast made him. Created. Notice a little lower than the angels. High rank. Notice and hast crowned him. Royalty. Notice with glory and honour. Dignity. Notice that humans are small yet crowned. Notice that we bear God's image. Notice that we are given honour. Notice that dignity is God's gift. Notice that it is not earned. Notice that every person carries it. Notice that sin dimmed the crown. Notice that Christ restores it. Notice that Hebrews applies this to Jesus. Notice that He was made lower for a time. Notice that He tasted death for us. Notice that He is crowned now. Notice that through Him humanity's crown returns. Hebrews 2 says, 'But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels.' Notice Jesus crowned. Today, treat every person as crowned by God. Christ restores the crown.
S: David said God made mankind a little lower than the angels and crowned them with glory and honour. Every person carries God-given dignity, and Hebrews shows Jesus restoring humanity's crown through His death and exaltation. Honour every person, for we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels, crowned with glory and honour.
P: Crowned. Psalms 8:5: 'and hast crowned him with glory and honour.' Jesus. We see Jesus, who was made a little lower. Live it out today.

== 8:6

Notice over the works of thy hands. God's creation. Notice thou hast put all things. Everything. Notice under his feet. Authority. Notice that God gave humans responsibility. Notice that dominion is stewardship. Notice that it is not abuse. Notice that we rule God's works, not our own. Notice that we answer to the Owner. Notice that this echoes Genesis. Notice that God blessed humanity. Notice that He told them to have dominion. Notice that rule is a blessing. Notice that it comes with care. Notice that sin distorted it. Notice that Christ models true rule. Notice that He served. Genesis 1 says, 'and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air.' Notice entrusted rule. Today, care for what God has entrusted to you. Dominion is stewardship.
S: David said God made mankind rulers over the works of His hands and put everything under their feet. Dominion is stewardship of God's creation, not ownership or abuse, for we answer to the Owner. Care for what He entrusted, for He said, have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air.
P: Dominion. Psalms 8:6: 'Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands.' Stewardship. Have dominion over the fish of the sea.
