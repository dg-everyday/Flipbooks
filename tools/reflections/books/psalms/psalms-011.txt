# Psalms 11: In the LORD put I my trust

== 11:1
T: In the LORD put I my trust
R: 'In the LORD put I my trust' Notice in the LORD. The right refuge. Notice put I my trust. Decision. Notice that friends urged him to flee like a bird to the mountains. Advice. Notice that David refused to run in panic. Notice that he had already found refuge. Notice that fleeing to mountains was natural advice. Notice that it was not wrong in itself. Notice that David's refuge was God first. Notice that location is not safety. Notice that God is. Notice that panic suggests escape. Notice that faith suggests trust. Notice that the psalm begins with confidence. Notice that another psalm says the same. Notice that God is a refuge and fortress. Notice that He is trustworthy. Notice that we choose where to hide. Psalm 91 says, 'He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.' Notice the fortress. Today, choose God as your refuge before you run anywhere else. He is your fortress.
S: David said, in the LORD I take refuge, so how can you tell me to flee like a bird to the mountains. Panic urges escape, but David's safety was God first rather than any place. Choose your refuge wisely, saying, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in Him will I trust.
P: Refuge. Psalms 11:1: 'In the LORD put I my trust.' Fortress. He is my refuge and my fortress. Trust Him with it today.

R: 'For, lo, the wicked bend their bow' Notice for, lo. Look. Notice the wicked bend their bow. Preparing attack. Notice that they set their arrow. Ready. Notice that they shoot from the shadows. Hidden. Notice at the upright in heart. Targets. Notice that the danger was real. Notice that attacks came secretly. Notice that the upright were targeted. Notice that faithfulness can attract hostility. Notice that hidden attacks are hard to defend. Notice that God sees the shadows. Notice that believers face unseen darts. Notice that Paul described spiritual armour. Notice that faith is a shield. Notice that it quenches fiery darts. Notice that it protects the upright. Notice that God provides defence. Notice that we need not panic. Ephesians 6 says, 'wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.' Notice faith's shield. Today, take up the shield of faith against hidden attacks. God sees the shadows.
S: Friends warned David that the wicked bend their bows and set arrows to shoot from the shadows at the upright. Faithfulness can attract hidden hostility, but God sees the shadows and gives His people faith as a shield. Take up that shield, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.
P: Hidden attack. Psalms 11:2: 'For, lo, the wicked bend their bow.' Shield. Quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. Walk in His ways today.

== 11:3

Notice that the friends saw social collapse. Notice that law and order were failing. Notice that they felt powerless. Notice that this is a real fear. Notice that societies do crumble. Notice that the righteous feel helpless then. Notice that the next verse answers. Notice that God's throne is not destroyed. Notice that the true foundation remains. Notice that Paul named it. Notice that it is Jesus Christ. Notice that no other foundation can be laid. Notice that it cannot be shaken. Notice that the righteous build there. Notice that they are not helpless. 1 Corinthians 3 says, 'For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.' Notice the sure foundation. Today, build on Christ when foundations around you shake. He cannot crumble.
S: The friends asked, when the foundations are being destroyed, what can the righteous do. Societies do crumble, but God's throne and the true foundation in Christ remain unshaken. Build on Him, for other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.
P: Foundations. Psalms 11:3: 'If the foundations be destroyed.' Sure foundation. Which is Jesus Christ. Let this shape your day. Take it to God in prayer.

== 11:4

R: 'The LORD is in his holy temple' Notice the LORD is in his holy temple. Present. Notice that His throne is in heaven. Reigning. Notice that His eyes behold. Watching. Notice that His eyelids test the children of men. Examining. Notice that this answers the fear. Notice that foundations may shake. Notice that God's throne does not. Notice that He is still in His temple. Notice that He still sees. Notice that He still tests. Notice that nothing has escaped Him. Notice that perspective changes everything. Notice that looking up steadies the heart. Notice that Isaiah heard God speak of His throne. Notice that heaven is His throne. Notice that earth is His footstool. Notice that no crisis reaches His seat. Isaiah 66 says, 'The heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool.' Notice the throne. Today, look up when the ground shakes. God is still on His throne.
S: David answered, the LORD is in His holy temple and on His heavenly throne; He observes everyone and examines them. When foundations shake, God's throne does not, and He still sees and tests all people. Look up and be steadied, for the heaven is My throne, and the earth is My footstool.
P: On His throne. Psalms 11:4: 'The LORD is in his holy temple.' Footstool. The heaven is my throne. Live it out today. Hold on to His word today.

== 11:5
T: The LORD trieth the righteous
R: 'The LORD trieth the righteous: but the wicked and him that loveth violence his soul hateth.' Notice the LORD trieth. Testing. Notice the righteous. The faithful. Notice but the wicked. Evildoers. Notice and him that loveth violence. Cruelty. Notice his soul hateth. Deep opposition. Notice that God tests the righteous. Notice that testing is not rejection. Notice that it refines. Notice that God opposes lovers of violence. Notice that cruelty is abhorrent to Him. Notice that the righteous are tested for growth. Notice that the wicked are opposed for judgment. Notice that the difference is purpose. Notice that James blessed those who endure. Notice that they receive a crown. Notice that testing leads to life. Notice that it is worth enduring. James 1 says, 'Blessed is the man that endureth temptation.' Notice blessed endurance. Today, see your trials as testing, not rejection. Endure for the crown.
S: David said the LORD examines the righteous, but the wicked and those who love violence He hates with passion. God tests the righteous to refine them, not to reject them, while He opposes cruelty. Endure your testing, for blessed is the man that endureth temptation: for when he is tried, he shall receive the crown of life.
P: Tested. Psalms 11:5: 'The LORD trieth the righteous.' Crown. Blessed is the man that endureth temptation. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.

Notice that Christ spoke of a cup. Notice that He prayed in the garden. Notice that He asked if it could pass. Notice that He drank it anyway. Notice that He drank the cup of judgment for us.
