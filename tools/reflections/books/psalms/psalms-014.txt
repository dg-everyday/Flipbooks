# Psalms 14: The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God

== 14:1
T: The fool hath said in his heart
R: 'The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God.' Notice the fool. Morally foolish. Notice hath said in his heart. Inner conviction. Notice there is no God. Denial. Notice that the psalm links unbelief and corruption. Notice that they are corrupt. Notice that none does good. Notice that the fool is not unintelligent. Notice that he is morally blind. Notice that the denial is in the heart. Notice that it shapes behaviour. Notice that living without God leads to corruption. Notice that wisdom begins with God. Notice that folly begins without Him. Notice that Paul described this. Notice that people claimed wisdom. Notice that they became fools. Notice that they exchanged God's glory. Romans 1 says, 'Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools.' Notice false wisdom. Today, guard your heart against living as if there is no God. Wisdom begins with Him.
S: The fool says in his heart there is no God, and such people are corrupt and do vile deeds. Denying God is a moral blindness of the heart rather than a lack of intelligence, and it shapes behaviour. Guard your heart, for professing themselves to be wise, they became fools.
P: The fool. Psalms 14:1: 'The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God.' False wisdom. Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools.

== 14:2
T: The LORD looked down from heaven
R: 'The LORD looked down from heaven upon the children of men, to see if there were any that did understand, and seek God.' Notice the LORD looked down. Divine survey. Notice from heaven. Above. Notice upon the children of men. Humanity. Notice to see if there were any. Searching. Notice that did understand. Wisdom. Notice and seek God. Pursuit. Notice that God searches humanity. Notice that He looks for seekers. Notice that He wants understanding. Notice that He values those who seek Him. Notice that this is a picture of longing. Notice that He looks for loyal hearts. Notice that He wants to strengthen them. Notice that God searches to help. Notice that seekers are noticed. Notice that we can be found seeking. 2 Chronicles 16 says, 'For the eyes of the LORD run to and fro throughout the whole earth, to shew himself strong.' Notice searching eyes. Today, be one God finds seeking Him. He looks to strengthen seekers.
S: The LORD looks down from heaven on all mankind to see if any understand and seek God. God searches humanity longing to find seekers, and His searching is to help and strengthen them. Be one He finds seeking, for the eyes of the LORD run to and fro throughout the whole earth, to shew Himself strong.
P: God searches. Psalms 14:2: 'to see if there were any that did understand, and seek God.' Strong. The eyes of the LORD run to and fro.

== 14:3
T: There is none that doeth good, no, not one
R: 'They are all gone aside, they are all together become filthy: there is none that doeth good, no, not one.' Notice they are all gone aside. Universal straying. Notice they are all together. Everyone. Notice become filthy. Corrupt. Notice there is none that doeth good. No exceptions. Notice no, not one. Emphatic. Notice that this is a sweeping verdict. Notice that it includes everyone. Notice that no one is good by nature. Notice that this humbles all. Notice that Paul quoted this. Notice that he used it to show universal need. Notice that righteousness comes as a gift. Notice that it comes through faith in Christ. Notice that the bad news prepares for good news. Notice that no one is beyond need. Notice that no one is beyond grace. Romans 3 says, 'Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.' Notice free justification. Today, admit your need and receive grace. None is good, but grace is for all.
S: The psalm says all have turned away and become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one. This sweeping verdict humbles everyone and prepares for the good news Paul drew from it. Admit your need and receive His gift, being justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.
P: Not one. Psalms 14:3: 'there is none that doeth good, no, not one.' Grace. Justified freely by his grace. Trust Him with it today.

== 14:4
T: And call not upon the LORD
R: 'and call not upon the LORD.' Notice that the psalmist asked if evildoers have no knowledge. Ignorance. Notice that they devour God's people. Cruelty. Notice and call not. No prayer. Notice upon the LORD. God ignored. Notice that cruelty and prayerlessness go together. Notice that the wicked do not call on God. Notice that they lack true knowledge. Notice that ignorance of God harms others. Notice that prayerlessness reveals the heart. Notice that knowing God leads to prayer. Notice that Hosea lamented lack of knowledge. Notice that people were destroyed by it. Notice that they rejected knowledge. Notice that knowing God is life. Notice that calling on Him is evidence of knowing. Notice that prayer protects. Notice that it keeps hearts soft. Hosea 4 says, 'My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.' Notice destructive ignorance. Today, call on the LORD. Prayer is evidence of knowing Him.
S: The psalmist asked whether evildoers never learn, those who devour God's people and never call on the LORD. Cruelty and prayerlessness go together, revealing a heart that lacks true knowledge of God. Keep calling on Him, for My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.
P: No prayer. Psalms 14:4: 'and call not upon the LORD.' Knowledge. My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. Walk in His ways today.

== 14:5
T: God is in the generation of the righteous
R: 'There were they in great fear: for God is in the generation of the righteous.' Notice there were they. The wicked. Notice in great fear. Terror. Notice for God. The reason. Notice is in the generation of the righteous. With His people. Notice that the oppressors were overwhelmed with dread. Notice that God stood with the righteous. Notice that His presence changed the balance. Notice that the wicked feared God's nearness. Notice that the righteous found comfort in it. Notice that the same presence brings fear and peace. Notice that God dwells among His people. Notice that this is their security. Notice that Jesus promised presence. Notice that He is among those gathered in His name. Notice that even two or three are enough. Notice that His presence is real. Notice that it protects. Matthew 18 says, 'there am I in the midst of them.' Notice Christ present. Today, take comfort that God is with His people. His presence is your security.
S: The psalmist said the wicked are overwhelmed with dread, for God is present in the company of the righteous. God's nearness terrifies oppressors but comforts His people, who find security in His presence. Take comfort in it, for Jesus said, where two or three are gathered together in My name, there am I in the midst of them.
P: God present. Psalms 14:5: 'for God is in the generation of the righteous.' In the midst. There am I in the midst of them.

== 14:6
T: The LORD is his refuge
R: 'Ye have shamed the counsel of the poor, because the LORD is his refuge.' Notice ye have shamed. Mocked. Notice the counsel of the poor. Their plans. Notice because the LORD. The reason. Notice is his refuge. Shelter. Notice that the wicked mock the plans of the poor. Notice that they ridicule their trust in God. Notice that faith looks foolish to scoffers. Notice that the poor still have refuge. Notice that God shelters them. Notice that mockery cannot remove it. Notice that God often chooses the humble. Notice that Paul said so. Notice that God chose the foolish things. Notice that He confounds the wise. Notice that the mocked are vindicated. Notice that trust in God is never foolish. Notice that it is the wisest refuge. 1 Corinthians 1 says, 'But God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise.' Notice God's choosing. Today, do not be shamed out of trusting God. He is your refuge.
S: The psalmist told evildoers that they frustrate the plans of the poor, but the LORD is their refuge. Scoffers mock simple trust in God, yet He shelters the humble and often chooses them to confound the proud. Do not be shamed out of trusting Him, for God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise.
P: Refuge. Psalms 14:6: 'because the LORD is his refuge.' Chosen. God hath chosen the foolish things of the world. Let this shape your day.
