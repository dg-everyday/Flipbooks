Notice that the psalm begins with a short prayer. Notice that trust is the ground. Notice that David asked for protection. Notice that he had already taken refuge. Notice that trust leads to asking. Notice that asking expresses trust. Notice that this psalm will end in joy. Notice that it begins in dependence. Notice that dependence is the door to joy. Notice that another psalm prays similarly. Notice that it asks God to keep the soul. Notice that it asks not to be ashamed. Notice that trust is never put to shame. Psalm 25 says, 'O keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I put my trust in thee.' Notice kept soul. Today, ask God to keep you because you trust Him. Dependence opens the door to joy.
S: David prayed, keep me safe, my God, for in You I take refuge. Trust leads to asking and asking expresses trust, and this psalm of dependence will end in fullness of joy. Ask Him to keep you, praying, O keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I put my trust in Thee.
