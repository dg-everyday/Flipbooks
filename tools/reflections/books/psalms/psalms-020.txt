# Psalms 20: We will remember the name of the LORD our God

== 20:1
T: The LORD hear thee in the day of trouble
R: 'The LORD hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee' Notice the LORD hear thee. A blessing. Notice in the day of trouble. Crisis. Notice the name of the God of Jacob. Covenant God. Notice defend thee. Protect. Notice that this is a prayer for someone else. Notice that the people prayed for their king. Notice that intercession is love. Notice that we can pray this for others. Notice that God's name defends. Notice that His name is His character. Notice that the God of Jacob helped a flawed man. Notice that He helps flawed people still. Notice that Proverbs called His name a tower. Notice that the righteous run into it. Notice that they are safe. Notice that His name is strong. Proverbs 18 says, 'The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.' Notice the strong tower. Today, pray this blessing over someone in trouble. God's name defends.
S: The people prayed for their king, may the LORD answer you in distress and the name of the God of Jacob protect you. Intercession is love, and God's name, His revealed character, is a refuge for flawed people. Pray this over someone today, for the name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.
P: Day of trouble. Psalms 20:1: 'The LORD hear thee in the day of trouble.' Strong tower. The name of the LORD is a strong tower.
