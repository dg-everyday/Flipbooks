# Psalms 23: The LORD is my shepherd

== 23:1
T: The LORD is my shepherd
R: 'The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.' Notice the LORD. The covenant God. Notice is my shepherd. Personal care. Notice I shall not want. Needs met. Notice that David had been a shepherd. Notice that he knew what sheep needed. Notice that he saw himself as a sheep. Notice that God is the Shepherd. Notice that the word my makes it personal. Notice that the Almighty cares for one sheep. Notice that lack is answered by the Shepherd. Notice that it does not mean having everything. Notice that Jesus called Himself the good shepherd. Notice that He lays down His life for the sheep. Notice that He knows them by name. Notice that this is the deepest security. Notice that the Shepherd died for us. John 10 says, 'I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep.' Notice the good shepherd. Today, say the LORD is my shepherd. You shall not lack what you need.
S: David said, the LORD is my shepherd; I lack nothing. A former shepherd himself, he knew God personally cares for each sheep, a care fulfilled in Jesus, who laid down His life. Rest in His care, for Jesus said, I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth His life for the sheep.
P: My shepherd. Psalms 23:1: 'The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.' Good shepherd. The good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep.

== 23:2
T: He leadeth me beside the still waters
R: 'He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters.' Notice he maketh me to lie down. Rest. Notice in green pastures. Abundance. Notice he leadeth me. Guidance. Notice beside the still waters. Calm refreshment. Notice that sheep do not lie down when anxious. Notice that the Shepherd brings rest. Notice that He chooses the pasture. Notice that He leads to water. Notice that still water is safe to drink. Notice that sheep fear rushing streams. Notice that God knows our fears. Notice that He leads gently. Notice that rest is His gift. Notice that sometimes He makes us rest. Notice that Revelation shows the Lamb as shepherd. Notice that He leads to living fountains. Notice that God wipes away tears. Notice that rest is eternal. Revelation 7 says, 'shall feed them, and shall lead them unto living fountains of waters.' Notice living fountains. Today, let the Shepherd lead you to rest. He brings you to still waters.
S: David said God makes him lie down in green pastures and leads him beside quiet waters. Sheep cannot rest when anxious, so the Shepherd provides peace, abundance and safe refreshment. Let Him lead you to rest, for the Lamb shall feed them, and shall lead them unto living fountains of waters.
P: Still waters. Psalms 23:2: 'he leadeth me beside the still waters.' Living fountains. Lead them unto living fountains of waters. Trust Him with it today.

== 23:3
T: He restoreth my soul
R: 'He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness' Notice he restoreth my soul. Renewal. Notice he leadeth me. Guidance. Notice in the paths of righteousness. Right ways. Notice that He does it for His name's sake. His honour. Notice that the soul gets weary. Notice that the Shepherd restores it. Notice that wandering sheep are brought back. Notice that right paths are His leading. Notice that His reputation is at stake. Notice that He leads well because of who He is. Notice that restoration is personal. Notice that Jesus told of a lost sheep. Notice that the shepherd searched. Notice that he found it. Notice that he carried it home. Notice that he rejoiced. Notice that restoration brings joy to God. Notice that no sheep is too lost. Luke 15 says, 'And when he hath found it, he layeth it on his shoulders, rejoicing.' Notice the rejoicing shepherd. Today, let God restore your weary soul. He carries you home rejoicing.
S: David said God refreshes his soul and guides him along right paths for His name's sake. The Shepherd restores weary and wandering souls, carrying them home with joy. Let Him restore you, for when He hath found it, He layeth it on His shoulders, rejoicing.
P: Restored. Psalms 23:3: 'He restoreth my soul.' Carried home. He layeth it on his shoulders, rejoicing. Walk in His ways today. Let this shape your day.

== 23:4
T: Thou art with me
R: 'Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me' Notice yea, though I walk. Moving through. Notice through the valley. Low places. Notice of the shadow of death. Deep darkness. Notice I will fear no evil. Courage. Notice for thou art with me. Presence. Notice that His rod and staff comfort. Protection. Notice that the psalm moves from he to thou. Notice that in the valley God becomes personal. Notice that we walk through, not stay. Notice that He is with us always. Notice that He is with us to the end. Notice that no valley is too dark. If you are walking through grief or the shadow of death, please lean on God and on people who love you; you are not alone. Matthew 28 says, 'and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.' Notice constant presence. Today, walk through your valley with the Shepherd. He is with you.
S: David said, even though I walk through the darkest valley, I will fear no evil, for You are with me; Your rod and staff comfort me. In the valley the psalm turns from he to You, for God's presence defeats fear and we walk through rather than stay. If you are in grief or darkness, lean on God and others, for He said, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.
P: With me. Psalms 23:4: 'I will fear no evil: for thou art with me.' Always. Lo, I am with you alway. Take it to God in prayer.

== 23:5
T: My cup runneth over
R: 'Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over.' Notice thou preparest a table. Provision. Notice before me. Personal. Notice in the presence of mine enemies. Despite danger. Notice thou anointest my head with oil. Honour. Notice my cup runneth over. Abundance. Notice that the Shepherd becomes a Host. Notice that He sets a table. Notice that enemies watch but cannot stop it. Notice that God honours His guest. Notice that oil refreshes and welcomes. Notice that the cup overflows. Notice that it is generous. Notice that Revelation describes a great feast. Notice that it is the marriage supper. Notice that the Lamb is the host. Notice that guests are blessed. Notice that every table now points to that one. Revelation 19 says, 'Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb.' Notice the great feast. Today, receive God's overflowing provision. He prepares a table for you.
S: David said God prepares a table before him in the presence of his enemies, anoints his head with oil, and makes his cup overflow. The Shepherd becomes a generous Host whose provision is more than enough, pointing to the great feast of the Lamb. Receive His abundance, for blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb.
P: Overflowing. Psalms 23:5: 'my cup runneth over.' The feast. The marriage supper of the Lamb. Live it out today. Hold on to His word today.

== 23:6
T: Goodness and mercy shall follow me
R: 'Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the LORD for ever.' Notice surely. Certainty. Notice goodness and mercy. Twin companions. Notice shall follow me. Pursue. Notice all the days of my life. Every day. Notice and I will dwell. Live. Notice in the house of the LORD. God's home. Notice for ever. Eternally. Notice that goodness and mercy pursue us. Notice that they chase us down. Notice that they come behind like sheepdogs. Notice that they never stop. Notice that every day is covered. Notice that Jesus promised to prepare a place. Notice that He will come again. Notice that He will receive us. Notice that we will be where He is. John 14 says, 'I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also.' Notice the promised home. Today, look back and see goodness and mercy following you. Home is with God forever.
S: David ended, surely goodness and mercy will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the LORD forever. God's goodness and mercy pursue His people every day, and the journey ends at home with Him. Look ahead with hope, for Jesus said, I will come again, and receive you unto Myself; that where I am, there ye may be also.
P: Following. Psalms 23:6: 'Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me.' Home. Where I am, there ye may be also. Trust Him with it today.
