# Psalms 24: Who is this King of glory

== 24:1
T: The earth and the fulness thereof
R: 'and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.' Notice that the earth belongs to the LORD. Ownership. Notice and the fulness thereof. Everything in it. Notice the world. All lands. Notice and they that dwell therein. All people. Notice that God owns everything. Notice that we are stewards, not owners. Notice that every person belongs to Him. Notice that this humbles possessiveness. Notice that it dignifies every life. Notice that nothing is outside His claim. Notice that Paul said all things were created by Christ. Notice that they were made through Him. Notice that they were made for Him. Notice that He holds them together. Notice that creation has a purpose. Notice that it exists for Christ. Notice that we exist for Him too. Notice that this gives meaning. Colossians 1 says, 'all things were created by him, and for him.' Notice created for Him. Today, hold your possessions loosely. Everything belongs to God.
S: David said the earth and everything in it belongs to the LORD, the world and all who live in it. God owns everything, so we are stewards rather than owners, and every person belongs to Him. Hold things loosely, for all things were created by Him, and for Him.
P: All His. Psalms 24:1: 'the world, and they that dwell therein.' For Him. All things were created by him, and for him. Trust Him with it today.

== 24:2
T: He hath founded it upon the seas
R: 'For he hath founded it upon the seas, and established it upon the floods.' Notice for he hath founded it. Built. Notice upon the seas. Over waters. Notice and established it. Made firm. Notice upon the floods. Over chaos. Notice that God built the earth above the waters. Notice that He gave it stability. Notice that the seas symbolised chaos. Notice that God set the world firmly over them. Notice that creation is secure. Notice that order overcomes chaos. Notice that this is why He owns it. Notice that Genesis records the gathering of waters. Notice that dry land appeared. Notice that God spoke it. Notice that it obeyed. Notice that He saw it was good. Notice that our world rests on His word. Genesis 1 says, 'Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear.' Notice dry land. Today, trust the God who set the earth firm above chaos. Your life rests on His word.
S: David said God founded the earth on the seas and established it on the waters. God set the world firmly above the waters of chaos, which is why it belongs to Him. Trust His stable order, for He said, let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear.
P: Founded. Psalms 24:2: 'For he hath founded it upon the seas.' Dry land. Let the dry land appear. Walk in His ways today.

== 24:3
T: Who shall ascend into the hill of the LORD
R: 'Who shall ascend into the hill of the LORD' Notice who shall ascend. Question. Notice into the hill of the LORD. God's mountain. Notice that he asked who may stand in His holy place. Access. Notice that the owner of all is also holy. Notice that approaching Him is serious. Notice that not everyone can come casually. Notice that holiness requires readiness. Notice that this question humbles us. Notice that we cannot climb by our own merit. Notice that God provides the way. Notice that Christ ascended for us. Notice that He opens access. Notice that Hebrews urges holiness. Notice that without it no one sees the Lord. Notice that God makes us holy in Christ. Notice that we pursue it too. Notice that both grace and effort meet. Hebrews 12 says, 'and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord.' Notice holiness required. Today, ask God to make you ready to approach Him. Christ opens the way.
S: David asked who may ascend the mountain of the LORD and who may stand in His holy place. Approaching the holy God is serious, and we cannot climb by our own merit, though Christ opens the way and makes us holy. Pursue holiness in His strength, for without it no man shall see the Lord.
P: Ascend. Psalms 24:3: 'Who shall ascend into the hill of the LORD.' Holiness. Without which no man shall see the Lord. Let this shape your day.

== 24:4
T: Clean hands, and a pure heart
R: 'He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart; who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity, nor sworn deceitfully.' Notice he that hath clean hands. Right actions. Notice and a pure heart. Right motives. Notice who hath not lifted up his soul. Worship direction. Notice unto vanity. Idols. Notice nor sworn deceitfully. Honest words. Notice that actions and motives both matter. Notice that hands and heart are named. Notice that outer and inner life must be clean. Notice that idolatry is excluded. Notice that deceit is excluded. Notice that God wants integrity through and through. Notice that God cleanses. Notice that James called sinners to cleansing. Notice that he urged clean hands. Notice that he urged purified hearts. Notice that drawing near brings God near. Notice that cleansing is the path. James 4 says, 'Cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts, ye double minded.' Notice cleansing call. Today, ask God for clean hands and a pure heart. Draw near and He draws near.
S: David said the one who may stand before God has clean hands and a pure heart, does not trust in an idol and does not swear by what is false. Both actions and motives matter, and God cleanses those who come to Him. Answer His call, cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts, ye double minded.
P: Clean and pure. Psalms 24:4: 'He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart.' Cleanse. Cleanse your hands, ye sinners. Take it to God in prayer.
