R: 'For I said in my haste, I am cut off from before thine eyes: nevertheless thou heardest the voice of my supplications' Notice for I said in my haste. Panic. Notice I am cut off. Despair. Notice from before thine eyes. Feeling unseen. Notice nevertheless. Yet. Notice thou heardest. Answered. Notice the voice of my supplications. Prayers. Notice that David admitted a hasty despair. Notice that he thought God had cut him off. Notice that he was wrong. Notice that God heard all along. Notice that he still looked toward God. Notice that God heard him too. Notice that hasty despair is common. Notice that God's nevertheless overrules it. If you feel cut off from God in a moment of panic, please talk to someone you trust; your feelings may be speaking too quickly. Jonah 2 says, 'Then I said, I am cast out of thy sight; yet I will look again toward thy holy temple.' Notice looking again. Today, do not trust hasty despair. God hears even then.
S: David admitted, in my alarm I said, I am cut off from Your sight; yet You heard my cry for mercy. Panic and despair judge too quickly and speak falsely, but God's nevertheless overrules them. If you feel cut off, talk to someone, and say with Jonah, I am cast out of Thy sight; yet I will look again toward Thy holy temple.
P: Hasty despair. Psalms 31:22: 'I said in my haste, I am cut off.' Look again. Yet I will look again toward thy holy temple.

== 31:23
T: O love the LORD, all ye his saints
R: 'O love the LORD, all ye his saints: for the LORD preserveth the faithful' Notice O love the LORD. Command. Notice all ye his saints. Faithful people. Notice for the LORD preserveth. Protects. Notice the faithful. Loyal ones. Notice that He pays back the proud in full. Justice. Notice that David's experience became exhortation. Notice that he urged love for God. Notice that love is the response to rescue. Notice that God preserves the faithful. Notice that He repays the proud. Notice that love is commanded. Notice that Jesus named the great commandment. Notice that it is love for God. Notice that it is with all the heart. Notice that it is with all the soul. Notice that it is with all the mind. Notice that love is wholehearted. Matthew 22 says, 'Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.' Notice the great command. Today, love the LORD with all your heart. He preserves the faithful.
S: David urged, love the LORD, all His faithful people; the LORD preserves those who are true to Him, but the proud He pays back in full. Rescue stirs love, and love for God is the great command that sums up faith. Love Him wholly, for thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.
P: Love Him. Psalms 31:23: 'O love the LORD, all ye his saints.' Great command. Thou shalt love the Lord thy God. Hold on to His word today.

Notice all ye that hope. Expectant. Notice in the LORD. Object of hope. Notice that the psalm ends with encouragement. Notice that it is for all who hope. Notice that courage is commanded. Notice that strength is given. Notice that hope is the condition. Notice that God strengthens hopeful hearts. Notice that despair and hope fought in this psalm. Notice that hope won. Notice that Paul urged steadfastness. Notice that he said watch. Notice that he said stand fast. Notice that he said be strong. Notice that courage is shared. Notice that we encourage each other. 1 Corinthians 16 says, 'Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong.' Notice standing fast. Today, take courage as you hope in the LORD. He strengthens your heart.
S: David ended, be strong and take heart, all you who hope in the LORD. Despair and hope struggled throughout this psalm, and hope won, as God strengthens the hearts of all who hope in Him. Take courage, for watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong.
P: Courage. Psalms 31:24: 'Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart.' Stand fast. Watch ye, stand fast in the faith. Trust Him with it today.
