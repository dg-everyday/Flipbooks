# Psalms 42: As the hart panteth after the water brooks

== 42:1
T: As the hart panteth after the water brooks
R: 'As the hart panteth after the water brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God.' Notice as the hart. A deer. Notice panteth. Longs desperately. Notice after the water brooks. Streams. Notice so panteth my soul. Inner longing. Notice after thee, O God. The true source. Notice that this psalm opens Book Two. Notice that it begins with thirst. Notice that the writer longed for God. Notice that a thirsty deer cannot wait. Notice that its need is urgent. Notice that the soul has such a need. Notice that only God satisfies it. Notice that other streams run dry. Notice that Jesus offered living water. Notice that He stood and cried out. Notice that He invited the thirsty. Notice that He alone satisfies. Notice that the invitation stands. John 7 says, 'If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink.' Notice the invitation. Today, bring your thirst to God. Only He satisfies the soul.
S: The psalmist said, as the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul pants for You, my God. The soul has an urgent thirst that only God can satisfy, for other streams run dry. Bring your thirst to Christ, who said, if any man thirst, let him come unto Me, and drink.
P: Thirst. Psalms 42:1: 'As the hart panteth after the water brooks.' Come. If any man thirst, let him come unto me. Trust Him with it today.

R: 'My soul thirsteth for God, for the living God' Notice my soul thirsteth. Deep need. Notice for God. Not things. Notice for the living God. Real and active. Notice that the writer asked when he could come before God. Longing. Notice that his thirst was specific. Notice that he wanted God Himself. Notice that he did not want only blessings. Notice that God is living. Notice that He is not an idea. Notice that He responds. Notice that the psalmist felt far from worship. Notice that distance increased longing. Notice that thirst for God is a good sign. Notice that it shows life. Notice that Jesus blessed such hunger. Notice that seekers are filled. Notice that longing is answered. Matthew 5 says, 'Blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled.' Notice filled seekers. Today, let your thirst draw you to the living God. Longing for Him will be filled.
S: The psalmist said, my soul thirsts for God, for the living God, and asked when he could go and meet with Him. He longed not only for blessings but for God Himself, and such thirst is a sign of spiritual life. Keep seeking, for blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled.
P: Living God. Psalms 42:2: 'My soul thirsteth for God, for the living God.' Filled. They shall be filled. Walk in His ways today.

Notice that they questioned his faith. Notice that grief can be all-consuming. Notice that God does not dismiss tears. Notice that He keeps them. If you have been weeping day and night, please reach out to someone you trust or a counsellor, and let them stand with you. Notice that another psalm says God records tears. Notice that none are lost. Notice that He cares for each. Psalm 56 says, 'put thou my tears into thy bottle' Notice treasured tears. Today, know that God keeps your tears. None are lost to Him.
S: The psalmist said his tears had been his food day and night while people asked him, where is your God. Grief can be all-consuming, so if you have been weeping day and night, please reach out to someone you trust or a counsellor. God does not waste your tears, for it is written, put Thou my tears into Thy bottle.
P: Tears. Psalms 42:3: 'My tears have been my meat day and night.' Kept. Put thou my tears into thy bottle. Let this shape your day.
