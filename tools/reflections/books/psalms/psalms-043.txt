# Psalms 43: O send out thy light and thy truth

== 43:1
T: Plead my cause against an ungodly nation
R: 'Judge me, O God, and plead my cause against an ungodly nation' Notice judge me, O God. Vindicate. Notice and plead my cause. Defend. Notice against an ungodly nation. Opponents. Notice deliver me from the deceitful and unjust man. Rescue. Notice that this psalm continues the last. Notice that the same voice speaks. Notice that he asked God to defend him. Notice that he faced deceit. Notice that he faced injustice. Notice that he trusted God's court. Notice that God is the best advocate. Notice that He sees truth. Notice that He exposes lies. Notice that Micah trusted this. Notice that he admitted his own sin. Notice that he still trusted God's defence. Notice that God would bring him to light. Micah 7 says, 'until he plead my cause, and execute judgment for me' Notice God pleading. Today, let God plead your cause. He is the best advocate.
S: The psalmist prayed, vindicate me, my God, and plead my cause against an unfaithful nation; rescue me from deceitful and wicked people. God is the best advocate, seeing the truth and exposing lies. Trust Him to defend you, until He plead my cause, and execute judgment for me.
P: Plead. Psalms 43:1: 'Judge me, O God, and plead my cause.' Advocate. Until he plead my cause. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.

== 43:2
T: Thou art the God of my strength
R: 'For thou art the God of my strength' Notice for thou art. Confession. Notice the God of my strength. Fortress. Notice that he asked why God had rejected him. Honest pain. Notice that he asked why he mourned under oppression. Grief. Notice that the writer knew God was his strength. Notice that he still felt rejected. Notice that feelings and truth clashed. Notice that he held both honestly. Notice that he felt cast off. Notice that God does not cast off those who come. Notice that Jesus promised this. Notice that all who come are received. Notice that none are turned away. Notice that this is certain. If you feel rejected by God or by others, please talk with someone you trust, and remember Christ's promise. Notice that welcome is guaranteed. John 6 says, 'him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.' Notice welcome. Today, hold on to God as your strength. He will not cast you out.
S: The psalmist said, You are God my stronghold, yet asked why God had rejected him. Truth and feelings can clash, and if you feel rejected by God or others, please talk with someone you trust and remember Christ's promise. Come to Him, for him that cometh to Me I will in no wise cast out.
P: Strength. Psalms 43:2: 'For thou art the God of my strength.' Not cast out. I will in no wise cast out. Let this shape your day.

== 43:3
T: O send out thy light and thy truth
R: 'O send out thy light and thy truth: let them lead me' Notice O send out. Plea. Notice thy light. Guidance. Notice and thy truth. Faithfulness. Notice let them lead me. Guides. Notice that they bring him to God's holy hill. Destination. Notice that the writer asked for guides. Notice that light and truth would lead. Notice that they would lead to God. Notice that the destination was worship. Notice that confusion needs light. Notice that lies need truth. Notice that God sends both. Notice that Jesus is both. Notice that He is the way. Notice that He is the truth. Notice that He is the life. Notice that He leads to the Father. Notice that light and truth have a name. John 14 says, 'I am the way, the truth, and the life' Notice the truth. Today, ask God to send His light and truth to lead you. Christ is both.
S: The psalmist prayed, send me Your light and Your faithful care; let them lead me to Your holy mountain. Light and truth guide the confused to God, and both are found in Christ. Follow Him, for He said, I am the way, the truth, and the life.
P: Light and truth. Psalms 43:3: 'O send out thy light and thy truth: let them lead me.' The truth. I am the way, the truth, and the life.

Delight. Notice that he would praise with the harp. Music. Notice that the writer anticipated joy. Notice that God was his delight. Notice that worship was his goal. Notice that God Himself is the joy. Notice that it is not only His gifts. Notice that this joy exceeds. Notice that it surpasses all others. Notice that it outlasts sorrow. Notice that Peter described it. Notice that believers love Christ unseen. Notice that they believe. Notice that they rejoice. Notice that the joy is unspeakable. Notice that it is full of glory. 1 Peter 1 says, 'ye rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory' Notice unspeakable joy. Today, find your joy in God Himself. He is your exceeding joy.
S: The psalmist said, then I will go to the altar of God, to God, my joy and my delight, and praise Him with the lyre. God Himself is the deepest joy, surpassing and outlasting every sorrow. Find your joy in Him, for ye rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory.
P: Exceeding joy. Psalms 43:4: 'unto God my exceeding joy.' Unspeakable. Joy unspeakable and full of glory. Take it to God in prayer. Live it out today.

== 43:5
T: Hope in God
R: 'hope in God: for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God.' Notice hope in God. Command. Notice for I shall yet praise him. Certainty. Notice who is the health of my countenance. Saviour. Notice and my God. Personal. Notice that the refrain comes a third time. Notice that the struggle is long. Notice that hope is repeated. Notice that it is chosen each time. Notice that the psalm ends with hope. Notice that sorrow does not win. Notice that hope is waiting. Notice that waiting rests on God's word. If you are in a long season of discouragement, please keep reaching out for help and keep holding to hope. Notice that another psalm describes waiting. Notice that the soul waits. Notice that hope rests in His word. Psalm 130 says, 'I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope.' Notice waiting hope. Today, choose hope again. You will yet praise Him.
S: The psalmist asked his soul a third time why it was downcast and answered, put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him. Hope is chosen again and again through long struggles, and if you are in a long season of discouragement, please keep reaching out for help. Hold on, saying, I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in His word do I hope.
P: Hope. Psalms 43:5: 'hope in God: for I shall yet praise him.' Waiting. In his word do I hope. Hold on to His word today.
