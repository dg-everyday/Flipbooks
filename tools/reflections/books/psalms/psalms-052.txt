# Psalms 52: I trust in the mercy of God for ever and ever

== 52:1
T: The goodness of God endureth continually
R: 'the goodness of God endureth continually.' Notice the goodness of God. Steadfast love. Notice endureth continually. Never ends. Notice that David wrote this about Doeg the Edomite. Notice that Doeg betrayed David's hiding place to Saul. Notice that innocent priests and their families were killed as a result. Notice that this was a grave evil. Notice that the victims deserve to be remembered. Notice that David asked why a man would boast of evil. Notice that he set God's goodness against it. Notice that evil boasts briefly. Notice that God's goodness lasts all day, every day. Notice that this is the anchor. Notice that another psalm repeats it often. Notice that God is good. Notice that His mercy endures. Notice that it never ends. Psalm 136 says, 'O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.' Notice enduring mercy. Today, set God's lasting goodness against any boasting evil. His goodness never ends.
S: David asked why the mighty man boasts of evil, for the goodness of God endures all day long, writing after Doeg betrayed him and innocent priests were killed. Evil boasts briefly, but God's goodness is the lasting anchor. Hold to it, for O give thanks unto the LORD; for He is good: for His mercy endureth for ever.
P: Enduring goodness. Psalms 52:1: 'the goodness of God endureth continually.' Mercy. His mercy endureth for ever. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.

Notice working deceitfully. Treacherous. Notice that Doeg's words did terrible harm. Notice that his report led to many deaths. Notice that the tongue can cut like a blade. Notice that words have consequences. Notice that deceit makes them sharper. Notice that harm can be done by speech alone. Notice that this sobers us. Notice that James described the tongue. Notice that it is small. Notice that it is powerful. Notice that it can set a forest ablaze. Notice that it can defile the whole person. Notice that it needs God's taming. James 3 says, 'And the tongue is a fire, a world of iniquity' Notice the fiery tongue. Today, remember that words can cut. Guard your tongue.
S: David said the tongue of the deceiver plots destruction, like a sharpened razor working deceit. Doeg's words led to many deaths, showing that speech alone can do terrible harm. Guard your tongue, for the tongue is a fire, a world of iniquity.
P: Razor tongue. Psalms 52:2: 'Thy tongue deviseth mischiefs; like a sharp rasor.' Fire. The tongue is a fire. Let this shape your day.

P: Devouring words. Psalms 52:4: 'Thou lovest all devouring words.' Power. Death and life are in the power of the tongue. Live it out today.
