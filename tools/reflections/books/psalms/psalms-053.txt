# Psalms 53: The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God

== 53:1
T: The fool hath said in his heart
R: 'The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God.' Notice the fool. Morally foolish. Notice hath said in his heart. Inner choice. Notice there is no God. Denial. Notice that this psalm nearly repeats Psalm 14. Notice that God thought it worth saying twice. Notice that the fool denies God in the heart. Notice that it is not mainly an intellectual position. Notice that it is a moral choice. Notice that denying God frees people to do as they please. Notice that corruption follows. Notice that faith begins differently. Notice that it believes God exists. Notice that it believes He rewards seekers. Notice that Hebrews explains this. Notice that faith pleases God. Notice that coming to Him requires belief. Notice that seeking is rewarded. Hebrews 11 says, 'for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.' Notice rewarded seeking. Today, choose to believe and seek God. He rewards those who seek Him.
S: David said the fool says in his heart, there is no God, repeating a truth God thought worth saying twice. Denying God is more a moral choice than an argument, and corruption follows it. Choose faith instead, for he that cometh to God must believe that He is, and that He is a rewarder of them that diligently seek Him.
P: The fool. Psalms 53:1: 'The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God.' Believe. He that cometh to God must believe that he is.

== 53:2
T: To see if there were any that did seek God
R: 'God looked down from heaven upon the children of men, to see if there were any that did understand, that did seek God.' Notice God looked down. Searching. Notice from heaven. Above. Notice upon the children of men. Humanity. Notice to see if there were any. Seeking. Notice that did understand. Insight. Notice that did seek God. Pursuit. Notice that God searches humanity. Notice that He looks for seekers. Notice that He looks for understanding. Notice that He is not indifferent. Notice that He wants to be found. Notice that the search is His initiative. Notice that He came looking. Notice that Christ came to seek. Notice that He seeks the lost. Notice that He saves them. Notice that He found Zacchaeus. Notice that He still seeks. Luke 19 says, 'For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.' Notice the seeking Saviour. Today, respond to the God who seeks you. Christ came to find the lost.
S: David said God looks down from heaven on all mankind to see if there are any who understand, any who seek God. God is not indifferent but searches for people, and in Christ He came looking Himself. Respond to Him, for the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.
P: Seeking. Psalms 53:2: 'God looked down from heaven upon the children of men.' Lost. To seek and to save that which was lost.

== 53:3
T: There is none that doeth good, no, not one
R: 'Every one of them is gone back: they are altogether become filthy; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.' Notice every one of them. All. Notice is gone back. Turned away. Notice they are altogether become filthy. Corrupt. Notice there is none that doeth good. No one. Notice no, not one. Emphatic. Notice that the verdict is universal. Notice that everyone has turned away. Notice that no exception exists. Notice that this includes the religious. Notice that it includes us. Notice that it is humbling. Notice that it levels everyone. Notice that it prepares for grace. Notice that Paul quoted this. Notice that he used it to show universal need. Notice that Jew and Gentile alike need grace. Notice that no one is righteous alone. Notice that Christ is the answer. Romans 3 says, 'As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one' Notice universal need. Today, admit your need of grace. No one is righteous alone.
S: David said everyone has turned away, all have become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one. The verdict is universal and humbling, levelling everyone and preparing us for grace. Admit your need, for as it is written, there is none righteous, no, not one.
P: Not one. Psalms 53:3: 'there is none that doeth good, no, not one.' None righteous. There is none righteous, no, not one. Trust Him with it today.

== 53:4
T: They have not called upon God
R: 'they have not called upon God.' Notice they have not called. Silence. Notice upon God. Prayerlessness. Notice that evildoers devour God's people. Notice that they act without knowledge. Notice that they do not pray. Notice that prayerlessness reveals the heart. Notice that it shows independence from God. Notice that it shows the fool's belief. Notice that not calling on God is practical atheism. Notice that it can affect believers too. Notice that we can live as if God is absent. Notice that prayer acknowledges Him. Notice that it expresses dependence. Notice that James addressed prayerlessness. Notice that people lack. Notice that they do not ask. Notice that God waits to be asked. Notice that He gives generously. James 4 says, 'yet ye have not, because ye ask not.' Notice unasked prayers. Today, call upon God. Prayerlessness lives as if He is absent.
S: David asked whether evildoers never learn, those who devour God's people and never call on God. Prayerlessness is practical atheism that lives as if God were absent, a danger even for believers. Call on Him today, for ye have not, because ye ask not.
P: Not called. Psalms 53:4: 'they have not called upon God.' Ask. Ye have not, because ye ask not. Walk in His ways today.

== 53:5
T: In great fear, where no fear was
R: 'There were they in great fear, where no fear was' Notice there were they. The attackers. Notice in great fear. Terror. Notice where no fear was. No real cause. Notice that God scattered those who attacked His people. Notice that He put them to shame. Notice that the attackers became afraid. Notice that their fear had no visible cause. Notice that God brought it. Notice that the hunters became the hunted. Notice that God defends His people. Notice that this differs slightly from Psalm 14. Notice that here God acts against the attackers. Notice that His protection is active. Notice that Isaiah promised this. Notice that weapons formed fail. Notice that accusing tongues are answered. Notice that this is the heritage of God's servants. Isaiah 54 says, 'No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper' Notice failed weapons. Today, trust God's active protection. No weapon formed against you will prosper.
S: David said the attackers were overwhelmed with dread where there was nothing to dread, for God scattered those who came against His people. God actively defends His own, turning the hunters into the frightened. Trust Him, for no weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper.
P: Great fear. Psalms 53:5: 'There were they in great fear, where no fear was.' Protected. No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper.

== 53:6
T: When God bringeth back the captivity
R: 'When God bringeth back the captivity of his people, Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel shall be glad.' Notice when God bringeth back. Restoration. Notice the captivity. Exile. Notice of his people. His own. Notice Jacob shall rejoice. Joy. Notice and Israel shall be glad. Gladness. Notice that the psalm longs for salvation. Notice that it looks to God's restoring. Notice that captivity ends. Notice that joy returns. Notice that restoration is God's work. Notice that it brings gladness. Notice that God promised it through Jeremiah. Notice that He would be found. Notice that He would turn captivity. Notice that He would gather His people. Notice that He would bring them home. Notice that restoration is His heart. Notice that it reaches us. Jeremiah 29 says, 'and I will turn away your captivity' Notice turned captivity. Today, hope in God's restoration. He brings His people home.
S: David longed for salvation, saying when God restores His people, Jacob will rejoice and Israel be glad. Captivity ends and joy returns when God restores, for restoration is His heart. Hope in Him, for He says, I will be found of you, and I will turn away your captivity.
P: Restored. Psalms 53:6: 'When God bringeth back the captivity of his people.' Turned. I will turn away your captivity. Let this shape your day.
