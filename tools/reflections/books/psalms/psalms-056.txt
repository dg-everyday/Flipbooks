Notice that pressure was daily. Notice that he asked for mercy. Notice that he did not ask for strength first. Notice that mercy was his need. Notice that God understands weakness. Notice that Christ shares our feelings. Notice that He was tested. Notice that He knows our infirmities. Notice that He sympathises. Notice that mercy flows from Him. Hebrews 4 says, 'For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities' Notice sympathetic Christ. Today, ask God for mercy under daily pressure. Christ understands.

Under daily pressure he asked first for mercy, and Christ understands our weakness. Bring it to Him, for we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities.
P: Mercy. Psalms 56:1: 'Be merciful unto me, O God.' Touched. Touched with the feeling of our infirmities. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.
