Notice that quiet trust is strength. Notice that anxiety is not. Notice that waiting is restful. Notice that it is confident. Notice that Isaiah described this. Notice that returning brings salvation. Notice that rest brings it. Notice that quietness is strength. Notice that confidence is strength. Notice that this is God's way. Isaiah 30 says, 'in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength' Notice quiet strength. Today, let your soul rest quietly in God. Salvation comes from Him.
S: David said, truly my soul finds rest in God; my salvation comes from Him. Quiet trust is strength, while anxious striving is not. Rest in Him, for in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength.
P: Waiting. Psalms 62:1: 'Truly my soul waiteth upon God.' Quietness. In quietness and in confidence shall be your strength. Trust Him with it today.

Notice that David asked how long his enemies would attack. Notice that they assaulted a man. Notice that they themselves were unstable. Notice that they were like a leaning wall. Notice that they were about to fall. Notice that attackers seem strong. Notice that they are not. Notice that their foundation is weak. Notice that David's was strong. Notice that Jesus told of two houses. Notice that one was on sand. Notice that storms came. Notice that it fell. Notice that the fall was great. Matthew 7 says, 'and it fell: and great was the fall of it.' Notice the fall. Today, see that opposition rests on weak foundations. It will fall.
S: David asked how long his enemies would assault a man, for they themselves were like a leaning wall and a tottering fence. Attackers may seem strong but rest on weak foundations that will fall. Stand firm on God, for of the house on sand it is said, it fell: and great was the fall of it.
P: Tottering. Psalms 62:3: 'as a bowing wall shall ye be.' Fallen. And great was the fall of it. Let this shape your day.

Notice that words and hearts did not match. Notice that Selah follows. Pause. Notice that double speech is dangerous. Notice that it deceives. Notice that James addressed it. Notice that blessing and cursing come from one mouth. Notice that this should not be. Notice that one spring cannot give both. Notice that consistency matters. James 3 says, 'Out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing.' Notice inconsistent speech. Today, let your words match your heart. Do not bless outwardly and curse inwardly.
S: David said his enemies take delight in lies; with their mouths they bless, but in their hearts they curse. Double speech is hypocrisy that deceives, for words and heart should match. Let yours agree, for out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing, and this ought not so to be.
P: Double speech. Psalms 62:4: 'they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly.' Same mouth. Out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing.
