Outward dryness matched his inner thirst, and he sought God first and eagerly. Seek Him early, for I love them that love Me; and those that seek Me early shall find Me.
P: Early. Psalms 63:1: 'O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee.' Found. Those that seek me early shall find me.
