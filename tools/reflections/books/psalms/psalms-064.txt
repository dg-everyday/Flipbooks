# Psalms 64: The righteous shall be glad in the LORD

== 64:1

Notice from fear of the enemy. Dread. Notice that David asked for protection from fear. Notice that fear itself was a threat. Notice that dread can paralyse. Notice that God guards against it. Notice that He protects life. Notice that He protects peace of mind. Notice that prayer is the first step. Notice that freedom from fear is God's gift. Notice that Zacharias praised this. Notice that God rescues His people. Notice that they serve without fear. Notice that they serve in holiness. Notice that this lasts all their days. Notice that fearless service is the goal. Luke 1 says, 'that we being delivered out of the hand of our enemies might serve him without fear' Notice fearless service. Today, ask God to guard you from fear. He gives freedom to serve.
S: David prayed, hear me, my God, as I voice my complaint; protect my life from the threat of the enemy. Fear itself can be a threat, and God guards both our lives and our peace of mind. Ask for it, that we being delivered out of the hand of our enemies might serve Him without fear.
P: Preserve. Psalms 64:1: 'preserve my life from fear of the enemy.' Without fear. Might serve him without fear. Trust Him with it today.

Notice that verbal attacks wound. Notice that they are deliberate. Notice that they are prepared. Notice that God's people speak differently. Notice that Paul urged careful speech. Notice that no corrupt words should come out. Notice that words should build up. Notice that they should give grace. Notice that they should help hearers. S: David said his enemies sharpen their tongues like swords and aim cruel words like deadly arrows. Verbal attacks are deliberate and wounding, but God's people are called to speak differently. Build others up, and let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth.
P: Bitter words. Psalms 64:3: 'Who whet their tongue like a sword.' Good speech. Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth. Let this shape your day.

S: David said they shoot from ambush at the innocent, suddenly and without fear. Integrity does not prevent attack, but a clear conscience answers slander in time. Keep yours, having a good conscience, that they may be ashamed that falsely accuse your good conversation in Christ.
P: Ambush. Psalms 64:4: 'That they may shoot in secret at the perfect.' Conscience. Having a good conscience. Take it to God in prayer.

S: David said they encourage each other in evil plans and talk about hiding their snares, asking who will see them. Secrecy emboldens wrongdoing, but God sees all and hidden things come to light. Live openly, for there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed.
P: Who sees. Psalms 64:5: 'they commune of laying snares privily.' Revealed. There is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed. Live it out today.
