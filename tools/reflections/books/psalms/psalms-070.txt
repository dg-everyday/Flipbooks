# Psalms 70: Make haste, O God, to deliver me

== 70:1
T: Make haste, O God, to deliver me
R: 'Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O LORD.' Notice make haste. Hurry. Notice O God. Address. Notice to deliver me. Rescue. Notice make haste to help me. Urgent aid. Notice O LORD. Covenant name. Notice that this short psalm repeats words from Psalm 40. Notice that urgent prayers are worth repeating. Notice that David begged God to hurry. Notice that some prayers are short and urgent. Notice that God welcomes them. Notice that not every prayer must be long. Notice that a cry is enough. Notice that God is near in trouble. Notice that He is present help. Notice that another psalm says so. Notice that He is refuge. Notice that He is strength. Notice that He is very present. Psalm 46 says, 'a very present help in trouble.' Notice present help. Today, pray a short, urgent prayer if that is all you have. God is very present.
S: David prayed, hasten, O God, to save me; come quickly, LORD, to help me, repeating words from an earlier psalm. Short, urgent prayers are welcome, for not every prayer must be long. Cry out to Him, for He is a very present help in trouble.
P: Make haste. Psalms 70:1: 'Make haste, O God, to deliver me.' Present. A very present help in trouble. Trust Him with it today.

== 70:2
T: Let them be ashamed and confounded
R: 'Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul' Notice let them be ashamed. Shamed. Notice and confounded. Confused. Notice that seek after my soul. Enemies. Notice that they would be turned back. Notice that they would be put to confusion. Notice that David asked God to stop his attackers. Notice that he asked that their plans fail. Notice that he left it to God. Notice that those who trust God are not confounded. Notice that shame belongs to attackers. Notice that confidence belongs to the faithful. Notice that Isaiah's Servant said so. Notice that God helps Him. Notice that He will not be confounded. Notice that He sets His face like flint. Notice that He knows He will not be ashamed. Notice that this is Christ's confidence. Isaiah 50 says, 'For the Lord GOD will help me; therefore shall I not be confounded' Notice confident trust. Today, trust that God will not let you be confounded. Shame belongs to attackers.
S: David prayed, may those who want to take my life be put to shame and confusion; may all who desire my ruin be turned back in disgrace. He left his attackers to God, trusting that the faithful will not be confounded. Share that confidence, for the Lord GOD will help me; therefore shall I not be confounded.
P: Confounded. Psalms 70:2: 'Let them be ashamed and confounded.' Not confounded. Therefore shall I not be confounded. Walk in His ways today. Let this shape your day.

== 70:3
T: That say, Aha, aha
R: 'Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha.' Notice let them be turned back. Retreat. Notice for a reward of their shame. Consequence. Notice that say, Aha, aha. Mockers. Notice that mockers gloat. Notice that they jeer at suffering. Notice that David asked God to turn them back. Notice that mockery has consequences. Notice that it offends God. Notice that mocking the suffering is serious. Notice that it insults their Maker. Notice that Proverbs warns about this. Notice that mocking the poor reproaches God. Notice that gloating over calamity is punished. Notice that God identifies with sufferers. Notice that He defends them. Notice that mockery is not harmless. Proverbs 17 says, 'Whoso mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker' Notice mocking condemned. Today, never mock those who suffer. God defends them.
S: David prayed, may those who say to me, aha, aha, turn back because of their shame. Mocking the suffering is not harmless, for God identifies with them and defends them. Never join the mockers, for whoso mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker.
P: Aha. Psalms 70:3: 'that say, Aha, aha.' Mocking. Whoso mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker. Take it to God in prayer. Live it out today.

== 70:4
T: Let God be magnified
R: 'Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.' Notice let all those that seek thee. Seekers. Notice rejoice and be glad in thee. Joy. Notice and let such as love thy salvation. Lovers of rescue. Notice say continually. Always. Notice let God be magnified. Exalted. Notice that seekers rejoice. Notice that they magnify God. Notice that their constant word is praise. Notice that God's greatness is their theme. Notice that this contrasts with mockers. Notice that mockers say aha. Notice that seekers say let God be magnified. Notice that speech reveals hearts. Notice that Acts shows this happening. Notice that people heard about Jesus. Notice that fear fell on them. Notice that His name was magnified. Acts 19 says, 'and the name of the Lord Jesus was magnified.' Notice Christ magnified. Today, let your constant word be let God be magnified. Seekers rejoice.
S: David prayed, may all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who long for Your saving help always say, the LORD is great. While mockers say aha, seekers say let God be magnified, for speech reveals the heart. Make it your word, as when the name of the Lord Jesus was magnified.
P: Magnified. Psalms 70:4: 'say continually, Let God be magnified.' Jesus magnified. The name of the Lord Jesus was magnified. Hold on to His word today.

== 70:5
T: But I am poor and needy
R: 'But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer' Notice but I am poor and needy. Humble. Notice make haste unto me. Hurry. Notice O God. Address. Notice thou art my help. Aid. Notice and my deliverer. Rescuer. Notice that he asked the LORD not to delay. Notice that David admitted his poverty. Notice that he was needy. Notice that he asked God to hurry. Notice that he named God as helper. Notice that need is no barrier. Notice that it is a qualification. Notice that God helps the needy. Notice that Jesus blessed spiritual poverty. Notice that the poor in spirit are blessed. Notice that the kingdom is theirs. Notice that admitting need opens the door. Notice that pride closes it. Notice that humility receives. Matthew 5 says, 'Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.' Notice the poor in spirit. Today, admit your need to God. The poor in spirit are blessed.
S: David ended, but as for me, I am poor and needy; come quickly to me, O God; You are my help and my deliverer; LORD, do not delay. Admitting need is not a barrier but a qualification for God's help, while pride closes the door. Come humbly, for blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
P: Needy. Psalms 70:5: 'But I am poor and needy.' Poor in spirit. Blessed are the poor in spirit. Trust Him with it today.
