== 78:24
T: Rained down manna
R: 'And had rained down manna upon them to eat, and had given them of the corn of heaven.' Notice and had rained down. Poured. Notice manna upon them. Bread from heaven. Notice to eat. Food. Notice and had given them. Gift. Notice of the corn of heaven. Heavenly grain. Notice that God fed them from heaven. Notice that bread fell daily. Notice that it was a gift. Notice that it sustained them. Notice that it pointed forward. Notice that Christ is the true bread. Notice that He came from heaven. Notice that Jesus explained this. Notice that Moses did not give the bread. Notice that the Father gives it. Notice that the true bread is from heaven. Notice that it gives life. Notice that it is Christ. John 6 says, 'but my Father giveth you the true bread from heaven.' Notice true bread. Today, feed on Christ, the true bread from heaven. He gives life.
S: Asaph said God rained down manna for the people to eat; He gave them the grain of heaven. The daily bread from heaven pointed forward to Christ, the true bread who gives life. Feed on Him, for My Father giveth you the true bread from heaven.
