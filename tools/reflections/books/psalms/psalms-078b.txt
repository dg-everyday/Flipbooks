Notice and their flocks. Herds. Notice to hot thunderbolts. Lightning. Notice that livestock were struck. Notice that warning had been given. Notice that some Egyptians listened. Notice that they brought their animals inside. Notice that heeding God's word saved them. Notice that mercy was offered even to Egyptians. Notice that warning is grace. Notice that Exodus records the response. Notice that some feared God's word. Notice that they acted. Notice that they protected their servants. Notice that they protected their cattle. Notice that listening saves. Exodus 9 says, 'He that feared the word of the LORD among the servants of Pharaoh made his servants and his cattle flee into the houses' Notice heeded warning. Today, heed God's warnings. Listening saves.
S: Asaph said God gave over their cattle to the hail, their livestock to bolts of lightning. Yet warning came first, and Egyptians who heeded it were spared, for God's warnings are grace. Heed them, like those who feared the word of the LORD and made their servants and cattle flee into the houses.
