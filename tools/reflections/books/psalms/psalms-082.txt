# Psalms 82: Defend the poor and fatherless

== 82:1
T: God standeth in the congregation of the mighty
R: 'God standeth in the congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the gods.' Notice God standeth. Presides. Notice in the congregation of the mighty. Great assembly. Notice he judgeth. Renders judgment. Notice among the gods. Rulers. Notice that God presides over rulers. Notice that earthly judges are called gods here. Notice that it means those given authority. Notice that God stands over them. Notice that He judges the judges. Notice that no authority is above Him. Notice that power is delegated. Notice that it is accountable. Notice that Paul affirmed this. Notice that authority comes from God. Notice that rulers are ordained by Him. Notice that they answer to Him. Notice that this sobers the powerful. Romans 13 says, 'For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.' Notice delegated power. Today, remember that all authority answers to God. He judges the judges.
S: Asaph said God presides in the great assembly; He renders judgment among the rulers, whom the psalm calls gods. All earthly authority is delegated by God and accountable to Him, which sobers the powerful. Remember it, for there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.
P: God presides. Psalms 82:1: 'God standeth in the congregation of the mighty.' Ordained. The powers that be are ordained of God. Trust Him with it today.

== 82:2

Notice of the wicked. Evildoers. Notice that Selah follows. Pause. Notice that God confronts corrupt judges. Notice that they favoured the wicked. Notice that they judged unfairly. Notice that partiality is condemned. Notice that justice must be blind to status. Notice that favouritism corrupts. Notice that it harms the poor. Notice that James warned against partiality. Notice that faith cannot show favouritism. Notice that rich and poor must be treated alike. Notice that this is serious. Notice that God sees it. James 2 says, 'with respect of persons.' Notice no favouritism. Today, refuse to show favouritism. Treat the poor and the powerful with the same respect. God condemns partiality.
S: God asked the rulers, how long will you defend the unjust and show partiality to the wicked, and the psalmist paused. Favouritism corrupts justice and harms the poor, and God condemns it. Refuse it, for have not the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Lord of glory, with respect of persons.
P: Unjust. Psalms 82:2: 'How long will ye judge unjustly.' Partiality. With respect of persons. Walk in His ways today. Let this shape your day.

== 82:3

Suffering. Notice and needy. Poor. Notice that God gives rulers a clear task. Notice that they must defend the weak. Notice that they must uphold rights. Notice that justice is for the vulnerable. Notice that this is God's standard. Notice that it applies to all with power. Notice that it applies to us. Notice that Jeremiah gave this command. Notice that it was to the king. Notice that justice must be done. Notice that the robbed must be rescued. Notice that the oppressor must be stopped. Notice that God expects action. S: God told the rulers, defend the weak and the fatherless; uphold the cause of the poor and the oppressed. Justice is meant for the vulnerable, and everyone with power is called to act on it. Do your part, for execute ye judgment and righteousness, and deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor.
P: Defend. Psalms 82:3: 'Defend the poor and fatherless.' Deliver. Deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor. Take it to God in prayer.

Notice that they refuse to understand. Notice that society's foundations shake. Notice that injustice destabilises everything. Notice that darkness spreads. Notice that light is needed. Notice that Jesus warned about darkness. Notice that light was with them. Notice that they should walk while they had it. Notice that darkness confuses. Notice that walkers in darkness do not know where they go. Notice that light shows the way. John 12 says, 'for he that walketh in darkness knoweth not whither he goeth.' Notice lost walkers. == 82:6

Notice that it did not make them divine. Notice that it made them accountable. Notice that Jesus quoted this verse. Notice that He answered accusers. Notice that they said He claimed to be God. Notice that He pointed to this psalm. Notice that if rulers were called gods, how much more Himself. Notice that He was sent by the Father. Notice that He is truly God's Son. John 10 says, 'I said, Ye are gods' Notice Jesus' argument. Today, remember that authority is a trust. Christ is the true Son.
S: God said, I said, you are gods; you are all sons of the Most High, giving rulers dignity and responsibility rather than divinity. Jesus quoted this when accused of claiming to be God, pointing to Himself as the One truly sent by the Father. Remember that authority is a trust, for it is written, I said, Ye are gods.
