# Psalms 87: Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God

== 87:1
T: His foundation is in the holy mountains
R: 'His foundation is in the holy mountains.' Notice his foundation. God's base. Notice is in the holy mountains. Sacred heights. Notice that God founded His city. Notice that it rests on holy ground. Notice that foundations matter. Notice that they determine stability. Notice that God's city is secure. Notice that it is built by Him. Notice that it is built on holiness. Notice that believers have a foundation. Notice that it is Christ. Notice that Paul said so. Notice that no other foundation can be laid. Notice that Christ is the only one. Notice that everything rests on Him. Notice that it holds. Notice that it is sure. 1 Corinthians 3 says, 'For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.' Notice the only foundation. Today, build your life on Christ. He is the only foundation.
S: The sons of Korah said God has founded His city on the holy mountain. Foundations determine stability, and God's people rest on the sure foundation He has laid in Christ. Build on Him, for other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.
P: Foundation. Psalms 87:1: 'His foundation is in the holy mountains.' Christ. Which is Jesus Christ. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.

== 87:2
T: The LORD loveth the gates of Zion
R: 'The LORD loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.' Notice the LORD loveth. Affection. Notice the gates of Zion. His city. Notice more than all the dwellings. Above others. Notice of Jacob. Israel. Notice that God loves His city. Notice that He loves it specially. Notice that Zion represents His people. Notice that He loves His gathered people. Notice that this love is deep. Notice that it is chosen. Notice that it is particular. Notice that Christ loves His church. Notice that Paul said so. Notice that husbands should love like this. Notice that Christ gave Himself. Notice that it was for the church. Notice that His love is sacrificial. Notice that it is complete. Ephesians 5 says, 'even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it' Notice sacrificial love. Today, rejoice that God loves His people. Christ gave Himself for them.
S: The psalmist said the LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the other dwellings of Jacob. God loves His gathered people deeply, as Christ loved the church sacrificially. Rejoice in it, for Christ also loved the church, and gave Himself for it.
P: Loveth. Psalms 87:2: 'The LORD loveth the gates of Zion.' Gave Himself. Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it. Let this shape your day.

== 87:3

R: 'Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God.' Notice glorious things. Splendid words. Notice are spoken of thee. Said about. Notice O city of God. Zion. Notice that Selah follows. Pause. Notice that God's city is praised. Notice that glorious things are said. Notice that its future is bright. Notice that this points beyond Jerusalem. Notice that it points to God's eternal city. Notice that John saw it. Notice that it came down from heaven. Notice that it was new. Notice that it was holy. Notice that it was prepared. Notice that it was beautiful. Notice that it was like a bride. Notice that glorious things are true of it. Revelation 21 says, 'And I John saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven' Notice the holy city. Today, look forward to God's glorious city. Glorious things are spoken of it.
S: The psalmist said glorious things are said of you, city of God, and he paused. God's city points beyond earthly Jerusalem to the new Jerusalem John saw. Look forward to it, for John saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven.
P: Glorious. Psalms 87:3: 'Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God.' New Jerusalem. The holy city, new Jerusalem. Take it to God in prayer.

== 87:4
T: Rahab and Babylon to them that know me
R: 'I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me' Notice I will make mention. Record. Notice of Rahab. Egypt. Notice and Babylon. Old enemy. Notice to them that know me. Among God's people. Notice that Philistia, Tyre and Ethiopia are listed too. Notice that God registers former enemies. Notice that Egypt is counted. Notice that Babylon is counted. Notice that they become citizens. Notice that this is astonishing grace. Notice that enemies become family. Notice that God's city welcomes nations. Notice that Isaiah foresaw this. Notice that God blessed Egypt. Notice that He called it His people. Notice that He blessed Assyria. Notice that He called it His handiwork. Notice that former foes are embraced. Isaiah 19 says, 'Blessed be Egypt my people, and Assyria the work of my hands' Notice embraced foes. Today, rejoice that God welcomes former enemies. His city is for all nations.
S: God said, I will record Rahab and Babylon among those who acknowledge Me; Philistia too, and Tyre, along with Cush. Former enemies become citizens of God's city, an astonishing grace that welcomes all nations. Rejoice in it, for God said, blessed be Egypt My people, and Assyria the work of My hands.
P: Enemies welcomed. Psalms 87:4: 'I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me.' Blessed. Blessed be Egypt my people.

== 87:5
T: This and that man was born in her
R: 'And of Zion it shall be said, This and that man was born in her: and the highest himself shall establish her.' Notice and of Zion. God's city. Notice it shall be said. Declared. Notice this and that man. Many people. Notice was born in her. Citizens. Notice and the highest himself. God. Notice shall establish her. Make secure. Notice that people from everywhere are born in Zion. Notice that citizenship is by birth. Notice that it is spiritual birth. Notice that God establishes it. Notice that belonging is new birth. Notice that it is not ethnic. Notice that it is God's gift. Notice that Jesus told Nicodemus this. Notice that he must be born again. Notice that otherwise he cannot see the kingdom. Notice that new birth is required. Notice that it is from above. John 3 says, 'Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.' Notice new birth. Today, rejoice in being born into God's city. Belonging is new birth.
S: The psalmist said of Zion, this one and that one were born in her, and the Most High Himself will establish her. Citizenship in God's city comes by spiritual birth, a gift He gives to people from every nation. Rejoice in it, for except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.
P: Born there. Psalms 87:5: 'This and that man was born in her.' Born again. Except a man be born again. Live it out today.

== 87:6

Notice that this man was born there. Recorded. Notice that Selah follows. Pause. Notice that God keeps a register. Notice that He writes names. Notice that each citizen is recorded. Notice that this is personal. Notice that it is secure. Notice that names are written by God. Notice that Jesus told His disciples this. Notice that they rejoiced at power. Notice that He redirected their joy. Notice that names in heaven matter more. Notice that this is true joy. Notice that it is lasting. Luke 10 says, 'but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.' Notice written names. Today, rejoice that your name is written by God. That is true joy.
S: The psalmist said the LORD will write in the register of the peoples, this one was born in Zion, and he paused. God personally records every citizen of His city, a secure and lasting belonging. Rejoice in it, for rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.
P: Written. Psalms 87:6: 'The LORD shall count, when he writeth up the people.' Names. Your names are written in heaven. Hold on to His word today.

Notice that all springs are there. Notice that life flows from God. Notice that every source of joy is in Him. Notice that nothing outside satisfies. Notice that everything good is there. Notice that Isaiah sang of this. Notice that we draw water with joy. Notice that it comes from salvation's wells. Notice that the wells are deep. Notice that the joy is real. S: The psalmist ended, as they make music they will sing, all my fountains are in you. Every source of life and joy flows from God, so His city is full of song. Draw from Him, for with joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation.
P: Springs. Psalms 87:7: 'all my springs are in thee.' Wells. With joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation. Trust Him with it today.
