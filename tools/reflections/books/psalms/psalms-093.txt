# Psalms 93: The LORD reigneth, he is clothed with majesty

== 93:1
T: The LORD reigneth
R: 'The LORD reigneth, he is clothed with majesty; the LORD is clothed with strength' Notice the LORD reigneth. Rules. Notice he is clothed with majesty. Splendour. Notice the LORD is clothed with strength. Power. Notice that He has girded Himself. Ready. Notice that the world is established. Firm. Notice that it cannot be moved. Secure. Notice that this psalm declares God's reign. Notice that He is robed in majesty. Notice that He is armed with strength. Notice that the world is steady because He reigns. Notice that His rule is the foundation. Notice that heaven celebrates it. Notice that a great multitude shouts. Notice that it sounds like many waters. Notice that it sounds like thunder. Notice that God reigns. Revelation 19 says, 'Alleluia: for the Lord God omnipotent reigneth.' Notice heaven's shout. Today, rest in God's reign. The world is steady because He rules. He is robed in majesty.
S: The psalmist declared, the LORD reigns, He is robed in majesty; the LORD is robed in majesty and armed with strength; indeed, the world is established, firm and secure. The world is steady because God reigns in majesty and strength. Rest in it, for Alleluia: for the Lord God omnipotent reigneth.

Notice that the repetition builds intensity. Notice that floods represent threats. Notice that they represent chaos. Notice that they are real. Notice that God's house stands. Notice that Jesus told of two builders. Notice that rain fell. Notice that floods came. Notice that winds beat. Notice that the house on rock stood. Notice that foundation matters. Matthew 7 says, 'and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock.' Notice the standing house. Today, stand firm when floods roar. Build on the rock. It will not fall.
