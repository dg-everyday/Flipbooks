# Psalms 117: O praise the LORD, all ye nations

== 117:1
T: O praise the LORD, all ye nations
R: 'O praise the LORD, all ye nations: praise him, all ye people.' Notice O praise the LORD. Worship. Notice all ye nations. Every country. Notice praise him. Again. Notice all ye people. Everyone. Notice that this is the shortest psalm. Notice that it is also one of the widest. Notice that it calls every nation. Notice that no people is left out. Notice that Israel's God is the world's God. Notice that praise is global. Notice that the gospel fulfils this. Notice that Paul quoted this verse. Notice that he showed Gentiles were always included. Notice that God planned it from the start. Notice that mercy reaches all. Notice that all peoples praise. Romans 15 says, 'Praise the Lord, all ye Gentiles; and laud him, all ye people.' Notice Gentile praise. Today, praise God with every nation. His family is worldwide.
S: The psalmist said, praise the LORD, all you nations; extol Him, all you peoples. The shortest psalm is one of the widest, calling every nation to praise, which the gospel fulfils. Join the worldwide song, praise the Lord, all ye Gentiles; and laud Him, all ye people.
P: All nations. Psalms 117:1: 'O praise the LORD, all ye nations.' Gentiles. Praise the Lord, all ye Gentiles. Trust Him with it today.

== 117:2
T: His merciful kindness is great toward us
R: 'For his merciful kindness is great toward us: and the truth of the LORD endureth for ever. Praise ye the LORD.' Notice for his merciful kindness. Steadfast love. Notice is great toward us. Mighty. Notice and the truth of the LORD. Faithfulness. Notice endureth for ever. Lasting. Notice praise ye the LORD. Hallelujah. Notice that two reasons are given. Notice that love is great. Notice that faithfulness lasts. Notice that these are reasons for every nation. Notice that mercy prevails. Notice that truth endures. Notice that the shortest psalm holds the gospel's heart. Notice that John described Christ this way. Notice that He was full of grace. Notice that He was full of truth. Notice that both came through Him. Notice that mercy and truth meet. John 1 says, 'but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.' Notice grace and truth. Today, praise God for great mercy and lasting truth. Both came in Christ.
S: The psalmist said, great is His love toward us, and the faithfulness of the LORD endures forever; praise the LORD. The shortest psalm holds the heart of the gospel, for mercy and truth meet in God. Praise Him, for grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.
P: Merciful kindness. Psalms 117:2: 'For his merciful kindness is great toward us.' Grace and truth. But grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.
