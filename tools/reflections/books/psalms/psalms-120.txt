# Psalms 120: I am for peace

Notice that he was in the deep. Notice that he cried. Notice that God heard. Notice that his voice reached the temple. Jonah 2 says, 'I cried by reason of mine affliction unto the LORD, and he heard me' Notice heard from distress. Today, start your journey to God from where you are. Cry out. He hears.
S: The psalmist said, I call on the LORD in my distress, and He answers me. This first of the Songs of Ascents, sung by pilgrims going up to Jerusalem, begins in trouble and far from home. Start where you are, for I cried by reason of mine affliction unto the LORD, and He heard me.

Notice that some environments are hostile. Notice that God sees this. Notice that He offers peace. If you live in a hostile or unsafe home, please reach out to someone you trust or a support service.
