# Psalms 121: My help cometh from the LORD

== 121:1
T: I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills
R: 'I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help.' Notice I will lift up. Raise. Notice mine eyes. Gaze. Notice unto the hills. Mountains. Notice from whence. From where. Notice cometh my help. Aid arrives. Notice that pilgrims saw the hills on the road. Notice that hills could hide danger. Notice that they also pointed to Jerusalem. Notice that the psalmist looked up. Notice that he asked where help comes from. Notice that the hills are not the answer. Notice that the answer is the next verse. Notice that looking up is the first step. Notice that the psalmist looked up elsewhere. Notice that he lifted his eyes to God. Notice that God dwells in heaven. Notice that our eyes wait. Notice that we look for mercy. Psalms 123 says, 'Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens.' Notice lifted eyes. Today, lift your eyes. Ask where help comes from. Look beyond the hills.
S: The psalmist said, I lift up my eyes to the mountains, where does my help come from. Pilgrims on the road saw hills that could hide danger, and the psalmist looked up and asked where true help lies. Look beyond them, saying, unto Thee lift I up mine eyes, O Thou that dwellest in the heavens.
P: Lift up. Psalms 121:1: 'I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills.' Lifted. Unto thee lift I up mine eyes. Trust Him with it today.

== 121:2
T: My help cometh from the LORD
R: 'My help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth.' Notice my help. Aid. Notice cometh from the LORD. Source. Notice which made heaven. Creator above. Notice and earth. Creator below. Notice that help comes from the Maker. Notice that it does not come from hills. Notice that the Creator is above creation. Notice that His help is unlimited. Notice that nothing is too hard. Notice that the One who made all can help in all. Notice that this is the answer. Notice that Jeremiah said the same. Notice that God made heaven and earth. Notice that it was by great power. Notice that nothing is too hard. Notice that this grounds trust. Notice that power and help combine. Jeremiah 32 says, 'and there is nothing too hard for thee' Notice nothing too hard. Today, look to the Maker for help. Nothing is too hard for Him.
S: The psalmist answered, my help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth. Help comes not from the hills but from their Creator, whose power is unlimited. Trust Him, for Thou hast made the heaven and the earth by Thy great power, and there is nothing too hard for Thee.
P: Help. Psalms 121:2: 'My help cometh from the LORD.' Nothing too hard. And there is nothing too hard for thee. Walk in His ways today.

== 121:3
T: He that keepeth thee will not slumber
R: 'He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber.' Notice he will not suffer. Allow. Notice thy foot. Your step. Notice to be moved. Slip. Notice he that keepeth thee. Your guardian. Notice will not slumber. Never dozes. Notice that God watches every step. Notice that He guards from slipping. Notice that He never dozes off. Notice that pilgrims walked rough roads. Notice that feet could slip. Notice that God steadied them. Notice that the word keep appears six times here. Notice that keeping is the theme. Notice that Jude praised God's keeping. Notice that He keeps from falling. Notice that He presents faultless. Notice that it is with joy. Notice that glory is His. Jude 1 says, 'Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling' Notice kept from falling. Today, trust God to steady your steps. He never dozes off.
S: The psalmist said, He will not let your foot slip; He who watches over you will not slumber. God guards every step on the rough road, and the word keep runs through this whole psalm. Rest in it, for now unto Him that is able to keep you from falling.
P: Not slumber. Psalms 121:3: 'he that keepeth thee will not slumber.' Keep. Him that is able to keep you from falling. Let this shape your day.

== 121:4
T: He shall neither slumber nor sleep
R: 'Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.' Notice behold. Look. Notice he that keepeth Israel. Guardian of His people. Notice shall neither slumber. Doze. Notice nor sleep. Rest. Notice that God never sleeps. Notice that He is always on watch. Notice that we can sleep because He does not. Notice that worry keeps us awake. Notice that trust lets us rest. Notice that God's vigilance frees us. Notice that Elijah mocked Baal. Notice that he suggested Baal was asleep. Notice that false gods sleep. Notice that the true God does not. Notice that He always hears. Notice that He always acts. 1 Kings 18 says, 'or peradventure he sleepeth, and must be awaked.' Notice sleeping idols. Today, rest because God never sleeps. Let His watch free you to sleep.
S: The psalmist said, indeed, He who watches over Israel will neither slumber nor sleep. Because God never sleeps, we can rest, for His vigilance frees us from worry. Trust Him, unlike Baal, of whom Elijah mocked, or peradventure he sleepeth, and must be awaked.
P: Neither slumber. Psalms 121:4: 'he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.' Sleepeth. Or peradventure he sleepeth, and must be awaked. Take it to God in prayer.
