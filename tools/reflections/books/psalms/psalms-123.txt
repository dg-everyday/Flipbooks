# Psalms 123: Unto thee lift I up mine eyes

== 123:1
T: Unto thee lift I up mine eyes
R: 'Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens.' Notice unto thee. To You. Notice lift I up. Raise. Notice mine eyes. Gaze. Notice O thou that dwellest. Who lives. Notice in the heavens. Above. Notice that the psalmist looked up. Notice that he looked past the hills. Notice that he looked to God's throne. Notice that looking up is prayer. Notice that it lifts perspective. Notice that it reminds us who rules. Notice that eyes follow the heart. Notice that Jesus lifted His eyes. Notice that He prayed. Notice that He looked to heaven. Notice that He spoke to the Father. Notice that the hour had come. Notice that He trusted. John 17 says, 'and lifted up his eyes to heaven, and said, Father, the hour is come' Notice lifted eyes. Today, lift your eyes to God. Let looking up lift your perspective. He rules.
S: The psalmist said, I lift up my eyes to You, to You who sit enthroned in heaven. Looking up to God's throne is prayer that lifts our perspective and reminds us who rules. Look up like Jesus, who lifted up His eyes to heaven, and said, Father, the hour is come.
P: Lift up. Psalms 123:1: 'Unto thee lift I up mine eyes.' Heaven. And lifted up his eyes to heaven. Trust Him with it today.

== 123:2
T: So our eyes wait upon the LORD our God
R: 'so our eyes wait upon the LORD our God, until that he have mercy upon us.' Notice so our eyes. Gaze. Notice wait upon. Look attentively. Notice the LORD our God. Personal God. Notice until that. Until. Notice he have mercy. Shows grace. Notice upon us. To us. Notice that servants watch their master's hand. Notice that they wait for a signal. Notice that maids watch their mistress. Notice that attention is total. Notice that God's people watch Him. Notice that they wait for mercy. Notice that waiting is attentive. Notice that it is patient. Notice that Micah watched like this. Notice that he looked to the LORD. Notice that he waited. Notice that he trusted God to hear. Notice that patience was hope. Notice that God would answer. Micah 7 says, 'Therefore I will look unto the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation' Notice watching. Today, watch God attentively. Wait for His mercy.
S: The psalmist said, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master, so our eyes look to the LORD our God till He shows us His mercy. Waiting on God is patient, attentive watching for His signal of grace. Watch like Micah, therefore I will look unto the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation.
P: Wait. Psalms 123:2: 'so our eyes wait upon the LORD our God.' Look. Therefore I will look unto the LORD. Walk in His ways today.

== 123:3
T: Have mercy upon us, O LORD
R: 'Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt.' Notice have mercy. Grace. Notice upon us. To us. Notice O LORD. Address. Notice have mercy upon us. Repeated. Notice for we are exceedingly filled. Overloaded. Notice with contempt. Scorn. Notice that the plea is repeated. Notice that contempt was overwhelming. Notice that God's people were despised. Notice that they cried for mercy. Notice that mercy answers contempt. Notice that God's view outweighs others'. Notice that scorn wounds. Notice that blind Bartimaeus cried like this. Notice that he was told to be quiet. Notice that he cried more. Notice that Jesus stopped. Notice that He called him. Notice that mercy came. Mark 10 says, 'Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me.' Notice persistent mercy. Today, cry for mercy when contempt overwhelms. God's view outweighs others'.
S: The psalmist prayed, have mercy on us, LORD, have mercy on us, for we have endured no end of contempt. When others' scorn overwhelms, God's mercy answers it, and His view outweighs theirs. Cry out like Bartimaeus, Jesus, Thou Son of David, have mercy on me.
P: Have mercy. Psalms 123:3: 'Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy upon us.' Mercy. Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me.

== 123:4
T: The scorning of those that are at ease
R: 'Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, and with the contempt of the proud.' Notice our soul. Inner self. Notice is exceedingly filled. Overloaded. Notice with the scorning. Ridicule. Notice of those that are at ease. The comfortable. Notice and with the contempt. Disdain. Notice of the proud. Arrogant. Notice that comfortable people mocked. Notice that ease bred arrogance. Notice that the proud looked down. Notice that this hurts. Notice that God sees it. Notice that He lifts the humble. Notice that comfort can harden. Notice that Amos warned the comfortable. Notice that they were at ease in Zion. Notice that they were careless. Notice that they ignored others' pain. Notice that God noticed. Amos 6 says, 'Woe to them that are at ease in Zion' Notice the warning. Today, guard against comfort becoming contempt. Show mercy to the scorned.
S: The psalmist said, we have endured no end of ridicule from the arrogant, of contempt from the proud. Comfort can breed arrogance that looks down on others, and God sees the pain it causes. Guard against it, for woe to them that are at ease in Zion.
P: At ease. Psalms 123:4: 'the scorning of those that are at ease.' Woe. Woe to them that are at ease in Zion. Let this shape your day.
