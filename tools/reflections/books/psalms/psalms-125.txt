# Psalms 125: As the mountains are round about Jerusalem

== 125:1
T: As mount Zion, which cannot be removed
R: 'They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever.' Notice they that trust. Those who rely. Notice in the LORD. On God. Notice shall be as mount Zion. Like the mountain. Notice which cannot be removed. Unshakable. Notice but abideth for ever. Endures. Notice that trust makes people steady. Notice that they become like a mountain. Notice that mountains do not move. Notice that storms pass over them. Notice that trust gives stability. Notice that it is not stubbornness. Notice that it is rootedness in God. Notice that pilgrims saw Zion. Notice that it was firm. Notice that Isaiah described trust. Notice that God keeps the trusting. Notice that their mind is stayed. Notice that peace follows. Isaiah 26 says, 'Trust ye in the LORD for ever: for in the LORD JEHOVAH is everlasting strength' Notice everlasting strength. Today, trust God and become steady as a mountain. Storms pass over.
S: The psalmist said, those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion, which cannot be shaken but endures forever. Trust gives a mountain's stability, rooted in God rather than in stubbornness. Stand firm, for trust ye in the LORD for ever: for in the LORD JEHOVAH is everlasting strength.
P: Mount Zion. Psalms 125:1: 'They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion.' Strength. In the LORD JEHOVAH is everlasting strength.

== 125:2
T: The LORD is round about his people
R: 'As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.' Notice as the mountains. Hills. Notice are round about Jerusalem. Surround the city. Notice so the LORD. God. Notice is round about his people. Surrounds them. Notice from henceforth. From now. Notice even for ever. Always. Notice that Jerusalem was ringed by hills. Notice that pilgrims saw them. Notice that the hills protected the city. Notice that God surrounds His people likewise. Notice that He is on every side. Notice that no direction is unguarded. Notice that this is forever. Notice that Zechariah heard this promise. Notice that God would be a wall. Notice that it would be fire. Notice that He would be glory within. Notice that protection and presence combine. Zechariah 2 says, 'For I, saith the LORD, will be unto her a wall of fire round about' Notice wall of fire. Today, know God surrounds you on every side. Forever.
S: The psalmist said, as the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the LORD surrounds His people both now and forevermore. Pilgrims saw the ring of hills around the city and knew God guards His people on every side. Rest in it, for I, saith the LORD, will be unto her a wall of fire round about.
P: Round about. Psalms 125:2: 'so the LORD is round about his people.' Wall. I will be unto her a wall of fire round about.

== 125:3
T: The rod of the wicked shall not rest
R: 'For the rod of the wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous' Notice for the rod. Sceptre. Notice of the wicked. Evil rule. Notice shall not rest. Remain. Notice upon the lot. Land. Notice of the righteous. God's people. Notice that evil rule will not last. Notice that it may come. Notice that it will not stay. Notice that God limits it. Notice that He does so lest the righteous be tempted. Notice that prolonged evil can wear down good people. Notice that God knows our limits. Notice that He sets an end. Notice that Paul promised this. Notice that temptation is common. Notice that God is faithful. Notice that He will not allow too much. Notice that He makes a way. 1 Corinthians 10 says, 'but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able' Notice limited trial. Today, trust that evil will not rest on you forever. God knows your limits.
S: The psalmist said, the sceptre of the wicked will not remain over the land allotted to the righteous, lest they be tempted to do wrong. God limits how long evil rules, knowing that prolonged pressure can wear down good people. Trust Him, for God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able.
P: Shall not rest. Psalms 125:3: 'the rod of the wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous.' Faithful. But God is faithful.

== 125:4
T: Do good, O LORD, unto those that be good
R: 'Do good, O LORD, unto those that be good, and to them that are upright in their hearts.' Notice do good. Bless. Notice O LORD. Address. Notice unto those that be good. The faithful. Notice and to them. Also. Notice that are upright in their hearts. Sincere. Notice that the psalmist prayed for blessing on the faithful. Notice that he asked God to do good. Notice that uprightness of heart matters. Notice that it is inward. Notice that it is sincere. Notice that God sees the heart. Notice that He rewards. Notice that this prayer can be prayed for others. Notice that Paul prayed for good. Notice that he prayed God would fulfil goodness. Notice that it was in the Thessalonians. Notice that God works good. Notice that He completes it. 2 Thessalonians 1 says, 'and fulfil all the good pleasure of his goodness' Notice fulfilled goodness. Today, pray God's good on faithful people. He sees sincere hearts.
S: The psalmist prayed, LORD, do good to those who are good, to those who are upright in heart. God sees sincere, upright hearts and delights to bless them. Pray this for others, that God would fulfil all the good pleasure of His goodness.
P: Do good. Psalms 125:4: 'Do good, O LORD, unto those that be good.' Goodness. And fulfil all the good pleasure of his goodness.

== 125:5
T: But peace shall be upon Israel
R: 'but peace shall be upon Israel.' Notice but peace. Wholeness. Notice shall be upon Israel. On God's people. Notice that those who turn to crooked ways are led away. Notice that the crooked path leads elsewhere. Notice that the psalm ends with peace. Notice that peace is God's gift. Notice that it rests on His people. Notice that crooked ways forfeit it. Notice that straight paths find it. Notice that this is a blessing. Notice that Paul ended a letter with it. Notice that it was on those who walk by the rule. Notice that it was mercy too. Notice that it was on God's Israel. Notice that peace and mercy go together. Notice that God gives both. Galatians 6 says, 'peace be on them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.' Notice peace and mercy. Today, walk straight paths and receive God's peace. It rests on His people.
S: The psalmist said those who turn to crooked ways will be banished with the evildoers, but peace be on Israel. Crooked paths forfeit peace, while God's peace rests on those who walk straight. Receive it, peace be on them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.
P: Peace. Psalms 125:5: 'but peace shall be upon Israel.' Peace and mercy. Peace be on them, and mercy. Trust Him with it today.
