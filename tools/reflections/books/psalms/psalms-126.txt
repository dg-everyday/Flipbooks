# Psalms 126: They that sow in tears shall reap in joy

== 126:1
T: We were like them that dream
R: 'When the LORD turned again the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream.' Notice when the LORD. God. Notice turned again. Restored. Notice the captivity of Zion. Exile. Notice we were like them. As if. Notice that dream. Dreaming. Notice that restoration felt unreal. Notice that it was too good to believe. Notice that joy was dreamlike. Notice that God's rescue can surprise. Notice that it exceeds hope. Notice that it feels like a dream. Notice that it is real. Notice that Peter felt this. Notice that an angel freed him. Notice that he thought it was a vision. Notice that he did not know it was real. Notice that then he came to himself. Notice that it was true. Acts 12 says, 'and wist not that it was true which was done by the angel; but thought he saw a vision.' Notice unreal rescue. Today, let God surprise you with restoration. It is real, not a dream.
S: The psalmist said, when the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion, we were like those who dreamed. God's rescue can exceed our hopes so much that it feels unreal. Let Him surprise you, like Peter, who wist not that it was true which was done by the angel; but thought he saw a vision.
P: Dream. Psalms 126:1: 'we were like them that dream.' Vision. But thought he saw a vision. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.

Notice that the world would rejoice. Notice that their sorrow would turn. Notice that it would become joy. John 16 says, 'but your sorrow shall be turned into joy.' Notice turned sorrow. Notice that harvest follows. Notice that tears become sheaves. Notice that Paul spoke of sowing. Notice that we do not grow weary. Notice that we will reap. Notice that it is in due season.
