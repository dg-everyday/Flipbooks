# Psalms 127: Except the LORD build the house

== 127:1
T: Except the LORD build the house
R: 'Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it' Notice except the LORD. Unless God. Notice build the house. Constructs. Notice they labour in vain. Work for nothing. Notice that build it. Builders. Notice that Solomon wrote this psalm. Notice that he built the temple. Notice that he knew God must build. Notice that human effort alone is vain. Notice that the watchman also needs God. Notice that security depends on Him. Notice that this is not laziness. Notice that it is dependence. Notice that we still build. Notice that God makes it last. Notice that Jesus spoke of foundations. Notice that some build on sand. Notice that some build on rock. Notice that hearing and doing matter. Matthew 7 says, 'which built his house upon a rock' Notice the rock. Today, invite God into what you build. Without Him it is vain.
S: Solomon wrote, unless the LORD builds the house, the builders labour in vain; unless the LORD watches over the city, the guards stand watch in vain. Human effort still matters, but only God makes what we build last. Build on Him, like the wise man which built his house upon a rock.
P: Build. Psalms 127:1: 'Except the LORD build the house.' Rock. Which built his house upon a rock. Trust Him with it today. Walk in His ways today.

== 127:2
T: He giveth his beloved sleep
R: 'It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep.' Notice it is vain. Useless. Notice for you to rise up early. Wake early. Notice to sit up late. Stay up. Notice to eat the bread of sorrows. Anxious toil. Notice for so he giveth. God provides. Notice his beloved sleep. Rest. Notice that anxious overwork is futile. Notice that God gives rest to those He loves. Notice that sleep is a gift. Notice that it is trust. If overwork or worry is costing you sleep, please talk to someone you trust or a doctor. Notice that Jesus slept in a storm. Notice that He trusted the Father. Notice that He rested. Notice that peace allowed sleep. Mark 4 says, 'And he was in the hinder part of the ship, asleep on a pillow' Notice peaceful sleep. Today, trust God enough to rest. Sleep is His gift.
S: Solomon wrote, in vain you rise early and stay up late, toiling for food to eat, for He grants sleep to those He loves. Anxious overwork is futile, and rest is a gift of trust, so if overwork or worry is costing you sleep, please talk to someone you trust or a doctor. Rest like Jesus, who in the storm was in the hinder part of the ship, asleep on a pillow.
P: Sleep. Psalms 127:2: 'for so he giveth his beloved sleep.' Asleep. Asleep on a pillow. Let this shape your day. Take it to God in prayer.
H: #DailyGrace #Psalms1272 #Rest #Trust #Sleep #BibleVerse

== 127:3
T: Children are an heritage of the LORD
R: 'Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD: and the fruit of the womb is his reward.' Notice lo. Look. Notice children. Sons and daughters. Notice are an heritage. Inheritance. Notice of the LORD. From God. Notice and the fruit of the womb. Offspring. Notice is his reward. Gift. Notice that children are gifts. Notice that they come from God. Notice that they are not property. Notice that they are entrusted. Notice that each child is precious. Notice that this is not a judgment on those without children. Notice that God's love does not depend on family size. If you long for children or grieve a loss, please lean on people you trust or a counsellor. Notice that Jesus welcomed children. Notice that disciples turned them away. Notice that He was displeased. Notice that He took them in His arms. Mark 10 says, 'Suffer the little children to come unto me' Notice welcomed children. Today, treasure children as gifts. God's love is not measured by family size.
S: Solomon wrote, children are a heritage from the LORD, offspring a reward from Him. Each child is a precious gift entrusted by God, yet this is no judgment on those without children, so if you long for children or grieve a loss, please lean on people you trust or a counsellor. Welcome children as Jesus did, saying, suffer the little children to come unto Me.
P: Heritage. Psalms 127:3: 'Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD.' Little children. Suffer the little children to come unto me. Live it out today.

== 127:4
T: As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man
R: 'As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man; so are children of the youth.' Notice as arrows. Like arrows. Notice are in the hand. Held. Notice of a mighty man. Warrior. Notice so are children. Likewise. Notice of the youth. Born young. Notice that children are like arrows. Notice that arrows are shaped. Notice that they are aimed. Notice that they are released. Notice that parents shape and send. Notice that children go farther than parents. Notice that they carry influence into the future. Notice that this is a picture of purpose. Notice that it is not about war. Notice that Proverbs spoke of training. Notice that we train a child in the way. Notice that it should go. Notice that when old, it stays. Proverbs 22 says, 'Train up a child in the way he should go' Notice training. Today, invest in the next generation. Shape and send them well.
S: Solomon wrote, like arrows in the hands of a warrior are children born in one's youth. Arrows are shaped, aimed and released, as children are raised and sent farther than their parents can go. Invest in them, for train up a child in the way he should go.
P: Arrows. Psalms 127:4: 'As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man.' Train. Train up a child in the way he should go.

== 127:5
T: Happy is the man that hath his quiver full
R: 'Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them' Notice happy is the man. Blessed. Notice that hath his quiver. Holder. Notice full of them. Many children. Notice that children are a blessing. Notice that they stand with their parents. Notice that they speak in the gate. Notice that family gives support. Notice that it is a strength. Notice that this is a general blessing. Notice that not everyone receives it this way. Notice that God gives spiritual family too. Notice that no one need be alone. Notice that Jesus redefined family. Notice that He pointed to His disciples. Notice that they were His family. Notice that doing God's will makes family. Notice that the church is family. Mark 3 says, 'For whosoever shall do the will of God, the same is my brother, and my sister, and mother.' Notice God's family. Today, thank God for family, natural and spiritual. No one need be alone.
S: Solomon wrote, blessed is the man whose quiver is full of them; they will not be put to shame when they contend with their opponents in court. Family gives strength and support, and God also gives spiritual family so no one need be alone. Belong to it, for whosoever shall do the will of God, the same is My brother, and My sister, and mother.
P: Quiver. Psalms 127:5: 'Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them.' Brother. Whosoever shall do the will of God, the same is my brother.
