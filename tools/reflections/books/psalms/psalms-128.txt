# Psalms 128: Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD

== 128:1
T: Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD
R: 'Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD; that walketh in his ways.' Notice blessed is every one. All. Notice that feareth the LORD. Reveres. Notice that walketh. Lives. Notice in his ways. God's paths. Notice that the blessing is for everyone. Notice that it is not limited by status. Notice that reverence leads to blessing. Notice that walking shows it. Notice that faith is lived daily. Notice that it is practical. Notice that it is ordinary. Notice that this psalm pictures home life. Notice that blessing reaches the table. Notice that Micah summarised God's ways. Notice that we do justly. Notice that we love mercy. Notice that we walk humbly. Notice that this is what God asks. Micah 6 says, 'and to walk humbly with thy God' Notice humble walking. Today, walk in God's ways in ordinary life. Blessing is for everyone who fears Him.
S: The psalmist said, blessed are all who fear the LORD, who walk in obedience to Him. The blessing is open to everyone and is lived out daily in ordinary life, even at home. Walk this way, to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God.
P: Blessed. Psalms 128:1: 'Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD.' Walk. And to walk humbly with thy God. Trust Him with it today.

== 128:3

Notice that the wife is pictured as a fruitful vine. Notice that home life is pictured as growth. Notice that the table is the centre. Notice that olive shoots grow slowly. Notice that they live long. Notice that they bear fruit for generations. Notice that this is one picture of blessing. Notice that not every home looks this way. Notice that single people and childless couples are equally loved. Notice that God's family welcomes all. Notice that Jesus ate with many. Notice that tables included outsiders. Notice that He welcomed sinners. Notice that the table was wide. Luke 15 says, 'This man receiveth sinners, and eateth with them.' Notice the wide table. Today, gather around your table with thanks. Make room for others.
S: The psalmist said, your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house; your children will be like olive shoots around your table. Notice that it reaches the home. Notice that it also reaches the community. Notice that personal and public blessing go together. Notice that we see the good of others. Notice that it lasts a lifetime. Notice that God's blessing is wide. Notice that David hoped for this. Notice that goodness would follow. Notice that mercy would follow.
