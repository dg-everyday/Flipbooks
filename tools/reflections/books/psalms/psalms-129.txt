# Psalms 129: Yet they have not prevailed against me

== 129:1
T: Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth
R: 'Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth, may Israel now say' Notice many a time. Often. Notice have they afflicted me. Oppressed. Notice from my youth. Since early days. Notice may Israel now say. Let all confess. Notice that Israel looked back on long suffering. Notice that it began early. Notice that it was repeated. Notice that the nation spoke as one person. Notice that honesty about suffering is healthy. Notice that it is named. Notice that it is not hidden. Notice that it is spoken together. Notice that God's people have often suffered. Notice that the early believers did too. Notice that they were scattered. Notice that they preached as they went. Notice that suffering did not stop them. Notice that the word spread. Acts 8 says, 'Therefore they that were scattered abroad went every where preaching the word.' Notice scattered but preaching. Today, name your long struggles honestly. God's people have endured before.
S: The psalmist said, they have greatly oppressed me from my youth, let Israel say. God's people have often suffered long and early, and naming it honestly together is healthy. Take heart from those who suffered before, for they that were scattered abroad went every where preaching the word.
P: Afflicted. Psalms 129:1: 'Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth.' Scattered. They that were scattered abroad went every where preaching.

== 129:2
T: Yet they have not prevailed against me
R: 'Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth: yet they have not prevailed against me.' Notice many a time. Often. Notice have they afflicted me. Oppressed. Notice from my youth. Long ago. Notice yet they have not prevailed. Did not win. Notice against me. Over me. Notice that oppression was long. Notice that it did not win. Notice that yet is the turning word. Notice that survival is victory. Notice that God preserved His people. Notice that they are still here. Notice that endurance testifies. Notice that Jesus promised this about His church. Notice that He would build it. Notice that it is on rock. Notice that hell's gates would not prevail. Notice that the church endures. Notice that God keeps it. Matthew 16 says, 'and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.' Notice not prevailing. Today, remember that oppression has not won. God preserves His people. You are still here.
S: The psalmist said, they have greatly oppressed me from my youth, but they have not gained the victory over me. Long oppression did not win, for God preserved His people and their survival testifies. Take heart, for the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.
P: Not prevailed. Psalms 129:2: 'yet they have not prevailed against me.' Prevail. The gates of hell shall not prevail against it. Trust Him with it today.

== 129:3
T: The plowers plowed upon my back
R: 'The plowers plowed upon my back: they made long their furrows.' Notice the plowers. Oppressors. Notice plowed upon my back. Deep wounds. Notice they made long. Extended. Notice their furrows. Cuts. Notice that this pictures cruel treatment. Notice that oppression left marks. Notice that the pain was deep. Notice that it was long. Notice that God saw it. Notice that it is recorded with dignity. Notice that victims are honoured. Notice that harm is named as harm. If you have been physically harmed or abused, please reach out to someone you trust or authorities who can protect you. Notice that this points to Christ. Notice that He was scourged. Notice that His back bore stripes. Notice that by them we are healed. Isaiah 53 says, 'and with his stripes we are healed.' Notice healing stripes. Today, know God sees every wound. Christ bore stripes. Seek help if harmed.
S: The psalmist said, plowmen have ploughed my back and made their furrows long, picturing cruel treatment that left deep marks. God sees such harm and honours those who suffer it, so if you have been physically harmed or abused, please reach out to someone you trust or authorities who can protect you. Christ bore wounds too, and with His stripes we are healed.
P: Plowed. Psalms 129:3: 'The plowers plowed upon my back.' Stripes. And with his stripes we are healed. Walk in His ways today. Let this shape your day.

== 129:4
T: He hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked
R: 'The LORD is righteous: he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked.' Notice the LORD is righteous. Just. Notice he hath cut asunder. Severed. Notice the cords. Ropes. Notice of the wicked. Oppressors. Notice that God cut the ropes. Notice that the plough was harnessed with cords. Notice that cutting them stopped the ploughing. Notice that God ended the oppression. Notice that His righteousness acted. Notice that justice freed the victim. Notice that bondage was broken. Notice that God is just. Notice that Isaiah spoke of this. Notice that God's chosen fast loosens bands. Notice that it undoes burdens. Notice that it frees the oppressed. Notice that it breaks every yoke. Isaiah 58 says, 'to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free' Notice loosed bands. Today, trust God to cut the cords that bind you. Join His work of freeing others.
S: The psalmist said, the LORD is righteous; He has cut me free from the cords of the wicked. Cutting the plough's ropes ended the ploughing, as God's justice ends oppression. Join His work, to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free.
P: Cut asunder. Psalms 129:4: 'he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked.' Loose. To loose the bands of wickedness. Take it to God in prayer.

Notice that this is a prayer against those who hate Zion. Notice that it asks God to turn them back. Notice that it hands justice to God. Notice that it is recorded, not a model of hatred. Notice that it does not take up arms. Notice that it asks for failure, not destruction. Notice that it is about stopping oppression. Notice that Christians pray differently now. Notice that we pray for enemies. Notice that we pray they turn. Notice that Jesus taught this. Notice that we love enemies. Notice that we bless. Notice that we pray. Matthew 5 says, 'Love your enemies, bless them that curse you' Notice enemy love. Today, pray that oppression fails and oppressors turn. Hand justice to God.
S: The psalmist prayed, may all who hate Zion be turned back in shame, handing justice to God and asking that oppression fail. This is recorded honestly, not as a model of hatred, and Christians now pray for enemies to turn. Follow Christ, who said, love your enemies, bless them that curse you.
P: Turned back. Psalms 129:5: 'Let them all be confounded and turned back.' Love. Love your enemies, bless them that curse you. Live it out today.

== 129:6
T: As the grass upon the housetops
R: 'Let them be as the grass upon the housetops, which withereth afore it groweth up' Notice let them be. May they be. Notice as the grass. Like grass. Notice upon the housetops. On flat roofs. Notice which withereth. Dries. Notice afore it groweth up. Before maturing. Notice that rooftop grass had no deep soil. Notice that it sprouted fast. Notice that it died fast. Notice that oppression is pictured as shallow. Notice that it lacks roots. Notice that it does not last. Notice that God's people have deep roots. Notice that they endure. Notice that Jesus spoke of shallow soil. Notice that seed sprang up quickly. Notice that it had no root. Notice that the sun scorched it. Notice that it withered. Matthew 13 says, 'and because they had no root, they withered away.' Notice rootless. Today, put down deep roots in God. Shallow things wither.
S: The psalmist said, may they be like grass on the roof, which withers before it can grow. Rooftop grass sprouted fast but had no soil, picturing how oppression lacks roots and does not last. Put down deep roots, unlike the seed of which it was said, because they had no root, they withered away.
P: Housetops. Psalms 129:6: 'Let them be as the grass upon the housetops.' No root. Because they had no root, they withered away. Hold on to His word today.

Notice that harvest was a time of blessing. Notice that oppression earns no such greeting. Notice that righteousness does. Notice that blessing is spoken. Notice that it is shared. Notice that Boaz greeted his workers. Notice that he said the LORD be with you. Notice that they answered. Notice that they blessed him back. Ruth 2 says, 'The LORD be with you. And they answered him, The LORD bless thee.' Notice harvest blessing. Today, speak a blessing over someone at work. Share God's favour.

Oppression earns no blessing, but righteousness is greeted with God's favour, spoken and shared. Bless others, as Boaz said to his reapers, the LORD be with you.
