# Psalms 131: As a weaned child

== 131:1
T: My heart is not haughty
R: 'LORD, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty' Notice LORD. Address. Notice my heart is not haughty. Not proud. Notice nor mine eyes. Gaze. Notice lofty. Arrogant. Notice that David renounced pride. Notice that he did not reach for things too great. Notice that he accepted limits. Notice that humility is restful. Notice that it does not need to know everything. Notice that it trusts God with mysteries. Notice that it is freeing. Notice that this is a short psalm. Notice that it is deep. Notice that Paul urged humble thinking. Notice that we should not think too highly. Notice that we think soberly. Notice that faith is measured. Notice that God gives it. Romans 12 says, 'not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think' Notice sober thinking. Today, set down pride. Accept your limits. Trust God with what is too high for you.
S: David said, my heart is not proud, LORD, my eyes are not haughty; I do not concern myself with great matters or things too wonderful for me. Humility accepts limits and trusts God with mysteries, which is restful and freeing. Think soberly, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think.
P: Not haughty. Psalms 131:1: 'LORD, my heart is not haughty.' Highly. Not to think of himself more highly than he ought. Trust Him with it today.

== 131:2
T: As a child that is weaned of his mother
R: 'Surely I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child that is weaned of his mother: my soul is even as a weaned child.' Notice surely I have behaved. Calmed. Notice and quieted myself. Stilled. Notice as a child. Like a child. Notice that is weaned. No longer nursing. Notice of his mother. With her. Notice my soul. Inner self. Notice is even as a weaned child. Content. Notice that a weaned child is calm with its mother. Notice that it no longer fusses for milk. Notice that it simply rests. Notice that it is content to be near. Notice that David's soul was like this. Notice that he rested in God's presence. Notice that this is maturity. Notice that Jesus valued childlike trust. Notice that we must become as children. Notice that it is the way in. Notice that humility enters. Matthew 18 says, 'Except ye be converted, and become as little children' Notice childlike trust. Today, quiet your soul. Rest near God without demanding. Be content.
S: David said, I have calmed and quieted myself; I am like a weaned child with its mother. A weaned child rests contentedly near its mother without fussing, and his soul rested in God the same way. Trust like a child, for except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.
P: Weaned child. Psalms 131:2: 'my soul is even as a weaned child.' Little children. Become as little children. Walk in His ways today.

== 131:3
T: Let Israel hope in the LORD
R: 'Let Israel hope in the LORD from henceforth and for ever.' Notice let Israel. All God's people. Notice hope. Trust. Notice in the LORD. God. Notice from henceforth. From now. Notice and for ever. Always. Notice that personal calm becomes communal invitation. Notice that David invited all to rest. Notice that hope is the response to humility. Notice that it is lasting. Notice that it starts now. Notice that it never ends. Notice that quiet trust is hope. Notice that Paul prayed for hope to abound. Notice that God is the God of hope. Notice that He fills with joy. Notice that He fills with peace. Notice that it is in believing. Notice that hope overflows. Romans 15 says, 'Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing' Notice God of hope. Today, hope in God from now on. Let quiet trust become lasting hope.
S: David said, Israel, put your hope in the LORD both now and forevermore. His personal calm became an invitation for all God's people to rest in lasting hope. Receive it, now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing.
P: Hope. Psalms 131:3: 'Let Israel hope in the LORD from henceforth and for ever.' God of hope. Now the God of hope fill you with all joy.
