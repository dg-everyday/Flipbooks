# Psalms 133: How good and how pleasant it is

== 133:1
T: Brethren to dwell together in unity
R: 'Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity' Notice behold. Look. Notice how good. Beneficial. Notice and how pleasant. Delightful. Notice it is for brethren. Family. Notice to dwell together. Live as one. Notice in unity. Harmony. Notice that unity is both good and pleasant. Notice that it is right. Notice that it is enjoyable. Notice that it is rare. Notice that it is worth pursuing. Notice that pilgrims from many tribes gathered. Notice that they lived as one. Notice that this was beautiful. Notice that Jesus prayed for unity. Notice that He asked that all be one. Notice that it would be like the Father and Son. Notice that the world would believe. Notice that unity is witness. John 17 says, 'That they all may be one' Notice prayed unity. Today, pursue unity with fellow believers. It is good and pleasant. It is a witness.
S: David said, how good and pleasant it is when God's people live together in unity. Unity is both right and delightful, rare and worth pursuing, as pilgrims from many tribes discovered. Pursue it, for Jesus prayed, that they all may be one.
P: Unity. Psalms 133:1: 'how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity.' One. That they all may be one.

== 133:2
T: Like the precious ointment upon the head
R: 'It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard' Notice it is like. Comparison. Notice the precious ointment. Costly oil. Notice upon the head. Poured. Notice that ran down. Flowed. Notice upon the beard. Downward. Notice that this pictures Aaron's anointing. Notice that oil flowed down to his robes. Notice that it was generous. Notice that it covered everything. Notice that unity flows the same way. Notice that it spreads from leaders down. Notice that it blesses everyone. Notice that it is fragrant. Notice that it is consecrating. Notice that Exodus records the anointing. Notice that oil was poured. Notice that Aaron was set apart. Notice that he was sanctified. Notice that it was holy. Exodus 29 says, 'and pour it upon his head, and anoint him.' Notice anointing. Today, let unity flow generously. It blesses everyone it touches.
S: David said unity is like precious oil poured on the head, running down on the beard, running down on Aaron's beard, down on the collar of his robe. Like the generous oil of Aaron's anointing, unity flows down and blesses everyone it touches. Let it flow, as when Moses was told, and pour it upon his head, and anoint him.
P: Ointment. Psalms 133:2: 'It is like the precious ointment upon the head.' Anoint. And pour it upon his head, and anoint him. Trust Him with it today.

== 133:3
T: There the LORD commanded the blessing
R: 'As the dew of Hermon, and as the dew that descended upon the mountains of Zion: for there the LORD commanded the blessing, even life for evermore.' Notice as the dew. Moisture. Notice of Hermon. Northern mountain. Notice and as the dew. Likewise. Notice that descended. Came down. Notice upon the mountains of Zion. Jerusalem. Notice for there. In unity. Notice the LORD commanded. Ordained. Notice the blessing. Favour. Notice even life for evermore. Eternal life. Notice that Hermon's dew was abundant. Notice that it refreshed. Notice that unity refreshes like dew. Notice that God commands blessing where unity is. Notice that life flows there. Notice that this is powerful. Notice that the early church showed it. Notice that they were of one heart. Notice that they shared. Notice that great grace was on them. Acts 4 says, 'And the multitude of them that believed were of one heart and of one soul' Notice one heart. Today, pursue unity. God commands blessing there.
S: David said unity is like the dew of Hermon falling on Mount Zion, for there the LORD bestows His blessing, even life forevermore. Unity refreshes like abundant dew, and God commands blessing where His people dwell as one. Seek it, for the multitude of them that believed were of one heart and of one soul.
P: Blessing. Psalms 133:3: 'for there the LORD commanded the blessing, even life for evermore.' One heart. Of one heart and of one soul.
