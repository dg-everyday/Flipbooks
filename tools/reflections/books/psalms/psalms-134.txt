# Psalms 134: Bless ye the LORD

== 134:1
T: Which by night stand in the house of the LORD
R: 'Behold, bless ye the LORD, all ye servants of the LORD, which by night stand in the house of the LORD.' Notice behold. Look. Notice bless ye the LORD. Praise God. Notice all ye servants. Every servant. Notice of the LORD. God's own. Notice which by night. During darkness. Notice stand in the house. Serve. Notice of the LORD. God's temple. Notice that this ends the Songs of Ascents. Notice that pilgrims leaving called to night workers. Notice that some served while others slept. Notice that night service is unseen. Notice that it is still worship. Notice that faithful work in hidden hours matters. Notice that God sees it. Notice that Anna served this way. Notice that she was elderly. Notice that she did not leave the temple. Notice that she served night and day. Notice that she prayed and fasted. Luke 2 says, 'but served God with fastings and prayers night and day.' Notice night service. Today, bless God in hidden hours. Unseen service is worship.
S: The psalmist said, praise the LORD, all you servants of the LORD who minister by night in the house of the LORD. This last Song of Ascents honours those who served unseen while others slept, for hidden faithfulness is worship. Serve like Anna, who served God with fastings and prayers night and day.
P: By night. Psalms 134:1: 'which by night stand in the house of the LORD.' Night and day. But served God with fastings and prayers night and day.

== 134:2
T: Lift up your hands in the sanctuary
R: 'Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the LORD.' Notice lift up. Raise. Notice your hands. Worship posture. Notice in the sanctuary. Holy place. Notice and bless the LORD. Praise God. Notice that worship involves the body. Notice that hands are raised. Notice that blessing God is an act. Notice that it is deliberate. Notice that it is wholehearted. Notice that night servants could still worship. Notice that duty became praise. Notice that work became worship. Notice that Nehemiah's people did this. Notice that Ezra blessed God. Notice that the people answered amen. Notice that they lifted their hands. Notice that they bowed. Notice that worship was whole-bodied. Nehemiah 8 says, 'And all the people answered, Amen, Amen, with lifting up their hands' Notice lifted hands. Today, let your work become worship. Lift your hands and bless the LORD.
S: The psalmist said, lift up your hands in the sanctuary and praise the LORD. Worship involves the body and turns ordinary duty into praise. Bless Him wholeheartedly, as when all the people answered, Amen, Amen, with lifting up their hands.
P: Lift up. Psalms 134:2: 'Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the LORD.' Amen. All the people answered, Amen, Amen. Trust Him with it today.

== 134:3
T: The LORD that made heaven and earth bless thee
R: 'The LORD that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion.' Notice the LORD. God. Notice that made heaven and earth. Creator. Notice bless thee. Favour you. Notice out of Zion. From His dwelling. Notice that the night servants answered. Notice that they blessed the pilgrims back. Notice that blessing flowed both ways. Notice that the Songs of Ascents end with blessing. Notice that the journey began in distress. Notice that it ends in blessing. Notice that the Creator blesses. Notice that the pilgrimage is complete. Notice that blessing sends people home. Notice that Aaron's blessing did this. Notice that God would bless. Notice that He would keep. Notice that His face would shine. Notice that He would give peace. Numbers 6 says, 'The LORD bless thee, and keep thee' Notice sending blessing. Today, receive God's blessing and pass it on. The journey ends in blessing.
S: The psalmist said, may the LORD bless you from Zion, He who is the Maker of heaven and earth. The Songs of Ascents began in distress and end in blessing exchanged between pilgrims and servants. Receive and share it, the LORD bless thee, and keep thee.
P: Bless thee. Psalms 134:3: 'The LORD that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion.' Keep thee. The LORD bless thee, and keep thee.
