# Psalms 137: By the rivers of Babylon

== 137:1
T: By the rivers of Babylon
R: 'By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat down, yea, we wept, when we remembered Zion.' Notice by the rivers. Waterside. Notice of Babylon. Exile. Notice there we sat down. Stopped. Notice yea, we wept. Cried. Notice when we remembered Zion. Home. Notice that this is a song of exiles. Notice that they had lost everything. Notice that their city was destroyed. Notice that they were taken far away. Notice that they sat and wept. Notice that grief is honest. Notice that memory hurt. Notice that weeping is allowed. If you are grieving a loss of home, family or homeland, please lean on people you trust or a counsellor. Notice that Jesus blessed mourners. Notice that they will be comforted. Notice that grief is seen. Notice that comfort is promised. Matthew 5 says, 'Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.' Notice comforted mourners. Today, let yourself grieve. God sees your tears. Comfort is promised.
S: The psalmist said, by the rivers of Babylon we sat and wept when we remembered Zion. This song of exiles who lost their city and home shows that honest grief is allowed, so if you are grieving a loss of home, family or homeland, please lean on people you trust or a counsellor. God sees, for blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.
P: Wept. Psalms 137:1: 'By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat down, yea, we wept.' Mourn. Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.

Notice that worship felt impossible far from home. Notice that the question is honest. Notice that the answer came later. Notice that God was with them in Babylon too. Notice that Daniel prayed there. Notice that Ezekiel saw visions there. Notice that worship is not bound to a place. Notice that God is everywhere. Notice that Jesus said this. Notice that true worship is not tied to a mountain. Notice that it is not tied to a city. Notice that it is in spirit. Notice that it is in truth. John 4 says, 'neither in this mountain, nor yet at Jerusalem, worship the Father.' Notice worship anywhere. Today, know you can worship anywhere. God is with you in strange lands.
S: The psalmist asked, how can we sing the songs of the LORD while in a foreign land. The honest question was answered later, for God was with His people in Babylon and worship is not bound to one place. Worship anywhere, for the hour cometh, when ye shall neither in this mountain, nor yet at Jerusalem, worship the Father.
