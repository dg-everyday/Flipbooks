T: Search me, O God, and know my heart
R: 'Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts' Notice search me. Examine. Notice O God. Address. Notice and know my heart. Understand. Notice try me. Test. Notice and know my thoughts. Anxieties. Notice that David turned from others to himself. Notice that after talking of enemies he asked God to search him. Notice that this is humility. Notice that it guards against self-righteousness. Notice that we need God to see what we miss. Notice that the heart can deceive itself. Notice that God knows it fully. Notice that this invites cleansing. Notice that Jeremiah said the heart is deceitful. Notice that it is desperately wicked. Notice that no one can know it. Notice that God searches it. Jeremiah 17 says, 'I the LORD search the heart, I try the reins' Notice searching God. Today, ask God to search your heart. Let Him show you what you miss.
S: David prayed, search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts. After speaking of enemies he turned the light on himself, guarding against self-righteousness, for our hearts can deceive us. Invite God's search, for I the LORD search the heart, I try the reins.
P: Search me. Psalms 139:23: 'Search me, O God, and know my heart.' Search. I the LORD search the heart. Live it out today.

== 139:24
T: Lead me in the way everlasting
R: 'And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.' Notice and see. Look. Notice if there be any wicked way. Offensive path. Notice in me. Within. Notice and lead me. Guide. Notice in the way everlasting. Eternal path. Notice that David asked God to find any wrong in him. Notice that he wanted it exposed. Notice that he wanted to be led. Notice that the psalm ends in surrender. Notice that being fully known leads to being fully led. Notice that the everlasting way is God's way. Notice that it leads to life. Notice that Jesus is the way. Notice that He said so. Notice that He is the truth. Notice that He is the life. Notice that He leads to the Father. John 14 says, 'I am the way, the truth, and the life' Notice the way. Today, ask God to lead you in the everlasting way. Follow Jesus.
S: David closed, see if there is any offensive way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting. Being fully known by God leads to surrender and to being fully led in His everlasting way. Follow Christ, who said, I am the way, the truth, and the life.
P: Way everlasting. Psalms 139:24: 'and lead me in the way everlasting.' The way. I am the way, the truth, and the life. Hold on to His word today.
