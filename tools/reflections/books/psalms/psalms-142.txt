# Psalms 142: Thou art my refuge and my portion

Notice with my voice. Repeated. Notice unto the LORD. To God. Notice did I make my supplication. Pleaded. Notice that this psalm was prayed in a cave. Notice that David was hiding. Notice that he was hunted. Notice that he prayed aloud. Notice that his voice mattered. Notice that prayer can be spoken. Notice that it need not be silent. Notice that crying aloud is honest. Notice that David hid in caves. Notice that Saul pursued him. Notice that he spared Saul's life. Notice that he trusted God. Notice that he waited. Notice that he prayed. 1 Samuel 24 says, 'The LORD therefore be judge, and judge between me and thee' Notice trusting judgment. Today, cry to God aloud if you need to. Your voice matters to Him.
S: David, hiding in a cave, said, I cry aloud to the LORD; I lift up my voice to the LORD for mercy. Hunted and hiding, he prayed aloud, for honest crying out is welcome to God. Trust Him with your cause, as David told Saul, the LORD therefore be judge, and judge between me and thee.

== 142:3

R: 'When my spirit was overwhelmed within me, then thou knewest my path.' Notice when my spirit. Inner self. Notice was overwhelmed. Faint. Notice within me. Inside. Notice then thou knewest. You knew. Notice my path. My way. Notice that David felt overwhelmed. Notice that God still knew his way. Notice that even when we lose track, God does not. Notice that traps were hidden. Notice that God saw them. Notice that being overwhelmed is human. Notice that God's knowledge is constant. If you feel overwhelmed, please reach out to someone you trust, a counsellor, or a doctor. Notice that Job trusted this. Notice that he could not find God. Notice that God knew his way. Notice that he would come forth as gold. Job 23 says, 'But he knoweth the way that I take' Notice known way. Today, when you are overwhelmed, trust that God knows your path.
S: David said, when my spirit grows faint within me, it is You who watch over my way. Trust Him, for He knoweth the way that I take: when He hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold.
P: Knewest. Psalms 142:3: 'then thou knewest my path.' Knoweth. But he knoweth the way that I take. Let this shape your day. Take it to God in prayer.

== 142:4
T: No man cared for my soul
R: 'I looked on my right hand, and beheld, but there was no man that would know me: refuge failed me; no man cared for my soul.' Notice I looked. Searched. Notice on my right hand. Beside me. Notice and beheld. Saw. Notice but there was no man. No one. Notice that would know me. Acknowledge me. Notice refuge failed me. No escape. Notice no man cared. No one concerned. Notice for my soul. For me. Notice that David felt completely alone. Notice that no one stood beside him. Notice that loneliness is painful. If you feel no one cares about you, please reach out to someone you trust, a helpline, or a counsellor; people do care. Notice that at his trial no one stood with him. Notice that all forsook him. Notice that the Lord stood with him. 2 Timothy 4 says, 'Notwithstanding the Lord stood with me, and strengthened me' Notice the Lord stood. Today, know God stands with you when others do not. Reach out.
S: David said, look and see, there is no one at my right hand; no one is concerned for me; I have no refuge; no one cares for my life. Loneliness is real and painful, so if you feel no one cares about you, please reach out to someone you trust, a helpline, or a counsellor, for people do care. God stands with you, as Paul wrote, notwithstanding the Lord stood with me, and strengthened me.
P: No man cared. Psalms 142:4: 'no man cared for my soul.' Stood with me. Notwithstanding the Lord stood with me. Live it out today.
