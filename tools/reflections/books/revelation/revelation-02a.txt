# Revelation 2: Be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life

To the messenger of the believers in Ephesus write. Notice these things saith he that holdeth the seven stars in his right hand. These are the words of Him who holds the seven stars in His right hand and walks among the seven golden lampstands. Notice that Jesus holds His messengers. Notice that He walks among His people. Notice that He is present, not absent. Notice that He sees each group closely. Notice that every letter begins with Jesus. Notice that we live as those He sees. Notice that Matthew 18 declared this. Matthew 18 says, 'For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.' Notice in the midst. Today, remember that Jesus walks among His people.
S: Jesus speaks to the believers in Ephesus as the One who holds the seven stars and walks among the seven golden lampstands. He is present among His people and sees each of them closely. Take heart, for where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.
P: Among us. Revelation 2:1: 'who walketh in the midst of the seven golden candlesticks.' Gathered. There am I in the midst of them. Know He is near today. Take it to God in prayer.

Notice and thou hast tried them which say they are apostles, and are not, and hast found them liars. You have tested those who claim to be apostles but are not, and have found them false. Notice that Jesus knows their work. Notice that He sees their effort. Notice that He sees their endurance. Notice that they would not tolerate evil. Notice that they tested teachers. Notice that discernment is praised. Notice that not every claim is true. Notice that testing is wise. Notice that we too work faithfully. Notice that we test what we hear. Notice that 1 John 4 declared this. 1 John 4 says, 'Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God' Notice try the spirits. Today, test what you hear by God's word.
S: Jesus tells Ephesus He knows their deeds, hard work and perseverance, and that they tested those who claimed to be apostles and found them false. Discernment is praised, for not every claim is true. Test wisely, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God.
P: Discern. Revelation 2:2: 'I know thy works, and thy labour, and thy patience.' Test. Try the spirits whether they are of God. Test what you hear today. Trust Him with it today.
