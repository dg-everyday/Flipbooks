Notice so Naomi returned. The journey completed. Notice and Ruth the Moabitess. Named with her origin. Notice her daughter in law, with her. Together. Notice which returned out of the country of Moab. Leaving the past behind. Notice and they came to Bethlehem. The house of bread. Notice in the beginning of barley harvest. A season of provision. Notice that the chapter ends with a hint of hope. Notice that they arrived just as harvest began. Notice that God's timing was already at work. Psalm 126 says, 'They that sow in tears shall reap in joy.' Notice that tears and harvest belong together. Today, take heart that God's timing is perfect. Your season of tears may be arriving at the beginning of harvest. Watch for His provision.
