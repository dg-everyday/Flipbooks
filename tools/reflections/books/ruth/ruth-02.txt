== 2:23
T: Unto the end of wheat harvest
R: 'So she kept fast by the maidens of Boaz to glean unto the end of barley harvest and of wheat harvest; and dwelt with her mother in law.' Notice so she kept fast by the maidens of Boaz. Faithful. Notice to glean unto the end of barley harvest. Through the season. Notice and of wheat harvest. Longer still. Notice and dwelt with her mother in law. Staying with Naomi. Notice that Ruth was faithful through two harvests. Notice that she remained loyal to Naomi throughout. Notice that months of quiet faithfulness passed. Notice that God's plan was unfolding slowly. First Corinthians 4 says, 'it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.' Notice that faithfulness over time is what God seeks. Today, be faithful through long seasons of ordinary work. God's plans often unfold slowly. Keep gleaning, keep serving, keep loving.
S: Ruth stayed close to Boaz's young women and gleaned until the end of the barley and wheat harvests, living with her mother-in-law. She was faithful through two harvests and loyal to Naomi throughout, as God's plan unfolded slowly. Be faithful through long seasons of ordinary work, for stewards are required to be found faithful.
P: Faithful through the seasons. Ruth 2:23: 'to glean unto the end of barley harvest and of wheat harvest.' Months of quiet faithfulness. It is required that a man be found faithful.
