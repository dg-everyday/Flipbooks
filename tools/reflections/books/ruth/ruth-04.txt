R: 'And Obed begat Jesse, and Jesse begat David.' Obed the father of Jesse, and Jesse the father of David. Notice and Obed begat Jesse. Ruth's grandson. Notice and Jesse begat David. Israel's king. Notice that the book ends with the name David. Notice that a story of famine, death and grief ended with a king. Notice that God was working all along toward this name. Notice that from David's line came Jesus. Revelation 22 records Jesus saying, 'I am the root and the offspring of David, and the bright and morning star.' Notice that Ruth's story points to Christ. Notice that the book of Judges ended with no king, and Ruth ends with David. Today, rejoice that God's story leads to the King. Behind every ordinary act of faithfulness, He is building something eternal. Jesus is the King Ruth's story was waiting for.
S: Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David, as the book ends with the name of Israel's king. A story of famine, death and grief ended with a king, and from David's line came Jesus, the root and offspring of David. Rejoice that God's story leads to the King, for behind ordinary faithfulness He is building something eternal.
