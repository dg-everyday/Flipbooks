# Zechariah 5: Behold a flying roll

== 5:1
T: Behold a flying roll
R: 'Then I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a flying roll.' Notice then I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked. I looked again. Notice and behold a flying roll. And there before me was a flying scroll. Notice that a scroll flies. Notice that it carries God's word. Notice that it moves swiftly. Notice that God's word goes out. Notice that it reaches everywhere. Notice that it cannot be stopped. Notice that it carries judgment and truth. Notice that God's word is active. Notice that it does its work. Notice that it does not return empty. Notice that the psalmist described its speed. Psalm 147 says, 'his word runneth very swiftly.' Notice his word runneth very swiftly. Today, remember that God's word is active and powerful.
S: Zechariah looked again and saw a flying scroll. God's word goes out swiftly and reaches everywhere, active and unstoppable. Remember its power, for his word runneth very swiftly.
P: A flying scroll. Zechariah 5:1: 'behold a flying roll.' Swift. His word runneth very swiftly. Heed God's word today. Take it to God in prayer.

S: The angel asked what Zechariah saw, and he described a flying scroll twenty cubits long and ten wide. The huge, unrolled scroll showed God's word is open and plain for all, so none can claim ignorance. Remember it is near, for the word is very nigh unto thee.
P: A huge scroll. Zechariah 5:2: 'the length thereof is twenty cubits.' Near. The word is very nigh unto thee. Read God's word today. Trust Him with it today.

== 5:3
T: Every one that stealeth shall be cut off
R: 'Then said he unto me, This is the curse that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth' Notice then said he unto me, This is the curse that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth. He said to me, This is the curse that is going out over the whole land. Notice for every one that stealeth shall be cut off as on this side according to it. For according to what it says on one side, every thief will be banished. Notice and every one that sweareth shall be cut off as on that side according to it. And according to what it says on the other, everyone who swears falsely will be banished. Notice that two sins are named. Notice that theft is one. Notice that honesty with people matters. Notice that honesty before God matters. Notice that Exodus commanded both. Exodus 20 says, 'Thou shalt not steal.' Notice thou shalt not steal. Today, be honest with both God and people.
S: The angel said the scroll was the curse going out over the land, banishing every thief according to one side and everyone who swears falsely according to the other. These two sins sum up the law, against neighbour and against God. Be honest with both, for thou shalt not steal.
P: Theft and false oaths. Zechariah 5:3: 'every one that stealeth shall be cut off.' Commanded. Thou shalt not steal. Be honest today. Let this shape your day.

== 5:4
T: It shall enter into the house of the thief
R: 'I will bring it forth, saith the LORD of hosts, and it shall enter into the house of the thief' Notice I will bring it forth, saith the LORD of hosts. The LORD Almighty declares, I will send it out. Notice and it shall enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name. And it will enter the house of the thief and the house of anyone who swears falsely by My name. Notice and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof. It will remain in that house and destroy it completely, both its timbers and its stones. Notice that God's word enters homes. Notice that Proverbs taught this. Proverbs 3 says, 'The curse of the LORD is in the house of the wicked: but he blesseth the habitation of the just.' Notice he blesseth the habitation of the just. Today, keep your home honest before God.
S: The LORD Almighty said He would send the scroll into the house of the thief and of anyone who swears falsely by His name, where it would remain and destroy it completely. Sin cannot hide indoors, for dishonesty ruins homes while integrity builds them. Keep your home honest, for the curse of the LORD is in the house of the wicked: but he blesseth the habitation of the just.
P: In the house. Zechariah 5:4: 'it shall enter into the house of the thief.' Blessed home. He blesseth the habitation of the just. Keep an honest home today. Take it to God in prayer.
