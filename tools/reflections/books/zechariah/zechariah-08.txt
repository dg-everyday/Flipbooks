R: 'We will go with you: for we have heard that God is with you.' Notice thus saith the LORD of hosts; In those days it shall come to pass. This is what the LORD Almighty says: In those days. Notice that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew. Ten people from all languages and nations will take firm hold of one Jew by the hem of his robe. Notice saying, We will go with you: for we have heard that God is with you. And say, Let us go with you, because we have heard that God is with you. Notice that people are drawn by God's presence. Notice that they want to go with His people. Notice that Ruth said similar words. Ruth 1 says, 'thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God' Notice thy God my God. Today, live so that others see God is with you.
S: The LORD Almighty said ten people from all nations would take hold of one Jew's robe and say, Let us go with you, because we have heard that God is with you. God's presence among His people draws others, for faithful lives point to Him. Live so others see it, as Ruth said, thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God.
P: God is with you. Zechariah 8:23: 'we have heard that God is with you.' My God. Thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God. Let your life point to God today. Trust Him with it today.
