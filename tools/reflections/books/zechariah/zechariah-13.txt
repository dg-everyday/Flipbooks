# Zechariah 13: A fountain opened for sin and for uncleanness

== 13:1
T: A fountain opened
R: 'In that day there shall be a fountain opened to the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness.' Notice in that day there shall be a fountain opened. On that day a fountain will be opened. Notice to the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness. For the house of David and Jerusalem's people, to cleanse them from sin and impurity. Notice that after mourning comes cleansing. Notice that the fountain flows freely. Notice that it washes away sin. Notice that it washes away uncleanness. Notice that it is opened by God. Notice that it follows looking on the pierced one. Notice that this points to the cross. Notice that anyone may come. Notice that John declared this cleansing. 1 John 1 says, 'the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin.' Notice cleanseth us from all sin. Today, come to the fountain of Christ and be made clean.
S: On that day a fountain would be opened for David's house and Jerusalem's people to cleanse them from sin and impurity. After mourning comes cleansing, flowing freely from the pierced one and pointing to the cross. Come and be made clean, for the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin.
P: A fountain opened. Zechariah 13:1: 'there shall be a fountain opened … for sin and for uncleanness.' Cleansed. The blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin. Come to Christ today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 13:2
T: I will cut off the names of the idols
R: 'I will cut off the names of the idols out of the land, and they shall no more be remembered' Notice I will cut off the names of the idols out of the land. I will banish the names of the idols from the land. Notice and they shall no more be remembered. And they will be remembered no more. Notice and also I will cause the prophets and the unclean spirit to pass out of the land. I will remove both the false prophets and the spirit of impurity from the land. Notice that cleansing goes further. Notice that idols are removed. Notice that even their names are forgotten. Notice that God purifies thoroughly. Notice that a cleansed heart has no room for idols. Notice that this is His work. Notice that we cooperate with it. Notice that John urged this. 1 John 5 says, 'Little children, keep yourselves from idols.' Notice keep yourselves from idols. Today, let God remove every idol from your heart.
S: The LORD said He would banish the names of the idols so they are remembered no more and remove the false prophets and the spirit of impurity. God purifies thoroughly, removing whatever pulls His people away from Him. Let Him clear your heart, for little children, keep yourselves from idols.
P: Idols cut off. Zechariah 13:2: 'I will cut off the names of the idols out of the land.' Kept. Little children, keep yourselves from idols. Put away idols today. Trust Him with it today.

== 13:3
T: Thou speakest lies in the name of the LORD
R: 'Thou shalt not live; for thou speakest lies in the name of the LORD' Notice thou shalt not live; for thou speakest lies in the name of the LORD. You must die, because you have told lies in the LORD's name. Notice that the verse pictures even a false prophet's own parents refusing to shield him. Notice that loyalty to truth comes first. Notice that false prophecy is taken seriously. Notice that lying in God's name is grave. Notice that the cleansed people will not tolerate it. Notice that family ties do not excuse falsehood. Notice that the point is zeal for truth. Notice that today we test teaching by Scripture. Notice that God's word is our standard. Notice that if anyone is being harmed at home, they deserve safety and help. Notice that John urged testing. 1 John 4 says, 'try the spirits whether they are of God' Notice try the spirits. Today, love the truth, and if anyone is being harmed, help them reach safe help.
S: The verse pictures a day when a false prophet's own parents refuse to shield him, saying he has told lies in the LORD's name. The point is zeal for truth, for lying in God's name is grave, and if anyone is being harmed at home, they deserve safety and help. Love the truth and test all teaching, for we are told to try the spirits whether they are of God.
P: Lies in His name. Zechariah 13:3: 'thou speakest lies in the name of the LORD.' Testing. Try the spirits whether they are of God. Love the truth today, and if anyone is being harmed, help them reach safe help. Let this shape your day.

== 13:4

Notice that the prophets shall be ashamed every one of his vision, when he hath prophesied. Every prophet will be ashamed of his prophetic vision. Notice neither shall they wear a rough garment to deceive. They will not put on a prophet's garment of hair in order to deceive. Notice that false prophets are ashamed. Notice that they stop pretending. Notice that they drop the costume. Notice that outward show is exposed. Notice that clothing cannot make a prophet. Notice that God looks at the heart. Notice that deception ends. Notice that truth prevails. Notice that outward religion can deceive. Notice that inward reality matters. Notice that Jesus warned of this. Matthew 7 says, 'Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing' Notice in sheep's clothing. Today, look beyond outward show to the truth.
S: On that day every false prophet would be ashamed of his vision and would no longer wear a garment of hair to deceive. Outward show was exposed, for clothing cannot make a prophet, and God looks at the heart. Look beyond appearances, for beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing.
P: Ashamed. Zechariah 13:4: 'the prophets shall be ashamed every one of his vision.' Sheep's clothing. Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing. Seek the truth today. Take it to God in prayer.

== 13:5

Notice I am an husbandman; for man taught me to keep cattle from my youth. I am a farmer; the land has been my livelihood since my youth. Notice that the false prophet denies it. Notice that he claims an honest trade. Notice that pretence is abandoned. Notice that false claims are dropped. Notice that honest work is better than false religion. Notice that ordinary callings are honourable. Notice that not everyone is called to preach. Notice that every honest job can honour God. Notice that faithfulness matters in all work. Notice that Paul taught this. Colossians 3 says, 'And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord' Notice as to the Lord. Today, honour God in your ordinary work.
S: The false prophet would say he was not a prophet but a farmer who had worked the land since youth. Pretence was abandoned, and honest work proved better than false religion, for every honest calling can honour God. Honour Him in your daily work, for whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord.
P: An honest trade. Zechariah 13:5: 'I am no prophet, I am an husbandman.' Heartily. Whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord. Honour God in your work today. Trust Him with it today.
