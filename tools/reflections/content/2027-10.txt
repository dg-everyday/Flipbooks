== Oct 03
R: Hebrews describes a different kind of sacrifice: 'By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name.' In the Old Testament, worshippers brought animals and grain as sacrifices. Now, through Jesus, we bring praise. Our thankful words become an offering to God. The writer calls it the fruit of our lips. Just as a healthy tree naturally produces fruit, a heart rooted in Christ naturally produces thanksgiving. And notice the word continually. Not only when life is easy, but in every season. Sometimes praise is called a sacrifice because it costs us something. It is easy to praise God when everything is going well. But giving thanks in hardship is a true sacrifice. It declares that God is good even when we do not feel it. Today, offer the sacrifice of praise. Speak your thanks to God out loud, especially for something you are tempted to complain about.
S: Hebrews calls us to offer God a continual sacrifice of praise, the fruit of lips that give thanks to His name. Thanksgiving grows naturally from a heart rooted in Christ, even in hard seasons. Offer your praise to God today, especially in a difficult area.

== Oct 04
R: Paul explains why God blesses His people: 'Being enriched in every thing to all bountifulness, which causeth through us thanksgiving to God.' God enriches us so that we can be generous. And when we are generous, others give thanks to God. It is a beautiful cycle. God gives to us, we give to others, and their gratitude flows back to God. Our blessings are not meant to stop with us. They are meant to flow through us. When we hoard what we have, the cycle breaks. When we share it, thanksgiving multiplies. This also frees us from discontentment. When we see our resources as tools for blessing others rather than measures of our worth, we find joy in giving. We stop comparing and start sharing. Today, think about how God has enriched you, whether with money, time, skills, or relationships. Then find a way to share that blessing. Let your generosity cause someone else to give thanks to God.
S: Paul teaches that God enriches us so that we can be generous, which leads others to give thanks to God. Our blessings are meant to flow through us, not stop with us. Share one of your blessings today and let it spark thanksgiving.
