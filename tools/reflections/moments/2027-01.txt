# January 2027: New Beginnings & Guidance (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Jan 01
Q: What unknown road ahead of you this year can you place in God's hand today?
P: Lord, You lead me on paths I have not known. Make the darkness light before me, straighten what is crooked, and never let go of my hand. Amen.

== Jan 02
Q: What choice in front of you today needs God's direction more than your own plans?
P: Lord, show me Your ways and teach me Your paths. Slow me down to listen, and lead me in the way You know is best. Amen.

== Jan 03
Q: Who has guided you in wisdom, and whom might you guide in turn?
P: Father, thank You for every right path You have led me on and every person You sent to teach me. Help me keep walking, and help me guide others well. Amen.

== Jan 04
Q: Which relationship or reunion can you entrust to God's direction and timing today?
P: Lord, direct my way and the ways of those I love. I trust You with the miles between us and with the doors that are not yet open. Amen.

== Jan 05
Q: Are you coming to God for guidance today, or only for approval of your own plans?
P: Lord, give me a meek and teachable heart. Help me lay down the need to be right, and guide me in Your way as I listen. Amen.

== Jan 06
Q: What ordinary choice today could you begin by quietly acknowledging God?
P: Lord, I want to acknowledge You in all my ways, in small choices as well as great ones. Turn my heart toward You through this ordinary day, and direct my paths. Amen.

== Jan 07
Q: Where is God still teaching you to grow, and whom can you encourage along the way?
P: Lord, You are not finished with me. Teach me in all wisdom, grow me into who You made me to be, and use me to encourage someone today. Amen.

== Jan 08
Q: What is overwhelming your spirit today that you can pour out honestly before God?
P: Lord, when my spirit is overwhelmed, You know my path. I pour out my heart to You, and I rest in knowing You walk this road with me. Amen.

== Jan 09
Q: How can Jesus' protective love shape the way you care for someone today?
P: Lord Jesus, You stepped forward so that I could go free. Thank You for the path You opened at such cost. Teach me to love others as You love me. Amen.

== Jan 10
Q: What one thing in your heart needs clearing to make room for the Lord?
P: Lord, meet me in my wilderness. Show me what needs clearing in my heart, and prepare me to welcome You more fully today. Amen.

== Jan 11
Q: What old label or past mistake do you need to stop letting define you?
P: Lord, in Christ I am a new creature. Free me from the old story that replays in my mind, and help me live today from the new one You are writing. Amen.

== Jan 12
Q: What question you cannot answer can you turn into wonder and trust today?
P: Lord, Your wisdom runs deeper than I can measure. When I cannot understand Your ways, teach me to trust You and to worship in wonder. Amen.

== Jan 13
Q: Where are you leaning on your own cleverness instead of simple trust in God?
P: Lord, thank You for a gospel simple enough for anyone to receive. Keep my faith humble, and let my trust in You be the foundation of my life. Amen.

== Jan 14
Q: Where do you need the courage to speak truthfully and kindly, whoever you are with?
P: Lord Jesus, You teach the way of God truly and show no favoritism. Let Your words shape my path, and help me speak truth with kindness to everyone. Amen.

== Jan 15
Q: Which of God's promises are you still waiting to see fulfilled?
P: Lord, You kept Your word through long years of waiting. While I wait, keep my heart ready, and help me trust that You will keep Your promises to me. Amen.

== Jan 16
Q: What direction from God are you hesitant to accept, and can you trust Him as your Redeemer in it?
P: Lord, my Redeemer, You teach me to profit and lead me in the way I should go. Help me receive Your direction as a gift and follow it with trust. Amen.

== Jan 17
Q: What would you bring to God today if you truly believed the way to Him is open?
P: Lord Jesus, You opened a new and living way into the Father's presence. I come near just as I am, with my prayers, my burdens, and my praise. Amen.

== Jan 18
Q: Where could you carry a little of God's coming hope into a hard place today?
P: Lord, You are making all things new. Let the hope of a new heaven and earth make me brave today, and help me bring light into someone's dark day. Amen.

== Jan 19
Q: Which voices around you draw you toward God, and which pull you away?
P: Lord, give me discernment to recognize the voices that lead toward life. When opposition rises, keep me close to the truth and steady in faith. Amen.

== Jan 20
Q: Where can a small act of love push back the darkness for someone today?
P: Lord, the darkness is passing and Your true light now shines. Let Your light shine through the way I treat others, especially the difficult ones. Amen.

== Jan 21
Q: Is there a decision about following Jesus that you have been putting off?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the way to life. Thank You for opening a road I could never have found on my own. Help me follow You wholeheartedly today. Amen.

== Jan 22
Q: Where have you felt cast aside, and how might God's view of your story be bigger than yours?
P: Lord, You have not cast away Your people, and You have not let go of me. Lift my eyes beyond my small view, and remind me that You are still writing my story. Amen.

== Jan 23
Q: How can a few quiet minutes in God's Word prepare your heart for today?
P: Lord, thank You for Your written Word that lights my way. As I read it, prepare my heart, level my hard roads, and make my paths straight. Amen.

== Jan 24
Q: What verse or song could you let make itself at home in your heart today?
P: Lord, let the word of Christ dwell in me richly. Fill my heart with Your wisdom and my mouth with songs of grace, so I can encourage others. Amen.

== Jan 25
Q: How would today feel different if you began it from God's acceptance rather than your own striving?
P: Father, thank You for the open heavens and the gentle Spirit. Let Your peace rest on me, and help me walk today in the light of Your love. Amen.

== Jan 26
Q: Who has shown you what following Jesus looks like, and who might be watching you?
P: Lord, thank You for the faithful people who have shown me Your ways. Make me faithful in ordinary things, so my life can point someone back to You. Amen.

== Jan 27
Q: Which thought keeps running on repeat that you can hand to God for renewal?
P: Lord, renew the spirit of my mind. Replace my anxious and unkind thoughts with Your truth, and lead me steadily in a new direction. Amen.

== Jan 28
Q: Where do you feel worn out, and how can you let God renew your inner life today?
P: Lord, my outward strength is limited, but Your renewal never runs out. Renew my inward self day by day, so that I do not faint. Amen.

== Jan 29
Q: What crooked place in your path keeps reopening an old wound?
P: Lord, give me courage to make straight paths for my feet. Help me remove what makes me stumble, so that what is wounded in me can be healed. Amen.

== Jan 30
Q: Is there someone you think is beyond God's reach whom you can pray for today?
P: Lord, Your light is brighter than the noonday sun and can break into any life. Open my eyes to Your light, and help me hope for those who seem far from You. Amen.

== Jan 31
Q: Where have you been trying to patch your life rather than surrender it to Jesus?
P: Lord Jesus, I do not want You to patch my old life. Do something fully new in me, and make me whole as I surrender to You. Amen.
