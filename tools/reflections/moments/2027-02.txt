# February 2027: Love & Faithful Relationships (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Feb 01
Q: Who could use your intentional tenderness and courtesy today?
P: Lord, make me tenderhearted and courteous. Slow me down to notice the people around me, and help me treat them with compassion and love today. Amen.

== Feb 02
Q: Whom can you bless today with words of grace, mercy, and peace?
P: Lord, thank You for the people who have poured their lives into mine. Help me love someone the way Paul loved Timothy, and fill their life with grace, mercy, and peace. Amen.

== Feb 03
Q: Before you try to love others today, how can you first receive God's love?
P: Lord, peace, love, and faith come from You, not from my own strength. Fill me with Your love first, then let it flow through me to those around me. Amen.

== Feb 04
Q: Who can you pray for today, and what heavy thing could you ask others to pray about for you?
P: Lord, I cannot carry everything alone. Teach me to strive together with others in prayer, to pray faithfully for my friends, and to humbly ask for their prayers too. Amen.

== Feb 05
Q: Who rarely gets invited whom you could include this week?
P: Lord, teach me a love that does not keep score. Open my eyes to those who cannot repay me, and let my kindness to them be a quiet offering to You. Amen.

== Feb 06
Q: What great thing has the Lord done for you that you could share with someone close to home?
P: Lord, You have had compassion on me and done great things for me. Give me a natural way to tell the people closest to me about Your mercy. Amen.

== Feb 07
Q: In what small way can you become more loving today, beginning with those closest to you?
P: Lord, add to my faith brotherly kindness, and to my kindness, love. Let me grow more loving, starting with the people closest to me. Amen.

== Feb 08
Q: Who needs to hear today that they are dearly loved and encouraged to stand fast in the Lord?
P: Lord, thank You for the people who are my joy. Help me tell them they are dearly loved, and use my words to help them stand fast in You. Amen.

== Feb 09
Q: Whom does the world overlook that you can honor as God does today?
P: Lord, You choose the poor of this world to be rich in faith. Help me see people the way You see them, and honor those the world passes by. Amen.

== Feb 10
Q: Who around you needs the patience and mercy God has shown you?
P: Lord, You are full of compassion, gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy. Let Your mercy toward me overflow in patience and grace toward others today. Amen.

== Feb 11
Q: What could you lay down today, in time, comfort, or pride, for the good of someone else?
P: Lord Jesus, You laid down Your life for me. Fill my heart with that love, and show me one way to give something of myself for someone else today. Amen.

== Feb 12
Q: Which piece of Christ's wardrobe, compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, or patience, do you most need to put on today?
P: Lord, I am chosen, holy, and beloved in You. Clothe my heart with mercy, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience as I step into this day. Amen.

== Feb 13
Q: What small step toward peace could you take in a strained relationship?
P: God of love and peace, be with me in my hardest relationships. Help me offer an encouraging word, extend grace, and take one step toward peace. Amen.

== Feb 14
Q: Where are you serving or loving faithfully without seeing results, and what keeps you going?
P: Lord, make me steadfast and unmoveable. When my work and my love seem unnoticed, remind me that nothing done for You is ever in vain. Amen.

== Feb 15
Q: How would you want to be treated if you were in the place of someone you find difficult?
P: Lord, teach me to love my neighbor as myself. Give me grace toward the person I find hardest to love, and let my love fulfill Your law. Amen.

== Feb 16
Q: How would today change if you truly rested in being beloved and chosen by God?
P: Father, thank You that I am beloved and chosen by You. Let that truth steady my heart, free me from seeking approval, and help me love others freely. Amen.

== Feb 17
Q: Who may feel separated from others because of their circumstances, and how can you reach out?
P: Lord, keep me from befriending people for what they can give me. Help me be the neighbor who stays when others leave, and reach out to someone who feels alone. Amen.

== Feb 18
Q: How can your love for God show up in the way you treat the people around you today?
P: Lord, I love You, and I want that love to spill over to every person I meet. Let each kindness I show today become an act of worship. Amen.

== Feb 19
Q: How can you be a friend today in a way that costs you something?
P: Lord Jesus, thank You for calling me Your friend and laying down Your life for me. Help me love my friends in a way that reflects Your great love. Amen.

== Feb 20
Q: What regret or guilt do you need to lay down in the forgiveness Jesus offers?
P: Lord Jesus, through You I am forgiven. I lay down my guilt and receive Your freedom, and I ask You to make me quick to forgive others. Amen.

== Feb 21
Q: What in your life feels shaken, and how can you anchor your heart in God's unfailing kindness?
P: Lord, even if the mountains depart, Your kindness will not depart from me. Anchor my heart in Your unshakable love, and make me steady and kind for others. Amen.

== Feb 22
Q: Where in your story can you see a 'But God' moment, and who needs to hear that their story is not over?
P: But God, You are rich in mercy, and You loved me with a great love. Thank You for stepping into my hopeless places. Help me share that hope with someone today. Amen.

== Feb 23
Q: What need have you noticed that you could meet today instead of shutting your heart?
P: Lord, keep my heart open to those in need. Let Your love dwell in me in ways that show up in real, generous action, however small. Amen.

== Feb 24
Q: Whose good news can you celebrate sincerely today?
P: Lord, thank You for the mercy You show to others. Free me from envy, and help me rejoice wholeheartedly with those who rejoice. Amen.

== Feb 25
Q: Do you treat everyone with the same warmth and respect, or do you play favorites?
P: Lord, my King, teach me to keep Your royal law. Help me show the same warmth and respect to everyone, because every person is someone You love. Amen.

== Feb 26
Q: How can you use your freedom in Christ to serve someone today?
P: Lord, You have called me to liberty. Keep me from using my freedom for myself, and teach me the joy of serving others in love. Amen.

== Feb 27
Q: Whom do you love only halfway, and how can you love them sincerely and fervently today?
P: Lord, purify my heart and deepen my love. Help me love others sincerely, with no hidden motives, and with a fervent, wholehearted love. Amen.

== Feb 28
Q: Which part of God's character, gracious, compassionate, slow to anger, or merciful, can you reflect to someone today?
P: Lord, You are gracious and full of compassion, slow to anger and of great mercy. Let Your character sink into my heart and shine through me to others today. Amen.
