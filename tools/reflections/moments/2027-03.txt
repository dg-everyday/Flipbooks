# March 2027: Faith & Trust (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Mar 01
Q: How could the way you handle money and small things become an act of faith today?
P: Lord, everything I have is entrusted to me by You. Make me faithful with small and ordinary things, and teach me to value the true riches that last. Amen.

== Mar 02
Q: How is your faith working, your love laboring, and your hope enduring today?
P: Lord, give my faith hands and feet. Let my faith work, my love labor, and my hope endure with patience, so my life brings You glory. Amen.

== Mar 03
Q: Whom do you need to see with fresh eyes, remembering there is no difference at the foot of the cross?
P: Lord, thank You for the righteousness that comes by faith in Jesus. I rest in Your gift, and I ask You to help me see every person as equally in need of Your grace. Amen.

== Mar 04
Q: Which failure makes you feel trapped, and can you trust Jesus to set you free from it?
P: Lord, I cannot free myself by my own efforts. I trust in Jesus and receive the promise You freely give to those who believe. Amen.

== Mar 05
Q: Who is sick or burdened that you could visit, call, or pray with in faith today?
P: Lord, You care for the whole person, body, heart, and soul. Raise up the sick, forgive the burdened, and make me faithful to pray for those who are hurting. Amen.

== Mar 06
Q: What hope, even a small one, can you choose to hold fast to today?
P: Lord Jesus, Son over God's house, thank You for making me part of Your dwelling place. Help me hold fast to my hope, trusting Your faithfulness to keep me to the end. Amen.

== Mar 07
Q: What suffering can you hand over to your faithful Creator today while you keep doing good?
P: Faithful Creator, I commit the keeping of my soul to You. Help me keep doing what is right while I suffer, and let me rest in Your faithful care. Amen.

== Mar 08
Q: Which lesser fear, failure, rejection, or others' opinions, is ruling you that reverence for God could quiet?
P: Lord, let my confidence rest in You, not in achievement or approval. Be my place of refuge, and be a refuge for the generation that comes after me. Amen.

== Mar 09
Q: Whose faith and joy could you help grow today with a kind word or a faithful example?
P: Lord, my days are Your gift. Use them for the good of others, and show me whose faith and joy I can help increase today. Amen.

== Mar 10
Q: Who handed the faith to you, and to whom are you passing it on?
P: Lord, thank You for the faithful people who taught me about You. Make me faithful to pass on what I have received to someone who will teach others also. Amen.

== Mar 11
Q: Looking back, where has God been your hope and trust all along?
P: Lord God, You have been my hope and my trust from my youth. Thank You for a lifetime of faithfulness, and help me trust You for all that lies ahead. Amen.

== Mar 12
Q: What request or thanks can you bring boldly to God in prayer today?
P: Lord, through faith in Jesus I have boldness and access to You. I come today with my requests, my thanks, and my heart, knowing I am always welcome. Amen.

== Mar 13
Q: Whom are you tempted to give up on, and how can love keep bearing, believing, hoping, and enduring?
P: Lord, You have never given up on me. Teach me a love that bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, and endures all things. Amen.

== Mar 14
Q: What word of hope or testimony could your faith speak to someone today?
P: Lord, I believe, and therefore I want to speak. Give my faith a voice today, and let my words carry hope and life to someone who needs it. Amen.

== Mar 15
Q: What doubt is taunting you, and how can you spread it out before the Lord as Hezekiah did?
P: Lord, when doubts ask what my confidence is, let me answer with Your faithfulness. I bring every threat and fear to You, trusting You to deliver me. Amen.

== Mar 16
Q: How can you diligently seek God today, believing that He is there and that He is good?
P: Lord, I believe that You are, and that You reward those who diligently seek You. Draw me near in faith today, and let my trust delight Your heart. Amen.

== Mar 17
Q: Where does your faith feel fragile, and how can you lean on God's faithfulness instead?
P: Lord, Your faithfulness does not depend on mine. When my faith feels weak, hold me fast, and keep me praying for those who do not yet believe. Amen.

== Mar 18
Q: What achievement or record are you still trusting in instead of Christ?
P: Lord Jesus, I set down my résumé before You. My confidence is not in my own record but in Your faithfulness, the foundation that will never crumble. Amen.

== Mar 19
Q: What has drawn your heart away that you can turn from, as you lean your trust on Jesus again?
P: Lord, I turn again toward You. Show me what has drawn my heart away, and help me turn from it and lean my full trust on Jesus. Amen.

== Mar 20
Q: How might it change your day to see yourself as one of God's saints in your own ordinary place?
P: Father, thank You for calling me holy and faithful in Christ. Let Your grace cover my shortcomings and Your peace settle my anxious thoughts today. Amen.

== Mar 21
Q: As you get ready today, which do you most need to put on: faith, love, or hope?
P: Lord, I belong to the day. Clothe me with the breastplate of faith and love, and guard my mind with the hope of salvation as I step into a hurting world. Amen.

== Mar 22
Q: How can you treasure and nurture the precious faith God has given you?
P: Lord Jesus, thank You for the precious faith You have given me, as precious as any apostle's. Help me treasure it and nurture it through prayer and Your Word. Amen.

== Mar 23
Q: What do you need to pour out honestly before God today?
P: Lord, You are my refuge at all times. I pour out my heart before You, the worry, the hurt, and the hope, and I rest in the shelter of Your presence. Amen.

== Mar 24
Q: What simple act of love could you offer someone today that would outlast the moment?
P: Lord, faith, hope, and love remain, and the greatest of these is love. Since love is Your very nature, let love shape every word and deed of mine today. Amen.

== Mar 25
Q: Where are you leaning on someone unreliable, and how can you become trustworthy for others?
P: Lord, You never fail. Let my deepest confidence rest in You, and make me the kind of friend others can lean on in their time of trouble. Amen.

== Mar 26
Q: What burden are you carrying today that you could rest in God's strong arms?
P: Lord, Your righteousness is near and Your salvation has gone forth. Thank You for the arms of Jesus stretched out on the cross. Hold me in arms strong enough to save. Amen.

== Mar 27
Q: What uncertainty could you anchor today in a God who cannot lie?
P: Lord, it is impossible for You to lie, and Your promises never change. When everything feels uncertain, I flee to You for refuge and hold fast to the hope You set before me. Amen.

== Mar 28
Q: Where has your faith been weak lately, and how does Jesus' faithfulness meet you there?
P: Lord Jesus, even when I believe not, You abide faithful. You restored Peter after he denied You; restore me where I have faltered, and let my hope rest on You. Amen.

== Mar 29
Q: What sorrow can you bring to Jesus today while still speaking your faith in Him?
P: Lord Jesus, meet me in my grief as You met Martha. Even through tears, help me say that I believe You are the Christ, the Son of God. Amen.

== Mar 30
Q: How would it change today to remember that you already stand in God's grace?
P: Lord, through Jesus I have access to the grace in which I stand. Free me from the fear of falling out of Your love, and fill me with hope of Your glory. Amen.

== Mar 31
Q: Where is God asking you for patient endurance, to keep trusting and keep obeying?
P: Lord, give me the patience of the saints. Help me keep Your commandments and hold on to the faith of Jesus, through every pressure, to the end. Amen.
