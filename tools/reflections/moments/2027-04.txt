# April 2027: Grace, Mercy & Redemption (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Apr 01
Q: What chain that Jesus has already broken are you still carrying?
P: Lord Jesus, You redeemed me with Your blood and forgave all my sins according to the riches of Your grace. Help me let go of broken chains and walk in Your freedom today. Amen.

== Apr 02
Q: What have you been hiding that you could bring honestly into God's light today?
P: God of my salvation, I cannot fix what I have done, but You can deliver me. I bring my failures into Your light, and I will sing of Your goodness. Amen.

== Apr 03
Q: How might it change the way you treat others to see each one as a fellow recipient of grace?
P: Lord Jesus, I am saved by Your grace, just like everyone else. Keep me humble, and help me treat every person as one who needs and receives Your grace. Amen.

== Apr 04
Q: What do you feel you lack today, and how has Christ become that very thing for you?
P: Lord Jesus, You are my wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, and redemption. Instead of looking within for what I lack, help me look to You and rest in Your sufficiency. Amen.

== Apr 05
Q: Where are you still keeping a scorecard with God instead of resting in what Christ has done?
P: Lord, I will not frustrate Your grace. I let go of my scorecard and rest in what Jesus has done. His death was not in vain; it was enough. Amen.

== Apr 06
Q: In what hard place do you need God to multiply His grace and peace today?
P: Father, thank You for choosing me, for the sanctifying work of Your Spirit, and for the blood of Jesus that cleanses me. Multiply Your grace and peace in my life today. Amen.

== Apr 07
Q: When guilt or temptation tries to rule you, how can you remember that grace is your King?
P: Lord, let grace reign on the throne of my life. When guilt or temptation tries to rule, remind me that I live under the kind reign of grace that leads to life. Amen.

== Apr 08
Q: When you feel unworthy, how does it help to know God's grace was given before you could earn it?
P: Lord, You gave me grace in Christ before the world began. When I feel unworthy, remind me of Your ancient grace, and help me walk in my holy calling. Amen.

== Apr 09
Q: How can you see people who are different from you as future voices in heaven's choir?
P: Worthy Lamb, You were slain and redeemed people from every nation and tongue. Teach me to celebrate Your diverse family and to join the song of praise to You. Amen.

== Apr 10
Q: Who is often treated unfairly that you could treat with justice and care today?
P: Lord, Your salvation is near. Help me keep judgment and do justice, treating others fairly and speaking up for those who have no voice. Amen.

== Apr 11
Q: What is weighing on your conscience that you need to confess and let God wash away?
P: Lord, You are faithful and just to forgive. I confess what weighs on my conscience, and I receive Your cleansing. Help me stop carrying what You have washed away. Amen.

== Apr 12
Q: When you face a choice today, which path is both right and merciful?
P: Lord, give me a heart that chases after righteousness and mercy. When I face a choice, lead me in the path that is both good and gracious. Amen.

== Apr 13
Q: What need can you bring boldly to the throne of grace today?
P: Lord, You invite me to come boldly to Your throne of grace. I bring You my need today, and I ask for mercy and grace to help right on time. Amen.

== Apr 14
Q: Are you living today from gratitude for grace or from pressure to earn it?
P: Lord, thank You for the gift of salvation, by grace through faith. Let gratitude, not pressure, shape how I live today. Amen.

== Apr 15
Q: What circumstance confines you, and how can grace and peace still reach you there?
P: Father, grace and peace come from You, even in hard places. Reach me where I feel confined, and let Your peace guard my heart today. Amen.

== Apr 16
Q: What do you need God to cleanse in your hands or heart today?
P: God of my salvation, cleanse my hands and purify my heart. I receive the righteousness and blessing You freely give, with gratitude and joy. Amen.

== Apr 17
Q: What shame has been speaking loudly in your heart, and how does God's everlasting salvation answer it?
P: Lord, Your salvation is everlasting, and in You I will never be put to shame. Silence the voice of shame in my heart with the truth of Your promise. Amen.

== Apr 18
Q: Whom could you wish grace and peace to before addressing a strained relationship?
P: Lord, before I speak of hurts or problems, help me extend grace. Bless the person I find difficult with grace and peace, and soften my words toward healing. Amen.

== Apr 19
Q: What small step could you take today to grow in grace and in the knowledge of Jesus?
P: Lord Jesus, I want to grow in grace and in the knowledge of You. Draw me deeper into friendship with You, and let my growth bring You glory. Amen.

== Apr 20
Q: What accusing voice needs to be silenced by Christ's once-for-all sacrifice?
P: Lord Jesus, You entered the holy place once by Your own blood and obtained eternal redemption for me. Let Your finished work silence every accusing voice in my heart. Amen.

== Apr 21
Q: Where have you been weighed down by pressure to perform spiritually?
P: Lord Jesus, You gave Yourself for my sins, and Your grace is not cheap. Free me from the anxiety of striving, and let Your peace fill my heart. Amen.

== Apr 22
Q: In your trouble today, how can you choose to pray rather than despair?
P: Lord, in the multitude of Your mercy, hear me. When trouble comes, help me turn to You in prayer, and let Your mercy overflow through me to others. Amen.

== Apr 23
Q: How can you let what you believe in your heart find its way to your lips today?
P: Lord Jesus, I believe in my heart that You are Lord. Give me courage to confess it with my mouth, to thank You out loud, and to share my faith with someone. Amen.

== Apr 24
Q: Who around you could use a little grace, love, or fellowship from you today?
P: Lord, thank You for the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Ghost. Help me pass that grace and love on to those around me. Amen.

== Apr 25
Q: What do you need to cry out to Jesus for, even if other voices tell you to be quiet?
P: Lord Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me. Open my eyes to see what I cannot see on my own, and help me follow You on the road ahead. Amen.

== Apr 26
Q: Where are you feeling that you fall short today, and how can you remember that grace is with you?
P: Lord Jesus, Your grace is with me. When the list feels long and I fall short, remind me that Your grace is enough for today and tomorrow. Amen.

== Apr 27
Q: What truth does Jesus want to speak into your life, and how can His grace hold you as He does?
P: Lord Jesus, You came full of grace and truth. Speak truth into the places in me that need change, and hold me with Your grace as You do. Amen.

== Apr 28
Q: What in your life needs God's restoring, strengthening, and settling after a season of suffering?
P: God of all grace, my suffering is only for a while. Restore what is broken in me, make me steady, give me strength, and settle my heart on You. Amen.

== Apr 29
Q: Where has God visited you recently, and how can you praise Him for it?
P: Blessed be the Lord, who has visited and redeemed His people. Open my eyes to the ways You have come near to me, and fill my mouth with praise. Amen.

== Apr 30
Q: Who in your home or circle can you pray for by name, asking God to bring them to faith?
P: Lord Jesus, I believe on You, and I thank You for saving me. I pray for those in my household by name: bring them to You and save them. Amen.
