# May 2027: Wisdom & Spiritual Growth (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== May 01
Q: Where do you need wisdom today, and have you asked God for it through His Word?
P: Lord, You give wisdom, and from Your mouth come knowledge and understanding. Open Your Word to me today, and give me the understanding I need. Amen.

== May 02
Q: What are you facing today that needs the Spirit's wisdom, counsel, or might?
P: Lord, the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, counsel and might, rested on Jesus. Let that same Spirit guide me today, so I do not rely on my own limited insight. Amen.

== May 03
Q: Are you willing to be taught by God, even when He challenges your opinions?
P: Lord, You are the beginning of all true knowledge. Give me a reverent and teachable heart, open to the instruction You give. Amen.

== May 04
Q: Whom can you pray for by name today, asking God to reveal Himself to them?
P: Father of glory, give me the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of You. Reveal Yourself to me, and to those I love, more deeply each day. Amen.

== May 05
Q: What part of God's character could you spend time getting to know better today?
P: Holy One, wisdom begins with reverence for You. As I come to know Your character and Your heart, give me understanding for my life. Amen.

== May 06
Q: Who are the people you grow alongside in faith, and how can you thank God for them?
P: Lord, knit my heart together with others in love. Thank You for the people I grow with, and fill us with the full assurance of understanding. Amen.

== May 07
Q: How can you show wisdom today through a humble, good deed that draws no attention to yourself?
P: Lord, let my wisdom be seen in how I live, not just in what I know. Give me the meekness of wisdom to serve quietly and respond gently today. Amen.

== May 08
Q: What falsehood do you need discernment to recognize, and how does God's reign give you courage?
P: Lord, give me wisdom and discernment to recognize what is false. Keep me faithful, and remind me that every power opposed to You is limited and temporary. Amen.

== May 09
Q: Who taught you wisdom along the way, and how could you thank them today?
P: Lord, thank You for the people who taught me wisdom with patience and love. Bless them today, and help me pass on what I have learned with the same gentleness. Amen.

== May 10
Q: How can you engage both your mind and your spirit in worship today?
P: Lord, I want to worship You with my spirit and with my understanding. Help me mean the words I sing and pray, and let my worship bear fruit. Amen.

== May 11
Q: What ability can you offer to God today, or what weakness can you trust Him to work through?
P: Lord, I offer You my abilities and my weaknesses. Teach me to depend on You and not on myself, and do Your mighty work through me. Amen.

== May 12
Q: What truth you already know could you put into practice today?
P: Lord, I do not want to be ever learning but never changed. Help me take one truth I already know and live it today, so my learning becomes living. Amen.

== May 13
Q: Where in your life are there leaves without fruit, and how can you invite Jesus to look closely?
P: Lord Jesus, look closely at my life. Where there are only leaves, grow genuine fruit, and keep me connected to You, the source of real life. Amen.

== May 14
Q: Which quality of heavenly wisdom, pure, peaceable, gentle, or merciful, most needs to grow in you?
P: Lord, give me the wisdom that is from above: pure, peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality or hypocrisy. Amen.

== May 15
Q: What step of obedience is God asking of you, trusting that understanding will follow?
P: Lord, the fear of You is the beginning of wisdom. Help me do what You have shown me, deepen my understanding as I obey, and let my life become a song of praise. Amen.

== May 16
Q: Before you teach or correct someone else, what does God want to teach you first?
P: Lord, search my heart before I correct anyone else. Keep me teachable, show me where I need to grow, and help me share what I learn with grace. Amen.

== May 17
Q: What feels unstable in your life right now, and how can God's wisdom be your anchor?
P: Lord, You are the stability of my times. When everything shifts around me, anchor me in Your wisdom, and let reverence for You be my treasure. Amen.

== May 18
Q: Where are you tempted to trade truth for approval or convenience?
P: Lord, help me buy the truth and never sell it. Give me courage to value truth more than approval, and to hold on to wisdom whatever it costs. Amen.

== May 19
Q: What gift has God given you to help others, and whose gifts have helped you grow?
P: Lord, thank You for the different gifts You give by Your Spirit. Show me how You have equipped me, and help me use what You have given for the good of others. Amen.

== May 20
Q: How can you spend unhurried time with Jesus today so that your life shows you have been with Him?
P: Lord Jesus, I want to be with You. Let time in Your presence shape my words and actions, so that others can tell I have been with You. Amen.

== May 21
Q: Where have you been searching for wisdom outside of Christ?
P: Lord Jesus, in You are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. Draw me deeper into You, and let me discover Your riches in Your Word. Amen.

== May 22
Q: Is there an old entanglement pulling at you that you need to run from to Jesus?
P: Lord Jesus, You set me free. Guard my heart from drifting back into what once enslaved me, and when I am pulled away, help me run to You. Amen.

== May 23
Q: What are you most afraid to lose, and can you treasure Jesus above it?
P: Lord Jesus, knowing You is worth more than anything I could gain or lose. Help me hold all things loosely and treasure You above all. Amen.

== May 24
Q: When doubts arise, what do you know for certain about Jesus?
P: Lord Jesus, Son of God, You have come and given me understanding to know the true God. When doubts arise, bring me back to what I know in You. Amen.

== May 25
Q: Where does your faith need to grow deeper roots to bear real fruit?
P: Lord Jesus, teach me what You want me to learn about fruitfulness. Deepen my roots in You, and let real fruit grow in my life. Amen.

== May 26
Q: How do you usually respond to correction, and what might you learn from it this time?
P: Lord, give me a heart that welcomes instruction. When I am corrected, help me listen instead of defending myself, so I may grow wiser. Amen.

== May 27
Q: Which area of your life, mind, body, spirit, or relationships, most needs to grow right now?
P: Lord Jesus, You grew in wisdom and stature and in favor with God and people. Help me grow in every area of my life, one small step at a time. Amen.

== May 28
Q: Who has helped you grow in faith, and whom are you investing in?
P: Lord, thank You for those who brought me Your truth. Make me faithful to the people I am investing in, so we may rejoice together on the day of the Lord Jesus. Amen.

== May 29
Q: How can you invite the Holy Spirit to be your teacher as you read God's Word today?
P: Holy Spirit, be my teacher. Open my eyes as I read Your Word, and help me rely on Your wisdom rather than my own. Amen.

== May 30
Q: What decision in front of you needs good judgment from God?
P: Lord, teach me good judgment and knowledge, for I believe Your commandments. Help me apply Your Word wisely in the decisions before me today. Amen.

== May 31
Q: Who has wandered or been slow to learn that you can keep praying for, including yourself?
P: Lord, You are a patient teacher. Thank You for not giving up on those who wander. Open the eyes of the ones I pray for, and keep teaching me too. Amen.
