# June 2027: Courage & Strength (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Jun 01
Q: Where are you spending your strength, and how could it be redirected toward what is good?
P: Lord, keep me from wasting my strength in the wrong places. Turn my energy toward justice, integrity, and protecting the vulnerable, with strength that comes from You. Amen.

== Jun 02
Q: What feels out of control in your life that you can thank God for reigning over?
P: Lord God Almighty, You are, You were, and You are to come. I give You thanks that You reign over all, even over what feels out of control in my life. Amen.

== Jun 03
Q: Where might God's power be working through humility or sacrifice in a way you did not expect?
P: Lord Jesus, I am amazed at Your mighty power. Help me trust Your strength even when it works through humility, sacrifice, and love rather than what I expected. Amen.

== Jun 04
Q: What task can you invite the Spirit to empower today, giving Him the credit afterward?
P: Holy Spirit, I cannot accomplish Your purposes in my own power. Work through my effort today, and let all the credit go to You. Amen.

== Jun 05
Q: What stronghold in your life can you bring before God in prayer and speak His truth over?
P: Lord, the weapons You give are mighty to pull down strongholds. I bring You the fears, lies, and habits that hold me, and I trust Your truth to bring them down. Amen.

== Jun 06
Q: What accusation do you keep listening to that Christ's victory has already overruled?
P: Lord Jesus, the accuser has been cast down by Your victory. When old accusations rise, help me stand in Your grace, knowing I am forgiven and free. Amen.

== Jun 07
Q: How does it steady you to know that the One upholding all things is also holding you?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the brightness of God's glory and uphold all things by Your word. You have purged my sins and sit enthroned on high. I rest in You today. Amen.

== Jun 08
Q: What battle are you facing today that you can welcome the King of glory into?
P: Lord, strong and mighty, mighty in battle, You fight for Your people. I lift up my head and open the gates of my heart to You, my King of glory. Amen.

== Jun 09
Q: What impossible situation are you tempted to limit God in?
P: Lord, You are the God of the living and You raise the dead. Keep me from underestimating You, and help me bring every impossible situation to You in faith. Amen.

== Jun 10
Q: To whom or what are you giving your strength, and is it leading toward the Lamb or away from Him?
P: Lord Jesus, You are King of kings and Lord of lords. Keep me from giving my strength to what opposes You, and help me give it wholly to You. Amen.

== Jun 11
Q: Who around you has a fearful heart and needs to hear, 'Be strong, fear not'?
P: Lord, You will come and save Your people. Strengthen my fearful heart, and give me words of courage for someone who needs to hear Your promise today. Amen.

== Jun 12
Q: What promise in Scripture can you hold on to and bring to God in prayer today?
P: Lord, teach me to know Your Scriptures and Your power. Help me find Your promises in Your Word, and pray them back to You with faith and expectation. Amen.

== Jun 13
Q: What challenge seems too big, and how might wisdom from God help where force cannot?
P: Lord, Your wisdom is greater than any strength. When I face walls that seem too high, give me Your insight to find the way through. Amen.

== Jun 14
Q: In a disagreement today, how can you speak truth with respect and leave judgment to God?
P: Lord, give me the strength of restraint. When I am frustrated, help me speak truth with respect, and leave judgment in Your hands. Amen.

== Jun 15
Q: What recent victory or answered prayer can you give God the glory for today?
P: Lord, be exalted in Your own strength. Every victory in my life comes from You. I will sing and praise Your power. Amen.

== Jun 16
Q: Who has drifted into harmful patterns that you could pray for and speak to with restoration in mind?
P: Lord Jesus, give me Your power and Your heart for those who have drifted. Help me pray for them faithfully and speak with love, aiming always for restoration. Amen.

== Jun 17
Q: Where have you been going through the motions of faith without seeking God's power?
P: Lord, I do not want the form of godliness without the power. Make my faith real and alive, and let Your Spirit work where I have been going through the motions. Amen.

== Jun 18
Q: What outcome do you need to release as you humble yourself under God's mighty hand?
P: Lord, I humble myself under Your mighty hand. I release my need to control the outcome, and I trust You to lift me up in due time. Amen.

== Jun 19
Q: Who do you say Jesus is, and how can you speak of Him with quiet boldness today?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the Christ, the Savior of the world. Give me Your quiet boldness to speak about You to those who are still wondering. Amen.

== Jun 20
Q: Who around you is hurting today, and how could you show them God's tender mercy?
P: Lord, You are full of compassion and tender mercy. Thank You for Your gentleness in my hardest days. Help me endure as Job did, and show Your kindness to someone who is hurting. Amen.

== Jun 21
Q: What is one thing you could do today that honors Jesus openly, even if it costs you something?
P: Lord Jesus, give me the boldness of Joseph of Arimathea. When love for You calls for courage, help me honor You openly, whatever it costs. Amen.

== Jun 22
Q: How could you offer your strength to Jesus as an act of worship today?
P: Worthy is the Lamb who was slain! Lord Jesus, I give You my praise, and I offer You my strength to serve, to love, and to honor You today. Amen.

== Jun 23
Q: Where can you speak about the risen Jesus with confidence, or show grace to someone in need?
P: Lord, let great power and great grace rest on me. Give me boldness to witness to Your resurrection, and help me care practically for someone in need. Amen.

== Jun 24
Q: What responsibility can you receive as a gift of grace and fulfill by leaning on God's power?
P: Lord, every calling I have is a gift of Your grace. Help me receive my responsibilities with gratitude, and fulfill them by the working of Your power. Amen.

== Jun 25
Q: What are you facing today that you need Christ's strength to endure with contentment?
P: Lord Jesus, I can do all things through You who strengthen me. Teach me contentment in every circumstance, and give me strength for whatever this day holds. Amen.

== Jun 26
Q: What fear could God's perfect love drive out of your heart today?
P: Lord, perfect Your love in me so that I may have boldness instead of fear. Let Your love drive out my fears and flow through me in practical acts of love. Amen.

== Jun 27
Q: Whose calling are you comparing yours to, and how can you embrace your own?
P: Lord, the same power that worked in Peter and Paul is at work in me. Free me from comparison, and help me embrace the calling You have given me. Amen.

== Jun 28
Q: Where is God calling you to be bold even after past hurts make you hesitate?
P: Lord, make me bold in You. When past hurts make me hesitate, let my courage rise again from You, and help me do what You have called me to do. Amen.

== Jun 29
Q: What strength do you have that you could acknowledge as coming from God?
P: Lord, all glory and strength belong to You. I give You the glory You deserve, and I receive Your strength and peace for whatever I face. Amen.

== Jun 30
Q: Where are you running on empty, and how can you wait on God for renewed strength?
P: Lord, You give power to the faint and increase strength to those who have no might. I bring You my weakness and wait on You. Renew me for the days ahead. Amen.
