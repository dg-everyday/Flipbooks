# July 2027: Peace, Rest & Renewal (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Jul 01
Q: Where could you create a small space of quiet to sit with God today?
P: Lord, make my heart a peaceable habitation, a sure dwelling, and a quiet resting place. Settle me in Your presence, and let Your peace be my home. Amen.

== Jul 02
Q: How does Jesus' promise of waking change the way you think about death and loss?
P: Lord Jesus, You call death sleep, and You are the One who will wake us. Take away my fear of the end, and let me rest in Your promise of life. Amen.

== Jul 03
Q: Where do you feel overlooked, and how does it comfort you that Jesus stands still for you?
P: Lord Jesus, You stand still for me and call my name. When I feel lost in the crowd, help me take comfort, rise, and come to You. Amen.

== Jul 04
Q: What invitation to rest from God have you been refusing to hear?
P: Lord, You offer rest to the weary and refreshing to the tired. Help me hear Your invitation, stop striving, and receive the rest of trusting You. Amen.

== Jul 05
Q: What burden have you been carrying that you can exchange for Jesus' yoke today?
P: Lord Jesus, You are meek and lowly in heart. I lay my heavy burden at Your feet and take Your yoke instead. Teach me to walk in step with You and find rest. Amen.

== Jul 06
Q: What long struggle can you bring to Jesus in faith, even if your faith feels small?
P: Lord Jesus, You welcome me and speak peace over my life. I reach out to You in my long struggle. Make me whole, and let me go in Your peace. Amen.

== Jul 07
Q: Whose comfort has refreshed you lately, and whom could you refresh today?
P: Lord, thank You for the people who have refreshed my spirit. Make me part of Your chain of comfort, and help me encourage someone today. Amen.

== Jul 08
Q: If you are in a quieter season, how can you use it to grow stronger in faith?
P: Lord, thank You for seasons of rest. Build me up in faith, let me walk in reverence for You and in the comfort of the Holy Spirit, and let my faith grow strong. Amen.

== Jul 09
Q: What storm do you need Jesus to speak 'Peace, be still' over today?
P: Lord Jesus, wind and waves obey Your voice. Speak Your peace over the storm in my life and over my anxious heart: Peace, be still. Amen.

== Jul 10
Q: Where can you offer God's peace today, whether or not it is received?
P: Lord, make me a carrier of Your peace in my home, my work, and my conversations. When my peace is not received, keep it with me, and lead me to hearts ready to receive it. Amen.

== Jul 11
Q: Where are you under attack, and how does it help to know the God of peace fights for you?
P: God of peace, You will crush the enemy under my feet. When temptation, discouragement, or fear attacks me, remind me that victory is certain and Your grace is enough. Amen.

== Jul 12
Q: Whom are you missing today, and how can the hope of the resurrection comfort you?
P: Lord Jesus, You died and rose again, and those who sleep in You are not lost. Comfort me in my grief, and fill me with hope of the day You will bring them with You. Amen.

== Jul 13
Q: Whom could you reach out to today to ask how they are really doing?
P: Lord Jesus, I trust You with my plans. Give me a caring heart for those I love, and help me reach out to someone today with genuine concern. Amen.

== Jul 14
Q: Who are the faithful few who have comforted you, and who needs your comfort today?
P: Lord, thank You for the faithful people who stayed beside me and comforted me. Make me a quiet comfort to someone who is weary today. Amen.

== Jul 15
Q: What blessings can you recall today to help your soul return to rest?
P: Lord, You have dealt bountifully with me. When my soul is anxious, remind me of Your faithfulness, and call me home to rest in You. Amen.

== Jul 16
Q: In what storm have you wondered whether Jesus cares, and how can you rest as He rested?
P: Lord Jesus, You care for me even when I cannot feel it. You are in the boat with me. Teach me to rest in the Father's care as You did, even in the storm. Amen.

== Jul 17
Q: Where have you grown spiritually sleepy, and what purposeful step can wake you up?
P: Lord, keep me from slothfulness and drift. Wake me up, feed my soul with Your Word, and let my rest renew me for a life fully awake to You. Amen.

== Jul 18
Q: When you face criticism, how can you rest in who God says you are instead of proving yourself?
P: Lord Jesus, You held Your peace when You were falsely accused. Give me Your peace, so I know when to be silent and when to speak, and rest in who You say I am. Amen.

== Jul 19
Q: Who is different from you whom you can pray for and show kindness to today?
P: Lord Jesus, You are Lord of all, and Your peace is for everyone. Help me see people who are different from me as those You love, and show them kindness. Amen.

== Jul 20
Q: How can the peace and mercy you receive from God overflow to others today?
P: Lord, I am a new creation in Christ. Let Your peace and mercy rest on me, and help me be gentle and merciful with others who are still learning. Amen.

== Jul 21
Q: How do you want to spend the rest of your time, and what would it look like to give it to God?
P: Lord, whatever has happened in my past, I give You the rest of my time. Let Your will guide my choices, and let me find rest in surrender. Amen.

== Jul 22
Q: What pressure to rush can you answer today by returning to God in quiet confidence?
P: Lord, in returning and rest I am saved; in quietness and confidence is my strength. When I feel pressure to rush, help me pause, return to You, and trust. Amen.

== Jul 23
Q: How might treating others as the beloved of God change one of your interactions today?
P: Father, thank You that I am beloved and called to be a saint. Let Your love free me from competition, and help me treat others as Your beloved too. Amen.

== Jul 24
Q: Whose faith is being tested that you could encourage and pray with today?
P: Lord, thank You for those who have strengthened my faith in hard times. Make me a Timothy to someone today, to establish and comfort them in their faith. Amen.

== Jul 25
Q: Is there a relationship where peace is missing, and what step toward reconciliation could you take?
P: Lord, as I look forward to Your new creation, help me live in peace and purity now. Give me courage to take a step toward reconciliation today. Amen.

== Jul 26
Q: What worries can you hand to God tonight so that you can lie down in peace?
P: Lord, You alone make me dwell in safety. I hand You my worries tonight, knowing You are awake when I sleep. I will lay me down in peace. Amen.

== Jul 27
Q: Whom are you called to guide with loving correction, and how do you receive God's correction?
P: Lord, give me wisdom and patience to guide the young people in my care with love. When You correct me, help me receive it as a sign of Your love. Amen.

== Jul 28
Q: Which relationship needs you to pursue peace, and how can you take one step toward it?
P: Lord, help me follow peace with all people, and holiness too. Show me one step I can take toward peace, and grow Your character in me. Amen.

== Jul 29
Q: What is following Jesus costing you, and how can you pray for those who are persecuted?
P: Lord, my soul is safe in Your keeping. Give me courage to stay faithful whatever it costs, and comfort and strengthen those who suffer for Your name. Amen.

== Jul 30
Q: What do you need to lay aside so that God's Word can be planted deep in your soul?
P: Lord, help me lay aside what pollutes my heart, the bitterness, the careless words, the unhealthy habits. Plant Your Word deep within me, and let it bear fruit. Amen.

== Jul 31
Q: Where is the risen Jesus sending you today, carrying His peace?
P: Risen Lord, You speak peace to my fears and send me as the Father sent You. Settle my heart with Your peace, and send me out into my family, work, and community. Amen.
