# August 2027: Service & Compassion (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Aug 01
Q: Who near you is poor and needy, in money, health, friendship, or hope, and how could you help?
P: Lord, I am poor and needy before You, and You are my help and my deliverer. Let the compassion You show me flow toward someone in need today. Amen.

== Aug 02
Q: Whom do you need to forgive, and how can remembering God's forgiveness help you take a step toward it?
P: Lord, You have forgiven me so much for Christ's sake. Make me kind and tenderhearted, and help me take a step toward forgiving the one who hurt me. Amen.

== Aug 03
Q: Who may feel forgotten that you could let know they are on your mind today?
P: Lord, I am poor and needy, yet You think upon me. Thank You for never forgetting me. Help me remember someone who feels forgotten today. Amen.

== Aug 04
Q: What possession has a grip on your heart, and how could giving loosen it?
P: Lord Jesus, You want my whole heart. Loosen the grip my possessions have on me, make me generous to those in need, and help me follow You. Amen.

== Aug 05
Q: Is your service flowing from time at Jesus' feet, or has it become anxious and resentful?
P: Lord Jesus, before I serve, let me sit at Your feet. Fill my heart with Your presence, so that I serve from fullness and joy rather than worry and resentment. Amen.

== Aug 06
Q: Is your compassion sincere, or is it sometimes about how you look to others?
P: Lord, search my heart and purify my motives. Let my compassion be real, and help me care for those in need without seeking credit. Amen.

== Aug 07
Q: How could you scatter some goodness to someone in need today?
P: Lord, You are able to make all grace abound. Make me a cheerful and generous giver, scattering goodness to those in need, knowing what I give is never lost. Amen.

== Aug 08
Q: Where could you serve today without seeking recognition?
P: Lord Jesus, You came not to be served but to serve and to give Your life for many. Make me a servant like You, content to serve quietly and unseen. Amen.

== Aug 09
Q: What earthly thing are you clinging to that you could place in God's hands?
P: Lord, nothing placed in Your hands can ever be lost. Help me hold earthly things loosely and give myself, and all I have, generously to You. Amen.

== Aug 10
Q: What wholehearted act of love for Jesus could you offer today, in worship or in service?
P: Lord Jesus, let my love for You be wholehearted, not measured. Receive my worship, and let it overflow into practical care for the poor. Amen.

== Aug 11
Q: What habit of generosity could you build so that compassion becomes part of who you are?
P: Lord, let compassion be woven into my everyday life as it was for You and Your disciples. Help me set aside something regularly for those in need. Amen.

== Aug 12
Q: How could you be part of God's answer for someone who is thirsty for water, hope, or love?
P: Lord, You hear the poor and needy, and You will not forsake them. Show me how to be part of Your answer for someone who is thirsty today. Amen.

== Aug 13
Q: How can your thanks for the victory God has given you overflow into giving today?
P: Thanks be to You, O God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ. Let my gratitude overflow in generous giving to someone today. Amen.

== Aug 14
Q: Whose worship or service have you been tempted to criticize, and what good could you do instead?
P: Lord Jesus, keep me from murmuring against others. Help me focus on my own heart, and turn my criticism into compassionate action for someone in need. Amen.

== Aug 15
Q: Has someone misunderstood your good intentions, and can you let it go with humility?
P: Lord, when my service is misunderstood, keep me from defensiveness. Help me keep serving with love and leave the outcome in Your hands. Amen.

== Aug 16
Q: Who around you has no helper, and how could you step in today?
P: Lord Jesus, compassionate King, You deliver the needy who cry out. Open my eyes to someone who has no helper, and make me a reflection of Your care. Amen.

== Aug 17
Q: How has God's grace made you generous, and is there anyone you need to make things right with?
P: Lord Jesus, You came to my house as You came to Zacchaeus. Let Your grace loosen my grip on what I have, and make me generous and quick to make things right. Amen.

== Aug 18
Q: How would you simply and honestly explain your hope to a friend who asked?
P: Lord, be set apart as holy in my heart. Make me ready to give a reason for the hope that is in me, with gentleness and respect. Amen.

== Aug 19
Q: Where are you frustrated by your own weakness, and how can you thank God for Jesus in it?
P: I thank You, God, through Jesus Christ my Lord. When I am frustrated by my weakness, help me keep serving You with a willing heart while You keep working in me. Amen.

== Aug 20
Q: How could you encourage someone you love toward service rather than status today?
P: Lord Jesus, greatness in Your kingdom is found in serving. Help me model that for the people I love, and serve someone today in Your spirit. Amen.

== Aug 21
Q: How does God's gift of His Son inspire you to give freely today?
P: Father, You have given me eternal life in Your Son. Thank You for not holding back Your most precious gift. Let Your generosity make me generous today. Amen.

== Aug 22
Q: What future concern can you trust to the Lord while you keep serving faithfully?
P: Lord, You will deliver me from every evil work and preserve me for Your heavenly kingdom. I trust You with my future. To You be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

== Aug 23
Q: Where is your voice needed to speak up for someone who cannot speak for themselves?
P: Lord, show me where my voice is needed. Give me courage to open my mouth, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy. Amen.

== Aug 24
Q: Has guilt been driving your service, and how can you let Christ cleanse your conscience?
P: Lord Jesus, Your blood purges my conscience from dead works. Free me from serving out of guilt, and let me serve the living God with a willing heart. Amen.

== Aug 25
Q: How could you serve in a way that gives life to those around you today?
P: Lord, make me an able minister of the new covenant. Help me serve in a way that points people to Your life-giving grace and not only to rules. Amen.

== Aug 26
Q: What honest need can you bring to Jesus today, even with uncertain faith?
P: Lord, I believe; help my unbelief. Have compassion on me in my need, and make me compassionate toward others who are struggling to believe. Amen.

== Aug 27
Q: How can you honor the name of Jesus with your words and actions today?
P: Lord Jesus, Your name is above every name. Help me honor You today, and follow Your example of humble, generous self-giving. Amen.

== Aug 28
Q: Where do you feel stretched beyond your ability, and can you humbly ask God for more grace?
P: Lord, You give more grace. I humble myself before You and admit my need. Meet me with Your grace, which never runs dry. Amen.

== Aug 29
Q: Who serves faithfully behind the scenes whom you could thank today?
P: Lord, thank You for the faithful people who serve quietly behind the scenes. Help me honor them, and make me beloved and faithful in my own quiet service. Amen.

== Aug 30
Q: How can the righteousness God has given you overflow into right living today?
P: Lord, no law could give me life, but You have made me right through Christ. Let Your gift of righteousness overflow into fairness, truth, and good works today. Amen.

== Aug 31
Q: How can you honor the gift of the Holy Spirit by the way you live and love today?
P: Father, thank You for giving me Your Holy Spirit. Help me honor that gift by living for You and sharing Your love freely with everyone I meet. Amen.
