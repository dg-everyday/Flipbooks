# September 2027: Hope & Perseverance (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Sep 01
Q: What are you waiting for that you cannot yet see, and how can hope give you patience in it?
P: Lord, You see what I cannot yet see. Steady my heart with hope, and give me patience to wait for all that You have promised. Amen.

== Sep 02
Q: Who shares your struggles as a companion, and where might God reveal Himself in your hard place?
P: Lord Jesus, I am not alone in my trials. Thank You for the companions You give me, and meet me in my hardest places as You met John on Patmos. Amen.

== Sep 03
Q: Are you tempted to quit the calling God has given you, and what would it take to finish well?
P: Lord, help me watch in all things, endure hardship, keep sharing the good news, and make full proof of the work You have given me. Amen.

== Sep 04
Q: What tribulation are you facing, and how can you continue in the faith through it?
P: Lord, You do not promise an easy road, but You promise to strengthen my soul. Help me continue in the faith through every hardship on the way to Your kingdom. Amen.

== Sep 05
Q: Where do you feel stretched or weary in serving, and how can you serve with patience?
P: Lord, give me much patience in afflictions, needs, and distresses. Help me serve You faithfully under pressure, as a testimony to Your grace. Amen.

== Sep 06
Q: What past faithfulness of God can you call to remembrance to strengthen you today?
P: Lord, You sustained me in former days, and You are the same God today. Help me remember Your faithfulness, and let those memories give me strength to keep going. Amen.

== Sep 07
Q: Which people in your life could you invest in, knowing they are part of a hope that lasts?
P: Lord Jesus, thank You for the people You have placed in my life. Help me love and invest in them, knowing that what we build in You will last forever. Amen.

== Sep 08
Q: Where are you being treated unfairly, and how can you respond with integrity before God?
P: Lord, You see when I suffer wrongfully. Help me respond with integrity rather than retaliation, and trust You to judge rightly. Amen.

== Sep 09
Q: Who is alone, grieving, or without stable family that you could visit or help today?
P: Father, let my faith be pure and practical. Show me someone in affliction whom I can visit, call, or help, and keep me unspotted from the world. Amen.

== Sep 10
Q: What passage of Jesus' words could you continue in today, reading, reflecting, and practicing it?
P: Lord Jesus, I want to be Your disciple indeed. Help me continue in Your word day by day, so that I may know the truth and be made free. Amen.

== Sep 11
Q: Are you still trying to pay back a debt that God has already forgiven?
P: Lord, You forgave a debt I could never repay. Help me stop trying to pay back what You have already released, and make me patient and merciful with others. Amen.

== Sep 12
Q: What are you waiting for, and is your deepest hope resting in God Himself?
P: Lord, what do I wait for? My hope is in You. I hold my hopes with open hands, and I rest my deepest hope in You alone. Amen.

== Sep 13
Q: Which believers, near or far, could you intentionally pray for today?
P: Lord, teach me to pray always, in the Spirit, with perseverance. Help me lift up other believers today, near and far, and strengthen Your whole family. Amen.

== Sep 14
Q: What simple gospel truth from the beginning do you need to let settle deeper into your heart?
P: Lord, let what I heard from the beginning abide in me. When new and confusing ideas come, keep me rooted in the simple truth about Jesus. Amen.

== Sep 15
Q: What important decision could you give God unhurried time in prayer about?
P: Lord Jesus, You prayed all night before You chose Your apostles. Teach me to turn to prayer first, and give me guidance for the decisions in front of me. Amen.

== Sep 16
Q: When the news feels overwhelming, how does knowing how the story ends give you hope?
P: Lord Jesus, You told us that hard days would come, and You told us how the story ends. When the world feels overwhelming, anchor my hope in Your return. Amen.

== Sep 17
Q: Where could your steady, consistent faithfulness make a difference over time?
P: Lord, thank You that You use steady faithfulness. Help me keep praying, sharing, and serving, even when I see no immediate results. Amen.

== Sep 18
Q: Where might you willingly set aside a right for the sake of others?
P: Lord, thank You for providing for those who serve You. Help me serve gladly, trusting You with my needs, and willing to set aside my rights out of love. Amen.

== Sep 19
Q: What plans or fears do you need to surrender as you give yourself afresh to the Lord?
P: Lord, before I give anything else, I give myself to You. I surrender my plans and fears, and I ask You to work through my surrendered heart. Amen.

== Sep 20
Q: What choice in front of you would honor God and last, even if it costs you something now?
P: Lord, help me look beyond the passing pleasures of the moment. Give me courage, like Moses, to choose faithfulness and to keep my eyes on the reward You give. Amen.

== Sep 21
Q: Where are you spending energy on what perishes instead of what lasts?
P: Lord Jesus, You give the food that endures to everlasting life. Help me seek what lasts, receive the life You give, and share Your generosity with others. Amen.

== Sep 22
Q: What plan can you hold with open hands today, saying, 'If the Lord will'?
P: Lord, my life is a vapor, and my future is in Your hands. I bring You my plans and hold them loosely. If You will, I will live and do this or that. Amen.

== Sep 23
Q: What promise from God's Word could you cling to while you wait in the depths?
P: Lord, I wait for You; my soul waits, and in Your word I hope. When I am in the depths, help me cling to Your promises until morning comes. Amen.

== Sep 24
Q: What need can you bring to Jesus today, receiving Him gladly as the crowd did?
P: Lord Jesus, I receive You gladly. I bring You my needs today, and I wait with eager expectation for Your return. Amen.

== Sep 25
Q: Is your perseverance still flowing from love for Jesus, or only from duty?
P: Lord Jesus, thank You for seeing every burden I carry and every labor I do for Your name. Renew my first love, so my perseverance flows from a warm heart toward You. Amen.

== Sep 26
Q: Whom are you tempted to repay for a wrong, and how can you wait on the Lord instead?
P: Lord, keep me from repaying evil for evil. I bring You my hurt and wait on You. Be my defender, and free me from the need for revenge. Amen.

== Sep 27
Q: What hardship are you facing that cannot separate you from the love of Christ?
P: Lord Jesus, nothing can separate me from Your love, not trouble, distress, or danger. Let me rest in Your unbreakable love and share it with someone who is suffering. Amen.

== Sep 28
Q: Who has asked you for patience or forgiveness, and how can you extend God's mercy to them?
P: Lord, You have forgiven me so much. Soften my heart, and help me show the same patience and mercy to those who owe me far less. Amen.

== Sep 29
Q: What imperfection has been keeping you from drawing near to God?
P: Lord, the law made nothing perfect, but Jesus brought a better hope. I will not hold back because of my imperfections. I draw near to You through Him. Amen.

== Sep 30
Q: If God feels distant right now, how can you keep waiting and looking for Him?
P: Lord, even when You seem hidden, I will wait upon You and look for You. Keep my hope alive, and let me see Your goodness in Your time. Amen.
