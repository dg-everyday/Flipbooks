# October 2027: Gratitude & Contentment (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Oct 01
Q: Before you ask God for anything today, what specific blessings can you thank Him for?
P: Blessing, glory, wisdom, thanksgiving, honour, power, and might be unto You, our God, for ever. Give me a grateful heart that sees Your goodness everywhere. Amen.

== Oct 02
Q: What song of thanksgiving could you sing to God today?
P: Lord, You heal the brokenhearted and call the stars by name. I sing to You with thanksgiving, and I delight in praising You today. Amen.

== Oct 03
Q: What are you tempted to complain about that you could thank God for instead?
P: Lord, through Jesus I offer You the sacrifice of praise. Even when I do not feel it, I declare that You are good, and I give thanks to Your name. Amen.

== Oct 04
Q: How has God enriched you, and how could you share that blessing so others give thanks?
P: Lord, You enrich me so that I can be generous. Let Your blessings flow through me to others, so that their gratitude rises back to You. Amen.

== Oct 05
Q: Which of God's mighty works in your life could you remember and praise Him for today?
P: Lord, I remember the mighty works You have done in my life and in the lives of others. Let my memories turn into praise, and strengthen me for what lies ahead. Amen.

== Oct 06
Q: What are you grateful for that you could share out loud so someone else can say Amen?
P: Lord, let my thanksgiving be shared and understood. Help me speak of Your goodness so others can join in and say Amen with me. Amen.

== Oct 07
Q: Whom could you rise up and call blessed with specific words of appreciation today?
P: Lord, thank You for the faithful people You have placed in my life. Help me honor them openly, and never take their love for granted. Amen.

== Oct 08
Q: Has the joy you felt when you first understood God's grace faded, and how could it be restored?
P: Lord, restore to me the joy I felt when I first knew Your grace. Renew my gratitude, and let it overflow in generosity toward others. Amen.

== Oct 09
Q: How could you weave praise into the ordinary moments of your day?
P: God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, You are blessed for evermore. Let praise fill my ordinary moments, and make me a person of truth. Amen.

== Oct 10
Q: Is God nudging you to turn from something, and can you see His guidance as a blessing?
P: Lord, You sent Jesus to bless me by turning me from my iniquities. Thank You for every destructive path You have turned me from. Help me follow Your guidance today. Amen.

== Oct 11
Q: Whom could you praise today for doing well, whether a coworker, public servant, or neighbor?
P: Lord, every good thing comes from You. Help me notice and praise those who do well, and let my own life be marked by doing good. Amen.

== Oct 12
Q: What has God done in your life that you could tell someone about plainly and truthfully?
P: Lord Jesus, once I was blind, but now I see. Give me courage to give You honest praise and tell someone plainly what You have done for me. Amen.

== Oct 13
Q: Looking back over your life, where can you see God's patient faithfulness in every season?
P: Blessed be the Lord from everlasting to everlasting. Thank You for Your patient faithfulness through every season of my life. I join all Your people in saying Amen. Amen.

== Oct 14
Q: Where can you show goodness, act rightly, or speak truthfully today?
P: Holy Spirit, grow Your fruit in me: goodness, righteousness, and truth. Let my life reflect the light of the One who made me new. Amen.

== Oct 15
Q: What about Jesus, His humility or His majesty, could you marvel at today?
P: Lord Jesus, You are fully human and God over all, blessed forever. I marvel at Your humility and Your majesty, and I give You my wholehearted praise. Amen.

== Oct 16
Q: Where could you take one step toward peace, between friends, in your family, or in your own heart?
P: Father, make me a peacemaker. Give me courage to build bridges and seek reconciliation, so that I reflect Your heart as Your child. Amen.

== Oct 17
Q: Where do you need to cry Hosanna, both as a plea for help and a shout of praise?
P: Hosanna, King Jesus! Save me where I need saving, and receive my praise. Blessed is Your kingdom that comes in the name of the Lord. Amen.

== Oct 18
Q: How could you bless and celebrate someone close to you today instead of taking them for granted?
P: Lord, thank You for the relationships You have given me. Help me rejoice in them, celebrate the people I love, and be content with Your good gifts. Amen.

== Oct 19
Q: When you are drawn into an argument, how can remembering God's goodness help you choose grace?
P: Lord, keep me from a contentious spirit. Fill my heart with gratitude for Your goodness, and help me choose grace and unity over the need to be right. Amen.

== Oct 20
Q: What injustice are you carrying that you can trust God to set right?
P: Lord, You have not forgotten those who suffer injustice. I praise You in advance for the justice You will bring, and I pray for those still waiting. Amen.

== Oct 21
Q: What small thing could you give thanks for and offer to God to multiply?
P: Lord Jesus, You gave thanks for five loaves and two fish. I thank You for what I have, even when it seems small, and I offer it to You to multiply. Amen.

== Oct 22
Q: What prayer has God answered that you have not yet thanked Him for?
P: Lord, You save, gather, and restore Your people so that we can give You thanks. I thank You now for the prayers You have answered. Amen.

== Oct 23
Q: How could you replace careless or unkind speech with thanksgiving today?
P: Lord, guard my words today. When I am tempted toward careless or unkind speech, fill my mouth with thanksgiving instead. Amen.

== Oct 24
Q: What wonderful things has God done that you can name as you praise Him today?
P: O Lord, You are my God. I will exalt You and praise Your name, for You have done wonderful things, and Your plans are faithful and true. Amen.

== Oct 25
Q: What empty place in your life can you bring to God, trusting His abundance?
P: Lord, You tell the barren to rejoice. I bring You my empty places and my unmet longings, and I trust that in Your presence there is always hope. Amen.

== Oct 26
Q: What ordinary task entrusted to you could you do faithfully today?
P: Lord, help me be found faithful in the ordinary tasks You have given me. Keep me serving well, and fill me with joy as I wait for Your return. Amen.

== Oct 27
Q: What midnight are you in, and how could you choose to pray and sing praises anyway?
P: Lord, even at midnight and in chains, You are worthy of praise. Help me praise You in my hardest moments, and let others hear of Your goodness. Amen.

== Oct 28
Q: How could remembering that Jesus gave thanks before His suffering shape your gratitude today?
P: Lord Jesus, You blessed the bread and gave Your body for me. Thank You for Your sacrifice. Teach me to give thanks even in hard days, and let gratitude shape my whole life. Amen.

== Oct 29
Q: What recent victory or blessing could you pause to bless God for?
P: Lord Jesus, my great High Priest, You bless me continually. I give You the credit for every victory and blessing in my life, and I thank You. Amen.

== Oct 30
Q: Who has been entrusted to your care, and how can you serve them faithfully today?
P: Lord, thank You for the people You have entrusted to my care. Help me serve them faithfully, giving them what they need at the right time. Amen.

== Oct 31
Q: How does it feel to know that God credits righteousness to you without your works?
P: Lord, thank You for the blessedness of being forgiven and made right with You, not by my works but by Your grace. I rest in Your gift today. Amen.
