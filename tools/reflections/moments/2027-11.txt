# November 2027: Prayer & God's Presence (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Nov 01
Q: What request can you bring to God today, resting on His faithfulness rather than your own record?
P: Lord, hear my prayer. I do not come on the strength of my own record, but on Your faithfulness and righteousness. Answer me, faithful God. Amen.

== Nov 02
Q: How does it encourage you that your prayers and quiet generosity are remembered before God?
P: Lord, thank You that You hear my prayers and remember my gifts. Help me keep praying and giving faithfully, trusting You to answer in Your perfect time. Amen.

== Nov 03
Q: What heavy thing can you pour out honestly before God, knowing He sees your tears?
P: Lord, You heard Hezekiah's prayer and saw his tears. I turn to You with what I am carrying. Thank You that I am not crying into empty air. Amen.

== Nov 04
Q: Whose names can you write down and pray for, longing to see them come to faith?
P: Lord, my heart's desire is for the people I love to be saved. I bring them to You by name. Keep me praying for them, for You are able to save. Amen.

== Nov 05
Q: What is crowding out your time with God, and how can you make room for prayer?
P: Lord Jesus, clear away whatever crowds out my time with You. Make my heart a house of prayer where I meet You, not a marketplace of distraction. Amen.

== Nov 06
Q: Where do you feel unsettled or weary as a sojourner, and how can you bring your tears to God?
P: Lord, I am a stranger and a sojourner with You. Hear my cry, do not be silent at my tears, and give me peace in Your presence along the way. Amen.

== Nov 07
Q: What anguish could you bring to God with honest prayer and surrender to His will?
P: Lord Jesus, You prayed with strong crying and tears, and You were heard. Teach me to pray honestly in my anguish and to find strength in surrender to the Father. Amen.

== Nov 08
Q: How could you take time to listen to God before you speak to Him today?
P: Lord our God, You are one Lord. Quiet my heart so I can hear You, and help me love You with all my heart, soul, mind, and strength. Amen.

== Nov 09
Q: Have you put off seeking God, and what would it look like to call on Him today?
P: Lord, You are near, and You can be found. I will not wait for a better time. I call on You today, and I thank You that You abundantly pardon. Amen.

== Nov 10
Q: How has God given you room in past distress, and who needs that same mercy from you?
P: God of my righteousness, You have enlarged me when I was in distress. Have mercy on me again, and help me give room and grace to someone else today. Amen.

== Nov 11
Q: How would you put your faith in Jesus into simple words today?
P: Lord Jesus, I believe with all my heart that You are the Christ, the Son of God. Thank You that the door to You is open to everyone who believes. Amen.

== Nov 12
Q: How does it change your praying to know your prayers rise before God like incense?
P: Lord, thank You that every prayer I have prayed has risen before You and none is forgotten. Help me pray boldly and faithfully, for myself and for others. Amen.

== Nov 13
Q: What could you speak honestly to God today, knowing He already sees and knows you?
P: Lord Jesus, You saw Nathanael under the fig tree, and You see me. Since You already know me and love me, help me speak to You honestly and without pretending. Amen.

== Nov 14
Q: What unspoken doubts, fears, or hopes could you lay before God openly today?
P: Lord, You hear even my unspoken thoughts. I lay my doubts, fears, frustrations, and hopes before You openly. Meet me there. Amen.

== Nov 15
Q: How could you pause today to remember that God dwells in you and you in Him?
P: Lord, I confess that Jesus is the Son of God. Thank You for making Your home in me. Help me turn my attention to You throughout this day. Amen.

== Nov 16
Q: When has God answered a need before you even asked, and how can you thank Him?
P: Lord, You are eager to answer Your children. Thank You for the times You answered before I even called. Help me pray today with confidence in Your attentive love. Amen.

== Nov 17
Q: What honest question can you bring to God while you keep walking in the light you have?
P: Lord Jesus, You were lifted up to draw all people to Yourself. I bring You my honest questions, and while I wait to understand, help me keep walking in Your light. Amen.

== Nov 18
Q: What doubt can you bring honestly to Jesus, and what evidence of His work can strengthen you?
P: Lord Jesus, You answered John's doubt with patience. I bring You my doubts honestly. Open my eyes to all You have done, and let it strengthen my faith. Amen.

== Nov 19
Q: How could you give generously to those closest to you and seek God together with them?
P: Lord, teach me a love that does not withhold what it owes. Help me give generously in my closest relationships, and draw us together in prayer. Amen.

== Nov 20
Q: Whose approval are you seeking, and what would it look like to live for God's approval alone?
P: Lord, free me from the need to please everyone. Let me live for You, whose love and approval I already have by grace. Amen.

== Nov 21
Q: What important conversation is ahead of you that you could pray about before you speak?
P: Lord, the answer of the tongue comes from You. Before I speak in hard conversations, prepare my heart and guide my words. Amen.

== Nov 22
Q: Who prays faithfully for you, and how could you serve God through persistent prayer?
P: Lord, thank You for the faithful people who pray for me. Make me like Anna, serving You with willing, persistent prayer, day and night. Amen.

== Nov 23
Q: How does it fill you with hope that God wants to dwell with you forever?
P: Lord, You have always wanted to dwell with Your people. Thank You for being with me now by Your Spirit. Fill me with hope for the day I will see You face to face. Amen.

== Nov 24
Q: Whom do you love who is facing a hard path, and can you entrust them into God's hands?
P: Lord, I pray for the one I love who is facing a hard path. I pour out my heart to You, and I trust them to Your care. Your will be done. Amen.

== Nov 25
Q: Whom can you thank God for today among the people who share your life?
P: Father, thank You for the people who share my table and my life. Most of all, thank You for fellowship with You and with Your Son Jesus Christ. Amen.

== Nov 26
Q: Whom could you make mention of in your prayers today, and how might you serve them?
P: Lord, You are my witness that I pray for others. Help me pray for someone specifically today, and show me how my prayers can lead me to serve them in love. Amen.

== Nov 27
Q: Do you give and serve to be seen, or out of love, and what could you give in secret today?
P: Lord, You look at the heart, not at outward show. Make my heart sincere, and help me give and serve out of love, even when no one else sees. Amen.

== Nov 28
Q: Before you pray today, is there anything you need to turn from so your heart is clean?
P: Lord, search my heart and show me anything that stands between us. Help me turn from it, wash me, and make me clean, so my prayers rise to You from an honest heart. Amen.

== Nov 29
Q: What cry of your heart can you bring to God, trusting that He understands even when others do not?
P: Lord Jesus, You cried out to the Father in deeper darkness than I will ever know. Hear the cry of my heart, and understand every word, even when others do not. Amen.

== Nov 30
Q: Where is the noise of the crowd louder than God's voice in your decisions?
P: Lord Jesus, You trusted the Father while the world sat in judgment. Help me listen to You above the noise of the crowd, and let Your voice guide my decisions. Amen.
