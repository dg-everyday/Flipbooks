# December 2027: Christ & God's Promises (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Dec 01
Q: What failure in your life could you see in the light of God's first promise of a Savior?
P: Lord, You answered our very first failure with a promise. Thank You that You did not abandon us. Help me rest today in the victory of the Savior You sent. Amen.

== Dec 02
Q: How could you share the blessing promised to Abraham, which reaches you through Christ, with someone today?
P: Lord, thank You that in Abraham's seed all the nations of the earth are blessed, and that the blessing reaches me through Christ. Help me share it with someone today. Amen.

== Dec 03
Q: Which of God's promises do you see only from far off, and how can you keep trusting Him?
P: Lord, Your promises often unfold slowly, but they always come to pass. Help me keep my eyes on You while I wait, trusting that what You have spoken You will do. Amen.

== Dec 04
Q: Among all the voices competing for your attention, how can you make time to listen to Jesus today?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the Prophet Moses promised. Quiet the many voices around me, and help me listen to Your words today and do what You say. Amen.

== Dec 05
Q: Is there a promise of God that seems slow in coming, and how can His faithfulness to David give you hope?
P: Lord, You kept Your promise to David, even when it took centuries. When Your promises seem slow, help me trust Your timing, for You always do what You say. Amen.

== Dec 06
Q: When the world seems to rage, how can you rest today in the unshakable kingdom of God's Son?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the Son of God, appointed by the Father to reign. When the nations rage, I bow before You and rest in a kingdom that cannot be shaken. Amen.

== Dec 07
Q: What part of your life could you place more fully under the righteous rule of the eternal King?
P: O God, Your throne is for ever and ever, and the sceptre of Your kingdom is righteous. I give You my loyalty and my trust today, my eternal King. Amen.

== Dec 08
Q: How could the King's compassion for the needy shape the way you treat someone today?
P: Lord Jesus, King of kings, all nations will bow before You. I bow before You too, and I ask You to make me compassionate toward the needy as You are. Amen.

== Dec 09
Q: What part of your life looks cut down and lifeless, and can you trust God to bring new growth there?
P: Lord, You brought a Branch out of the stump of Jesse. I bring You the places in my life that look lifeless. Bring forth new growth, for nothing is too hard for You. Amen.

== Dec 10
Q: What are you waiting on God for, and how can your waiting become hopeful today?
P: Lord, You are our God, and we have waited for You. Make my waiting hopeful rather than anxious, and let me rejoice even now in Your salvation. Amen.

== Dec 11
Q: Where do you feel like a bruised reed or a flickering flame that needs Jesus' gentleness?
P: Lord Jesus, You will not break a bruised reed or quench a smoking flax. Uphold me with Your gentleness today, and restore what is weak in me. Amen.

== Dec 12
Q: Who in your life may feel far from God, and how could you carry Christ's light to them?
P: Lord Jesus, You are a light to the nations and salvation to the end of the earth. Let Your light shine on me, and help me carry it to someone who feels far from You. Amen.

== Dec 13
Q: How could you bring a word of good news to someone today?
P: Lord, how beautiful are the feet of those who bring good tidings of peace. Give me an opportunity today to share a word of hope and to tell someone that You reign. Amen.

== Dec 14
Q: What wound or guilt could you bring to the One whose suffering purchased your peace?
P: Lord Jesus, You were wounded for my transgressions and bruised for my iniquities. Thank You for the peace You purchased with Your suffering. By Your stripes I am healed. Amen.

== Dec 15
Q: Where have you grown weary, and how could you rise today and reflect Christ's light to others?
P: Lord, Your light has come, and Your glory has risen upon me. Lift me out of weariness, and let me shine today with Your kindness, joy, and hope. Amen.

== Dec 16
Q: What injustice troubles you, and how can you place your hope in the righteous King?
P: Lord Jesus, righteous Branch of David, You will execute judgment and justice in the earth. Whatever injustice troubles me today, I place my hope in You. Amen.

== Dec 17
Q: How does it help you to know that your King came lowly so that anyone could come near?
P: Lord Jesus, You are just and bring salvation, yet You came lowly. I rejoice in my humble King, and I come near to You today without fear. Amen.

== Dec 18
Q: Where does God seem silent, and how can you keep seeking Him while He prepares the way?
P: Lord, silence is not absence. When You seem quiet, help me keep seeking You, trusting that You are preparing the way and will come in Your time. Amen.

== Dec 19
Q: What small beginning in your life could God use for something great?
P: Lord, You chose little Bethlehem to bring forth the Ruler of Your people. Thank You that You do great things through small beginnings, and use my small offerings too. Amen.

== Dec 20
Q: Where are you still trying to earn approval, and how can you rest in being beloved through Jesus?
P: Father, Jesus is Your beloved Son, in whom You are well pleased. Thank You that through Him Your love reaches me. Help me rest in Your love instead of striving. Amen.

== Dec 21
Q: What place in your heart most needs to hear that God's love reaches you through His beloved Son?
P: Father, the love You have for Your Son reaches me through Christ. Let Your words of love fill the dark places in my heart, and help me live as Your beloved child. Amen.

== Dec 22
Q: Who around you is brokenhearted, and how could you share the good news with them?
P: Lord Jesus, You came to heal the brokenhearted and set the captives free. Meet me in my pain, and help me bring Your good news to those who are hurting. Amen.

== Dec 23
Q: What guilt could you let the Lamb of God lift from your heart today?
P: Lord Jesus, Lamb of God, You take away the sin of the world. I look to You today, and I ask You to lift the weight of guilt from my heart. Amen.

== Dec 24
Q: What hunger in your heart can only Jesus satisfy?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the bread of life. I come to You with a hungry heart. Fill me with Yourself, satisfy my soul, and help me stop looking for bread that cannot feed me. Amen.

== Dec 25
Q: Where do you need Christ's light today, and who could you share it with?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the light of the world. Shine into the dark places of my heart, help me walk in the light of life, and let me share Your light with someone who needs it. Amen.

== Dec 26
Q: How could you listen for the voice of the Good Shepherd and follow Him today?
P: Lord Jesus, my Good Shepherd, You gave Your life for the sheep. Know me, guide me, and protect me today, and help me hear Your voice and follow You. Amen.

== Dec 27
Q: What grief could you bring to Jesus, who is the resurrection and the life?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the resurrection and the life. You wept with Your grieving friends. I bring You my sorrow, and I trust that death is not the end of the story. Amen.

== Dec 28
Q: As you look back on this year and ahead to the next, how can you make Jesus your way, truth, and life?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the way, the truth, and the life. As this year ends and a new one begins, help me follow You, trust You, and abide in You. Amen.

== Dec 29
Q: Which verse from this year has shaped you most, and how can you give thanks for it?
P: Lord, thank You for Your Word, written so that I might believe that Jesus is the Christ and have life in His name. Let what I have read this year keep bearing fruit. Amen.

== Dec 30
Q: How could you share the name of Jesus with love and humility with someone who is still searching?
P: Lord Jesus, there is no other name under heaven by which we must be saved. I call on Your name with gratitude, and I ask You to help me share it with love. Amen.

== Dec 31
Q: What would it mean to pray 'Come, Lord Jesus' over your heart, your home, and your new year?
P: Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus. Come into my heart, my home, and my new year, and come again to make all things right. Amen.
