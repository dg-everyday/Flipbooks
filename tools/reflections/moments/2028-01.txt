# January 2028: Seeking God First (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Jan 01
Q: What are you carrying into this new year that needs God's fresh mercy today?
P: Lord, Your mercies are new every morning, and great is Your faithfulness. I bring You what I carry into this year. Meet me with fresh mercy for today. Amen.

== Jan 02
Q: What would change in your plans this year if God's kingdom truly came first?
P: Father, I want to seek Your kingdom and Your righteousness first. Take the top place in my plans this year, and help me trust You with everything that follows. Amen.

== Jan 03
Q: What is one way you could seek God with your whole heart today?
P: Lord, You promised to be found by those who search for You with all their heart. I do not want to seek You halfway. Draw my whole heart to You today. Amen.

== Jan 04
Q: If you could ask God for only one thing, what would it be, and why?
P: Lord, let my one desire be to dwell near You and behold Your beauty. Gather my scattered wants into that one longing, and keep me close to You all my days. Amen.

== Jan 05
Q: What dry place in your soul have you been trying to satisfy with something other than God?
P: O God, You are my God, and my soul thirsts for You. Forgive me for chasing what cannot satisfy. Refresh my dry places this morning with Yourself. Amen.

== Jan 06
Q: What would it look like to seek God from right where you are today, without waiting to fix everything first?
P: Lord, I do not have to be in a better place to seek You. I seek You from right here, just as I am. Thank You that You are found by those who look for You. Amen.

== Jan 07
Q: What longing in your heart could you bring honestly to God today?
P: Lord, as the hart pants after the water brooks, so my soul pants after You. I bring You my thirst without pretending. Refresh me with Your living presence. Amen.

== Jan 08
Q: When your thoughts start to spin today, what truth about God could you lean your mind on?
P: Lord, keep me in perfect peace as my mind stays on You. When my thoughts begin to race, turn them back to You, and steady my heart with trust. Amen.

== Jan 09
Q: Is there an old guilt or an old success that is still steering you, and can you leave it with God?
P: Lord, I give You my past, both the failures that shame me and the victories I lean on. Help me forget what is behind and reach forward to what You have prepared. Amen.

== Jan 10
Q: What is one step you could take today toward what God is calling you to?
P: Father, I press toward the mark for the prize of Your high calling in Christ Jesus. Give me purpose and endurance today, and keep my eyes on the finish line. Amen.

== Jan 11
Q: Where might God already be doing a new thing in your life that you have not noticed?
P: Lord, You make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the desert. Open my eyes to the new thing You are doing, and help me stop looking only behind me. Amen.

== Jan 12
Q: If you numbered your days honestly, what would you spend more of today on?
P: Lord, teach me to number my days, that I may apply my heart to wisdom. Help me spend today on what truly lasts and let go of what does not matter. Amen.

== Jan 13
Q: What piece of today's work could you consciously place in God's hands before you begin?
P: Lord, I commit my work to You before it begins. Settle my thoughts, steady my hands, and let what I do today be established by You. Amen.

== Jan 14
Q: What part of your life have you committed to God but quietly taken back?
P: Lord, I commit my way to You and choose to trust You with it. When I am tempted to take the load back, remind me that You will bring it to pass. Amen.

== Jan 15
Q: What is one reason you could enjoy God today, not just obey Him?
P: Lord, I want to delight in You, not only serve You. Shape the desires of my heart as I enjoy Your goodness, until what I want is what You want. Amen.

== Jan 16
Q: Where could you find a quiet place to meet with God before today's demands begin?
P: Lord Jesus, even You rose early to pray alone. Teach me to seek the Father before the noise of the day, and let my strength come from time with Him. Amen.

== Jan 17
Q: What prayer could you bring to God this morning and then watch expectantly for His answer?
P: Lord, in the morning I direct my prayer to You and look up. Give me eyes to see Your hand at work today, and a heart that expects You to answer. Amen.

== Jan 18
Q: What could you do today to press on in knowing God, not just knowing about Him?
P: Lord, I want to follow on to know You. Your coming is as sure as the morning and as refreshing as the rain. Draw me deeper into knowing You today. Amen.

== Jan 19
Q: Where has your heart grown hard from neglect, and how could you break up that ground today?
P: Lord, break up the fallow ground of my heart. Soften what neglect has hardened, and prepare me to receive Your rain of righteousness. It is time to seek You. Amen.

== Jan 20
Q: Is your walk with God more activity than relationship right now, and how could you seek Him personally today?
P: Lord, I do not want only to be busy for You. I want to know You. Help me seek You Yourself today, and find the life You promise. Amen.

== Jan 21
Q: Where do you most need God to make you more gentle and humble today?
P: Lord, I seek You, Your righteousness, and meekness. Humility does not come once for all, so keep working it into me. Make me gentle, upright, and close to You. Amen.

== Jan 22
Q: What moments in your ordinary day could become reminders to turn your heart toward God?
P: Lord, I want to seek Your face continually, not only now and then. Turn my heart toward You again and again through this day, in the small moments as well as the large. Amen.

== Jan 23
Q: What step of humility could you take before God today?
P: Lord, I humble myself before You. Show me where I need to turn, and hear me as I pray and seek Your face. Forgive, and bring Your healing to my life. Amen.

== Jan 24
Q: What do you know about God's character that helps you trust Him today?
P: Lord, those who know Your name will put their trust in You, for You have not forsaken those who seek You. Help me know You better, so my trust in You grows. Amen.

== Jan 25
Q: What need are you trying to meet in your own strength that you could trust God to supply?
P: Father, even strong lions go hungry, but those who seek You lack no good thing. I stop relying on my own strength and trust You to supply what is truly good. Amen.

== Jan 26
Q: What good activity has been crowding out time at Jesus' feet?
P: Lord Jesus, like Mary, I want to choose the good part. Quiet my busy heart, help me sit at Your feet today, and let me listen to Your word. Amen.

== Jan 27
Q: What prayer have you set down that you could pick up and keep knocking on today?
P: Father, You told us to ask, seek, and knock. I bring back the prayer I had set aside, trusting that You are a loving Father who hears. Amen.

== Jan 28
Q: Which choice today could be shaped by heaven's priorities instead of earth's?
P: Lord, I have been raised with Christ, so help me seek those things which are above. Let heaven's priorities shape my choices, my time, and my words today. Amen.

== Jan 29
Q: What has been filling your mind lately, and how could you set it on things above today?
P: Lord, I set my affection on things above, not on things on the earth. Where my thoughts have drifted, draw them back to You and to Your goodness. Amen.

== Jan 30
Q: Where are you most likely to wander from God's ways, and how could you ask Him to keep you?
P: Lord, with my whole heart I have sought You. O let me not wander from Your commandments. Keep me close to Your Word and on Your path today. Amen.

== Jan 31
Q: How could you keep the Lord before you through every day of this year?
P: Lord, I set You always before me. Because You are at my right hand, I shall not be moved. Help me keep You in view through every day of this year. Amen.
