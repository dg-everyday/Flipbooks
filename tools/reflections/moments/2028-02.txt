# February 2028: Love, Friendship & Family (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Feb 01
Q: Who stood by you in a hard season, and who might need you to stand by them now?
P: Lord, thank You for the friends who loved me at all times, even in adversity. Make me that kind of friend to someone who is struggling today. Amen.

== Feb 02
Q: What step of friendliness could you take toward someone today?
P: Lord, help me show myself friendly, and give me courage to reach out first. Thank You that You are the Friend who sticks closer than a brother. Amen.

== Feb 03
Q: What task could you share today, or whom could you come alongside?
P: Lord, You made us for partnership, not isolation. Show me someone I can work with and walk beside today, and let us both be stronger for it. Amen.

== Feb 04
Q: Who around you has fallen or is struggling, and how could you help lift them up?
P: Lord, open my eyes to anyone who has fallen and has no one to help them rise. Make me the friend who reaches out a hand today. Amen.

== Feb 05
Q: Who needs your loyal love right now, even when leaving would be easier?
P: Lord, give me the loyal love of Ruth, love that stays when it would be easier to go. Help me be faithful to the people You have placed in my life. Amen.

== Feb 06
Q: Is there someone you could choose to support today instead of seeing them as a rival?
P: Lord, thank You for friends whose hearts are knit with mine. Free me from rivalry and envy, and teach me to love others as Jonathan loved David. Amen.

== Feb 07
Q: Who helps sharpen you, and how could you be a sharpening friend to someone else?
P: Lord, as iron sharpens iron, let my friendships make us both better. Give me friends who help me grow, and make me that kind of friend in return. Amen.

== Feb 08
Q: Is there honest counsel you need to receive with grace, or a truth you need to speak in love?
P: Lord, faithful are the wounds of a friend. Give me humility to receive honest words, and wisdom to speak hard truths with love and care. Amen.

== Feb 09
Q: What would you say to Jesus today if you spoke to Him as a trusted friend?
P: Lord Jesus, You call me Your friend and share the Father's words with me. Thank You for that friendship. Let me talk with You today with an open heart. Amen.

== Feb 10
Q: Whom could you honour today ahead of yourself?
P: Lord, give me kindly affection for the people around me. Free me from seeking recognition, and help me find joy in honouring others today. Amen.

== Feb 11
Q: Whose joy or sorrow could you share with your whole heart today?
P: Lord, teach me to rejoice with those who rejoice and weep with those who weep. Give me a humble heart that enters fully into the lives of others. Amen.

== Feb 12
Q: Whose burden could you help carry today, even in a small way?
P: Lord Jesus, You carried my load when I could not. Show me someone whose burden I can help bear today, and let my help be love in action. Amen.

== Feb 13
Q: Who needs your patience and active kindness today?
P: Lord, Your love is patient and kind. Where I am quick to envy, boast, or grow impatient, change my heart, and let me show kindness to someone today. Amen.

== Feb 14
Q: Where is tension likely to rise today, and how could you prepare a soft answer?
P: Lord, a soft answer turns away wrath. When tension rises today, give me the strength to answer gently, and guard my tongue from harsh words. Amen.

== Feb 15
Q: Is there a grudge you are keeping score on that you could let go of today?
P: Lord, love keeps no record of wrongs. Help me let go of the grudges I have been holding, and teach me to think the best of others. Amen.

== Feb 16
Q: Who tests your patience, and how could you bear with them in love today?
P: Lord, clothe me with humility, gentleness, and patience. Help me bear with others in love, remembering how patiently You bear with me. Amen.

== Feb 17
Q: Where has your kindness or patience grown cold, and how could you put on love today?
P: Lord, above all things I put on love, the bond that holds everything together. Warm my heart, and let love shape every kindness I show today. Amen.

== Feb 18
Q: Which young person in your life could you pray for and set an example for today?
P: Lord, I lift up the young people in my life. Help me show them Your way more by my example than by my words, and keep them on Your path. Amen.

== Feb 19
Q: How could you honour your parents today, or give thanks to God for them?
P: Lord, thank You for my father and mother. Help me honour them with respect and gratitude, even where they were imperfect, and bless them by Your grace. Amen.

== Feb 20
Q: Does your leadership build up those in your care, or wear them down?
P: Lord, whatever authority You have given me, help me use it to build others up and not crush them. Teach me to nurture and instruct with Your patience. Amen.

== Feb 21
Q: How could you treat the children in your life as a treasure from God today?
P: Lord, children are a heritage from You. Thank You for the children in my life. Help me treat them as treasures entrusted to me, with patience and love. Amen.

== Feb 22
Q: Where could you help build unity today instead of division?
P: Lord, how good and how pleasant it is when Your people dwell together in unity. Make me a peacemaker today, and help me join others rather than divide them. Amen.

== Feb 23
Q: What do you want to be at the center of your home, and what choice today would show it?
P: Lord, as for me and my house, we will serve You. Help me choose You again today, and let my home be a place where You are honoured. Amen.

== Feb 24
Q: How could kindness govern the way you speak at home today?
P: Lord, let the law of kindness be on my tongue. Fill my words with wisdom and gentleness, and let them make my home a warmer place today. Amen.

== Feb 25
Q: Who could you show undeserved kindness to today, as God has shown it to you?
P: Lord, You sought me out and gave me a place at Your table when I deserved nothing. Help me show that same kindness to someone else today. Amen.

== Feb 26
Q: What encouraging words have you been thinking about someone that you could say out loud today?
P: Lord, help me comfort and build up the people around me. Let me speak the encouraging words I have been holding back, and strengthen someone through them. Amen.

== Feb 27
Q: Who is one person you could stir up toward love and good works today?
P: Lord, help me consider the people around me with care. Show me how to stir someone toward love and good works, and keep us close to one another. Amen.

== Feb 28
Q: Who is new or unfamiliar around you that you could welcome today?
P: Lord, help me not to forget kindness to strangers. Open my eyes to someone new around me, and let me welcome them as You have welcomed me. Amen.

== Feb 29
Q: What loving thing could you do with this extra day that you would not otherwise have done?
P: Lord, love is of You, and everyone who loves is born of You and knows You. Thank You for this extra day. Let me spend some of it loving someone well. Amen.
