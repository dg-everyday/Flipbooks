# March 2028: Faith & Trust in Storms (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Mar 01
Q: What question you cannot answer could you hand to God today instead of leaning on your own understanding?
P: Lord, I trust You with all my heart and lean not on my own understanding. You see the whole picture when I see only a part. I lean on You today. Amen.

== Mar 02
Q: What trouble are you facing today that you could run to God with first?
P: O God, You are my refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. When everything around me shakes, I run to You. Be near me today. Amen.

== Mar 03
Q: Where could you stop striving today and spend a few quiet minutes remembering that God is God?
P: Lord, I choose to be still and know that You are God. Quiet my frantic striving, and let me rest in the truth that You are in control. Amen.

== Mar 04
Q: What waters or fires are you passing through, and how does God's presence change how you face them?
P: Lord, You promised to be with me when I pass through the waters and the fire. I do not ask for an easy road, but I trust You to carry me through. Amen.

== Mar 05
Q: In the storm you are facing, what helps you remember that Jesus is in the boat with you?
P: Lord Jesus, You are in the boat with me, and the wind and the sea obey You. When fear rises in my storm, remind me that You are near. Amen.

== Mar 06
Q: Could Jesus be coming to you in your storm in a way you have not yet recognized?
P: Lord Jesus, You say, be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid. Open my eyes to recognize You in my storm today, and let Your voice calm my fear. Amen.

== Mar 07
Q: Where do you feel yourself sinking, and could you call out to Jesus right now?
P: Lord Jesus, when I begin to sink, You reach out and catch me. Even when my faith falters, Your hand holds me. Save me, Lord, and hold me fast today. Amen.

== Mar 08
Q: What fear is present in you today, and how could you choose to trust God in the middle of it?
P: Lord, what time I am afraid, I will trust in You. I do not wait for fear to leave before I trust. I choose to trust You now, even while I am afraid. Amen.

== Mar 09
Q: What dark valley are you walking through, and how does the Shepherd's presence comfort you?
P: Lord, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for You are with me. Walk with me through this dark place, my Shepherd. Amen.

== Mar 10
Q: How could you stay close enough to God today to rest in His shadow?
P: Lord, I want to dwell in Your secret place, not just visit it. Keep me close enough to rest under Your shadow today, safe in Your presence. Amen.

== Mar 11
Q: What fear could you answer today by saying aloud that the Lord is your refuge?
P: Lord, You are my refuge and my fortress, my God, in You will I trust. When fear is loud, let me speak this truth and stand on it today. Amen.

== Mar 12
Q: What trouble could you bring to God today, knowing that He is good and knows you by name?
P: Lord, You are good, a stronghold in the day of trouble, and You know those who trust in You. Thank You that I am known by You. I trust You today. Amen.

== Mar 13
Q: Where are you at your wits' end, and could you cry out to God there?
P: Lord, when my strength runs out, You are still able. I cry out to You in my storm. Make the storm a calm, and bring me to the haven I need. Amen.

== Mar 14
Q: Which phrase of Isaiah 41:10 does your heart most need to hear today?
P: Lord, I will not fear, for You are with me. I will not be dismayed, for You are my God. Strengthen me, help me, and uphold me with Your right hand. Amen.

== Mar 15
Q: What are you facing today that you need to remember God is able to handle?
P: Lord, You are able to deliver. Whatever furnace I face today, help me trust Your power more than any threat around me. Amen.

== Mar 16
Q: Is your trust in God tied to a particular outcome, and could you settle in your heart to trust Him even if not?
P: Lord, I believe You are able, and I ask You boldly. But even if You do not answer as I hope, I will still trust You and refuse to bow to anything else. Amen.

== Mar 17
Q: What is failing in your life right now, and how could you still choose to rejoice in God today?
P: Lord, even if the fields fail and the stalls are empty, I will rejoice in You. Let my joy rest on who You are, not on what I have. Amen.

== Mar 18
Q: What honest question could you bring to God today while still holding on to Him?
P: Lord, though I do not understand everything, I will trust in You. I bring You my honest questions, and I choose to hold on to You. Amen.

== Mar 19
Q: Where do you feel you have no power and no plan, and how could you fix your eyes on God there?
P: Lord, I do not know what to do, but my eyes are upon You. I admit my limits, and I look to You for the help I cannot give myself. Amen.

== Mar 20
Q: What battle have you been carrying alone that you could hand over to God today?
P: Lord, the battle is not mine but Yours. I hand You the struggle I have been carrying, and I choose to praise You while You fight for me. Amen.

== Mar 21
Q: Where do you feel trapped today, and how could you stand still and watch for God to make a way?
P: Lord, when I feel trapped with nowhere to turn, help me stand still and see Your salvation. You made a way through the sea, and You can make a way for me. Amen.

== Mar 22
Q: What fight could you stop striving in today and trust the Lord to fight for you?
P: Lord, You shall fight for me, and I shall hold my peace. Calm my anxious striving, and let me rest in Your promise to step in for me today. Amen.

== Mar 23
Q: Whose opinion or opposition have you been fearing, and how does knowing God is on your side change that?
P: Lord, You are on my side, and I will not fear what people can do to me. Free me from the fear of others, and let me rest secure in Your love. Amen.

== Mar 24
Q: What hard thing could you trust God to weave into His good purpose?
P: Lord, I know that all things work together for good to them that love You. I do not see how yet, but I trust that nothing is wasted in Your hands. Amen.

== Mar 25
Q: Where do you feel outnumbered, and how does God's gift of His Son prove He is for you?
P: Father, if You are for me, who can be against me? You did not spare Your own Son. When I feel outnumbered, remind me that You are on my side. Amen.

== Mar 26
Q: Where do you need God to open your eyes to His presence around you today?
P: Lord, open my eyes as You opened the eyes of Elisha's servant. Let me see that those who are with me are more than those who are against me. Amen.

== Mar 27
Q: What lesser source of help have you been leaning on, and how could you look to the Lord instead?
P: Lord, my help comes from You, the Maker of heaven and earth. Nothing is too hard for You. Turn my eyes from lesser helps to You today. Amen.

== Mar 28
Q: Where does your confidence really rest right now, in people or in the Lord?
P: Lord, blessed is the one who trusts in You and whose hope You are. Where I have relied on anything else, turn my trust back to You today. Amen.

== Mar 29
Q: How could you push your roots deeper into God during this season?
P: Lord, make me like a tree planted by the waters, green and fruitful even in drought. Draw my roots deeper into You, so I do not wither when the heat comes. Amen.

== Mar 30
Q: What is troubling your heart today, and could you name it and bring it to Jesus?
P: Lord Jesus, You tell me not to let my heart be troubled. I believe in God, and I believe in You. I bring You what troubles me and rest my heart in You. Amen.

== Mar 31
Q: What chariots and horses are you tempted to trust in, and how could you place your confidence in God?
P: Lord, some trust in chariots and some in horses, but I will remember Your name. Only You never fail. I place my confidence in You today. Amen.
