# April 2028: Forgiveness & Reconciliation (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Apr 01
Q: What forgiven sin have you been carrying again that God has already removed?
P: Lord, You have removed my transgressions as far as the east is from the west. Help me receive Your forgiveness fully, and let what You have removed stay removed. Amen.

== Apr 02
Q: Is there anything hidden you could bring to God today so you can know the joy of forgiveness?
P: Lord, blessed is the one whose sin is forgiven and covered. I bring You what I have kept hidden, and I ask You to give me the joy of being forgiven. Amen.

== Apr 03
Q: What have you been hiding from God that you could honestly confess to Him today?
P: Lord, I stop hiding and acknowledge my sin to You. Thank You that You forgive the iniquity of my sin. Free me from the weight of keeping secrets from You. Amen.

== Apr 04
Q: What stain do you feel you cannot remove, and could you bring it to God as you are today?
P: Lord, You invite me to come and reason with You. Though my sins be as scarlet, You can make them white as snow. I come to You just as I am. Amen.

== Apr 05
Q: Do you approach God as if He forgives reluctantly, and how would it change things to know He delights in mercy?
P: Lord, who is a God like You, who pardons iniquity and delights in mercy? Thank You that You are glad to forgive. I come to You today with confidence. Amen.

== Apr 06
Q: Is there a sin God has buried that you keep going back to dig up?
P: Lord, You have cast my sins into the depths of the sea. Help me stop searching for what You have buried, and let me walk away from it in freedom. Amen.

== Apr 07
Q: Whose debt against you could you ask God for grace to release today?
P: Father, forgive me my debts, as I forgive my debtors. You have shown me such mercy; make my heart merciful too, and help me release what I am owed. Amen.

== Apr 08
Q: Who is one person you could take a step toward forgiving today?
P: Father, You have forgiven me so much. Where unforgiveness has taken root in me, soften my heart, and help me take one step toward forgiving today. Amen.

== Apr 09
Q: Is there someone you have been keeping count with, waiting to stop forgiving?
P: Lord Jesus, like Peter, I want to know where forgiveness can stop. I bring You my count of offences. Teach me the forgiveness that does not keep a tally. Amen.

== Apr 10
Q: What scorecard against someone could you put away today?
P: Lord Jesus, You told me to forgive until seventy times seven. When I remember how much You have forgiven me, help me put away my scorecard for good. Amen.

== Apr 11
Q: Where could you choose mercy over judgment today?
P: Lord Jesus, keep me from a critical and condemning spirit. Teach me to forgive freely and to measure others with the same mercy You have shown me. Amen.

== Apr 12
Q: If you have wandered from God, what step toward home could you take today?
P: Father, You saw the prodigal from far off and ran to meet him. If I have wandered, help me turn toward home, knowing You are eager to welcome me. Amen.

== Apr 13
Q: Is there grace shown to someone else that you have resented, and could you rejoice in it instead?
P: Father, You rejoice over every one who comes home. Free me from the older brother's resentment, and help me rejoice in grace shown to others, as heaven rejoices over me. Amen.

== Apr 14
Q: What wrong done to you could you trust God to bring good out of?
P: Lord, what others meant for evil, You can mean for good. Help me trust Your purpose in the wrongs done to me, and give me a heart that forgives instead of seeking revenge. Amen.

== Apr 15
Q: Is there someone whose healing you could seek as you forgive them, as Joseph did?
P: Lord, give me a heart that forgives like Joseph's, one that seeks the healing of those who hurt me, not just their release. Show me Your purpose in my story. Amen.

== Apr 16
Q: Which broken relationship in your life could you pray for today?
P: Lord, You softened the hearts of Jacob and Esau after many years. I bring You a broken relationship in my life. Soften our hearts, and bring reconciliation in Your time. Amen.

== Apr 17
Q: Who might have something against you, and what first step toward peace could you take?
P: Lord, my relationship with You is bound up with my relationships with others. Show me where I need to make peace, and give me courage to take the first step. Amen.

== Apr 18
Q: Is there a root of bitterness growing from an old hurt that God could help you put away?
P: Lord, search my heart for any bitterness, anger, or malice. Heal the hurts where it has taken root, and help me put it away, with Your grace. Amen.

== Apr 19
Q: When forgiving feels impossible, how could remembering Christ's forgiveness of you help?
P: Lord Jesus, You forgave me completely, freely, and at great cost. When forgiving others feels impossible, let me start by remembering how You forgave me. Amen.

== Apr 20
Q: Where are you tempted to repay evil for evil, and what good could you choose instead?
P: Lord, I leave it to You to make things right. Keep me from repaying evil for evil, and help me do what is honest and good in the sight of all. Amen.

== Apr 21
Q: What part of making peace lies with you today, and what could you leave with God?
P: Lord, as much as lies in me, help me live peaceably with all. Show me my part, give me grace to do it, and help me leave the rest with You. Amen.

== Apr 22
Q: What good could you do today for someone who has wronged you?
P: Lord, do not let me be overcome of evil. Help me overcome evil with good, and show me one kind thing I can do today for someone who has hurt me. Amen.

== Apr 23
Q: What minor offence could you choose to let pass today?
P: Lord, give me discretion that is slow to anger. Help me see that not every small wrong needs to be answered, and give me grace to let one pass today. Amen.

== Apr 24
Q: How could your words today cover someone with grace instead of stirring up strife?
P: Lord, hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all sins. Guard my tongue from exposing others, and let my words cover with grace today. Amen.

== Apr 25
Q: Where could you help bring peace between people today?
P: Lord, You reconciled me to Yourself through Christ and gave me the ministry of reconciliation. Make me a messenger of peace, and show me where peace is needed today. Amen.

== Apr 26
Q: Who in your life needs to hear that God is not counting their trespasses against them?
P: Lord, You were in Christ, reconciling the world to Yourself and not counting our trespasses. Thank You for this good news. Help me share it with someone today. Amen.

== Apr 27
Q: Where are you still trying to earn God's love by your performance?
P: Father, You showed Your love while I was still a sinner. Your love was never a reward for my goodness. Help me rest today in a love I do not have to earn. Amen.

== Apr 28
Q: What fear could be calmed by knowing your security rests on God's commitment, not your ability?
P: Lord, You reconciled me when I was Your enemy; how much more will You keep me now. Let my security rest on Your faithfulness, and calm my fears today. Amen.

== Apr 29
Q: Is there someone you could welcome back or speak up for, as Paul did for Onesimus?
P: Lord Jesus, You stand in my place and pay my debt before the Father. Make me a peacemaker, and give me a welcoming heart toward someone who needs to be received. Amen.

== Apr 30
Q: How would you walk differently today if you truly believed God remembers your forgiven sins no more?
P: Lord, You are merciful to my unrighteousness and remember my sins no more. Thank You for that promise. Help me walk in the freedom You have given me. Amen.
