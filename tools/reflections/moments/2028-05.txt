# May 2028: God's Word & Obedience (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== May 01
Q: What next step do you need God's Word to light for you today?
P: Lord, Your word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path. I do not need to see the whole road. Give me light for the next faithful step today. Amen.

== May 02
Q: Which verse could you begin hiding in your heart today?
P: Lord, I want to hide Your word in my heart, that I might not sin against You. Help me store it deep, so it rises up to guard me when temptation comes. Amen.

== May 03
Q: What challenge today could you begin by letting God's Word fill your mind?
P: Lord, let Your word not depart out of my mouth, and help me meditate on it and do it. Shape my life by Scripture, and lead me in Your way today. Amen.

== May 04
Q: What would help your mind return to God's Word more often through the day?
P: Lord, I want to delight in Your law, not just read it. Grow my love for Your Word, until my thoughts return to it day and night. Amen.

== May 05
Q: Where are you impatient for fruit, and how could you stay rooted in God's Word while you wait?
P: Lord, make me like a tree planted by the rivers of water, bringing forth fruit in its season. Keep my roots deep in Your Word, and let my growth come in Your time. Amen.

== May 06
Q: How might you read your Bible differently today, knowing God breathed it?
P: Lord, all Scripture is given by Your inspiration. Use it to teach, correct, and train me in righteousness. Do not only inform me through Your Word; transform me. Amen.

== May 07
Q: What might God's Word be searching in your heart today?
P: Lord, Your word is living and powerful, sharper than any sword. As I read it, let it read me, and search the thoughts and intents of my heart. Amen.

== May 08
Q: What fading thing have you been leaning on that you could exchange for God's unchanging Word?
P: Lord, the grass withers and the flower fades, but Your word stands for ever. Anchor me today in the Word that never changes. Amen.

== May 09
Q: Has your soul been fed by God's Word today, or only your body by bread?
P: Lord, I do not live by bread alone but by every word that proceeds out of Your mouth. Feed my soul with Your Word today, as surely as You feed my body. Amen.

== May 10
Q: What has God shown you in His Word that you could act on today?
P: Lord, keep me from hearing Your Word without doing it. Show me one thing to put into practice today, and give me the grace to obey. Amen.

== May 11
Q: Is there something Jesus has said that you call Him Lord about but have not yet done?
P: Lord Jesus, I do not want to call You Lord and not do what You say. Search my heart, and bring my everyday choices into line with my words. Amen.

== May 12
Q: Which part of your life is built on sand, and how could hearing and doing Jesus' words strengthen it?
P: Lord Jesus, I want to build my house upon the rock. When the storms come, let my life stand firm because I have heard Your words and done them. Amen.

== May 13
Q: How could your love for Jesus show itself in obedience today?
P: Lord Jesus, You said that if we love You, we will keep Your commandments. Let my obedience flow from love, not fear, and let my love be seen in how I live. Amen.

== May 14
Q: Is there something God has asked of you that you have been replacing with a religious gesture?
P: Lord, to obey is better than sacrifice. Keep me from offering You gestures in place of obedience. Give me a listening heart that does what You ask. Amen.

== May 15
Q: Which verse could you carry with you today and let settle in your heart?
P: Lord, let Your words be in my heart, not just on a page. Let them shape my desires and my decisions, and keep me close to You today. Amen.

== May 16
Q: What ordinary moment today could you use to talk about God with someone at home?
P: Lord, help me speak of You at home, along the way, at bedtime, and in the morning. Let my faith be passed on through simple, everyday conversation. Amen.

== May 17
Q: Where is your soul weary, and how could God's Word restore it today?
P: Lord, Your law is perfect, restoring the soul, and Your testimony makes wise the simple. Restore my weary heart today through Your Word. Amen.

== May 18
Q: What would help you savour God's Word slowly today instead of rushing through it?
P: Lord, Your words are more to be desired than gold and sweeter than honey. Give me a taste for Your Word, and help me savour it slowly today. Amen.

== May 19
Q: What wonder might you see in Scripture today if you read expecting God to open your eyes?
P: Lord, open my eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of Your law. I cannot see them on my own. Show me the marvels of Your Word today. Amen.

== May 20
Q: What feels unclear in your life right now that you could bring into the light of God's Word?
P: Lord, the entrance of Your words gives light and understanding to the simple. Shine Your Word into what feels confused in my life, and give me understanding. Amen.

== May 21
Q: In a hard season, how could you take in God's Word deeply enough for it to become your joy?
P: Lord, Your words were found, and I did eat them, and they became the joy of my heart. Even in hardship, let Your Word nourish me and fill me with joy. Amen.

== May 22
Q: What have you heard lately that you could test carefully against God's Word?
P: Lord, give me an eager heart to receive Your truth, and a careful mind to search the Scriptures. Help me hold together openness and discernment. Amen.

== May 23
Q: How could you look for Jesus as you read God's Word today?
P: Lord Jesus, the Scriptures testify of You. Keep me from reading for knowledge alone. Show me Yourself in Your Word, and draw me to come to You. Amen.

== May 24
Q: If your faith feels weak, how could you feed it with God's Word today, and with whom could you share it?
P: Lord, faith comes by hearing, and hearing by Your word. Where my faith feels weak, strengthen it through Scripture, and help me share Your word with someone else. Amen.

== May 25
Q: What passage could you read more carefully today to understand what God actually means?
P: Lord, help me study Your Word with diligence and care, as a workman who needs not to be ashamed. Keep me from careless reading, and lead me into Your truth. Amen.

== May 26
Q: Is there something you teach or tell others that you need to live out more fully first?
P: Lord, prepare my heart as You prepared Ezra's, to seek Your law, to do it, and then to teach it. Let what I say to others first be true in my own life. Amen.

== May 27
Q: Is there a passage that confuses you, and who could help you understand it?
P: Lord, I want not only to hear Your Word but to understand it. Give me wisdom and good teachers, and help me make Your Word clear to others too. Amen.

== May 28
Q: What is God asking of you at this stage of your life, whatever your age?
P: Lord, Abram departed as You had spoken, even at seventy-five. Whatever my age or stage of life, help me respond to Your call with ready obedience. Amen.

== May 29
Q: Where is God asking you to step forward without seeing where the road leads?
P: Lord, by faith Abraham went out, not knowing where he went. When I cannot see the road ahead, help me trust You and take the step You ask of me. Amen.

== May 30
Q: What problem could you bring to Jesus today, ready to do whatever He tells you?
P: Lord Jesus, I bring my need to You, and I am ready to do whatever You say, even when it seems strange. Work in my life as You did at Cana. Amen.

== May 31
Q: Where is weariness or past experience keeping you from obeying what Jesus says?
P: Lord Jesus, even when I am weary and nothing has worked, I will say, nevertheless at Your word. Help me obey You and trust You with the results. Amen.
