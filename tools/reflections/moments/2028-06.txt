# June 2028: Endurance & Standing Firm (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Jun 01
Q: What trial are you facing, and how might it look different through God's eyes?
P: Lord, help me count it all joy when I fall into trials. I do not pretend the pain away, but I ask You to let me see this trial from Your perspective. Amen.

== Jun 02
Q: How might the testing you are going through be building endurance in you?
P: Lord, the trying of my faith works patience. Like a muscle strengthened by resistance, let my faith grow stronger through this season. Do not let it be wasted. Amen.

== Jun 03
Q: Where do you need to keep holding on today, out of love for God?
P: Lord, blessed is the one who endures temptation. Let my endurance be love holding on to You, and help me keep going today, knowing You see. Amen.

== Jun 04
Q: Where are you most tempted right now, and what way of escape might God be giving you?
P: Lord, You are faithful, and You will not let me be tempted beyond what I can bear. Open my eyes to the way of escape You provide, and give me will to take it. Amen.

== Jun 05
Q: Which piece of God's armour do you most need to put on before today's battles?
P: Lord, I put on the whole armour of God, that I may stand against the wiles of the devil. Clothe me with Your truth, faith, and Word before I face today. Amen.

== Jun 06
Q: Having done all you can, where do you need simply to keep standing today?
P: Lord, I have done what I can. Now help me stand. When I cannot advance, keep me from giving up ground, and hold me firm in the evil day. Amen.

== Jun 07
Q: Where do you need to be alert, steady, and courageous today, and how could love shape it?
P: Lord, help me watch, stand fast in the faith, and be strong. Give me courage under pressure, and let all that I do today be done in love. Amen.

== Jun 08
Q: What trouble are you in right now that God might be using to build lasting strength in you?
P: Lord, tribulation works patience, and patience experience, and experience hope. In my trouble today, help me trust that You are building something lasting in me. Amen.

== Jun 09
Q: Is your hope resting on circumstances, or on God's love already poured into your heart?
P: Lord, hope in You does not make ashamed, because Your love is shed abroad in my heart by the Holy Ghost. Anchor my hope in Your love today, not in my circumstances. Amen.

== Jun 10
Q: Where do you feel pressed or perplexed, and how could you trust God to keep you from being crushed?
P: Lord, I am troubled on every side, yet not distressed; perplexed, but not in despair. Your power works through my weakness. Keep me from being crushed today. Amen.

== Jun 11
Q: How does your trouble look when you set it on the scale with eternity?
P: Lord, my affliction is light and but for a moment beside the eternal weight of glory. Give me Your perspective, so I can carry my trials with hope. Amen.

== Jun 12
Q: What hardship has God not removed, and how could you rest in His sufficient grace there?
P: Lord, Your grace is sufficient for me, and Your strength is made perfect in weakness. Where You have not removed my hardship, let me rest in grace that is enough. Amen.

== Jun 13
Q: Has a hard season made you wonder if God has forgotten you, and how could you meet it with a steady heart?
P: Lord, help me not to think it strange when fiery trials come. Steady my heart, and remind me that hardship is no sign You have forgotten me. I am still Yours. Amen.

== Jun 14
Q: What might the Refiner be burning away in you through this trial?
P: Lord, the trial of my faith is more precious than gold that perishes. Refine me in the fire, burn away what is false, and let my tested faith bring You glory. Amen.

== Jun 15
Q: When you cannot see God in your trial, what helps you trust that He knows your way?
P: Lord, even when I cannot find You, You know the way that I take. When You have tried me, I shall come forth as gold. Help me trust Your purpose today. Amen.

== Jun 16
Q: What trouble are you facing today that you could trust your Deliverer with?
P: Lord, many are the afflictions of the righteous, but You deliver them out of them all. No trouble has the final word. I count on You as my Deliverer today. Amen.

== Jun 17
Q: What has God taught you through a hard season that comfort never could?
P: Lord, it is good for me that I have been afflicted, that I might learn Your statutes. Thank You for the lessons You have taught me through difficulty. Amen.

== Jun 18
Q: What trouble could you face today with peace, knowing Jesus has overcome the world?
P: Lord Jesus, in the world I will have tribulation, but in You I have peace. Thank You that You have overcome the world. Let me be of good cheer today. Amen.

== Jun 19
Q: What weight or sin is slowing you down, and could you lay it aside today?
P: Lord, show me every weight and the sin that so easily besets me. Help me lay them aside and run with patience the race You have set before me. Amen.

== Jun 20
Q: Where have your eyes drifted from Jesus, and how could you look back to Him today?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the author and finisher of my faith. You endured the cross for the joy set before You. Keep my eyes on You as I run today. Amen.

== Jun 21
Q: When you feel worn down today, what about Jesus' endurance could you stop and consider?
P: Lord Jesus, You endured such hostility, yet You did not give up. When I grow weary and faint in my mind, help me stop and consider You. Amen.

== Jun 22
Q: What good are you tired of doing that you could keep planting today?
P: Lord, help me not to be weary in well doing, for in due season I shall reap if I faint not. Keep me planting faithfully, and let the harvest come in Your time. Amen.

== Jun 23
Q: What tiredness could you bring to God today and exchange for His strength?
P: Lord, those who wait upon You shall renew their strength. I bring You my weariness, and I wait on You. Lift me up on wings like eagles today. Amen.

== Jun 24
Q: Where do you need to choose courage today because God goes with you?
P: Lord, You command me to be strong and of a good courage, for You are with me wherever I go. Help me choose courage today because You are near. Amen.

== Jun 25
Q: What has failed you lately, and how does God's promise never to fail or forsake you help?
P: Lord, You go with me, and You will not fail me nor forsake me. When people and plans let me down, I hold on to Your faithful presence. Amen.

== Jun 26
Q: What small step of courage could you take today, trusting God to strengthen your heart?
P: Lord, my hope is in You. Help me be of good courage and take the next step, and strengthen my heart as You have promised. Amen.

== Jun 27
Q: What giant are you facing, and how could you face it in the name of the Lord?
P: Lord of hosts, I do not face my giants in my own strength. I come in Your name. Let my confidence rest in Your power and not in my weapons. Amen.

== Jun 28
Q: When no one else is encouraging you, how could you encourage yourself in the Lord today?
P: Lord my God, when everything around me falls apart and no one stands with me, I turn to You. Strengthen me as You strengthened David, and remind me of Your faithfulness. Amen.

== Jun 29
Q: What steady habit of prayer could you build now that would hold you in times of testing?
P: Lord, Daniel prayed as he always had, even under pressure. Help me build steady habits of prayer now, so that my faith holds firm when testing comes. Amen.

== Jun 30
Q: What would it look like to keep fighting, running, and believing today?
P: Lord, help me fight a good fight, finish my course, and keep the faith. I do not ask for an easy life, but for a faithful one that endures to the end. Amen.
