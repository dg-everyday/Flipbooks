# July 2028: Joy, Praise & Worship (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Jul 01
Q: Where do you need the joy of the Lord to be your strength today?
P: Lord, the joy of the Lord is my strength. Where I am weak or downcast, fill me with joy rooted in You, and give me fresh courage for today. Amen.

== Jul 02
Q: What earthly joy have you been expecting to satisfy you that only God can?
P: Lord, in Your presence is fulness of joy, and at Your right hand are pleasures for evermore. Thank You for every good gift, but satisfy me with Yourself today. Amen.

== Jul 03
Q: How could you do today's work gladly, as service to the Lord?
P: Lord, I want to serve You with gladness and come before You with singing. Let my work today flow from gratitude, not fear, and let it be done for You. Amen.

== Jul 04
Q: Before you ask God for anything today, what could you thank and praise Him for?
P: Lord, I enter Your gates with thanksgiving and Your courts with praise. Before I bring You my requests, I thank You for who You are and all You have done. Amen.

== Jul 05
Q: How could you rejoice in today, even if it is not an easy one, because it is the day the Lord has made?
P: Lord, this is the day which You have made. Easy or hard, it belongs to You. Help me rejoice and be glad in it, knowing whose day it is. Amen.

== Jul 06
Q: What situation are you in today where you could still choose to rejoice in the Lord?
P: Lord, I choose to rejoice in You always, and again I say, rejoice. My joy does not rest on my circumstances but on who You are. Fill me with that joy today. Amen.

== Jul 07
Q: How could you return to joy again and again throughout today?
P: Lord, help me rejoice evermore. Let a steady gladness run beneath every part of my day, as I remember again and again who You are. Amen.

== Jul 08
Q: How could praise become your first response to whatever happens today?
P: Lord, I will bless You at all times; Your praise shall continually be in my mouth. Make praise my habit, and let it shape the way I see everything today. Amen.

== Jul 09
Q: What has God done for you that you could share with someone and praise Him for together?
P: Lord, I want to magnify You with others and exalt Your name together. Give me an opportunity today to share what You have done, and let our joy multiply. Amen.

== Jul 10
Q: Where could you sing to the Lord today, even quietly, with a glad heart?
P: Lord, Rock of my salvation, I make a joyful noise to You. Thank You that You delight in a glad heart, not a perfect voice. Let me sing to You today. Amen.

== Jul 11
Q: What need for control could you release as you bow your heart before your Maker today?
P: Lord my Maker, I bow and kneel before You. You are great, and I am Yours. Take my need for control as I bow my heart before You today. Amen.

== Jul 12
Q: What about God's holiness could you reflect on today until it moves your heart to worship?
P: Lord, I worship You in the beauty of holiness. Your goodness is radiant and pure. Open my eyes to the beauty of who You are, and fill me with awe today. Amen.

== Jul 13
Q: What would it mean today to tell your soul to bless the Lord, even before your feelings follow?
P: Bless the Lord, O my soul, and all that is within me, bless His holy name. Lord, I bring all of myself to worship You today, whatever I am feeling. Amen.

== Jul 14
Q: What benefits of God could you list today and thank Him for one by one?
P: Lord, I will not forget all Your benefits. Thank You for forgiving, healing, redeeming, and crowning me with lovingkindness and tender mercies. Amen.

== Jul 15
Q: How could you use some of today's breaths to praise the Lord?
P: Lord, every breath I take is Your gift. Let everything that has breath praise You, and let my breath praise You today. Amen.

== Jul 16
Q: How could you worship God today with both your heart and your mind, wherever you are?
P: Father, You are seeking those who worship You in spirit and in truth. Engage my heart and fill my mind with what is true about You, wherever I am today. Amen.

== Jul 17
Q: What would it look like to abide in Jesus today so that His joy fills you?
P: Lord Jesus, You spoke so that Your joy might remain in me and my joy might be full. Help me abide in You today, and fill me with Your joy. Amen.

== Jul 18
Q: Where do you need God to fill you with joy and peace as you trust Him today?
P: God of hope, fill me with all joy and peace in believing, that I may abound in hope through the power of the Holy Ghost. Renew my trust in You today. Amen.

== Jul 19
Q: If you are in a night season, what helps you hold on until the morning?
P: Lord, weeping may endure for a night, but joy comes in the morning. If I am in the night, help me hold on, trusting that morning is coming. Amen.

== Jul 20
Q: What great things has God done for you that you could name and rejoice in today?
P: Lord, You have done great things for me, and I am glad. Help me remember Your works today, and let my remembering turn into joy and praise. Amen.

== Jul 21
Q: What are you sowing in tears that you could keep sowing in faith today?
P: Lord, those who sow in tears shall reap in joy. Even when I am weeping, help me keep sowing faithfully, trusting You for the harvest. Amen.

== Jul 22
Q: What heaviness could you bring to Jesus today in exchange for the garment of praise?
P: Lord, You give beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, and the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness. I bring You my heaviness, and I put on praise. Amen.

== Jul 23
Q: What fear could you answer today by declaring that God is your strength and your song?
P: Lord, You are my salvation; I will trust and not be afraid. You are my strength and my song. Let this be the song of my heart today. Amen.

== Jul 24
Q: What deliverance in your life could you remember and turn into a song today?
P: Lord, You are my strength and song, and You have become my salvation. As I remember how You have rescued me, let my thanks rise up in praise today. Amen.

== Jul 25
Q: Is fear of what others think holding back your worship, and how could you give God your whole heart today?
P: Lord, David worshipped You with all his might and did not care how he looked. Free me from fear of others' opinions, and let me worship You with my whole heart. Amen.

== Jul 26
Q: What story of God's faithfulness in your life could you share with someone today?
P: Lord, You lifted me out of the pit and put a new song in my mouth. Let my praise be a witness, and help me share my story so that others may trust You. Amen.

== Jul 27
Q: What would change in your day if you truly believed God's lovingkindness is better than life?
P: Lord, Your lovingkindness is better than life; therefore my lips shall praise You. Let the greatness of Your love move me to worship today. Amen.

== Jul 28
Q: Who could you pray for today, longing for them to return to God?
P: Lord, there is joy in heaven over one sinner who repents. Give me Your glad heart for the lost. I pray for those I know who are far from You. Amen.

== Jul 29
Q: How might your day change if you let it sink in that God rejoices over you?
P: Lord, You are in the midst of Your people, mighty to save, and You rejoice over me with singing. Help me believe that You delight in me, and rest in Your love today. Amen.

== Jul 30
Q: How could you begin and end today with thanks to the Lord?
P: Lord, it is a good thing to give thanks to You. Let me declare Your lovingkindness in the morning and Your faithfulness every night. Amen.

== Jul 31
Q: What could you tell God today about why He is worthy of your praise?
P: Lord, You are worthy to receive glory and honour and power, for You created all things. I join heaven's worship today and lay my crowns before You. Amen.
