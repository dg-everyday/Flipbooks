# August 2028: Generosity & Good Works (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Aug 01
Q: How could you discover the joy of giving in some way today?
P: Lord Jesus, You said it is more blessed to give than to receive. Free me from always wanting more, and let me find real blessing in giving today. Amen.

== Aug 02
Q: Is there a gift you have been giving grudgingly that you could give gladly instead?
P: Lord, You love a cheerful giver, and You have been so generous to me. Take away any grudging spirit, and let me give gladly from a thankful heart. Amen.

== Aug 03
Q: Where could you sow generously today with your time, kindness, or resources?
P: Lord, help me sow bountifully and not sparingly. Let me be generous with my time, my kindness, and what I have, and trust You for the harvest. Amen.

== Aug 04
Q: In what way, besides money, could you be generous with someone today?
P: Lord Jesus, You promised good measure, pressed down and running over. Make me generous with mercy, kindness, and patience, as well as with what I own. Amen.

== Aug 05
Q: If you feel dry today, whom could you refresh with encouragement or help?
P: Lord, the one who waters others shall be watered also. If I feel dry, help me pour out encouragement to someone else, and refresh me as I do. Amen.

== Aug 06
Q: Who in need could you show compassion to today, knowing God takes it personally?
P: Lord, showing pity to the poor is lending to You. Thank You that no kindness to the needy is wasted. Show me someone I can help today. Amen.

== Aug 07
Q: Are you giving God your first and best, or what is left over?
P: Lord, I want to honour You with my substance and the firstfruits of all I have. Help me give You my first and best as a statement of trust in You. Amen.

== Aug 08
Q: What good have you been meaning to do that you could do today?
P: Lord, keep me from withholding good when it is in my power to do it. Forgive my delays, and help me do the good I have been putting off today. Amen.

== Aug 09
Q: Where is fear of not having enough holding back your giving?
P: Lord, You invited Your people to prove You and see the windows of heaven opened. Help me trust You to provide, and let that trust free me to give. Amen.

== Aug 10
Q: What could you give today with your whole heart, however small it seems?
P: Lord Jesus, You saw the widow's two mites and called them more than all the rest. Thank You that You measure the heart, not the size. Help me give what I can wholeheartedly. Amen.

== Aug 11
Q: What generous thing could you do in secret today, for God's eyes only?
P: Father, You see in secret. Free me from the need to be noticed, and let me give quietly today, trusting that You see and that Your reward is enough. Amen.

== Aug 12
Q: How could an ordinary good work today point someone toward God instead of toward you?
P: Father, let my light so shine that others may see my good works and glorify You. Let my life point people to You, not to myself. Amen.

== Aug 13
Q: Who among the least around you could you serve today as if serving Christ?
P: Lord Jesus, what I do for the least of these, I do for You. Open my eyes to the hungry, the stranger, the sick, and the lonely, and let me serve You through them. Amen.

== Aug 14
Q: What simple kindness, like a cup of cold water, could you offer today?
P: Lord Jesus, You notice even a cup of cold water given in Your name. Help me not to overlook small kindnesses, and show me one I can offer today. Amen.

== Aug 15
Q: What good work might God have prepared in advance for you to walk in today?
P: Lord, I am Your workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works. Show me the good works You have prepared for me today, and help me walk in them. Amen.

== Aug 16
Q: How could you think ahead today about blessing someone, and then follow through?
P: Lord, help me be careful to maintain good works. Give me thoughtfulness to plan how I can bless someone today, and faithfulness to follow through. Amen.

== Aug 17
Q: Who is watching your example, and what would you want them to copy?
P: Lord, make me a pattern of good works in all things. Help me live today in a way I would be glad for others to follow, for Your sake. Amen.

== Aug 18
Q: What opportunity to do good might come your way today, and how could you be ready for it?
P: Lord, as I have opportunity, help me do good to all. Open my eyes to the chances You give me today, and let me not let them slip by. Amen.

== Aug 19
Q: What sacrifice of goodness or sharing could you offer God today?
P: Lord, to do good and to share are sacrifices that please You. Help me not to forget them, even when they cost me something. Amen.

== Aug 20
Q: Where are you tempted to trust in what you have rather than in the God who gives it?
P: Lord, keep me from trusting in uncertain riches. You richly give me all things to enjoy. Let my trust rest in You, the Giver, not in the gifts. Amen.

== Aug 21
Q: Whatever your finances, how could you be rich in good works today?
P: Lord, make me rich in good works, ready to give and willing to share. Help me lay up treasure that lasts beyond this life. Amen.

== Aug 22
Q: Whom could you help today, and might God lift your own gloom as you do?
P: Lord, as I pour myself out for the hungry and afflicted, let my light rise in the darkness. Use my serving to bless others and to lift my own heart. Amen.

== Aug 23
Q: Who near you is in need, and how could you open your hand to them today?
P: Lord, the needs around me are great, but they are no excuse to close my hand. Open my heart and my hand wide to someone in need today. Amen.

== Aug 24
Q: Who needs you to be a neighbour today, whether or not you think they deserve it?
P: Lord Jesus, You told me to go and do likewise. Help me stop asking who deserves my love, and show mercy to whoever needs a neighbour today. Amen.

== Aug 25
Q: What simple skill of yours could you use to bless someone today?
P: Lord, Dorcas used a simple skill to bless many. Show me how to use the gifts and skills You have given me to serve someone today. Amen.

== Aug 26
Q: What do you have that you could hold more loosely and share with someone in need?
P: Lord, the early believers were of one heart and shared so that none lacked. Loosen my grip on what I own, and help me share with someone in need. Amen.

== Aug 27
Q: How could remembering what Christ gave up for you inspire your generosity today?
P: Lord Jesus, though You were rich, You became poor for my sake, that I might become rich. Let gratitude for Your generosity make me generous today. Amen.

== Aug 28
Q: What could you offer God today from your little, trusting Him to stretch it?
P: Lord, You made the widow's flour and oil last through the famine. Help me trust You when I give from little, knowing You can stretch whatever I offer. Amen.

== Aug 29
Q: Whose need could you notice today if you looked at them with a bountiful eye?
P: Lord, give me a bountiful eye that sees the needs of others with kindness. Help me see people as You see them, and move me to give. Amen.

== Aug 30
Q: Where has the desire for more crept into your heart, and how could you let it go?
P: Lord Jesus, my life does not consist in the abundance of the things I possess. Search my heart for covetousness, and teach me to be content in You. Amen.

== Aug 31
Q: Looking at where your treasure goes, where is your heart, and where do you want it to be?
P: Lord Jesus, where my treasure is, there my heart will be also. Help me invest my treasure where I want my heart to be, in You and in Your kingdom. Amen.
