# September 2028: Patience & Waiting on God (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Sep 01
Q: What are you waiting for right now, and what would it take courage to do while you wait on the Lord?
P: Lord, I wait on You. Give me courage while I wait, and strengthen my heart as You have promised. I will wait, I say, on You. Amen.

== Sep 02
Q: What has you fretting today that you could bring to God and rest in Him instead?
P: Lord, I will rest in You and wait patiently for You. Take away my fretting, and restore my peace as I trust You with what I cannot control. Amen.

== Sep 03
Q: What cry could you keep bringing to God today, trusting that He leans in to hear?
P: Lord, I waited patiently for You, and You inclined unto me and heard my cry. Even when the waiting is long, help me believe You are near and listening. Amen.

== Sep 04
Q: How have you found God good while you were waiting for something?
P: Lord, You are good to those who wait for You and seek You. As I wait today, let me seek You, and let me find You good. Amen.

== Sep 05
Q: Where could you replace frantic demanding with quiet, hopeful waiting today?
P: Lord, it is good to hope and quietly wait for Your salvation. Calm my anxious demands, and teach me the quiet trust of one who knows You are good. Amen.

== Sep 06
Q: What might God be preparing for you during this season of waiting?
P: Lord, no eye has seen what You have prepared for those who wait for You. Help me trust that my waiting is not wasted, and that You are preparing something good. Amen.

== Sep 07
Q: Which promise of God are you holding on to while it seems to tarry?
P: Lord, Your vision is for an appointed time. Though it tarry, it will surely come. Help me hold on to Your promises and wait for Your time, which is never late. Amen.

== Sep 08
Q: What season are you in right now, and how could you embrace it rather than resist it?
P: Lord, to every thing there is a season. Show me what season I am in, help me embrace it, and let me trust You with its timing. Amen.

== Sep 09
Q: What tangled part of your life could you trust God to make beautiful in His time?
P: Lord, You make everything beautiful in its time. I cannot see Your whole work, but I trust You are weaving something beautiful in my life. Amen.

== Sep 10
Q: What part is yours to do faithfully today, and what must you wait on God to provide?
P: Lord, like the farmer waiting for the rain, help me do my part faithfully and wait patiently for what only You can give. Amen.

== Sep 11
Q: What changing circumstance could you face with a heart established in the hope of Christ's return?
P: Lord, help me be patient and establish my heart, for Your coming draws near. When circumstances shift, keep my heart steady and anchored in You. Amen.

== Sep 12
Q: How could rejoicing in hope, patience in trouble, and steady prayer shape your day?
P: Lord, help me rejoice in hope, be patient in tribulation, and continue instant in prayer. Let prayer keep my hope and patience alive today. Amen.

== Sep 13
Q: Where do you most need the Spirit to grow patience in you?
P: Lord, longsuffering is fruit of Your Spirit, not something I can produce on my own. Keep me close to You today, and grow patience in me. Amen.

== Sep 14
Q: Is pride making you impatient anywhere, and how could humility help you keep going to the end?
P: Lord, the end of a thing is better than its beginning, and the patient in spirit is better than the proud. Humble me, and help me patiently finish well. Amen.

== Sep 15
Q: When anger rises today, what could help you pause and seek understanding before you respond?
P: Lord, the one who is slow to wrath is of great understanding. When anger rises today, help me pause, see more clearly, and respond with wisdom. Amen.

== Sep 16
Q: Where do you most need God's help to rule your own spirit?
P: Lord, ruling my own spirit is greater than taking a city. Show me where I most lack self-control, and give me Your strength to rule my spirit there. Amen.

== Sep 17
Q: In which conversation today could you practise listening first?
P: Lord, make me swift to hear, slow to speak, and slow to wrath. Help me listen well today before I speak, and fill my conversations with Your peace. Amen.

== Sep 18
Q: Who could you share God's love with during the time His patience gives?
P: Lord, You are not slack concerning Your promise, but patient, not willing that any should perish. Thank You for Your mercy. Help me share Your love with someone today. Amen.

== Sep 19
Q: Where is impatience rising in you, and how could God's eternal view of time calm it?
P: Lord, with You one day is as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day. You are never rushed and never late. Let me rest in Your timing today. Amen.

== Sep 20
Q: Is there a promise of God you are tempted to give up on, and what helps you keep waiting?
P: Lord, You visited Sarah at the set time You had spoken. Your timing may seem late, but it is never wrong. Help me hold on to Your promises today. Amen.

== Sep 21
Q: How could a deeper love for God change the way you experience waiting?
P: Lord, Jacob's love made seven years seem like a few days. Deepen my love for You, so that my waiting becomes devotion rather than frustration. Amen.

== Sep 22
Q: What long-held prayer could you bring to God again today?
P: Lord, Hannah prayed through years of grief, and You heard her. I bring You my long-held prayers again today, trusting that You hear and care. Amen.

== Sep 23
Q: If you are in a dark season, how could you watch for God like a watchman waits for morning?
P: Lord, my soul waits for You more than they that watch for the morning. In my dark season, give me eager hope that Your coming is as sure as the dawn. Amen.

== Sep 24
Q: Who has disappointed you, and how could you turn your eyes to the Lord instead?
P: Lord, when people disappoint me, I will look unto You. I will wait for the God of my salvation, for You will hear me. Amen.

== Sep 25
Q: Is there something you have been holding back from God, though He waits to be gracious to you?
P: Lord, You wait so that You may be gracious to me and have mercy on me. I turn to You today, thankful that You are eager to bless and not reluctant. Amen.

== Sep 26
Q: What would faithful, active waiting look like for you today?
P: Lord, Simeon was just and devout while he waited, and You kept Your word to him. Help me live faithfully as I wait, trusting You to keep Your promises. Amen.

== Sep 27
Q: Where is God calling you to endure patiently as Abraham did?
P: Lord, Abraham patiently endured and obtained the promise. Your promises are sure. Give me patience to endure, and keep me from giving up. Amen.

== Sep 28
Q: Have you done what God asked and are still waiting, and how could you be patient in that gap?
P: Lord, I have need of patience, that after doing Your will I might receive the promise. Fill the gap between my obedience and Your answer with patient trust. Amen.

== Sep 29
Q: Where do you need God's strength to wait with joy instead of frustration today?
P: Lord, strengthen me with all might, according to Your glorious power, unto all patience and longsuffering with joyfulness. Help me wait with joy today. Amen.

== Sep 30
Q: What season or answer could you entrust into God's hand as this month ends?
P: Lord, my times are in Your hand. Every season and every answer rests with You. I entrust my times to You and rest in Your care. Amen.
