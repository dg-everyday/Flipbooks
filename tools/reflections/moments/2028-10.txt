# October 2028: Integrity & Holy Living (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Oct 01
Q: Which of these three could you live out in an ordinary way today: doing justly, loving mercy, or walking humbly?
P: Lord, You have shown me what is good: to do justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with You. Help me live these out in ordinary ways today. Amen.

== Oct 02
Q: Where might the upright path cost you something today, and will you choose it anyway?
P: Lord, the one who walks uprightly walks surely. Give me courage to choose honesty today, even where it costs me, and let me enjoy the freedom of a clear conscience. Amen.

== Oct 03
Q: Which decision today could become clearer if you let integrity guide it?
P: Lord, let integrity guide my choices today. Keep me from crooked ways, and make my path clear as I commit myself to honesty before You. Amen.

== Oct 04
Q: What honest choice today could become a blessing to those who come after you?
P: Lord, the just one who walks in integrity leaves a blessing to their children. Help me make honest choices today that bless others for years to come. Amen.

== Oct 05
Q: Is there anything in your heart you have not been completely honest with God about?
P: Lord, I want to speak the truth in my heart. Help me be completely honest with You today, and with myself, about what is really inside me. Amen.

== Oct 06
Q: Where do your actions or your motives need God's cleansing today?
P: Lord, I want clean hands and a pure heart. You care about what I do and why I do it. Cleanse my actions, purify my motives, and keep my words honest. Amen.

== Oct 07
Q: What in your heart do you need God to make new, not just patch up?
P: Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me. Only You can make my heart new. Do Your creating work in me today. Amen.

== Oct 08
Q: Are you willing to ask God to search your heart today, trusting He searches to heal?
P: Search me, O God, and know my heart; try me, and know my thoughts. I open my heart to You, trusting that You search me to heal and not to condemn. Amen.

== Oct 09
Q: What might God want to change in you today so He can lead you on a better path?
P: Lord, see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting. Show me what needs to change, and give me grace to follow where You lead. Amen.

== Oct 10
Q: Before you speak to anyone today, could you pray that your words and thoughts would please God?
P: Lord, let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in Your sight, O Lord, my strength and my redeemer. Amen.

== Oct 11
Q: What is dividing your heart between God and the world right now?
P: Lord, blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God. Remove whatever divides my heart, and give me single-minded devotion to You. Amen.

== Oct 12
Q: Is there a promise, large or small, you need to keep today?
P: Lord Jesus, let my yes be yes and my no be no. Make me reliable in my word, and help me keep every promise I make today. Amen.

== Oct 13
Q: What area of your life have you kept separate from God that you could invite His holiness into?
P: Lord, You who called me are holy. Make me holy in all my conduct, not just in some moments. I invite You into the part of my life I have kept apart from You. Amen.

== Oct 14
Q: What would it look like to offer yourself to God again today as a living sacrifice?
P: Lord, in view of all Your mercies, I present myself to You as a living sacrifice. Take all of me again today, for this is my reasonable service. Amen.

== Oct 15
Q: Where is the world pressing you into its mould, and how could God's Word renew your mind there?
P: Lord, keep me from being conformed to this world. Transform me by the renewing of my mind, and fill my thinking with Your truth today. Amen.

== Oct 16
Q: Which of your thoughts today needs to pass through Paul's filter of what is true, pure, and lovely?
P: Lord, help me think on whatever is true, honest, just, pure, lovely, and of good report. Guard my thoughts today, and shape my character through them. Amen.

== Oct 17
Q: What could you settle in your heart today before temptation comes?
P: Lord, Daniel purposed in his heart not to defile himself. Help me decide ahead of time what I will and will not do, and give me wisdom and grace to stand. Amen.

== Oct 18
Q: Where are you tempted today, and how does remembering that sin is against God help you stand?
P: Lord, how can I do this great wickedness and sin against You? When temptation comes today, help me stand firm as Joseph did, and be with me as You were with him. Amen.

== Oct 19
Q: Is anyone pressuring you to compromise the truth, and how could you hold fast to it today?
P: Lord, like Job, I will hold fast my integrity and not let it go. When others press me to compromise, give me courage to hold on to what is true. Amen.

== Oct 20
Q: What boundaries do you need to set with your eyes today?
P: Lord, help me make a covenant with my eyes. Guard what I choose to look at, keep my heart pure, and give me strength to turn away from what dishonours You. Amen.

== Oct 21
Q: What have you been allowing into your heart that you need to guard against?
P: Lord, help me keep my heart with all diligence, for out of it are the issues of life. Show me what I am letting in, and help me guard my heart well today. Amen.

== Oct 22
Q: Where could you choose good character over gain today?
P: Lord, a good name is rather to be chosen than great riches. Help me value trustworthy character above money, and choose what is right today. Amen.

== Oct 23
Q: Where could you be more careful to deal truly in your words and actions today?
P: Lord, lying lips are an abomination to You, but those who deal truly are Your delight. Make me honest in every word and every action today. Amen.

== Oct 24
Q: Is there someone you need to speak the truth to in love today?
P: Lord, help me put away lying and speak truth with those around me, for we are members one of another. Let my truth always be spoken in love. Amen.

== Oct 25
Q: Before you speak today, how could you make sure your words will build someone up?
P: Lord, let no corrupt communication come out of my mouth. Let my words build others up and give grace to those who hear them today. Amen.

== Oct 26
Q: Which task today could you do heartily, as for the Lord and not for people?
P: Lord, whatever I do today, help me do it heartily, as to You and not to people. Turn my work into worship, and free me from needing others' approval. Amen.

== Oct 27
Q: What small, everyday choice today could be a test of your faithfulness?
P: Lord Jesus, the one who is faithful in the least is faithful also in much. Help me be faithful in the little things today, where integrity is built. Amen.

== Oct 28
Q: In which area could you set a godly example today: word, conduct, love, spirit, faith, or purity?
P: Lord, help me be an example in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, and in purity. Let my character speak louder than my age or title. Amen.

== Oct 29
Q: What do you need to flee from today, and what do you need to pursue?
P: Lord, help me flee what draws me from You and follow righteousness, faith, love, and peace. Surround me with companions who call on You from a pure heart. Amen.

== Oct 30
Q: Is there anything you are hiding from God that you could bring into the light today?
P: Lord, I want to walk in the light as You are in the light. I hide nothing from You today. Thank You that the blood of Jesus cleanses me from all sin. Amen.

== Oct 31
Q: Where is integrity most tested for you at home, and what commitment could you make there?
P: Lord, help me walk within my house with a perfect heart. Let my integrity be real at home first, where it matters most and is tested most. Amen.
