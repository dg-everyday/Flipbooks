# November 2028: The Character of God (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Nov 01
Q: Which of God's own words about Himself, merciful, gracious, longsuffering, or abundant in goodness, do you most need today?
P: Lord, You are merciful and gracious, longsuffering, and abundant in goodness and truth. Thank You for describing Yourself this way, even to people who had failed You. I rest in who You are. Amen.

== Nov 02
Q: What are you facing today that you could lean on the great I AM for?
P: Lord, You are I AM THAT I AM. You depend on nothing, and You have no beginning or end. Whatever I face today, I lean on You. Amen.

== Nov 03
Q: When your own faithfulness wavers, how does God's unchanging nature give you hope?
P: Lord, You do not change, and that is why I am not consumed. Thank You that my hope rests on Your faithfulness, not mine. Amen.

== Nov 04
Q: Which promise of God do you need to trust He will keep today?
P: Lord, You are not a man that You should lie. What You have spoken, You will do. Help me trust Your promises today, every one of them. Amen.

== Nov 05
Q: What are you leaning on that is shifting, and how could you rest your full weight on the Rock?
P: Lord, You are the Rock; Your work is perfect, and all Your ways are just and true. In a shifting world, I rest my full weight on You today. Amen.

== Nov 06
Q: How does seeing God's holiness change the way you see yourself and His grace?
P: Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts. Like Isaiah, I see my own need before You. Thank You for Your cleansing grace that takes my sin away. Amen.

== Nov 07
Q: Where are you weary, and how does it help to know that God never faints or grows tired?
P: Everlasting God, You never faint and never grow weary, and Your understanding is beyond searching. When I run out of strength, let me rest in Yours. Amen.

== Nov 08
Q: Where have God's ways surprised you, and how could you trust Him there?
P: Lord, Your thoughts are not my thoughts, and Your ways are not my ways. Thank You for Your abundant willingness to forgive. Help me trust You when You surprise me. Amen.

== Nov 09
Q: What situation could you look up from today and trust to God's higher ways?
P: Lord, as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are Your ways higher than mine. Thank You that Your mercy and plans are greater than I can imagine. I trust You today. Amen.

== Nov 10
Q: What impossible situation could you bring to the God who made heaven and earth today?
P: Ah Lord GOD, You made the heaven and the earth by Your great power, and there is nothing too hard for You. I bring You my impossible situation today. Amen.

== Nov 11
Q: Where do you still feel you must earn God's love, and how does His everlasting love answer that?
P: Lord, You have loved me with an everlasting love, and with lovingkindness You have drawn me. Let this truth sink deep into my heart today. Amen.

== Nov 12
Q: Wherever you go today, how could you remember that God is there?
P: Lord, there is nowhere I can go from Your presence. Wherever I am today, You are there. Thank You for the comfort of Your nearness. Amen.

== Nov 13
Q: What could you thank God for about the way He made you, and how could you see others as His work too?
P: Lord, I will praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Thank You for the care You took in making me, and help me see others as Your marvellous works too. Amen.

== Nov 14
Q: If God knows every star by name, how does it change things to know He knows yours?
P: Lord, You count the stars and call them all by name. You are vast in power, yet You know me personally. Thank You that I am never lost to You. Amen.

== Nov 15
Q: What broken place in your heart could you bring to God for healing today?
P: Lord, You heal the broken in heart and bind up their wounds. You see the pain I hide. I bring my broken heart to You and ask for Your patient, healing care. Amen.

== Nov 16
Q: Where do you need God's fatherly compassion for your weakness today?
P: Father, as a father pities his children, so You pity those who fear You. You know my frame and remember that I am dust. Show me Your compassion today. Amen.

== Nov 17
Q: How does knowing your short life is held by an eternal God bring you peace today?
P: Lord, from everlasting to everlasting, You are God. My life is short, but it is held in Your eternal hands. I rest today in the One who was before all things. Amen.

== Nov 18
Q: What plan of yours has fallen apart, and how could you trust God's plans instead?
P: Lord, Your counsel stands for ever, and the thoughts of Your heart to all generations. When my plans fall apart, help me trust Yours. Amen.

== Nov 19
Q: Where do you feel unseen right now, and how does it help to know God sees you?
P: Lord, You are the God who sees. When I feel invisible or overlooked, remind me that You see me and know exactly where I am. Amen.

== Nov 20
Q: What need could you trust the Lord to provide for in a way you cannot plan?
P: Lord, You are Jehovah-jireh, the Lord who provides. You see my needs before I do. Help me trust You to provide in ways I cannot plan. Amen.

== Nov 21
Q: What bitter place in your life could you bring to the Lord who heals?
P: Lord, You made the bitter waters sweet and revealed Yourself as the Lord who heals. I bring You my bitter places today. Heal me in body, heart, and life. Amen.

== Nov 22
Q: Where is fear keeping you hidden, and how could you receive God's peace there today?
P: Lord, You are Jehovah-shalom, the Lord who is peace. When fear has me hiding, speak peace to me as You did to Gideon. Amen.

== Nov 23
Q: How could God's love flow through you to someone today?
P: Lord, You are love. Let that truth sink deep into my heart, and let Your love flow through me to the people I meet today. Amen.

== Nov 24
Q: What part of your life could you bring into God's light today?
P: Lord, You are light, and in You is no darkness at all. Thank You for Your perfect goodness. Help me step into Your light and walk there today. Amen.

== Nov 25
Q: How has a trial shown you something about God that comfort never could?
P: Lord, there is none holy like You, and there is no rock like our God. Thank You for showing Yourself through every trial and every answer. I praise You for who You are. Amen.

== Nov 26
Q: What in creation could you look at today to remind you of God's greatness?
P: Lord, You laid the foundations of the earth. When I do not have all the answers, let a fresh vision of Your greatness be enough for me today. Amen.

== Nov 27
Q: How would it reshape your day to see that all things are of God, through God, and to God?
P: Lord, of You, and through You, and to You are all things. My life begins, continues, and finds its purpose in You. To You be glory for ever. Amen.

== Nov 28
Q: What good things in your life could you trace back to the Father of lights today?
P: Father of lights, every good and perfect gift comes from You, and You never change. Help me trace the good in my life back to You and thank You for it today. Amen.

== Nov 29
Q: What feels uncertain right now that you could hold up against the unchanging Christ?
P: Lord Jesus, You are the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever. When everything else shifts, I hold on to You. Your love and promises never change. Amen.

== Nov 30
Q: Where have you seen God's goodness to all this month, and how could you thank Him for it?
P: Lord, You are good to all, and Your tender mercies are over all Your works. As this month ends, I thank You for Your goodness to me and to all You have made. Amen.
