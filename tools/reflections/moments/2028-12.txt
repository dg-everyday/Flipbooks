# December 2028: Eternal Hope & Heaven (Daily Moment questions and prayers)

== Dec 01
Q: What troubles your heart today that the promise of a place prepared for you could calm?
P: Lord Jesus, in Your Father's house are many mansions, and You have gone to prepare a place for me. When my heart is troubled, let me rest in that assurance. Amen.

== Dec 02
Q: How does the hope of being with Jesus forever lift your heart today?
P: Lord Jesus, You promised to come again and receive me to Yourself, that where You are, I may be also. Let the hope of being with You lift my heart today. Amen.

== Dec 03
Q: How could you give thanks today for the gift of everlasting life, or receive it for the first time?
P: Father, You so loved the world that You gave Your only begotten Son. Thank You for everlasting life that begins now with You and never ends. I believe. Amen.

== Dec 04
Q: Where have you been trying to hold on in your own strength instead of resting in Jesus' hand?
P: Lord Jesus, You give eternal life, and no one can pluck me out of Your hand. My security rests on how tightly You hold me. I rest in Your strong hand today. Amen.

== Dec 05
Q: What suffering are you carrying that you could weigh against the glory to come?
P: Lord, the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory that shall be revealed. Help me weigh my trials against eternity today. Amen.

== Dec 06
Q: What fear of being separated from God's love could you lay down today?
P: Lord, nothing in all creation can separate me from Your love, which is in Christ Jesus my Lord. Let this unbreakable love be my anchor today. Amen.

== Dec 07
Q: How might your view of the future change if you believed God's plans exceed your best dreams?
P: Lord, eye has not seen, nor ear heard, the things You have prepared for those who love You. Enlarge my hope, and help me trust that Your plans are better than my dreams. Amen.

== Dec 08
Q: What fear of death or loss could you bring under Christ's victory over the grave?
P: Lord, O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory? Thank You for Christ's victory over death. Give me comfort and courage because of it today. Amen.

== Dec 09
Q: How could knowing your true home is eternal give you heart today?
P: Lord, if this earthly tent is dissolved, I have a building from You, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens. Thank You that my true home is with You. Amen.

== Dec 10
Q: What passing trouble could you lift your gaze from today toward what is eternal?
P: Lord, help me look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen. Lift my eyes from passing troubles to what is eternal today. Amen.

== Dec 11
Q: If you finished the sentence, for me to live is, what would you honestly write today?
P: Lord Jesus, for me to live is Christ, and to die is gain. Be my life today, and give every part of it purpose in You. Amen.

== Dec 12
Q: How could living as a citizen of heaven shape one choice you make today?
P: Lord, my citizenship is in heaven, from where I look for my Saviour. Let that true allegiance shape how I live, speak, and choose today. Amen.

== Dec 13
Q: What part of who you are in Christ are you longing to see revealed?
P: Lord Jesus, You are my life, and when You appear, I shall appear with You in glory. Thank You that my true life is hidden with You and safe until that day. Amen.

== Dec 14
Q: Whom are you missing, and how does the promise of being ever with the Lord comfort you?
P: Lord, the dead in Christ shall rise, and so shall we ever be with You. Comfort my heart with the promise of reunion, and hold on to me as I hold on to this hope. Amen.

== Dec 15
Q: Who is grieving that you could reach out to with comfort and hope today?
P: Lord, You told us to comfort one another with these words. Show me someone who is grieving today, and help me share Your hope with gentleness and love. Amen.

== Dec 16
Q: How could the blessed hope of Christ's appearing shape the way you live today?
P: Lord, I look for the blessed hope and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ. Let that hope shape how I live and how I see my troubles today. Amen.

== Dec 17
Q: Where does your hope feel weak, and how does a living Saviour make it alive?
P: Father, in Your abundant mercy You have given me a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ. My hope is alive because He is alive. Help me hold on to it today. Amen.

== Dec 18
Q: What earthly security are you clinging to that cannot compare with the inheritance kept for you?
P: Lord, You have kept for me an inheritance incorruptible, undefiled, and that fades not away. Help me hold earthly things loosely, knowing my true inheritance is safe with You. Amen.

== Dec 19
Q: Where are you frustrated by slow growth, and how does the promise that you shall be like Him encourage you?
P: Father, I am Your child now, and when Christ appears, I shall be like Him, for I shall see Him as He is. When my growth feels slow, give me patience and hope. Amen.

== Dec 20
Q: What longing in your heart might be pointing you toward the better country God has prepared?
P: Lord, I desire a better country, that is, a heavenly one. Thank You that You are not ashamed to be called my God. Let my longings point me to the city You have prepared. Amen.

== Dec 21
Q: What earthly thing could you hold more loosely today as you seek the city to come?
P: Lord, here I have no continuing city, but I seek one to come. Help me hold earthly things loosely and set my heart on the city that will never fall. Amen.

== Dec 22
Q: Looking back, where can you see that goodness and mercy have followed you?
P: Lord, surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in Your house for ever. Thank You for pursuing me with goodness. Bring me home. Amen.

== Dec 23
Q: What have you been looking to for satisfaction that only seeing God's face could truly give?
P: Lord, I shall behold Your face in righteousness, and I shall be satisfied when I awake with Your likeness. Let my hope rest on seeing You. Amen.

== Dec 24
Q: Who could you point toward God today?
P: Lord, those who turn many to righteousness shall shine as the stars for ever. Give me wisdom and love to point someone toward You today. Amen.

== Dec 25
Q: What tears could you bring to God today, holding on to the day He will wipe them all away?
P: Lord, You will swallow up death in victory and wipe away tears from off all faces. You have spoken it, and it is certain. Hold me in that hope today. Amen.

== Dec 26
Q: If you are hurting today, how does the hope of a world without pain help you hold on?
P: Lord, You will wipe away all tears, and there shall be no more death, sorrow, crying, or pain. If I am hurting today, help me hold on to that promised world. Amen.

== Dec 27
Q: What is broken in your life that you could bring to the One who makes all things new?
P: Lord, You said, Behold, I make all things new, and Your words are true and faithful. I bring You what is broken in my life, and I trust You to restore it. Amen.

== Dec 28
Q: What darkness, fear, or sorrow could you hold up against the promise of a day without night?
P: Lord God, in Your city there is no night, for You Yourself give light. Help me hold on to that promise when darkness, fear, or sorrow press on me today. Amen.

== Dec 29
Q: Where are you investing your life, and is it in treasure that lasts?
P: Lord Jesus, help me lay up treasures in heaven, where nothing can corrupt or steal them. Let my love and service for You be an investment that lasts. Amen.

== Dec 30
Q: What would loving Christ's appearing look like in the way you live today?
P: Lord, a crown of righteousness is laid up for all who love Your appearing. Thank You that no faithfulness is forgotten. Help me live today longing for Your return. Amen.

== Dec 31
Q: As this year ends, what could you thank God for, and how could you step into the new year under His grace?
P: Lord Jesus, Your grace has carried me through this whole year. I close it under Your grace, and I step into the new one wrapped in it. The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with us all. Amen.
